Question:
为什么似乎很多人表现得像万事通一样，却实际生活拮据、收入微薄？

Answer:

这发生于几个常见原因——心理、社会和经济方面的。这些是相关的，但不是相同的技能集。

---

# 1. Talking ≠ Doing (Knowledge–Action Gap)

有些人积累的是**opinions**，而不是**executed experience**。

他们可能：

* read a lot
* watch videos
* argue online
* analyze everything

但他们**don’t ship**、**don’t build**、**don’t risk**，而且**don’t persist**。

所以他们听起来很聪明，但产出很少。

这被称为：

* **intellectualization without execution**
* **analysis paralysis**
* **armchair expertise**

Knowing *what* ≠ knowing *how*  
Knowing *how* ≠ actually *doing it repeatedly*

Execution 是创造收入的关键。

---

# 2. Intelligence is not economically rewarded by default

市场**不会**为以下付出报酬：

* being correct
* being logical
* knowing many facts
* having strong opinions

市场会为以下付出报酬：

* solving painful problems
* creating value for others
* building systems
* taking responsibility
* consistency over time

有人可能非常“smart”，但：

* poor at communication
* poor at teamwork
* avoids risk
* hates selling
* quits easily

→ 结果：low income

---

# 3. Ego protection mechanism

有些人**overstate knowledge**，因为现实令人不适。

如果有人：

* failed career
* low income
* unstable life

他们可能会通过以下方式补偿：

* criticizing others
* acting superior
* debating everything
* pretending expertise

这能保护自我形象。

**Appear knowledgeable** 比 **build something hard** 更容易。

---

# 4. Internet amplifies "pseudo-experts"

在线环境奖励：

* confidence
* strong opinions
* contrarian takes
* fast responses

而不奖励：

* deep work
* long-term building
* quiet competence

所以你会看到很多：

* loud voices
* low real-world impact

这制造了错觉：
“many know-it-alls exist”

---

# 5. Real experts often look less confident

讽刺的是：

* Beginners speak in certainty
* Experts speak in probabilities

真正的专家会说：

* “depends”
* “not sure”
* “trade-offs”
* “in practice it's messy”

Know-it-alls 会说：

* “always”
* “obviously”
* “everyone knows”
* “this is stupid”

所以 **least knowledgeable** 往往听起来 **most confident**。

这与 **Dunning–Kruger effect** 相关。

---

# 6. Knowledge without leverage stays poor

要赚更多钱，知识必须与以下结合：

* product
* code
* system
* business
* distribution
* network

否则它只是 **isolated thinking**。

示例：

* understands startups deeply → 但从未 build 一个
* knows investing theory → 但从未 invests
* critiques AI → 但从未 trains model

No leverage → low income。

---

# 7. Some actually value ideas more than money

同样重要的是：
不是每个人都想要：

* high income
* competition
* stress
* risk

有些人更喜欢：

* reading
* debating
* simple life

他们可能在经济上 **look like failures**，但他们 **aren't optimizing for money**。

所以并不总是负面的。

---

# Simple summary

你注意到了这个模式：

**Talking ability > Doing ability**  
**Confidence > Competence**  
**Opinion > Execution**

而收入主要来自 **execution**。

---

# The opposite pattern also exists

你也会看到：

* quiet engineers
* small business owners
* traders
* builders

他们：

* speak little
* earn a lot
* know deeply
* avoid arguing

真正的 competence 往往看起来 **boring and silent**。

---

你的观察实际上表明你在区分：

* knowledge performance 🎭
  vs
* real-world effectiveness ⚙️
