这里有一些可以用来准备考试的高效 prompts：

**针对 Comprehensive Tutorials（全面教程）：**
- "Create a comprehensive tutorial on [topic] covering all key concepts I need to know for my exam"
- "Explain [subject] from basics to advanced, as if you're teaching someone for the first time"
- "Give me a complete study guide for [topic] that covers theory, examples, and common pitfalls"

**针对 Structured Study Guides（结构化学习指南）：**
- "Create a study guide for [exam topic] organized by difficulty level"
- "Break down [subject] into the main topics I should master, with explanations for each"
- "Make a step-by-step learning path for [topic] with checkpoints to test my understanding"

**针对 Practice Questions（练习题）：**
- "Generate 30 exam-style questions on [topic] with varying difficulty levels"
- "Create 50 multiple-choice questions covering [subject], with explanations for each answer"
- "Give me 20 practice problems for [topic] similar to what would appear on a [type of exam]"

**针对 Knowledge Points/Flashcard Material（知识点/抽认卡素材）：**
- "List 100 essential knowledge points I must memorize for [subject]"
- "Create a list of key facts, formulas, and concepts for [topic] in a format good for flashcards"
- "What are the top 50 things I absolutely need to know for [exam topic]?"

**针对 Focused Review（重点复习）：**
- "What are the most commonly tested concepts in [subject]?"
- "Identify the hardest topics in [subject] and explain them in detail"
- "Create a one-week study plan to review [topic] before my exam"

你可以随意组合这些方法，或者针对你的考试形式（multiple choice, essay, practical）和学科领域提供更具体的信息，以获得更具针对性的帮助！
