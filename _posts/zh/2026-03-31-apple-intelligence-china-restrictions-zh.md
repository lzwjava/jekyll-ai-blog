Question: Why is Apple Intelligence not showing up on my iPhone with iOS 26.4 and a US App Store account while I am in China?

Answer: Even with a **US Apple Account** and **iOS 26.4**，Apple Intelligence is subject to strict regional and hardware-level locks when used within Mainland China. There are several reasons why the setting may be missing from your device:

### 1\. Hardware Origin (The "CH/A" Lock)

If your iPhone was originally purchased in **Mainland China** (model number ends in `CH/A`), Apple Intelligence is hardware-locked. Changing your Apple Account region or your device region to the US will not enable the feature. These models are physically restricted from accessing the global version of Apple Intelligence due to regulatory requirements.

### 2\. Physical Location Tracking

Apple uses **GPS and IP-based geofencing** to determine your current location. Even if you have a US-purchased iPhone and a US Apple Account, Apple Intelligence is designed to be disabled when the device detects it is physically located within Mainland China. This is a common restriction for Apple's AI services to ensure compliance with local laws.

### 3\. Recent Launch in China

While there are reports (as of March 30, 2026) that Apple Intelligence has just begun a rollout in China, this version is likely a **localized variant** powered by a partner like Alibaba. This local version:

* Requires a **Mainland China Apple Account**.
* May not be compatible with the "global" AI features linked to a US Store account.
* Is being rolled out in stages, so it might not appear for everyone immediately.

### 4\. Language and Siri Settings

To see the setting on a compatible **non-CH/A** device, you typically must:

* Set the **Device Language** to English (US).
* Set the **Siri Language** to English (US).
* Ensure your **Region** is set to the United States.
