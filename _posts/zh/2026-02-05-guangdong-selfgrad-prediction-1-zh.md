Question: How to use the graduation prediction module in the Guangdong Province Self-Study Examination Management System?

Answer: 

The **graduation prediction module** (毕业预测模块) in the **广东省高等教育自学考试管理系统** (Guangdong Higher Education Self-Study Examination Management System) allows candidates to check whether they meet the graduation requirements for their chosen major before formally applying for graduation. The system automatically handles course substitutions (新旧课程顶替) based on professional plan changes and shows if all required courses, credits, and other conditions (e.g., practice links, thesis) are satisfied.

### Step-by-step guide to use the graduation prediction module

1. **Log in to the system**  
   Open a browser and go to the official Guangdong Self-Study Examination Management System:  
   https://www.eeagd.edu.cn/selfec/  
   Click **考生入口** (Candidate Entrance) and log in using your candidate number (准考证号) and password.

2. **Enter the graduation management section**  
   After logging in, locate and click **毕业管理** (Graduation Management) in the main menu or navigation bar.

3. **Find and enter the graduation prediction function**  
   In the graduation management module, look for and click **毕业预测** (Graduation Prediction).  
   (In some interfaces, it may appear directly under "毕业申请及图像采集" or as a separate button/option.)

4. **Select your graduation parameters**  
   Fill in or confirm the following key information (the system may auto-fill some based on your record):  
   - **毕业专业** (Graduation Major) — select the major you intend to graduate from.  
   - **建设主考学校** (Main Examining Institution) — choose the main school for your major.  
   - **承办主考学校** (Undertaking Main Examining Institution) — select the specific undertaking school (especially important for some majors, e.g., Shenzhen University).  
   Click **下一步** (Next) to proceed.

5. **Review and confirm course substitutions (if needed)**  
   The system usually performs automatic course substitution based on plan changes.  
   If you disagree with the default substitutions (e.g., due to old/new plan differences), you can manually select alternative valid courses/top substitutions in the provided interface.  
   After adjustments (if any), continue to the next step.

6. **Run the prediction and view the result**  
   Click **预测** or **提交** / **下一步** to start the prediction.  
   The system will display one of the following results:  
   - **预测成功** / **系统预测成功，以教育部最终审核结果为准** → You currently meet graduation conditions (but final approval depends on the Ministry of Education/provincial review).  
   - Failure message → Lists missing courses, insufficient credits, uncompleted practice/thesis, or other unmet requirements.  
   Take a screenshot of the result for your records (especially useful when applying for thesis or formal graduation).

### Important notes

- Graduation prediction is **free** and can be done **anytime** (not limited to graduation application windows).  
- It is **strongly recommended** to run prediction **before** applying for graduation or registering for a thesis/graduation project. Many schools require a successful prediction (only thesis remaining) as a prerequisite for thesis registration.  
- If the major has undergone multiple plan changes, carefully check and adjust course substitutions.  
- The prediction result is for reference only — the official graduation result comes after formal application and provincial/Ministry review.

Official system URL (always use this): https://www.eeagd.edu.cn/selfec/
