问题：“pickled”是什么意思？

答案：

**“Pickled”** 根据上下文有几种含义。以下是最常见的几种：

1. **食物（最常见的日常含义）**
   Pickled = 用醋、盐水或某种酸咸液体腌制保存
   示例：
   - pickled cucumbers = pickles
   - pickled onions
   - pickled herring
   - pickled ginger
   → 这种食物味道酸爽，通常脆脆的。

2. **俚语 – 非常醉/醉酒**
   非正式/幽默的方式表示某人极度醉酒
   示例：
   - "He got completely pickled at the party."
   - "She came home pickled after the bar crawl."
   → 类似于：wasted, smashed, hammered, sloshed

3. **用任何强力物质保存（较少见的比喻用法）**
   有时用于比喻：
   - "His brain is pickled from years of heavy drinking."
   - "pickled in whiskey" = 长期大量饮酒

快速用法总结：

| 上下文            | 含义                          | 示例句子                                    |
|-------------------|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Food              | Preserved in vinegar/brine    | I love pickled jalapeños on my tacos.       |
| Slang (informal)  | Extremely drunk               | Uncle Ted was pickled by midnight.          |
| Figurative        | Affected long-term (alcohol)  | His liver is completely pickled.            |

如今最主要的两种含义是：

- 腌制的酸味蔬菜/水果
- 非常醉（幽默俚语）
