**Question:** What are the iPhone 14's performance characteristics and physical dimensions?

**Answer:**

**Performance (A15 Bionic, 5-core GPU variant):**

- Chip: A15 Bionic (same as iPhone 13 Pro, not a new chip — Apple reserved the A16 for the 14 Pro that year)
- 6-core CPU (2 performance + 4 efficiency), 5-core GPU, 16-core Neural Engine (~15.8 TOPS)
- 6 GB RAM (LPDDR4X)
- Geekbench 6 ballpark: ~2,200 single-core / ~5,400 multi-core
- Still very fast in 2026 for daily use; on-device ML via Core ML / MPS is fine for small models, but it doesn't qualify for Apple Intelligence (that requires A17 Pro or newer, i.e. iPhone 15 Pro+)

**Size (iPhone 14, 6.1"):**

- 146.7 × 71.5 × 7.8 mm, 172 g
- 6.1" Super Retina XDR OLED, 2532×1170 (460 ppi), 60 Hz (no ProMotion)
- Storage: 128 / 256 / 512 GB

**iPhone 14 Plus (6.7"):**

- 160.8 × 78.1 × 7.8 mm, 203 g
- 2778×1284, also 60 Hz

**Battery:** ~3,279 mAh (14) / ~4,325 mAh (14 Plus) — the Plus was the battery champion of that generation.

Practical take: in 2026 the iPhone 14 is a 4-year-old design. The A15 still handles everything smoothly, but you're missing USB-C (it's Lightning), 120 Hz, Dynamic Island, and Apple Intelligence. If you're buying used/cheap for a dev test device, it's fine — for a daily phone, an iPhone 15 or 16 is a meaningfully better value now. Apple discontinued the 14 from its lineup in 2024/2025, so it's second-hand or third-party retail only.
