**Question:** How does China plan to build a high-level (high-standard) socialist market economy?

**Answer:**

This is one of China's most significant long-term policy goals, formally codified at the **Third Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee** in July 2024. Here's a structured breakdown:

---

### 1. The Strategic Goal & Timeline

By 2035, China aims to have finished building a high-standard socialist market economy in all respects, further improved the system of socialism with Chinese characteristics, generally modernized its system and capacity for governance, and basically realized socialist modernization.

This goal signals that China's "primary stage of socialism" — a concept formally proposed in 1987 to describe the country's early stage of development — is reaching its endpoint.

---

### 2. Core Principle: Balancing Market and State

The framework holds that the market should play the decisive role in resource allocation while the government better fulfills its own role. It upholds China's basic socialist economic systems, pursues greater self-reliance in science and technology, and promotes high-standard opening up.

---

### 3. Key Reform Pillars

**① Market System Reform**
The 14th Five-Year Plan frames this around: stimulating the vitality of various market entities, constructing a high-standard market system, establishing modern fiscal, taxation, and financial systems, and enhancing the economic governance capacity of government.

**② State-Owned & Private Enterprise Balance**
The approach aims to balance state-owned and private enterprises, with the private sector playing a crucial role in innovation, tax revenue, GDP, and employment. The focus includes deepening reforms such as market-based pricing, privatization, and financial liberalization.

**③ Technology & Innovation ("New Productive Forces")**
High-quality development — or "new productive forces" — centers on China's exploitation of science and technology to become a great power, with the private sector co-opted provided entrepreneurs serve the Party's strategic goals.

**④ Social Reforms (Hukou & Local Finance)**
Key initiatives include Hukou reform to enhance mobility and access to social benefits for 20% of the population, and local government budget reforms to improve transparency and fiscal accountability.

**⑤ Green & Sustainable Development**
The 2024 Third Plenum emphasized a comprehensive approach to green and low-carbon development, alongside scientific research and technological upgrades in China's industrial sector.

---

### 4. Modern Market Infrastructure

A high-standard socialist market economy requires not only a modern market system grounded in the broadening and deepening of the division of labor, but also a market endowed with strong capacity for developmental regulation — one that can serve as the fundamental condition, the primary mechanism for resource allocation, and the essential vehicle for advancing Chinese modernization.

---

### 5. Scale & Execution

The 2024 Third Plenum proposed 60 significant reform measures across 15 major areas, with over 300 reforms to be completed by 2029 — marking the 80th anniversary of the PRC. The upcoming 15th Five-Year Plan is expected to address these 300-plus reform tasks, serving as a "critical bridge" toward Xi's 2035 goal.

---

**In Summary**, the strategy rests on five pillars: **market liberalization + state guidance, SOE/private balance, tech self-reliance, social equity reforms, and green development** — all under Party leadership as the overarching organizing principle.
