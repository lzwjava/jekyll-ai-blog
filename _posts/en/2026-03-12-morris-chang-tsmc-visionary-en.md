### Founding TSMC

* In 1987, Chang founded **TSMC** in Taiwan under the encouragement of the Taiwanese government.
* **TSMC’s Innovation:**

  * Introduced the **pure-play foundry model**, focusing solely on manufacturing semiconductors for other companies rather than designing their own chips.
  * This allowed fabless semiconductor companies like **Qualcomm, NVIDIA, and AMD** to thrive without investing in costly fabrication plants.
* Under Chang’s leadership, TSMC became a global leader in semiconductor manufacturing, pioneering advanced nodes and leading innovations in chip production.
