**Question:** Did VANCL (凡客) move their whole team to Hangzhou?

**Answer:**

Yes — but with a striking caveat. In Lei Jun's (雷军) September 2025 annual speech, he revealed that Chen Nian (陈年, VANCL's founder) told him that after attending the Xiaomi SU7 launch event, he decided to move the entire company to Hangzhou and completely pivot to live-streaming e-commerce (直播电商) from scratch.

Lei Jun casually asked: "Moving the whole company to Hangzhou — that's a big deal. How many people did you bring?" Chen Nian replied: "Three or four."

At that moment, Lei Jun realized that VANCL — which once had over 10,000 employees — had essentially been reduced to zero. Chen Nian reportedly responded: "Lei Jun could cross industries to make cars; so can I."

So the "whole team move to Hangzhou" was technically true, but the entire headcount was only **3–4 people**. The company had gone from 13,000 employees at its peak to a tiny skeleton crew. The Hangzhou move was aimed at reinventing VANCL as a live-streaming brand on platforms like Douyin (TikTok).

VANCL's official flagship store on Douyin now has 1.453 million followers.
