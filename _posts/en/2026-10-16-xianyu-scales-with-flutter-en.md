**Question:** Does Xianyu (闲鱼) use Flutter?

**Answer:**

Yes, absolutely. Alibaba scales China's largest second-hand marketplace — Xianyu — with Flutter. When the Xianyu team was challenged to improve their user experience by developing an app that was consistent across platforms, had beautiful graphics, and still loaded fast, they turned to Flutter.

Xianyu is actually one of Flutter's **most famous and earliest large-scale adopters**, and it's featured prominently on Flutter's official showcase page.

A few notable technical details:

- The Xianyu team used Flutter not as a replacement for Native but as part of an enhanced cross-end solution. Flutter's ease of implementation suited their goal of bringing product detail pages online as quickly as possible, with the implementation of functions as a priority.

- The Alibaba team successfully optimized Flutter for new uses unique to Xianyu's marketplace, implementing an OpenGL process for the entire UI rendering process to reduce CPU and GPU resource overhead.

- The Xianyu team also compared Flutter against React Native in a competitive evaluation, ultimately choosing Flutter as the superior cross-platform solution for their architecture.

Xianyu's team has been one of the **biggest contributors** to the Flutter community in China, frequently publishing deep technical articles about their Flutter optimizations and open-sourcing tools like **Fish Redux** (a Flutter state management framework they built).
