Question: Give an introduction to the company MSI (msi.com).

Answer:

MSI (Micro-Star International Co., Ltd.) is a Taiwanese multinational hardware company, founded on August 4, 1986, by Hsu Hsiang, Huang Chin-Ching, Lin Wen-Tung, Yu Hsien-Neng, and Lu Chi-Lung, headquartered in New Taipei City, Taiwan, and listed on the Taiwan Stock Exchange.

**Core business:** MSI designs, develops, and provides computer hardware and related products and services — laptops, desktop computers, motherboards, graphics cards, all-in-one computers, servers, industrial computers, peripherals, and automotive infotainment systems. It started as a motherboard/GPU component maker and expanded into full systems as the gaming PC market grew.

**Global footprint:** MSI has branch offices across the Americas, Europe, Asia, Australia, and South Africa, with a presence in over 120 countries as of 2015. Manufacturing expanded into mainland China, with a Bao'an plant in Shenzhen (2000) and R&D facilities in Kunshan (2001), and in January 2025 MSI partnered with Syrma SGS to build laptops in Chennai, India, transferring technology for localized production.

**Brand identity:** MSI's biggest consumer-facing reputation is in **gaming hardware** — gaming laptops, desktop PCs, and PC components under lines like Raider, Stealth, Titan (laptops) and MEG/MPG/MAG (desktops/motherboards). It has a lower market share than HP, Dell, Asus, or Lenovo overall because it focuses primarily on the gaming niche, though it has since broadened into business/productivity ("Prestige" series) and creator laptops.

**Recent strategic pivot (relevant to your world):** MSI is explicitly repositioning around AI infrastructure. Since 1986 MSI has evolved from a motherboard pioneer into a global leader in high-performance computing, expanding into servers, automotive electronics, and EV charging, with a strategic focus on AI and enterprise IT. MSI has built an ecosystem spanning AI PCs, AI servers, and edge computing. On the enterprise side, its LinkedIn activity shows GPU-accelerated AI servers, multi-node and ORv3 architectures for hyperscale cloud and enterprise, and AI-RAN platforms for telecom — i.e., MSI is trying to be a hardware supplier into the same AI-infra buildout (GPU servers, data center gear) that underlies the model-training work you're doing, rather than just a gaming brand.

**Other notes:**
- MSI has partnered with eSports orgs (Fnatic, Cloud9, and others) and sponsors Monster Energy Yamaha MotoGP with PC hardware.
- In 2020, an MSI subsidiary was reported scalping RTX 3080/3090 cards on eBay — a minor reputational blip during the GPU shortage era.
- There's also an unrelated **MSI (Premium Surfaces)**, a US flooring/countertop/tile distributor founded in 1975, headquartered in Orange, California, with ~$2.5B annual revenue — different company, same acronym, not to be confused with Micro-Star International.

Not directly relevant to your MoE/agent work, but worth knowing if you ever spec out a local GPU server for cheaper on-prem training/inference — MSI is one of the vendors now actively competing in that server hardware segment alongside Supermicro, ASUS, and Gigabyte.
