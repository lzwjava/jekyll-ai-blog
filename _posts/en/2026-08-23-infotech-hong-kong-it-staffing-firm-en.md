Question: Intro to InfoTech Services (Hong Kong) Limited (infotech.com.hk)

Answer:

InfoTech Services (Hong Kong) Limited is a Hong Kong-based IT recruitment and staffing company. It is **not a software product company**; its core business is **ICT talent supply, recruitment, and IT contract staffing**.

### Company profile

* **Founded:** 1989
* **Headquarters:** Admiralty, Hong Kong (Lippo Centre)
* **Industry:** IT staffing / recruitment / human resources services ([LinkedIn][1])
* **Main market:** Hong Kong enterprises, banks, financial institutions, IT vendors, and government departments. ([LinkedIn][1])

InfoTech describes itself as one of Hong Kong's longest-established and largest IT specialist agencies. It has been involved in IT recruitment since the late 1980s and expanded into IT contract staffing and government IT staffing projects.

---

### Main services

1. **Permanent IT recruitment**

   Helps companies hire:

   * Software engineers
   * System analysts
   * Project managers
   * Infrastructure engineers
   * Data / AI / cybersecurity roles

2. **IT contract staffing / secondment**

This is probably their strongest area.

Example:

A Hong Kong government department needs 50 Java developers for a 3-year project.

Instead of directly hiring:

* Government contracts InfoTech
* InfoTech hires engineers
* Engineers work inside the government department

This model is very common in Hong Kong. InfoTech has supplied IT contract staff for Hong Kong government projects for decades.

3. **Executive search**

For senior IT positions:

* CTO
* IT managers
* Enterprise architects
* Senior consultants

4. **Payroll outsourcing**

They can employ staff on behalf of clients and handle HR/payroll administration.
