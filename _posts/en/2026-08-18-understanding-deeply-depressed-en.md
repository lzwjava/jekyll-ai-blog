Question: What does **“deeply depressed”** mean?

Answer: **“Deeply depressed”** means experiencing a very strong and persistent state of sadness, hopelessness, emptiness, or loss of interest in life.

For example:

> “After losing his company, he became deeply depressed.”

This suggests it wasn't just **“feeling sad for a few days”**. It implies the person's emotional state is significantly affected—possibly affecting sleep, appetite, motivation, work, relationships, and their ability to enjoy things.

### “Depressed” vs. “deeply depressed”

* **I’m depressed.** → I feel very sad/down, possibly temporarily.
* **I’m deeply depressed.** → The feeling is **much more intense and profound**.
* **I’m clinically depressed.** → A medical/psychological term referring to a depressive disorder diagnosed using specific criteria.

Also, **“deeply depressed” isn't itself a medical diagnosis**. It's mainly an expression describing the severity of someone's emotional state.
