Question: How is Beijing Chen Jinglun Middle School Jiaming Branch (北京市陈经纶中学嘉铭分校)? Is it a good school?

Answer:

## Overview

Chen Jinglun Middle School Jiaming Branch (嘉铭分校) was founded in 2003. It is the first branch school established by the Chen Jinglun Middle School Education Group, and also the first nine-year integrated (K-9) school in Chaoyang District. The school is a public institution located at No. 86 Beiyuan Road, Chaoyang District, Beijing, and supports boarding students.

---

## Academic Performance — Impressive

This is one of the school's strongest selling points:

Starting from 2016, the school won the top average score in Chaoyang District's high school entrance exam (中考) for three consecutive years, entering the top tier of Beijing's junior high schools. Beijing media even called it "Beijing's most impressive school." In 2020, it ranked first in Chaoyang in raw scores, and in 2021, it ranked first including bonus points.

In those years, the average exam scores surpassed the admission cutoff for top Chaoyang high schools by several points, meaning nearly 70% of students could easily gain admission to the best high schools in the district.

---

## School Structure

Jiaming Branch is the largest nine-year integrated day and boarding school in Beijing, with four campuses and five locations. It has an East Campus and West Campus for the primary school division — grades 1 to 5 are taught separately, while grade 6 onwards are merged. The middle school's exam results have been officially described as ranking first in Chaoyang for three consecutive years.

---

## Curriculum & Extracurriculars

Across 6 campuses, the school offers 82 course categories totaling 193 individual courses, with 397 class hours of after-school services per week. The school has partnered with the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences for academician-level courses, and with China University of Political Science and Law for supplementary instruction.

Students win nearly tens of thousands of awards each year at international, national, and city/district-level competitions. The school has been recognized as a National Olympic Demonstration School and a Beijing Ice and Snow Featured School, and has won over 100 championships in national and local competitions. Students have achieved excellence in robotics, science, dance, cheerleading, and short film competitions.

---

## Faculty

The school has 75 faculty and staff, including 63 full-time teachers, all holding bachelor's degrees or above. More than 90% have over four years of experience in their respective teaching positions, and many have served as district or city-level subject leaders.

---

## Rankings

According to a 2025 ranking of Chaoyang District's Category-1 primary schools, Jiaming Branch ranked 6th among second-tier Category-1 primary schools in Chaoyang, based on a comprehensive evaluation of teaching quality, faculty strength, and facilities.

---

## Points to Note

The school places a heavy emphasis on academic results and strictly monitors scores, which means students can experience significant academic pressure. Parents who wish to enroll their children should be prepared to be actively involved in their child's learning journey.

The school currently has no high school division. The main Chen Jinglun High School is relatively far away, near Hujiалou, so there is some student attrition after junior high graduation.

---

## Summary Verdict

**Yes, it is a good school** — particularly its middle school (junior high) division is considered one of the top performers in Chaoyang District. The primary school is also well-regarded, though slightly less dominant in rankings. It is a strong choice for families living within its enrollment zone, especially for those prioritizing academic achievement.
