Question: Should I start publishing WeChat Moments, given that I currently only run an AI WeChat group/community?

Answer:

Yes. For your current stage, **WeChat Moments is probably a higher-leverage channel than only running private groups**.

Your current setup:

* Blog: public technical reputation (`lzwjava.github.io`, `lzwjava.com`)
* WeChat groups: private community
* Personal network: existing engineers / AI people / business contacts

The missing layer is **personal distribution**.

A good ecosystem looks like:

```
Public content (blog / GitHub)
          ↓
WeChat Moments (personal brand + trust)
          ↓
WeChat groups (community)
          ↓
Consulting / AI projects / partnerships
```

Without Moments, people inside your network don't know:

* you are training GPT models
* you are experimenting with LLM infra
* you build agents
* you run an AI community
* you can deliver AI projects

Your achievements are mostly invisible.

For an AI consultant / OPC direction, **trust compounds**. People usually don't search "AI consultant Guangzhou"; they remember:

> "That guy who keeps posting about LLM training and AI projects."

Then opportunities come.

### Posting frequency

Do not treat it like a social media job.

A good cadence:

* 3-5 posts/week
* 80% technical/building
* 20% personal/travel/life

Your audience is likely:

* engineers
* founders
* investors
* recruiters
* potential clients

They don't need daily entertainment.

---

### Important: Don't over-polish

Your advantage is authenticity.

A post like:

> "Spent 8 hours debugging CUDA. Flash-attn failed because CUDA 13.2 mismatch. Finally switched to SDPA."

is actually valuable.

Most AI influencers only repost papers. Very few actually train models.
