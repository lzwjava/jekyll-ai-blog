Question: Who is 卢柯 (Ke Lu)?

Answer: A Chinese materials scientist, not related to AI/ML — pure metallurgy/nanomaterials.

Key facts:

- Born May 23, 1965, in Huachi County, Gansu, China.
- Bachelor's from East China Institute of Technology (now Nanjing University of Science and Technology), PhD from the Institute of Metal Research (IMR), Chinese Academy of Sciences in January 1990, became a researcher there three years later.
- Elected to the Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2003 at age 38 — one of the youngest academicians in CAS history.
- Founding director of Shenyang National Laboratory for Materials Science (SYNL) since 2001; before that directed the State Key Laboratory for Rapidly Solidified Non-equilibrium Alloys, IMR, CAS from 1997 to 2001.
- Core scientific contribution: discovery of nano-twinned materials and the nano-twin strengthening methodology — normally you strengthen a metal (alloying, strain-hardening) at the cost of ductility/conductivity, but by generating a high density of nanoscale twins within grains, pure copper was strengthened by an order of magnitude while retaining good ductility and conductivity. This won him the 2020 Future Science Prize in Physical Science, for pioneering the use of nano-twinned and gradient nano structures to achieve high strength, toughness, and conductivity in copper.
- Also did later work on SMGT (surface mechanical grinding treatment) processing of nickel, discovering ultra-hard, ultra-stable nano-laminated structures with low-angle boundaries.
- Output: 380+ peer-reviewed papers, 30 patents, 100+ invited conference talks, 20,000+ ISI citations. Also member of German National Academy of Sciences Leopoldina (2005) and TWAS (2004), editor of Progress in Materials Science (Elsevier), reviewing editor of Science (AAAS).
- Also a first-class CAS natural science prize winner (1993), 2021 Asian Scientist 100 laureate, and politically active as vice chair of the Jiusan Society central committee since 2012 and vice chair of the Liaoning CPPCC committee.
- Currently: President of the Liaoning Academy of Materials.
