**Question:** What is the official textbook for this course (Course 15040 / Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era)?

---

**Answer:**The official textbook for this course is:

---

### 📗 Main Textbook (必用教材)

**《习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想概论》**

| Item | Detail |
| ------ | -------- |
| **Full Title** | 习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想概论 |
| **English Title** | Introduction to Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era |
| **Publisher** | 高等教育出版社 + 人民出版社 (jointly) |
| **Edition** | 2023 (current standard edition) |
| **Organized by** | 中宣部 + 教育部 (Propaganda Dept + Ministry of Education) |

This is the **first comprehensive and systematic** unified textbook on Xi Jinping Thought, and is the **authoritative text** for university ideological and political theory courses. The book consists of an introduction, **17 main chapters**, and a conclusion. Each chapter includes learning objectives, chapter summaries, and discussion questions, as well as excerpts from Xi Jinping's important statements, knowledge expansions, and charts.

---

### 📚 Structure of the Book

The textbook fully reflects the latest achievements of Marxism adapted to Chinese conditions, the great practices and transformations of the New Era, and the academic consensus research results.

The 17 chapters cover roughly these major themes:

| Chapters | Theme |
| ---------- | ------- |
| 导论 (Intro) | Overview of Xi Jinping Thought |
| Ch. 1–3 | New Era, historical mission, Two Centenary Goals |
| Ch. 4–6 | 五位一体 overall layout |
| Ch. 7–9 | 四个全面 strategic layout |
| Ch. 10–12 | Economy, innovation, rural revitalization |
| Ch. 13–14 | Culture, social governance |
| Ch. 15–16 | Ecological civilization, national defense |
| Ch. 17 | Party building (从严治党) |
| 结语 (Conclusion) | Building a community with shared future |

### 💡 Practical Tips

This textbook was jointly organized by the Central Propaganda Department and Ministry of Education, and **all undergraduate and vocational college students nationwide are required to study it** — making it one of the most widely used textbooks in China.

For exam purposes, priority order of what to read:

```
① Main textbook chapters (概论) — most tested
② 20th Party Congress Report — key source of definitions
③ 学习纲要 2023 — good for fill-in-the-blank phrases
④ 习近平谈治国理政 — useful for essay quotes
```
