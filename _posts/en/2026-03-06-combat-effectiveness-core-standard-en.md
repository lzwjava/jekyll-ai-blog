Question: Regarding the exam question: The People's Army's primary duty and core mission is to prepare for and fight wars. What is the only fundamental standard that must be established?
A. Leadership
B. Organizational capability
C. Combat effectiveness (战斗力)
D. Execution ability

**Correct Answer: C — Combat Effectiveness (战斗力)**

---

Answer:

## Correct Answer: C — Combat Effectiveness (战斗力)

### Why C is Correct

Preparing for and winning wars is always the fundamental duty and core mission (主责主业) of the People's Army, and must always be placed in the primary position — it must never deviate from this, and must never slacken.

Firmly establishing **combat effectiveness (战斗力)** as the only and fundamental standard is a requirement for effectively fulfilling the Army's core functions. The military is first and foremost a combat force. Although the specific tasks carried out by the PLA differ across different periods, its fundamental function as a combat force has never changed.

The word "only (唯一)" emphasizes its unparalleled status, while "fundamental (根本)" emphasizes that it is the source and foundation of all military activities. Upholding the combat effectiveness standard means that all work must ultimately serve to enhance the military's combat capability. If work cannot be measured against this standard, it loses its fundamental meaning and value, no matter how much effort is invested.

---

### Why the Other Options Are Wrong

| Option | Why It's Incorrect |
| -------- | ------------------- |
| A — Leadership (领导力) | Leadership is important in general management, but it is not the designated "only and fundamental standard" for the military's core mission. |
| B — Organizational capability (组织力) | Organizational ability supports military functioning but is subordinate to combat effectiveness; it is a means, not the ultimate standard. |
| D — Execution ability (执行力) | Execution is a capability required to carry out orders, but again it is instrumental, not the overarching standard of military value. |

---

### Key Theoretical Points to Remember

Combat effectiveness (战斗力) is the fundamental standard for military development. The PLA's core function is fighting wars, and the combat effectiveness standard is the only and fundamental standard for military construction. Emphasizing the combat effectiveness standard is both a requirement for effectively fulfilling the PLA's core function and a requirement for improving the quality and efficiency of military development.

Regardless of circumstances and conditions, the entire focus must always revolve around the core mission of training and preparing for war — upholding combat effectiveness as the only and fundamental standard, concentrating all thought on fighting and directing all work toward fighting.

In summary: the People's Army exists to fight and win wars. Everything — leadership, organization, execution — is only valuable insofar as it contributes to **combat effectiveness**. That is why 战斗力 (combat effectiveness) is the one and only fundamental standard.
