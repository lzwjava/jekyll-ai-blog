**Question:** Who is 唐绍轩 (Tang Shaoxuan) and what is his NOIP/IOI competitive programming story?

**Answer:**

唐绍轩 (Tang Shaoxuan) is one of China's most remarkable competitive programmers of the modern era — notable not just for winning gold at IOI 2022, but for **achieving a perfect score (600/600, AK — "All Killed")**, ranking **1st out of 349 contestants worldwide**.

## Background

Tang Shaoxuan is from Shandong Pingyi (平邑), and attended Pingyi No. 1 Middle School (平邑第一中学). He began learning computer programming in the **sixth grade of primary school**, under the guidance of his school's informatics coach, Liu Minbo (刘闽波).

This is notable because Pingyi is a relatively small, non-elite city — not Beijing, Shanghai, or Hangzhou. Tang Shaoxuan was **the first IOI contestant from Shandong province in over a decade**, representing a historic "breakthrough of zero" for Shandong informatics. The school had only been running an informatics program for a few years before producing a national team member.

## Competition Path

As early as **2020**, Tang Shaoxuan — still a **junior high school student** — participated in the IOI competition and won a **silver medal (Category D)**. In December of that same year, he was selected for the **Tsinghua University Informatics Winter Camp** and successfully signed a **first-tier guaranteed admission contract with Tsinghua University (清华大学签一等约)**.

That means by the time he was in junior high, he'd already locked in Tsinghua — one of China's top two universities — through competition merit alone.

## IOI 2022 — Perfect Score, World #1

At IOI 2022, the Chinese team swept the top four gold medals. **Dai Jiangqi and Tang Shaoxuan both achieved a perfect score of 600 points**, tying for 1st place. Zhang Junkai and Zhou Hangrui followed with 555 points each, tying for 3rd.

The official IOI stats confirm: Tang Shaoxuan scored **100/100 on all six tasks**, for a total of **600.00 points**, placing **1st out of 349 contestants** (100th percentile), winning the gold medal.

Getting an AK (perfect score) at IOI is extraordinarily rare. Tang Shaoxuan is named as one of only four Chinese contestants to achieve AK status at IOI in nearly ten years, alongside Deng Mingyang (2021), Dai Jiangqi (2022), and Zhou Kangyang (2024).
