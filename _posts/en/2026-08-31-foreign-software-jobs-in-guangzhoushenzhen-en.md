**Shenzhen — Major Foreign Tech Companies**

Shenzhen is home to over 17,000 tech companies, and foreign multinationals actively recruit software engineers, hardware engineers, AI/ML specialists, and R&D engineers there.

**Guangzhou — Major Foreign Companies**
