## Baidu Commercial AI Technology Innovation Competition (CTI)

**cti.baidu.com** is the official website for the **Baidu Commercial AI Technology Innovation Competition** (百度商业AI技术创新大赛), abbreviated as **CTI**.

---

### Overview

The CTI competition was first launched in 2023. It is a **national-level technology innovation contest** open to the general public, including participants from universities, research institutions, and internet companies. Its goal is to explore cutting-edge commercial technologies and discover and cultivate outstanding talent.

---

### 2026 Edition: Geek Summer Camp

The 2026 edition has been upgraded to a **"Geek Summer Camp"** format. Through an invitation-based registration system, **100 teams** passionate about AI technology and innovation will be selected to participate. These teams will engage in learning and exchanges with technical experts from Baidu and NVIDIA.

---

### 2026 Competition Theme

The theme this year is **"Generative Recommendation Ad Ranking Inference Performance Optimization"** (生成式推荐广告排序推理性能优化).

The background: Traditional ad ranking models struggle to meet personalized recommendation needs. Generative ad ranking models, powered by the **Transformer architecture**, are better at modeling long user behavior sequences and capturing evolving user interests — producing more attractive personalized ads and improving click-through rates. However, challenges remain: large model parameter sizes, complex attention computation, extremely long historical sequences, and quantization impacts on inference accuracy.

Participants are expected to propose innovative solutions from angles such as **framework optimization, algorithm innovation, and high-performance computing**.

---

### Prizes & Incentives

Winning teams will receive **generous cash prizes** and also have the opportunity to obtain **direct access to Baidu's campus recruitment and internship programs**.

---

### Registration

Participants must register using an **AI Studio account** (aistudio.baidu.com) with real-name authentication. Each person may only register once — duplicate registrations or use of secondary accounts will result in disqualification.

---

### Organizers & Partners

- **Host:** Baidu Commercial (百度商业)
- **Technical Partner:** NVIDIA
- **Guided by:** Baidu's VP of Mobile Ecosystem and NVIDIA's Global VP

---

### Previous Competitions

The website also archives past editions: 2023, 2024, and 2025, making it a continuing series of AI innovation events.
