## HKUST (Guangzhou) — Overview

### Origin & Founding

HKUST(GZ) is a joint venture university established on June 29, 2022, jointly founded by The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and Guangzhou University, located in Nansha, Guangzhou. The site near Qingsheng railway station was deliberately chosen — it's only 30 minutes from Hong Kong West Kowloon station, making cross-border commuting and collaboration practical.

The campus covers 1,669 acres (675 ha) — massive compared to the original Clear Water Bay campus (60 ha). It's described as the world's first university to experiment with an innovative academic structure.

---

### Academic Structure — The Hub System

This is the most distinctive thing about HKUST(GZ). Unlike disciplinary-based approaches, it incorporates four Academic Hubs — Function, Information, Systems, and Society — and 16 cutting-edge Research Thrust Areas that are responsive to ever-changing worldwide trends.

**Information Hub** (most relevant to you): primarily comprises four thrust areas: Artificial Intelligence (AI), Data Science and Analytics (DSA), Internet of Things (IoT), and Computational Media and Arts (CMA).

**Systems Hub**: hosts thrust areas in Bioscience & Biomedical Engineering, Intelligent Transportation, Robotics & Autonomous Systems, and Smart Manufacturing.

**Society Hub**: structures its research and teaching around four thrust areas — Carbon Neutrality and Climate Change, Financial Technology, Innovation Policy and Entrepreneurship, and Urban Governance and Design.

**Function Hub** covers areas like big data, design thinking, sustainable environmental science, and related foundational disciplines.

---

### Cross-disciplinary Philosophy

All research postgraduate students are co-supervised by at least two supervisors from different disciplines — a mandatory requirement to ensure cross-disciplinary thinking in research topics.

Programs at HKUST(GZ) do not duplicate existing ones offered by the Clearwater Bay campus — it focuses on cross-disciplinary thematic programs to complement those in Hong Kong. English is the medium of instruction.
