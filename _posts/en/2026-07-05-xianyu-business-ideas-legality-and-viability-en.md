Question: Are these listed ideas for starting a business on Xianyu (Idle Fish marketplace in China) viable, and which ones are legal or recommended?

Answer:

Most of the ideas you listed fall into very different categories in terms of legality, platform policy risk, and long-term business sustainability. Xianyu is mainly a second-hand marketplace, but it is often used for services and “informal gigs,” which makes enforcement uneven. However, that does **not** mean high-risk or illegal activities are safe or stable.

Below is a practical breakdown:

---

## 1. Generally **legit service-based ideas (can work if done properly)**

### ✔ Remote software setup / system reinstall

* Helping users install software, configure systems, fix bugs
* This is a real demand segment (especially Windows + office + basic IT support)
* Risk: low, as long as you don’t pirate software or bypass licensing

### ✔ Hardware rental (if properly sourced)

* Renting GPUs, phones, test devices, etc.
* Works better if you have actual inventory and clear contracts
* Risk: medium (damage, disputes), but legal if properly operated

### ✔ Knowledge-based services (if original)

* Tutorials, consulting, technical guidance
* Can be monetized as “paid guidance / consulting”
* Must be original content

---

## 2. **High-risk / policy-violating (likely to get banned)**

### ⚠ “Traffic generation / follower monetization”

* “1 yuan per follower, optimize to homepage, earn 1000 followers/day”
* This is essentially artificial engagement manipulation
* Platforms actively suppress this (spam, fraud signals)

### ⚠ Content / course reselling or “information gap arbitrage”

* Reselling paid courses, paid communities, or downloaded content
* Often violates copyright or platform rules

### ⚠ Software tool promotion + comment section funneling

* Using listings purely to redirect traffic off-platform
* Usually considered spam marketing behavior

---

## 3. **Illegal or strongly non-compliant (avoid completely)**

### ❌ Virtual account resale / subscription trading

* Selling streaming, SaaS, or platform accounts
* Often violates terms of service + fraud risk

### ❌ Pirated software / cracked AI tools / activation keys

* This is copyright infringement in most jurisdictions
* High risk of account bans, legal consequences, and payment freezing

### ❌ Anti-collection / “debt evasion” services

* These can be classified as facilitating fraud or illegal avoidance activities
* Very high legal risk in China and elsewhere

---

## 4. Reality check on the “100k/month” model

The claim:

> “Optimize listing → 100 followers/day → 30 RMB per follower → 100k/month”

This is not realistic in a stable way because:

* Xianyu algorithm detects spam/fake engagement patterns
* Conversion rate from “followers” to paying customers is usually very low
* High churn + account bans are common in aggressive traffic tactics
* Market competition is already saturated with similar “side hustle funnels”

In practice, most people who try this:

* Burn accounts quickly
* Get low-quality traffic
* Struggle to maintain consistent income
