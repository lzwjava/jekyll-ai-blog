Question: Plan a 15-day trip to Australia.

Answer: 

Here is a realistic and popular **15-day itinerary** for first-time visitors to Australia. It focuses on the country's most iconic highlights: vibrant cities, the **Great Barrier Reef**, the Red Centre (Uluru), and some coastal/outback variety. This plan minimizes excessive jet lag and domestic flights (usually 4–5 internal flights) while covering the "big four" experiences most people want to see.

**Assumptions**:
- You are a first-time visitor.
- Traveling as a couple or small group (not backpacker budget or family with very young children).
- Moderate budget (comfortable 4-star hotels, some guided tours).
- Best in shoulder seasons (avoid mid-summer Dec–Feb heat in the centre or wet season Dec–Mar in the north if possible).

### 15-Day Classic Australia Highlights Itinerary

**Day 1: Arrive in Sydney**  
Arrive in **Sydney**. Transfer to city hotel. Afternoon/evening: Walk Circular Quay, see Sydney Opera House & Harbour Bridge at sunset, enjoy dinner with views.  
*Overnight: Sydney*

**Day 2: Sydney – City & Harbour**  
Morning harbour cruise or ferry to Manly. Afternoon: Sydney Opera House guided tour, The Rocks historic area, Royal Botanic Garden. Evening: Darling Harbour or rooftop bar.  
*Overnight: Sydney*

**Day 3: Blue Mountains day trip**  
Full-day tour to Blue Mountains (usually includes Scenic World, Three Sisters, Wentworth Falls, wildlife). Return late afternoon.  
*Overnight: Sydney*

**Day 4: Sydney – Bondi & coastal walk**  
Morning: Bondi Beach + Bondi to Coogee coastal walk (or shorter version). Afternoon: free time (Paddington markets if Saturday, or beach relaxation).  
*Overnight: Sydney*

**Day 5: Sydney → Uluru (Ayers Rock)**  
Morning flight to **Uluru** (~3.5 hours). Afternoon: Uluru Base Walk (easy 10.6 km flat path) or shorter sunset viewing spots. Evening: Sounds of Silence dinner (very popular – book early).  
*Overnight: Yulara (near Uluru)*

**Day 6: Uluru & Kata Tjuta**  
Sunrise at Uluru, then Kata Tjuta (The Olgas) Valley of the Winds walk (moderate, stunning views). Afternoon: Cultural Centre + dot-painting workshop or free time.  
*Overnight: Yulara*

**Day 7: Uluru → Cairns / Port Douglas**  
Morning flight to **Cairns** (~2.5–3 hours, often via Sydney). Transfer to hotel or north to Port Douglas (1-hour drive – recommended for nicer beaches/resorts). Afternoon/evening relax.  
*Overnight: Cairns or Port Douglas*

**Day 8: Great Barrier Reef – Outer Reef**  
Full-day boat trip to the **Outer Great Barrier Reef** (better coral & visibility than inner sites). Snorkel, introductory dive option, glass-bottom boat, buffet lunch.  
*Overnight: Cairns / Port Douglas*

**Day 9: Daintree Rainforest & Cape Tribulation**  
Full-day tour: Mossman Gorge, Daintree River cruise (spot crocodiles), Cape Tribulation beach walk, rainforest boardwalks. One of the few places where reef meets ancient rainforest.  
*Overnight: Cairns / Port Douglas*

**Day 10: Free day or optional reef/whitsundays**  
Option A: Relax / Kuranda Scenic Railway + Skyrail.  
Option B: Fly to Airlie Beach (~1 h) and do 2-night/3-day Whitsundays sailing trip (Whitehaven Beach, Hill Inlet) – join Days 10–12 then fly to Melbourne Day 13.  
Most people choose relaxation or Kuranda.  
*Overnight: Cairns / Port Douglas*

**Day 11: Cairns → Melbourne**  
Morning flight to **Melbourne** (~3 hours). Afternoon: Federation Square, street art laneways (Hosier Lane), Yarra River walk, early dinner in a laneway restaurant.  
*Overnight: Melbourne*

**Day 12: Melbourne city & culture**  
Queen Victoria Market, NGV (National Gallery), Royal Botanic Gardens, Degraves Street coffee culture, rooftop bars or Southbank. Evening: Lygon Street Italian food or rooftop drinks.  
*Overnight: Melbourne*

**Day 13: Great Ocean Road day trip**  
Full-day small-group tour: Twelve Apostles, Loch Ard Gorge, London Bridge, koalas in the wild, Apollo Bay. Long day (~12 hours) but one of the world's great coastal drives.  
*Overnight: Melbourne*

**Day 14: Melbourne free day or Phillip Island**  
Option A: Free day – shopping, wineries in Yarra Valley (day trip), or St Kilda penguins at sunset.  
Option B: Phillip Island day trip (penguin parade, koalas, seals).  
*Overnight: Melbourne*

**Day 15: Depart Melbourne or fly to Sydney**  
Depending on your international flight: depart from Melbourne, or morning flight back to Sydney for evening departure.

### Tips
- Book domestic flights early (Qantas, Virgin Australia, Jetstar).
- Reef & Uluru tours book out → reserve 3–6 months ahead.
- Consider travel insurance that covers adventure activities.
- Pack layers: Sydney/Melbourne can be cool, Reef/Uluru hot.
- If you prefer beaches over culture, swap Melbourne days for more Queensland time (Whitsundays or Gold Coast).

This itinerary gives you cities + reef + outback + wildlife/coast in 15 days without feeling completely rushed.
