**Question:** Which university is better — Swiss UMEF or California Science and Technology University (CSTU)?

**Answer:**

Both are niche, small private institutions offering online programs to Chinese students. Here's a detailed head-to-head:

---

### ⚠️ The Critical Issue They Share: NEITHER Online Degree is Recognized in China

This applies to **both** programs equally. On January 28, 2023, the CSCSE — China's international qualification recognition authority under the Ministry of Education — announced that its temporary measures for recognizing distance-learning qualifications were cancelled from the start of the 2023 spring semester. The CSCSE emphasized that courses designed as fully online programs from the start will not be recognized by the Ministry of Education.

The fact that CSTU appears on the CSCSE institution list **does not** mean its online degrees will be verified — the list only records institutions whose previous students have applied for verification. The CSCSE will not verify qualifications delivered online, including cross-border online degrees or diplomas.

### Accreditation Quality

Swiss UMEF was the first and, until now unique, private federally accredited institution as a University of Applied Sciences Institute in the Canton of Geneva. This is Switzerland's highest accreditation body.

CSTU's accreditor — ACCSC — is a legitimate US Dept of Education-recognized body, but it specifically focuses on career and vocational schools. CSTU was accredited by the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges (ACCSC) in June 2022. This is a national accreditor, a lower tier than regional accreditors (like WASC or HLC) which are used by traditional universities. Neither institution holds regional accreditation, which is the gold standard in the US.

**Edge: Swiss UMEF** for the strength and prestige of its accreditation body.
