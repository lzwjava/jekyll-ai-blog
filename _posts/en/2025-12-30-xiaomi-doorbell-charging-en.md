Question: How to replace or charge the battery in a Xiaomi smart doorbell?

Answer: 

Xiaomi smart doorbells, particularly popular models like the **Xiaomi Smart Doorbell 3** and **Xiaomi Smart Doorbell 3S**, use a built-in rechargeable lithium battery (typically 5200mAh capacity). These batteries are **not user-replaceable** — the design emphasizes long battery life (up to 4-5 months per charge, depending on usage) to avoid the need for frequent charging or replacement.

### How to Charge the Battery:
1. **Disable the removal alarm** (if enabled) in the Mi Home/Xiaomi Home app to prevent false alerts.
2. Use the provided **removal pin** (included in the box) — insert it into the small pinhole at the top of the doorbell and press firmly.
3. Push the doorbell **upward** to slide it off the mounting base.
4. Locate the **USB Type-C charging port** (usually at the bottom of the doorbell).
5. Connect a **USB Type-C cable** (the one provided or any compatible cable) to the port and plug it into a power adapter.
   - Recommended: A safety-certified **5V/2A** adapter for optimal charging (full charge in about 3-5 hours).
   - You can use a lower-current adapter (e.g., 5V/1A), but it will take longer.
6. The doorbell button light will show a **breathing white light** while charging and turn **solid white** when fully charged.
7. Once charged, slide the doorbell back onto the mounting base until it clicks into place (it powers on automatically).

### Notes:
- Battery life varies based on usage (e.g., motion detection frequency, video recordings, Wi-Fi signal strength, temperature).
- Some models (e.g., Doorbell 3S) support optional **hardwired power** (8-24V AC) for continuous operation without relying on the battery.
- Check battery level in the Mi Home/Xiaomi Home app — it may not update in real-time; press the doorbell button or trigger motion to sync the status.
- If the battery no longer holds a charge after years of use, contact Xiaomi support, as the battery is integrated and not meant for user replacement.
