**Question:** Introduction to Tilde Research, and how is it related to Nathan Chen, the AI researcher?

**Answer:**

**Tilde Research — Overview**

Tilde Research is a moonshot AI lab advancing mechanistic interpretability, new architectures, and pretraining science. It is backed by Khosla Ventures, Yufei Zhao, and researchers at OpenAI and Anthropic.

The lab launched publicly in November 2024 and closed an $8M seed round led by Khosla Ventures in mid-2025, with Soma Capital among the investors. It is based in Palo Alto, California.

The mission, as stated on their website, is to deeply understand the inner mechanisms of models in order to advance the frontier of intelligence — because models compress the universe into their weights through beautiful, intricate, and complex mechanisms, and we understand much less than we can build.

Tilde Research provides full-stack interpretability tools for AI systems, including general interpreter models and control mechanisms to guide how features flow through inference pipelines — helping human experts collaborate with AI by aligning model decisions with human input and existing fine-tuning methods.

**Connection to Nathan Chen (陈广宇)**

Before joining Moonshot AI, Nathan Chen worked at Tilde Research. Specifically, he served as a Research Contributor at Tilde Research in 2025, working on applied interpretability research and exploring how neural networks process and represent information internally.

His own GitHub profile confirms this: he describes himself as currently working on ML research at Kimi, previously at Tilde, with interests in efficient attention and hardware-aligned ML algorithms in general.

His own autobiographical essay describes how what started as a hackathon led him to meet an entrepreneurial mentor team, then join a top-tier research group — and eventually land an internship at a Silicon Valley AI startup. That startup was Tilde Research.

After Tilde, Chen Guangyu joined the Moonshot AI (Kimi) team in November 2025, where he became the co-first author of the groundbreaking "Attention Residuals" paper alongside Su Jianlin (inventor of RoPE).

Most recently, Anthropic researcher Sholto Douglas publicly invited him to the Bay Area team, and Chen confirmed he is heading to San Francisco to join Anthropic's Claude team to work on scaling RL — moving within a day of leaving Moonshot.

In short, Tilde Research was the crucial early Silicon Valley research experience that helped launch Nathan Chen's trajectory from teenage hackathon winner to co-authoring a paper that impressed Elon Musk and Andrej Karpathy.
