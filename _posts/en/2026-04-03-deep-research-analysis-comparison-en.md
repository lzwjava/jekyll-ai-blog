Question: How good is the Deep Research feature in ChatGPT?

Answer: ChatGPT's **Deep Research** (now largely integrated into the **GPT-5.4 Thinking/Pro** workflows as of 2026) is a high-performance tool designed for complex, multi-step information gathering. Unlike standard chat, which provides immediate answers, Deep Research acts as an "agent" that spends several minutes browsing the web, synthesizing multiple sources, and constructing a detailed report.

### Key Strengths
* **Comprehensive Synthesis:** It excels at breaking down a broad query into sub-topics, researching them individually, and then merging them into a cohesive narrative with professional formatting.
* **Reasoning Capabilities:** Powered by the latest reasoning models (like **o3** and the **GPT-5 series**), it performs exceptionally well on complex benchmarks. It famously scored **26.6%** on the "Humanity's Last Exam" benchmark, significantly outperforming earlier models.
* **Source Integration:** It can connect to external data through **MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers**, allowing it to pull information from apps like Google Drive, Notion, and Slack to supplement its web findings.
* **Time Efficiency:** For professional-grade reports that would take a human hours to compile, the tool typically finishes in **5 to 30 minutes**.

### Significant Limitations
* **Speed vs. Accuracy:** While it is faster than a human, it is much slower than "instant" search tools like Perplexity. It also still suffers from occasional **factual hallucinations** or incorrect inferences, especially when dealing with rumors or ambiguous data.
* **Verification Requirement:** Experts warn against using the output verbatim. Because the AI can be highly confident even when wrong, human oversight is still required to verify the footnotes and citations.
* **Usage Quotas:** Access is heavily tiered. As of early 2026, **Plus** users typically get around **25 queries per month**, while the **$200/month Pro tier** offers significantly higher "maximum" access.

### Comparison: ChatGPT vs. Perplexity

| Feature | ChatGPT (Deep Research) | Perplexity (Deep Research) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Best For** | Deep synthesis, analysis, & report writing. | Fast, factual, & citation-heavy search. |
| **Citations** | Built into the final report as footnotes. | Inline citations by default for every claim. |
| **Speed** | Slow (5–30 minutes). | Fast (under 3 minutes). |
| **Focus** | Reasoning and "connecting the dots." | Accuracy and real-time data retrieval. |
