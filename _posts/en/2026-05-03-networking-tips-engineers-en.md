Question: What are concise, practical points I can share in a WeChat engineer group to encourage networking and interaction?

Answer:

Here are clean, actionable points you can drop directly into the group:

---

### Core tips

* Start small: add 1–2 people, not everyone
* Prioritize active members (they’re more open to connecting)
* Always include a short, clear intro when adding someone
* Mention a **specific reason** for connecting (topic, message, shared interest)

---

### Good connection habits

* Keep messages short and direct
* Focus on exchanging value (ideas, resources, experience)
* Follow up only if there’s mutual interest
* Respect others’ time and attention

---

### What to avoid

* No mass-adding people without context
* No spam or hard selling
* Don’t expect replies from everyone
* Don’t take rejection personally

---

### Simple intro template

* “Hi, I saw your message about [topic]…”
* “I’m working on [X], also interested in [Y]…”
* “Happy to connect and exchange ideas.”

---

### Mindset

* Useful connections often come from small initiatives
* Many people are willing to help if the request is reasonable
* In the AI era, high-quality information and connections require proactive effort

---

### Optional support

* You can offer to help match people with similar interests
* Encourage people to reach out privately if unsure

---

These points work well because they are **clear, low-effort, and respectful of engineer communication style**.
