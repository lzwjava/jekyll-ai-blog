Question: What NYU programs are related to AI?

Answer:

NYU does not have only one "AI Master's" program like CMU MSAII. AI study at NYU is distributed mainly across **Computer Science**, **Data Science**, and some specialized engineering programs. The main ones are:

---

## 1. NYU Courant — MS in Computer Science (AI Concentration)

New York University Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences

This is probably the closest equivalent to a traditional AI/ML master's. ([CS NYU][1])

Program:
**M.S. in Computer Science**

AI-related areas:

* Artificial Intelligence
* Machine Learning
* Natural Language Processing
* Computer Vision
* Robotics
* Algorithms
* Systems

Typical courses:

* Machine Learning
* Deep Learning
* Natural Language Processing
* Artificial Intelligence
* Reinforcement Learning
* Computer Vision

Strength:

* Strong CS foundation
* Better if you want:

  * ML Engineer
  * AI Research Engineer
  * PhD path
  * LLM research

NYU Courant is also famous because of AI researchers like Yann LeCun. ([Business Insider][2])

---

## 2. NYU Center for Data Science — MS in Data Science

NYU Center for Data Science

This is another very AI-heavy program. ([NYU Center for Data Science][3])

Program:
**Master of Science in Data Science (MSDS)**

AI-related tracks:

* Natural Language Processing
* Machine Learning
* Big Data
* Statistics / Mathematical foundations

The NLP track is especially relevant for LLM work. ([NYU Center for Data Science][4])

Typical courses:

* Machine Learning
* Deep Learning
* Natural Language Processing
* Probability
* Statistics
* Data Mining
* Large-scale data processing

Good for:

* Data Scientist
* ML Engineer
* Applied AI Engineer

The program is 36 credits and normally 2 years full-time. ([NYU Center for Data Science][5])

---

## 3. NYU Tandon — MS Computer Science (AI / ML)

NYU Tandon School of Engineering

Program:
**MS Computer Science**

AI electives include:

* Machine Learning
* Artificial Intelligence
* Data Science
* Computer Vision
* Responsible Computing

It is more engineering-oriented compared with Courant. ([NYU Bulletins][6])

Good for:

* Software engineers moving into AI
* Industry jobs
* Applied ML

---

## 4. NYU Tandon — MS Artificial Intelligence (if available)

NYU has had AI-focused master's offerings through engineering, but availability and naming have changed over time. The strongest established AI routes remain:

1. MS Computer Science (Courant)
2. MS Data Science (CDS)
3. MS Computer Science (Tandon)
