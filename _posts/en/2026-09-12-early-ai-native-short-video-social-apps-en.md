Question: Are there already **AI-native short-video social platforms** where the content is generated by AI, with an infinite-scroll feed and native iOS/Android apps?

Answer: **Yes — but the category is still extremely early.** I found a few that are much closer to what you're describing than “AI video generator + export to TikTok.”

### 1. AICines — closest to your idea

AICines is probably the clearest example.

[AICines on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aicines-ai-social-media/id6769682599?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Their positioning is essentially:

> **TikTok where every video is AI-generated.**

It has:

* infinite personalized feed
* AI-generated short films / reels / mini-scenes
* follow / like / comment / save
* AI text-to-video + image-to-video
* remix / recreate other people's videos
* creator leaderboard
* creators earn credits when their work is remixed
* native iPhone/iPad app

The interesting part is that **generation and consumption are in the same product loop**. ([App Store][1])

That's much more interesting than Runway/Veo/Kling-style products.

---

### 2. Clip AI — AI-native short-video feed

Clip AI is another pretty direct example.

It describes itself as an **AI-powered short-video app**, with:

* endless short-video feed
* AI-generated videos
* personalized entertainment
* AI characters / chat
* Android app
* 1M+ downloads on Google Play

[Clip AI on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=joinclip.ai&utm_source=chatgpt.com)

This is more entertainment/social than “AI cinema.” ([Google Play][2])

---

### 3. Havira — more AI-native creation + social discovery

[Havira](https://havira.app/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

This one is interesting because the product starts with:

> pick a theme → describe an idea → add a photo → AI makes the video

and explicitly has:

* showcases
* featured videos
* videos from friends
* social feed
* iOS + Android
* multiple underlying video models

It also abstracts the model layer — currently mentioning Veo, Kling and fal.ai — so users don't need to care which model generated the clip. ([Havira][3])

### The important distinction

There are actually **three different categories** emerging:

| Product     | Generate | Consume feed | Social graph | AI-only content |
| ----------- | -------: | -----------: | -----------: | --------------: |
| TikTok      |        ❌ |            ✅ |            ✅ |               ❌ |
| Sora        |        ✅ |            ✅ |         some |       mostly AI |
| Kling       |        ✅ |      limited |            ❌ |               ❌ |
| VIBE        |        ✅ |            ❌ |            ❌ |               ❌ |
| **AICines** |        ✅ |        **✅** |        **✅** |           **✅** |
| **Clip AI** |        ✅ |        **✅** |        **✅** |      **mostly** |
| **Havira**  |        ✅ |        **✅** |         some |          mostly |

So **AICines is the one I'd investigate first** if you're thinking about the product category rather than simply looking for an AI video generator.

The really interesting product thesis is:

```text
             Traditional TikTok
                    │
              human creates
                    ↓
              video uploaded
                    ↓
             recommendation
                    ↓
                 viewer


             AI-native video
                    │
              user has IDEA
                    ↓
             AI generates video
                    ↓
            instantly published
                    ↓
             recommendation
                    ↓
                 viewer
                    │
                    ↓
             remix / mutate
                    │
                    ↓
             AI generates again
```

That last loop is the important one.

Instead of:

**watch → watch → watch**

you can get:

**watch → remix → generate → publish → watch → remix**

That potentially makes the **content graph generative**, rather than merely UGC.

Also worth noting: there are now mobile AI-video generators such as VIBE, but they're primarily **creation utilities**, not social networks. VIBE supports iOS/Android and multiple video models, but the product loop is still generate → export/share rather than generate → publish → recommendation → remix. ([VIBE: AI Video Generator][4])
