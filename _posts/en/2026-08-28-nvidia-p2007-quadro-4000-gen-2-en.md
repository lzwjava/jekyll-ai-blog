**Question:** What is the NVIDIA P2007, and what graphics card does it correspond to?

**Answer:**

The NVIDIA P2007 is the board/model number for the **NVIDIA Quadro 4000 (Gen 2)** — a professional workstation graphics card.

Here are the key specs:

- **GPU Model:** GF100-827 (GF100), based on the **Fermi** architecture, built on a 40 nm fabrication process with 3.2 billion transistors.
- **CUDA Cores:** 256, with 32 TMUs and 32 ROPs.
- **Memory:** 2048 MB GDDR5, 256-bit bus, 89.9 GB/s bandwidth.
- **Interface:** PCI-Express 2.0 x16, with 1× DVI-I DualLink and 2× DisplayPort outputs.
- **TDP:** 142 W, requiring a 1× 6-pin power connector.
- **API Support:** DirectX 12, OpenGL 4.5, OpenCL 1.1.

In short, the P2007 is a **professional/workstation GPU** (Quadro line), not a consumer GeForce card. It was designed for CAD, 3D design, animation, and video production workloads.
