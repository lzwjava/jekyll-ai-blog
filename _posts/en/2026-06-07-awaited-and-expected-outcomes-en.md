**anticipated** (adjective or past tense verb):

1. **Expected or foreseen** — something you believe will happen based on available information
   - *Example:* "The anticipated release date for the model is next month."

2. **Looked forward to** — something desired or awaited
   - *Example:* "The anticipated launch generated buzz in the community."

3. **Past tense of anticipate** — to expect, predict, or prepare for something in advance
   - *Example:* "We anticipated the server traffic spike and scaled accordingly."

**In context:**
- "The anticipated performance improvement came from optimizing the attention mechanism."
- "We anticipated GPU memory constraints and used gradient checkpointing."

It's commonly used in technical writing to signal expected outcomes or planned features.
