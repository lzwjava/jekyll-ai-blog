The build hit a hard stop: the iPhone 13 has **Developer Mode disabled** — Apple requires it on before any development app can be installed.

## Please enable Developer Mode on the iPhone 13

1. On the phone: **Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode**
2. Toggle **Developer Mode ON**
3. The phone will ask to **restart** — let it restart
4. After reboot, an alert appears ("Turn on Developer Mode?") → confirm (Face ID/passcode)

> ℹ️ This setting only shows up the first time you plug a device into dev tooling — if you don't see "Developer Mode" in Privacy & Security, reboot the phone once with it still connected, then check again.
