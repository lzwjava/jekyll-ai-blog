**Question:** Who founded Monetag, where is it headquartered, and how many employees does it have?

**Answer:**

**Company Overview**

Monetag was originally part of the larger PropellerAds ecosystem, one of the internet's most well-known alternative ad networks. PropellerAds started back in 2011, building its reputation through innovative formats like push ads and pop-unders. In 2022, the monetization side of PropellerAds was rebranded as Monetag to better reflect its growing autonomy and specialized focus on publisher monetization.

In short: **Monetag = the publisher-side brand** of PropellerAds, while PropellerAds continues as the advertiser-facing platform.

**More Context**

- Monetag is an unfunded company based in Limassol, Cyprus, founded in 2012. It operates as an AI-based platform for monetization of desktop and app audiences and has not raised any funding yet.

- Monetag is directly affiliated with PropellerAds ad network that sells traffic to marketers, but it operates independently, covering primarily the publisher side of traffic distribution.

- When the rebrand happened, the new name reflected the goal to fully focus on publishers and developing high-tech monetization solutions, with all existing publisher accounts, settings, ad zones, and tags remaining unchanged.

> ⚠️ Note: The **founder's name is not publicly disclosed** by the company. Peter Efimov is listed as CEO but is noted in a client testimonial as "CEO of App-Shake," suggesting he may be a client rather than Monetag's internal CEO. Founder information for PropellerAds/Monetag has never been officially published.
