## 🚗 Parking Guide for Zhuhai Museum

### Museum's Own Parking Lot (Recommended First Choice)

**Zhuhai Museum** is located at No. 88 Haihong Road, Xiangzhou District, and has a **ground-level parking lot** with **free parking** .

- ✅ **Free of charge**
- ⚠️ Limited spaces available on a first-come-first-served basis; if full, you'll need to find nearby alternatives

### 🔔 Important Reminders

1. **Entrance Adjustment**: Since September 6, 2025, the parking lot entrance has been relocated to **Qinglv Middle Road (Haitian Park side)**; the original Haihong Road entrance is now an **exit-only**

3. **Reservation Required**: The museum requires advance booking through its official WeChat public account, with timed entry
