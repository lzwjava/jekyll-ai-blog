Question: Besides Google AdSense, what other ways can a website use to generate revenue?

Answer:

For a technical blog / AI-focused site like yours (engineering articles, AI experiments, tutorials), AdSense is usually one of the lowest-value monetization methods. Higher-value options are closer to your audience's intent.

## 1. Sponsorships (usually highest ROI for technical blogs)

Companies pay for:

* sponsored articles
* newsletter sponsorship
* banner placement
* tool reviews
* developer tool integrations

Examples:

* AI infrastructure companies
* cloud GPU providers
* developer tools
* SaaS companies

Typical pricing:

* Small technical blog: $100–$1,000 per sponsored post
* Strong niche audience: $1,000–$10,000+

A blog with 50k–100k monthly readers in AI/dev can often outperform AdSense with just a few sponsors.
