Question: What does "proliferating" mean?

Answer: **Proliferating** means rapidly increasing in number, spreading widely, or growing at a fast rate. It comes from the verb "to proliferate."

It can describe:

- **Living things** — cells, bacteria, or organisms multiplying quickly
- **Ideas or objects** — something spreading or becoming more widespread in a short time
- **Problems or phenomena** — issues growing out of control

**Examples:**

- "Social media platforms have been *proliferating* over the past decade."
- "Cancer cells begin *proliferating* uncontrollably."
- "Misinformation is *proliferating* online."

The adjective form is **proliferative**, and the noun is **proliferation** (e.g., "nuclear proliferation").
