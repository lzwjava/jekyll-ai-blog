**Question: If someone earns $150k–$200k/year in San Francisco, what is the equivalent income level in Guangzhou?**

A rough purchasing-power comparison:

* **$150k USD/year in San Francisco ≈ ¥600k–¥900k CNY/year in Guangzhou**
* **$200k USD/year in San Francisco ≈ ¥800k–¥1.2M CNY/year in Guangzhou**

Monthly equivalent:

| San Francisco salary             | Guangzhou equivalent lifestyle |
| -------------------------------- | -----------------------------: |
| $150k/year (~$12.5k/month gross) |                ¥50k–¥75k/month |
| $200k/year (~$16.7k/month gross) |               ¥65k–¥100k/month |

The reason is housing. San Francisco is extremely expensive, especially rent/housing. Some cost comparisons estimate SF is around 2–3× more expensive than Guangzhou overall, with housing differences even larger. ([Expatistan, cost of living comparisons][1])

But there is another perspective: **career market value**.

For a software/AI engineer:

* **$150k–200k SF salary** = strong senior engineer level in US tech.
* In Guangzhou, a comparable lifestyle may only need **¥500k–800k/year**, but the *same technical value* may not map directly because China salaries are lower.
* A Guangzhou AI engineer earning:

  * ¥300k/year → comfortable middle class
  * ¥500k/year → very strong
  * ¥800k–1M/year → top-tier local engineering income

For someone who already owns a house in Guangzhou (no rent), the gap becomes much bigger. A person earning **¥500k–700k/year in Guangzhou with owned housing** may feel financially similar to a Bay Area engineer earning **$180k–220k**, because SF rent alone can easily consume $40k–60k/year. ([Zumper - Apartments for Rent & Houses][2])

For your situation (AI engineer + Guangzhou house + family), the comparison is roughly:

* **US path:** $180k AI engineer salary → high income, but expensive housing, taxes, childcare.
* **Guangzhou path:** ¥600k–800k/year AI income → similar lifestyle comfort, especially with your own home.

However, the **wealth accumulation potential** is different:

* US AI jobs give higher USD income, stock options, global career capital.
* Guangzhou gives lower costs, family support, and existing assets.

So a practical target:

* Guangzhou: **¥50k/month+**
* US Bay Area: **$150k–200k/year**

are roughly in the same "high-skilled engineer, upper-middle-class lifestyle" category.
