**Question:** What is the minimum registered capital (注册资金) required to register a company in Guangzhou?

---

**Answer:**

**For most industries: RMB 0 (no statutory minimum)**

Under China's current framework — reinforced by the 2024 Company Law amendments that took effect July 1, 2024 — there is **no mandatory minimum registered capital** for most industries. This builds on the 2013 reform that replaced the old mandatory minimum system with a subscription-based model.

Guangzhou's State Administration for Market Regulation (GZ-SAMR) reviews capital declarations for **reasonableness** against the proposed business scope and the implied 12–36 month operating burn. Set capital too low and the license application may stall.

---

**Key Rule — 2024 Company Law (5-Year Payment Rule)**

All new LLCs must have their registered capital **fully paid within 5 years** of registration. This is actively enforced as of 2026.

---

**Practical Recommendation**

A **recommended minimum is ¥100,000 RMB**, even though no law mandates it — it signals financial credibility to regulators and banks.

---

**Industry-Specific Exceptions**

Some sectors **do** have statutory minimums, for example:

| Industry | Minimum Capital |
|---|---|
| Banking / Finance | Varies (set by CBIRC) |
| Insurance | RMB 200M+ |
| Securities | RMB 50M+ |
| Travel agencies | RMB 300,000+ |

Financial institutions must verify 100% of the minimum registered capital before obtaining a business license, with funds held in a dedicated escrow account.
