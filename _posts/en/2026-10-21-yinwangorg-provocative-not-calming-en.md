**Question:** Can the website yinwang.org calm people down?

**Answer:**

Not really — and quite the opposite for many readers.

**yinwang.org** is the personal blog of **Wang Yin (王垠)**, a well-known and polarizing figure in Chinese programming circles. Wang Yin graduated from Sichuan University, was admitted to Tsinghua University's computer science PhD program, and later studied at Cornell and Indiana University — famously withdrawing from multiple PhD programs and writing lengthy public essays about each departure. His blog is titled *"当然我在扯淡"* (Chinese, "Of Course I'm Bullshitting") and *"Surely I am Joking"* in English — a nod to Feynman's memoir.

His writing style is:
- **Opinionated and sharp** — he frequently criticizes programming languages, tech companies, academic institutions, and industry culture
- **Provocative** — his critical articles tend to cut straight to what he sees as absurdity and often resonate strongly with readers, but also generate fierce disagreement
- **Controversial** — he is known for criticizing many programming languages, which led him to eventually design his own called "Yin"

For most readers, his posts spark **debate and strong reactions**, not calm. He has a reputation for being very bold and opinionated, which draws both admiration and criticism. Reading him is more like a mental sparring match than a relaxing experience.

That said, if you personally enjoy **clear, confident, no-nonsense thinking** and don't mind disagreeing with the author, some people do find his directness *clarifying* rather than stressful. But "calming" in a soothing sense? Almost certainly not.
