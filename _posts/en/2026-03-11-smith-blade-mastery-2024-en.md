### Overview and Lore

Smith and Blade refers to Gan Jiang & Mo Ye, legendary blacksmith figures from Chinese mythology known as the Gan Jiang & Mo Ye duo, originating from the Misty Marshes faction. Gan Jiang was a master swordsmith from the Misty Marshes with an obsession with swords and blades, while his wife Mo Ye sacrificed herself by throwing into the forge, granting him a divine blade, after which they became spiritually merged—Gan Jiang became one with the sword, and the blade transformed into his wife's likeness.

### Hero Role and Class

Gan and Mo is a Mage in Honor of Kings known for their long burst damage capabilities. This is a ranged magic damage dealer that excels at dealing significant damage from distance.

### Core Ability Mechanics

**Passive - Forever Entwined (Sword Energy System):**
When releasing Flying Sword skills, Mo will pair with Gan's Male Sword, sending out the Female Sword to deal equivalent damage. The Flying Sword skills share a maximum number of uses. **Skill 1 - Guard Her Grave (Defensive Tool):**
Gan thrusts the Sword Tomb forward twice, pushing back and knocking up enemies while retreating. The Sword Tomb deals 300/350/400/450/500/550 (+50% Magic Power) magic damage and knocks back enemies. Enemies hit by the Sword Tomb have their Magic Defense reduced by 150/180/210/240/270/300 for 3 seconds. This skill provides self-protection and repositioning capability.

**Skill 2 - Coupled Blades Short:**

**Ultimate - Freshly Forged:**
The ultimate skill refreshes the cooldown of the flying swords while dealing additional damage, providing burst potential.
