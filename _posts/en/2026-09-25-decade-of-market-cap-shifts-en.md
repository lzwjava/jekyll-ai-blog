**Question:** How has the ranking of the world's largest companies by market cap changed over the last 10 years?

**Answer:**

The shift over the past decade has been dramatic — a near-total dominance by U.S. tech, and the rise of AI chips as the defining value driver.

### 🔑 Key Shifts

**1. AI & Chips Rewrote the Rankings**
NVIDIA had one of its biggest increases in market cap in recent years, jumping from an estimated $364B in 2022 to over $1.2T in 2023, and has since surged to become the world's most valuable company — a position unimaginable in 2015.

**2. Tech Has Completely Taken Over**
As of 2026, except for Berkshire Hathaway, all of the top 10 market cap companies are part of the technology sector. In 2015, the top 10 included major oil companies (Exxon, PetroChina) and banks (Wells Fargo, ICBC).

**3. Energy & Finance Were Pushed Out**
In 2015, the financial sector led the top 100 at $3.2 trillion, with tech at $2.8 trillion. Today, that relationship has completely reversed — tech companies command orders of magnitude more value.

**4. China's Giants Faded**
PetroChina and ICBC were both in the global top 10 in 2015. Neither appears in the top 10 today — though Tencent Holdings remains relevant at ~$562B as of the most recent rankings.

**5. Market Caps Have Exploded in Scale**
The #1 company in 2015 (Apple) had a market cap of ~$650B. Today, NVIDIA leads with a market cap of approximately $4.74 trillion — a 7× increase in what it takes to be #1.
