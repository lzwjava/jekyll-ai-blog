Question: What does this information about Namecheap domain registration and auto-renewal mean?

Answer:

Here’s a clear breakdown of what the message is saying:

1. **Refund Policy**

   * Normally, **domain registrations and renewals are final**—you cannot get your money back.
   * **Exception:** If you registered your domain **within the last 5 days (120 hours)**, you may be eligible for a **cancellation and refund**.
   * Refunds are **not available** for:

     * Premium domains
     * Domains restricted by the registry

   **What you should do:** Check your domain registration date. If it’s less than 5 days old, you can request a refund.

2. **Auto-Renewal**

   * Domains are usually set to **auto-renew**, meaning Namecheap will automatically charge you the renewal fee when your registration period ends.
   * You can **disable auto-renewal** to avoid being charged for the next year:

     1. Log in to Namecheap.
     2. Go to **Domain List**.
     3. Click **Manage** next to your domain.
     4. Turn off **auto-renewal**.

3. **What Happens if You Don’t Want the Domain**

   * You **cannot cancel or delete it immediately**.
   * If you just **do nothing**, the domain will **expire** after the registration period and eventually be released back to the public.
   * Other options:

     * Sell it on **Namecheap Market**
     * Transfer it to someone else

4. **Reference**

   * More details can be found here: [Namecheap guide for unwanted domains](https://www.namecheap.com/support/knowledgebase/article.aspx/10045/46/what-to-do-with-an-unwanted-domain/)

**In short:** If your domain is very new, you might get a refund. If not, you can just **turn off auto-renewal** and let it expire, or sell/transfer it.
