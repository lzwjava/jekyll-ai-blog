Question: What does Stripe say in its 2025 annual letter?

Answer:

Stripe published its 2025 annual letter on February 24, 2026, authored by cofounders Patrick and John Collison. The letter highlights a strong year for Stripe and the broader internet economy.

Key points from the letter include:

- **Performance Metrics**: Businesses on Stripe generated $1.9 trillion in total payment volume in 2025, a 34% increase from 2024, equivalent to about 1.6% of global GDP. Stripe powers over 5 million businesses (directly or via platforms), including all top AI companies, 90% of the Dow Jones Industrial Average, 80% of the Nasdaq 100, and 25% of Delaware corporations created via Stripe Atlas. Link (Stripe's one-click payment product) is used by over 200 million people.
- **Profitability and Investments**: Stripe remained "robustly profitable," enabling heavy investment in product development (over 350 updates in 2025) and acquisitions, such as Privy (programmable wallets) and Metronome (usage-based billing, now part of Stripe's Revenue suite on track for $1 billion annual run rate).
- **Trends and Observations**:
  - The 2025 startup class showed unprecedented speed and growth, with newer companies growing faster than established ones.
  - Rise of "global-by-default" businesses, with over half (57%) of new Stripe sign-ups outside the US, reflecting faster international expansion without traditional country-by-country models.
  - Progress in stablecoins: Payment volume doubled to $400 billion in 2025; Stripe's acquired platform Bridge saw volume more than quadruple.
  - Evolution of "agentic commerce" (AI agents in commerce), described in levels, with early stages underway.
  - Other themes include escaping "low revenue mode" for businesses, working capital improvements via Stripe Capital (45% funding volume growth, supporting over 81,000 businesses), and broader economic shifts (e.g., software/AI driving US GDP growth, profit shifts in sectors like airlines and healthcare toward tech).
- **Overall Tone**: The letter portrays 2025 as an acceleration year for the internet economy, with Stripe positioned at the center of programmable finance, AI integration, and global growth. It emphasizes trends worth watching as change accelerates.

The full letter is available on Stripe's site, with a PDF version also linked in announcements.
