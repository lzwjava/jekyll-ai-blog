**Question:** Which is the best university in Sri Lanka?

**Answer:**

There's no single winner — it depends on the field — but three names dominate:

- **University of Colombo** — the consensus overall #1. It's Sri Lanka's highest-ranked university in QS World Rankings 2026 (#1001–1200 band) and ranks #1 nationally by total H-index, founded 1921. Strongest for medicine, law, and sciences.
- **University of Peradeniya** — the classic research-intensive rival, #2 locally in QS (#1201–1400 band), with the most beautiful campus and strong engineering/agriculture/medicine programs. It appears in the largest number of international rankings among Sri Lankan universities.
- **University of Moratuwa** — relevant to your world: it's the undisputed top school for **engineering, computer science, and IT**, ranked #3 locally in QS 2026. Moratuwa CS grads dominate Sri Lanka's tech industry and consistently place well in Google Summer of Code and international competitive programming. If someone's going into software/AI, Moratuwa > Colombo.

Different ranking methodologies disagree on #1 — one meta-ranking puts University of Kelaniya first, while US News ranks University of Sri Jayewardenepura highest globally — but the practical shorthand is: **Colombo for overall prestige, Moratuwa for engineering/CS, Peradeniya for research breadth**. All state universities are essentially free for local students who qualify via A-Level z-scores, which makes admission extremely competitive.
