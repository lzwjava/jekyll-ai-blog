Question: What does "城域网" mean?

Answer: **城域网** (chéngyù wǎng) means **Metropolitan Area Network (MAN)**.

It is a type of computer network that spans a city or a large campus — larger than a Local Area Network (LAN) but smaller than a Wide Area Network (WAN). It typically covers a geographic range of about **5 to 50 kilometers**, connecting multiple LANs within a metropolitan area such as a city or town.

Key characteristics:
- Covers a city or metropolitan region
- Higher speed than WAN, often using fiber optic connections
- Used by governments, ISPs, and large organizations to interconnect buildings or districts
- Common technologies include Ethernet, SONET/SDH, and carrier Ethernet

Breaking down the characters:
- 城 (chéng) = city
- 域 (yù) = area / region
- 网 (wǎng) = network
