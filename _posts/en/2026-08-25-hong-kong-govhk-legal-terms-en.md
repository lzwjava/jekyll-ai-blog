Question: Explain the Hong Kong GovHK Copyright Notice & Disclaimer page.

Answer:

This page is basically the **legal terms for using Hong Kong government's website content**. It has two main parts:

1. **Copyright rules** — what you can copy/use.
2. **Disclaimer** — what responsibility the government accepts or does not accept.

Source: ([Government of Hong Kong][1])

---

## 1. Copyright Notice

### A. Government owns most content

> Unless otherwise indicated, contents on GovHK are copyrighted by the HKSAR Government.

Meaning:

* Text, documents, explanations, forms, instructions, etc. are generally owned by the government.
* You cannot assume "government website = free for anything". ([Government of Hong Kong][1])

---

## 2. Text content: relatively open use

Government text can usually be copied for:

* personal use
* internal company use

Example:

✅ You copy QMAS requirements into your own notes.

✅ Your company internally summarizes immigration rules.

But conditions:

* Do not sell the copied content.
* Do not use it to promote a business.
* Keep it accurate.
* Mention the source (GovHK). ([Government of Hong Kong][1])

Example:

> Source: GovHK ([www.gov.hk](http://www.gov.hk))

---

## 3. Images / videos / graphics: stricter

For:

* photos
* diagrams
* videos
* audio
* graphics

you normally need written permission first. ([Government of Hong Kong][1])

Example:

You cannot simply download the Hong Kong government logo/photo and put it on your commercial website.

---

## 4. Commercial usage restriction

The important distinction:

### Personal research

Allowed:

```
Download QMAS PDF
Read requirements
Use AI to analyze rules
Make personal notes
```

### Commercial product

Need permission:

```
Create a paid immigration course
Copy GovHK text into your paid course
Sell a QMAS guide using government screenshots
```

because that is using government material for commercial benefit. ([Government of Hong Kong][1])

---

# Disclaimer section

This is the government's "don't sue us" section.

## 1. Information is "AS IS"

Meaning:

> We provide information, but we do not promise it is perfect.

They say:

* information may contain errors
* information may become outdated
* website may have technical problems
* no guarantee it fits your specific situation

([Government of Hong Kong][1])

Example:

You read:

```
QMAS requires X points
```

Then rules change next month.

Government says:

> You are responsible for checking the latest rules.

---

## 2. Government is not responsible for your decisions

Example:

You read a Hong Kong immigration page.

Then:

* quit your job
* move family
* spend money

and later discover your application fails.

The government disclaimer says:

> We are not liable for your losses because you relied on website information.

([Government of Hong Kong][1])

---

## 3. External links are not endorsements

If GovHK links to:

```
https://some-other-site.com
```

it does not mean:

* Hong Kong government approves that site
* government guarantees that site's accuracy

([Government of Hong Kong][1])

---

## 4. They can change information anytime

The government reserves the right to:

* remove information
* edit pages
* suspend services

without prior notice. ([Government of Hong Kong][1])
