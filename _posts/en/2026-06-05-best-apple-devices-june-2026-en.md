**Question:** What are the best iPhones and Macs available right now in June 2026?

**Answer:**

## iPhone Lineup (June 2026)

The **iPhone 17** is the best choice for most buyers at $799, packing the A19 chip and 8GB RAM, ensuring smooth performance. It's getting the attention because the base model now covers all necessary bases for $300 or $400 less than Pro models, with a new 6.3-inch display and "ProMotion" at 120Hz (up from 60Hz).

If you want the absolute flagship: The iPhone 17 Pro Max offers the fastest A19 Pro chip, a 6.9-inch screen, 3,000 nits peak brightness, and anti-glare coating, even available in a 2TB variant for video recording.

Budget option: The iPhone 17e is the cheapest model with the newer A19 chip and longer battery life than iPhone 16.

If you want larger without Pro pricing: The iPhone 16 Plus offers a larger screen and longer battery life for video and reading.

Current OS: iOS 26 (Apple switched from version numbers to release years).

## Mac Lineup (June 2026)

The **MacBook Air (M5) is the best MacBook for most buyers** because it offers the strongest balance of price, portability, battery life, and performance. A 15-inch M5 MacBook Air launched in early 2026 alongside the 13-inch model.

For your M2 Air → upgrade path: M5 is the current generation. Amazon shows 15-inch M5 MacBook Air (512GB) at around $1,149.99 with discounts.

If you need raw power: The MacBook Pro M5 Pro 16-inch is for power users who do video editing, ML work, or heavy compilation.

Budget entry point: The **MacBook Neo** is the budget pick that handles web browsing and apps in a compact form factor with fun colors.

Three-tier structure now: Neo (budget, A18 Pro) → Air M5 (mainstream) → Pro M5/M5 Max (premium).
