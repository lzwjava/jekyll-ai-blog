**Question:** What is Skywell Corp (skywellcorp.com)?

**Answer:**

**Skywell** is a Chinese electric vehicle manufacturer whose official website is [skywellcorp.com](https://www.skywellcorp.com).

**Origins & Background**

Skywell was established in 2017 as a collaboration between Skyworth — one of the world's largest consumer electronics manufacturers — and the Nanjing Golden Dragon Bus Company. The company was previously known as Nanjing Golden Dragon and became one of China's leading electric bus manufacturers before pivoting to smaller passenger vehicles.

**What They Make**

Skywell specializes in researching, developing, manufacturing, and selling electric vehicles, including public buses, passenger cars, and commercial vehicles, along with battery electric technology.

Their flagship passenger car is the **BE11**, an electric SUV. The BE11 was first launched in China in 2020 under the name ET5, and since then the company has expanded to 12 production facilities across China and exports to 100 countries worldwide.

**BE11 Highlights**

The BE11 is powered by a 204 PS / 320 Nm motor and can be specified with either a 72 kWh or 86 kWh battery, offering WLTP ranges of 248 miles and 304 miles respectively. Standard equipment includes LED headlights, 19-inch alloy wheels, keyless entry, a panoramic sunroof, a 360-degree camera, and a 12.8-inch central touchscreen with wireless smartphone integration.

**UK Market Entry**

Skywell began importing cars to the UK in 2024, with distribution handled by Innovation Automotive. The BE11 costs from £36,995 for the standard-range model and £39,995 for the long-range variant, competing against cars like the Skoda Enyaq, Nissan Ariya, and Ford Mustang Mach-E.

**2026 Update**

In January 2026, Skywell announced the new BE11 2026 Model Year, featuring significant technical upgrades, new technology features, and improved driver comfort — just 12 months after its UK introduction.
