Question: Expand the L-1 transfer route using a Hong Kong company → US subsidiary structure, especially for my entrepreneur/AI engineer profile.

Answer:

For your situation, L-1 is interesting because you are not just a job seeker. You already have:

* software engineering experience
* AI consulting/business direction
* company-building experience
* international client possibility

L-1 is basically a **"move yourself from your foreign company to your US company"** visa.

The core structure:

```
                 Ownership
                    |
                    |
        +-----------------------+
        |                       |
        v                       v

 Hong Kong Company        US Company
 (foreign entity)         (subsidiary/affiliate)

        |
        |
  You work here 1+ year
        |
        |
        v

       L-1 transfer

        |
        |
        v

   Work in USA
   Build US business
```

The US entity and foreign entity must have a qualifying relationship (parent, subsidiary, affiliate, branch). You generally need to have worked for the foreign company for one continuous year within the previous three years before applying. The US role must be managerial, executive, or involve specialized knowledge. ([Family Assistance Management][1])

# L-1A vs L-1B for you

## L-1A (manager/executive)

Probably better.

US role:

```
CEO
Founder
Engineering Director
AI Product Lead
```

Evidence:

* you own company
* you make decisions
* you manage people
* you control budgets
* you build US operation

Maximum stay can reach 7 years. ([LegalClarity][2])

---

## L-1B (specialized knowledge)

Alternative:

```
AI Infrastructure Engineer
LLM Training Specialist
```

Argument:

"My knowledge of our AI systems, models, training pipeline, and proprietary technology is specialized."

Evidence:

* internal AI platform
* proprietary code
* models
* customers using your technology

Maximum stay is generally shorter than L-1A (commonly up to 5 years). ([LegalClarity][2])

# The "new office" L-1 case

This is the part entrepreneurs like.

You don't need an existing huge US company.

You can say:

```
HK company
     |
     |
opens
     |
     v
US office
```

USCIS allows L-1 beneficiaries to come to establish a new US office if requirements are met. The company needs evidence such as premises, qualifying relationship, and ability to start doing business. ([Family Assistance Management][1])

But the challenge:

# The bigger picture: L-1 → Green Card

A common entrepreneur route:

```
Hong Kong company
        |
        |
       L-1A
        |
        |
 US company grows
        |
        |
      EB-1C
        |
        |
    Green Card
```

EB-1C is the multinational manager/executive immigrant route. L-1A is often used as preparation because both focus on multinational managerial roles.
