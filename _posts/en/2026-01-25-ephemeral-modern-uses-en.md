Question: What does "ephemeral" mean?

Answer:

**Ephemeral** means lasting for a very short time, temporary, short-lived, or quickly passing.

It is most commonly used in these contexts:

- **General / everyday English**
  Something that disappears or changes rapidly.
  Example: "The beauty of cherry blossoms is ephemeral."

- **Social media / tech** (very common modern usage)
  Content that automatically disappears after being viewed or after a short period (usually within 24 hours).
  Examples:
  - Snapchat stories and messages
  - Instagram Stories
  - WhatsApp disappearing messages
  - Fleets (Twitter's old ephemeral feature)
  → "I posted an ephemeral story on Instagram."

- **Biology / ecology**
  Short-lived organisms or stages of life.
  Example: "Mayflies are ephemeral insects — most adults live only for a few hours or days."

- **Computing / programming**
  Temporary data that exists only briefly.
  Examples:
  - Ephemeral ports (short-lived TCP/UDP ports)
  - Ephemeral storage (temporary disk space in containers/Kubernetes)
  - Ephemeral keys (cryptographic keys used only once or for a very short time)

**Quick synonym list**

- fleeting
- transient
- momentary
- short-lived
- temporary
- passing

**Antonyms**

- permanent
- lasting
- enduring
- eternal
