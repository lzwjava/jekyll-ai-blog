For Spring Festival 2026, Guangzhou offers a variety of kid-friendly activities at its parks and play areas. Here are some highlights and how to participate:

### 1. Guangzhou People’s Park
- **Activities**: During Spring Festival, the park is decorated with red lanterns and hosts a stamp design competition exhibition. Kids can explore ancient trees, enjoy the fountain area, and watch local dance performances. The park is also a great place for a family stroll and to learn about different plants.
- **How to Play**: Simply visit the park; no admission fee or reservation is required. It’s accessible by subway and recommended for a short visit of 0.5–1 hour.

### 2. Guangzhou Children’s Park
- **Activities**: This park is especially popular with families. Kids can enjoy open grassy areas, playgrounds, and nature trails. It’s a relaxed environment for children to run around and play, supervised by parents or grandparents.

### 3. Spring Festival Lantern Festival (Yuexiu Park and other parks)
- **Activities**: The 2026 Guangzhou Spring Festival Lantern Festival features over 1,200 activities, including Cantonese opera performances, cosplay parades, APEC-themed markets, and traditional lantern riddle games. Yuexiu Park is the main venue, with seven other parks participating. There are also immersive digital experiences, such as a 100-meter digital scroll showcasing Guangzhou’s history and an XR sensory dome theater.
- **How to Play**: Visit Yuexiu Park or other participating parks. The festival runs through March 8, 2026. You can participate in lantern riddle games, watch performances, and explore the light displays. Some activities may require on-site registration or participation in group events.

### 4. Guangzhou Haixinsha Carnival
- **Activities**: Located at Haixinsha Asian Games Park, this carnival offers a variety of attractions, including games, performances, and interactive experiences designed for families and kids. It runs until March 8, 2026.
- **How to Play**: Visit the carnival area and participate in the games and activities available. The space is large and designed for holiday fun.

### 5. Chimelong Safari Park
- **Activities**: While not a traditional “play park,” Chimelong Safari Park is a popular family destination. Kids can see animals, participate in educational activities, and enjoy science stations.
- **How to Play**: Purchase tickets and spend a day exploring the park’s attractions and interactive exhibits.

### 6. Flower Street and Temple Fairs
- **Activities**: Guangzhou’s Flower Street is a must-visit during Spring Festival, featuring vibrant flower markets and traditional decorations. Temple fairs offer local snacks, crafts, and performances.
- **How to Play**: Walk through the flower markets, buy flowers or decorations, and enjoy local snacks and performances at temple fairs.
