Question: What chemical materials are used in a GPU board (e.g., NVIDIA RTX cards)?

Answer:
A modern graphics card (such as an NVIDIA RTX card) is a complex electronic assembly composed of several key components, each relying on specific chemical elements and synthetic materials:

### 1. The Silicon Die (GPU Processor)

* **Ultra-Pure Silicon ($\text{Si}$):** The core component (wafer) forming the semiconductor matrix.
* **Dopants:** Elements like **Boron ($\text{B}$)**, **Phosphorus ($\text{P}$)**, and **Arsenic ($\text{As}$)** are implanted to alter silicon's electrical properties to build transistors.
* **Interconnect Metals:** **Copper ($\text{Cu}$)**, **Aluminum ($\text{Al}$)**, **Cobalt ($\text{Co}$)**, and **Tungsten ($\text{W}$)** are used in microscopic layers to connect transistors.
* **Dielectrics & Semiconductors:** **Silicon Dioxide ($\text{SiO}_2$)**, **Titanium Nitride ($\text{TiN}$)**, and **Tantalum/Tantalum Nitride ($\text{Ta/TaN}$)** serve as insulating layers and barriers.

### 2. Printed Circuit Board (PCB)

* **FR-4 Substrate:** A composite of woven **fiberglass cloth** bound with **Epoxy Resin** (polyepoxide) to provide structural strength and electrical insulation.
* **Copper Foil:** Pure copper sheets laminated onto the substrate to create signal pathways, power lines, and ground planes.
* **Solder Mask:** Typically a photopolymer epoxy resin (often green, black, or custom colors) applied to protect copper traces from oxidation and short circuits.
* **Surface Finishes:** Chemical coatings like **ENIG (Electroless Nickel Immersion Gold)** or **OSP (Organic Solderability Preservatives)** preserve exposed pads.

### 3. Electrical Components (VRMs, Capacitors, Memory)

* **Capacitors:** Aluminum polymer caps (using **Aluminum** and conductive polymers) or MLCCs (Multi-Layer Ceramic Capacitors) using **Barium Titanate ($\text{BaTiO}_3$)**, **Nickel**, and **Tin**.
* **Inductors / Chokes:** Ferrite cores composed of **Iron Oxide ($\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3$)** mixed with **Manganese**, **Zinc**, or **Nickel**, bound in synthetic resins.
* **Solder Alloys:** Lead-free solders usually composed of **Tin-Silver-Copper ($\text{SAC305}$: $\text{Sn-Ag-Cu}$)** or **Tin-Bismuth ($\text{Sn-Bi}$)** for surface-mount soldering.

### 4. Thermal & Cooling Systems

* **Thermal Interface Materials (TIM):**
* **Thermal Paste/Grease:** Silicone polymer oil blended with conductive ceramic or metal fillers like **Zinc Oxide ($\text{ZnO}$)**, **Aluminum Oxide ($\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$)**, or micronized **Silver/Aluminum** particles.
* **Liquid Metal (High-End/Modded):** Liquid alloys primarily made of **Gallium ($\text{Ga}$)**, **Indium ($\text{In}$)**, and **Tin ($\text{Sn}$)**.
* **Phase-Change Materials (PCM):** Polyolefin or paraffin wax blends with thermal fillers that melt/soften under operational heat.

* **Heatsink & Heatpipes:** **Copper ($\text{Cu}$)** for vapor chambers and core contact surfaces; **Aluminum ($\text{Al}$)** for radiator fins; internal heatpipes contain small amounts of distilled **Water** or **Methanol** under partial vacuum.

### 5. Outer Shroud, Frame, and Packaging

* **Plastics:** **ABS (Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene)** and **Polycarbonate (PC)** for fan blades and outer shrouds.
* **Structural Metals:** Anodized **Aluminum** or **Steel** for backplates, PCI brackets, and internal structural frames.
