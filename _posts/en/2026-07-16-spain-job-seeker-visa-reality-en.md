Question: What is Spain’s Job Seeker Visa on My Spanish Residency?

Answer:

This page is about the **Spain Job Seeker Visa / residence permit** — a way for certain non-EU foreigners to stay in Spain while looking for work. However, the marketing around this visa is a bit misleading. ([MySpanishResidency][1])

The key point:

**Spain does NOT currently have a general "move to Spain for 1 year and find any job" visa for everyone.** ([Inclusión y Migraciones][2])

There are several different things people mix together:

## 1. Post-study job search residence (real and common)

If you study in Spain and graduate from a qualifying Spanish university, you may get a residence authorization to search for employment or start a business. It is mainly designed for international students who already studied in Spain. ([Inclusión y Migraciones][3])

Example:

```
China citizen
    |
    v
Spain Master degree
    |
    v
Graduate
    |
    v
Job search residence
    |
    v
Find company
    |
    v
Convert to work permit
```

This route is realistic.

---

## 2. "Job seeker visa from abroad" (the confusing one)

Many websites advertise:

> "Come to Spain without a job offer and search for work."

But the actual rules are much narrower. The current framework restricts this route to specific categories, such as:

* descendants of Spaniards of origin
* specific shortage occupations / regional programs when opened

It is **not** like Germany's Opportunity Card where many skilled workers can apply freely. ([Reddit][4])

## Compared with other European "job seeker" visas

| Country                      | Open job search visa? |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Germany Opportunity Card     | ✅ relatively open     |
| Portugal job seeker visa     | ✅ more open           |
| Spain job seeker visa        | ⚠️ limited            |
| Netherlands orientation year | mostly graduates      |
| UK Graduate visa             | graduates only        |

So if your goal is:

> "I want to enter Europe first, then find an AI job"

Spain is not the strongest option.

Germany is probably much better for an AI engineer.
