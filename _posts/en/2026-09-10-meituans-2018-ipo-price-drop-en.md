Question: **How was Meituan’s stock price in 2018?**

Answer: Meituan **wasn't publicly traded for most of 2018**. It IPO'd on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on **September 20, 2018**, at an IPO price of **HK$69**. ([Wikipedia][1])

The trajectory was pretty brutal:

| Date                     | Meituan 3690 |
| ------------------------ | -----------: |
| IPO price — Sep 2018     | **HK$69.00** |
| First-day close — Sep 20 | **HK$72.90** |
| Sep 2018 monthly         |    ~HK$68.75 |
| Oct 2018                 |    ~HK$50.70 |
| Nov 2018                 |    ~HK$52.45 |
| Dec 2018                 | **HK$43.90** |

([Digrin][2])

So by the end of 2018, Meituan had fallen from **HK$69 → ~HK$44**, roughly **−36%** from the IPO price.

And this makes Wang Xing's 2019 Li Auto investment more interesting: when he invested in Li Auto in **August 2019**, Meituan had recovered to around **HK$74**, but it had gone through a huge **HK$69 → HK$43** drawdown just months earlier. ([Digrin][2])

In other words, **Wang was already sitting through a very volatile public-market period while making a ~$300M private EV bet.**
