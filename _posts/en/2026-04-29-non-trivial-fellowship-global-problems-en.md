Question: What is the Non-Trivial Fellowship and what does it entail?

Answer: The **Non-Trivial Fellowship** is an intensive, global online program designed for ambitious young people (typically aged 14–20) who want to tackle the world’s most urgent and "non-trivial" problems. It is backed by **Non-Trivial**, a non-profit organization focused on identifying and supporting talent that can have a significant positive impact on the future of humanity.

### Core Philosophy
The term "non-trivial" refers to problems that are not easily solved and have high stakes for the future. The fellowship encourages students to move away from conventional paths—like simply chasing grades or prestige—and instead focus on **high-impact fields** such as:
* **Artificial Intelligence Safety:** Ensuring advanced AI aligns with human values.
* **Biosecurity:** Preventing global pandemics and biological threats.
* **Climate Change:** Developing scalable solutions for environmental crises.
* **Effective Altruism:** Using evidence and reason to figure out how to benefit others as much as possible.

### What the Fellowship Includes
* **8-Week Online Program:** Fellows engage in a structured curriculum that covers research, strategic thinking, and "heavy-lifting" problem-solving.
* **Mentorship:** Access to experts and researchers who are already working on global priorities.
* **Equity-Free Funding:** Participants can receive grants (often starting around $500 with potential for more) to kickstart their own high-impact projects or research.
* **Global Network:** Connection to a community of like-minded, high-achieving peers from around the world.

### Who Should Apply?
The program seeks individuals who demonstrate:
1.  **High Agency:** The drive to take initiative without waiting for permission.
2.  **Exceptional Ability:** Strong analytical skills or a track record of deep interest in complex subjects.
3.  **Altruistic Motivation:** A genuine desire to solve problems that affect millions or billions of lives.

It is particularly well-suited for students who feel "bored" by standard high school curricula and want to dive into research or entrepreneurship that matters on a global scale.
