This question asks about the overall writing technique used in "The Biography of Guo the Camel Planter" (《种树郭橐驼传》) by Liu Zongyuan. The correct answer is **B (analogy/类比)**, but you chose C.

## Why It's Analogy (类比)

The entire essay uses **tree planting as an extended analogy for governing people**. Here's how the systematic comparison works:

### The Analogical Structure

**1. Basic principle:**
- Tree planting → Governing people
- "Following the nature of trees to fulfill their natural tendencies" (顺木之天，以致其性)
- ↓↓↓ analogous to ↓↓↓
- "Following the nature of people to fulfill their natural tendencies" (顺民之天以致民之性)

**2. The caring approach:**
- "When planting, treat them like children" (其莳也若子)
- ↓↓↓ analogous to ↓↓↓
- Officials should care for and protect the common people

**3. The hands-off approach:**
- "Once planted, leave them as if abandoned" (其置也若弃)
- ↓↓↓ analogous to ↓↓↓
- Let the people rest and recuperate without excessive interference (休养生息)

**4. The negative example:**
- "Other planters love too deeply, worry too frequently" (爱之太殷，忧之太勤)
- ↓↓↓ analogous to ↓↓↓
- "Those in authority issue too many troublesome orders" (长人者好烦其令)

## Layer-by-Layer Analogy (层层类比，环环相应)

The essay systematically corresponds every aspect of tree cultivation to governance:
- By explaining tree planting principles thoroughly
- The principles of governing people are also made clear
- This creates a complete, coherent argumentative structure

## Why Not the Other Options?

**C - Contrast/Foil (反衬):** While there is some contrast (good vs. bad tree planters), this is a **supporting technique**, not the **overall method**. The essay's main structure is built on the systematic tree-to-governance analogy.

**A - Comparison (对比):** There is comparison between Guo and other planters, but again, this serves the larger analogical framework.

**D - Setting off/Highlighting (烘托):** This technique enhances atmosphere or character, not the primary method here.

## Key Takeaway

类比 (analogy) means using one complete system (A) to systematically explain another system (B). In this essay: **tree planting (A) = governance (B)** throughout the entire text, making it the **overall/总体** writing method.
