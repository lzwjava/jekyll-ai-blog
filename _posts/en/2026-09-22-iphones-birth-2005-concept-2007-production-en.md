Question: **Was the first iPhone born in Shenzhen’s Longhua district in 2005?**

Answer: **Almost — but the dates and wording need a correction.**

* **2005:** iPhone development began. Apple’s project was still secret; this was the *birth of the product concept/development*, not the manufacturing of a finished iPhone. ([Patent Application Status Tool][1])
* **2007:** The first physical iPhones entered production at **Foxconn**, including its Shenzhen manufacturing operations. Historical accounts describe Foxconn opening a new Shenzhen factory section specifically for the iPhone manufacturing bid. ([Wikipedia][2])
* **January 9, 2007:** Steve Jobs publicly unveiled the iPhone. ([Apple][3])
* **June 29, 2007:** The first iPhone went on sale in the US. ([Apple][4])

So a more accurate statement would be:

> **“The iPhone was conceived and developed starting in 2005, while its early mass production was carried out by Foxconn in Shenzhen, including Longhua, in 2007.”**

The interesting part is that **Shenzhen wasn't where the iPhone was invented, but it was a crucial part of turning Apple's 2005 prototype into a manufacturable product in 2007.** ([Wikipedia][2])
