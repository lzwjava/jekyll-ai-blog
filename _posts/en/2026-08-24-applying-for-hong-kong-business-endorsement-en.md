Question: **How do I apply for the Hong Kong business endorsement (商务签注 / S endorsement) on the Mainland Exit-Entry Permit?**

Answer: **If you're eligible, you apply through the Mainland public-security exit/entry authority (公安出入境), not Hong Kong Immigration.** Shenzhen has a specific process for this. ([WSBS][1])

### 1. The basic process

You need:

```text
往来港澳通行证
        +
赴香港商务签注（S）
        ↓
Hong Kong
```

If you don't already have the **EEP / 港澳通行证**, apply for that first. The National Immigration Administration lists the basic documents as application form, photo, Chinese ID, and fingerprints where required. ([NIA Government Service Platform][2])

For the **business endorsement**, the official Shenzhen process is roughly:

```text
1. Your mainland company / business qualification
              ↓
2. Enterprise applies for HK/Macau business qualification
              ↓
3. You are registered as a business traveler
              ↓
4. Apply for 港澳通行证 + S endorsement
              ↓
5. Travel to HK
```

Shenzhen specifically has an **“企业、经营单位申办港澳商务签注登记备案”** process. ([Shenzhen Public Security Bureau][3])

### 2. The important catch for you

If you're thinking:

> “I'll create a Hong Kong company, then get a Shenzhen S endorsement because I own that Hong Kong company.”

**That's not necessarily enough.**

The S endorsement is fundamentally a **Mainland-side business travel endorsement**, and the qualification is tied to the relevant Mainland enterprise/business-person rules. Shenzhen's official process specifically talks about **深圳市企业、经营单位** applying for Hong Kong/Macau business qualification. ([Shenzhen Public Security Bureau][4])

So if you currently have:

```text
Mainland individual
        +
Hong Kong Ltd
        ↓
Can I automatically get S endorsement?
```

**Don't assume yes.**

You should first ask Shenzhen Exit-Entry:

> “I am the shareholder/director of a Hong Kong company. Can I use this Hong Kong company as the business basis for applying for a Hong Kong 商务签注? If not, what Mainland enterprise qualification do I need?”

---

### 3. If you qualify, the good news

Since May 2024, Mainland residents can apply for Hong Kong/Macau business endorsements at **any Mainland public-security exit/entry window**, and the maximum stay per visit for an S endorsement was increased from **7 days to 14 days**. ([Shenzhen Public Security Bureau][5])

For Shenzhen, the official online process is:

1. Make an appointment through **深圳公安** / Shenzhen Public Security.
2. Go to the designated exit-entry hall.
3. Submit documents.
4. Shenzhen Public Security reviews the application.
5. Approval is normally listed as **1 working day**.
6. Document production is listed as **4 working days** after approval. ([WSBS][1])

### 4. What you can actually do in Hong Kong

This is where our previous discussion matters.

With the S endorsement, you can enter HK as a **visitor for legitimate business activities**, such as:

* meeting clients
* negotiating contracts
* submitting tenders
* business meetings
* trade fairs/exhibitions
* product orientation
* supervising installation/packaging

Hong Kong Immigration explicitly lists these activities. ([Immigration Department Hong Kong][6])

But:

```text
S endorsement
    ↓
visitor
    ↓
❌ employment
❌ paid consulting work
❌ unpaid employment
```

Hong Kong Immigration explicitly says a visitor **cannot take up employment, whether paid or unpaid**. ([Immigration Department Hong Kong][6])

So for your proposed AI consultancy:

```text
GOOD:

Guangzhou/Shenzhen
      ↓
S endorsement
      ↓
HK
      ↓
meet potential client
      ↓
discuss AI project
      ↓
negotiate/sign contract
      ↓
return Mainland


NOT GOOD:

S endorsement
      ↓
HK
      ↓
sit in HK office every day
      ↓
develop software for HK clients
      ↓
receive salary / consulting compensation
```

The second scenario needs an appropriate **Hong Kong employment permission**.

Interestingly, Hong Kong Immigration specifically addresses your kind of situation: Mainland business executives can enter with a business endorsement to **prepare for setting up a Hong Kong company**, and after establishment, there is an employment route under the **Admission Scheme for Mainland Talents and Professionals (ASMTP)**. ([Immigration Department Hong Kong][7])

So your practical sequence could be:

```text
Mainland
   │
   ├── establish HK company
   │
   ├── obtain legitimate business endorsement if eligible
   │
   ▼
HK business visits
   │
   ├── find clients
   ├── meetings
   ├── contracts
   └── establish business
   │
   ▼
HK company has genuine operation
   │
   ▼
ASMTP / appropriate employment permission
   │
   ▼
Actually live + work in Hong Kong
```

**That is much cleaner legally than trying to use the S endorsement as a de facto HK work visa.** ([Immigration Department Hong Kong][7])
