**Question:** Are companies like Infosys and Wipro listed in the Fortune/Forbes Global 2000?

**Answer:** Yes — but it's worth clarifying the list name first. The widely referenced ranking is the **Forbes Global 2000** (not "Fortune 2000"), which ranks the world's largest publicly traded companies across four metrics: sales, profits, assets, and market value.

Both Infosys and Wipro are regularly featured on it:

**Infosys** is one of India's largest IT firms and a consistent Forbes Global 2000 presence. With FY2026 revenues of roughly $19 billion (₹181,638 crore) and over 328,000 employees, it is publicly traded on the NYSE, BSE, and NSE. Infosys also markets itself explicitly to Fortune 2000-scale enterprise clients — the company was previously recognized as one of 74 Global High Performers selected from the Forbes Global 2000 list. More recently, Infosys was ranked in the Top 3 IT services brands in the world for the fifth consecutive year by Brand Finance's Global 500 2026 report.

**Wipro** is also on the Forbes Global 2000. It is described as a Fortune 2000 service provider — a global IT services company with 230,000+ employees delivering digital, cloud, AI, and cybersecurity services to Fortune 2000 organizations across multiple industries. Wipro posted FY2026 revenues of approximately $10 billion (₹96,497 crore) and is publicly traded on the BSE, NSE, and NYSE.

In terms of scale relative to peers, among India's largest IT service providers on the Forbes Global 2000, the ranking order runs: Tata Consultancy Services (the largest), followed by Infosys, Wipro, HCL Technologies, and Tech Mahindra.

The most recent Forbes Global 2000 (2026 edition, published June 24, 2026) was compiled using data from FactSet Research, measuring sales, profits, assets, and market value, with market value calculations based on closing prices as of May 15, 2026. Both Infosys and Wipro appear on that list.
