**Question:** How many videos can one upload to WeChat Video Channel (微信视频号) per day?

**Answer:**

Based on available information, WeChat Video Channel has **no fixed daily limit** on the number of videos you can post. According to early WeChat Video Channel rules, you can publish multiple posts per day with no restriction on the number of times.

However, there are **per-video technical limits** to be aware of:

- **Video length:** WeChat Video Channel has extended the maximum video length to 1 hour, and the maximum file size was raised from 1 GB to 2 GB.
- **Images:** Up to 9 photos per post
- **Caption:** Up to 1,000 characters

**Practical notes:**

- While there's no hard daily cap published, posting excessively in a short period may trigger spam detection.
- Content quality and originality matter — WeChat's algorithm favors original content and may suppress accounts that mass-upload low-quality or duplicated videos.
- Individual account standing and verification status may affect actual limits in practice.
