**Question:** How is "Hermes" (as in the AI agent framework) pronounced?

**Answer:**

It's pronounced **"HER-meez"** — like the Greek god Hermes.

- **HER** (rhymes with "her") + **MEEZ** (rhymes with "bees")
- IPA: /ˈhɜːrmiːz/

Not "her-MENGZ" or anything close to that. The agent framework is named after the Greek messenger god, so it follows the standard English pronunciation of that name.
