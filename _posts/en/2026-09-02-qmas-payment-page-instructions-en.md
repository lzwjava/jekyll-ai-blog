Question: What does this payment page mean?

Answer: This is the **payment instruction page for your Hong Kong QMAS application fee**. It is basically warning you how to safely complete the HK$600 payment.

The important parts:

1. **Transaction reference number**

   * Save the transaction reference number shown on the page.
   * It can be used to ask the Hong Kong Immigration Department/payment provider about the payment later.
   * Take a screenshot or print the page if useful.

2. **Do NOT close the page after clicking `Pay`**

   * After you click **Pay**, wait until you see the **acknowledgement/confirmation page**.
   * Don't close the browser, go back, or navigate away.
   * Otherwise, the payment might be processed but the application system may not receive the confirmation correctly.

3. **Merchant Name**

   * This only matters if you're paying by **credit card**.
   * It's likely the merchant name that will appear during the card payment/verification process.

4. **PPS payment**

   * PPS Shop&Buy is a Hong Kong electronic payment service.
   * It **doesn't currently work in mobile/tablet browsers**.
   * Use a desktop computer if you choose PPS.

5. **Refunds**

   * If a refund is exceptionally required and you paid by credit card, they can normally refund the **same credit card account**.

6. **Credit-card gateway errors**

   * You might see an error page or wait several minutes after submitting the card payment.
   * Don't immediately assume the payment failed.
   * Wait a little, then retry or use another payment method if necessary.

7. **3-D Secure / card authentication**

   * Your bank may require additional verification:

     * **J/Secure** → JCB
     * **Mastercard SecureCode** → Mastercard
     * **Verified by Visa** → Visa
   * This is normal online card authentication. If authentication fails, contact your card issuer.

### For your situation

Since you're paying the **HK$600 QMAS application fee**, the practical flow is:

```text
QMAS application
      ↓
Proceed to payment
      ↓
Select payment method
      ↓
Pay HK$600
      ↓
Complete bank/card authentication if required
      ↓
WAIT — don't close the page
      ↓
Acknowledgement / payment confirmation
      ↓
Save transaction reference + confirmation
```

**Most important:** if you click **Pay** and the browser appears to hang for several minutes, **don't immediately click Pay repeatedly**. You want to avoid accidentally creating uncertainty about whether the first transaction went through.
