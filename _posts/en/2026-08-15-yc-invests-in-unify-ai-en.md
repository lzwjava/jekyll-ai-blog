Question: Did YC invest in Unify AI (unify.ai)?

Answer: **Yes. Y Combinator invested in Unify.** ([Y Combinator][1])

There are actually two related Unify stories:

1. **Unify (YC W23) — LLM routing infrastructure**

   * Founder: Daniel Lenton
   * Batch: **YC Winter 2023**
   * Product: automatically routes prompts to the best LLM/provider based on quality, cost, and latency. ([Y Combinator][2])
   * YC profile lists it as an active YC company. ([Y Combinator][1])

2. Later, Unify evolved toward **AI teammates / virtual employees**:

   * "AI teammates for everyone else"
   * Agents that learn workflows, operate tools, and handle repetitive business tasks. ([Unify][3])

Funding:

* YC was an investor.
* Unify also raised an **$8M round** with investors including YC, SignalFire, M12, Lunar Ventures, A. Capital, etc. ([TechCrunch][4])

Why YC likely liked it:

* Strong technical founder background (Imperial College ML/robotics PhD).
* Very YC-style thesis: AI infrastructure + a painful developer/business workflow problem.
* Timing: 2023 was the explosion of LLM applications; model selection/router problems were obvious because GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, Llama etc. created complexity. ([Y Combinator][2])

Interesting point: Unify is a good example of YC's pattern:

* Start with a narrow technical wedge (**LLM routing**)
* Learn from users
* Move toward a much larger vision (**AI coworkers/agents**) ([Y Combinator][1])

This is similar to many YC companies: the initial product is often not the final company; the important part is finding a strong founder + fast iteration loop.
