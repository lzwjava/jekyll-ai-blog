Question: Is CSTU.edu authentic, and is California Science and Technology University a legitimate university for earning a degree?
Answer: Your assessment is directionally reasonable, but one important claim is incorrect: a .edu domain does not prove that an institution is currently accredited by a U.S. Department of Education–recognized accreditor.

You need to distinguish three separate questions:

1. Authenticity: Is the website operated by the institution it claims to represent?
2. Legal authorization: Is the institution authorized to operate in California?
3. Accreditation and degree value: Is its accreditation recognized, and will its degree satisfy employers, other universities, and immigration or credential-evaluation requirements?
These are independent questions. A school can be genuine and legally authorized to operate while still lacking recognized institutional accreditation.
What the official records actually show
The strongest evidence changes the verdict: CSTU appears to have genuine, recognized institutional accreditation, not merely a California operating license.
1. Institutional accreditation: confirmed by official records

The Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges (ACCSC) announced CSTU's initial accreditation in June 2022 for a three-year term. ACCSC is recognized by the U.S. Department of Education.

Before enrolling, get written answers to these three questions:

1. Is CSTU's ACCSC accreditation currently active? Confirm the current status and effective dates directly with ACCSC.
