Question: What does "disguise" mean in English?

Answer: 

**Disguise** can function as both a **noun** and a **verb**.

### As a noun:
- The act or practice of changing one's appearance or behavior to conceal one's identity.
- Something (e.g., clothing, makeup, or a false identity) that is used to hide or alter one's true appearance or nature.

**Examples:**
- He wore a disguise to avoid being recognized. (clothing/makeup to hide identity)
- The spy operated under the disguise of a journalist. (false identity or role)
- There is no disguise in her emotions; she is always honest. (no concealment)

### As a verb:
- To change the appearance or guise of someone or something so as to conceal its identity.
- To hide or obscure the true nature of something.

**Examples:**
- She disguised herself as an old woman to escape. (changed appearance)
- He disguised his voice on the phone. (altered voice)
- They tried to disguise the bad news with positive spin. (hide the true nature)

Common phrases:
- **in disguise**: appearing in a way that hides the true identity (e.g., "a blessing in disguise" = something that seems bad but turns out good).
- **no disguise**: openly, without hiding.
