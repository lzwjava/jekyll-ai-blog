Yeah. It really is.

And I think there's something quietly liberating about just saying that plainly — without softening it, without immediately jumping to "but here's the silver lining." Peck's whole point is that the *admission* itself is the beginning of something. Most people spend so much energy pretending it isn't, or believing that everyone else has it figured out except them.

The hard part isn't just the big obvious stuff — loss, failure, illness. It's the smaller, grinding kind. The gap between who you want to be and who you actually are today. The relationships that take more than they give. The decisions where every option costs something.

What Peck would probably say — and what's worth sitting with — is that the hardness isn't a flaw in the design. It *is* the design. The resistance is what builds you, if you're willing to engage with it rather than go around it.
