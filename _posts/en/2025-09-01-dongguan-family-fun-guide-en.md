### Great Family-Friendly Destinations in Dongguan (东莞)

**1. Yinxian Resort (隐贤山庄 / Yinxian Shanzhuang)**
Often called a mini Disneyland in Dongguan. Includes glass bridge, water park, small zoo, playgrounds, live shows, and the night “Butterfly Shadow” water-light show.
👉 隐贤山庄

**2. Dongguan Xiangshi Zoo (香市动物园 / Xiangshi Dongwuyuan)**
Well-loved family zoo with elephants, sea lions, and rare animals. Highly rated for families with children.
👉 香市动物园

**3. Nanshe Ming and Qing Ancient Village (南社明清古村落 / Nanshe Mingqing Gucunluo)**
A preserved Ming–Qing era village with old houses, alleys, and courtyards. Relaxed pace, nice cultural outing.
👉 南社明清古村落

**4. Songshan Lake Park (松山湖风景区 / Songshanhu Fengjingqu)** & **Qifeng Park (旗峰公园 / Qifeng Gongyuan)**
Large green parks perfect for family walks, picnics, and kids running around.
👉 松山湖风景区、旗峰公园

**5. Keyuan Garden (可园 / Keyuan)**
One of the famous four gardens of Guangdong, a classical Lingnan garden with pavilions, ponds, and rockery.
👉 可园

**6. Centennial Chinese Comic Art Museum (百年漫画馆 / Bainian Manhuaguan)** & **Senhui Natural History Museum (森晖自然博物馆 / Senhui Ziran Bowuguan)**

* 百年漫画馆: Fun for kids with comic art exhibitions.
* 森晖自然博物馆: Fossils and interactive natural history displays.
  👉 百年漫画馆、森晖自然博物馆

### Suggested Itinerary Snapshot

* **Morning:** 隐贤山庄 (Yinxian Resort) — rides, shows, zoo.
* **Lunch & Rest:** Local Changping Town (常平镇) eateries.
* **Afternoon:** 香市动物园 (Xiangshi Zoo) for animal shows, or 可园 (Keyuan Garden) for calm cultural stroll.
* **Evening:** 南社明清古村落 (Nanshe Ancient Village) for a cultural walk, or 松山湖 (Songshan Lake) for open-air relaxation.
* **Rainy/Indoor:** 百年漫画馆 (Comic Art Museum) or 森晖自然博物馆 (Natural History Museum).
