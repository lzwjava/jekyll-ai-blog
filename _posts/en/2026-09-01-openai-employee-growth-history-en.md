**Question:** How many employees does OpenAI have, tracing its growth over recent years?

**Answer:**

OpenAI has undergone one of the most dramatic workforce expansions in tech history. Here's the headcount trajectory:

| Year | Employees |
| ------ | ----------- |
| 2022 | ~2,400 |
| 2023 (Nov) | ~770–3,049 |
| 2024 | ~3,531–4,440 |
| 2025 | ~6,879–7,850 |
| 2026 | ~7,000–7,850+ |

Breaking this down:

- In November 2023, OpenAI had roughly 770 employees. Headcount has since grown close to tenfold.

- By September 2024, OpenAI employed 3,531 individuals, a significant increase from the 770 employees in November 2023.

- By 2025, the workforce reached approximately 6,879 employees, and climbed to around 7,832 in 2026 — a roughly 5% year-over-year increase.

- As of 2026, employee counts range from 4,500 (per the Financial Times) to 7,850 (per Revelio Labs), depending on methodology — the gap reflects how each source counts contractors, subsidiaries, and part-time staff, not a true contradiction.

- OpenAI plans to reach approximately 8,000 employees by end of 2026, and is currently the largest independent AI lab by headcount — roughly 2x Anthropic's size.

In terms of composition, engineering makes up around 56% of staff, and OpenAI pays at the top of the market — the Wall Street Journal described its average compensation package as the richest in startup history.
