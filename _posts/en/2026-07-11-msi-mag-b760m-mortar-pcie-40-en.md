Question: MSI MAG B760M Mortar Motherboard, does it support PCIe 4.0?

Answer:
Yes, the MSI MAG B760M Mortar motherboard fully supports PCIe 4.0 across several slots:

* **M.2 NVMe Storage:** Both of its onboard M.2 slots support PCIe 4.0 x4 speeds (up to 64 Gbps) for ultra-fast solid-state drives.
* **PCIe Expansion Slots:** While the primary top slot (PCI_E1) is upgraded to ultra-fast **PCIe 5.0 x16** for the graphics card, the bottom full-length slot (PCI_E3) operates at **PCIe 4.0 x4**.

Because PCIe technology is backward compatible, you can safely use any PCIe 4.0 graphics card or expansion card in the top PCIe 5.0 slot, and it will run flawlessly at its maximum PCIe 4.0 speeds.
