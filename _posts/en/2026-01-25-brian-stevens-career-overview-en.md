**Question:** Provide a comprehensive introduction to Brian Stevens.

**Answer:**

Brian Stevens is a prominent technology executive known for his leadership in open source software, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence. He has held chief technology officer (CTO) roles at major companies including **Red Hat** and **Google Cloud**, and has significantly influenced enterprise infrastructure, virtualization, Linux, and AI acceleration technologies.

### Early Career
Stevens began his career at **Digital Equipment Corporation (DEC)**, where he spent 14 years as a senior technical staff member. He contributed to the architecture and development of the Tru64 UNIX operating system and clustering products. He was also involved in early work on the X Window System. He earned a bachelor's degree in computer science from the University of New Hampshire and a master's degree in computer systems from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute.

### Red Hat (2001–2014)
In 2001, Stevens joined **Red Hat**, a leading open source solutions provider. He initially served as Vice President of Operating System, Storage, and Clustering Development. He was later promoted to **Chief Technology Officer (CTO)** and Executive Vice President of Worldwide Engineering. During his tenure, he played a key role in Red Hat's enterprise expansion, including advancements in Linux, virtualization, middleware, storage, big data, and early cloud technologies. He helped position Red Hat as a major force in open source enterprise solutions. He stepped down as CTO in 2014.

### Google Cloud (2014–2019)
After leaving Red Hat, Stevens joined **Google** as Vice President and **CTO of Google Cloud**. He led the technical vision for Google's public cloud platform during a period of rapid growth, helping transform it from an emerging player into a major enterprise competitor (eventually reaching significant scale, with run-rate revenue in the tens of billions). His background in open source and Linux aligned well with Google's cloud strategy, including support for containers (Kubernetes) and various Linux distributions.

### Neural Magic and Return to Red Hat (2019–present)
After Google, Stevens became **CEO** of **Neural Magic**, a Boston-based startup focused on software-based AI acceleration. Neural Magic developed techniques for running sparse deep learning models on commodity CPUs at GPU-like performance, reducing dependency on expensive specialized hardware.

In 2025, **Red Hat** acquired Neural Magic, bringing Stevens back to the company. He now serves as **Senior Vice President and Chief Technology Officer (CTO) for AI** at Red Hat. In this role, he drives the company's vision for open, hybrid AI infrastructure, emphasizing open source as the foundation for the AI era. He has been involved in initiatives around AI inference, model optimization, and partnerships (e.g., with Mistral AI and contributions to distributed inference projects).
