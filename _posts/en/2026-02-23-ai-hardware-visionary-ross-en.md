### 🚀 Building Groq: A New Paradigm for AI

In 2016, Ross identified a critical gap: the prevailing computing architectures, like CPUs and GPUs, were not optimized for the rapidly emerging needs of AI . He left Google, reportedly taking **seven of the ten core members of the original TPU team** with him, to found Groq and build a solution from the ground up .

- **Founding a Company**: Groq was established in 2016 with a clear mission: to build hardware literally "designed for inference. Not adapted for it" . Ross's vision was to create a chip that could handle AI workloads with unprecedented speed and efficiency.
- **The Language Processing Unit (LPU)**: Groq's breakthrough innovation is its **Language Processing Unit (LPU)**. Unlike traditional GPUs, the LPU is a novel **Tensor Streaming Processor** architecture specifically designed for the sequential nature of large language models . When Groq demonstrated the LPU in 2024, it stunned the tech world by running LLMs at speeds **10x faster than NVIDIA GPUs** in some benchmarks, generating up to 500 tokens per second .
- **Financial Growth and Challenges**: Under Ross's leadership, Groq has attracted significant investment from top-tier firms like BlackRock, Cisco, and Samsung, achieving a valuation of approximately **$6.9 billion** . However, the path has not been without its hurdles. The LPU's reliance on SRAM memory, while fast, is expensive and limits capacity, requiring massive clusters of chips to run the largest AI models. This has led to questions about its cost-effectiveness compared to established solutions . Ross has had to navigate these technical and market challenges while managing rapid growth and revising revenue forecasts .
