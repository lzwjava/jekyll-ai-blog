Question: What are the Western equivalents of Xianyu (闲鱼), China's second-hand marketplace?

Answer:

The closest Western equivalents are **not just eBay**. Xianyu is a mix of several platforms:

* **eBay** → global second-hand marketplace
* **Facebook Marketplace** → local C2C trading
* **Craigslist** → old-school local classifieds
* **Mercari** → mobile-first casual resale
* **OfferUp** → local used goods
* **Swappa** → used electronics
* **Back Market** → refurbished electronics (more professional)

([TheTradingPost.ai][1])

A rough mapping:

| China     | Western equivalent                   | Difference                                     |
| --------- | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 闲鱼 Xianyu | eBay + Facebook Marketplace combined | Xianyu has stronger "treasure hunting" culture |
| 淘宝二手      | eBay                                 | More shipping, less local                      |
| 闲鱼个人卖家    | Facebook Marketplace                 | Direct person-to-person                        |
| 闲鱼数码区     | eBay + Swappa                        | Electronics focus                              |
| 闲鱼捡漏      | Craigslist / Facebook Marketplace    | Finding undervalued items                      |

([CNCartGo][2])

For **GPU / PC hardware**, the closest Western places are:

### 1. eBay (most similar for GPUs)

Best for:

* RTX 3090
* Tesla cards
* workstation GPUs
* old CPUs
* server hardware

Why:

* worldwide buyers
* auction system
* buyer protection
* lots of used enterprise hardware

Example:

```
Search:
"RTX 3090 used"
"Tesla P100 16GB"
"MI50 accelerator"
"A6000 used"
```

### 2. Facebook Marketplace

More like local Xianyu.

Good for:

* people upgrading PCs
* gaming rigs
* monitors
* desks
* random electronics

Example:

```
Seller:
"Bought RTX 4090, selling RTX 3090"
Buyer:
"Need cheap AI GPU"
```

This is probably closest to the feeling of browsing Xianyu locally. ([Nifty][3])

### 3. Craigslist

Very old but still useful.

The culture is:

> "I have this thing in my garage. Come pick it up."

Very similar to early Xianyu.

Good for:

* servers
* workstation PCs
* old GPUs
* networking equipment

### 4. Mercari

Probably the closest app experience.

People sell:

* phones
* cameras
* clothes
* collectibles
* electronics

More casual than eBay. ([App Vulture][4])

### 5. Swappa

For tech people.

Mostly:

* phones
* laptops
* tablets
* GPUs sometimes

Cleaner than Xianyu because sellers are usually genuine users.

For an **AI engineer hunting cheap GPUs**, I would rank:

```
China:
闲鱼 > 淘宝二手 > 京东拍拍

USA:
eBay
  |
Facebook Marketplace
  |
Craigslist
  |
Reddit hardware communities
  |
ServerPartDeals / refurbished vendors
```

The interesting difference:

**Xianyu is unusually powerful because China has:**

* huge electronics manufacturing ecosystem
* massive PC enthusiast market
* lots of university/company surplus
* lower logistics cost
* dense population

So you can find crazy things:

* RTX 3090 for cheap
* Tesla M60/P100
* engineering samples
* server pulls

The Western market is more fragmented. You often need multiple sites.

For your GPU hunting style (3090, Tesla, MI50, old accelerators), eBay + Facebook Marketplace are probably the closest. ([TheTradingPost.ai][1])
