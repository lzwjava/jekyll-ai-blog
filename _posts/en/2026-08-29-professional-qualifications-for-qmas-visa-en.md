**Question:** What is "Professional Qualification" in the context of the QMAS visa application's Academic/Professional Attainment section?

**Answer:**

According to the QMAS Guidance Notes from Hong Kong's Immigration Department, "Professional Qualification" in this section refers to credentials awarded by recognised professional bodies that demonstrate a high level of technical expertise or skill — distinct from standard academic degrees.

Here is the official definition and scope:

**You should select your degree level or professional qualifications from the pull-down list. Do not include academic or professional attainment that is not at the college or university level.** A professional training or membership can be recognised as being at the college or university level **if its entry requirement is college completion or a bachelor's degree**. If you hold professional qualifications awarded by a **nationally or internationally recognised/acclaimed professional body** which demonstrate that you have a **very high level of technical expertise or skill**, you may also state the qualifications here.

**In practice, this includes things like:**

| Type | Examples |
| ------ | --------- |
| Chartered/licensed professional designations | CPA, CFA, ACCA, PMP, FRM |
| Engineering certifications | PE (Professional Engineer), CEng |
| Legal qualifications | Bar admission, Solicitor qualification |
| Medical/healthcare licences | Medical registration, nursing board certification |
| Architecture/surveying designations | RIBA, RICS |

**Key rules to keep in mind:**

- Only include qualifications **at college/university level or above** — do not list short courses, high school-level training, or basic trade certificates
- A professional **membership** counts only if its entry requirement is a bachelor's degree or higher
- The awarding body must be **nationally or internationally recognised**

So essentially, "Professional Qualification" covers your **formal credentials from recognised professional bodies** (e.g., CPA, CFA, CEng), while "Degree Level" covers your **university academic degrees** (Bachelor's, Master's, PhD). Both go into the same "Academic/Professional Attainment" section of the QMAS application form.
