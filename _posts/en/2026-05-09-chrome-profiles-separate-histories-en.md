Question: In Chrome, do different profiles have separate browser histories?

Answer:

Yes. In Google Chrome, each profile functions as an independent browser environment. This means:

* **Separate browser history**: Each profile keeps its own history of visited websites.
* **Separate bookmarks, cookies, and extensions**: Everything is isolated between profiles.
* **Independent logins**: You can be signed into different Google accounts in each profile without overlap.

So if you switch to another Chrome profile, it will not show the history from your other profiles.
