Question: Do you actually need to remove the DDR4 heat spreader?

Answer: **No.** If the RAM works normally, there is generally **no reason to remove the orange/black heat spreader**.

For normal use:

* Keep it installed.
* It provides some mechanical protection and heat spreading.
* DDR4-2666 at normal voltage doesn't need bare-chip cooling.
* Removing it introduces a small risk of damaging the DRAM packages or PCB.

Only remove it if you specifically want to **inspect the DRAM chip markings, identify the memory ICs, or use the module for repair/learning experiments**.
