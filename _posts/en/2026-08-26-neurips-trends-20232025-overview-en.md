**Question:** What are the hot happenings / trending topics at NeurIPS in recent years?

---

**Answer:**

Here's a rundown of the major trends across NeurIPS 2023, 2024, and 2025:

---

### NeurIPS 2023 (New Orleans)

NeurIPS 2023 featured new developments in Language Modeling, Reinforcement Learning, Machine Learning Optimization, Representation Learning, and Diffusion Models, with 3,584 main papers, 58 workshops, and 14 tutorials.

Key themes:

- **LLM Dominance & Democratization:** The conference showcased efforts to reduce the cost and massive resource requirements for training LLMs, with open-source projects like LLaMA-2 and LLM-360 aiming to democratize access.
- **Transformer Alternatives:** Research explored replacing the transformer's "attention" mechanism with more efficient functions. Accepted papers showcased alternatives such as Hyena and Liquid neural networks.
- **Responsible AI:** The conference highlighted an increased focus on responsible AI, encompassing topics like fairness.

---

### NeurIPS 2024 (Vancouver)

The 38th annual NeurIPS (Dec 10–15) saw over 4,000 papers accepted, along with 56 workshops and 14 tutorials.

Key themes:

- **Best Papers:** Award-winning papers covered a new autoregressive model for vision (next-scale image prediction), higher-order derivative methods for supervised learning, improved LLM training, diffusion-based text-to-image inference, and a diverse benchmark dataset for LLM alignment.
- **AI Verification & Safety:** A prominent EXPO talk focused on how AI-enabled systems are being adopted in safety-critical industries like aerospace and automotive, and the challenges of verifying non-deterministic neural networks.
- **Hardware-Native Neural Nets:** Stanford researcher Felix Petersen introduced neural networks built directly from computer chips' logic gates, using differentiable relaxations for backpropagation — using fewer than one-tenth of the logic gates and one-thousandth of the processing time compared to binary neural networks for image classification.
- **LLM Alignment:** The PRISM dataset, collected from 75 countries, stood out for studying LLM alignment with diverse human feedback and multicultural perspectives, benchmarking over 20 state-of-the-art models.

---

### NeurIPS 2025 (San Diego)

NeurIPS 2025 attracted over 5,200 accepted papers (24.5% of ~21,575 submissions), with major trends including an emphasis on large language and foundation models, reproducibility, data-centric research, and explicit attention to societal impacts of AI.

Key themes:

- **Agentic AI:** Major tech companies showcased compelling demonstrations of agentic AI systems — Google's MOMENTUM agent, Meta's DINO V3 multimodal advances — highlighting a shift toward applied, workflow-centric AI.
- **Efficiency over Scaling:** The conversation shifted from "make models bigger" to "make models more predictable, efficient, and controllable," with new gated attention architectures improving LLM stability.
- **Best Paper Topics:** The 2025 Best Paper Awards covered diffusion model theory, self-supervised reinforcement learning, attention mechanisms for LLMs, reasoning in LLMs, online learning theory, neural scaling laws, and benchmarking LLM diversity — including a paper on LLM output homogenization.
- **New Journal Track:** For the first time, NeurIPS introduced a Journal Track, featuring 34 top papers from JMLR and the Annals of Statistics, covering topics like robust RL and privacy/generalization.

---

**Overall Trend (2023–2025):** The arc is clear — from foundational LLM research and diffusion models → to alignment, safety, and efficiency → to agentic AI and real-world deployment. Each year the conference has grown massively in submissions and scope.
