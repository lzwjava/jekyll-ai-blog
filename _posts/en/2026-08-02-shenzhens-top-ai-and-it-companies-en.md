Question: What are the top AI/IT companies in Shenzhen?

Answer:

**Big tech (foundational infra + models)**
- **Tencent** — WeChat, cloud, Hunyuan LLM family, biggest single employer of AI talent in Shenzhen.
- **Huawei** — Ascend chips, MindSpore framework, Pangu models, HarmonyOS. Core alternative to Nvidia/CUDA stack domestically.
- **DJI** — not "AI company" on paper but heavy in embodied AI (drones, computer vision, flight autonomy).

**AI-specific / robotics**
- **UBTECH** — humanoid robots (Walker series), listed on HKEX, one of the more visible embodied-AI plays out of Shenzhen.
- **IntelliFusion (云天励飞)** — computer vision, edge AI chips, government/smart-city contracts.
- **MINIEYE** — autonomous driving perception stack.
- **HAI ROBOTICS** — warehouse/logistics robotics, well-funded.
- **X Square Robot** — newer robotics startup, raised >1B yuan Series A, raised over one billion yuan in its Series A round.
- **iCarbonX** — bio/health data + AI, less relevant if you're doing LLM/agent work.

**Context worth knowing**
- Shenzhen is not the LLM foundation-model center of China — that's mostly Beijing/Hangzhou (DeepSeek is Hangzhou, Zhipu/Baichuan/Moonshot are Beijing). Shenzhen's strength is hardware + chips + robotics + applied CV, riding on Huawei/Tencent infra and the PRD manufacturing supply chain.
- If you're hiring or partnering for GPU/model-training work specifically, Huawei (Ascend/CANN ecosystem) is the one to know cold since it's the domestic CUDA-alternative you'd hit if any client wants non-Nvidia inference.
