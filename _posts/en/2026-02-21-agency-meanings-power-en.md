The word **"agency"** has several meanings depending on the context:

1. **Organization or Business**:
   An agency is a company or organization that provides a specific service, often on behalf of others. Examples include:
   - **Advertising agency** (e.g., Ogilvy)
   - **Travel agency** (e.g., Expedia)
   - **Government agency** (e.g., NASA, EPA)

2. **Action or Intervention**:
   Agency refers to the capacity or ability of a person or group to act independently and make their own choices. For example:
   - *"She demonstrated agency by starting her own business."*
   - In philosophy or sociology, it often contrasts with "structure," highlighting individual freedom vs. societal constraints.

3. **Legal or Representation**:
   An agency relationship exists when one party (the agent) acts on behalf of another (the principal), such as a lawyer representing a client.
