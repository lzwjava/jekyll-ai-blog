**Question:** How can a Chinese citizen get an India visa to visit India?

**Answer:**

Good news — India resumed issuing tourist visas to Chinese citizens from July 2025, after a ~5-year pause due to border tensions. As of November 2025, India's Ministry of External Affairs confirmed the tourism and business visa regime for Chinese nationals is fully functional.

---

### 🗂️ Visa Types Available for Chinese Citizens

| Type | Purpose | Notes |
| ------ | --------- | ------- |
| **Tourist Visa** | Leisure travel | Standard embassy visa |
| **e-B-4 Business Visa** | Manufacturing/production business | Online application only |
| **Student / Immigration Visa** | Study or residency | Requires Indian mission |

---

### 📋 How to Apply (Tourist Visa)

**Step 1 — Apply at an Indian Mission in China**

The Indian Embassy in Beijing announced that Chinese citizens can apply for tourist visas, with guidelines and documentation available at visa application centres in **Beijing, Shanghai, and Guangzhou**.

**Step 2 — Prepare Your Documents**

Typical requirements:

- Valid Chinese passport (6+ months validity)
- Passport-size photo
- Completed visa application form
- Proof of travel itinerary (flight bookings, hotel)
- Proof of sufficient funds
- Sponsorship/invitation letter (if applicable)

**Step 3 — Submit & Wait**

Processing for e-visa routes can be 1–3 days; standard embassy processing varies.

---

### 💼 Business Travel (e-B-4 Visa)

India rolled out a new **e-B-4 (e-Production Investment Business) Visa** from January 1, 2026, allowing eligible Chinese nationals to apply entirely online without visiting the Indian Embassy. It is aimed at Chinese business professionals for specific industrial and operational activities.

---

### ⚠️ Important Notes

- Some sources note that **e-Visa may not be available** for Chinese citizens due to ongoing military tension between China and India — the main route remains the **embassy visa**.
- Always verify current rules directly with the Indian Embassy before applying, as policies can change.
