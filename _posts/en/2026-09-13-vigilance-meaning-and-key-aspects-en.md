Question: What does "vigilance" mean?

Answer: Vigilance is the state of being watchful, alert, and attentive — especially to potential danger, threats, or problems. It involves maintaining careful, sustained attention over time so that nothing important goes unnoticed.

Key aspects of vigilance include:

- **Alertness** – Being mentally awake and ready to respond quickly
- **Sustained attention** – Keeping focus over an extended period, not just momentarily
- **Awareness** – Noticing changes in one's environment or situation
- **Proactive caution** – Anticipating risks before they become problems

It is commonly used in contexts like security ("stay vigilant for suspicious activity"), health ("vigilance over symptoms"), and everyday safety.

The adjective form is **vigilant**, and the adverb is **vigilantly**.

> *"Eternal vigilance is the price of liberty."* — often attributed to Thomas Jefferson
