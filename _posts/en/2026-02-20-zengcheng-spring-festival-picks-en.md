Question: Where are the best places to visit in Zengcheng, Guangzhou, during this Spring Festival?

Answer: For the 2026 Spring Festival (the Year of the Horse), Zengcheng offers a blend of traditional cultural events and nature-focused leisure. Since the festival is currently in full swing, here are the top spots to visit:

### 1. Zengcheng Spring Flower Market & Zengcheng Square

As is tradition, the main **Zengcheng Flower Market** is a must-visit. It typically runs through the early days of the festival at **Zengcheng Square** and along **Lihu Street**.

* **What to do:** Shop for festive plants like kumquat trees and orchids, enjoy local snacks like **Zhengguo wontons**, and experience the lively "crowd-strolling" culture.

### 2. Baishuizhai Scenic Area

If you want to escape the city crowds, head to **Baishuizhai** to see the famous "White Water Fairy" waterfall.

* **Highlights:** Hike the 9,999-step "Heavenly Ladder" or enjoy the mountain air. During the Spring Festival, the area often features traditional lion dance performances at the entrance.

### 3. Lihu Happy Bay (Ice and Snow Kingdom)

For a more modern family outing, **Lihu Happy Bay** (located near the Zengjiang Gallery) is a popular choice.

* **Highlights:** Ride the **Ferris wheel** for a panoramic view of the district or visit the **Happy Bay Ice and Snow Kingdom** to experience indoor sub-zero activities like ice sculptures and slides—a unique "winter" experience in the southern heat.

### 4. Hot Spring Resorts

Zengcheng is famous for its high-quality hot springs. This is the peak season for a relaxing soak.

* **Top Choices:** **Forest Sea Water Park (Skov River Vodaland)**, **Xiangjiang Health Valley**, or **Sanying Hot Spring Resort**. Many of these resorts host special Lunar New Year gala dinners and small-scale fireworks or lantern displays for guests.

### 5. Dabuwei Village & 1978 Art Village

* **Dabuwei Village:** Known for its "Flower Sea," it is particularly romantic in early spring with fields of Gesang flowers and hydrangeas. It often hosts local "Village Galas" during the festival.
* **1978 Art Village:** A renovated industrial site perfect for "Instagrammable" photos, featuring boutique cafes, art galleries, and often creative New Year markets.

---

> **Tip:** If you are looking for a massive spectacle, the **Bai'etan Fireworks Display** is happening in central Guangzhou (Liwan District), but be prepared for heavy traffic if you travel out of Zengcheng.
