**Question:** What platforms can help me handle social insurance and payroll for my Guangzhou company, and what are the other choices?

---

**Answer:**

Yes, 51社保 is legitimate. Here's the full landscape of options, from DIY to fully outsourced:

---

## Option 1: DIY — Do It Yourself (Free)

Handle everything directly via government portals. Best if you have only 1–3 people and time to learn.

| Tool | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **广东政务服务网** (gdsi.gov.cn) | Register social insurance, add/remove employees |
| **广州住房公积金管理中心** (gzhfgj.gz.gov.cn) | Housing fund registration & monthly payments |
| **广州税务局电子税务局** | Pay social insurance (now collected by tax bureau) |
| **粤省事** (WeChat mini-program) | Check your own contribution records |

Pros: Free. Cons: Steep learning curve, easy to make errors on base amounts or deadlines.

## Option 2: SaaS Platforms — Software to Manage It Yourself

These give you a dashboard to manage payroll + social insurance without manual government portal work.

**51社保 (51Shebao)** — the one you mentioned.
Beijing-based HR tech company providing cloud-based social security, payroll, and benefits management. Serves over 100,000 enterprise customers across more than 100 cities, backed by SoftBank China Venture Capital, SIG China, and Zhaopin. Good for startups wanting a self-serve SaaS tool.

**薪人薪事 (XinRenXinShi)**
An all-in-one HR SaaS + AI platform covering recruitment, onboarding, attendance, scheduling, payroll, performance, and training — aimed at mid-to-large companies wanting full digital HR management with data-driven analytics.

**易路软件 (eRoad Software)**
Founded in 2004, offers a SaaS product called People+ for payroll, social insurance, taxes, and benefits for large enterprises, plus eSalary-eTax for small companies. Clients include LVMH and Levi's. More enterprise-grade.

| Stage | Recommendation |
|---|---|

| 1–5 employees | **51社保** SaaS or **亲亲小保** — low cost, easy to start |
| 5–20 employees | **51社保** or **人力窝** outsourcing — saves real admin time |
| 20+ or multi-city | **人力窝** or **中智** full outsourcing |

---

## ⚠️ One Key Rule Regardless of Platform

The only legally recognized model is **人事代理 (HR agency)**: your company keeps its own social insurance account, the labor relationship and enrollment entity are your company, and the service provider only handles administrative tasks like adding/removing employees, base amount declarations, and filing. No ratio limits, no position restrictions.

Any platform that says they'll enroll your employees under *their* company name — avoid it. That's the illegal "挂靠" model.
