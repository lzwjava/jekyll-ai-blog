**Question:** What is an introduction to IELTS Ready Premium?

**Answer:**

**IELTS Ready Premium** is the official advanced online preparation platform provided by the **British Council** for IELTS test takers. It serves as a comprehensive, personalized preparation toolkit designed to help candidates achieve their target band score.

It is **exclusively available free of charge** (valued at approximately US$170) to anyone who books their IELTS test through the British Council. Access is unlimited from the time of booking until about 7 days after the main test date (Listening/Reading/Writing).

The platform is powered by GEL IELTS Prep and combines official content, adaptive learning features, and modern tools like AI feedback to make preparation more effective and targeted.

### Key Features
- **40 full-length practice tests** covering all four skills (Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking), with automatic scoring for Listening and Reading
- **Detailed feedback and analysis** — especially for Reading and Listening questions; optional paid AI scoring/feedback for Writing and Speaking (sometimes offered free or at low cost like US$9.99 per task)
- **Personalized study plan** that adapts based on your performance and progress
- **Writing practice** with model answers to compare and improve
- **Live and on-demand expert masterclasses** and skills-based video lessons
- **Interactive exercises**, tutorials, and downloadable resources for all sections
- **Mini-mock tests** (occasionally offered free with AI feedback on Writing/Speaking)
- Realistic exam interface, timed/untimed modes, and progress tracking

Many users report that consistent use of the mock tests and targeted practice on weak areas (guided by the platform's analytics) helped them significantly improve their scores, especially in Listening and Reading.

It is widely regarded as one of the most valuable free official resources available when booking with the British Council.
