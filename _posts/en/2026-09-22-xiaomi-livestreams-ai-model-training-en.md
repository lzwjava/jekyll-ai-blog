**Question:** What is the page at `https://mimo.xiaomi.com/rl/#overview`?

**Answer:**

That URL is a **live public dashboard** where Xiaomi's MiMo AI team streams the real-time training metrics of their next-generation models — **MiMo-V2.6-Pro** and **MiMo-V2.6-Flash** — as they are actively being trained.

Here's what it means in detail:

**What MiMo is:** MiMo is a family of large language models (LLMs) developed by Xiaomi, initially released in April 2025. It is a reasoning-focused model used as a key AI component in Xiaomi's "Human × Car × Home" ecosystem.

**What this dashboard is:** Xiaomi's MiMo team is livestreaming the reinforcement-learning (RL) training stage of two unreleased models — MiMo-V2.6-Pro and MiMo-V2.6-Flash — through this public dashboard. The page displays training metrics directly from the trainer's logs, including reward curves, rollout counts, step timing, and running compute costs.

**Why it's notable:** Frontier labs usually only report post-training results after training is complete. Here, anyone can watch it while it is still running.

**What the dashboard shows:** The page displays the model training progress, token consumption, training cost, number of samples, and changes in multiple internal indicators. Both MiMo-V2.6-Pro and MiMo-V2.6-Flash versions are shown. As of mid-September 2026, the total cumulative training cost of both versions had exceeded $1.16 million USD.

**The motivation behind it:** Luo Fuli, Head of Xiaomi MiMo, stated the team had been researching one single question for nearly half a year: to what extent can Reinforcement Learning (RL) be further scaled? She disclosed the team is expanding the scale in three dimensions — computing resources, training environment and task system, and the reward evaluation mechanism.

In short, it's a transparent, live window into Xiaomi's AI model training — something quite unusual in the industry.
