Question: **For QMAS, can work on a Hong Kong project while employed/contracted by a Guangzhou outsourcing company count as “work experience with international exposure”?**

Answer: **Generally, no — not merely because the project/client is in Hong Kong.**

The official QMAS definition is quite literal:

> “work experience outside the applicant’s home country/territory.” ([Immigration Department Hong Kong][1])

So the key distinction is **where you actually performed the work**, not where the client/project is located.

### Your example

Suppose:

* Employer: Guangzhou company
* Employment contract: Guangzhou
* You physically worked in Guangzhou
* Client/project: Hong Kong digital bank
* You developed backend/API/payment systems for the Hong Kong project

I would **not tick the international-exposure box solely on this basis**.

Why? You have **international exposure**, in the ordinary business sense, but QMAS's criterion specifically says the work experience must be **outside your home country/territory**. ([Immigration Department Hong Kong][1])

### But this case is different

If you were:

* employed by the Guangzhou outsourcing company;
* assigned to the Hong Kong project;
* **physically worked in Hong Kong** for a substantial period;
* with evidence such as Hong Kong entry/exit records, assignment letters, accommodation, work records, etc.

then that period has a much stronger basis for being counted as international-exposure experience.

The employment contract being with a Mainland company **doesn't automatically disqualify it**. The important fact is that the actual graduate/specialist-level work was performed outside your home country/territory.

### Think of it this way

| Situation                                                        |                        International exposure? |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------: |
| Guangzhou → Guangzhou, HK client                                 |                                  ❌ Probably no |
| Guangzhou → Guangzhou, HK digital-bank project                   |                                  ❌ Probably no |
| Guangzhou company → physically assigned to HK                    |                                  ✅ Strong case |
| Guangzhou company → travel to HK regularly for project work      | 🟡 Potentially, depending on duration/evidence |
| HK employer → physically working in HK                           |                                          ✅ Yes |
| Guangzhou company → remote work from Guangzhou for US/HK company |        ❌ Not under the literal QMAS definition |

The official guidance is unusually clear: criterion (9) is **not simply “international client experience”**; it is specifically **graduate/specialist-level work experience outside the applicant's home country/territory**. ([Immigration Department Hong Kong][2])

So for your QMAS application, I'd separate these two concepts:

**International project/client exposure** ≠ **QMAS international work experience**

Your Hong Kong digital-banking project can still be useful elsewhere in the application — particularly when demonstrating the **nature and level of your specialist work** and potentially your finance/technology experience — but I wouldn't count it toward the **2-year international-exposure criterion** unless you actually worked outside Mainland China.
