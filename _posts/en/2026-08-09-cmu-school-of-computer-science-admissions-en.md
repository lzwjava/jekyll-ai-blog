## Question: Introduction to CMU School of Computer Science Graduate Admissions

## Answer:

Carnegie Mellon University School of Computer Science (CMU SCS) graduate admissions is the gateway for one of the world's strongest computer science graduate schools. It covers master's and PhD programs across areas such as:

* Artificial Intelligence
* Machine Learning
* Computer Systems
* Programming Languages
* Human-Computer Interaction
* Robotics
* Computer Vision
* Language Technologies
* Software Engineering

CMU SCS has **seven departments/institutes** and offers many professional and research-oriented master's programs. ([CMU School of Computer Science][1])

---

## 1. Major Graduate Programs

Some important CMU AI/CS master's programs:

| Program                                           | Focus                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| MS Computer Science (MSCS)                        | General CS, systems, algorithms, theory, AI |
| MS Machine Learning (MSML)                        | ML research + advanced ML engineering       |
| MS Artificial Intelligence and Innovation (MSAII) | AI + product innovation + entrepreneurship  |
| MS Language Technologies (MSLT)                   | NLP, LLMs, speech                           |
| MS Computer Vision (MSCV)                         | Vision models, perception                   |
| MS Robotics (MSR)                                 | Robotics + AI                               |
| Master of Software Engineering (MSE)              | Large-scale software engineering            |

The MSCS program is particularly famous but extremely competitive. ([CMU Computer Science][2])

# 2. Application Process

CMU SCS uses a centralized graduate application system. Applicants can apply to multiple graduate programs through the SCS application portal. ([CMU School of Computer Science][1])

For Fall 2027 admission:

* Application opens: September 9, 2026
* Early deadline: November 18, 2026
* Final deadline: December 9, 2026

([CMU School of Computer Science][3])

---

# 3. Required Materials

Typical MSCS application requires:

### 1. Transcript

You submit transcripts from **all colleges/universities attended**, even if you did not graduate from them. ([CMU Computer Science][2])

### 2. Resume/CV

CMU likes evidence of:

* Strong engineering ability
* Research experience
* Open-source contribution
* Publications
* Significant projects

### 3. Statement of Purpose (SOP)

Usually 1–2 pages:

* Your technical background
* Research interests
* Why CMU
* Career objectives

CMU explicitly asks applicants to describe research interests and relevant experience. ([CMU Computer Science][2])

### 4. Recommendation Letters

Three letters.

Strong letters usually come from:

* Professors/research advisors
* Technical managers
* Senior engineers who know your work deeply

CMU recommends at least two letters from faculty or recent employers. ([CMU Computer Science][2])

---

# 4. GRE / English Requirements

## GRE

CMU SCS programs vary. For MSCS:

* GRE is not universally mandatory
* It is often recommended, especially if your academic record does not clearly demonstrate mathematical ability

A strong GRE Quant score can help applicants with non-traditional backgrounds. ([CMU Computer Science][2])

## English

Accepted:

* TOEFL
* IELTS
* Duolingo (alternative)

CMU prefers TOEFL or IELTS. ([CMU School of Computer Science][1])

# 5. How Competitive Is CMU?

Very competitive.

Typical successful applicants have:

### Academic background

Usually:

* Strong CS degree
* GPA 3.7+
* Strong mathematics:

  * Linear algebra
  * Probability
  * Algorithms
  * Statistics

### Research profile

For AI-focused programs:

Strong signals:

* ML papers
* Research assistant experience
* Open-source ML contributions
* Model training experience
* Reproducible experiments

### Industry profile

For professional master's:

Strong signals:

* Large-scale systems
* Distributed computing
* Production ML systems
* Startup experience

# 7. Cost

CMU master's programs are expensive.

Typical CMU graduate tuition is around:

* ~$55k–70k USD/year tuition
* Living expenses in Pittsburgh:

  * ~$20k–30k/year

Total:

**~$80k–100k USD for a 1.5–2 year master's**

Most professional master's programs do **not** provide funding. ([CMU Computer Science][2])
