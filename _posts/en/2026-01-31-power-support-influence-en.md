**Patronage** has a few related meanings:

**Main definitions:**

1. **Support or sponsorship** - The backing, encouragement, or financial support given by a patron to an artist, cause, organization, or institution. For example, "The museum relies on the patronage of wealthy donors" or "Renaissance artists depended on royal patronage."

2. **Being a customer** - The act of giving your business to a store, restaurant, or service. For example, "Thank you for your patronage" means "thank you for being our customer."

3. **Political appointments** - The power to make appointments to government jobs or grant favors, often based on political connections rather than merit. This is sometimes called the "patronage system" or "spoils system."

4. **Condescending attitude** - Treating someone in a condescending way, as if they're inferior (though this is more commonly expressed as being "patronizing").

The word comes from the idea of a "patron" - someone who supports or protects others. In historical contexts, wealthy patrons would financially support artists, musicians, or writers, allowing them to create their work.
