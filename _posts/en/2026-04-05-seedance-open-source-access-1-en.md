## Is Seedance Open Source?## Is Seedance Open Source?

**No, Seedance is NOT open source.**

Seedance is a proprietary, closed-source AI model developed by the SEED team at ByteDance (the parent company of TikTok). Unlike Stable Diffusion, which offers model weights for public download, Seedance functions similarly to OpenAI's Sora or Midjourney — the technology is kept private to protect commercial advantages. The model is exclusively available through ByteDance's commercial products and you cannot find a GitHub repository with the inference code, nor run it offline on your local machine.

The "open source" rumors likely stem from confusion with older, less capable research papers released by ByteDance, but the Seedance engine remains strictly behind a corporate firewall.
