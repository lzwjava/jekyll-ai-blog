## Question: Intro to Axonex AI ([https://www.axonex.ai](https://www.axonex.ai))

## Answer:

Axonex Intelligence Limited (Axonex) is a Hong Kong-based AI and robotics company focused on **embodied AI, robotics, IoT, and smart facility management**. The company’s vision is to build intelligent systems that connect **AI software + robots + physical environments** to improve automation, safety, and operational efficiency. ([Axonex][1])

Axonex is a subsidiary of Mint Incorporation Limited, a Hong Kong company listed on NASDAQ (ticker: MIMI). Mint historically operated in interior design and fit-out services, then expanded into AI and robotics through Axonex.

### Core business areas

**1. AI Robotics**

* Autonomous security patrol robots
* Service robots for commercial environments
* Productivity robots for retail, logistics, and industrial workflows
* Companion robots ([Axonex][2])

Examples:

* **NEX** — a semi-humanoid service robot designed for workplace tasks, combining AI vision, robotic manipulation, navigation, and conversational AI.
* **Patrol Series** — autonomous robots for security and facility management, including surveillance, anomaly detection, and environmental monitoring.

**2. AI Intelligence Platform**
Axonex develops software platforms for managing robot fleets and physical environments:

* Robot fleet monitoring
* Remote control
* IoT integration
* Video AI analytics
* Automated reporting
* Digital twin visualization ([Axonex][3])

**3. Smart Facility Management**
Target industries include:

* Shopping malls
* Residential communities
* Government facilities
* Warehouses
* Industrial sites
* Transport hubs ([Axonex][2])

### Technology positioning

Axonex is positioned around the emerging **embodied AI** trend:


Compared with pure software AI companies, Axonex focuses on bringing AI into physical environments — similar to the direction of companies working on humanoid robots, autonomous systems, and smart factories.
