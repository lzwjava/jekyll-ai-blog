**Question:** How many years of social insurance contributions do males or females need in China to get retired?

**Answer:**

China has recently reformed its retirement system, effective **January 1, 2025**. Here's a summary:

---

## ✅ Minimum Contribution Years (养老保险缴费年限)

| Period | Minimum Years Required |
|---|---|
| Before 2030 | **15 years** |
| From 2030 onward (gradual) | Increasing to **20 years** (+6 months per year) |

By January 1, 2030, the required minimum monthly contribution period to receive a basic pension will gradually increase from 15 to 20 years, with an increase of six months each year.

---

## 👨 Male Retirement Age

Over the next 15 years, the statutory retirement age for male employees will gradually increase from **60 to 63 years old**. For male employees born after September 1, 1976, the statutory retirement age will be **63 years old**.

---

## 👩 Female Retirement Age

For female employees, the statutory retirement age will be raised from **50 to 55 years old** for non-management positions, and from **55 to 58 years old** for those in management positions.

---

## 📌 Summary Table

| Gender | Position | Old Age | New Age (by 2039) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Male | All | 60 | **63** |
| Female | Non-management (worker) | 50 | **55** |
| Female | Management/Technical | 55 | **58** |

---

## ⚠️ Special Notes

- Under the PRC Social Insurance Law, individuals who have participated in basic pension insurance can receive a basic pension on a monthly basis if they have accumulated **15 years of contributions** by the time they reach the statutory retirement age.
- Employees who reach the statutory retirement age but have not met the minimum contribution period can still receive their monthly basic pension after fulfilling the required years through **extended contributions or a one-time payment**.
