Question: Does the ASUS B85M-F use DDR3?

Answer: **Yes.** The ASUS B85M-F uses **DDR3 desktop RAM**.

* **RAM:** 2 × DIMM, DDR3
* **Supported speeds:** DDR3-1600 / 1333 MHz
* **Maximum:** 16 GB (2 × 8 GB)
* **CPU:** i3-4170, LGA1150
* **GPU:** PCIe x16 → Tesla P100

So a cheap **8 GB DDR3 1600 MHz** stick is enough to start testing the P100.
