**WLSA Shanghai** is a high-profile international high school in Shanghai, here's the quick breakdown:

**What it is:** WLSA Shanghai Academy is an international school founded in Shanghai in 2012 by the World Leading Schools Association (WLSA), located in Baoshan District. It offers AP courses, A-Level courses, and the WLSA-unique Global Impact Program (GIP), and receives support from 56 top secondary schools worldwide plus universities like Tsinghua and Yale.

**The org behind it:** WLSA was founded in 2011, composed of founding members including Eton College, Groton School, Geelong Grammar School, Harvard-Westlake School, Beijing No. 4 High School, and the High School Affiliated to Fudan University (FDFZ).

**Who runs it:** It's chaired by Tony Little, the former Headmaster of Eton College.

**The curriculum:** WLSA Shanghai Academy offers two curricula — a Sino-American Program (AP courses) and an A-Level Program — alongside the WLSA Global Impact Program (GIP). The GIP focuses on research, critical thinking, ethics, and a capstone thesis defense in Grade 12.

**In short:** It's one of China's more prestigious elite international high schools, targeting Chinese students aiming for top Western universities (think Ivy League, Oxbridge). Very well-connected, expensive, and academically rigorous. Think of it as the "international track" alternative to Beijing No. 4 or Shanghai's top gaokao schools — but aimed at kids going abroad rather than taking the gaokao.
