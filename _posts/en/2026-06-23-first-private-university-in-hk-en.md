**Question:** What is Hong Kong Shue Yan University?

**Answer:**

Hong Kong Shue Yan University (HKSYU, 香港樹仁大學) is a private, self-funded university in Hong Kong — historically significant as the **first private university in Hong Kong** to be granted full university status.**Answer:**

**Hong Kong Shue Yan University (HKSYU / 香港樹仁大學)** — quick factual overview:

**Origins**

Founded in 1971 as Hong Kong Shue Yan College by Dr. Henry Hu (a barrister and Legislative Councillor) and Dr. Chung Chi-yung (formerly faculty head at Hong Kong Baptist College). Dr. Hu invested his personal savings from his legal practice to purchase a three-story house in Happy Valley as the initial campus.

**Why It Stayed Private**

The government offered funding in the late 1970s contingent on following its model for higher education — Shue Yan refused, insisting on four-year programmes. This meant operating without government funding, which also prevented it from formally calling itself a university under the three-year degree requirement then in effect.

**Milestone: First Private University in HK**

On 19 December 2006, Shue Yan College became the first private university in Hong Kong by order of the Chief Executive in Council, and was renamed Hong Kong Shue Yan University. Notably, it was the first time a university in HK was established via Executive Council resolution — all other HK universities were established by Legislative Council ordinance.

**Scale & Academics**

Currently over 4,000 students. Campus is at Braemar Hill, North Point, comprising the Main Academic Building, Library Complex, Student Residential Complex, and Research Complex. 12 departments across Faculties of Arts, Commerce, and Social Sciences — covering Chinese Literature, English, History, Journalism, Accounting, Business, Economics, Law, Psychology, Social Work, Sociology, and Applied Data Science.

**Rankings**

QS World University Rankings 2025: #342 in Asia, #171 in Eastern Asia. Top-ranked private university in Hong Kong for five consecutive years in QS rankings.

**Motto**

「敦仁博物」— "Cultivating virtues of benevolence; broadening horizon and knowledge."

**Positioning in HK's Education Landscape**

HKSYU is distinct from the 8 UGC-funded public universities (HKU, HKUST, CUHK, etc.). It's self-funded, doesn't participate in JUPAS (the centralized admissions system), and leans into liberal arts + traditional Chinese culture as its academic identity. Practically speaking, it's the go-to path for students who miss the UGC cutoffs but want a full four-year Hong Kong degree.
