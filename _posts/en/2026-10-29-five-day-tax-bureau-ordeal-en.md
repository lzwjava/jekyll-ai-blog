**Question:** What does this Chinese passage mean — a person's multi-day ordeal at the tax bureau trying to get an invoice issued?

**Answer:**

The passage describes a grueling **5-day bureaucratic marathon** at a Chinese tax bureau in Haidian District, Beijing, to get a **VAT invoice (增值税发票)** issued on behalf of the company. Here's what happened, day by day:

**Background:**
The person's company had outsourced its accounting, but they chose not to renew the contract this year. With zero financial knowledge, they had to handle everything themselves. The company had missed quarterly tax filings for Q3 and Q4, resulting in a **¥200 fine** — and any outstanding fine blocks the ability to get a tax-bureau-issued invoice.

**Day 1 (Jan 15):** Arrived at the tax bureau, knew nothing, asked around from floor 1 to floor 2, room to room. Located "Director Fan" (范所长) to request the fine be waived. The office closes at 5:30 PM — ran out of time after waiting in multiple queues.

**Day 2 (Jan 16):** Came back to file the missing back-taxes — both VAT (增值税) and income tax (所得税).

**Day 3 (Jan 17):** Returned to find Director Fan again to officially clear the fine. Waited for the system to remove the penalty record.

**Day 4 (Jan 18):** Discovered a new requirement: before getting an invoice issued, the company must sign a **tripartite agreement (三方协议)** with a bank — authorizing the bank to automatically deduct VAT on the company's behalf. Rushed to China Merchants Bank at Wudaokou, signed the agreement. By the time that was done — 5:30 PM again.

**Day 5 (Jan 19):** Went back to Haidian 5th Tax Office, only to find the invoice-issuing machine had been **relocated to the 14th Tax Office**. Went to the 14th office, applied online, filled out forms, got the invoice printed. **Finally done.**

**Key concepts explained:**

| Term | Meaning |

| 三方协议 | Tripartite agreement between company, bank, and tax bureau for auto-deducting taxes |
| 范所长 | Director Fan — the official with authority to lift the fine |
