**Question:** What is 共享发展 (Shared Development) as it appears in the course textbook?

---

**Answer:**

共享发展 is one of the **五大发展理念 (Five Development Concepts/Philosophies)**, which is a major framework in the New Era course. You need to know the whole set first, then zoom into 共享.

---

### First — The Full Five Development Concepts (五大发展理念)

| # | Chinese | English |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | 创新发展 | Innovative Development |
| 2 | 协调发展 | Coordinated Development |
| 3 | 绿色发展 | Green Development |
| 4 | 开放发展 | Open Development |
| 5 | **共享发展** | **Shared Development** |

> Mnemonic: **创协绿开共** — try reading it as a rhythm

---

### What Is 共享发展 Specifically?

The textbook defines it around **4 core ideas**:

---

**① 共享的主体 — Who shares?**
> 全体人民 — ALL the people, not just some

- Not just urban residents — includes rural
- Not just the wealthy — includes low-income groups
- The key word is **全体 (all/entire)** — nobody left behind

---

**② 共享的内容 — What is shared?**
> 共享发展成果 — sharing the **fruits/results** of development

Covers **5 areas**:

| Area | Chinese |
|------|---------|
| Education | 教育 |
| Employment | 就业 |
| Income | 收入 |
| Social security | 社会保障 |
| Healthcare | 医疗卫生 |

---

**③ 共享的方式 — How is it shared?**
> 渐进共享 — **gradual** sharing

- Not overnight equality — realistic and step-by-step
- Based on China's current stage of development
- The textbook explicitly says: 共同富裕是一个**长期**的历史过程

---

**④ 共享的目标 — What is the goal?**
> 共同富裕 — **Common Prosperity**

- This is the ultimate destination of 共享发展
- Directly connects to 中国式现代化本质要求 point #6

---

### 🔗 Key Relationships to Other Concepts

| Concept | Connection to 共享发展 |
|---------|----------------------|
| 共同富裕 | The **goal** of 共享发展 |
| 中国式现代化 | 共享发展 is one of its **supporting pillars** |
| 以人民为中心 | The **philosophical root** — development is FOR the people |
| 全面建成小康社会 | The **historical achievement** that proved 共享 works |

---

### 🧠 How to Understand It Intuitively

Think of the difference between two growth models:

| Western Model | 共享发展 Model |
|--------------|--------------|
| Grow first, distribute later | Grow AND distribute together |
| Trickle-down economics | Institutionalized sharing |
| GDP focus | People's wellbeing focus |
| Some get rich first | All get rich together (gradually) |

> The textbook argues that Western modernization created wealth for the few — 共享发展 is China's answer to that problem.

---

### 📝 Exam Tips

1. **Most tested phrase** — 共享发展就是要使**全体人民**在共建共享发展中有**更多获得感** — memorize this sentence

2. **"获得感"** (sense of gain/fulfillment) is a key Xi Jinping term that almost always appears with 共享发展

3. **Common essay question**: *"How does 共享发展 reflect the principle of 以人民为中心?"*
   - Answer: because it puts people's actual living standards — not just economic numbers — at the center of development goals

4. **Don't confuse** 共享发展 (shared development — a process) with 共同富裕 (common prosperity — the end goal). Examiners love to test this distinction.
