**Question:** Can a Chinese self-study (自考) associate degree (大专, with 13 subjects passed) be used to apply to Illinois Institute of Technology (Illinois Tech) as a transfer student and work toward a US bachelor's degree? What is the process?

---

**Answer:**

**Yes, it is possible** — your 自考大专 certificate is recognized in the US system. Here's the full picture:

**Step 1 — Get your credential evaluated (required)**

WES (World Education Services) does evaluate Chinese self-study programs. If you earned a Zhuanke graduation certificate (专科毕业证书) through the self-study exam system, WES equates it to a US associate's degree. For a course-by-course evaluation, WES requires your examination results instead of a standard academic transcript.

WES is NACES-accredited, which matters because:

Illinois Tech requires you to submit an official transfer credit evaluation, and Illinois Tech accepts any NACES-accredited translation/evaluation.

**Step 2 — Apply as an International Transfer Student at Illinois Tech**

Transfer students are those who have attended another institute of higher education after finishing secondary school. Illinois Tech requires official or certified copies of transcripts from all universities/colleges attended, an official transfer credit evaluation, English proficiency scores, proof of funds, and a passport copy.

For English proficiency: the minimum IELTS score is 6.5 (undergraduate), or TOEFL 80+.

**Step 3 — Transfer credit and remaining coursework**

A maximum of 68 semester hours of transfer credit is accepted from a two-year college. Your final 45 semester hours must be completed at IIT. Transfer credit is accepted for courses completed with a grade equivalent of "C" or better.

This means in practice you'd likely need **about 2 years** of additional study at Illinois Tech to earn the bachelor's degree, depending on which credits transfer and which program you enter.

**Your specific situation summary:**

| Factor | Assessment |
|---|---|
| 自考大专 (13 subjects passed) | Recognized by WES as a US associate's degree ✅ |
| Dropped out of 北京林业大学 | You still need to submit that transcript ✅ |
| 10+ years work experience | Helpful for your application, but IIT focuses primarily on academic credentials for undergrad |
| Credential evaluation needed | WES course-by-course evaluation recommended |
| Time to finish bachelor's at IIT | ~2 years remaining (≈45 credit hours minimum at IIT) |

**Important note:** Course matching matters. IIT will do a formal transfer credit evaluation to determine how many of your subjects apply toward your chosen program. You should contact IIT admissions directly (admission@illinoistech.edu) to ask which of your 13 self-study subjects might be recognized for your intended major.
