**Question:** Where is Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) located?

**Answer:**

HKUST(GZ) is located in **Nansha District, Guangzhou, Guangdong Province, China**. Specifically on Duxue Road in the Qingsheng Hub area of Nansha.

It's a separate campus from the original HKUST in Clear Water Bay, Hong Kong — established in 2022 as a legally independent institution under a unique collaboration framework between Hong Kong and mainland China. Nansha is in the southernmost part of Guangzhou, close to the border with the Greater Bay Area cluster (about 70km from central Guangzhou, and roughly 60km from central Hong Kong by high-speed rail).
