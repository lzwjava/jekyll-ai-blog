**Question:** What universities does Hong Kong have?

**Answer:**

Hong Kong has **8 public universities**, and for the first time, all eight made it to the *Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2026*. Here they are, along with their 2026 global standings:

**🏛️ The 8 Public (UGC-Funded) Universities**

| University | QS 2026 | THE 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| **University of Hong Kong (HKU)** | #11 | #33 |
| **Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)** | #32 | #41 |
| **Hong Kong University of Science & Technology (HKUST)** | #44 | #58 |
| **City University of Hong Kong (CityU)** | #63 | #75 |
| **Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU)** | #54 | #83 |
| **Hong Kong Baptist University (HKBU)** | #244 | 201–250 |
| **Education University of Hong Kong (EdUHK)** | — | Listed for 1st time |
| **Lingnan University** | — | Listed for 1st time |

Hong Kong is the only city in the world with **five universities ranked among the global top 100** in the THE World University Rankings 2026.

**Other Notable Institutions**

- **Hong Kong Metropolitan University (HKMU)** – formerly the Open University of Hong Kong, focuses on flexible and professional education.
- There are around **20 universities in total** in Hong Kong when including private and self-financing institutions.

Although Hong Kong is now governed by China, its universities still reflect much of the educational structure established during its time as a British colony, and most programmes are taught in English.
