Question: Is Za Bank's Visa card virtual, not physical?

Answer:

No, **Za Bank's Visa card (called ZA Card)** is **not only virtual**—it offers **both virtual and physical versions**.

- Upon opening a savings account with Za Bank (a digital/virtual bank in Hong Kong), you automatically get access to a **free virtual ZA Card** displayed in the app. This virtual card is mainly for online transactions, digital wallet payments, and other approved uses.
- You can also apply for a **physical ZA Card** through the app, which will be mailed to your address. The physical version supports additional uses like in-person contactless payments (Visa payWave), ATM cash withdrawals (free at over 3,000 ATMs in Hong Kong and millions worldwide), and point-of-sale transactions.

Key features include a customizable card number (you choose the last 6 digits), enhanced security (no CVV, expiry date, or signature on the physical card), no annual fees, and support for Apple Pay/Google Pay.

In summary, the ZA Card starts as virtual but you can get a physical one too, making it versatile for both online and offline use.
