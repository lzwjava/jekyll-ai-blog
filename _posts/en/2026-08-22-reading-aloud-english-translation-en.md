**Question:** How do you say "朗读" in English?

**Answer:** "朗读" means **read aloud** (or **read out loud**). It refers to the act of reading text aloud, often clearly and expressively.

You can also use **recite** when it involves reading from memory, or **narrate** in a storytelling context.
