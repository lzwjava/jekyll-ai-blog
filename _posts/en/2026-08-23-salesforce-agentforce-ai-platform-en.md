**Question:** What is the Salesforce Agentforce platform?

**Answer:**

## What is Agentforce?

Salesforce Agentforce is an AI platform built directly inside Salesforce. It lets you create AI agents that complete entire tasks on their own — not just answer questions. This is the main difference between Agentforce and older tools like chatbots: a chatbot answers a question and stops there.

Salesforce first introduced Agentforce in 2024. By 2026, it has become the center of the entire Salesforce product line — Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, and Marketing Cloud have all been rebuilt around it. Put another way, Agentforce matters because Salesforce has moved from being a system you log into, to a system that can act on your behalf.

---

## How It Works — Core Architecture

Agentforce is built on the **Atlas Reasoning Engine** for planning and **Data Cloud** for grounding. The **Trust Layer** sits underneath, applying data masking, audit logging, and toxicity and bias detection to every agent action — Salesforce's answer to the governance question every enterprise asks before letting an agent touch production data.

Agents use a **Reason–Act–Observe loop** for human-like decision-making, providing real-time unified customer data context with security, compliance, and data masking built in.

The platform has 4 key layers:

| Layer | Role |
|---|---|
| **Atlas Reasoning Engine** | AI planning & decision-making |
| **Data 360** (formerly Data Cloud) | Unified customer data foundation |
| **Trust Layer** | Governance, audit logs, data masking |
| **Agent Builder** | Low-code/no-code agent creation |

---

## Key Features

The platform includes: a conversational agent builder for creating AI-powered experiences; hybrid reasoning that combines deterministic business logic with AI reasoning; voice capabilities for natural agent interactions; built-in observability for monitoring and troubleshooting; and enterprise-grade security inherited from the Salesforce Platform.

Agentforce 360 (the latest major version) embeds virtual agents across the entire ecosystem — including Slack, which has become the primary workspace for interacting with AI agents and customer data. Users can query customer data or complete Salesforce tasks directly through chat, with enterprise-grade controls for privacy and governance.

## What It's Best For

- **Customer service** — resolving support cases autonomously
- **Sales automation** — lead management, opportunity updates
- **Marketing** — campaign briefs, audience segmentation
- **HR & IT operations** — internal employee-facing workflows

All agents pull from the same source of truth, helping them proactively understand the customer's complete history — incorporating insights from voice conversations, chats, texts, past purchases, and marketing activity into every interaction.
