**Question:** Who is Samir Menon, and what has he co-founded with Neil Movva?

**Answer:**

Samir Menon is a Stanford-trained software engineer and repeat founder who has built two companies alongside Neil Movva.

**Background & Early Connection**

Samir Menon serves as Co-founder and CTO of Sail Research. He comes from Apple, where he worked in security engineering at scale. He and Neil Movva met on the first day of freshman year at Stanford — they took the same classes and saw the same academic counselor.

**Company 1 — Blyss (2022)**

Blyss is a seed-stage company based in San Francisco, founded in 2022 by Samir Menon and Neil Movva. It focuses on encrypted data privacy solutions, enabling applications to process sensitive data without exposing it — even to the platform itself. It raised $500K in funding.

**Company 2 — Sail Research (2026)**

Sail Research is an inference platform purpose-built for long-horizon, asynchronous AI agents. While Neil Movva was considered one of the best engineers at Together AI, Neil himself calls Samir the strongest programmer he met at Stanford.

Sail Research launched from stealth with $80 million in seed and Series A funding at a $450 million valuation, backed by Kleiner Perkins and Sequoia.

Sequoia's Konstantine Buhler described the pair as "two of the most brilliant young AI infrastructure architects in the valley," with big-company experience at Apple, NVIDIA, and Intel, high-growth experience at Together AI, and repeat-founder experience at Blyss.
