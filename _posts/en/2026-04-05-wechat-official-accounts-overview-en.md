## Overview of mp.weixin.qq.com — WeChat Official Accounts Platform

### What is it?

With over 1.3 billion monthly active users, WeChat is one of the most powerful and widely used messaging and lifestyle platforms in China. Developed by Tencent and known as Weixin (微信) in Mainland China, the app plays an essential role in daily life, supporting messaging, social sharing, mobile payments, service bookings, and online shopping.

**`mp.weixin.qq.com`** is the **WeChat Official Accounts Platform** (微信公众平台) — the web-based backend portal where individuals, media, and businesses register and manage their official presence on WeChat. WeChat Official Accounts function as business profiles within the WeChat ecosystem, enabling organizations to establish direct communication channels with Chinese consumers. These accounts act as digital storefronts and communication hubs, allowing businesses to share content, provide customer service, and facilitate transactions within China's dominant social platform.

---

### Account Types Available

WeChat offers four main types of official accounts: subscription accounts, service accounts, enterprise accounts, and mini programs. Each serves a unique purpose, so choosing the right account type is essential for aligning with your business goals and building an effective marketing and customer engagement strategy.

Here is a breakdown of the main three:

**1. Subscription Account (订阅号)**
Subscription accounts allow one push per day, are open to both individuals and enterprises, and are suitable for content creators and self-media. Individuals without business licenses can only choose this type.

**2. Service Account (服务号)**
Service accounts allow only four pushes per month, require enterprise or organization credentials, and are suitable for enterprise services and e-commerce. These two types cannot be converted to each other — what you choose at the start is permanent.

Service accounts provide greater functionality and are the better choice for businesses focused on sales as opposed to content generation. Service accounts can link to a mini program in six different ways.

**3. Mini Programs (小程序)**
Mini Programs empower developers and businesses to revolutionize their services on WeChat with convenient development and an excellent user experience.

### Key Features of the Platform

From the admin dashboard, you can manage content, view analytics, respond to followers, and customise menus and auto-replies.

Service accounts support payment processing with WeChat Pay integration, customer service features including multi-representative support systems, and business integration with external systems, customer databases, and analytics platforms.

Once verified, API features such as auto-replies, message templates, QR code generation, and user management become accessible.

### How to Register (Step-by-Step)

**Step 1 — Visit the platform**
Visit `mp.weixin.qq.com` directly in your browser. Beware of phishing sites — only use this exact official URL. Once on the homepage, click "Register Now" in the top right corner.

**Step 2 — Choose account type**
Select your preferred account type during initial setup. This selection is permanent. You must also establish login credentials using a unique email address.

**Step 3 — Prepare your email**
The email you fill in must never have been bound to any WeChat product, including WeChat, Mini Programs, or Enterprise WeChat. If your commonly-used email is already occupied, register a new one — QQ Mail is a practical option.

**Step 4 — Identity verification**
Identity card information must be authentic and consistent with bank card details linked with WeChat Pay. It is recommended to complete real-name verification for WeChat Pay along with bank card binding beforehand to avoid obstacles during registration.

**Step 5 — For businesses / overseas applicants**
Go to `mp.weixin.qq.com` to begin the process. Near the end, you will need to make a payment via WeChat Pay. After a few days (for Chinese businesses) or a couple of weeks (for foreign businesses), a Tencent staff member will call to confirm whether the account name can be used.

### Important Notes for Overseas / Foreign Users

Accounts opened on the English language platform are not visible to Chinese users. If you are trying to open an account to reach users inside China, you must register through the Chinese platform (微信公众平台) at `mp.weixin.qq.com`.

If you set up under a non-Chinese business entity, you will face several limitations: you can only use a Service Account, not a Subscription Account, and you will not be able to transfer the account to another business at a later time. Also, you will usually not be able to use WeChat Pay to accept payments directly.

Annual verification fees are approximately 300 RMB (roughly $45 USD) for verified status. Additional charges may apply for advanced API integrations and transaction processing fees for WeChat Pay integration.

### Platform Interface Language

The WeChat backend has been available in English since November 2017. If you are located overseas or using your computer in English, the backend at `mp.weixin.qq.com` will automatically display in English.

### Mobile Access (2025 Update)

In 2025, a new feature allows you to register an Official Account directly in WeChat on your phone — no need to open a computer. The path is: Discover → Official Accounts → Upper right avatar → My Official Accounts. However, the mobile interface has relatively simple functions; for formal operation, using a computer is still recommended.
