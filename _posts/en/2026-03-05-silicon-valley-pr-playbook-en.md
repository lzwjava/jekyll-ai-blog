Question: What are some ideas from Lulu Cheng Meservey, the Silicon Valley market and communications expert?

Answer:

Lulu Cheng Meservey is a prominent communications expert in Silicon Valley, known for her work with companies like Substack, Activision Blizzard, Anduril, and others. She is the founder of Rostra (a firm focused on founder-led communications) and often shares insights on modern PR, narrative building, and direct audience engagement. Her key ideas challenge traditional PR and emphasize authenticity, directness, and strategic storytelling in a social media-driven world.

Here are some of her notable ideas and principles:

- **Go Direct**: Traditional PR and media gatekeepers are outdated or "dead." Founders and leaders should communicate directly to audiences via platforms like X (Twitter), podcasts, and personal channels to control their narrative and build authentic connections. This approach helps bypass filtered media and fosters trust.

- **Build a Reality Distortion Field**: Great leaders create compelling visions that inspire and rally people around ambitious ideas. This involves standing out, finding your tribe, using powerful contrasts or "foils," and taking calculated reputational risks to shift perceptions.

- **Convincing People to Care**: The core skill for founders is persuasion—convincing employees to join, investors to fund, partners to collaborate, and customers to engage. Everything starts with clearly communicating why your idea matters more than the competition in a crowded market.

- **Find Overlaps in Audience Concerns**: To communicate effectively, align your message with what your target audience already cares about deeply (e.g., geopolitical fears for defense tech audiences) rather than just pushing product features. This creates resonance and avoids "marketing drill" that feels disconnected.

- **Embrace Controversy and Boldness**: Great ideas and people often start as controversial or mocked. If you're not offending anyone, you're likely not innovating enough. Silicon Valley trends (like defense tech) prove that shifting from taboo to mainstream is possible through strong narratives.

- **Direct Engagement Over Intermediaries**: Tech journalism is increasingly disconnected from Silicon Valley realities. Founders going direct accelerates this, making personal platforms more powerful for shaping opinions and reaching key stakeholders.

- **Aspire to 10x Impact**: Push for ambitious goals beyond small improvements (e.g., 5% efficiency gains). Aim to create products or services so exceptional that people would happily pay 10x more, driving true differentiation.

- **Crisis Communication Lessons (e.g., from SVB)**: In crises, prioritize reaching customers through their actual channels (social media, chats, forums) rather than formal releases. A communication failure can accelerate or cause collapse even more than financial issues.

Her newsletter "Flack" and podcast appearances expand on these tactical and strategic ideas for winning attention without being "cringe" or wasting time.
