Question: What is the content of the provided link from Guangdong University of Foreign Studies?

Answer: This webpage is an official announcement from the Self-Study Examination Office of the School of Higher Continuing Education at Guangdong University of Foreign Studies (GDUFS). Published on May 14, 2026, it notifies students about the release and review process for the **April 2026 Self-Study Examination Practical Assessment Results**.

Here are the key details outlined in the notice:

* **Result Availability:** The practical assessment results were released on May 15, 2026, via the Qida Management System. Students are advised to check them promptly, as the data will be cleared before the next registration window. Passing grades will be synced to the Guangdong Provincial Self-Study Examination Management System by mid-June 2026 (prior to graduation applications), while failing grades will not be uploaded.
* **How to Query:** Students can log in using their examination permit number and password at the designated portal: `http://service.qidaedu.com/practiceLogin.jsp`.
* **Score Re-evaluation (Appeals):**
* **Application Window:** May 15–18, 2026 (based on the postmark date if mailed).
* **Process:** Students must download the application form attached to the announcement, fill it out, and mail it to the GDUFS Self-Study Examination Office.
* **Scope:** The review only checks for objective clerical or addition errors (e.g., miscalculations or missed grading sections). It does not re-evaluate the subjective strictness of the grading or regrade the paper.
* **Feedback:** The office will conduct reviews on May 20, 2026. Only students whose scores are found to contain errors will receive an email notification with corrected results; if the score is correct, no response will be sent.
