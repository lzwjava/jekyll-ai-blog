**Question:** What CPUs does the ASUS Z97-K R2.0 motherboard support?

**Answer:**

The ASUS Z97-K R2.0 uses the **LGA1150 socket** with the Intel Z97 chipset, supporting the following CPUs:**Question:** What CPUs does the ASUS Z97-K R2.0 support?

**Answer:**

According to the ASUS official CPU support list for the Z97-K, the board supports the following processor families via the LGA1150 socket:

---

### 🟢 Intel Core (4th Gen — Haswell)

- **Core i7:** i7-4765T, i7-4770, i7-4770K, i7-4770S, i7-4770T, i7-4771, i7-4785T, i7-4790, i7-4790K, i7-4790S, i7-4790T
- **Core i5:** (full series, same generation)
- **Core i3:** i3-4130, i3-4130T, i3-4150, and others


---

### 🟢 Intel Core (5th Gen — Broadwell, needs BIOS update)

- **Core i5-5675C** (3.1GHz, 65W) — requires BIOS 2604 or later, and VGA driver 10.18.14.4206 or later
- **Core i7-5775C** (3.3GHz, 65W) — same BIOS requirement


---

### 🟢 Intel Pentium (Haswell)

- G3220, G3220T, G3240, G3240T, G3250, G3258, G3260, G3420, G3430, G3450, G3460, G3460T, G3470


---

### 🟢 Intel Celeron (Haswell)

- G1820, G1820T, G1830, G1840, G1840T, G1850


---

### 🟡 Intel Xeon E3 V3 (works with caveats)

- Xeon E3-1220 V3, E3-1220L V3, E3-1231 V3 — supported, but "some features may not work when installed on 8-series chipsets," as Xeon processors are designed for server platforms
