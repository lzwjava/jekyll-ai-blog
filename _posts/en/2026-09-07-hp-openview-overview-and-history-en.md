**Question:** What is HP OpenView, and what is it used for?

**Answer:**

HP OpenView was a family of enterprise IT management software developed by Hewlett-Packard. Here's an overview:

**What It Is**

HP OpenView is the former name for a Hewlett-Packard product family consisting of network and systems management products. In 2007, it was rebranded as HP BTO (Business Technology Optimization) Software when it became part of the HP Software Division. HP Software was eventually sold to Micro Focus.

**Core Purpose**

HP OpenView software provided large-scale system and network management of an organization's IT infrastructure. It included optional modules from HP as well as third-party management software, which connected within a common framework and communicated with one another.

**Origins & History**

HP OpenView was first released in 1987, initially intended to handle HP's own networking hardware. Subsequent updates added support for a broad variety of network devices from other manufacturers. Over time, it evolved into a group of software packages offering network administration, performance monitoring, and capacity planning.

**Supported Technologies**

HP OpenView supports servers, routers, switches, and storage devices, along with multiple operating systems including Windows, Unix, and Linux. Network protocols supported include SNMP, TCP/IP, and HTTP.

**Core Product — Network Node Manager (NNM)**

The foundational OpenView product was Network Node Manager (NNM), network monitoring software based on SNMP. NNM was used to manage networks and could be used in conjunction with other management software such as CiscoWorks.

**Key Modules / Products in the Suite**

The suite included many components, among the most notable:

- **HP Operations Manager (OM)** — a flexible monitoring software that helps system administrators detect and solve problems in networks, systems, and applications. It collects data from managed nodes and generates messages about events, then initiates automatic or manual actions to address problems.
- **HP OpenView Performance Manager (OVPM)** — for monitoring and reporting on system performance.
- **HP OpenView Service Desk** — for IT service management workflows.
- **HP OpenView Storage Area Manager** — providing comprehensive, centralized management across distributed, heterogeneous storage networks.
- **HP OpenView Dashboard** — a web portal for HP OpenView management products, formerly known as Service Information Portal (SIP).

**Later Evolution**

HP OpenView and the company's Business Service Management (BSM) software were combined in 2011 to create HP Business Service Management. The portfolio was eventually acquired by Micro Focus, where many of the products continue to exist today.
