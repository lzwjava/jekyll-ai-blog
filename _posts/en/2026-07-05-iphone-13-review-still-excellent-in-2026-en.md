**Question:** How good is the iPhone 13?

**Answer:**

The **Apple iPhone 13** is still a **very good smartphone in 2026**, especially if you can buy it at a reasonable price (new old stock or refurbished). It is no longer a flagship, but it remains fast, reliable, and receives modern iOS updates.

### Pros

**1. Very fast performance**

* Uses the **A15 Bionic** chip.
* Apps open instantly.
* Smooth multitasking.
* Easily handles:

  * ChatGPT
  * Web browsing
  * YouTube
  * WeChat
  * Coding apps
  * Most games

Even after several years, the A15 is still faster than many mid-range Android phones. ([TechRadar][1])

---

**2. Excellent cameras**

* 12 MP wide camera
* 12 MP ultrawide camera

Strengths:

* Great daylight photos
* Excellent HDR
* Very natural colors
* Outstanding video recording (one of Apple's biggest strengths)
* Strong low-light performance

If you mainly take photos of family, travel, or food, it's still an excellent camera. ([TechRadar][1])

---

**3. Great battery life**

Compared with the iPhone 12:

* noticeably longer battery life
* can usually last a full day with normal use

---

**4. Premium build quality**

* Aluminum frame
* Glass back
* IP68 water resistance
* Ceramic Shield front

It still feels like a premium phone.

---

### Cons

**1. Only a 60 Hz display**

This is the biggest drawback.

Many Android phones around the same price now offer:

* 90 Hz
* 120 Hz

Scrolling on the iPhone 13 isn't as smooth as on newer Pro iPhones or many Android phones.

---

**2. No telephoto camera**

You only get:

* Wide
* Ultrawide

No optical zoom.

---

**3. Charging is relatively slow**

* 20 W wired charging
* 15 W MagSafe

Many Android phones now support 67 W, 80 W, or even over 100 W charging.

---

### Software support

Apple typically provides software updates for many years.

The iPhone 13 continues to receive recent iOS releases, making it a safe choice if you plan to keep it for several more years. ([PhoneArena][2])

---

### Who should buy it?

It's a great choice if you want:

* a dependable iPhone
* excellent cameras
* long software support
* fast everyday performance
* good battery life

---

### Who should skip it?

Consider a newer model if you want:

* 120 Hz display
* better zoom camera
* Apple Intelligence features available only on newer iPhones
* significantly longer future software support

---

### Is it good for AI apps?

Yes.

It runs:

* ChatGPT
* Claude
* Gemini
* Perplexity
* most modern AI apps

very smoothly. Since these apps mostly run models in the cloud, the A15 processor is more than powerful enough for an excellent experience.

### Final verdict

If you can find an **iPhone 13** for around **US$300–450 (used or refurbished)**, it's one of the best-value iPhones available. If you're buying new at a much higher price, it's usually worth comparing it with a newer iPhone, as the additional cost may bring better longevity and newer features. ([Tom's Guide][3])
