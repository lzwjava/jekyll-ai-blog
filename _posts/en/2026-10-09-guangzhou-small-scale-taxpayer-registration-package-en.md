### 📋 Small-Scale Taxpayer (小规模纳税人) — Key Facts

- Annual VAT-taxable revenue **under ¥5 million**
- Issues **普通发票** (ordinary invoices); can apply for 增值税专用发票 under certain conditions
- A new company is considered a VAT small-scale taxpayer upon incorporation by default. You can later upgrade to General Taxpayer (一般纳税人) if turnover exceeds the threshold
- VAT rate is currently **3%** (often reduced to 1% under preferential policies)

---

### 📁 Required Documents for Registration

You need to prepare:

1. **Company name** (preferably 3–5 candidates in case of conflicts)
2. **Registered capital amount** (no minimum; can be subscribed, not paid-up)
3. **Legal representative & shareholder ID** (front + back of Chinese national ID)
4. **Business scope** (main activities your company will conduct)

---

### ⏱️ Registration Timeline

- Business license: **~3 working days** after submission
- Guangzhou's "Starting a Business" platform integrates services for business license applications, first-time invoice applications, tax management equipment distribution, and other business-starting affairs in one submission
- After license approval, **bank account opening** is coordinated separately (typically 1–2 weeks depending on the bank)

---

### 🏢 Address Hosting Note

The **¥1,500/year address** is a virtual registered address (地址托管). This is legitimate for registration purposes in Guangzhou but:

- Is **not** a physical operating office
- Some industries (e.g., food, medical) may require an actual physical address
- Confirm the address is in a commercially-zoned property (商业用途), as residential addresses are not accepted

---

### ✅ What You Get After Registration

1. **营业执照** — Business License
2. **5 official company seals** — Company seal (公章), financial seal (财章), legal rep personal seal (法人章), contract seal (合同章), invoice seal (发票章)
3. **Tax registration** (automatic via "one-stop" system)
4. **Bank account opening appointment** (you still attend in person, usually)

---

### ⚠️ Things to Clarify with the Service Provider

- Is the address in Guangzhou's main urban districts (天河、越秀 etc.) or outer districts? This can affect credibility with banks/clients
- What happens if you need to issue **VAT special invoices** (增值税专用发票)?
- Are there extra fees if your monthly transaction volume is high?
- Does ¥2,468 cover **individual income tax** filings if you have employees?

---

This is a competitive package for **micro/small businesses, sole operators, or e-commerce startups** in Guangzhou looking for low administrative overhead. If your annual revenue is expected to stay under ¥5 million and you don't need to claim input VAT credits, the small-scale status is typically sufficient.
