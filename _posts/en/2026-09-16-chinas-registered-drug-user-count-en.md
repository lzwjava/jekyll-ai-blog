Question: **How many people in China use drugs?**

Answer: China is harder to compare directly with the U.S., because China primarily reports **registered drug users**, not a nationwide survey estimating everyone who used an illicit drug in the past year.

* **2024:** about **747,000 registered drug users** in China, according to Chinese authorities. That's about **0.054% of the population**. ([China State Council Information Office][1])
* **2023:** **896,000 registered users**. ([Chinese Embassy in the USA][2])
* In 2024, China also reported **88,000 cases involving narcotic/psychotropic drug abuse**, including newer substances such as etomidate. ([China State Council Information Office][1])

**Important:** 747,000 is **not equivalent to the U.S. figure of ~69 million past-year users**. China's number is a *registered-user* measure, so it likely doesn't capture all recreational/occasional illicit drug use. UNODC also notes that prevalence estimates are often unavailable for China. ([Data Portal UNODC][3])

So if your question is **"US vs China, how many people actually consume illicit drugs?"**, there isn't a clean apples-to-apples number. The U.S. has much better population-survey data for this.
