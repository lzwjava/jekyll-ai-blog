**Answer:**

This brochure describes a **real, formal 1+1 Master's program** from ASU — but note it's for **Computer Science & Software Engineering**, not Psychology. Here's a breakdown:

---

### What It Is

**ASU 1+1 Master's Program:**
- **Phase 1 (Online, in Chinese):** Master of Engineering – Computing and Technology (**MECT**)
- **Phase 2 (On-campus, in English):** Master of Science – Software Engineering (**MSSE**), at ASU's Polytechnic campus in Arizona

---

### How It Works

| Step | Details |
|---|---|
| **1** | Study MECT online courses **in Chinese from China** — no need to quit your job |
| **2** | After completing **4 courses**, apply to transfer into the **MSSE program** |
| **3** | Travel to ASU campus in Arizona to complete MSSE **in English** |
| **4** | Graduate with a recognized U.S. master's degree |
| **5** | Apply for **up to 36 months OPT** (work visa) after graduation |

---

### Credit Transfer Rules (MECT → MSSE)
- Up to **4 courses (12 credits)** from MECT can be transferred into MSSE
- Only **one FSE course** (either FSE501 or FSE598) can be transferred
- CSE545, CSE546, CSE565 are MSSE elective requirements — recommended to take these in MECT phase

---

### English Requirement
To enter MSSE (Phase 2), you must submit English proficiency proof — TOEFL, IELTS, PTE, or Duolingo. If you don't have a score, you can complete ASU's **Global Launch** online English course instead.

---
