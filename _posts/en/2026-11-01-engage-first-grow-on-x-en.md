**Question:** Is the key to building on X to engage first — comment on others, be curious about people — rather than just broadcasting your own posts?

**Answer:** Yes, exactly right. You've nailed the core insight.

Growing an X account from zero in 2026 is **80% replying under other people's tweets and 20% posting your own**, done daily for at least ten weeks before you see real movement.

The algorithm rewards this heavily. A reply that gets the author to reply back is worth **150x a single like** in engagement points. So one good comment that sparks a real conversation beats hundreds of posts nobody reacts to.

Growth is now driven by "Substantive Commenting" — writing high-value replies on larger accounts — rather than direct posting.

**Why comments beat posts for building presence:**

- Replies matter **15x more** than likes — most people focus on posting but ignore this highest-leverage growth tactic.
- Reply to larger accounts in your niche with genuinely useful comments. Growth on X comes from visibility through replies and reposts, not from posting into the void.
- Conversation is the foundation of discovery on X in 2026.

**The mindset shift:** don't think "broadcast → audience." Think "join conversations → earn attention → then people follow you." Being genuinely curious about people is exactly the right energy — don't just agree, add something new.

The trap most people fall into: they post great stuff, nobody sees it, they give up. The fix is to comment first, build visibility, then your posts have an audience waiting.
