Good question. Here's what applies to your situation:

**You likely don't need to pay** because:
- Your Lenovo Xiaoxin came with Windows 10 pre-installed, which means it already has a license tied to your device
- When you reinstall Windows 10 on the same hardware, that original license should still be valid
- Windows 10 licenses are typically hardware-locked to the device they came with

**How it works:**
- When you reinstall Windows 10 using installation media, it will automatically recognize and activate using your existing license
- You won't need to purchase a new license key

**Important notes:**
- Make sure you're reinstalling the same version of Windows 10 that came with your laptop (Home, Pro, etc.)
- Keep your laptop connected to the internet during installation so Windows can activate automatically
- If activation fails, you can contact Microsoft support with proof that you own the device

**Potential issue:**
- If your license was somehow lost or your system doesn't recognize it, you might need to purchase a new one (around $120 USD)
- But this is unlikely if you're reinstalling on the same device

So in most cases, **no additional payment needed**—you're simply restoring the Windows 10 that already came with your computer. Just make sure to use the proper installation media and keep your internet connection active during setup.
