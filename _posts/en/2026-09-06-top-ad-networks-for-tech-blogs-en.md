**Question:** For a tech blog covering AI/LLM, backend engineering, DevOps, and GPU hardware — what ad networks are recommended?

**Answer:**

Your blog profile is actually quite strong from an advertiser perspective. Tech/AI/developer content attracts high-value advertisers (SaaS tools, cloud platforms, dev tools), meaning you can get significantly better CPMs than a general lifestyle blog. Here's what I'd recommend, matched to your specific content:

---

**Tier 1 — Best Fit for Your Niche (Developer-Specific Networks)**

These are purpose-built for exactly the audience you have — engineers, AI practitioners, and developers:

**EthicalAds** — Privacy-first, no tracking, contextual ads only. EthicalAds gives publishers a 70% revenue share with a $50 minimum payout paid monthly, and in January 2026 distributed $124,300 to publishers. Traffic requirement is approximately 50,000 monthly pageviews, though they review applications manually — the bigger gate is audience fit: your site needs to serve a technical audience. Perfect for an AI/backend blog. No popups, no intrusive formats — just clean, relevant ads from dev tool companies.

**Carbon Ads (by BuySellAds)** — Carbon is a native advertising network owned by BuySellAds that makes it easy for advertisers to reach technical audiences like developers, designers, and tech-savvy consumers. Their publisher network includes Dribbble, Bootstrap, Laravel, Font Awesome, and many more. It's invite-only and curated, so harder to get in, but very clean and brand-safe.

---

**Tier 2 — Good General Options That Work Well for Tech**

**Google AdSense / Google Ad Manager** — Still the most reliable starting point. Tech blogs benefit from networks that serve software and SaaS advertisers, and Google's contextual targeting does pick up on AI/LLM/DevOps keywords well, often delivering above-average CPMs for technical content. Best if you're just getting started and want zero friction.

**Media.net** — Powered by Yahoo/Bing Network, it specializes in contextual advertising that actually makes sense, and works especially well for finance, technology, and business content with high-intent keywords — which matches your AI/infrastructure topics closely.

**Ezoic** — Ezoic offers AI-powered ad optimization that can boost your RPM without hurting the user experience, and their advertiser network is more diversified across different geographical locations and demographics — useful if your blog traffic isn't mainly from the US. Since your profile suggests a Chinese/HK/global reader base, this matters.

---

**Tier 3 — Skip for Your Use Case**

**Monetag** — Not ideal here. Your readers are developers and engineers who are tech-savvy and use ad blockers. Networks that rely on behavioral tracking face ad-blocker rates north of 50% among technical users. Monetag's popunders and interstitials would likely annoy your audience and damage trust for minimal gain.

---

**My Recommended Path for You**

Given your traffic is likely still growing (personal notes blog), here's a practical sequence:

| Stage | Traffic | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| Now | Any | Google AdSense — easiest approval |
| Growing | 10K+ pageviews | Add Media.net alongside AdSense |
| Established | 50K+ pageviews | Apply to EthicalAds (best fit for your niche) |
| Premium | Invite only | Carbon Ads via BuySellAds |

EthicalAds is the long-term target — it's privacy-respecting, non-intrusive, and the advertisers are exactly what your readers would actually care about (AI tools, dev tools, cloud services).
