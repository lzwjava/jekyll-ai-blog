Question: **For your profile, what salary should you ask for in China’s major cities?**

Answer: **I would quote ¥45k–55k/month base, with ¥50k/month as your anchor.** I would not lead with ¥30k–35k anymore.

I checked your current resume. The strongest parts are not simply “12 years Java experience”; they are the combination of **long engineering experience + banking/enterprise production experience + startup/founder experience + unusually strong AI/LLM self-directed work**. Your resume shows the HSBC AI/full-stack role, GPT-2/nanoGPT training, H200/MI300X/4070 experience, agents, RAG, PyTorch/ROCm/FlashAttention, and substantial open-source work. ([Zhiwei Li][1])

### My target numbers

| Target      | Monthly base | Annual base | What I'd do                          |
| ----------- | -----------: | ----------: | ------------------------------------ |
| **Floor**   |         ¥35k |       ¥420k | Only if role is unusually valuable   |
| **Good**    |      ¥40–45k |   ¥480–540k | Very reasonable                      |
| **Target**  |     **¥50k** |   **¥600k** | **My recommendation**                |
| **Strong**  |      ¥55–60k |   ¥660–720k | Ask when role fits AI/LLM/infra well |
| **Stretch** |        ¥70k+ |      ¥840k+ | Top AI company / exceptional role    |

Current market data supports this range. Levels.fyi currently puts **Senior Software Engineer in Shenzhen at ~¥605k total compensation median**, with the 75th percentile around ¥751k. ([Levels.fyi][2]) Robert Half's 2026 China data puts the median Algorithm Engineer at ¥562k and Architect at ¥900k. ([Robert Half][3])

So **¥600k base/TC territory isn't crazy for your experience**, although you should distinguish yourself from a conventional senior Java engineer.

### By city

I'd roughly use:

* **Beijing:** ¥45–60k/month
* **Shanghai:** ¥45–60k
* **Shenzhen:** **¥45–60k**
* **Hangzhou:** ¥40–55k
* Guangzhou: ¥35–50k
* Other cities: ¥30–45k

For a genuinely good **LLM/AI infrastructure/agent engineering** position, I'd push toward the upper end.

Interestingly, one 2026 salary dataset puts senior AI engineers at roughly ¥708k/year in Beijing, ¥533k in Shanghai and ¥542k in Shenzhen, although I would treat those figures as directional rather than precise because AI titles are extremely heterogeneous. ([9cv9 Career Blog][4])

### The important distinction for you

I would **not** market yourself primarily as:

> Senior Java / Full Stack Engineer, 12 years

That puts you into a much larger and more commoditized pool.

I'd position you closer to:

> **Senior AI Engineer / LLM Engineer / AI Infrastructure Engineer**
> 12 years software engineering + production enterprise systems + hands-on LLM training/inference/agents

Your recent work gives you a particularly unusual story:

```text
12 yrs software engineering
        │

        ├── banking production systems
        ├── startup + consulting
        │
```

That combination is much more valuable than another person who has merely taken an LLM course.

### But there is one big constraint

Your **lack of a bachelor's degree is probably the biggest salary ceiling**, not your engineering ability.

Your resume explicitly says you dropped out of Beijing Forestry University and are pursuing an associate degree through self-study. ([Zhiwei Li][1])

For startups, small AI companies, open-source-oriented teams and founder-led companies, this may barely matter.

For large Chinese companies, especially HR-filtered positions, it can be a hard filter:

```text
Your actual engineering ability
        ↓
potentially ¥500k–800k+ market value

        BUT

学历筛选
        ↓
some positions reject before technical interview
```

So I wouldn't solve this by lowering your expected salary. I'd solve it by **targeting companies where technical evidence dominates credential screening**.

### What I would actually say to a recruiter

If they ask:

> “你的期望薪资是多少？”

I'd say:

> **“如果是 AI/LLM/AI Infra 方向，我目前期望月薪大概 50K 左右，具体也可以结合岗位职责、技术方向和整体 package 谈。”**

That is much better than saying:

> “50K–60K，不能低于50K。”

The first gives you a **¥50k anchor without unnecessarily closing negotiation**.

And if the job is conventional Java/Spring enterprise development, I'd lower the target rather than pretending it has the same market value.

**My personal recommendation for you:**

```text
Expected: 50K/month
Negotiation range: 45K–60K
Acceptable floor: ~40K
Target annual package: 600K–750K
Exceptional AI role: 800K+
```

I would especially target **Shenzhen / Shanghai / Beijing / Hangzhou AI startups, model companies, AI infra companies and technically strong software companies**, rather than ordinary outsourcing positions.
