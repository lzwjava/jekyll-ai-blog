**Question:** You have a 120 sqm house in Guangzhou, Zengcheng. Can you (1) put a double bed in a room to host 2–3 student/fresh-graduate employees from Guangdong universities, and (2) run an AI agent/software consultancy business from there?

**Answer:**

**Short answer: Yes to both, with conditions.** Here's the breakdown:

---

### 1. Registering an AI/Software Consultancy at a Residential Address

Guangzhou has a specific government policy — originally issued as 穗府办规〔2021〕2号, updated as 穗府办规〔2024〕4号 — that explicitly allows certain businesses to register at a **residential address**. Businesses in categories including consulting/planning (咨询策划), e-commerce, software and information technology services, industrial design, and agency consulting are permitted to use residential premises as their registered business address, provided they are pollution-free and pose no safety hazards. An AI agent/software consultancy fits squarely in this allowed category.

**To do it legally:**
- You need a "场地使用证明" (premises use certificate) from the local community/government body with neighbor consent
- You cannot run catering, manufacturing, entertainment, or anything involving hazardous materials from the same address
- The registered address must have legal ownership or usage rights — your own house deed works fine for this

---

### 2. Placing a Double Bed / Letting People Live There

There is **no law in China prohibiting** you from letting employees or interns live in your home. Your 120 sqm house in Zengcheng is private property; what you do inside (hosting, sharing rooms) is your business. This is an informal employer-provided accommodation arrangement, not a commercial dormitory. Just ensure basic fire safety and livable conditions.

Practically, 120 sqm with 3 people working + sleeping + an owner is tight but workable if you dedicate one room as a shared bedroom.

---

### 3. Hiring Students vs. Fresh Graduates — Key Legal Difference

This matters a lot in China:

| | **Current students (interns)** | **Fresh graduates (已毕业)** |
|---|---|---|
| Labor law applies? | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |
| Need labor contract? | No (need a tripartite agreement with their school) | Yes — within 1 month of start |
| Social insurance required? | No | Yes (五险一金) |
| Minimum wage applies? | In practice, yes in Guangdong | Strictly yes |
| Min. wage in Guangzhou | — | **¥2,680/month** (from Sept 1, 2026) |

Once a student intern graduates, the relationship is considered an employment relationship covered by the Labor Contract Law. The employer must then comply with social insurance and minimum wage requirements, and if no written contract is signed within one month, the employee may claim double salary for each unpaid month.

As of September 1, 2026, Guangzhou's minimum wage (first-tier) was raised to ¥2,680/month; the part-time hourly minimum is ¥25.4/hour.

**Practical suggestion:** Hiring current students as interns is simpler and cheaper in the short term. If they're already graduated, budget for their salary + ~30–40% on top for social insurance contributions, and sign a proper contract immediately.

---

### 4. Steps to Set This Up

1. **Register your company** — in 2026 Guangzhou, the registration process is fully digitized via the "一网通办" platform — you can complete name reservation, document submission, e-signing, and business license issuance entirely online. Include "信息技术咨询服务" and "软件信息技术服务" in your business scope. (If you're a foreign national, you need a WFOE — more complex, ~2 months.)
2. **Obtain premises certificate** from your local 居委会/街道办 for the residential-address business registration
3. **Register for tax** — consulting/software services are subject to 6% VAT (small businesses may qualify for simplified 3% rate)
4. **Sign contracts and enroll social insurance** for any graduated employees from day one

---

**Bottom line:** This is a perfectly viable setup for a lean AI consultancy. The residential address is legally usable for this type of business in Guangzhou; housing people there is a private matter with no legal barrier; hiring students as interns is low-friction, but fresh grads need proper employment contracts and social insurance. Consult a local 工商代理 (business registration agent) to handle the paperwork — it's typically a few hundred RMB and much faster than doing it yourself.
