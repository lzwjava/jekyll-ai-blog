Question: What are the video outputs of the NVIDIA Quadro 4000?

Answer: If you mean the **NVIDIA Quadro 4000 (Fermi, 2010)**, it has:

* **4× DisplayPort 1.1**
* **1× DVI-I dual-link**
* Supports up to **5 displays** simultaneously
* Maximum digital resolution: roughly **2560×1600 per DisplayPort/DVI** depending on output/configuration
* It is **not HDMI-native**; DisplayPort → HDMI adapters can be used.

If you mean the newer **Quadro RTX 4000**, that's a completely different card: **3× DisplayPort 1.4 + 1× VirtualLink USB-C**.
