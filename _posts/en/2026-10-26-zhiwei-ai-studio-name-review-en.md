**Question:** Is "智维 AI 工作室" a good name for an AI studio?

**Answer:**

Breaking it down first:

| Part | Pinyin | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 智 | Zhì | Intelligence, wisdom |
| 维 | Wéi | Maintain, uphold; also "dimension" |
| AI | AI | Artificial Intelligence |
| 工作室 | Gōngzuò Shì | Studio / Workshop |

---

### ✅ What Works Well

- **"智维"** is a smart pairing — reads as *"intelligent maintenance"* or *"wisdom in every dimension"*; the dual meaning of 维 (uphold + dimension) adds depth
- **Mixing Chinese + AI** is on-trend — used by top names like `MiniMax`, `Moonshot AI`, `零一万物`; feels modern and internationally legible
- **工作室** signals a boutique, creative, human-centered operation — great for freelancers, small teams, or creative AI agencies; less corporate than 科技 or 集团
- The name is **short, clean, and easy to say**: Zhì Wéi AI — rhythmically satisfying

---

### ⚠️ Things to Consider

| Concern | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Scale perception** | 工作室 ("studio") feels small and indie — excellent for a boutique/agency, but may limit perceived scale if you plan to grow into a larger company |
| **"智维" availability** | 智维 is a relatively common character combo — worth checking trademark/domain availability |
| **AI in the middle feels slightly awkward** | 智维 · AI · 工作室 has three separate "chunks" — could feel a bit segmented visually |
| **No clear vertical** | The name is general-purpose; doesn't hint at *what kind* of AI (creative? enterprise? dev tools?) |

---

### 🔧 Possible Tweaks

| Variant | Feel |
| --- | --- |
| **智维AI工作室** (no spaces) | Tighter, cleaner as a single unit |
| **智维Studio** | More international, drops the formality |
| **智维创作室** | 创作 = creative work; better if you're a creative AI studio |
| **智维实验室** | 实验室 = lab; feels more R&D / techy |
| **ZhiWei AI Studio** | Full English romanization — clean for international branding |

---

### 🏆 Verdict

> **Solid name — especially for a small, boutique AI studio or agency.**
> The "智维" core is strong, the AI tag is clear, and 工作室 gives it a creative, approachable personality. If you're building a lean, quality-focused AI team rather than a large corporation, this name fits well.

**Score: 8 / 10** — clean, meaningful, modern. Just verify the trademark is clear before committing.
