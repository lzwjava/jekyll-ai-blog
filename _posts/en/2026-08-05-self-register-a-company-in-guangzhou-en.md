Of course. **Registering a company entirely by yourself is completely feasible today.** The official process in Guangzhou has become highly digitalized and convenient.

You can use Guangzhou’s “One-Stop Enterprise Startup Platform” to complete the entire process online. In the fastest cases, the registration can be completed within **0.5 to 1 working day**.

## 💡 Important Reminders

* **The registered address is the key issue:** Make sure the address is real and valid. Some agencies provide “virtual addresses,” but some may not comply with regulations. If inspected and found invalid, the company may be listed as having abnormal operations, which can affect its reputation.
* **File taxes on time:** After registration, even if the company has no revenue, you still need to submit tax declarations on schedule (including zero declarations). Annual business reports must also be submitted on time. Otherwise, the company may face penalties or be placed on an abnormal operations list.

If your documents are complete and you understand the process, registering a company yourself is generally straightforward. If you encounter specific issues during the process, you can call Guangzhou’s **12345 government service hotline** and ask for the business environment service line.
