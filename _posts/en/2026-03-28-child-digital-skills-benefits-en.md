For a nearly 5-year-old, what your daughter is doing is actually **ahead of average** for this age group:

## 📺 About 小马宝莉 (My Little Pony) — Is It Good Content?

Absolutely. Research backs this up:

Academic analysis finds that *My Little Pony* improves three key mental health outcomes: social-emotional skill development, emotion regulation, and self-awareness.

The show's friendship themes can instruct children on building interpersonal relationships, its teamwork plots illustrate the power of unity, and its self-discovery arcs support psychological and behavioral development.

It is also worth noting that the Chinese Mandarin dub (小马宝莉：友谊的魔力) was aired on CCTV-14 and is available on major Chinese streaming platforms including iQiyi, Youku, and Bilibili — so it is fully accessible in China without any VPN.

## ⚠️ One Small Caution

Researchers do note a limitation: real-world social complexity and emotional problems are oversimplified in the show, which may lead to children developing somewhat unrealistic self-awareness. Educators are encouraged to supplement animated content with real-world examples.

This is minor and easy to address — just talk with her about what she watches!
