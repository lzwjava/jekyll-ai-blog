**Question:** What ports are on the NVIDIA Quadro 410 card, particularly near the RoHS compliance label?

**Answer:**

The NVIDIA Quadro 410 has **two display output ports** on its bracket:

1. **1× Dual-Link DVI-I** — supports resolutions up to 2560×1600 @ 60 Hz
2. **1× DisplayPort 1.2** — supports resolutions up to 3840×2160 @ 60 Hz

According to the official PNY datasheet, the display connectors are: **Dual Link DVI-I (×1)** and **DisplayPort 1.2 (×1)**.

The card also connects to the motherboard via a **PCI Express 2.0 ×16** slot, and requires **no additional power connector** — so there is no PCIe power plug on the card itself.

The **RoHS label** is typically printed on the back of the PCB or on the bracket sticker. The ports (DVI-I and DisplayPort) sit right on the I/O bracket, which is where you'd find the RoHS / compliance markings nearby.
