This intensive 7-day review plan will help you systematically prepare for your Guangdong Self-Study Examination in Switching and Routing Technology.

## Daily Study Schedule (3-4 hours per day)

### **Day 1: Network Fundamentals & OSI Model**
- **Morning (2 hours):** Review OSI seven-layer model and TCP/IP protocol suite, focusing on data encapsulation and the role of each layer
- **Afternoon (1.5 hours):** Study network topologies, transmission media, and basic network devices (hubs, switches, routers)
- **Evening (30 min):** Practice questions on protocol layers and their functions

### **Day 2: Switching Technology Basics**
- **Morning (2 hours):** Master Ethernet fundamentals, MAC addresses, collision domains, and broadcast domains
- **Afternoon (1.5 hours):** Study switch operation principles including MAC address learning, forwarding, and filtering
- **Evening (30 min):** Review VLAN concepts and benefits; practice conceptual questions

### **Day 3: Advanced Switching**
- **Morning (2 hours):** Deep dive into VLAN configuration, trunk ports, and inter-VLAN routing
- **Afternoon (1 hour):** Study Spanning Tree Protocol (STP) - purpose, operation, and port states
- **Evening (1 hour):** Review switch security features and practice VLAN configuration scenarios

### **Day 4: Routing Fundamentals**
- **Morning (2 hours):** Study routing principles, routing tables, and packet forwarding decisions
- **Afternoon (1.5 hours):** Learn static routing configuration and administrative distance concepts
- **Evening (30 min):** Practice calculating network addresses and subnet masks

### **Day 5: Dynamic Routing Protocols**
- **Morning (2 hours):** Study distance-vector protocols (RIP) and link-state protocols (OSPF) - algorithms, metrics, and convergence
- **Afternoon (1.5 hours):** Review routing protocol configuration and troubleshooting basics
- **Evening (30 min):** Compare routing protocols and practice protocol selection scenarios

### **Day 6: Advanced Topics & Integration**
- **Morning (1.5 hours):** Review NAT/PAT concepts and ACL (Access Control Lists) fundamentals
- **Afternoon (1.5 hours):** Study network design principles combining switching and routing
- **Evening (1 hour):** Work through comprehensive configuration scenarios integrating VLANs, routing, and security

### **Day 7: Review & Practice Exam**
- **Morning (2 hours):** Take a full-length practice exam under timed conditions
- **Afternoon (1.5 hours):** Review weak areas identified from practice exam, focusing on command syntax and concepts you struggled with
- **Evening (30 min):** Create a quick-reference sheet with key commands, protocols, and formulas for final review

## Study Tips

**Focus Areas:** Pay special attention to practical configuration commands, protocol operation mechanisms, and troubleshooting methodologies, as these are commonly tested.

**Active Learning:** Don't just read - draw network diagrams, create comparison tables for protocols, and work through configuration examples on paper.

**Practice Questions:** Complete at least 20-30 practice questions daily, reviewing explanations for both correct and incorrect answers.

**Memorization Aids:** Create flashcards for port numbers, protocol characteristics, command syntax, and administrative distances.

Good luck with your exam preparation! Stay focused, take regular breaks, and ensure you understand concepts rather than just memorizing facts.
