**Question:** How does China implement 高水平科技自立自强 (High-Level Self-Reliance and Self-Improvement in Science and Technology) according to the course textbook?

---**Question:** How does China implement 高水平科技自立自强 (High-Level Self-Reliance and Self-Strength in Science & Technology)?

---

**Answer:**

This topic falls under the **创新发展** chapter of the textbook and is one of the most important exam topics. Xi Jinping established "四个面向" as the strategic orientation and mapped out the strategic path toward achieving high-level science and technology self-reliance, making the landmark statement that "关键核心技术是要不来、买不来、讨不来的" (critical core technologies cannot be begged, bought, or borrowed).

---

### First — Why Is It Needed? (为什么)

Chinese-style modernization must rely on scientific and technological modernization as its support, and achieving high-quality development requires science and technology innovation to cultivate new momentum. High-level science and technology self-reliance is an original and landmark concept in Xi Jinping's economic thought.

In simple terms: without controlling your own technology, you can be "strangled" (卡脖子) by foreign powers at any time — especially in chips, AI, and advanced manufacturing.

---

### The Core Framework — 4 Strategic Orientations (四个面向)

Xi Jinping proposed "坚持四个面向" — constantly advancing into the breadth and depth of science and technology:

| # | Chinese | Meaning |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | 面向世界科技前沿 | Toward the world's scientific frontiers |
| 2 | 面向经济主战场 | Toward the main economic battlefield |
| 3 | 面向国家重大需求 | Toward major national needs |
| 4 | 面向人民生命健康 | Toward people's life and health |

> **Mnemonic:** 世经国民 (World → Economy → Nation → People)

---

### How to Implement It — 5 Key Paths (实现路径)

---

**① 加强基础研究 — Strengthen Basic Research**

Basic research is the source of the entire scientific system and the "general headquarters" of all technological problems — it must be strengthened with greater force and more practical measures.

This means increasing the continuity of funding for basic research, encouraging long-term research, and strengthening incentives for innovative and original basic research projects, especially disruptive ones.

---

**② 攻克关键核心技术 — Win the Battle for Core Technologies**

Self-reliance in science and technology — on top of independent innovation — must further improve the quality of scientific and technological innovation, not only to solve "stranglehold" technical problems, but also to open up new tracks in frontier technology development and cultivate new growth momentum.

In key areas such as new-generation information technology, high-end manufacturing, biotechnology, new energy, and new materials, China continues to invest in order to break through international technology blockades and achieve autonomous control of core technologies — with major breakthroughs already achieved in 5G, AI, quantum communication, semiconductors, and biopharmaceuticals.

---

**③ 健全新型举国体制 — Build the New Whole-Nation System**

The new whole-nation system (新型举国体制) is a key measure for responding to the profound transformation of scientific research paradigms, and a unique advantage for pooling national strategic scientific and technological forces and social resources to tackle major scientific and technological challenges. It brings together national laboratories, national research institutions, high-level research universities, and leading technology enterprises to implement major science and technology projects of national significance, gradually building a tiered, collaborative, and moderately competitive science and technology innovation system.

---

**④ 一体推进教育科技人才 — Integrate Education, Technology & Talent**

Science and technology innovation relies on talent, and talent cultivation relies on education. It is necessary to integrate the innovation chain, industrial chain, and talent chain; to advance education development, scientific and technological innovation, and talent cultivation as one; and to achieve a virtuous cycle and mutual integration of education, science and technology, and talent.

The textbook's key formula is:

```
科技是第一生产力
人才是第一资源
创新是第一动力
```

---

**⑤ 推动原创性颠覆性创新 — Drive Original & Disruptive Innovation**

Original and disruptive scientific and technological innovation is the key for China to achieve a "overtaking on the curve" in the technology field. Through the new whole-nation system, China is actively exploring in areas such as quantum science, gene editing, new energy vehicles, and robotics — encouraging enterprises and research institutions to jointly participate, accelerating the commercialization of scientific achievements, and cultivating emerging industries with global influence.

### 📝 Exam Tips

1. **Must memorize** the Four Orientations (四个面向) in exact order — very commonly blanked out in exams

2. **Key famous quote** to use in essays:
   > 关键核心技术是**要不来、买不来、讨不来**的

3. **Most tested distinction** — 科技自立 vs 科技自强:
   - 自立 = standing on your own feet (not dependent)
   - 自强 = becoming stronger (leading, not just catching up)
   - **高水平** = not just surviving, but leading globally

4. **Common essay angle**: *"Why can't China just buy advanced technology from abroad?"* — Answer using the 卡脖子 logic and the famous quote above
