Question: **Is using many WeChat accounts + content distribution (tech/GPU/LLM/AI) + video + finding AI agent/LLM consulting projects a good revenue strategy?**

Answer:

The core strategy is reasonable: **build distribution first, then convert attention into consulting revenue**. For an AI consultant, trust and reputation are usually the bottleneck, not coding ability.

The important part is not "10–20 accounts" itself. The important part is building a **repeatable content → trust → conversation → paid project funnel**.

A possible model:

```
Technical work
      |
      v
Public content (WeChat Moments, articles, videos)
      |
      v
People recognize "this guy knows AI infra/LLM"
      |
      v
Private conversations
      |
      v
Small paid diagnosis ($500-$2000)
      |
      v
Implementation project ($5k-$50k+)
```

For your background, the positioning is stronger if you avoid being another "AI news account". You have actual hands-on signals:

* trained GPT-2 760M on MI300X
* LLM inference optimization
* agents/CLI tools
* enterprise experience
* GPU repair/lab experiments
* software engineering background

That combination is rare.

## The risk: content without a business pipeline

Many people spend years posting AI information and get:

* lots of likes
* no customers
* no revenue

Because the audience does not know:

> "What problem can I hire this person to solve?"

Your content should repeatedly answer:

### Example positioning

Bad:

> "Today OpenAI released a new model."

Good:

> "A manufacturing company has 5000 PDF manuals. Here is how I built a RAG agent that answers technicians' questions locally with 8GB VRAM."

The second creates buying intent.

## WeChat Moments is actually valuable

For consulting, Moments has a unique advantage:

It is closer to a professional network than a public platform.

A customer may watch you for 3 months before asking:

> "Can you help us build this?"

So post:

* what you built today
* screenshots of experiments
* benchmarks
* failures
* customer-like demos

Example:

```
Today:
RTX 4070 12GB running Qwen model.

Experiment:
vLLM vs llama.cpp

Result:
X tokens/sec difference.

Interesting because:
small companies can avoid expensive GPU servers.
```

This creates credibility.
