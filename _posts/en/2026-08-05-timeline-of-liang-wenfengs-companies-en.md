Question: When did Liang Wenfeng register his companies? Did he return to Chengdu first, spend years there, and only later start Yakebi?

Answer:

The timeline is roughly:

```
2002-2010
  Zhejiang University
  - Studied electronic information engineering
  - Did master's degree
  - Started exploring machine learning / quantitative trading ideas

~2010-2013
  Returned to Chengdu after graduation
  - Tried several AI-related entrepreneurial ideas
  - Those attempts did not succeed
  - Later shifted focus toward finance + AI
```

The "came back to Chengdu and spent some years before registering a company" story is generally how his early biography is described, but the exact Chengdu period is not very well documented publicly. ([Wikipedia][1])

Then:

```
2013-09-10
  Founded:
  杭州雅克比投资管理有限公司 (Hangzhou Yakebi Investment Management Co., Ltd.)

  Founder:
  梁文锋 + 徐进 (Zhejiang University alumnus)

  Business:
  quantitative investment / AI-assisted trading
```

The company registration date was **September 10, 2013**. ([Zhipin][2])

Then:

```
2015
  Founded:
  杭州幻方科技有限公司 / 幻方量化 (High-Flyer related)

  Direction:
  quantitative hedge fund
  machine learning + trading
```

([Volcengine Developer Community][3])

Later:

```
2023-07-17
  Founded:
  杭州深度求索人工智能基础技术研究有限公司 (DeepSeek)

  Direction:
  foundation models / AGI research
```

([Wikipedia][4])

So your understanding is mostly correct:

**Liang Wenfeng did not immediately graduate → register AI company.**

The path was more like:

```
Zhejiang University
        |
        v
Back to Chengdu
(experimenting with AI ideas, failed attempts)
        |
        v
Quant trading + AI
        |
        v
2013 Yakebi
        |
        v
2015 Huanfang / High-Flyer
        |
        v
2023 DeepSeek
```

The interesting part is that DeepSeek was not his first startup. By the time he entered LLMs, he already had about **10 years of company-building experience**, a profitable quantitative trading business, and large-scale computing experience. ([Business Insider Japan][5])

This is also why DeepSeek's origin story is different from many AI startups: it was closer to **"a quant research lab evolving into an AI research lab"** rather than a normal software startup. ([Wikipedia][4])
