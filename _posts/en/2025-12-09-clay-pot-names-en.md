Question: What is the English name for "wa guan" (the small earthenware or clay pot used for steaming food)?

Answer:

"Wa guan" (瓦罐) refers to a traditional Chinese **clay pot** or **earthenware pot**, often called a **sand pot**, **claypot**, or **sandy pot** in English.

The specific small-sized version commonly used for individual portions of soup, stew, or steamed rice dishes (especially in Cantonese or Guangdong cuisine) is most accurately translated as:

- **Mini clay pot**
- **Small sand pot**
- **Earthenware steaming pot**
- **Chinese clay stewing pot**

In restaurants and on English menus, you will most often see it listed as **“clay pot”** or **“mini clay pot”** (e.g., “Clay Pot Rice” = 煲仔饭, using a very similar small wa guan).

Sometimes it is also called a **“Chinese sandpot”** or **“sandy pot”** because traditional ones are made from a special sandy clay from regions like Yixing or Guangdong that can withstand direct flame and retain heat very well.

So in short:
**Wa guan = (small) Chinese clay pot / sand pot**
