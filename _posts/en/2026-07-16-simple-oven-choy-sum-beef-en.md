Question: How to make a very simple oven version: just put choy sum and sliced beef together and cook?

Answer:

Yes, you can make it very simple. The easiest method:

### Simple oven choy sum + beef

**Ingredients**

* Choy sum
* Thin sliced beef
* Cooking oil
* Salt
* Soy sauce (optional)

### Steps

1. Preheat oven:

```
220°C (430°F)
```

2. Put choy sum on a baking tray.

3. Put sliced beef on top.

4. Add:

```
1–2 spoons oil
a little salt
a little soy sauce (optional)
```

5. Mix everything with your hands/chopsticks.

6. Bake:

```
10–12 minutes
```

7. Take out, mix once, check beef is fully cooked.

That's it.

For better results without extra work:

* Put **beef under the choy sum leaves** → beef gets more moisture.
* Cut thick choy sum stems smaller; leave leaves bigger.
* Do not overcrowd the tray; otherwise it will steam instead of roast.

A lazy "one-tray meal" version:

```
tray
 ├── choy sum stems
 ├── beef slices
 └── choy sum leaves

220°C
10 min
```

Serve with rice. It is basically an oven version of a quick Chinese stir-fry.
