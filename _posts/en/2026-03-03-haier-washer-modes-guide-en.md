Question: How to choose the right mode on a Haier washer with options like 新衣, 混合, 丝绸, 摇篮柔洗, 定时, etc.?

Answer:

Haier washing machines (especially models popular in China and Asia) offer specialized cycles tailored to different fabric types and needs. Here's a breakdown of the modes you mentioned, based on common Haier washer programs and standard Chinese labeling:

- **新衣 (New Clothes)**: This is a "New Garment" or "First Wash" cycle. Use it for brand-new clothes to remove excess dye, manufacturing residues, chemicals, or starch. It usually uses more water, a gentle rinse, and sometimes lower temperature to prevent color bleeding onto other items. Ideal for the very first wash of dark or brightly colored new items.

- **混合 (Mixed / Mixed Load)**: This is the everyday "Mixed" or "Cotton + Synthetic" cycle — the most versatile and commonly used one. It suits blended fabrics, cotton-poly mixes, everyday clothes like t-shirts, jeans, towels, bedsheets, etc. It balances cleaning power, water usage, and gentleness. Choose this when you don't have a full load of one specific fabric type.

- **丝绸 (Silk)**: The delicate "Silk" cycle. Designed for silk, satin, lingerie, thin delicate fabrics, or items labeled "hand wash" or "dry clean only" (if machine-washable). It uses very low agitation (slow drum movement), low spin speed, cooler water, and short wash time to prevent damage, stretching, or snagging.

- **摇篮柔洗 (Cradle Gentle Wash / Baby / Ultra Gentle)**: This is a very gentle "Cradle" or "Soft Cradle Wash" mode (摇篮 means cradle, implying rocking/gentle motion). It mimics a soft rocking action with minimal mechanical stress. Perfect for wool, cashmere, baby clothes, knitted items, underwear, swimwear, or anything extremely delicate. It often includes extra rinse for better detergent removal and uses very slow spin to avoid wrinkles or deformation.

- **定时 (Timer / Delay Start / Timed Wash)**: This is not a wash program itself but a **delay timer** function (定时 = timer/delay). You select your desired wash cycle first, then press the 定时 button to set a delay (e.g., 2h, 4h, 8h, up to 24h usually). The machine starts automatically after the set time — useful if you want washing to finish when you get home or to take advantage of off-peak electricity rates.

### Quick Guide: How to Choose the Right Mode
1. **Check the care label** on each garment (the small tag inside). It shows recommended washing method (e.g., hand wash, gentle, normal, water temp).
2. **Sort clothes** by fabric type, color, and dirt level:
   - All everyday cotton/blends → **混合** (safest default choice)
   - New colorful clothes → **新衣**
   - Silk, lingerie, thin synthetics → **丝绸**
   - Wool, cashmere, baby clothes, very delicate knits → **摇篮柔洗**
   - Other common modes you might see (not listed but frequent on Haier):
     - Cotton/强力 (Strong/Cotton) — heavily soiled cottons
     - 羊毛 (Wool) — sometimes separate from 摇篮
     - 快洗/速洗 (Quick Wash) — lightly soiled, small loads in 15–30 min
     - 筒清洁 (Drum Clean) — self-clean the machine
3. **General rule**:
   - If unsure → use **混合** (it's designed for mixed everyday laundry and won't damage most clothes).
   - When in doubt about delicacy → choose the gentler cycle (丝绸 or 摇篮柔洗) rather than risk damage.
4. Add the **定时** delay after selecting your wash program if needed.

Always separate colors (to avoid bleeding), don't overload, and use the right amount of HE/low-foam detergent. For best results, refer to your specific model's user manual (often available on Haier’s website by entering your model number).
