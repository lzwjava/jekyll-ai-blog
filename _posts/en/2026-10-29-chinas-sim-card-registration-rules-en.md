**Question:** How can you register multiple SIM (telephone) cards in China, and what are the rules?

---

**Answer:**

China has strict **real-name registration (实名制)** rules for all SIM cards, enforced by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT). Here's what you need to know:

### 📋 Legal Requirements

**For Chinese Citizens:**
- Telecom giants China Telecom, China Mobile, China Unicom, and China Railcom are required to register all users by verifying accounts against state-issued identification cards (身份证, shenfenzheng).
- Since December 1, 2019, mobile users purchasing SIM cards in China must also undergo a mandatory facial recognition procedure, per MIIT policy.
- Chinese citizens are generally limited to **5 SIM cards total** per national ID across all operators.

**For Foreigners:**
- Foreigners can bring their passports or Foreign Permanent Resident ID Cards to service branches of China Telecom, China Mobile, and China Unicom to purchase a SIM card and activate mobile communication services.
- Any individual with a valid passport can apply for a maximum of **five SIM cards**, each with a different phone number.
- Since 2024, foreigners no longer need a residence permit or Chinese ID to get a SIM card — the store will scan your passport and take a photo for real-name registration, which is required by Chinese law.

### 🏪 Where to Register

- The store will record the customer's ID number and name — for foreigners, showing a passport suffices — entering that information into a company database along with the new SIM card's serial number. Only then will the SIM card be activated.
- You must go to **official carrier stores** only: China Mobile (中国移动), China Unicom (中国联通), or China Telecom (中国电信).
- Street newsstands in China, where cell phone accounts were once conveniently sold, are prohibited from selling SIM cards.

### ⚠️ Important Limits & Warnings

| Category | SIM Card Limit |
|---|---|
| Chinese citizen (per person) | ~5 cards total (across all carriers) |
| Foreigner (per passport) | Up to 5 cards |
| Corporate/Business account | Higher limits available |

- Registering cards under someone else's identity or beyond the legal limit is **illegal** in China and can result in service termination and legal penalties.
- China Mobile, China Unicom, and China Telecom have implemented AI-driven identity verification systems for all new accounts.

### 📱 Steps to Register Multiple Cards Legally

1. Visit an **official carrier store** in person (not a third-party shop).
2. Bring your **national ID card** (Chinese citizens) or **passport** (foreigners) — original document required.
3. Complete **facial recognition scan** at the store.
4. Choose your plan and number for each card.
5. Repeat the process for each additional card, staying within the legal limit.
