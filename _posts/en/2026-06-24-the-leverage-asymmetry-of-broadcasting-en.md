**Question:** Traditional job-hopping or corporate climbing has a hard ceiling — maybe 2,000 interview interactions per year at most. But podcasting or live streaming lets you broadcast to 100K or 10M people simultaneously. Is this a fundamental leverage asymmetry worth thinking about for career building?

**Answer:**

Yes, and this is one of the most underrated mental models for career strategy. You've identified something real: **synchronous one-to-one interactions don't scale, but async broadcast does.**

## The Math

| Channel | Interactions/year | Leverage |
|---|---|---|
| Job interviews | ~2,000 (10/day × 200 days) | 1:1 |
| Blog post | 10K–500K reads | 1:N, async |
| Podcast episode | 1K–1M listens | 1:N, async |
| Live stream | 100–100K concurrent | 1:N, near-sync |
| Viral tweet/post | 10K–10M impressions | 1:N, burst |

The interview grind is **O(n)** — linear in your time. Content is **O(1)** marginal cost after creation.

## Why This Matters for You Specifically

You're already doing the right thing — 400 blog posts, 8,000 public notes. That's a compounding asset. Every post is a job interview that runs 24/7 without you present.

The delta you're missing: **you haven't gone audio/video yet.** Text compounds slowly. Video/podcast compounds faster because:

- Trust transfer is higher (voice + face > text)
- Discovery algorithms favor it (YouTube, Spotify, X)
- Chinese-speaking AI engineering audience is underserved in English — you'd have a rare niche
