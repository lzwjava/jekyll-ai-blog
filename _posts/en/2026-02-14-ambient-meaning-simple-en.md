**Question:** What does "ambient" mean?

**Answer:**

**Ambient** means:

1. **Relating to the surrounding area or atmosphere**
   → existing in, or relating to, the immediate surroundings
   Common everyday usage

   Examples:
   - ambient temperature = the temperature of the surrounding air
   - ambient noise = background noise in an environment
   - ambient lighting = soft, general lighting (not direct/spotlight)

2. **Ambient music / ambient genre**
   A style of music that:
   - creates atmosphere
   - is usually slow, calm, atmospheric
   - often lacks strong beat or vocals
   - is meant to be "heard but not actively listened to" (Brian Eno's famous definition)

   Famous ambient artists: Brian Eno, Aphex Twin (selected works), Max Richter, Tycho, Biosphere

3. **Other common technical / scientific meanings**
   - ambient pressure = normal atmospheric pressure at that location
   - ambient air quality = the quality of the air around us
   - ambient conditions = the normal environmental conditions (temperature, humidity, etc.)
