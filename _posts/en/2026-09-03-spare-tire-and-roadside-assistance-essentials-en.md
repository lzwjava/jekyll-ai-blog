Question: Should we keep a spare tire and repair tools in the car for long trips, and can we call emergency roadside assistance for help if the tire breaks and the car cannot move?

Answer: Yes, carrying a spare tire and basic repair tools is essential for long-distance travel, as it allows you to replace a damaged tire yourself or get assistance from roadside emergency services if the car is immobilized.

### Essential Items for Long-Distance Travel

* **Spare Tire:** Ensures you can replace a blown-out tire immediately without relying on a tow truck.
* **Car Jack:** Used to safely lift the vehicle off the ground to swap the tire.
* **Lug Wrench:** Required to loosen and tighten the wheel nuts.
* **Tire Pressure Gauge & Portable Inflator:** Keeps the spare inflated properly before installation.

### Handling Roadside Emergencies

* **Self-Service:** If you have the tools and a spare, you can swap the damaged tire on the spot and drive to the nearest service station.
* **Roadside Assistance:** If you cannot change the tire yourself or lack a spare, you can call emergency roadside services (such as AAA or local towing services) to assist with tire replacement or tow the vehicle to a repair shop.
