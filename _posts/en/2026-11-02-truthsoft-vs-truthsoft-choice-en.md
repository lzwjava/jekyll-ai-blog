**Question:** Between "TruthSoft" and "Truthsoft", which should be chosen?

**Answer:** Go with **TruthSoft** (camelCase).

Here's why:

- **Readability** — The capital "S" visually separates the two meaningful words (*Truth* + *Soft*), making it instantly scannable.
- **Tech industry convention** — CamelCase is the dominant pattern for condensed tech brand names: GitHub, LinkedIn, HubSpot, SoundCloud, PayPal, WordPress, OpenAI. It signals "tech company" at a glance.
- **Domain alignment** — `truthsoft.dev` already reads as two words to a trained eye; the CamelCase brand name reinforces that reading.
- **"Truthsoft"** (all lowercase-start) looks like an afterthought — easy to misread as a single made-up word with no clear meaning.

**Recommendation:** **TruthSoft** — clean, modern, consistent with industry norms.
