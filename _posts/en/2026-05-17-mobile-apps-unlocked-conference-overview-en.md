Question: What is the "MAU Conference" in Las Vegas?

Answer:

The "MAU Conference" in Las Vegas refers to the **Mobile Apps Unlocked (MAU) Conference**, which is a major industry event for **mobile app marketing, growth, and user acquisition**. It brings together **mobile marketers, app developers, and industry leaders** to discuss trends, strategies, and technologies for increasing app downloads, engagement, and monetization.

Key features of the conference include:

* **Workshops and panels** on mobile advertising, retention strategies, and app store optimization (ASO).
* **Networking opportunities** with app marketers, tech providers, and investors.
* **Exhibitions and demos** of the latest marketing tools, analytics platforms, and app growth solutions.
* Focus on **user acquisition (UA), retention, monetization, and mobile growth metrics**.

Despite being called “MAU” (which in tech usually means “Monthly Active Users”), in this context it is the **name of the conference**, not a metric.
