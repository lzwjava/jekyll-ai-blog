Question: What is "两个确立" (The Two Establishments)?

Answer: "两个确立" (Liǎng gè quèlì) refers to a key political resolution adopted by the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) at the **Sixth Plenary Session of the 19th Central Committee in November 2021**.Question: What is "两个确立" (The Two Establishments)?

Answer: "两个确立" (Liǎng gè quèlì), or the "Two Establishments," is a CCP political slogan designed to reinforce General Secretary Xi Jinping's rule. It was formally put forward at the Sixth Plenary Session of the 19th Central Committee of the CCP in November 2021, though it first appeared in CCP discourse as early as 2018.

**The Two Establishments refer to:**

1. **Establishing Xi Jinping's core leadership** — establishing the core leadership of Comrade Xi Jinping in the CPC Central Committee and the whole Party.

2. **Establishing Xi Jinping Thought as the guiding ideology** — establishing the guiding role of Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era.

**Official Justification**

According to the CCP resolution, the Two Establishments reflect the common wish of all CPC members, the People's Liberation Army, and all ethnic groups, and are of "decisive significance" to the development of the Party and China in the new era, as well as to the historic process of the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation.

At a press conference on November 12, 2021, Central Policy Research Office director Jiang Jinquan elaborated on the Two Establishments, calling them "the call of the times, the choice of history, and the will of the people," and described Xi Jinping as "the well-deserved core of the Party, the leader of the people, and the commander-in-chief of the army."

**Political Significance**

Notably, while the related "Two Safeguards" (两个维护) refer to the leader by his current position as "General Secretary Xi Jinping," the Two Establishments refer to him as "Comrade Xi Jinping," thereby promoting him as an individual with authority independent of his particular position in the Party apparatus. This is a significant distinction, as it personalizes and entrenches his authority beyond any formal title.

**Relationship to Other Slogans**

The Two Establishments are closely linked to other CCP political frameworks such as the "Two Safeguards" (两个维护) and the "Four Consciousnesses" (四个意识), all of which collectively serve to consolidate Xi's centralized authority within the Party.
