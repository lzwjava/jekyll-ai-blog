**Question:** What are the differences between the NVIDIA CMP 50HX and CMP 90HX GPU cards?

**Answer:**

These are both NVIDIA **CMP (Crypto Mining Processor)** cards, designed specifically for cryptocurrency mining (not gaming). Here are the key differences:

**Architecture & Process Node**

- The CMP 50HX is based on the older **Turing architecture** (12nm), using the **TU102 GPU**, with a board design similar to the RTX 2080 Ti.
- The CMP 90HX is the only card of the four to use the newer **Ampere architecture** (Samsung 8nm), based on the **GA102-100 GPU**, sharing the same board (PG132) as the RTX 3080.

**Performance (Hash Rate)**

- The CMP 90HX's hash rate nearly doubles that of the CMP 50HX.

**Power Consumption**

- The CMP 50HX has about 28% lower power consumption than the 90HX.
- The CMP 90HX has a significantly higher TDP of 320W.

**VRAM**

- Both cards feature **10GB of VRAM**.

**Summary Table:**

| Feature | CMP 50HX | CMP 90HX |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Architecture | Turing (12nm) | Ampere (8nm) |
| GPU | TU102 | GA102-100 |
| VRAM | 10GB | 10GB |
| TDP | ~250W | 320W |
| Hash Rate | Baseline | ~2× the 50HX |

In short, the **90HX is the more powerful card** with better mining performance and a newer architecture, but it consumes significantly more power. The **50HX is more power-efficient** but falls behind on raw hash rate.
