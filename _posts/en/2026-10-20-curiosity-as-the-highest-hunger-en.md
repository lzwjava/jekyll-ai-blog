**Question:** What did Zhang Yiming (ByteDance founder) mean by "幸福的探索者 / 艰辛的攀登者" and what does that mindset mean for work?

---

**Answer:**

The source of these phrases is a Weibo post Zhang Yiming wrote, riffing on Steve Jobs' famous "stay hungry." He wrote: *"Jobs said 'stay hungry.' I think hunger has three levels: **greed**, **achievement motivation**, and **curiosity**. These three focus respectively on: instant results, the continuous process, and the vast unknown. And they correspond exactly to three types of people: the lowly speculator (卑劣的投机者), the arduous climber (艰辛的攀登者), and the happy explorer (幸福的探索者)."*

Here's the full framework he laid out:

| Drive | Focus | Type of Person |
|---|---|---|
| Greed | Instant results | 卑劣的投机者 — Base Speculator |
| Achievement motivation | Ongoing process | 艰辛的攀登者 — Arduous Climber |
| **Curiosity** | **The vast unknown** | **幸福的探索者 — Happy Explorer** |

**What this means for work:**

Zhang is ranking three fundamentally different *reasons* to work hard:

1. **Speculator** — grinding purely for the payout. Transactional and short-sighted.
2. **Arduous Climber** — driven by goals and achievement. Admirable, but work is a grind — it's *arduous*. The focus is on results, not the journey, so it can become exhausting and joyless.
3. **Happy Explorer** — driven by *curiosity about the unknown*. Work feels like discovery, not struggle. This is Zhang's ideal: he saw genuine curiosity as the highest form of "stay hungry," because it keeps you intrinsically motivated, not just chasing the next summit.

This connects directly to his other well-known principles: he believed ordinary people could do extraordinary things by maintaining a grounded, calm mindset, and he repeatedly emphasized that work is a process of accumulating credibility, not just a series of wins.

He also cautioned that an "all-in" attitude is a form of mental laziness — real depth comes from staying curious and thinking long-term, not from burning intensity toward a single goal.

**Practical takeaway:** The "happy explorer" mindset means you work hard *because you genuinely want to understand*, not because you fear falling behind or want a reward. That kind of hunger is self-renewing — it doesn't run out the way ambition or greed eventually does.
