**Question:** Does Elon Musk have a bad relationship with his father?

**Answer:**

Yes — the relationship between Elon Musk and his father, **Errol Musk**, is widely described as deeply strained and mutually hostile. Here's a breakdown:

---

### 🔴 Elon's View of His Father

Elon Musk has frequently expressed strong disapproval of his father, who had connections with South Africa's apartheid regime and has faced numerous criminal accusations.

In a widely cited 2017 *Rolling Stone* interview, Elon described his father as someone who "will plan evil," saying: "He was such a terrible human being, you have no idea. Almost every crime you can possibly think of, he has done. Almost every evil thing you could possibly think of, he has done. It's so terrible, you can't believe it."

Elon also added: "I tried threats, rewards, intellectual arguments, emotional arguments, everything to try to change my father for the better, and he, no way, it just got worse."

Elon Musk has also said that he will not allow his children to meet their grandfather.

---

### 🔴 Errol's View of Elon

The criticism flows both ways. In a radio interview on the Australian *Kyle and Jackie O Show*, 76-year-old Errol Musk claimed he isn't proud of his billionaire offspring, saying all members of the Musk clan have done interesting things.

Errol also branded Elon a bad father, saying he was never there for his large group of children — this coming from a man Elon has previously accused of doing "almost every evil thing you can possibly think of."

Errol has also publicly stated that "Elon needs to grow up," calling him essentially a man-child despite his billions.

---

### 🔴 Controversy Around Errol Musk

Elon's estranged father has been accused of child sexual abuse involving five of his children and stepchildren, according to a *New York Times* investigation, based on police reports, court records, and interviews with family members. Errol denied the allegations.

Errol's most prominent personal controversy was fathering a child with his stepdaughter, a woman 42 years his junior.

---

### 🟡 A Complicated Legacy

Despite all this, Elon has acknowledged that his father is a "brilliant engineer" and that he has "learned a lot from him" — while also calling their relationship "complicated."

Elon's biographer Walter Isaacson noted that in 2021, Elon said President Trump reminded him of his father — a telling psychological detail.

In short, this is one of the most publicly hostile father-son relationships in the public eye — marked by mutual criticism, estrangement, and deep unresolved wounds.
