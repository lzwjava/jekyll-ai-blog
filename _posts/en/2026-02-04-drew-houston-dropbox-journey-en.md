The genesis of Dropbox came from a personal frustration. In 2007, while Houston was a student at MIT and taking a bus trip from Boston to New York, he forgot his USB flash drive and couldn't work on his files. This moment of frustration sparked the idea for a seamless file synchronization service that would allow people to access their files from anywhere.
