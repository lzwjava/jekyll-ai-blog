**Question: Provide a comprehensive introduction to Phil Duan, Tesla AI Director.**

**Answer:**

**Phil Duan** (Chinese name: 段鹏飞 / Duan Pengfei) is a prominent engineering leader at Tesla, currently serving as **Director of Engineering** at Tesla AI (focused on Autopilot / Full Self-Driving systems). He plays a key role in advancing Tesla's autonomous driving technology, particularly through large-scale AI and end-to-end neural network approaches.

### Background and Education
- Phil Duan holds a **PhD in electronic engineering** (with a focus on avionics) from **Ohio University**, where his dissertation earned the 2019 RTCA William E. Jackson Award.
- He is an alumnus of **Wuhan University of Technology** in China.

### Career at Tesla
- Duan first joined Tesla in **2017** as a Senior Software Engineer, contributing to core Full Self-Driving (FSD) system development.
- He briefly left Tesla to work at **Amazon's Zoox** (2019–2020) due to the intense work environment but returned, stating that only Tesla could deliver true breakthroughs in the field.
- Over the years, he progressed rapidly through roles including:
  - Staff Software Engineer → developed the groundbreaking **Occupancy Network** (a key perception model for 3D scene understanding).
  - Senior Staff Software Engineer (around March 2022) → co-led AI development for **FSD v12** and **v13**, and led the AI team for data and perception.
  - Principal Software Engineer (2025) → led AI development for **FSD v14** and the **Tesla Robotaxi** launch (including unsupervised FSD enabling the ride-hailing service).
- In **early February 2026**, Tesla promoted him to **Director of Engineering for Autopilot** (sometimes referred to as Director of Engineering at Tesla AI), recognizing his leadership in major milestones like Robotaxi deployment and FSD advancements.

### Key Contributions
- Helped build the **world’s largest physical AI data engine**, leveraging Tesla's massive vehicle fleet for data collection, auto-labeling, and training.
- Pioneered innovations such as the **Occupancy Network**, auto-labeling pipelines, and vision foundation models for autonomous driving (highlighted in talks like his CVPR 2023 E2EAD workshop presentation).
- His teams have focused on scaling self-driving through end-to-end learning, fleet learning, data/perception, and solving autonomy at scale.
- He has represented Tesla in technical discussions and conferences, sharing progress on vision-based AI for self-driving.

### Current Role (as of March 2026)
As Director of Engineering at Tesla AI, his team concentrates on solving self-driving at scale. He has been actively involved in the successful Robotaxi launch and ongoing FSD improvements. Duan is also known for recruiting talent, often directing interested candidates to DM him for Tesla AI opportunities.

Phil Duan's rapid rise highlights the growing influence of technical talent (including Chinese-origin engineers) in Tesla's AI and autonomy leadership. His work has been central to Tesla's shift toward becoming an AI-first company with camera-only, end-to-end neural networks powering Full Self-Driving and Robotaxi capabilities.
