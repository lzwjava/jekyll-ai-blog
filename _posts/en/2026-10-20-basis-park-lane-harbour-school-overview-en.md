**Question:** What is 惠州贝赛思 (BASIS International School Park Lane Harbour)?

**Answer:**

惠州贝赛思 refers to **BASIS International School Park Lane Harbour**, an international school located in Huizhou, Guangdong Province, China.

**Overview:**

- The school opened in 2018 and was the second BASIS International School in China to offer a weekday boarding program. Its campus is nestled in the hills of a master-planned beach community in the municipality of Huizhou.
- It is situated within a resort development at Daya Bay, near Huizhou, with a capacity of up to 1,200 students. It offers both day and boarding options for grades 5–12.

**Curriculum & Mission:**

- BASIS Global's mission is to empower students to achieve at globally competitive levels through a transformative coeducational K-12 American program, focusing on providing world-class education to both expatriate families and local citizens alike.

**Academic Achievements:**

- BASIS students consistently gain admission to prestigious universities worldwide such as Harvard, Princeton, Oxford, MIT, and Cornell, with nearly 100% of graduates accepted into Top 50 U.S. universities and similarly rated institutions in the UK and Canada.
- The school recognized its first early graduate in 2021, who received early acceptance to MIT.

**Facilities:**

- The campus features state-of-the-art classrooms, a soccer field and track, gymnasium, basketball and tennis courts, an indoor heated swimming pool, expansive library, Apple computer lab, cafeteria, black-box theatre, auditorium, and student boarding facilities.

**Current Scale:**

- As of its latest profile, the school has around 450 students, predominantly local Chinese students, and has accumulated over 73 teacher reviews on international platforms.

In short, it is a well-regarded private international school offering an American-style rigorous curriculum in a scenic coastal setting near Huizhou.
