Question: What is the fundamental thought reflected in the essay "Oligarch's Relationship to the State" (Gua Ren Zhi Yu Guo Ye)?

Answer:

The correct answer is **D: Minben (People as the Root / People-Centric)**.

### Detailed Explanation

1. **Source and Author**:
    * The essay **"Gua Ren Zhi Yu Guo Ye"** (寡人之于国也) is an excerpt from **"Mencius" (Mengzi)**, specifically from the chapter *Liang Hui Wang I* (梁惠王上).
    * The author, **Mencius** (Meng Ke), was a key representative of the Confucian school during the Warring States period, succeeding and developing the teachings of Confucius.

2. **Core Philosophy**:
    * The central theme of this essay is Mencius's political philosophy of **"Benevolent Government" (Ren Zheng)** and the **"Kingly Way" (Wang Dao)**.
    * Mencius argues that a ruler's legitimacy and the strength of the state depend entirely on the well-being of the common people. He famously stated: **"The people are the most important element in a nation; the spirits of the land and grain are the next; the sovereign is the lightest."** (民为贵，社稷次之，君为轻).
    * In the text, Mencius critiques King Hui of Liang for focusing on minor relief efforts while failing to address the root causes of suffering (such as war, excessive taxation, and neglect of agriculture). He outlines specific policies (like not interfering with farming seasons, protecting forests, and providing education) that ensure the people have enough food and clothing. This focus on the livelihood and welfare of the masses defines the **Minben (People-Centric)** ideology.

3. **Analysis of Other Options**:
    * **A: Non-war (Fei Zhan)**: While Mencius opposes aggressive and unjust wars ("He who delights in killing people cannot succeed in unifying the world"), his opposition to war is a *consequence* of his people-centric view, not the sole fundamental thought. He supports defensive or punitive wars that save the people.
    * **B: Filial Piety and Fraternal Love (Xiao Ti)**: This is a crucial ethical concept in Confucianism regarding family relationships and social order. While Mencius mentions teaching these values in schools, it is a *method* to achieve social harmony within the broader framework of Benevolent Government, not the overarching political principle of the essay itself. Your selection of this option likely confused a specific detail with the main thesis.
    * **C: Loyalty to the Monarch (Zhong Jun)**: Mencius actually challenged blind loyalty. He believed that if a ruler acts like a tyrant, he loses the mandate to rule and can be justly removed. Therefore, absolute loyalty to the person of the king is not the core message; rather, the ruler must be loyal to the people.

### Conclusion

The essay systematically expounds on the idea that the foundation of a stable and prosperous state lies in prioritizing the needs and rights of the common people. Therefore, the fundamental thought is **Minben (People as the Root)**.
