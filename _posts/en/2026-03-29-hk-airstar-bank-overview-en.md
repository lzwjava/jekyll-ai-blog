Question: What is Airstar Bank (天星銀行) in Hong Kong?

Answer: **Airstar Bank** (天星銀行) is one of the eight licensed virtual banks in Hong Kong. Launched in June 2020, it is a digital-only lender that operates without physical branches, focusing on providing high-efficiency retail banking and SME services via its mobile app.

---

### ## Core Background & Ownership
Airstar is a joint venture that combines global technology leadership with local financial expertise. Its primary shareholders are:
* **Xiaomi Corporation:** The global electronics and IoT giant (the majority stakeholder through its subsidiary).
* **Futu Holdings (Futu Group):** The parent company of Futubull. In 2024, Futu became a major shareholder (holding roughly **44.11%** indirectly), significantly strengthening the bank's integration with investment and wealth management services.
* **AMTD Group:** A leading Asian financial services conglomerate that was a founding partner.

---

### ## Key Features & Services (2026)
Airstar markets itself as "Everyone's Bank," focusing on low barriers to entry and competitive rates.

#### **1. High-Interest Savings**
* **Stellar Savings:** Offers competitive tiered interest rates on HKD balances. While rates fluctuate, it historically provides significantly higher returns than traditional banks for the first **HKD 50,000 to HKD 300,000**.
* **No Minimum Balance:** There are no maintenance fees or minimum deposit requirements to keep an account active.

#### **2. Flexible Time Deposits**
* **Early Withdrawal without Penalty:** One of Airstar’s most popular features. Unlike traditional banks that charge high fees for breaking a fixed-term deposit, Airstar often allows users to withdraw funds early while still earning a base interest rate.
* **Multiple Currencies:** Supports **HKD, USD, and CNY** deposits.

#### **3. Instant Personal Loans**
* **24/7 Approval:** The entire loan process is automated within the app. Users can often get approval and disbursement in as little as **5 minutes**.
* **Transparent Pricing:** Features an annualized percentage rate (APR) that is often lower for new customers, with no handling fees.

#### **4. Global Remittance & Forex**
* Integrated with the **Faster Payment System (FPS)** for instant local transfers.
* Supports 24/7 real-time foreign exchange between HKD, USD, and CNY.

### ## Security & Protection
* **Licensed Bank:** Regulated by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA).
* **Deposit Protection:** Airstar is a member of the **Deposit Protection Scheme (DPS)**. This means your eligible deposits are protected up to a limit of **HKD 800,000** per depositor (an increase from the previous HKD 500,000 limit).

### ## Why Choose Airstar?
* **Best for:** Small savers who want high flexibility, and individuals who already use **Futubull (富途牛牛)** or **Xiaomi** products, as the integration between these ecosystems continues to grow.
* **Fast Onboarding:** You can typically open an account in under 5 minutes using just your HKID and a smartphone.
