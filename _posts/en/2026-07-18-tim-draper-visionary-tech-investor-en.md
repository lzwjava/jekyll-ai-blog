## Background

* Born: 1958, USA
* Education:

  * Bachelor’s degree from Stanford University
  * MBA from Harvard Business School

He is the third generation of a VC family:

* Grandfather: William Henry Draper Jr. — one of the first Silicon Valley venture capitalists.
* Father: William H. Draper III — founder of Sutter Hill Ventures.

So Tim grew up inside the venture capital ecosystem.

## Bitcoin bet

Tim Draper is especially famous because of his Bitcoin investment.

In 2014:

* The US government auctioned about **30,000 BTC** seized from Silk Road.
* Draper bought the Bitcoin at roughly **$600/BTC**.

At Bitcoin's later peaks, that investment became worth hundreds of millions of dollars.

He has remained one of Bitcoin's strongest supporters, arguing:

> Bitcoin is a technological revolution similar to the Internet.

His thesis:

```
Internet = decentralized information
Bitcoin = decentralized money
AI = decentralized intelligence
```

## Investment philosophy

Draper is a classic **high-risk, high-upside VC**.

He likes:

* founders with strong technical vision
* markets that can become huge
* technologies that replace existing systems

His pattern:

```
small probability of success
        ×
massive market impact
        =
venture capital return
```

Example:

```
100 investments

70 fail
20 return small money
10 become huge

one Tesla / Coinbase / Hotmail
can pay for everything
```
