I'll help you understand the "Four Greats" (四个伟大, sì gè wěidà), an important political concept in contemporary Chinese politics.

## Overview

The "Four Greats" is a slogan introduced by Xi Jinping that outlines four interconnected strategic objectives for the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) and the Chinese nation. It was formally articulated around 2017 and has become a cornerstone of Xi Jinping's political ideology.

## The Four Components

The Four Greats consist of:

1. **Great Struggle** (伟大斗争, wěidà dòuzhēng) - Refers to the ongoing ideological and political struggle to maintain CCP leadership, combat corruption, resist foreign interference, and overcome various challenges facing the party and nation.

2. **Great Project** (伟大工程, wěidà gōngchéng) - Specifically refers to "the great new project of Party building," focusing on strengthening the CCP's organizational capacity, discipline, ideological purity, and leadership role in all aspects of Chinese society.

3. **Great Cause** (伟大事业, wěidà shìyè) - Denotes "the great cause of socialism with Chinese characteristics," emphasizing the unique path of socialist development that China has pursued since reform and opening up.

4. **Great Dream** (伟大梦想, wěidà mèngxiǎng) - Refers to the "Chinese Dream" of national rejuvenation, including goals like achieving a "moderately prosperous society," becoming a modern socialist country, and restoring China to a position of global prominence.

## Context and Significance

These four concepts are presented as mutually reinforcing: the Great Struggle clears obstacles, the Great Project provides organizational strength, the Great Cause offers the developmental path, and the Great Dream provides the ultimate vision. Together, they form a comprehensive framework for understanding CCP governance strategy under Xi Jinping's leadership and are frequently referenced in official discourse,党建 (party building) materials, and political education.
