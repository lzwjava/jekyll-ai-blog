**Question:** How do I register a company in Guangzhou (2026)?

**Answer:**

The whole thing is now online via the official 广州市开办企业一网通平台 (Guangzhou One-Stop Enterprise Registration Platform) at <https://scjgj.gz.gov.cn/ywt/> — all districts in Guangzhou run the "one-network application, one-window pickup" model through this platform. No agency needed for a simple software/tech LLC. Here's the pipeline:

## 0. Decisions before you touch the platform

**Entity type.** A limited liability company (有限责任公司) is the choice for ~90% of founders — shareholders are liable only up to their subscribed capital. Sole proprietorships have lower tax but unlimited liability. For an AI/software business you want an LLC (e.g. 广州XX科技有限公司), full stop — it's what lets you sign B2B contracts, take investment later, and cap your personal risk (relevant given your mortgage).

**Name.** Format is "广州 + 字号 + 行业 + 有限公司", with the distinctive part being 2–6 characters; it can't be identical or near-identical to any existing Guangzhou company, or one deregistered/renamed less than a year ago. Prepare 3–5 candidates and pre-check them on both the platform and the National Enterprise Credit Information System; also check China Trademark Net for conflicts.

**Registered capital — this matters in 2026.** Subscription (认缴) still applies with no capital verification, but the amended Company Law requires paid-in capital within 5 years. Don't vanity-fill a large number — 10–50万 is typical for a startup, 50–100万 for tech companies. Too high creates real paid-in obligations and joint debt liability; too low can hurt credibility for contracts and bidding. For a solo AI tooling company, 10–50万 CNY subscribed is sensible.

**Address — the main gotcha.** Pure residential addresses can't be used. Compliant options: commercial office/shop (property cert + lease), government-registered park "red-book" addresses (usable for business registration, tax, and bank account opening), or a business-secretary hosted address — low cost, good fit for e-commerce/consulting/tech companies with no physical operations. Hosted addresses run roughly 1,200–5,000 CNY/year. Avoid unregistered virtual addresses — random inspections in 2026 will flag you as 经营异常 (abnormal operation). For you: a registered park address (园区红本地址) is the cheapest legit route.

**People.** Minimum: 1 legal representative (can also be a shareholder), and 1 supervisor who cannot be the legal rep — a spouse or relative works. Your wife as supervisor is the standard pattern (note: if she's a shareholder too, a two-person husband-wife company has some debt-commingling considerations; her as supervisor-only is simpler).

## 1. The actual flow (all online, 1–5 days)

1. **Name declaration** — log into the 一网通 platform, "名称自主申报" module; the system auto-checks duplicates. Approved names are locked for 30 days.
2. **Establishment filing** — fill in the setup info, upload lease contract (or address proof) and articles of association; all shareholders and the legal rep complete real-name verification and e-signature via 粤信签 or e签宝. Some districts have "instant smart approval" as fast as 0.5 working days. The platform auto-generates the articles of association — no drafting needed.
3. **License issuance** — free mail delivery or in-person pickup; the license date is your company's founding date.
4. **Seals** — Guangzhou provides free carving of seals for new companies (company, finance, legal-rep, invoice, contract), with police filing synced automatically.
5. **Tax registration** — complete tax registration within 30 days of getting the license or face fines / abnormal-taxpayer status. Done via 广东省电子税务局. Most tax type assignments sync automatically, but log in and confirm.
6. **Bank account** — the legal rep must show up in person for bank account opening (~500–1,500 CNY); everything else can be delegated.
7. **Ongoing compliance** — monthly/quarterly filings even at zero revenue. Bookkeeping agencies charge ~200–300 CNY/month for small-scale taxpayers. As an engineer you *could* self-file via the e-tax portal, but 200 CNY/month to not think about it is good leverage math.

**Total cost estimate (solo tech LLC):** ~0 CNY government fees + ~1,200–5,000/yr address + ~1,000 bank + ~2,400–3,600/yr bookkeeping ≈ **5,000–10,000 CNY year one.**

**Useful apps:** 粤商通 APP for e-signature, license download, and changes; 广东省电子税务局 for tax filing and invoices; 穗好办 for progress tracking.

## 2. Things specific to your situation

- **Bank contractor conflict.** Before registering anything with you as legal rep/shareholder, check your contract (and the outsourcing agency's terms) for outside-business-activity clauses. Banks are strict about this even for contractors. A common workaround people use is registering under a spouse's name — your wife as legal rep + shareholder — but that requires her buy-in, which per your own playbook you'd earn with results first.
- **Tax angle for side revenue.** For small revenue experiments (your 1K CNY/month validation targets), a small-scale taxpayer (小规模纳税人) LLC currently enjoys VAT exemption below the monthly threshold — verify the current threshold at registration time since these policies get extended year to year.
- **Timing.** Registration takes days and costs almost nothing, but a dormant company still needs monthly filings and annual reports forever. Don't register until you have your first paying customer or a contract that requires an entity (e.g., an app store developer account, 对公 payments, invoicing/发票 for a B2B customer). Empty-shell companies are pure compliance tax.
- **Ignore the agency-recommendation content** in most search results — it's SEO from the agencies themselves. If you do outsource, the going rate is a few hundred CNY for registration handling; anything bundled and expensive is markup.
