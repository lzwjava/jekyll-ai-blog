Question: Is the statement about 1000Base-SX correct — that it uses multimode fiber with short-wavelength laser, and supports maximum full-duplex transmission distances of 275m and 550m?

Answer:

Yes, that statement is correct. Here's a breakdown:

- **Medium**: Multimode optical fiber (MMF)
- **Light source**: Short-wavelength laser (850 nm)
- **Full-duplex max distance**:
  - **275m** — using 62.5 µm multimode fiber
  - **550m** — using 50 µm multimode fiber

So option C is indeed the correct answer if those are the stated values in the question.

For contrast:
- **1000Base-LX** uses single-mode fiber with long-wavelength laser (~1300 nm), reaching up to 5 km
- **1000Base-T** uses Cat5e/Cat6 copper, up to 100m
- **1000Base-CX** uses short copper cable (twinax), up to 25m
