**Question:** What is the AMD AI Developer Program about?

**Answer:**

The AMD AI Developer Program is AMD's free developer ecosystem for building AI on AMD hardware (ROCm stack, Instinct GPUs, Radeon, Ryzen AI). Launched Dec 2025, it's basically AMD's answer to NVIDIA's developer program — trying to build ecosystem loyalty among ML engineers who'd otherwise default to CUDA.

**What you get as a member:**

- **$100 AMD Developer Cloud credit** — activate GPU instances, run pre-configured notebooks, launch real workloads immediately. No AMD hardware required to join.
- **Community access** — member-only Discord channels for technical support, discussions, and direct access to AMD engineers.
- **AI Academy courses** — earn points by completing courses, participating in challenges, engaging in forums. Points unlock additional benefits and training.
- **Monthly office hours, hands-on workshops, early invitations to technical events** (like AMD AI DevDay).
- **Showcase opportunities** — get your projects featured on AMD Developer channels and recognized at AMD events.
- **Monthly hardware sweepstakes** — automatic entry to win AMD Radeon GPUs or Ryzen AI PCs.

**Why it's relevant to you specifically:**

You already have access to AMD MI300X via AMD Dev Cloud (~$2/hr). Joining the program gets you $100 in credits — about 50 free hours on MI300X — which is non-trivial for model training runs. The MI300X has 192GB HBM3, so it's legitimately useful for full fine-tuning of 70B+ models that won't fit on your RTX 4070.

The ROCm stack is maturing fast. They're shipping Day-0 support for new models like Qwen 3.5 on Instinct GPUs, which signals they're serious about framework parity with CUDA.

**How to join:**

Create a free AMD account, complete the AI Developer Program form, verify email, access the portal, join the Discord, then activate cloud credit via the member perks page.

Sign up at: [amd.com/en/developer/ai-dev-program.html](https://www.amd.com/en/developer/ai-dev-program.html)
