**Question:** Are there any other big-name ad networks for blog monetization besides the ones already mentioned?

**Answer:**

Yes — here are the heavy hitters that bloggers consider "graduating" to as their traffic grows:

---

### 🏆 Premium / Big Name Networks

**1. Mediavine**
One of the most reputable ad management platforms among lifestyle publishers, known for higher RPMs. For 2026, Mediavine accepts sites with either $5,000 or more in annual ad revenue or at least 50,000 monthly sessions. They also have an entry-level tier called **Mediavine Journey**, which runs the same ad technology as Mediavine Official but with a lower minimum traffic requirement (1,000 monthly sessions) — with RPMs still meaningfully higher than Google AdSense for most blogs.

**2. Raptive (formerly AdThrive)**
Raptive sits at the top of the food chain — they merged CafeMedia and AdThrive, creating the largest premium lifestyle network. Best niches: food, travel, parenting, personal finance, and wellness. Big news in 2026: Raptive dropped its entry point from 100,000 pageviews down to 25,000, opening premium monetization to a lot of sites that couldn't qualify a year ago.

**3. Amazon Native Shopping Ads**
Amazon's affiliate-style ad program places product recommendation units on your blog. Great for product-review or shopping-oriented blogs since you earn a commission when visitors buy.

**4. SHE Media**
SHE Media specializes in serving female-focused audiences, with ad campaigns tailored to niches like lifestyle, parenting, wellness, beauty, and fashion. If your blog fits one of those themes, SHE Media could be a great way to monetize with ads and access brand partnerships.

**5. Sovrn**
Networks like Sovrn use a CPA (Cost Per Action) model — you earn when a visitor completes a specific action like a purchase or sign-up. Best for product review and comparison content.

### 💡 Pro Tip

The most profitable publishers in 2026 use a blended approach — running CPM display ads alongside CPC native ads and CPA affiliate links. This diversification protects revenue. A common upgrade path is: **AdSense → Ezoic → Monumetric → Mediavine or Raptive** as your traffic scales.
