Question: How fast is 10BASE-T twisted pair?

Answer: 10BASE-T operates at **10 Mbps** (megabits per second) — that's the "10" in its name.

Key specs:

- **Speed:** 10 Mbps (half or full duplex)
- **Cable:** Category 3 (Cat3) UTP minimum; Cat5 also works
- **Max segment length:** 100 meters (328 ft)
- **Connector:** RJ-45
- **Standard:** IEEE 802.3i (1990)
- **Topology:** Star (via hub or switch)

It was the dominant Ethernet standard through the early-to-mid 1990s, before being largely replaced by **100BASE-TX** (Fast Ethernet, 100 Mbps) and eventually **1000BASE-T** (Gigabit Ethernet).
