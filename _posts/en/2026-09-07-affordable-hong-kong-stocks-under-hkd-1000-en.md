**Question:** Which Hong Kong stocks can I invest in with around HKD 1,000 per lot (not like Meituan where 100 shares × ~HKD 80 = ~HKD 8,000 minimum)?

**Answer:**

Great question — you've identified the core challenge of HKEX investing. On the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, a "board lot" (minimum trading unit) varies by stock, and most lots are designed to fall within a HK$1,000–HK$10,000 range. The number of shares per lot is set by the listed company itself, and this design aims to keep the entry cost per lot at a level generally acceptable to the public — usually a few thousand Hong Kong dollars.

Here are reputable, liquid HKEX stocks where one lot costs roughly HKD 1,000–2,000, making them accessible with a small budget:

---

**🔵 Energy / State-Owned (Defensive + Dividend)**

**Sinopec (0386.HK)** — *~HKD 4–5/share, lot size: 2,000 shares ≈ ~HKD 800–1,000/lot*
China Petroleum & Chemical (Sinopec) is one of Asia's largest integrated oil companies, with income from refining and marketing of oil products, and China's largest petrol station network with over 30,000 stations. Sinopec offers a low valuation and high dividend yield.

**PetroChina (0857.HK)** — *~HKD 9–10/share, lot size: 1,000 shares ≈ ~HKD 9,000–10,000/lot*
Note: PetroChina's lot size makes it slightly above budget. Worth watching but confirm your broker's current lot size before buying.

---

**📡 Telecom / Infrastructure (Stable, Low Volatility)**

**China Tower (0788.HK)** — *~HKD 9–10/share, lot size: 1,000 shares ≈ ~HKD 9,000–10,000/lot*
China Tower is a state-owned company providing telecom tower construction, maintenance, and ancillary services across mainland China, established by merging the tower businesses of China Mobile, China Unicom, and China Telecom. Its lot size has come down — confirm current lot on HKEX before buying.

**China Unicom (0762.HK)** — *~HKD 6–7/share, lot size: 1,000–2,000 shares ≈ ~HKD 1,000–1,400/lot*
China Unicom is one of China's major telecom operators. At ~HKD 6–7/share, depending on the lot size assigned, entry can be affordable.

**China Telecom (0728.HK)** — *~HKD 4/share, with a larger lot size ≈ ~HKD 800–1,000/lot*
Another major state-owned telecom — very stable, pays dividends.

**🏦 Banking (Dividend-Paying Blue Chips)**

**China Construction Bank (0939.HK)** — *~HKD 9/share, lot size: 1,000 shares ≈ ~HKD 9,000/lot*
BOC Hong Kong (2388.HK), one of Hong Kong's three note-issuing banks, benefits from widening interest margins and offers a positive earnings outlook — and similar large state banks like CCB trade at single-digit HKD prices, but lot sizes can make entry cost ~HKD 9,000. Check your broker.

**⚡ Utilities (Very Defensive)**

The Hong Kong and China Gas Company (0003.HK) has a highly monopolistic business with stable cash flow and a strong dividend history. It trades at around HKD 7–9 per share with a 500-share lot — roughly HKD 3,500–4,500 to enter.

**⚠️ Key Things to Know Before Buying**

1. **Always check the board lot size first** — it changes. Use [HKEX Market Data](https://www.hkex.com.hk) or your broker's app.
2. HKEX is currently reviewing board lot standardization, proposing to limit lot sizes to a maximum of 10,000 shares (down from 44 different possible lot sizes), which could make many stocks more affordable in the future.
3. **Sinopec (0386.HK) is the standout pick** for your ~HKD 1,000 budget — it's a Hang Seng constituent, pays dividends twice a year, and the per-lot cost is within range.
4. This is general information, not financial advice — prices change daily.
