Question: **What is [https://www.taylorcoulter.com.hk](https://www.taylorcoulter.com.hk), and is it reliable?**

Answer: **Yes — it appears to be a legitimate Hong Kong recruitment/IT staffing company, not an obvious scam.** I would be comfortable applying for jobs through it, but I would still verify the specific recruiter/job before sending sensitive documents.

### What Taylor Coulter is

Taylor Coulter Limited is a **Hong Kong recruitment and IT contracting company**.

Their business includes:

* Permanent / fixed-term recruitment
* IT contract staffing
* Executive search
* IT consultancy / outsourcing
* Contractor payroll administration ([Taylor Coulter Limited][1])

They claim to have **14+ years** of recruitment experience, and HKTDC lists the Hong Kong company as established in **2010**, with **101–200 Hong Kong staff**. ([Taylor Coulter Limited][2])

### The strongest legitimacy signal: HK Government contracts

This is the interesting part.

Taylor Coulter says it has been a **Government of Hong Kong T-Contract supplier since 2016**, supplying IT professionals to government departments/statutory bodies. ([Taylor Coulter Limited][3])

Their site currently has actual T-Contract jobs, including:

> Contract Analyst Programmer

and the requirements mention **LLMs, GenAI, Qwen, DeepSeek, RAG, Dify, n8n, Open WebUI, Python, JavaScript, SQL, Linux, Docker**, etc. ([Taylor Coulter Limited][4])

That's particularly relevant to you.

They also list a physical Hong Kong office:

**Room 702, Chinachem Century Tower, 178 Gloucester Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong**

HKTDC independently lists the same company and address. ([HKTDC Sourcing][6])

The important distinction is that **Taylor Coulter may be the staffing vendor, not the ultimate employer**. For a T-Contract role, you could technically be employed/paid by Taylor Coulter while working on-site or for a Hong Kong government department.

So before accepting anything, ask:

```text
Who is the ultimate client?
Is this a T-Contract position?
Who is my legal employer?
Who pays my salary?
Is the contract renewable?
What is the actual work location?
What is the salary range?
What benefits are included?
Is there a probation period?
```

And **never pay a recruiter a placement fee** or send passport/bank credentials merely to "process" an application.

**Bottom line: I'd rate Taylor Coulter as a legitimate recruitment/staffing company, with particularly strong evidence from its Hong Kong Government contracting business.** If you found a specific Taylor Coulter job you're considering, send me the job URL and I can inspect **the actual position, likely end client, salary/contract structure, and whether it's worth applying**.
