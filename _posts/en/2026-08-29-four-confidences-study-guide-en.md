---

### What Are the Four Confidences?

The Four Confidences (四个自信) is a political concept introduced by CCP General Secretary Xi Jinping. The concept includes:

1. **Confidence in the Path (道路自信)** — confidence in the socialist path with Chinese characteristics
2. **Confidence in Theory (理论自信)** — confidence in the theoretical framework guiding China
3. **Confidence in the System (制度自信)** — confidence in China's political and governance system
4. **Confidence in Culture (文化自信)** — confidence in Chinese socialist culture

The doctrine was first introduced at the 18th National Congress of the CCP in November 2012 (under Hu Jintao as "Three Confidences"), and Xi Jinping added "Confidence in Culture" as the fourth pillar in December 2014. In 2021, the concept was further expanded to include "Historical Confidence" (历史自信).

---

### How Does It Fit into the "New Era" Framework?

China's "foreign work in the New Era" and domestic governance in the New Era are explicitly guided by the Four Confidences: confidence in the socialist path, theory, system, and culture with Chinese characteristics.

The textbook for this type of course aims to "strengthen the Four Confidences" and cultivate students who love the Party, the country, and socialism.

---

### What Should You Do to Study for This Course?

Here are practical tips:

**1. Memorize the four items precisely** — Exam questions often ask you to list and explain each confidence. Use the mnemonic: **Path → Theory → System → Culture** (道理制文).

**2. Understand the origin and evolution** — Know that it started as "Three Confidences" (2012), became Four (2014), and gained a fifth "Historical Confidence" (2021).

**3. Connect each confidence to real examples:**
- Path → China's development model (not copying Western liberal democracy)
- Theory → Xi Jinping Thought as the latest Marxist adaptation
- System → People's Congress system, CCP leadership
- Culture → Traditional Chinese culture + socialist values

**4. Link it to related key concepts** the course likely tests together:
- "Two Establishments" (两个确立)
- "Four Consciousnesses" (四个意识)
- "Two Safeguards" (两个维护)

**5. Practice essay-style answers** — Chinese political theory exams often ask you to "explain the significance of…" or "how to strengthen X in the New Era."
