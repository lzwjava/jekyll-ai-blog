**Question:** What does the Ezoic rejection message mean, and what is Ezoic?

**Answer:**

**Ezoic** is an AI-powered website monetization and optimization platform that helps website publishers earn revenue through ads. It offers tools like ad testing, site speed optimization, and analytics to help publishers maximize their ad earnings.

The message you received is a **rejection notice** from Ezoic, meaning your website was not accepted into their program. Here's what it means:

- **Minimum traffic requirement:** Ezoic requires at least **250,000 active users per month** to qualify for their platform (this is their higher-tier program).
- **Your site didn't qualify:** Your current traffic is below that threshold.

**What you can do instead:**

1. **Ezoic Access Now** — Ezoic previously had a lower-tier entry program (formerly called "Access Now") for smaller sites. Check if it's still available.
2. **Google AdSense** — No minimum traffic requirement; good for beginner/small sites.
3. **Mediavine Journey** — Requires ~10,000 sessions/month; great for growing sites.
4. **Monumetric** — Requires ~10,000 page views/month.
5. **Grow your traffic** — Focus on SEO and content to reach Ezoic's threshold, then reapply.
