If you're looking for something quick and easy to prepare, here are some popular **instant drinks** you can consider, depending on your taste and purpose (e.g., energy, relaxation, nutrition):

### 🟤 **Hot Beverages**

- **Instant Coffee** – Just add hot water, comes in many varieties (black, 3-in-1, flavored).
- **Instant Tea** – Like milk tea, lemon tea, or matcha; just add hot water.
- **Hot Chocolate** – Cozy and sweet, usually powder-based.
- **Herbal Instant Teas** – Ginger, chamomile, ginseng, etc., often used for health benefits.

### 🟡 **Cold Beverages**

- **Instant Lemonade** or **Fruit Punch** – Powder mix + cold water = refreshing.
- **Electrolyte Powders** – Like Gatorade or Pocari Sweat powders, good for hydration.
- **Protein Shakes** – Powdered protein (whey, soy, etc.) mixed with water or milk.
- **Meal Replacement Shakes** – Like Soylent or Huel, good for nutrition on the go.

### 🟢 **Health or Functional Drinks**

- **Green Juice Powders** – With vegetables, spirulina, etc., often used for detox.
- **Collagen or Beauty Drinks** – Often flavored, just mix with water.
- **Vitamin C Powder** – Tangy and immune-boosting.
- **Chinese Herbal Drink Powders** – Like chrysanthemum, goji berry, etc.

### 🧊 Other Ideas

- **Instant Bubble Tea Kits** – Some come with tapioca and milk powder.
- **Matcha Latte Mix** – For a quick café-style drink at home.
