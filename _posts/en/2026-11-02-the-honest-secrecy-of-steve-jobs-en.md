**Question:** Steve Jobs seemed open and frank in person — so why was he so secretive about products? Isn't that a contradiction?

**Answer:**

Yes — that's exactly the paradox. And it's one of the most fascinating things about him.

---

### He Was Two Different Things at Once

It was one of his many odd contradictions: he could distort reality, yet he was also **brutally honest** most of the time.

Jobs told his biographer Isaacson directly: "If something sucks, I tell people to their face. It's my job to be honest."

So in *personal interactions*, yes — he was blunt, direct, even brutal. But **product secrecy was a completely separate domain**, driven by strategy, not personality.

---

### The Key Distinction: Personal Honesty vs. Business Control

Jobs was frank about his *opinions* — he'd tell an engineer their work was terrible to their face. But he kept *product plans* secret as a **deliberate competitive weapon**.

The most interesting thing is how he turned his personality traits — his **perfectionism, elitism, and control-freakishness** — into business processes that produced fabulous high-tech products.

In other words, the same need for control that made him brutally honest in meetings, also made him lock down every product detail from the outside world.

---

### He Was Also Private as a Person

In reality, the public knew very little about him. "He was incredibly secretive and private" — the mass reaction to his death made it seem as though Jobs was a friend to millions, but that sense of intimacy was largely an illusion.

---

### The Real Paradox

Apple's success was something "only a **complete control freak** could have pulled off." Jobs himself said: "We're brutally honest with each other... we had some rip-roaring arguments where we're yelling at each other" — yet that same man would never breathe a word about what Apple was building next.

---

So in short: his frankness was about **people and ideas in the room**. His secrecy was about **protecting the magic before the curtain rose**. Two sides of the same controlling, perfectionist mind.
