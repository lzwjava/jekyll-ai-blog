This passage outlines key requirements for China's **Belt and Road Initiative (BRI)** (一带一路). Here's a breakdown:

## The Four Main Requirements:

**1. Balance "bringing in" and "going out"** (引进来和走出去并重)
- Emphasizes **two-way investment cooperation** rather than one-directional flows
- China should both attract foreign investment domestically AND invest abroad
- Creates mutual economic engagement rather than a one-sided approach

**2. Uphold the "co-consultation, co-construction, co-sharing" principle** (共商共建共享)
- **Co-consultation**: Discuss and plan together with partner countries
- **Co-construction**: Build projects jointly, not imposed unilaterally
- **Co-sharing**: Share benefits and outcomes equally
- This principle stresses partnership over dominance

**3. Strengthen open cooperation in innovation** (加强创新能力开放合作)
Three specific areas:
- **Technological innovation cooperation**: Joint R&D, tech transfer
- **Theoretical innovation exchange**: Sharing ideas and development models
- **Innovative talent exchange**: People-to-people exchanges, educational cooperation

**4. Link BRI more closely with building a "community of shared future for mankind"** (人类命运共同体)
- Connects the initiative to China's broader foreign policy vision
- Frames BRI as serving global common interests, not just Chinese interests
- Emphasizes interconnected destiny and collective development

## Context:
These requirements reflect China's official framing of BRI as a **multilateral, collaborative framework** focused on mutual benefit, though international observers often debate whether implementation matches these stated principles.
