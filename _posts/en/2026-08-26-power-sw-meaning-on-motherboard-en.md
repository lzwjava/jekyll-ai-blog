Question: What does "SW" mean in "Power SW" on a motherboard pin header?

Answer: **SW** stands for **Switch**. So "Power SW" means **Power Switch**.

It's the 2-pin connector on your motherboard's front panel header that connects to the physical power button on your PC case. When you press the power button, it completes the circuit through these pins and signals the motherboard to turn the system on or off.

**Common front panel header pins you'll see nearby:**

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Power SW** | Power Switch |
| **Reset SW** | Reset Switch |
| **Power LED+/-** | Power indicator light |
| **HDD LED** | Hard drive activity light |

The connector is usually a small 2-pin plug coming from your case's power button cable, and it's typically labeled `PWR SW`, `POWER SW`, or `PW SW` depending on the motherboard brand.
