**Question:** How many engineers and employees does Nvidia have in recent years?

**Answer:**

Here's a breakdown of Nvidia's workforce growth over recent years, including its technical/engineering composition:

**Total Headcount by Year (Fiscal Year)**

| Fiscal Year | Total Employees | R&D / Technical | Sales, Ops & Admin |
|---|---|---|---|

**Key workforce facts:**

- As of the end of fiscal year 2023, Nvidia had 26,196 employees across 35 countries — 19,532 in research and development, and 6,664 in sales, marketing, operations, and administrative roles. About 80% of the workforce was technical, and about 50% held advanced degrees.

- By fiscal year 2024, that grew to approximately 29,600 employees across 36 countries — 22,200 in R&D and 7,400 in sales/operations. The technical share rose to 83%, with 49% holding advanced degrees.

- In fiscal year 2025, Nvidia employed around 36,000 people — a ~21.6% jump from the prior year.

- By 2026, Nvidia's headcount has grown further to an estimated 43,511 employees, with Engineering accounting for roughly 37.1% of the total workforce.

- As of FY2025, 82% of the workforce is technical and 51% hold advanced degrees — and over 41% of new hires came through employee referrals.

- Nvidia's turnover rate is notably low at around 2.5%, well below the industry average, and its revenue per employee has reached $3.6–4.6 million — far higher than most tech peers.

Despite its relatively small headcount compared to giants like Microsoft or Amazon, Nvidia's workforce is highly specialized — predominantly engineers and researchers driving its AI/GPU dominance.Nvidia has grown its headcount by over 66% in just three years — driven almost entirely by the AI boom.
