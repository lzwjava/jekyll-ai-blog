**Question:** How can I bring my spouse and two young daughters (ages 5 and 2) to the USA?

---

**Answer:**

Good news — your situation is one of the *most straightforward* cases in US family immigration. A spouse and unmarried children under 21 are classified as **Immediate Relatives**, which means no waiting list for a visa.

---

### Your Eligibility (depends on your status)

**If you are a US Citizen:**
Your spouse and children under 21 do not have to wait for a family preference visa — they qualify as immediate relatives, and there is no annual limit on visas for this group.

**If you are a Green Card Holder (LPR):**
Spouses and unmarried children of Green Card holders fall under family preference categories, which have annual visa limits and result in a waiting list. So the process takes longer.

---

### Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1 — File Form I-130** (Petition for Alien Relative) for each family member to establish the qualifying relationship. The 2026 filing fee is **$625 online** or **$675 by paper**.

**Step 2 — Wait for USCIS Approval.** For immediate relatives (your case), this moves faster since no visa number needs to become available.

**Step 3 — Consular Processing or Adjustment of Status:**
Once approved, the petition either allows your relative to apply for adjustment of status if they are already in the United States, or initiates consular processing if they are abroad.

**Step 4 — Your family gets their Green Card** upon approval of the final step.

---

### Important Note for 2026

The US has expanded travel and visa restrictions starting January 1, 2026. Certain family members of US citizens will no longer have special exceptions if they are from one of the travel ban countries — they will likely be denied a visa or entry. Only in very rare cases will the government consider an exception. Check if your family's home country is on the ban list before filing.
