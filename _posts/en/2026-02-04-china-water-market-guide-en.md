Here are other water options you'll find on JD.com in China:

**By water type:**

**天然矿泉水 (natural mineral water)** - From natural springs, contains minerals. Premium brands like Evergrande Ice Spring/恒大冰泉, Tibet 5100/西藏5100, Kunlun Mountain/昆仑山

**纯净水 (purified water)** - Heavily filtered, minimal minerals. Very clean taste. Brands like Wahaha/娃哈哈纯净水, C'estbon/怡宝

**矿物质水 (mineralized water)** - Purified water with minerals added back. Common and affordable.

**苏打水 (soda water)** - Carbonated or naturally alkaline. Brands like Watson's/屈臣氏苏打水, Paris Water/巴黎水 (Perrier)

**气泡水 (sparkling water)** - Carbonated. Popular brands: Genki Forest/元気森林 (flavored), San Pellegrino, Perrier

**婴儿水 (baby water)** - Low mineral content, marketed for infants but drinkable by anyone

**功能水 (functional water)** - Added vitamins, electrolytes, or other ingredients

**By source/region:**
- Glacier/snow mountain water (冰川水)
- Volcanic water (火山水)
- Deep sea water (深海水)

**Price tiers:**
- Budget: 15-25 CNY per 24 bottles (Wahaha, local brands)
- Mid: 30-50 CNY (Nongfu Spring premium lines)
- Premium: 60+ CNY (imported, Tibet 5100, Evian)

Since you like variety at good value, maybe try some 苏打水 or 气泡水 for something different? The sparkling options give you a totally different experience while still being affordable.
