Guangzhou Cultural Park (文化公园) is open and hosting its **"2026 Welcoming Spring Flower Fair and Lantern Festival"** during the Spring Festival season. Here are the key details:

**Event Highlights:**
- The event is themed **"岭南春韵 荔映花城" (Lingnan Spring Rhyme, Lychee Reflecting Flower City)**, featuring a combination of flower exhibitions, lantern displays, and cultural performances.
- The **Flower Fair** runs from **February 16th (Lunar New Year’s Eve) to March 3rd (Lantern Festival)**, with the **Lantern Festival lights** specifically lit from **February 25th to March 3rd, 18:30–21:30** each evening.
- The park is decorated with **22 groups of flower and lantern displays**, over 22,000 potted flowers (including peach blossoms and kumquats), and 1,800 decorative items, creating a vibrant and festive atmosphere.
- Cultural activities include **calligraphy exhibitions, folk performances, lion dances, riddle-solving, and spring couplet writing**, offering a rich experience of traditional Cantonese culture.

**Location and Access:**
- The park is located in **Liwan District, Guangzhou**, and is easily accessible via **Metro Lines 6 or 8 (Cultural Park Station)**.
- **Admission is free**, and no advance booking is required.

**Tips for Visitors:**
- The event is family-friendly and offers a great opportunity to experience local traditions and enjoy the festive decorations.
- For the latest updates, you can follow the official WeChat account **"广州本地宝" (Guangzhou Local Treasure)** and reply with **"春节" (Spring Festival)** for real-time information.
