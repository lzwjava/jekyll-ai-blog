**Question:** What is the current status of Dataland (迪兰) as a brand?

---

**Answer:**

Dataland (迪兰) is effectively **dead as a brand** as of 2024. Here's a summary of its current state:

**Brand Status — Officially Defunct**

Dataland is an officially defunct graphics card brand as of May 2024. Formerly a China-exclusive brand owned by TUL Corporation (the parent company of PowerColor), it was phased out to consolidate TUL's presence under the PowerColor brand.

**Why it died**

Dataland was actually the mainland China sales agent for TUL Group's PowerColor brand. When PowerColor itself entered the Chinese market directly under its own name, Dataland had no room left to operate.

**Last products ever made**

The brand's journey ended with the Radeon RX 6000 series — specifically the RX 6750 XT X-Serial and RX 6900 XT X-Serial. No RX 7000 or newer series were ever produced under the Dataland name.

**Physical closure**

Dataland's official website has expired and closed. Their flagship stores on JD.com and Taobao have also shut down.

**What happened to TUL Group's other brands**

TUL Group had several GPU brands: Vertex3D/VTX3D (targeted at Europe, now bankrupt), Diamond Multimedia (no longer makes gaming GPUs, only active in North America), and Sparkle — which has now joined the Intel camp and returned to China under the new name "撼与" (Hanyu), selling Arc series GPUs.

---

**In short:** If you own a Dataland R9 270 or any other Dataland card, there is no longer any official support, warranty service, or new products from this brand. It's a piece of Chinese GPU history now.
