## Question

For the QMAS application, how should you fill in **“Total income in the past year”**, especially if your income is in RMB rather than HKD?

## Answer

This field is asking for your **gross annual income during the 12 months immediately before your QMAS application**, and the system lets you add separate income records when the income is in different currencies.

For your situation, I would do this:

### 1. Use your actual employment income

If, for example, during the past 12 months you received:

* Salary from your Guangzhou employer: **RMB 360,000**
* Freelance/consulting income: **RMB 120,000**

You can enter them as separate income records, or combine them if the form structure permits:

| Income                     | Currency |          Amount |
| -------------------------- | -------: | --------------: |
| Employment salary          |  CNY/RMB |         360,000 |
| Consulting/business income |  CNY/RMB |         120,000 |
| **Total**                  |          | **480,000 RMB** |

The QMAS GPT explicitly considers **annual income** as one of its assessment criteria. ([Hong Kong Government News][1])

### 2. Don't convert RMB yourself unless the form asks you to

The wording you quoted is important:

> “You may add additional income record(s) if there is income denominated in a different currency.”

This strongly suggests the application expects you to enter the **original currency + amount** for each income record, rather than manually converting everything to HKD.

So if your salary is RMB:

**Currency:** CNY / RMB
**Income:** actual RMB amount

If you also had USD income, add another record:

**Currency:** USD
**Income:** actual USD amount

I would **not fabricate an HKD conversion** unless the QMAS form explicitly has an HKD conversion field.

### 3. What period?

Because you're applying now, **August 29, 2026**, the safest interpretation is the preceding 12 months:

**August 29, 2025 → August 28/29, 2026**

Use the actual dates defined by the form if it displays a specific period.

For salary, calculate the **actual gross salary received/earned**, including normal salary and relevant bonuses, rather than simply:

`monthly salary × 12`

if your actual payments differ.

### 4. What documents should you prepare?

I'd build one PDF evidence package containing:

```text
QMAS_Annual_Income_Proof.pdf

1. Employment contract
2. Salary certificates / employer income certificate
3. Payslips
4. Bank statements showing salary deposits
5. Tax/income records, if available
6. Bonus documentation, if applicable
```

This is particularly useful because Immigration Department can verify information and documents submitted by QMAS applicants. The government explicitly says applicants must provide accurate, complete and truthful information with supporting documents. ([Labour Department][2])

### Important distinction

Don't confuse this with the **HK$2 million income requirement** you may have seen elsewhere.

That **HK$2 million** figure is relevant to the **extension route** for certain QMAS entrants: documentary proof of assessable income for salaries tax of at least HK$2 million in the previous year of assessment. ([GovHK][3])

For your **initial QMAS application under the General Points Test**, annual income is one of the assessment criteria; it is not simply a requirement that everyone must earn HK$2 million. ([Hong Kong Government News][1])
