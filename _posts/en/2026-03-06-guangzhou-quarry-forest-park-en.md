## Xiancun Wudieling Forest Park (仙村五叠岭森林公园)

### Overview

Wudieling Forest Park is located at the former abandoned quarry site of Wudieling in Jigang Village, Xiancun Town, Zengcheng District, Guangzhou, Guangdong Province. The park covers a total area of 33.3 hectares. It is one of the township-level forest parks in Zengcheng District, and is considered a notable ecotourism destination in the eastern part of Guangzhou.

---

### Origins: From Quarry to Park

One of the most distinctive aspects of Wudieling Forest Park is its fascinating backstory. The park's original form was a quarry. The massive boulders left behind from early mining and excavation operations feature clear rock textures, and stand tall and magnificent — a dramatic and beautiful landscape in themselves. The transformation of this site from an industrial wasteland into a thriving forest park is a compelling example of ecological restoration and adaptive reuse of land in Guangdong.

---

### Natural Features

The park has a diverse terrain, with forest coverage reaching 80%. Inside the park, there are wide lakes, extensive lychee orchards, and a colorful and spectacular sea of crape myrtle flowers (紫薇花海). These elements together create a rich and varied landscape. The contrast between the rugged quarry rock scenery and the lush greenery and floral fields is one of the park's most appealing characteristics.

Key natural highlights include:

- **Giant Boulder Landscape** — remnants of the old quarry, featuring dramatic stone formations with clear rock textures
- **Wide Lakes** — open water bodies that add a calm, scenic element to the park
- **Lychee Orchards** — extensive groves that reflect the region's identity as part of China's famous "lychee hometown" (荔枝之乡) in Zengcheng
- **Crape Myrtle Flower Sea (紫薇花海)** — vibrant seasonal blooms that attract many visitors, especially in summer

---

### Park Planning & Functions

The overall planning goal is to position the forest park as an ecological forest park that integrates fitness hiking, leisure sightseeing, and science education, covering four major functional aspects: ecological function, recreational function, science education function, and social function.

The park is designed to serve both local residents and visiting tourists, acting as a green lung and recreation space on the southeastern edge of Guangzhou.

---

### Activities for Visitors

Visitors can engage in activities such as hiking, camping, picnicking, and photography inside the park. The park also provides facilities including restrooms and rest pavilions for visitor convenience. The park is open year-round.

The park features excellent mountain trails suitable for walking or cycling, where visitors can enjoy fresh air and beautiful natural scenery.

Popular activities include:

- **Hiking** on mountain trails through forest and boulder terrain
- **Photography** of the dramatic quarry boulders, flower fields, and lake views
- **Cycling** along the park's scenic routes
- **Picnicking** in open areas surrounded by nature
- **Camping** for those seeking an overnight nature experience
- **Sunrise viewing** — the elevated terrain offers scenic vistas over the surrounding landscape

---

### Admission & Access

The ticket price for Wudieling Forest Park is 20 yuan per person, which is relatively affordable.

For transportation:

- By self-driving: navigate to "仙村五叠岭森林公园" via GPS. The park is located on the east side of Zhuxian Road (朱仙路), Xiancun Town, Zengcheng District, Guangzhou.
- By public bus: take Zengcheng Bus No. 55 to the "Wudieling Station" (五叠岭站).

---

### Context within Zengcheng's Forest Park Network

Zengcheng District has numerous forest parks, including Wudieling Forest Park among many others, and importantly, all of these forest parks are free or low-cost — truly natural "oxygen bars" for the public.

The Xiancun Wudieling Forest Park was one of the first completed forest parks under Guangzhou's major ecological construction initiative, completed as part of the city's push to expand its forest park network.

---

### Best Time to Visit

- **Summer (June–August)**: Best time to see the crape myrtle flower sea in full bloom
- **Spring (March–May)**: Lush green recovery of the forest, pleasant temperatures for hiking
- **Autumn (September–November)**: Comfortable weather for outdoor activities; the lychee orchards add visual interest
- **Winter**: The dramatic quarry boulder landscape is visible year-round and especially photogenic in clear winter light

---

### Summary

Wudieling Forest Park is a hidden gem in Guangzhou's eastern suburbs — a place where industrial history meets ecological restoration. Its dramatic boulder scenery, colorful flower fields, lychee groves, and lake landscapes make it a richly rewarding destination for nature lovers, hikers, photographers, and families looking for a peaceful escape from the city without traveling far.
