**Versatile** is an adjective that means able to adapt or be adapted to many different functions, activities, skills, or uses. It describes something (or someone) that can easily switch between tasks or serve multiple purposes effectively.

### Key Definitions from Reliable Sources:
- **Merriam-Webster**: Embracing a variety of subjects, fields, or skills; turning with ease from one thing to another; having many uses or applications.
- **Cambridge Dictionary**: Able to change easily from one activity to another or able to be used for many different purposes.
- **Oxford Learner's Dictionary**: Able to do many different things or to be used for many different purposes.

The word comes from Latin *versatilis*, meaning "turning around" or "revolving," from *vertere* ("to turn").

### Examples:
- For people: A **versatile actor** can play dramatic roles, comedies, and action parts equally well.
- For athletes: A **versatile player** might excel in multiple positions, like defense and offense.
- For objects: A Swiss Army knife or multi-tool is versatile because it combines many functions (knife, screwdriver, etc.).
