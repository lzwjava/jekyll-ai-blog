**Question:** In the IELTS computer-based test, can we adjust the text size and set contrast to black and white (or high contrast/dark mode)?

**Answer:**

Yes, in the **computer-delivered IELTS test** (also called IELTS on computer), you can adjust the text size (font size) and change the background color/contrast for better comfort.

- **Text size adjustment** — You can change the font size, typically with options like small, normal/medium, or large. Many test-takers choose larger sizes (e.g., large) for Reading and Listening to reduce scrolling and eye strain.
- **Contrast / background color** — You can adjust the background color, including options like a black (dark) background, which creates high contrast (often white or light text on black). This is commonly recommended to reduce eye strain, similar to dark mode. There may be a few preset options (e.g., 3 choices reported by some candidates).

These settings are available via a **'Settings' button** (usually in the upper right corner of the screen) at the start of the test or during it. The interface is designed to be user-friendly, and these features help personalize the experience without needing special approval for basic adjustments.

Note: These apply to the standard computer-based test at test centers (Listening, Reading, Writing sections). For significant visual impairments requiring more advanced accommodations (e.g., very large print, screen readers, or modified formats), you should contact your test center in advance for special arrangements.

You can try these features yourself using the official free familiarisation/practice tests on the British Council, IDP, or IELTS.org websites to see exactly how the settings work.
