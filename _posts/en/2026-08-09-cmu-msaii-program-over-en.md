Question: Intro to Carnegie Mellon University Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence and Innovation (MSAII)

Answer:

Carnegie Mellon University's **Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence and Innovation (MSAII)** is a professional AI master's program under the Language Technologies Institute (LTI) in the School of Computer Science. It is designed for people who want to **build and deploy AI products**, not only study AI theory. ([Language Technologies Institute][1])

The program's philosophy is:

> **AI engineering + entrepreneurship + product innovation**

It is different from a pure research AI degree (for example, MS Machine Learning). The goal is to train people who can discover AI opportunities, build large-scale AI systems, and turn them into products or companies. ([MSAI Institute][2])

---

## 1. Program positioning

CMU has several AI-related master's programs:

| Program                                | Main focus                                                |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| MS Machine Learning                    | Deep ML theory, research, algorithms                      |
| MS Computer Science                    | General advanced CS                                       |
| MIIS (Intelligent Information Systems) | NLP, information extraction, language/video understanding |
| **MSAII**                              | AI engineering + product + startup mindset                |
| MCDS                                   | Data engineering, large-scale systems                     |

MSAII is closer to:

```
AI Engineer
+
Technical Product Builder
+
AI Startup Founder
```

rather than:

```
AI Research Scientist
```

([Language Technologies Institute][3])

# 2. Curriculum structure

The program is usually **4 semesters (2 years)**. Students complete around 192–195 units including core courses, knowledge requirements, electives, internship/practicum, and capstone. ([Language Technologies Institute][4])

# 6. Main concern

The biggest issue is admission.

CMU SCS master's programs are highly competitive. MSAII is not "easy CMU"; it still expects strong CS/AI preparation. Typical applicants have backgrounds in:

* Computer Science
* Software Engineering
* Bioinformatics
* Engineering fields

([Language Technologies Institute][4])
