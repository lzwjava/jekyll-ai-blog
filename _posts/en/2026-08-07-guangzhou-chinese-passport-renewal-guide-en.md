Question: How to renew a Chinese passport in Guangzhou?

Answer:

If you mean **renew a Chinese ordinary passport (中华人民共和国普通护照)** in Guangzhou, the process is:

## 1. Check eligibility

You can apply for **passport renewal (换发)** if:

* Passport is close to expiry
* Passport pages are full
* Passport is damaged
* Personal information changed
* Other situations requiring replacement

Guangzhou accepts applications from Chinese citizens at the exit-entry administration offices. ([Gaj][1])

---

## 2. Make an appointment

Recommended methods:

### Option A — WeChat / Alipay

Search:

```
移民局12367
```

or

```
穗好办
```

Then:

```
出入境
→ 中国公民出入境预约
→ 护照
```

Guangzhou's government service platform supports passport appointment through the "穗好办" app and mini-program. ([Guangzhou Gov Data Admin][2])

---

## 3. Go to the Exit-Entry Administration office

Bring:

* Chinese ID card (身份证)
* Old passport
* Appointment information
* Passport photo / photo collection (usually can be done onsite)
* Other documents if your situation requires them

Main Guangzhou citizen exit-entry service hall:

Guangzhou Public Security Bureau Exit-Entry Administration Reception Hall

There are also district-level exit-entry halls. ([Gaj][3])

---

## 4. On-site process

Usually:

```
Queue / check appointment
        ↓
Photo + fingerprint collection
        ↓
Submit application
        ↓
Pay fee
        ↓
Receive pickup notice
```

You can choose:

* Pick up yourself
* Mail delivery (EMS)

---

## 5. Processing time

Normally around:

```
7–15 working days
```

but holidays and workload can affect it.

---

## 6. Cost

A normal passport renewal is usually around:

```
¥120 RMB
```

(payment methods: WeChat Pay / Alipay / bank card)

---

## Practical advice

Since you are in Guangzhou:

1. Renew **before your passport has less than 6 months validity** if you plan:

   * US visa application
   * overseas study
   * international travel

2. Do it early in August because many students and travelers renew before autumn travel.

3. Keep the old passport after renewal if it contains visas (US visa, Schengen, Japan, etc.). Usually old visas remain usable with the new passport if both passports are carried.
