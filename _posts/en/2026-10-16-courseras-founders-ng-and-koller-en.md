Question: Who is the founder of Coursera?
Answer: Coursera was co-founded in 2012 by two Stanford University computer science professors, **Andrew Ng** and **Daphne Koller**.

* **Andrew Ng**: An influential computer scientist and AI pioneer who leads DeepLearning.AI and AI Fund, and serves as Chairman of Coursera's board.
* **Daphne Koller**: A renowned computer scientist, MacArthur Fellow, and founder and CEO of Insitro.
