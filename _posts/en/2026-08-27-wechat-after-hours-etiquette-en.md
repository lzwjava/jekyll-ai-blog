**Question:** Is it considered unprofessional or inappropriate to send WeChat messages to professional contacts outside of working hours?

**Answer:**

Yes, generally speaking, sending WeChat messages to professional connections outside of working hours can be seen as inconsiderate or unprofessional, especially in certain contexts. Here's a breakdown:

**Why it can be problematic:**

- **Disrupts work-life balance** — It puts pressure on the recipient to respond even during their personal time, which can cause stress or resentment.
- **Cultural expectations are shifting** — While China has historically had a strong "always-on" work culture, attitudes are changing, particularly among younger professionals and after-hours contact is increasingly viewed negatively.
- **Legal context in some countries** — Some countries (France, Australia, etc.) have "right to disconnect" laws, though China does not yet have a formal nationwide law on this.
- **It signals poor time management** — Sending non-urgent messages at night or on weekends may imply you didn't plan your work well during business hours.

**When it might be acceptable:**

- The matter is **genuinely urgent** and time-sensitive.
- You have a **close working relationship** where both parties have agreed to flexible communication.
- You use WeChat's **"Schedule Send"** feature to queue the message for the next business day.
- The recipient is in a **different time zone** and the timing aligns with their working hours.

**Best Practice:**

If it's not urgent, draft the message and schedule it to send during business hours. This shows respect for boundaries while ensuring nothing is forgotten.
