Question: What CPUs does the MSI Z170A SLI PLUS motherboard support, and what is its socket?

Answer:

**Socket:** The board uses an Intel LGA1151 socket (often written "Socket 1151" or "LGA 1151") with the Intel Z170 chipset.

**Supported CPUs:** Intel 6th Generation (Skylake) Pentium, Celeron, Core i3, Core i5 and Core i7 processors. Examples are the i3-6100, i5-6500, i5-6600K, i7-6700 and i7-6700K. The Z170 chipset also allows overclocking on "K" model CPUs.

**Compatibility notes:**
- The listing says "supports Intel 6th gen CPU," which matches MSI's launch spec. 7th-gen (Kaby Lake) CPUs such as the i7-7700K use the same LGA1151 socket, and many Z170 boards support them after a BIOS update. I didn't confirm this for this exact board, so check MSI's CPU support list before buying one.
- 8th and 9th gen Intel CPUs use a different LGA1151 revision and will **not** work on this board, even though the socket name is the same.
- The board takes 4x DDR4 DIMM slots, dual channel, max 64GB. It does not take DDR3 RAM.
- It has one fully capable PCIe 3.0 x4 M.2 slot, up to 2280 size.
- The rear panel offers HDMI, DVI-D and VGA outputs from the CPU's integrated graphics, so a CPU with onboard graphics can run without a graphics card.
- Since it's a used board, ask the seller to confirm the BIOS version if you plan to use a 7th-gen CPU. Also check that the socket pins aren't bent.
