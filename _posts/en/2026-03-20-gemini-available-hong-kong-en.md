**Question: Is Gemini now open to Hong Kong users?**

**Answer:**

Yes, as of March 2026, Google has started rolling out Gemini (the AI chatbot and web app) to all users in Hong Kong. 

- This is a gradual rollout that began around mid-March 2026.
- It starts with the Gemini web app (accessible at gemini.google.com), allowing general public access without needing a VPN or workarounds like before.
- The mobile app version is planned to follow later.
- Previously, access in Hong Kong was limited (e.g., only via Google Workspace for business users or through VPNs for personal use), but restrictions have now been lifted for the web experience.
- This aligns with Hong Kong's push for AI adoption and Google's efforts to expand in the region.

Note that availability can depend on phased rollout, so some users might gain access immediately while others see it soon after. The official Google support page lists over 230 countries/territories for the web app (Hong Kong is included in such expansions), though mainland China remains limited to Workspace only.
