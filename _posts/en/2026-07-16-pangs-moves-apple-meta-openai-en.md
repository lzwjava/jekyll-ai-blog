Question: Where is Ruoming Pang (庞若明) now? He was Apple AI head and then moved to Meta?

Answer:

The timeline changed again. Ruoming Pang is **not at Meta anymore**. His recent moves are:

1. **Google → Apple (2021)**

   * Ruoming Pang previously worked at Google on speech/ML systems.
   * Joined Apple in 2021.
   * At Apple, he became the leader of the **Foundation Models team**, responsible for Apple's internal AI models that power parts of Apple Intelligence (on-device models, summarization, etc.). ([Bloomberg][1])

2. **Apple → Meta (July 2025)**

   * He left Apple and joined Meta's new **Meta Superintelligence Labs**.
   * His role was around AI infrastructure/model development.
   * Reports said Meta offered a compensation package reportedly above $200M over several years. ([Reuters][2])

3. **Meta → OpenAI (February 2026)**

   * He later left Meta after about seven months.
   * He joined **OpenAI**, after being recruited for months.
   * At Meta he had been leading AI infrastructure efforts inside Meta Superintelligence Labs. ([The Information][3])

So the short version:

```
Google
  ↓
Apple (Foundation Models lead)
  ↓  July 2025
Meta Superintelligence Labs
  ↓  Feb 2026

```

([Reuters][4])

The interesting part is the pattern: he became one of the symbols of the **AI talent war**. The big labs are now moving researchers like NBA free agents:

* Meta aggressively recruited from Apple/OpenAI/Google.
* OpenAI then recruited back from Meta.
* Compensation is reaching executive/elite-researcher levels. ([Reuters][5])

For someone tracking LLM engineering, his background is especially interesting because he is not mainly famous for a single transformer paper like some researchers; his strength is **production-scale AI systems, speech models, on-device models, and ML infrastructure**. ([arXiv][6])
