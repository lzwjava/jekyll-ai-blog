Question: What is the C2 proficient level?

Answer:

The **C2 Proficient level** is the highest level in the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), representing mastery of the English language. It is often described as "near-native" or "bilingual" proficiency, equivalent to that of a highly educated native speaker in terms of precision, fluency, and sophistication.

### Key Characteristics

- **Overall Description**: At C2, you can understand with ease virtually everything heard or read. You can express yourself spontaneously, very fluently, and precisely, differentiating finer shades of meaning even in the most complex situations.
- **Listening**: Understand any kind of spoken language, whether live or broadcast, even when delivered at fast native speed, including unfamiliar accents, colloquialisms, or specialized terminology.
- **Reading**: Read with ease virtually all forms of written language, including abstract, structurally or linguistically complex texts such as manuals, specialized articles, and literary works. Appreciate subtle distinctions in style and implicit meanings.
- **Speaking**: Express ideas fluently and convey finer nuances of meaning precisely. Participate actively in academic or professional discussions, summarize information from different sources, and reconstruct arguments coherently.
- **Writing**: Produce clear, smoothly flowing text in an appropriate style on complex subjects, with effective logical structure.

### In the Context of EF SET

- The EF SET scores C2 as **71–100** out of 100 (primarily based on reading and listening skills).
- Achieving C2 on the EF SET certificate indicates you have proficient-level receptive skills comparable to advanced native speakers.

C2 is not strictly "native" level (as native speakers may vary in education and vocabulary), but it signifies exceptional command suitable for the highest academic, professional, or literary contexts.
