**Question:** What are the major humanoid robot companies in China?

**Answer:**

China dominates the humanoid robot space right now. Over 140 domestic manufacturers and more than 330 humanoid models have been unveiled, with Chinese firms producing roughly 87–90% of all humanoid robots shipped globally in 2025. Here are the key players:

---

## Tier 1: Market Leaders by Shipments

### 1. **Unitree Robotics** (宇树科技) — Hangzhou
China's largest humanoid robot company — sold 5,500 humanoid robots in 2025, making it the world's top seller by its own figures. Known for the **G1** (~$16K) and **H1** models. Closed Series C at $3B valuation, targeting $7B at IPO. Manufactures motors, reducers, and sensors in-house. Their CEO Wang Xingxing publicly compared the current moment to "1–3 years before ChatGPT."

### 2. **AgiBot** (智元机器人) — Shanghai
Led the world with 5,168 humanoid units in 2025 (39% global share per Omdia). Their flagship is the **A2**, certified for China, US, and European markets with advanced NLP for human-robot interaction. Shipped more humanoid robots last year than any other company according to Omdia's ranking.

---

## Tier 2: Established Players

### 3. **UBTECH Robotics** (优必选) — Shenzhen
The first known humanoid company out of China, publicly listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange. Their **Walker S1/S2** industrial robots are already deployed on NIO and Zeekr EV assembly lines. Cumulative Walker series orders surpassed 800M CNY. Targeting 10,000 units/year capacity by 2026.

### 4. **Fourier Intelligence** (傅利叶智能) — Shanghai
Medical/rehab robotics background, now pivoting to general humanoids. Strong in exoskeleton tech. Listed among the top 6 by 2025 shipments.

### 5. **Leju Robotics** (乐聚机器人) — Shenzhen
Among the top humanoid makers by 2025 shipments. Known for affordable bipedal robots targeting research institutions.

### 6. **Engine AI** — Shenzhen
Relatively newer entrant, also in the top shipment rankings for 2025.

---

## Tier 3: Notable Startups & EV-Backed Players

### 7. **Galbot** — Beijing
Has raised more than $300M in fresh funding, reportedly pushing its valuation to $3B — one of the largest financings in China's humanoid robotics sector. Their **G1** appeared at the Spring Festival Gala.

### 8. **Xpeng Robotics** (小鹏汽车) — Guangzhou
EV maker Xpeng debuted its second-generation humanoid **"Iron"** and plans mass production. Classic EV→robotics pivot leveraging shared supply chain.

### 9. **DEEP Robotics** — Hangzhou
Their **DR02** is built for industrial all-weather deployment with IP66 protection — differentiating on durability vs. lab performance.

### 10. **Robot Era** (星动纪元) — Beijing
Their **L7** targets logistics, manufacturing, and research with a modular design. Spun out of Tsinghua.

### 11. **LimX Dynamics** — Shenzhen
Backed by Alibaba, JD.com, and Lenovo, raised $69.31M as of July 2025. Released the **Oli** full-sized humanoid in mid-2025. Building an agentic AI OS called **COSA** for real-time motion adjustment.

### 12. **Kepler** — Shanghai
Demonstrated robots capable of handling, transporting, and sorting materials for factory floors.

### 13. **Siasun** (新松机器人) — Shenyang
China's largest listed industrial robot company, now entering humanoids. State-backed, with Shenyang Automation Institute roots.

### 14. **MagicLab** / **Noetix**
Appeared at the 2026 Spring Festival Gala alongside Unitree and Galbot — newer entrants gaining visibility.

---

## Macro Context

In 2025, Chinese humanoid-related investment reached 39.8B RMB (~$5.5B) across 325 deals — 326% year-over-year growth. The government's **2025 Humanoid Robot Action Plan** targets 100,000 deployed humanoids by 2027.

The structural edge: China has a robust hardware supply chain built through the EV sector — sensors, batteries, actuators — allowing companies to iterate far faster than Western competitors, and the robots are significantly cheaper.

Key risk to watch: China's top economic planner NDRC has warned about a bubble risk, noting 150+ companies bringing out similar products — classic China boom/consolidation cycle incoming, similar to EVs.
