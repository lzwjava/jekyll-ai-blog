Question: Which address can be used for Guangzhou company registration under the “residence declaration system”?

Answer:

In Guangzhou, the “住所（经营场所）自主承诺申报制” means you can declare your registered company address yourself instead of always providing a traditional commercial property certificate. You still need to provide a **real, reachable address** and take responsibility for its authenticity. ([Guangzhou Government][1])

Typical options:

### 1. Your own home address (住宅)

Possible in some cases, depending on district and business type.

Example:

```
广东省广州市XX区XX街道XX小区XX栋XX室
```

You declare:

* address information
* property owner information
* usage purpose
* contact person

However:

* Some industries are restricted.
* The address may be inspected.
* If government letters cannot reach you, it can create problems. ([Guangzhou Government][1])

For an AI/software consulting company, this is often easier than for manufacturing, food, etc.

---

### 2. Office / coworking space

Usually the safest option.

Examples:

```
广州市天河区某某路XX号XX室
```

You rent:

* coworking desk
* shared office
* incubator office

The provider gives you:

* lease agreement
* address usage proof

Many startup companies use this.

---

### 3. Industrial park / incubator address

Guangzhou supports addresses from:

* science parks
* technology parks
* entrepreneurship incubators
* government-supported innovation spaces

These can often provide official site-use certificates. ([Guangzhou Government][2])

Examples:

* Tianhe software park
* Huangpu science city
* university innovation parks

---

### 4. “集群注册地址” / registered address service

Many accounting companies provide this.

Example:

```
广州市天河区XX商务秘书有限公司
```

Your company registration address is their office address.

Advantages:

* cheap
* no need to rent a full office

Risks:

* choose a legitimate provider
* make sure they handle government inspections
* avoid fake “empty addresses”

---

For your situation (AI consulting + agent development + possible startup), I would probably choose:

**Option A (lowest cost):**

```
home address + residence declaration
```

if your district accepts it.

**Option B (more professional):**

```
Tianhe / Huangpu coworking or incubator address
```

because:

* easier for bank account opening
* looks more credible to enterprise clients
* easier if you hire employees later

Guangzhou’s rules explicitly allow flexible arrangements such as multiple companies sharing one registered address (“一址多照”) under certain conditions, and allow companies to add business locations separately (“一照多址”). ([Guizhou Government][3])

For an AI company doing consulting, software, and model training, I would avoid random virtual addresses and use either your real home address or a recognized incubator/coworking address.
