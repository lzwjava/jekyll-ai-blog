Question: How many users does Linux Mint have?

Answer:

There is **no official or precise public number** for the total number of Linux Mint users worldwide, as Linux Mint (like most Linux distributions) does not track individual installations, require registration, or collect telemetry data by default.

Estimates vary widely and are indirect, based on proxies like forum members, download counts, social media likes, mirror traffic, or market share assumptions. Here's a realistic overview as of early 2026:

### Common Estimates from Community Sources
- Older rough calculations (e.g., from forums/Facebook groups around 2024–2025): 1–7 million users globally.
  - One common method multiplies forum members (~115,000 in some posts) or Facebook likes (~132,000) by a factor (e.g., ×10 to ×40) to account for non-active community users → leading to claims of 1.3 million to 7+ million.
  - These are highly speculative and often overstated.
- Linux Mint forums discussions (2010s–2025): Estimates ranged from under 1 million to several million, often tied to site visits or package reviews (~17,000–58,000 unique reviewers in past years).

### Market Share Context (2025–2026)
- Global **desktop Linux** market share is around **4%** (StatCounter data for Jan 2026: ~4.02%; some reports claim 4.7% for 2025 overall, or higher in specific regions like the US at ~5%).
  - Total desktop computers worldwide: Roughly 1–2 billion active devices (estimates vary).
  - This puts total desktop Linux users in the range of **40–80 million** (conservative) to over 100 million if including broader metrics.
- Linux Mint is one of the most popular desktop distros (often ranked #2–#4 behind Ubuntu on DistroWatch page hits, Steam surveys, and community polls), but it holds only a **fraction** of the Linux desktop share.
  - Ubuntu typically leads Linux desktop adoption, followed by distros like Mint, Fedora, Arch, etc.
  - Steam Hardware Survey (gaming-focused): Linux Mint appears in the top few non-Arch Linux distros, but no exact percentage breakdown for Mint alone.
  - Realistic guess: Linux Mint likely has **several million active users** (perhaps **2–10 million** worldwide), making it one of the larger consumer-oriented distros.

### Why Exact Numbers Are Hard to Get
- No forced user tracking (privacy-focused).
- Many users download via torrents (not counted on official mirrors).
- Installs can be on old/offline machines or VMs.
- Growth in 2025–2026 (post-Windows 10 EOL) likely increased numbers, but no hard data exists.

In short: Linux Mint probably has **a few million users** (most estimates land in the low millions), but it's impossible to know exactly. It's one of the most popular beginner-friendly Linux distros, with a strong, growing community.
