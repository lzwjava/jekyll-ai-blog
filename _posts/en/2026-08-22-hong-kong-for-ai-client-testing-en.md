Question: Should I go to Hong Kong to find AI/software clients?

Answer:

For your background, **yes, Hong Kong is one of the better places to test an AI consulting/client path**, but I would treat it as a **market exploration + sales trip**, not an immediate relocation.

Your profile fits HK unusually well:

* 11 years software engineering
* full-stack + backend
* recent LLM training/inference experience
* can communicate with Chinese companies
* can deliver AI automation, agents, RAG, fine-tuning, internal tools

Hong Kong has a different market from mainland China:

* Many companies have money but lack AI engineering talent.
