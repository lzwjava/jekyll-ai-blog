It sounds like your Ultra Mobile plan is expiring soon and you want to keep your service going. Based on the page you shared, here's what to do:

**You want the Data Pass** — specifically either:
- **$5 for 500MB / 1 day**, or
- **$10 for 1GB / 7 days**

But more importantly, if your **monthly plan** is about to stop, you likely need to **renew your plan**, not just buy a data pass. Data passes are add-ons, not plan replacements.

---

## What you should do

1. **Go to [Manage Plan](https://my.ultramobile.com/account/plan)** — check if your plan is expiring and renew it there.

2. **Enable Auto Renew** at [Auto Recharge](https://my.ultramobile.com/account/auto-recharge) — this prevents your plan from stopping automatically each month.

3. **Or recharge your PayGo Wallet** at [Recharge PayGo Wallet](https://my.ultramobile.com/account/paygo) if you're on a pay-as-you-go setup.

---

**Don't just buy a Data Pass** — that only gives you data for 1 or 7 days and won't keep your phone number or plan active long-term.

Go to **Manage Plan → Renew** first. That's the right fix.
