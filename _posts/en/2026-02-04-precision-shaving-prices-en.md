Harry's razors have built a strong reputation for several reasons:

**Quality at a good price point** - They use German-engineered blades manufactured in their own factory (they bought a blade factory in Eisfeld, Germany). This vertical integration lets them control quality while keeping costs lower than traditional brands like Gillette.

**Blade sharpness and design** - Their blades are made from Swedish steel and go through a precision honing process. The cartridges typically have 5 blades with a flex hinge and lubricating strip, which helps provide a close, comfortable shave.

**Ergonomic handles** - The weighted, rubberized handles are designed for good grip and control, making them comfortable to use.

**Direct-to-consumer model** - By selling primarily online and cutting out retail middlemen, they can offer better value. This was part of the original disruption strategy when they launched.

**Simple, effective approach** - They focus on doing the basics really well rather than adding gimmicks. No vibrating handles or unnecessary features - just well-made razors at reasonable prices.

The brand has become popular because they found a sweet spot between expensive cartridge razors from legacy brands and ultra-cheap disposables, offering quality comparable to premium options but at a more accessible price. Many people find they get a shave quality similar to much more expensive razors.
