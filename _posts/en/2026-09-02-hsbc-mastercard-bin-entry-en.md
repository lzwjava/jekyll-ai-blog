```text

```

### Important

* **BIN = first 6 digits of the card number**
* It is **not** your CVV/security code.
* It is **not** your HSBC account number.
* It is **not** the last 6 digits.
* If your Mastercard is `XXXXXX...`, enter the first six digits exactly as printed on the card.
* Don't send me your actual card number.
