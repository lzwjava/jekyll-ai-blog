# CR2032 Battery — Comprehensive Introduction

## 1. What Is the CR2032?

A button cell (also called a watch battery or coin battery) is a small battery made of a single electrochemical cell, shaped as a squat cylinder typically 5 to 25 mm in diameter and 1 to 6 mm high — resembling a button.

The CR2032 is a lithium coin cell battery that has become the standard power source for small electronic devices. Those letters and numbers are not random — they tell you exactly what you're getting.

A CR2032 battery is a **(C)** lithium chemistry battery with a **(R)** round shape, a diameter of **(20)** 20 millimeters, and a height of **(32)** 3.2 millimeters.

**CR** stands for Lithium Manganese Dioxide (LiMnO₂) chemistry and follows the IEC (International Electrotechnical Commission) naming standard for button cell batteries. The **C** indicates manganese dioxide as the positive electrode material, and **R** denotes a round (cylindrical) shape.

## 2. Key Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Diameter | 20 mm |
| Height (Thickness) | 3.2 mm |
| Nominal Voltage | 3.0 V |
| Typical Capacity | ~220 mAh |
| Chemistry | Lithium Manganese Dioxide (LiMnO₂) |
| Self-discharge Rate | < 1% per year |
| Operating Temperature | -20°C to +60°C (varies by brand) |
| Shelf Life | Up to 10 years (stored properly) |

It has a nominal voltage output of 3V, although it starts a little high at 3.2V and slowly drifts down to 2.5V as it is used. The capacity is 220 mAh assuming a draw of constant 0.2 mA.

At 25°C, CR2032 3V button batteries lose less than 1% of their charge each year. When stored correctly, they can last up to 5 years in service.

---

## 3. Voltage Behavior

The performance curve of CR2032 batteries is different from regular AA or AAA batteries. Instead of gradually getting weaker, coin cells maintain steady voltage for most of their life, then drop off quickly when they're nearly dead. This means your device works normally right up until the battery needs replacement.

CR2032 batteries with voltages between 2.7V and 2.9V are still usable, while readings below 2.7V indicate the end of their life.

## 4. Common Applications

The CR2032 battery is used in a wide variety of devices and applications including computer motherboards (CMOS/BIOS), car key fobs, watches, calculators, PDAs, electronic organizers, garage door openers, toys, games, door chimes, pet collars, LED lights, pedometers, calorie counters, stopwatches, and medical devices.

Fitness trackers, smartwatches, and other wearable devices also use CR2032 batteries. Their steady voltage helps with clear communication and data gathering.

## 5. Equivalent / Alternative Names

The CR2032 battery is also known as the **5004LC, BR2032, DL2032, ECR2032, KCR2032, and KECR2032**, depending on the manufacturer.

## 6. Comparing Similar Coin Cells

Other lithium coin cell batteries may have a similar voltage, diameter, or height as the CR2032 but may not work in devices that require a CR2032. For example, a **CR2016** has the same diameter and voltage as the CR2032, but has half the height. A **CR1632** would have the same height and voltage but a diameter 4mm too short.

A quick comparison of 2032-family alternatives:

| Battery | Voltage | Rechargeable | Capacity | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CR2032** | 3.0 V | No | ~220 mAh | Most common, long shelf life |
| **BR2032** | 2.8–3.0 V | No | ~190 mAh | Better for extreme temperatures, extra-long shelf life |
| **LIR2032** | 3.6–3.7 V | Yes | ~50–70 mAh | Rechargeable but higher voltage — not always compatible |
| **ML2032** | 3.0 V | Yes | ~65 mAh | Rechargeable; designed to be charged by the device itself |

If you need a non-rechargeable 2032 battery and are not sure which one to take, the CR2032 battery from reputable brands with good reviews is almost always the right choice.

## 7. Can It Be Recharged?

The regular CR2032 battery **cannot** be recharged. It is a lithium primary battery meant for single use. Attempting to recharge it can lead to leakage, rupture, or even explosion due to the buildup of gases inside the battery casing.

You can safely replace most CR2032 coin cell batteries with a BR2032 type lithium battery. You cannot, however, always substitute an LiR2032 rechargeable battery for either a CR2032 or BR2032 type without running safety risks, including overheating and fire.

## 8. Storage Best Practices

Store CR2032 batteries in a cool, dry place (10°C–25°C) in their original packaging to prevent short circuits. Avoid humidity, stacking loose batteries, or storing near metal objects. Do not refrigerate, as condensation can cause damage.

If you keep spare CR2032 batteries around, use the "first in, first out" principle — mark batteries with purchase dates and use older ones first.

---

## 9. Safety Warnings

Button cells are **dangerous for small children**. When swallowed, they can cause severe internal burns and significant injury or death. The battery reacts with bodily fluids, creating a circuit that can release an alkali strong enough to burn through human tissue, and can create a hole in the esophagus lining in as little as two hours.

Keep them away from children to prevent accidental ingestion.

---

## 10. Proper Disposal

To safely dispose of non-rechargeable CR2032 batteries, recycle at designated facilities, check local regulations, and avoid regular trash disposal. Non-rechargeable batteries contain harmful substances like lithium, which, if disposed of improperly, can leak into the environment, causing soil and water pollution.

---

## 11. Recommended Brands

In testing, **Murata, Panasonic, and Maxell** CR2032 cells consistently outperform generic brands in stability and lifespan. Generic or no-name CR2032 cells often have higher internal resistance and unstable voltage under load, leading to resets, wireless dropouts, or early low-battery alerts.
