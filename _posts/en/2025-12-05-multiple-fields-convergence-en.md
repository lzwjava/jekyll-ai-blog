**Question:** What does "convergence" mean?

**Answer:**

Convergence generally means **the process of coming together, meeting, or uniting toward a common point or result**. The exact meaning depends on the context:

| Context              | Meaning of Convergence                                                                 | Example                                      |
|----------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| General/Everyday     | Two or more things moving toward the same point or becoming similar                   | “The convergence of roads at the city center” |
| Mathematics          | A sequence or series approaching a specific limit value as it progresses               | The series ∑(1/n²) converges to π²/6 |
| Infinite series/sequences | The partial sums approach a finite limit                                            | Converges vs. diverges               |
| Technology/Media     | The merging of previously separate industries or technologies (e.g., telecom, internet, TV) | “Media convergence” – phone, TV, and internet on one device |
| Economics            | Poorer economies growing faster than rich ones, causing incomes to converge over time  | Sigma-convergence, beta-convergence  |
| Biology/Evolution    | Different species developing similar traits independently (e.g., wings in birds and bats) | Convergent evolution                |
| Optics/Photography   | Light rays or lenses focusing to a single point                                        | Lens convergence                     |
| Machine Learning     | An optimization algorithm (e.g., gradient descent) reaching a minimum loss            | “The model has converged”            |

In short: **convergence = things that were separate or different becoming closer, united, or reaching the same endpoint**.
