**Question:** How does Neusoft (东软集团) work — its business model, revenue segments, and key operations?

**Answer:**

Neusoft (东软集团股份有限公司, ticker: 600718.SS) is one of China's largest IT solution companies. Here's how it works:

---

**🏢 Background**

Founded in 1991 and headquartered in Shenyang, China, Neusoft operates globally, serving over 100 car models in more than 100 countries and maintaining significant presence in healthcare, automotive, and enterprise IT sectors.

---

**💼 Business Model**

Neusoft's business model revolves around offering customized IT services and solutions, which include software development, system integration, and consulting services. The company generates revenue through service contracts, software licensing, and consulting fees. It leverages technologies like big data, AI, and IoT to help clients enhance competitiveness.

---

**📊 Four Main Revenue Segments (FY2025)**

In 2025, the revenue of the four sectors reached:
- **Healthcare & Social Security** — CNY 1.902 billion (gross margin: 34.98%)
- **Smart Vehicle Connectivity** — CNY 4.687 billion (gross margin: 13.62%) — now the largest segment (~40% of total)
- **Smart Cities** — CNY 2.1 billion (gross margin: 21.88%)
- **Enterprise Connectivity & Others** — CNY 3.348 billion (gross margin: 26.84%)

---

**🔑 Key Product Lines**

The company's main product lines include:
- **Healthcare**: hospital management systems, medical imaging, automated medical analysis and study cloud platforms
- **Automotive**: intelligent cockpit systems, vehicle computing platforms, in-vehicle infotainment, cluster instruments, navigation, ADAS/ADS, EV powertrain systems, and vehicle-cloud platforms
- **Enterprise IT**: data integration, automated testing, BPO, product engineering, cloud services, human capital management, financial management, network security

---

**🤖 AI Strategy**

Neusoft has integrated AI technology with solutions to accelerate "AI+" empowerment in industrial transformation, launching its Large Language Model Systems Engineering (LLM-SE) which has been used in healthcare, human resources and social security, medical insurance, media, finance, operation & maintenance, and other fields.

In 2025, the company signed new contracts for vertical domain AI applications worth CNY 1.073 billion, a year-on-year increase of about 58%; contracts in AI+healthcare reached CNY 691 million, up about 42% year-on-year.

---

**🏥 Healthcare Reach**

Neusoft ranks among the top three in China's overall healthcare data intelligence solutions market and holds the number one market share in the clinical data intelligence segment. It serves over 20,000 clients across more than 110 countries and regions, providing medical data services for approximately 700 million people.

---

**⚠️ Current Challenge**

Smart vehicle connectivity — the largest business segment — has the lowest gross margin (13.62%). This is a core reason for Neusoft's profit problems: revenue is growing, but the revenue structure has become heavier, dragging down the overall gross margin. In FY2025, total revenues reached CNY 12.04 billion but net income was a loss of CNY 342.85 million.
