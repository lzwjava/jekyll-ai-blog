Question: How can a mainland Chinese citizen get the right to work in Hong Kong?

Answer:

A mainland Chinese citizen **cannot simply move to Hong Kong and work** with a normal 港澳通行证 (Two-way Permit). You need an **employment visa / entry permit** or another immigration status that gives you the right to work. ([Immigration Department][1])

The main routes are:

## 1. Get hired by a Hong Kong company → ASMTP (most common)

The usual route for mainland professionals is:

**Admission Scheme for Mainland Talents and Professionals (ASMTP)**.

Flow:

```
Hong Kong company offers you a job
        |
        v
Company sponsors your application
        |
        v
Hong Kong Immigration approves
        |
        v
You get employment entry permit
        |
        v
You can live + work in HK
```

Requirements usually include:

* Relevant professional skills / experience
* A genuine Hong Kong job offer
* Salary matching Hong Kong market level
* Your skills should be valuable and not easily filled by local workforce

A degree is normally expected, but strong technical experience and achievements can sometimes help. ([Immigration Department][2])

For an experienced AI/software engineer, this is probably the most realistic employment route.

---

## 2. Start a Hong Kong company → Entrepreneur / Investment route

You can create a Hong Kong company, but:

**owning a company does not automatically give you a work visa.**

You need to show that your business is real and contributes to Hong Kong, for example:

* business plan
* customers/contracts
* investment
* revenue
* hiring
* technology contribution

Entrepreneurs can apply under investment-related admission arrangements if they can demonstrate economic contribution. ([HK Engage][3])

A realistic example:

```
Create HK Limited
        |
        v
Get HK software clients
        |
        v
Show business activity
        |
        v
Apply as entrepreneur
        |
        v
Operate company in HK
```

---

## 3. Top Talent Pass Scheme (if you qualify)

Hong Kong also has talent schemes. Some mainland residents may qualify depending on income, university background, achievements, etc. ([Immigration Department][1])

This can be attractive because it is not tied to one employer in the same way.

---

## 4. Technology Talent Admission Scheme (TechTAS)

For AI/software R&D roles:

TechTAS is designed for technology talent working in areas like:

* artificial intelligence
* data analytics
* cybersecurity
* robotics
* fintech

But normally:

```
HK technology company
        |
        v
gets TechTAS quota
        |
        v
sponsors your employment visa
```

The company needs eligibility/quota, and you need to be doing qualifying technology R&D work. ([Immigration Department][4])

---

## For your situation (AI engineer + software consulting)

A practical path:

```
Step 1:
Visit HK frequently using 港澳通行证

Step 2:
Find HK clients:
- AI consulting
- LLM deployment
- software engineering

Step 3:
Either:
A. Join HK company → ASMTP
or
B. Build HK company → entrepreneur route

Step 4:
Get employment/residence status
```

Your profile (full-stack + LLM training + AI infrastructure experience) fits better than a normal software developer because Hong Kong is actively trying to attract technology talent. ([Immigration Department][4])

One important point: **you can own a Hong Kong company while living in mainland China, but you cannot use that company alone to freely work in Hong Kong without the appropriate immigration approval.** ([Immigration Department][1])
