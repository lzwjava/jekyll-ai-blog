**Question:** How do we understand that the centralized and unified leadership of the Party Central Committee is the Party's highest principle (党中央集中统一领导是党的最高原则)?

---**Question:** How do we understand that the centralized and unified leadership of the Party Central Committee is the Party's highest principle (党中央集中统一领导是党的最高原则)?

---

**Answer:**

This topic comes from **Chapter 3** of the textbook: *"坚持党的全面领导"*. It is one of the most politically important and heavily tested topics in the entire course.

---

### The Official Statement (官方表述)

The 20th Party Congress Report states: "The most essential characteristic of socialism with Chinese characteristics is the leadership of the Chinese Communist Party; the greatest advantage of the socialist system with Chinese characteristics is the leadership of the Chinese Communist Party; the Chinese Communist Party is the highest political leadership force; and upholding the centralized and unified leadership of the Party Central Committee is the highest political principle."

---

### The Core Textbook Phrase

Xi Jinping stated:
> **"坚持党的领导，首先是坚持党中央权威和集中统一领导，这是党的领导的最高原则，任何时候任何情况下都不能含糊、不能动摇。"**

Translation: Upholding Party leadership means first and foremost upholding the authority and centralized unified leadership of the Party Central Committee — this is the highest principle of Party leadership, and must never be blurred or shaken under any circumstances or at any time.

---

### How to Understand It — 4 Angles

---

**① 从地位理解 — Understanding Its Position**

The 20th Party Congress made clear that the most essential characteristic of socialism with Chinese characteristics is CCP leadership, the greatest advantage of the socialist system with Chinese characteristics is CCP leadership, and the CCP is the highest political leadership force — upholding the centralized and unified leadership of the Party Central Committee is the highest political principle.

In other words, within the entire political system, Party Central Committee leadership sits at the top of everything — above all government organs, military, and social organizations.

---

**② 从功能理解 — Understanding Its Function**

The Party Central Committee is the brain and command center; the decision-making power over the Party's and the country's major policies rests with the Party Central Committee. Any Party organization and member must uphold the Party Central Committee's supreme authority through concrete actions, and must submit to the Party Central Committee's centralized and unified leadership. The centralized and unified leadership of the Party Central Committee is the solid support for the entire Party and the people when storms strike, the reliable guarantee for overcoming all difficulties and risks on the road forward, and the key to the Party maintaining unity and strong combat effectiveness and achieving continued victory.

---

**③ 从历史理解 — Understanding from History**

The 105-year struggle of the CCP proves: whenever the Party Central Committee's authority and centralized unified leadership were well upheld, the Party's cause flourished; whenever they were not, the Party's cause suffered setbacks. Before the Zunyi Conference, the Party was not yet mature — from Chen Duxiu to Wang Ming, none could serve as the core of leadership, and no mature Party Central Committee was formed, which was an important reason why the Party and the people's cause repeatedly suffered setbacks in the early revolution and even faced the danger of failure. After the Zunyi Conference, because Mao Zedong served as the leadership core, the Party formed a stable and mature central leadership collective and achieved victory in the Chinese revolution.

The lesson of history is clear: **a strong, stable core of leadership = the Party succeeds; no stable core = the Party fails.**

---

**④ 从新时代理解 — Understanding in the New Era**

Since the 18th Party Congress, the fundamental reason why the Party and the country's cause has achieved historic achievements and undergone historic transformations is that Xi Jinping's position as the core of the Party Central Committee and the core of the whole Party was established, and Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era was established as the guiding ideology — thereby firmly upholding the authority and centralized unified leadership of the Party Central Committee with Xi Jinping as its core.

This is why the "Two Establishments" (两个确立) are said to have "decisive significance."

### 🔗 Three Concepts That Always Appear Together

These three are the **practical requirements** of upholding Party Central Committee leadership — exams test them together constantly:

| Concept | Chinese | Meaning |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Four Consciousnesses | 四个意识 | Political, overall situation, core, and alignment consciousness |
| Four Confidences | 四个自信 | Path, theory, system, culture |
| Two Safeguards | 两个维护 | Safeguard Xi's core position; safeguard Party Central Committee authority |

The logical chain is:
```
党中央集中统一领导（最高原则）
        ↓
两个确立（前提条件）
        ↓
两个维护（行动要求）
        ↓

```

### 🏛️ Simple Analogy to Understand It

> Think of the Party as a **large orchestra**:
> - Each instrument (ministry, region, army) plays its own part
> - Without a **conductor** (Party Central Committee), every section plays on its own → chaos
> - With a strong conductor → unified, powerful, beautiful performance
> - The conductor's authority is not about personal power — it is what makes **collective coordination possible**

### ⚠️ Common Misunderstanding to Avoid

| Wrong idea | Correct understanding |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Centralized leadership = one person dictating | ❌ It is collective leadership with a stable core |
| It contradicts democracy | ❌ It is democratic centralism — democracy within the Party feeds into centralized decision-making |
| It only matters at the top | ❌ Every Party member and organization must submit to it in all circumstances |
