**Question:** What is the Technology Talent Admission Scheme (TechTAS) and how does it work?

**Answer:**

TechTAS is a Hong Kong immigration program designed to attract non-local technology talent. Here's an overview of the key aspects:

**What it is**

TechTAS provides a fast-track arrangement for eligible companies to admit non-local technology talent to undertake research and development (R&D) work in Hong Kong. Eligible companies must first apply for a quota from the Innovation and Technology Commission (ITC), and a company allotted with a quota can then sponsor an eligible person to apply for an employment visa/entry permit.

**Who qualifies (applicants)**

The applicant must be engaged principally in conducting R&D in a technology area, and must hold a STEM degree from a well-recognised university ranked among the top 100 for STEM-related subjects according to QS, Shanghai Ranking, Times Higher Education, or US News rankings. Those with a Bachelor's degree need at least one year of relevant work experience, while Master's or Doctoral degree holders have no such requirement.

**Initial stay & extensions**

Persons admitted under TechTAS are normally granted an initial stay of 36 months on employment condition (or the duration of the employment contract, whichever is shorter). Extensions follow a 3-2 year pattern.

**Top-tier pathway**

Applicants who have been employed under TechTAS for at least two years and have an assessable income for salaries tax of not less than HK$2 million in the previous tax year may apply for top-tier status, which grants a 5-year extension on time limitation only — without other conditions of stay.

**Right of Abode**

Persons admitted under TechTAS who have ordinarily resided in Hong Kong for a continuous period of not less than seven years may apply for the right of abode.

**Processing time**

It normally takes two weeks to process employment visa/entry permit applications upon receipt of all required documents and the relevant application fee.

**Exclusions**

TechTAS does not apply to nationals of Afghanistan, Cuba, and the Democratic People's Republic of Korea.
