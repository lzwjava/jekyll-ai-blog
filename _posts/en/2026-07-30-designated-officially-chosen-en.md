**Designated** means **officially chosen, appointed, assigned, or specified for a particular purpose or role**.

Examples:

* **A designated driver** is a person chosen to drive so others can avoid driving after drinking alcohol.
* **Please park in the designated area.** (The area has been specifically assigned for parking.)
* **She is the designated team leader.** (She has been officially appointed as the leader.)

**Synonyms:** assigned, appointed, selected, specified, authorized.

**Example sentence:**

> "Students must sit in their designated seats during the exam."

In simple words, **designated = officially chosen or set aside for a specific purpose**.
