Question: What are the most impressive and crazy things people use OpenClaw (Claude Code) for? Building large apps, running many tasks simultaneously?

Answer:## The Craziest Things People Do With OpenClaw

OpenClaw has exploded to nearly **200K GitHub stars** and people are doing absolutely wild things with it. Here's the best of it:

---

### 🚗 #1 — Negotiated a Car Purchase Autonomously

One user set up their agent to negotiate with multiple car dealers simultaneously via browser, email, and iMessage at the same time. The result was $4,200 in savings on their car purchase. The AI switched between web browsers, messaging apps, and authentication systems — all without human involvement.

---

### 🌙 #2 — Coded All Night While the Developer Slept

One developer let their OpenClaw manage coding agents from 12:30 AM to 7:00 AM while they slept. The results were five bugs fixed and deployed automatically. What made it work was context — the AI had been watching the developer work all day and understood the full project history.

---

### 🛒 #3 — Full Autonomous Grocery Ordering

One user's OpenClaw managed grocery ordering after their cleaning lady sent a shopping request via message. The agent logged into the supermarket website using shared credentials from 1Password and placed the order automatically.

---

### 💸 #4 — Replaced $7,400/month in Business Software

One venture capital firm was paying $6,000 for Affinity and $1,400 for Monday.com — and replaced both with a custom OpenClaw setup, saving nearly $90K a year.

---

### 📧 #5 — Mass Email Unsubscribe at Scale

One user automated unsubscribing from 500+ emails without any manual work. The agent read, identified, clicked unsubscribe links, and confirmed — across hundreds of emails autonomously.

---

### ✈️ #6 — Flight Search Tool Built in 20 Minutes

Developers built flight search tools through messaging apps in under 20 minutes. Text your OpenClaw "find me cheapest flight to Tokyo next Friday" and it actually searches and returns results.

---

### 📊 #7 — Monitoring 6 Platforms Simultaneously

People have their agent monitoring six platforms simultaneously — responding on their behalf, filing things away, and surfacing what matters — across Telegram, Discord, X, WhatsApp, Email, and their file system.

---

### 🌍 The Scale of the Ecosystem

The ClawHub public registry now hosts over 13,700 community-built skills as of late February 2026. Skills range from Google Ads management, triathlon training plans, ADHD daily planning, to tamper-resistant audit watchdogs.

---

### ⚠️ The Crazy Side Has a Dark Side Too

Security firms including CrowdStrike and Bitdefender have warned about tool poisoning and malicious skills being uploaded to the registry — some contain data-exfiltration scripts. A prompt injection via Slack could theoretically tell OpenClaw to wipe your entire hard drive or send all secrets from your .env file.

So the power is real — but so is the risk if you install random community skills without vetting them.
