**Question:** What is EDPS Systems Limited, the Hong Kong-based company?

**Answer:**

EDPS Systems Limited was established in December 1974 by a group of computer specialists whose goal was to maximize work efficiency through effective computer applications.

Since its founding, EDPS has grown into a leading IT contract staff provider in Hong Kong, specializing in delivering high-quality IT professionals to meet client needs.

Here's a quick overview:

**Core Business**
EDPS is a premier provider of IT contract staffing (T-contract) services in Hong Kong, and has been selected by the government as one of the suppliers for IT contract staff services for 50 years. The current T-contract (T26) is a 48-month term that started on February 1, 2023.

**Scale & Workforce**
EDPS employs approximately 1,000 computer professionals, supported by a strong team of project managers, system analysts, programmers, and technical support experts.

**Services Offered**
Services include IT secondment, where highly skilled professionals are placed directly into client teams to drive projects to success, as well as post-engagement staffing solutions.

**Location & Contact**
The company is headquartered at Room 1202, 12/F, Harcourt House, 39 Gloucester Road, Wanchai, Hong Kong.

**Community & Culture**
EDPS also runs initiatives such as the EDPS Innovation Scholarship, the EDPS Academy for staff learning, and long service award ceremonies, reflecting a commitment to both employee development and community engagement.
