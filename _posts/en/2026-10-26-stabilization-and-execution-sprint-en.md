## 4. What not to do during these three weeks

- Don't make a master's degree your immediate escape route. Keep the academic path alive, but don't take on a large financial commitment to improve your resume while unemployed.
- Don't spend months building Fun Live before validating demand. Your existing product experience is an advantage; use it as evidence of your ability to build products. Revisit a rebuild when cash flow is more stable.
- Don't treat consulting and employment as mutually exclusive. A job can pay the bills while a small consulting business creates customer relationships and future independence.
- Don't confuse learning with progress. Reading about models is useful, but a benchmark, a merged contribution, a deployed service, an interview, or a paid pilot produces external evidence.
- Don't decide to sell your house purely because you feel overwhelmed. Equally, don't keep it solely because selling feels like failure. Protecting your family's future matters more than preserving a past investment.
