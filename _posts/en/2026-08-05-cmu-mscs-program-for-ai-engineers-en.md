Carnegie Mellon University MS in Computer Science (MSCS) is one of the strongest professional CS master's programs in the world, especially for **AI, systems, and software engineering**. ([CMU Computer Science][1])

For your background (11 years engineering, LLM training, vLLM/SGLang interest, GPT-2 training, no traditional bachelor's degree), this program is interesting but has some major constraints.

## 1. What is the program?

CMU MSCS is:

* A **coursework-focused master's**
* Usually completed in **3 semesters**
* Flexible curriculum: you design your course plan with an advisor
* Research is optional, not the main purpose ([CMU Computer Science][1])

Graduation requirements:

* 96–108 units of qualifying master's courses (~8 courses)
* AI course requirement
* Systems course requirement
* Theory foundations course requirement
* Maintain 3.0 QPA ([CMU Computer Science][2])

Typical CMU courses you might care about:

```
AI:
- Deep Learning
- Advanced NLP
- Machine Learning

Systems:
- Advanced Operating Systems
- Distributed Systems
- Computer Architecture

Theory:
- Algorithms
- Complexity
```

For someone wanting LLM infrastructure, the systems + AI combination is exactly aligned.

## 2. Admission requirements

The biggest issue:

CMU expects:

> bachelor's degree + strong mathematical/programming background

They explicitly say a CS bachelor's is not required, but a technical undergraduate background is strongly helpful. ([CMU Computer Science][1])

Application:

* Transcripts
* Statement of purpose
* Recommendations
* English test (TOEFL/IELTS if applicable)
* GRE is **strongly recommended**, especially if math ability is not obvious ([CMU Computer Science][3])
