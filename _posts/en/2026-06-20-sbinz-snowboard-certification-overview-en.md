**Question:** What is the SBINZ certificate from New Zealand?

**Answer:**

SBINZ stands for **Snowboard Instruction New Zealand** — it's the snowboard instructor certification body operating under the **NZSIA (New Zealand Snowsports Instructors Alliance)**.

SBINZ is one of the four divisions within the NZSIA, responsible for the education and certification of snowboard instructors in New Zealand. Through international relationships, they've built a qualification framework that caters to New Zealand's environment while also preparing instructors to teach internationally across diverse cultures and conditions.

### Certification Levels

**Level 1** — Entry-level qualification, aimed at teaching domestically, focused on working with beginners.

**Level 2** — More focused on instructors working internationally; equivalent to many Level 2 certifications around the world.

**Level 3** — Renowned for extremely high standards in both riding and teaching; produces instructors capable of performing in any condition, at any level.

Beyond these, SBINZ also offers a **Children's Teaching Certification**, a **Park and Pipe Certification**, and a **Trainer's Certification**.

### International Recognition

SBINZ is an active member of **ISIA** (the International Snowboard Instructors' Alliance), which regulates equivalency between different countries' certifications and allows members to work as snowboard instructors internationally.

### Progression Requirements

Courses must be completed in structured progression — you need to pass each level before moving on to the next. For example, you must hold a Level 1 certification before enrolling in a Level 2 course. Higher-level courses may also have additional prerequisites like specific module completions.
