**California Science and Technology University (CSTU)** is a small, employment-oriented private school located in Silicon Valley. Its credentials are compliant but not high-tier. Its biggest selling points are its location and practical courses; its biggest risk is that **claims about CSCSE (Chinese Ministry of Education) certification lack official confirmation**, and it has a record of an administrative penalty from California’s BPPE.

### ✅ Accreditation and compliance: legitimate, but with flaws

- **U.S. accreditation**: It holds **ACCSC** (Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges) accreditation, which is recognized by the U.S. Department of Education. The school is also approved to operate by California’s BPPE.
- **CSCSE certification (key uncertainty)**: Some Chinese-language promotions (e.g., Xiaohongshu, WeChat public accounts) claim its degrees can be certified by CSCSE, but **there is no authoritative confirmation on the CSCSE official website in the search results**. Such claims mostly come from agents or promotional articles. **You must verify this yourself on the CSCSE official website**; do not rely solely on the school’s or agents’ claims.
- **Administrative penalty record**: During a 2023 unannounced inspection, California’s BPPE found **noncompliant recordkeeping** (failure to provide student information supporting STRF reporting). The initial fine was $1,001, later settled at $500. This does not mean the school is illegal, but it indicates past issues with administrative compliance.

### 🎓 Positioning and scale: a small Silicon Valley applied-sciences school

- **Basic positioning**: Founded in 2011 in Milpitas, Silicon Valley. It focuses on **computer science (AI/data science direction) and business administration** at the bachelor’s/master’s levels, aimed at working professionals.
- **Facilities and scale**: Classrooms are in an office area, **no housing is provided**, the student-faculty ratio cap is 35:1, and the scale is very small.
- **Course characteristics**: Student reviews generally mention that professors have industry experience, courses are practical, and the school is close to Silicon Valley companies. However, such reviews come from Google Maps with limited sample size, so their reference value is limited.

### 💡 Key differences from Swiss Business School (SBS)

- **Accreditation level**: SBS has **Swiss official SAC institutional accreditation**, a clearer framework; CSTU mainly has U.S. **ACCSC vocational accreditation**, which is a different level.
- **CSCSE risk**: Information about SBS’s CSCSE recognition is relatively clearer; for CSTU, **you need to verify the certification claims on the official website yourself**—this is the biggest decision risk.
- **Reputation**: SBS has CEO Magazine online MBA rankings and QS five-star rating for online learning; CSTU **has no mainstream business rankings**, and its reputation is limited to the local Silicon Valley applied-sciences circle.
