## Zhang Xuefeng's Death — What Happened

Zhang Xuefeng (张雪峰), whose real name is Zhang Zibiao (张子彪), passed away on **March 24, 2026**, at the age of **41**. He died in Suzhou, Jiangsu Province, China.

### Cause of Death

Zhang died following a **sudden cardiac arrest** (心源性猝死 — cardiogenic sudden death). His company, Suzhou Fengxue Weilai Education Technology Co., confirmed his death in an official obituary issued on Tuesday evening.

### Background on His Life and Career

Born in May 1984 in Fuyu County, Heilongjiang Province, Zhang rose to prominence as a tutor for college and postgraduate entrance exams, and later became a prominent online influencer widely recognized for his expertise in college application and postgraduate admission counseling.

He had amassed a following of 30 million fans and built a fortune of at least US$100 million, becoming the country's most successful extracurricular tutor.
