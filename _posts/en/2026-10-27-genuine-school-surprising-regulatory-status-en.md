### 3. What the school claims vs. what it is

Its headline claims are *"Accredited and recognised by ACBSP and IACBE"* plus **eduQua**.

### 4. Assessment of authenticity — the important caveat

- **Authentic website, authentic organization:** no impersonation. SWISS UMEF is the real, long-running private business school in Geneva (formerly *"UMEF University of Management and Economics"*).
- **But it is NOT a Swiss state-recognized university.** In Switzerland, "university" status and degrees are regulated by the **State Secretariat for Education, Research and Innovation (SERI)** and the cantons. SWISS UMEF is a **private institution under private (commercial) law** — it does not appear among Switzerland's recognized universities and **cannot award Swiss federal/cantonal degrees**. That is exactly why the current branding carefully says *"private higher education institution founded in accordance with Swiss law"* — and has dropped the word "University" from its name (Swiss authorities previously objected to the "University of Management and Economics" title).
- **The "accreditations" do not change that:**
  - **ACBSP / IACBE** are *US program-level business accreditors* recognized by **CHEA** in the US. They accredit individual business programs, and that recognition is not reciprocal with any Swiss state recognition.
  - **eduQua** is a Swiss quality label for **continuing-education providers (Weiterbildung)** — not a university accreditation.
  - The system is a *quality-assurance/marketing* layer, not a higher-education licensing body within the Swiss education system.

### Bottom line

- **Authenticity:** Yes — this is the real website of the genuine SWISS UMEF school in Geneva. Nothing points to a fake or cloned site.
- **Status — read carefully before enrolling:** it is a **private, non-state-recognized Swiss institution**. Its degrees are not Swiss federal degrees, may not be recognized for equivalency in your home country, and the "international accreditations" are US program-level business accreditations plus an eduQua continuing-ed label. The combination of a very recent domain (2022), Wix hosting, heavy "Accredited & Recognised" marketing, and AI degrees marketed to international students is a pattern worth extra scrutiny for credential recognition — independently verify degree recognition with your embassy and with SERI/your country's education authorities before paying any fees.
