## How I would approach the next 12 months

- Build: Publish a serious inference or model-training project with reproducible benchmarks.
- Apply: Target U.S. AI labs, infrastructure startups, and applied AI teams. Apply based on demonstrated capability rather than waiting for a degree.
- Network: Develop relationships with engineers, founders, and researchers through open source and technical writing.
- Evaluate education: Pursue a master's only if it provides a worthwhile combination of credential recognition, access to internships, local networks, and a viable U.S. work pathway.
- Keep building your company: Consulting and AI products can create revenue, customers, and relationships independently of whether a U.S. employer hires you.

One important reality: some U.S. positions have specific work-authorization requirements. A strong technical profile does not itself provide permission to work in the United States, so the employment and immigration pathways need to be evaluated separately.
