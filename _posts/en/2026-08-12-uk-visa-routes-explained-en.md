Question: UK visa routes: visitor, student, short-term study, technical work, spouse, talent — how do they work?

Answer:

The UK system is roughly divided into **visit, study, work, family, and talent routes**. The main ones you listed map like this: ([GOV.UK][1])

| Chinese concept | UK visa route | Main purpose | Can work? | Long-term path? |
| --------------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------- |
| 访问 | Standard Visitor visa | Tourism, business visits, visiting people | ❌ No normal employment | ❌ |
| 学生 | Student visa | University degree, master's, PhD | ✅ Limited hours during study | ✅ Possible |
| 短期学习 | Short-term Study visa | Mainly English courses 6–11 months | ❌ | ❌ |
| 技术工作 | Skilled Worker visa | UK employer hires skilled worker | ✅ Full job | ✅ ILR possible |
| 配偶 | Spouse/Partner visa | Join British citizen/settled partner | ✅ | ✅ |
| 人才 | Global Talent visa | Exceptional talent in tech/science/arts | ✅ No employer sponsorship needed | ✅ |

## 1. Visitor visa (访问)

For Chinese citizens, usually this is the **Standard Visitor visa**.

Allowed:

* tourism
* conferences
* meetings
* some business activities
* visiting friends/family

Not allowed:

* taking a UK job
* working remotely for a UK employer
* doing normal software engineering projects inside UK

You can usually stay up to 6 months per visit. ([UKCISA][2])

Example:

> Chinese software engineer goes London for 2 weeks to meet a startup → OK.

> Chinese engineer goes London for 6 months to write code for a UK company → not OK.

---

## 2. Student visa (学生)

This is the classic route:

China → UK university → Master's/PhD → Graduate visa → job.

Typical path:

```
Student visa
      |
      v
Graduate visa (post-study work)
      |
      v
Skilled Worker / Global Talent
      |
      v
ILR (permanent residence)
```

Student visa allows limited work during study, but the purpose must remain education. ([GOV.UK][1])

For AI/software people:

Good universities can create:

* UK network
* internships
* research connections
* employer opportunities

But cost is high:

* tuition often £25k–50k/year
* living cost £15k–20k/year

---

## 3. Short-term study visa (短期学习)

This is not a "cheap student visa".

Main use:

* English language course
* 6–11 months

Cannot:

* work
* convert easily into work route

([UKCISA][3])

Example:

```
China
 |
English course UK 9 months
 |
return
```

Not:

```
English course
 |
find software job
 |
stay
```

---

## 4. Skilled Worker visa (技术工作)

This is the normal foreign engineer route.

Need:

1. UK employer
2. Employer has sponsor licence
3. Eligible occupation
4. Salary requirement

Flow:

```
Find UK company
        |
        v
Job offer + sponsorship
        |
        v
Skilled Worker visa
        |
        v
Work 5 years
        |
        v
ILR
```

The UK has tightened some Skilled Worker rules recently, especially around salary and eligible occupations. ([House of Commons Library][4])

For software engineers:

Possible employers:

* Google UK
* DeepMind
* ARM
* fintech companies
* AI startups
* universities

---

## 5. Spouse visa (配偶)

If your spouse is:

* British citizen
* settled person (ILR)
* qualifying status

then spouse route exists.

This is family-based, not skill-based.

---

## 6. Global Talent visa (人才)

This is the interesting one for AI engineers.

Target:

* researchers
* AI engineers
* founders
* technical leaders

Advantages:

```
No employer sponsorship
|
change jobs freely
|
can start company
|
strong settlement route
```

Usually needs evidence:

* publications
* open source impact
* patents
* awards
* leading technical work
* recognition

The UK has been actively expanding Global Talent routes for researchers and engineers. ([Reuters][5])

For someone with:

* LLM training experience
* open-source projects
* AI products
* papers/blog visibility
* startup experience

this route is more interesting than a normal worker visa.

## Compared with US

|                     | UK               | US              |
| ------------------- | ---------------- | --------------- |
| Student → work      | easier           | harder          |
| Work visa lottery   | ❌ no H1B lottery | ✅ H1B lottery   |
| Talent route        | Global Talent    | O-1 / EB routes |
| Startup flexibility | better           | depends         |
| AI market size      | smaller          | much larger     |

For a senior AI engineer/founder profile, realistic UK paths are:

```
Option A:
AI Master's UK
      |
Graduate visa
      |
AI job
      |
Global Talent / Skilled Worker


Option B:
Direct Global Talent
      |
UK startup / research / consulting


Option C:
Find UK sponsor
      |
Skilled Worker
```

The UK is generally easier than the US for immigration mechanics, but the US has a much larger AI ecosystem. ([GOV.UK][1])
