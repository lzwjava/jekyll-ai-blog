**The real story:** When Lisa Su took over in 2014, AMD was near bankruptcy — losing money, market share collapsing against Intel on CPU and NVIDIA on GPU. Her playbook was exactly what you said: cut the org complexity (divested manufacturing via GlobalFoundries spinoff already done before her, but she cleaned up the rest), bet on great silicon (Zen architecture, RDNA, CDNA), and chase where customers actually needed help (hyperscalers needing alternatives to Intel EPYC is a perfect example of customer obsession).

The Zen CPU architecture starting from 2017 was the inflection point — genuinely better IPC than Intel at the time. Then EPYC for servers compounded it. Now the AI wave is the third act with Instinct MI series GPUs.

It's a textbook execution of "fewer bets, but make them great products." Classic focus strategy done right.
