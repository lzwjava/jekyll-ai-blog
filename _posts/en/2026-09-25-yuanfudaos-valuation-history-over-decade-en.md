**Question:** What is 猿辅导 (Yuanfudao)'s valuation history over the last decade?

**Answer:**

Here's a timeline of Yuanfudao's valuation journey from its founding through the present:

**2012–2016 — Early Stage**
Founded in 2012, Yuanfudao focused on K-12 online tutoring services. By the end of 2016, it had reaped RMB 120 million in annual revenue.

**2017 — ~$1 Billion (Unicorn)**
Yuanfudao completed a Series E round worth $120 million led by Warburg Pincus, pushing its valuation to $1 billion — a milestone reached in less than two years after launching its tutoring service. Tencent also participated through a prior $40 million Series D+ investment.

**2018 — ~$3 Billion**
Tencent led a $300 million round in late 2018 that also featured Warburg Pincus, Matrix Partners China, and IDG Capital, at a valuation of more than $3 billion.

**March 2020 — $7.8 Billion**
During the COVID-19 pandemic, Yuanfudao raised $1 billion in a Series G round led by Tencent and Hillhouse Capital, lifting its valuation to $7.8 billion.

**October 2020 — $15.5 Billion (Peak)**
Yuanfudao was valued at $15.5 billion following a $2.2 billion Series G funding round led by Tencent, DST Global, and Hillhouse Capital — positioning it as the most highly valued education technology company globally at that time.

**Early 2021 — Sought $20 Billion**
Yuanfudao was seeking fresh funding at a valuation of more than $20 billion, planning to raise at least $1 billion, and was eyeing a possible stock market debut in 2022.

**July 2021 — Regulatory Collapse**
China's Ministry of Education released the "Double Reduction Policy" on July 24, 2021, imposing harsh regulations that effectively marked the end of an era for the for-profit tutoring industry. More than $100 billion was wiped off China's education market cap industry-wide. Yuanfudao, being a private company, was not listed, but its IPO plans were shelved and new fundraising froze.

**2022–Present — Pivot & Undisclosed**
Post-crackdown, Yuanfudao pivoted its business model toward AI-powered learning hardware. Its fastest-growing market segment is now lower-tier cities, where learning device penetration has reached 28% in third-tier cities with unit sales increasing 120% year-over-year. No new public valuation has been disclosed since the 2020 peak.
