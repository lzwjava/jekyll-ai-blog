ChatGPT started gaining **persistent memory** only recently — it rolled out gradually from late 2023 to 2024, and by 2025 it became stable enough that:

* Memory can survive across chats.
* A *new* chat may reference information you shared in earlier sessions.
* The system can use your long-term preferences or background to give smoother answers.

Before this feature, every new conversation was “blank.” Even early 2024 versions had memory disabled for most users or limited to short experiments.

**So the behavior you’re seeing became common in 2024–2025**, after OpenAI fully released and improved long-term memory for users.
