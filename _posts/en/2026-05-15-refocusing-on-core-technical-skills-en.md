* **Daily 1–2 hours** for Java/Angular deep dive.
* **Weekly review** of what you’ve learned and apply it to work problems.
* **Set small measurable milestones** for both work and personal projects.
* **Keep AI and group work light**, maybe weekend or evening exploration.

This way, you regain control, feel progress, and avoid burnout.
