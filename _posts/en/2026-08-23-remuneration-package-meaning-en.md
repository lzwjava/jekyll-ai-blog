**Question: What does “remuneration package” mean?**

**Answer:**

“Remuneration package” means the **total compensation package** an employer offers an employee, not just the basic salary.

It usually includes:

* **Base salary** — monthly/yearly pay
* **Bonus** — performance bonus, annual bonus, commission
* **Benefits** — health insurance, dental insurance, life insurance
* **Retirement contributions** — MPF in Hong Kong, 401(k) in the US, pension schemes
* **Allowances** — housing allowance, transportation allowance, meal allowance, phone allowance
* **Stock/equity** — shares, RSUs, options (common in tech companies)
* **Paid leave** — annual leave, sick leave, holidays
* **Other perks** — training budget, flexible work, company car, relocation support

Example:

> Salary: HKD 50,000/month
> Annual bonus: 2 months salary
> MPF contribution
> Medical insurance
> 20 days annual leave

The whole thing together is the **remuneration package**.

In job applications (especially Hong Kong, UK, Singapore, Australia), when they ask:

> "What is your expected remuneration package?"

they are asking:

> "What total compensation do you expect?"

A good answer is usually a range:

> "My expected remuneration package is around HKD 800,000–1,000,000 per annum, depending on the overall benefits and role scope."

In Hong Kong tech jobs, recruiters often use **"remuneration package"** instead of just **"salary"** because the package can include bonus and benefits.
