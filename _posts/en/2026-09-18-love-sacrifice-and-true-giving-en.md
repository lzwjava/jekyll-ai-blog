**Question: What does “麦琪的礼物” (*The Gift of the Magi*) say?**

**Answer:**

The Gift of the Magi is a famous short story by O. Henry. Its core message is about **love, sacrifice, and the true meaning of giving**.

The story:

* A young poor couple, **Jim and Della**, love each other deeply.
* Christmas is coming, but they have very little money.
* Della wants to buy Jim a meaningful gift, so she sells her beautiful long hair and buys a chain for Jim's prized watch.
* Jim wants to buy Della a gift, so he sells his watch and buys expensive combs for Della's hair.
* When they exchange gifts, they discover that each person's gift is now unusable:

  * Della has no long hair for the combs.
  * Jim has no watch for the chain.
* But they realize the gifts are valuable because they represent their love and willingness to sacrifice for each other.

The famous idea of the story is:

> The greatest gift is not the object itself, but the love and sacrifice behind it.

The title refers to the **Magi (the three wise men)** from the Bible who brought gifts to the baby Jesus. O. Henry compares Jim and Della's sacrifice to those wise men, suggesting that **true wisdom is understanding love and generosity**.

A deeper interpretation:

1. **Love is measured by sacrifice**

   Jim and Della do not have wealth, but they are rich in their willingness to give up what is most valuable to them.

2. **Material value is not the same as human value**

   The watch chain and hair combs become useless objects, but the emotional meaning becomes priceless.

3. **Irony reveals a higher truth**

   The ending is ironic: their gifts "fail" practically, but succeed emotionally. The useless gifts become the most meaningful gifts.

4. **True wealth is emotional, not financial**

   O. Henry wrote this during a period when many people struggled economically. The story argues that dignity and love are not determined by money.

In one sentence:

**“麦琪的礼物” says that the greatest gifts are not expensive things; they are acts of love where people willingly give something precious for someone they care about.**
