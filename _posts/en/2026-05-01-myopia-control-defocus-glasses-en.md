Question: What are “离焦眼镜” (defocus glasses)?

Answer:

“离焦眼镜” usually refers to **defocus glasses**, a type of optical lens used mainly for **myopia (nearsightedness) control**, especially in children and young adults.

### What it means in simple terms

Normal glasses correct vision by focusing light **directly on the retina** so you see clearly.

Defocus glasses are designed differently:
They correct the central vision (so you can still see clearly), but they intentionally create a **slight myopic defocus in the peripheral vision** of the retina.

### Why they are used

The idea comes from eye-growth research:

* When the peripheral retina receives “myopic defocus” signals
* It may **slow down axial eye elongation**
* This can help **slow the progression of myopia**

### Common types / related products

* Defocus spectacle lenses (e.g., DIMS technology, HAL lenses)
* Myopia control lenses like “defocus incorporated multiple segments”
* Similar concept: orthokeratology (night lenses), but that is contact lens-based

### Important note

They are **not a cure for myopia**, only a method to **slow progression**. Effectiveness varies depending on:

* age
* degree of myopia
* daily wearing time
* eye behavior (near work, screen time, outdoor time)
