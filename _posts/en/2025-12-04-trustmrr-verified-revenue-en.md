Question: What is TrustMRR (from <https://trustmrr.com/>)?

Answer:

TrustMRR is a specialized online database that aggregates and displays verified monthly recurring revenue (MRR) data from startups. It serves as a transparent resource for tracking startup financial performance, with all revenue figures authenticated through direct integrations with payment platforms like Stripe, LemonSqueezy, and Polar—updated on an hourly basis.

### Key Features

- **Leaderboard**: Ranks top startups by all-time MRR, including details like founder names, current MRR totals, and month-over-month (MoM) growth rates. For example, leading entries include Arcads AI at $956,415 MRR (37% MoM growth) and TrimRx at $898,899 MRR (38% MoM growth).
- **Search and Stats**: Users can search for specific startups, view overall statistics, browse by categories, and explore acquisition histories.
- **Add Your Startup**: Founders can submit their own revenue data for verification and inclusion.
- **Buy/Sell Marketplace**: A new section for buying and selling Micro-SaaS businesses.
- **Interactive Game**: A fun "$1 vs $1,000,000" feature to visualize revenue scaling.

Primarily aimed at startup founders, investors, and entrepreneurs interested in benchmarking against real, verified metrics or scouting Micro-SaaS opportunities. The platform itself is listed on its leaderboard with $21,083 MRR and 15% MoM growth, indicating it's a bootstrapped SaaS tool.
