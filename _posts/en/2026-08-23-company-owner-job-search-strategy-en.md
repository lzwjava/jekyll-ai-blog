**Question: If I apply Hong Kong JobsDB jobs as an individual, but I also own a Hong Kong company, will employers consider me?**

Answer:

Yes, they can consider you, but your Hong Kong company is usually a **secondary advantage**, not a replacement for being a normal job candidate.

For JobsDB Hong Kong, most employers are hiring individuals directly (full-time employees). The platform has thousands of ICT/software roles, including backend, full-stack, AI, and system analyst positions. ([Jobsdb Hong Kong][1])

Your situation would look like:

```
Candidate:
Zhiwei Li
- 11 years software engineering
- Java/backend/full stack
- AI/LLM training experience
- IELTS 6.5
- Hong Kong company owner
- Open to employment / contract
```

This can actually be attractive because it signals:

* You understand business
* You can communicate with clients
* You may have entrepreneurial ability
* You can potentially help them with AI transformation projects

But there are some caveats.

## Case 1: Apply as normal employee (best chance)

Example:

> "I am applying for Senior Software Engineer / AI Engineer. I currently operate a Hong Kong registered technology company, but I am looking for a full-time role."

Most companies will treat you normally.

They mainly care:

* technical ability
* English communication
* salary expectation
* visa/work authorization

Your company ownership is just an interesting background.

---

## Case 2: Try to convert employment into B2B contract

Example:

> "Instead of employment, my Hong Kong company can provide software consulting services."

This is possible, especially for:

* startups
* SMEs
* AI consulting
* outsourced development
* system integration

Hong Kong has a strong SME and consulting ecosystem. Many companies already use external vendors.

However, large companies usually prefer:

```
Employee
    |
    + MPF
    + payroll
    + insurance
    + HR process
```

rather than:

```
Your HK Ltd
    |
    + invoice
    + service agreement
```

because procurement/legal departments are involved.

---

## Case 3: Use your HK company as credibility

This is probably your strongest angle.

Instead of:

> "I am a developer looking for a job."

Position yourself as:

> "AI engineer with experience building LLM systems and running a Hong Kong technology company."

For Hong Kong companies, this can be valuable.

Examples:

* build internal AI agents
* LLM knowledge systems
* automation
* software modernization
* cloud migration

Your background (training GPT-2, running AI infrastructure, building agents) is more differentiated than a normal full-stack applicant.

---

## Immigration point

Owning a Hong Kong company does **not automatically give you the right to work in Hong Kong**.

If you are a mainland Chinese citizen, you still need an appropriate immigration route, such as:

* employment visa
* entrepreneur/investment-related route
* other eligible schemes

Employers will often ask:

> "Do you have Hong Kong work authorization?"

Your answer matters.
