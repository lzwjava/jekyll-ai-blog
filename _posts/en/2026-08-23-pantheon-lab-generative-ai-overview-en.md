**Question:** What is Pantheon Lab, and what does this company do?

**Answer:**

Pantheon Lab is a **generative AI company** focused on digital human technology and advanced digital assistant solutions. Their tagline — *"Inherit · Connect · Imagine"* — reflects their mission to inspire creativity by using deep learning to humanize machines visually, virtually, and intellectually.

**Core Products & Services**

- **AIDOL Studio** — A SaaS platform that uses hyper-realistic virtual humans to automate video production with deep learning, cutting production time from days to minutes.
- **AIDOL** — An AI-powered digital assistant capable of online customer service, virtual assistant functions, and real-time data delivery.
- **Chat API** — Currently in waitlist stage for developers.

**Who They Serve**

Their clients span multiple industries and geographies, including notable names like HSBC, MTR (Hong Kong), Singapore Post, KFC (Taiwan), Hong Kong Airport, Toyota, and the National Gallery Singapore.

**Strategic Partners**

They work alongside major tech players including Apple, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Google Cloud, AWS, and Oracle, as well as regional accelerators like HKSTP and 500 Global.

**Certifications & Awards**

The company holds **ISO/IEC 27001** certification (information security). On the recognition front, they've won awards including the X-PITCH 2022 Silver Award (Startup of the Year), the KrAsia First Runner-Up, and multiple SciTech Challenge 2021 honors.

**In Short:** Pantheon Lab sits at the intersection of generative AI, synthetic media, and the metaverse — building tools that let businesses deploy lifelike digital humans for marketing, customer engagement, and brand automation.
