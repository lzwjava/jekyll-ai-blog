**Question:** What is an introduction to the MSI PRO B760M-E D5 (DDR5) motherboard?

**Answer:**

The MSI PRO B760M-E D5 is a budget-friendly Micro-ATX motherboard aimed at professionals and everyday builders who want a reliable, modern platform without overspending.

---

**Platform & CPU Support**

It uses the Intel B760 chipset with an LGA 1700 socket, supporting Intel Core 12th, 13th, and 14th Gen processors, as well as Pentium Gold and Celeron processors.

---

**Memory**

It comes with 2x DDR5 slots and supports up to 128GB of RAM, with speeds ranging from DDR5 4800 (JEDEC) up to DDR5 6400+ (OC). MSI's Memory Boost technology helps deliver clean, high-frequency DDR5 signals, and XMP profiles are tested and certified by MSI's OC Lab.

---

**Form Factor & Layout**

It is a **Micro-ATX (mATX)** board, making it compact and compatible with most mid-tower and mini-tower cases.

---

**Expansion & Storage**

The board features a PCIe Gen 4 x16 slot with up to 64 GB/s bandwidth, and supports the latest storage standards including an M.2 Gen 4 slot for ultra-fast NVMe drives.

---

**Cooling & Power**

A chipset heatsink provides passive cooling, and MSI's AI Engine (Frozr AI Cooling) detects CPU and GPU temperatures to automatically adjust fan speeds. Core Boost technology combines MSI's premium power layout for faster and more precise current delivery to the CPU, suitable for multi-core workloads and overclocking.

---

**Connectivity**

For networking, it includes a Realtek RTL8111H Gigabit LAN chip. Display output is provided via one HDMI port. Audio is handled by a 7.1-channel HD audio solution.

---

**Who It's For**

The PRO series is tailored to professionals and users who value productivity and multitasking efficiency, striking a balance between performance and quality at an accessible price point.
