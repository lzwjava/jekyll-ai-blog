## Should you pursue a master's degree overseas?

My recommendation is to keep it as an option, not make it the center of your plan.

| Route                   | What it buys you                                                                         | My assessment                                       |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Online master's         | Structured learning, potentially a recognized credential                                 | Good if affordable and compatible with consulting   |
| In-person US master's   | Campus network, access to founders, internships, and a possible immigration pathway      | Worth investigating if you can justify the cost     |
| Direct entrepreneurship | Customers, revenue, product experience, and international relationships                  | Start immediately, regardless of education          |
| Overseas employment     | Income, local experience, professional connections, and potentially a relocation pathway | A practical bridge if the right opportunity appears |

One important constraint: admission, a degree, and permission to work in the United States are three different things. Check each program's actual eligibility and immigration implications before paying a deposit. Do not assume an online degree creates a US relocation pathway.

Your existing engineering and entrepreneurial record is valuable, but you should also investigate how each school's bachelor's-degree requirements affect admission.
