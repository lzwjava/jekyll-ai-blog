**Question:** What tech startups or companies established in Macao hire software engineers and technical staff?

**Answer:**

Macao's tech scene is still developing, and the honest picture is that it's relatively small compared to neighboring Hong Kong or Shenzhen. Here's what's actually there:

---

**🏢 Established Local Tech & IT Companies**

- **CTM (Companhia de Telecomunicações de Macau)** — Macao's main telecom operator; regularly hires network, software, and IT engineers.
- **DCL (Data Center & IT)** — Founded in 1992, DCL is a professional IT infrastructure solution provider in Hong Kong, Macau, and Singapore, specializing in E&M and IT infrastructure solutions for financial services and other industries. In recent years they've shifted toward product R&D, hiring engineers for their datacenter product line.
- **Macau Pass** — Local fintech/payments company behind the city's transit and e-payment card; employs software developers for payment systems.
- **PCI Group** — Founded in 1989 and headquartered in Beijing, PCI Group is a provider of audiovisual and technology solutions across Greater China, with offices including Macau, delivering audio/visual tech solutions.

---

**🚀 Startups & Scale-ups (Macao-born)**

- **UUTrip / U-Tran-i** — Founded by Jacky Song, UUTrip is a Macao-based AI tourism app startup that combines travel industry needs with artificial intelligence, and secured a government contract. U-Tran-i is a machine translation software product from the same founder.
- **Macau New Technologies Incubator Centre (NIMC)** — Launched in 2001, this is Macao's first large-scale tech incubator, and remains the hub for early-stage startups that hire junior engineers.

---

**🎰 Gaming Companies with Large Tech Teams**

The casino operators (MGM Macau, Sands China, Wynn, Melco, SJM, Galaxy) all maintain internal IT/software teams and increasingly hire engineers for:

- Data analytics & BI
- Cybersecurity
- Casino management systems
- Mobile/app development

---

**📈 Growing Sectors to Watch**

Macao is increasingly positioning itself as a financial hub, particularly with its focus on fintech, blockchain, and digital currencies. The government also implemented a temporary licensing system for non-financial institutions involved in fintech-related activities, with the purpose of promoting innovation in financial products.

The Macao government committed to invest at least 5 billion patacas (~$621 million USD) in science and technology research between 2024 and 2028.

---

**⚠️ Reality Check**

Foreign businesses cite a constant shortage of skilled workers as a top constraint, and Macau attracted 521 highly qualified professionals to non-gaming sectors in 2024 through a new talent recruitment program. The market is small — most serious software engineering roles at scale remain in Hong Kong, Shenzhen, or remote positions.
