Question: For Genshin Impact, which region has the most users?

Answer: 
China has the most users for Genshin Impact by a significant margin. The game operates separate servers for Mainland China (CN servers) and international regions (Americas, Europe, Asia, and Taiwan/Hong Kong/Macao). According to official demographics from the 2025 Shanghai Top Anime IP Selection, Genshin Impact has over 300 million total players worldwide, with **50% in China (approximately 150 million) and 50% overseas**. This split is corroborated across multiple sources tracking downloads, active users, and revenue.

### Key Evidence:
- **Downloads (lifetime, Google Play + App Store, as of June 2025)**: China leads with 50.83 million, far ahead of the US (25.2 million), Indonesia (16.6 million), Russia (16.56 million), and Brazil (11.88 million).
- **Active Players (June 2025 estimate)**: Asia-Pacific region (including China) accounts for 45% of global active users (~6.84 million), with China specifically at 2.736 million (40% share). For comparison, North America is 24% (~3.65 million), Europe 21% (~3.19 million).
- **Concurrent Players (server status)**: Among international servers, Asia has the highest at 385,000 online, followed by Americas (185,000), Europe (165,000), and TW/HK/MO (112,000). China servers are not publicly tracked in the same way but are known to dwarf these due to population size.
- **Account Creation (UID estimates from community tracking)**: CN servers have exceeded 200 million accounts as of early 2024, with Asia international at ~116 million, NA/EU ~80 million each, and TW/HK/MO ~9 million.

While Southeast Asian countries like the Philippines show the highest *relative interest* (e.g., Google Trends-based metrics), absolute user numbers are dominated by China's massive population and the game's home-market popularity. International user breakdowns (excluding CN) often list the US highest (~12%), but this still trails China's volume.

**Note**: Player counts combine registered users, monthly actives (15-65 million globally depending on source/month), and downloads. CN data is from separate apps/stores, explaining why global trackers sometimes underrepresent it.
