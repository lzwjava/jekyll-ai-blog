**Question:** Explain this notice from the Guangzhou "One-Network" (一网通) business registration system.

**Answer:**

This is the user agreement / disclaimer shown when you start registering a new company in Guangzhou through the 一网通 (Yiwangtong, "One Network") system, run by the Guangzhou Market Supervision Administration. It's the single-form portal that bundles everything a new company needs: name declaration, incorporation registration, company seal (chop) application, bank account opening, tax registration, invoice (fapiao) application, social insurance account, housing fund account, and employment registration — all from one submission.

The key operational rules you actually need to care about:

**Deadlines (item 2)** — Once you start an application, you have **8 hours** to finish entering all information and initiate e-signature, and **1 month** to complete online signing. Miss either and the system releases your reserved company name and kills the application; you start over.

**Abuse penalty (item 3)** — If 5+ of your applications get terminated for expiry within 30 days, you're banned from the system for 3 months.

**Company name risk (item 4)** — Name declaration is self-service, checked against the national name rules (禁限用规则, 相同相近比对规则). Passing the automated check is *not* a guarantee: the local registrar can still reject it, and even after registration you can face trademark/infringement disputes or be forced to change an "unsuitable" name. The risk is on you.

**Fraud penalty (item 5)** — Submitting fake materials (fake registered address, unsuitable names, etc.) gets the agent credit-blacklisted and banned for 1 year.

**VAT taxpayer timing (item 7)** — You can't apply for **general taxpayer status (一般纳税人)** in the last 3 working days of a month. Applications signed in that window get pushed to the tax system with a delay. Relevant if you want general taxpayer status from day one for VAT deduction.

**Documents (items 8–9)** — Extra uploads needed only for pre-approval licensed business scopes, famous-trademark names, or non-standard addresses. Otherwise the system auto-generates your application forms, shareholder resolutions, articles of association, etc. for digital signing, and pulls ID cards/licenses from the electronic certificate database — no manual uploads. You can replace the auto-generated documents in the preview step if you want custom articles.

**The "gift package" clause (item 12)** — After successful registration, if you don't collect the 大礼包 (the starter package with your license, chops, etc.) within **6 months**, all your applications are treated as withdrawn. Don't forget to pick it up.

**Antitrust notice (item 13)** — Boilerplate merger-control reminder: only applies if combined China revenue of merging parties exceeds ¥4B (or ¥12B globally) with at least two parties each above ¥800M in China. Irrelevant for a normal new company.

**Liability disclaimers (items 10, 14, 15)** — You're legally responsible for the truthfulness of everything submitted; the system takes no responsibility for data loss due to force majeure; the Guangzhou Market Supervision Administration can change the terms without notice.

Practical takeaway if you're registering a company: prepare all information (name candidates, shareholders, registered address, business scope, registered capital, articles) *before* starting the form so the 8-hour window is trivial, get all signatories to complete e-signing promptly, avoid month-end if you need general taxpayer status, and collect the gift package once approved.
