Question: **How do I apply for a Singapore Employment Pass (EP)?**

Answer: The important point is that **you normally cannot apply for an EP yourself**. You first need a Singapore job offer; then the **Singapore employer or its appointed employment agent applies to MOM on your behalf**. ([Ministry of Manpower Singapore][1])

### 1. Get a Singapore job offer

For your profile, the natural route is something like:

```text
Singapore AI/LLM / Software Engineer job
            ↓
Employer offers e.g. S$8k–15k/month
            ↓
Employer checks EP eligibility
            ↓
Employer submits EP application to MOM
            ↓
MOM approves → IPA letter
            ↓
You enter Singapore
            ↓
Employer issues EP
```

You don't need to already be living in Singapore.

### 2. Meet the salary requirement

As of **September 2026**, the minimum EP qualifying salary is:

* **S$5,600/month** for most sectors
* **S$6,200/month** for financial services
* The threshold increases with age. ([Ministry of Manpower Singapore][2])

At your age, the ordinary-sector threshold is already higher than S$5,600 because MOM progressively increases the required salary with age.

More importantly, **passing the minimum salary isn't enough**. Most applications must also pass **COMPASS**, a points-based assessment. ([Ministry of Manpower Singapore][2])

### 3. Employer has to pass COMPASS

COMPASS evaluates things such as:

```text
C1: candidate salary
C2: candidate qualifications
C3: nationality diversity
C4: support for local employment
C5: shortage occupation skills
C6: strategic economic priorities
```

The salary criterion compares your fixed monthly salary with the local PMET salary distribution in your sector. ([Ministry of Manpower Singapore][3])

For an **AI/LLM engineer**, C5 can potentially be particularly interesting because Singapore's **Shortage Occupation List** provides bonus points for qualifying occupations. The current SOL applies to EP applications from 1 January 2026. ([Ministry of Manpower Singapore][4])

### 4. The employer does the actual application

The company uses MOM's **EP eService / myMOM Portal**. ([Ministry of Manpower Singapore][5])

They'll generally need information/documents such as:

* your passport
* employment details
* salary
* job title / occupation
* educational qualifications where applicable
* company information
* supporting documents requested by MOM

MOM has a specific current document checklist. ([Ministry of Manpower Singapore][6])

The employer should also normally advertise the position on **MyCareersFuture** and satisfy the Fair Consideration Framework requirements before applying. ([Ministry of Manpower Singapore][7])

### 5. Processing

For an online EP application, MOM says it is generally **processed or updated within 10 business days**. Applications involving an overseas company without a Singapore-registered entity can take **up to 6 weeks for most cases**. ([Ministry of Manpower Singapore][7])

If approved, you receive an **In-Principle Approval (IPA)** letter.

Then:

```text
IPA
 ↓
enter Singapore
 ↓
employer issues EP
 ↓
EP becomes your legal work authorization
```

You can check an application using MOM's work-pass status service with your passport number if you don't yet have a FIN. ([Ministry of Manpower Singapore][8])

### For you specifically

I would **not target the S$5,600 floor**.

Given your ~12 years of software experience and AI/LLM positioning, I'd target something like:

```text
AI Engineer / LLM Engineer
AI Infrastructure Engineer
ML Infrastructure Engineer
Software Engineer — AI Platform
AI Agent Engineer
```

with **S$8k–12k+ monthly** as a practical target rather than trying to barely qualify for EP.

There's also an important 2027 change: **new EP applications from 1 January 2027 will have a minimum qualifying salary of S$6,000**, with the age-adjusted threshold rising accordingly. ([Ministry of Manpower Singapore][9])

So if you're planning a Singapore move in 2027, you should evaluate your application using the **2027 thresholds**, not today's S$5,600 number.
