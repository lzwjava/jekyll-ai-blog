Question: **How many people in the U.S. use drugs?**

Answer: The latest federal data is for **2025**.

* **~24.0% of Americans age 12+** used an illicit drug in the **past year** — roughly **69 million people**.
* **16.4%** used an illicit drug in the **past month** — roughly **47 million people**.
* **21.2%** used marijuana in the past year — roughly **61 million people**.
* Cocaine: **1.7%** (~5 million)
* Methamphetamine: **0.9%** (~2.6 million)
* Heroin: **0.2%** (~0.6 million)

These categories overlap: someone can use multiple drugs, so you **cannot add the numbers together**. ([SAMHSA][1])

For context, the 2024 survey found **25.5% / 73.6 million** people age 12+ used illicit drugs in the past year, so the 2025 estimate represents a decline. ([SAMHSA][2])
