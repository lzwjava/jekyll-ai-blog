**Question: Do many mainland Chinese people run startups/businesses in Hong Kong and get software projects there as individuals or small companies?**

Answer:

Yes. This is a real and common pattern, especially among **software engineers, AI consultants, fintech developers, SaaS founders, and cross-border service companies**.

Hong Kong has historically been a bridge between mainland China and international markets. A significant portion of Hong Kong startups are founded by non-locals, and mainland China is one of the largest sources of non-local founders. ([LegCo][1])

Typical models:

### 1. Mainland engineer → Hong Kong company → international clients

Example:

```
Guangzhou/Shenzhen engineer
        |
        v
Hong Kong Limited Company
        |
        +---- HK clients
        +---- Singapore clients
        +---- US/EU clients
        +---- Mainland subcontracting team
```

The Hong Kong company becomes the "front office":

* sign contracts
* invoice clients
* receive USD/HKD payments
* build international credibility

Engineering work can be done by:

* founder himself
* mainland remote developers
* small team in Shenzhen/Guangzhou

This is very common in:

* AI consulting
* web/mobile development
* enterprise software
* fintech systems
* cloud migration
* automation/agent development

---

### 2. Individual freelancer → HK company

A senior engineer may do:

```
Client:
Hong Kong trading company

Need:
AI customer service agent

Contract:
HK Limited Company

Price:
HK$200k project

Implementation:
Founder + 1-2 developers
```

The advantage is not that Hong Kong magically creates customers. The advantage is:

* easier international communication
* stronger business image
* easier banking/payment
* closer to financial companies
* access to HK startup ecosystem

Hong Kong officially supports different company structures including limited companies and individual proprietorships. ([Invest Hong Kong | InvestHK][2])

---

### 3. Shenzhen/Guangzhou people especially do this

Geography matters:

```
Shenzhen
   |
   | 30-60 minutes
   |
Hong Kong
```

Many founders live/work in the Greater Bay Area:

* engineering in Shenzhen
* company registration in HK
* customers in HK/Singapore/US

Hong Kong startup data shows a substantial non-local founder population, with mainland China being the largest source among non-local founders. ([LegCo][1])

---

### 4. Software project sources in Hong Kong

Common clients:

* banks
* insurance companies
* logistics companies
* trading companies
* universities
* startups
* SMEs

Especially:

**AI projects now:**

* RAG knowledge systems
* internal copilots
* document automation
* customer service agents

A senior AI engineer with your background can position as:

> "AI automation consultant for Hong Kong SMEs"

rather than competing as a cheap programmer.

---

### Reality check

The difficult part is **not registering a HK company**.

The difficult part is:

```
Company registration: easy
        |
        v
Getting first HK clients: hard
        |
        v
Building reputation: harder
```

Most successful people already have:

* mainland network
* previous clients
* LinkedIn presence
* technical reputation
* English ability

---

For someone with your profile (11 years engineering, AI/LLM experience, English IELTS 6.5, Guangzhou location), the more realistic path is:

```
Guangzhou base
       |
       v
Hong Kong Ltd
       |
       v
Target HK AI consulting projects
       |
       v
Gradually build HK/international client base
```

This is exactly the kind of cross-border model Hong Kong is designed for: a gateway between mainland China and global markets. ([Invest Hong Kong | InvestHK][3])
