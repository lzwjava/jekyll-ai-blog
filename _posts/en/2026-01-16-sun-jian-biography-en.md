**Question:** Give a comprehensive introduction to Sun Jian, the AI scientist.

**Answer:**

Sun Jian (孙剑, 1976/1977 – June 14, 2022) was a highly influential Chinese AI scientist and computer vision expert, widely recognized for his groundbreaking contributions to **deep learning**, **computer vision**, and **computational photography**.

He earned his bachelor's (1997), master's (2000), and Ph.D. (2003) degrees in electrical engineering / pattern recognition and intelligent systems from Xi'an Jiaotong University. After graduation, he joined Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA) in 2003, where he spent 13 years rising to roles such as Lead Researcher, Senior Researcher, Principal Researcher, and eventually Partner Research Manager (including time in the US). During this period, he focused on computer vision, computational photography, and related areas, transferring numerous technologies into Microsoft products like Windows, Office, Bing, Xbox, and Azure.

In 2016, he left Microsoft to join **Megvii Technology** (旷视科技, also known as Face++), one of China's leading AI companies specializing in facial recognition and computer vision. At Megvii, he served as **Chief Scientist** and **Managing Director of Megvii Research** (the company's core research arm, which he helped establish and grow). He led major innovations, including:

- **ShuffleNet** — an efficient convolutional neural network designed for mobile devices, enabling high-performance deep learning on resource-constrained hardware.
- **Brain++** — Megvii's core AI productivity platform for accelerating model development and deployment.
- The open-source deep learning framework **MegEngine** (天元).

His most famous academic contribution is co-authoring the **ResNet** (Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition) paper with Kaiming He, Xiangyu Zhang, and Shaoqing Ren. Published in 2016, ResNet introduced residual connections that solved the vanishing gradient problem in very deep neural networks, enabling training of networks with hundreds or even thousands of layers. ResNet won the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC) 2015 (achieving a top-5 error rate of ~3.57%) and received the **CVPR 2016 Best Paper Award**. It became one of the most cited and influential architectures in modern deep learning.

Earlier, in 2009, he co-authored the seminal paper "Single Image Haze Removal Using Dark Channel Prior," which won the **CVPR Best Paper Award** (the first for an Asian team) and remains foundational in image dehazing and enhancement.

Sun Jian published over 100 papers in top venues (CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, SIGGRAPH, PAMI), with Google Scholar citations exceeding **700,000** and an h-index around 121 at the time of his passing. He held dozens of patents (many from his Microsoft years) and received numerous honors, including:

- MIT Technology Review "Innovators Under 35" (2010)
- National Natural Science Award (Second Class, China)
- Ho Leung Ho Lee Foundation Youth Innovation Award
- AI2000 Most Influential Scholar Award in Computer Vision

He also served as the inaugural dean of the **School of Artificial Intelligence** at Xi'an Jiaotong University (appointed in 2019), bridging academia and industry.

Tragically, Sun Jian passed away suddenly on June 14, 2022, at age 45 due to an unspecified illness. His death was widely mourned in the global AI community as a major loss, given his role in advancing both fundamental research and practical AI deployment in China. He is remembered for his dedication to science, mentorship, and efforts to push the boundaries of visual perception toward human-level capabilities.
