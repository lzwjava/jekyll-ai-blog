Question: Is PET similar to IELTS band 4?

Answer:

Yes — **PET (B1 Preliminary)** is roughly around **IELTS 4.0 to 5.0**, but it’s not a perfect one-to-one match.

### Simple comparison:

* **PET (B1 level)**
  → Intermediate English
  → Can handle everyday situations (school, travel, simple discussions)

* **IELTS band ~4.0**
  → Limited user
  → Can communicate basic ideas, but with frequent mistakes and difficulty

* **IELTS band ~5.0**
  → Modest user
  → Basic communication is possible, but still struggles with accuracy and fluency

### So how do they relate?

* PET is usually considered **around IELTS 4.5 (B1 boundary zone)**
* It sits **between IELTS 4 and IELTS 5**

### Important difference

* **PET is an exam for general English level (B1 certificate)**
* **IELTS measures academic/professional readiness for study or immigration**

👉 So PET is not “equal to IELTS 4”, but it is **in the same general mid-low intermediate range**

### Easy way to remember:

* KET ≈ IELTS 3.0–3.5 (A2)
* PET ≈ IELTS 4.0–5.0 (B1)
* FCE ≈ IELTS 5.5–6.5 (B2)
