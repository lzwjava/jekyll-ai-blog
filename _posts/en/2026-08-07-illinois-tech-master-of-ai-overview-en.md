## Question

Intro to Master of Artificial Intelligence at Illinois Institute of Technology (Illinois Tech)

## Answer

Illinois Institute of Technology (Illinois Tech / IIT Chicago) offers a **Master of Artificial Intelligence (M.A.S. in Artificial Intelligence)** through its Department of Computer Science. It is a **STEM-designated master's program** and is available both **on-campus and online**. ([Illinois Institute of Technology][1])

The program is mainly designed for people who want to become:

* Machine Learning Engineer
* AI Engineer
* AI Specialist
* Applied AI Researcher

rather than a pure theoretical AI researcher. ([Illinois Institute of Technology][1])

---

## Program Structure

Degree:

* **Master of Artificial Intelligence (M.A.S.)**
* Department: Computer Science
* Credits: **30 credit hours** ([Illinois Institute of Technology Catalog][2])

Core areas:

* Machine Learning
* Deep Learning
* Natural Language Processing (NLP)
* Computer Vision
* Probabilistic Reasoning
* Data Analytics
* AI applications in other domains ([Illinois Institute of Technology Catalog][2])

## Admission Requirements

Generally:

* Bachelor's degree required
* GPA around 3.0/4.0 recommended
* English test may be required (TOEFL/PTE/IELTS depending on applicant)
* GRE is **not required** for master's programs at Illinois Tech ([Illinois Institute of Technology][3])
