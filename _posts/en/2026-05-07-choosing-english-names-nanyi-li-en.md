**Answer:**

## Understanding the Name 李南依 (Nanyi Li)

First, let's understand what her Chinese name means:

- **南 (Nán)** — means "south," often evoking warmth, brightness, and direction
- **依 (Yī)** — means "to lean on," "to rely on," "gentle," or "graceful dependence" — a very tender, soft feminine character

Together, the name 南依 carries a feel of **gentle warmth from the south** — soft, graceful, and close/affectionate.
