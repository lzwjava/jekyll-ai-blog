**Question: Explain the Illinois Tech graduate application education/transcript requirements.**

**Answer:**

This section is asking you to declare **every post-secondary institution (after high school) you attended**, not only schools where you successfully obtained a degree.

The key points:

### 1. List every college/university you attended

> "Please list all colleges/universities from which you earned a degree or anticipate earning a degree. You must include all institutions, regardless if a degree was earned or not..."

Meaning:

You must include:

* Universities where you graduated ✅
* Universities where you studied but did not graduate ✅
* Schools where you transferred credits from ✅
* Your current unfinished degree programs ✅

Example:

If someone:

* Studied at Beijing Forestry University for 1 year, dropped out
* Later studied self-study undergraduate courses at Guangdong University of Foreign Studies

They should still list both institutions.

Do **not** hide unfinished study history.

---

### 2. US school applicants

This is only for people who attended US universities.

They want:

✅ Official transcript from the university registrar

They do **not accept**:

* Screenshots from student portals
* Degree audit reports
* Student-created transcript PDFs

---

### 3. International applicants (China included)

For Chinese applicants:

You need:

1. Official transcript from every university attended

For example:

* Beijing Forestry University transcript
* Guangdong University of Foreign Studies transcript

Even if you did not graduate.

The transcript must come from the original school.

---

### 4. Degree certificate requirement

Only required if you already graduated.

Example:

You graduated from a 4-year bachelor's program in 2024:

Need:

* Transcript
* Degree certificate
* Graduation certificate

If you are still studying:

Example:

"Expected graduation 2027"

Then:

* Transcript required
* Degree certificate not required yet

---

### 5. Chinese education documents

For China specifically:

They require:

If graduated:

Chinese:

* 学位证 (degree certificate)
* 毕业证 (graduation certificate)
* 成绩单 (transcript)

English:

* English translations of these documents

Both languages are needed.

They say:

> native language transcript in addition to any documents issued in English

Meaning:

Do not upload only an English translation.

Upload:

```
Chinese transcript.pdf
+
English transcript translation.pdf
```

---

### 6. Translation requirement

If your document is Chinese:

Bad:

```
English transcript only
```

Good:

```
Chinese original transcript
+
official English translation
```

The translation should normally be issued by:

* university
* certified translation service

---

### 7. Semester/course requirement

For a normal 4-year bachelor's degree:

They want enough academic history:

```
At least 6 semesters
courses + grades + credits
```

For a 3-year bachelor's:

```
At least 4 semesters
```

They want evidence you completed meaningful university study.

---

### 8. Reverse side of transcript

Chinese transcripts often have:

* grading scale
* explanation of GPA
* university stamp instructions

They want both sides scanned.

Example:

```
Transcript front page
+
Transcript back page
```
