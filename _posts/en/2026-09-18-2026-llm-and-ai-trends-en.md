**Question:** What are the recent trends in LLM/AI technology?

**Answer:**

The LLM landscape in 2026 is defined by several converging trends:

---

### 1. 🧠 Multimodal Intelligence
Multimodal models that can parse text, images, audio, and even video are now a top priority. Companies like Google (Gemini), OpenAI, and Mistral are racing to offer native multimodal support, with reasoning models and large multimodal models (LMMs) considered the two most important recent developments.

---

### 2. 🤖 Agentic AI
Agentic AI — systems that not only generate answers but plan and execute multi-step tasks autonomously — is classified as one of three transformative forces for 2026. These systems can understand goals, interact with tools and APIs, and adapt and self-improve. Gartner predicts that by 2028, 80% of customer-facing processes will be handled by multi-agent AI.

---

### 3. 🔍 Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
RAG is now the standard approach to ground model outputs in facts. MIT researchers emphasize that RAG is essential to reduce hallucinations, and it has become the baseline for enterprise deployments. Hallucination rates still range from 15% to 82.7% depending on the model and prompt, making RAG pipelines critical.

---

### 4. ⚡ Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) Architectures
Instead of activating all parameters at once, MoE models route queries through specialist "experts," providing a strong price–performance trade-off. This design reduces compute requirements and allows models to scale — potentially enabling 10x larger models without 10x higher costs.

---

### 5. 📏 Extended Context Windows
Next-generation models promise context windows up to 200k tokens or beyond, enabling them to read entire knowledge bases or code repositories. This unlocks new workflows like end-to-end documentation summarization and project-level code analysis.

---

### 6. 🌐 Edge & Sovereign AI
Lightweight models like Gemini Nano bring powerful capabilities to smartphones and IoT devices, while sovereign AI emphasizes data localization and regional compliance. Governments and enterprises are increasingly investing in region-specific AI platforms to meet security and compliance requirements — Gartner predicts 35% of countries will be locked into region-specific AI platforms by 2027.

---

### 7. 🔒 Safety, Governance & Regulation
Safety remains a key differentiator. The EU AI Act introduces risk categories and mandates transparency, while regulations in the US and China are also evolving. Gartner warns that "death by AI" legal claims will exceed 2,000 by 2026, and 75% of hiring processes will include AI proficiency tests by 2027.

---

### 8. 🏥 Industry Verticalization
Domain-specific LLMs are being built for healthcare, law, finance, coding, and robotics. By 2026, 80% of initial healthcare diagnoses will involve AI analysis, and 58% of consumers have already replaced search engines with generative AI tools.
