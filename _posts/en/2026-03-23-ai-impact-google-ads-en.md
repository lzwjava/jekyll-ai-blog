Question: Do Google Ads or Google Search now have significant impact after ChatGPT has been out for more than 3 years?

Answer:

Yes — but the impact is **real yet not catastrophic**. ChatGPT and AI search have **changed user behavior**, **reduced some search traffic**, and **forced Google to redesign ads**, but **Google Search and Google Ads are still extremely dominant**.

Here is the current reality (2025–2026):

---

# 1) Yes — ChatGPT is reducing some traditional Google searches

* AI search tools captured **12–15% of global search share in 2025**, up from ~5% earlier.
* Google’s search market share **fell below 90% for the first time in a decade**.
* Some data shows **Google searches per U.S. user dropped nearly 20% (2024→2025)**.
* Publisher traffic from Google **fell by about one-third globally**.
  ([ALM Corp][1])

This means:

* fewer simple informational searches
* fewer “click through” to websites
* more answers directly in AI chat

This **does affect ad impressions**, especially:

* informational keywords
* SEO content sites
* affiliate marketing pages

---

# 2) But Google is still massively bigger

Even after ChatGPT growth:

* Google still processes **~373× more searches than ChatGPT**
* Google still controls **90%+ mobile search**
* Google search revenue **still grew ~10% year-over-year**
  ([advancedwebranking.com][2])

Also:

* ChatGPT may handle **~17% of digital queries**, but Google still has **~78%**
  ([Basis][3])

So impact = noticeable, but not existential.

---

# 3) Google Ads is NOT shrinking — it's evolving

Instead of collapsing, ads are moving into AI:

* AI-powered advertising expected to **grow 63% in 2026**
* reaching **$57B and ~12% of total ad spend**
  ([Business Insider][4])

This means:

* ads inside AI answers
* shopping ads in AI results
* conversational ads
* automated targeting (Performance Max, etc.)

So ads are **moving**, not disappearing.

---

# 4) Big structural change: “Search → Answer”

This is the biggest shift:

Old:

```
Google → links → click → website → ads
```

New:

```
ChatGPT / AI → answer directly → fewer clicks
```

AI answers **reduce the need to click links**, which threatens ad-based models.
([LinkedIn][5])

That’s why:

* Google added AI Overviews
* Google adds ads inside AI answers
* Google pushes shopping results in AI

---

# 5) Surprisingly — people use Google MORE in some cases

One dataset shows:

* **Google usage increased to 12.6 sessions/week** after AI tools emerged
  ([Position Digital][6])

Reason:

* people use ChatGPT for thinking
* then Google for navigation / buying / verifying

So AI **complements** search, not just replaces it.

---

# Bottom line

Short answer:

* Yes → ChatGPT **does impact Google search & ads**
* But → **Google still dominates**
* Ads → **not shrinking, just moving into AI**
* Future → **AI search + traditional search coexist**

Most likely future:

```
ChatGPT → thinking / research
Google → navigation / shopping / local
```

And Google Ads remain strong because:

* buying intent still happens in Google
* businesses still bid on keywords
* AI answers still include ads
