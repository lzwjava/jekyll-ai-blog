Question: If a mainland Chinese citizen creates a Hong Kong company and hires himself, can he use that to live/work in Hong Kong?

Answer:

Yes, **this is a real business model**, but there are two separate issues:

1. **Can you create a Hong Kong company?**
2. **Can you use that company to get the right to live and work in Hong Kong?**

They are not the same.

### 1. Create a Hong Kong company: Yes

A mainland Chinese citizen can generally:

* incorporate a Hong Kong Limited Company
* become shareholder/director
* open a company bank account (subject to bank checks)
* sign software consulting contracts
* invoice Hong Kong/international clients

Example:

```
Hong Kong AI Technology Limited

Shareholder:
    Zhiwei Li 100%

Business:
    AI consulting
    LLM deployment
    software development

Clients:
    Hong Kong companies
    Singapore companies
    US companies
```

This is common among mainland entrepreneurs.

---

### 2. "Hire myself": Possible, but immigration is the hard part

Your HK company can technically employ you:

```
Company:
    HK AI Limited

Employee:
    Zhiwei Li

Position:
    AI Engineer / Director

Salary:
    HKD 50k/month
```

But immigration authorities will ask:

* Is this a genuine business?
* Does the company have real operations?
* Does it create economic value?
* Are there customers/contracts?
* Is salary reasonable?
* Does the company have financial ability?

A newly created shell company just paying yourself usually will not be enough. Hong Kong immigration looks for evidence such as business plans, contracts, financial records, investment, and operations. ([Immigration Department Hong Kong][1])

---

### 3. For mainland residents, the route is different

Important point:

The normal "entrepreneur visa" under Hong Kong's General Employment Policy is **not applicable to Chinese residents of Mainland China**. ([Immigration Department Hong Kong][1])

For mainland professionals, a common route is:

```
Mainland person
       |
       v
HK company
       |
       v
Apply under Admission Scheme for Mainland Talents and Professionals (ASMTP)
       |
       v
Work visa
       |
       v
Live/work in Hong Kong
```

The HK company becomes your sponsor/employer. The company needs to demonstrate real business background, employment contract, financial standing, and business activities. ([Government of Hong Kong][2])

---

### 4. Practical path for someone like you (AI/software consultant)

A realistic approach:

**Phase 1: Test market**

Use 港澳通行证:

```
Guangzhou
    |
1 hour train
    |
Hong Kong

Meet:
- startups
- fintech companies
- AI companies
- software outsourcing clients
```

Do not work illegally on tourist entry, but networking/business meetings are common.

---

**Phase 2: Create HK entity**

Example:

```
HK AI Consulting Limited

Services:
- LLM fine-tuning
- AI agents
- enterprise automation
- software outsourcing
```

Get:

* first customers
* invoices
* contracts
* revenue history

---

**Phase 3: Move if business works**

Then:

```
HK company revenue
        |
        v
employment / talent visa application
        |
        v
move family to HK
```

A successful AI consulting business with international clients is much stronger than "I created a company and hired myself".

---

Given your background (AI engineering, LLM training, software consulting), Hong Kong is actually one of the more natural places to try this because:

* close to Guangzhou/Shenzhen
* many fintech/trading companies need AI
* English business environment
* international clients
* low company setup friction

The key is **build a real HK business first, then use immigration as a consequence**, not the other way around. ([investhk.gov.hk][3])
