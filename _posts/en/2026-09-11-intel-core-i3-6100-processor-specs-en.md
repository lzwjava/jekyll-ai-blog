Question: Can you introduce the Intel Core i3-6100 processor?

Answer: The **Intel Core i3-6100** is a 6th-Generation budget desktop processor based on Intel's 14nm "Skylake" architecture, originally released in late 2015.

**Key Specifications:**

* **Cores/Threads:** 2 Cores / 4 Threads (via Hyper-Threading)
* **Base Clock Speed:** 3.70 GHz (No Turbo Boost)
* **Socket:** LGA 1151 (compatible with 100-series and 200-series motherboards)
* **Integrated Graphics:** Intel HD Graphics 530
* **Power Draw (TDP):** 51W (very low power and cool-running)
* **Memory Support:** DDR4-2133 / DDR3L-1600

**Best Uses:**

* Basic office work, web browsing, and media consumption.
* Low-cost test benches (diagnosing hardware, testing motherboards or GPUs).
* Budget home servers, NAS builds, or light home theater PCs (HTPC).

**Limitations:**

* Dual-core architecture heavily bottlenecks modern multitasking and modern gaming.
* Lacks official Windows 11 support (requires workaround installation).
