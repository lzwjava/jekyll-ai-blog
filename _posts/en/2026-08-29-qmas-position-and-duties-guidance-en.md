Question: **How should I fill “Name of Position” and “Nature of Duties” in the QMAS work-experience section?**

Answer:

The QMAS Guidance Notes are quite explicit: **“Name of Position” should be your exact official job title**, matching your employment records/supporting documents. “Nature of Duties” should be a **brief description of the essence of your work**, e.g. “computer software development.” ([Immigration Department Hong Kong][1])

For your case, I would **not write a long job description**. The goal is to make the nature of your professional experience immediately clear, especially if you're relying on it for the **innovation and technology** work-experience criterion.

### Example: Backend Engineer

**Name of Position**

```text
Backend Engineer
```

**Nature of Duties**

```text
Backend software development, system design and development, API development, database engineering, and cloud-based software systems.
```

### If the actual title is “Software Engineer”

Use:

**Name of Position**

```text
Software Engineer
```

**Nature of Duties**

```text
Software development, backend system design, API development, database engineering, and software system maintenance.
```

### For an AI/ML role

If your official title is actually AI Engineer:

**Name of Position**

```text
AI Engineer
```

**Nature of Duties**

```text
Artificial intelligence and machine learning development, LLM application development, model training and fine-tuning, and AI system engineering.
```

### Important for QMAS

Don't try to make the title sound more impressive than your documents. If your employment certificate says **“Backend Engineer”**, put exactly **“Backend Engineer”**, rather than changing it to “AI Engineer.”

But **Nature of Duties can explain what you actually did**. This matters because QMAS says whether work experience belongs to a specific field/industry depends primarily on the **nature of your role, position or designation**, rather than merely your employer's industry. ([Immigration Department Hong Kong][1])

So I'd structure your entries like:

| Field                | What to put                                                                                               |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Name of Position** | Exact title on employment certificate                                                                     |
| **Nature of Duties** | 1 concise sentence describing actual professional duties                                                  |
| **Avoid**            | Generic “software engineer” for everything                                                                |
| **Good**             | Explicitly mention backend development / AI / ML / cloud / distributed systems where genuinely applicable |

For your **QMAS application specifically**, I'd optimize each job's wording to clearly establish **graduate/specialist-level technology work** without exaggerating anything.
