Question: Is TeamViewer the best choice for remote control between two Macs?

Answer:

Short answer: **No — it’s not always the “best,” but it *is one of the easiest and most reliable***. The right choice depends on what you care about: simplicity, performance, privacy, or cost.

---

# 🧠 1. When TeamViewer *is* the best choice

TeamViewer is still one of the most popular tools because:

* ✅ Very easy setup (ID + password, no networking knowledge)
* ✅ Works perfectly across Mac ↔ Mac
* ✅ Free for personal use
* ✅ Strong security + cross-platform support ([TSplus][1])
* ✅ Full features: screen control, file transfer, chat, etc.

👉 For your use case (helping your wife fix something remotely):
**TeamViewer is honestly one of the best “plug-and-play” options.**

---

# ⚠️ 2. Why it’s NOT always the best

Some downsides:

* 💰 Expensive for business use ([Atera][2])
* 🔐 Past security concerns (e.g. 2024 breach mentioned by analysts) ([sobrii][3])
* ⚡ Can be slower than newer tools
* 🤖 Sometimes falsely detects “commercial use” (common complaint)

---

# 🚀 3. Better alternatives (depending on your goal)

## 🟢 Best simple & free (recommended alternative)

* Chrome Remote Desktop

  * Works in browser
  * Super easy setup
  * No ID/password sharing needed (Google account login)
  * Best for family use
  * Often recommended for personal users ([SoftLinked][4])

👉 If you want **even simpler than TeamViewer → use this**

---

## ⚡ Best performance (faster than TeamViewer)

* AnyDesk

  * Lower latency, smoother control
  * Lightweight
  * Good for slow networks

---

## 💻 Best for Mac ecosystem (advanced)

* Apple Remote Desktop

  * Native Apple tool
  * More complex
  * Usually overkill for home use

---

## 🔒 Best for privacy / tech users

* RustDesk

  * Open-source, self-hostable
  * Full control of data
  * More setup required

---

# 🧭 4. Clear recommendation (for YOU)

Based on your situation (helping your wife remotely):

### 🥇 Best choice (practical)

* **TeamViewer** → easiest to start immediately

### 🥈 Even better (long-term)

* **Chrome Remote Desktop** → simpler, no passwords, less friction

---

# 💡 My honest take

* If you want **fastest setup right now → use TeamViewer**
* If you want **least friction long-term → switch to Chrome Remote Desktop**
