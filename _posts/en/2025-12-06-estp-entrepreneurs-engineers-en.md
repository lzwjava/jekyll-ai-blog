Question: Who are some famous entrepreneurs and engineers with the ESTP personality type (Myers-Briggs)?

ESTPs (Extraverted, Sensing, Thinking, Perceiving) are often called “The Entrepreneur” or “The Dynamo” in MBTI typology. Here are notable confirmed or strongly typed ESTPs in entrepreneurship and engineering:
