**Question:** How does the 港澳通行证 (Mainland Travel Permit for HK/Macau) renewal/replacement process work, and what does that SMS notification mean?

---

**Answer:**

That SMS is **100% legitimate** — it's the official payment prompt after your application was reviewed and approved. Here's how the full process works:

---

## 📋 Overall Process Flow

**Step 1 — Online Application**
Go to the "移民局12367" WeChat (or Alipay) mini-program, tap **【中国公民服务】→【证件补换发网上申请】**, complete face recognition, fill in personal info, upload your photo + handwritten signature + ID card front/back, then submit.

**Step 2 — Backend Review**
After the online application is submitted, it goes through a backend review before payment is enabled. The system will notify you via SMS and in-app message to pay online. You can also check under the mini-program or APP's **【我的申请】→【全部】** to find applications that have been approved and are awaiting payment.

**Step 3 — Pay the Fee (← You Are Here)**
That SMS means your application passed the review. You now need to pay the **document production fee (证件工本费)** within **7 days**, or the application will be automatically cancelled.

Where to pay:

- WeChat mini-program: **移民局12367** → 【我的申请】
- Alipay mini-program: same path
- 移民局 APP → 【我的申请】

**Step 4 — Document Production & Collection**
The permit will be issued within **20 working days** after the application is accepted. The cost for renewals or replacements on the mainland is **¥230 RMB**. You pick it up at the exit-entry office you selected (or have it mailed, if that option was chosen).

---

## ⚠️ Key Things to Remember

- **Pay within 7 days** — missing this deadline cancels the application and you'd need to reapply from scratch.
- The online renewal/replacement service is currently available for residents with household registration (户籍) in one of **20 pilot cities**: Beijing, Tianjin, Shenyang, Harbin, Shanghai, Nanjing, Hangzhou, Ningbo, Hefei, Xiamen, Jinan, Qingdao, Zhengzhou, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Zhuhai, Chongqing, Chengdu, Kunming, and Xi'an — for residents aged 16 and above (excluding government workers and active military).
- The permit is valid for **5 years**.
