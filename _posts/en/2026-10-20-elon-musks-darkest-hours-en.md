**Question:** How did Elon Musk navigate his darkest periods in life and business?

---

**Answer:**

Elon Musk's journey is marked by multiple severe hardships — personal and professional — that he had to push through at different stages of his life.

---

### 🧒 Childhood: Bullying & Abusive Home

Musk's struggles began in childhood. He faced his parents' divorce and was beaten and bullied by a group of boys for several years at school, largely due to his bookish, know-it-all reputation. His father, Errol Musk, was also reportedly physically and emotionally abusive toward him when he was young.

---

### 💼 Early Career: Repeatedly Pushed Out

He was ousted from Zip2 in 1996 — a company he co-founded — and later pushed out of PayPal in 2000 while serving as CEO. During his time at Zip2, Musk slept in the office, worked tirelessly, and faced repeated rejections from investors. His ousting was a setback, but he retained invaluable experience and wealth for future ventures.

---

### 🚀 2008: The Worst Year — Tesla & SpaceX Both Near Bankruptcy

This was arguably his darkest hour. In 2006 the first SpaceX rocket exploded. A second exploded the following year, and in 2008 a third explosion occurred — this time with NASA satellites on board. After this disaster, both SpaceX and Tesla were staring bankruptcy in the face.

He said the recession hit automakers especially hard and the company's critical fundraising round fell apart as the banking system unraveled. Instead of giving up, Musk said he and a handful of existing investors patched together enough money to keep operations alive. The final financing deal was completed at the last possible hour, on Christmas Eve 2008.

Musk noted that during Tesla's darkest months, he put in all the money he had, even borrowing from friends to cover rent. Demonstrating that level of commitment helped convince investors to back Tesla even as major automakers were entering bankruptcy.

---

### 💡 His Mindset: What Got Him Through

Musk himself stated: "While things are going really well these days, I think it's always important to remember that when you're creating a company, there are very dark times and it's about getting through those dark times. That's the difference between success and failure."

His key strategies were:

- **All-in commitment** — He risked everything he had personally, including borrowing money from friends
- **Belief in the mission** — Founders, he argued, must truly believe — intellectually and emotionally — that what they are building matters.
- **Hiring for passion** — He emphasized hiring people who are passionate: "Then they will stay during the dark times."
- **Extremely high stress tolerance** — His struggle story resulted in him having an extremely high stress tolerance. He perseveres even when a situation gets difficult.
