**Question:** What is an introduction to Swire Properties Limited (太古地產有限公司)?

**Answer:**

**Overview**

Swire Properties Limited (太古地產) is a property developer, owner, and operator of mixed-use, principally commercial properties in Hong Kong and mainland China. Founded and headquartered in Hong Kong in 1972, it is listed on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK: 1972), and is a subsidiary of the publicly listed Swire Pacific Limited.

**Business Segments**

The company operates in three main areas: property investment (development, leasing, and management of commercial, retail, and residential properties as a long-term investment); property trading (development of luxury residential apartments for sale); and hotel investment and operations through Swire Hotels.

**Key Assets**

Swire Properties is best known for its flagship developments:

- **Pacific Place** – a landmark mixed-use complex in Admiralty, Hong Kong
- **Taikoo Place** – a major commercial district in Quarry Bay, Hong Kong

In November 2024, Swire Properties announced the completion of the Taikoo Place Redevelopment Project, which commenced in 2014, transforming Taikoo Place into an industry-leading global business district. Six Pacific Place is now the newest addition to the Pacific Place office portfolio, with handover of office floors to tenants underway.

**Mainland China & Southeast Asia Presence**

In the Chinese Mainland, Swire Properties has six completed mixed-use developments, including Taikoo Li Sanlitun and INDIGO Phase One in Beijing, HKRI Taikoo Hui and Taikoo Li Qiantan in Shanghai, Taikoo Hui Guangzhou, and Taikoo Li Chengdu. In addition to Hong Kong and the Chinese Mainland, the company has a presence in Indonesia, Vietnam, Singapore, Thailand, and Miami, USA.

**Investment Strategy**

Swire Properties has been executing a HK$100 billion investment plan, 65% of which has now been committed. HK$30 billion has been allocated to future expansion plans at Taikoo Place and Pacific Place, its core commercial portfolios in Hong Kong.

**Sustainability**

AI and IoT sensors are deployed across Hong Kong and Mainland China assets to drive real-time energy management and predictive maintenance, supporting the company's SD 2030 strategy and SBTi-aligned pathway to net-zero emissions by 2050.

**Leadership**

The company is currently chaired by Guy Bradley (since August 2021), with Tim Blackburn serving as Chief Executive (since August 2021), and employs approximately 4,500 people.
