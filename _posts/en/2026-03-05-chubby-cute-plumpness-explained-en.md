Question: What does "chubby" mean?

Answer:

**Chubby** is an English adjective that describes someone (or something) that is **slightly fat in a pleasant, cute, or healthy-looking way** — not obese or very overweight.

### Main meanings and common uses

- **For people** (most frequent use):
  Having a soft, rounded, slightly plump body — usually considered attractive or adorable rather than negative.
  Examples:
  - a chubby baby / chubby cheeks
  - chubby toddler thighs
  - "He's a bit chubby but very cute."
  - "She has a chubby face and dimples."

- Typical positive/affectionate feeling:
  Chubby often sounds **endearing**, especially when talking about babies, young children, or people with soft round features.
  Compare:
  - chubby = cute-plump
  - fat = more neutral or negative
  - overweight / obese = medical or clearly negative terms

- **For animals**:
  - A chubby hamster / chubby cat / chubby puppy (again — cute and round)

- **For objects** (less common):
  - chubby fingers, chubby crayons, chubby bottle (short and thick shape)

### Quick comparison of similar words

- **skinny / thin** ← opposite end
- **slim / slender** = attractively thin
- **chubby** = pleasantly plump
- **chunky / stocky** = strong + thick build
- **fat / overweight** = more clearly heavy

In short: **chubby** = soft, round, slightly fat in an usually **cute / lovable** way.
