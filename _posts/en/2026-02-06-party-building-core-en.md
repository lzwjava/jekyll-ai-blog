## Question Analysis

**Correct Answer: B - Institutional/System Building (制度建设)**

The key phrase is "贯穿...各项建设之中" (runs through all aspects of construction), which specifically refers to institutional building.

## The Five Aspects of Comprehensive Strict Party Governance

According to the material (p.197), comprehensive strict Party governance includes:

1. **Ideological building** (思想建设)
2. **Organizational building** (组织建设)
3. **Work style building** (作风建设)
4. **Institutional building runs through all the above** (制度建设贯穿党的各项建设之中)
5. **Anti-corruption struggle**

## Why B (Institutional Building) is Correct

Institutional building is characterized as having:

- **Fundamental nature** (根本性)
- **Comprehensive nature** (全局性)
- **Stability** (稳定性)
- **Long-term nature** (长期性)

It is described as the "long-term strategy and fundamental strategy" (长远之策、根本之策) for comprehensive strict Party governance, which is why it **runs through all other aspects**.

## Why A (Political Building) is Wrong

While political building is extremely important and was emphasized as taking the **leading position** (首位) in Party building in the new era, the question specifically asks what "runs through" (贯穿) all aspects—and the text explicitly states this is **institutional building**.

## Key Distinction

- **Political building**: Takes the leading/primary position
- **Institutional building**: Runs through and underpins all other aspects

This is a subtle but important difference in Chinese political theory terminology!
