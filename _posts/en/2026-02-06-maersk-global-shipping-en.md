A.P. Møller – Mærsk A/S (commonly known as Maersk) is a Danish integrated logistics and shipping conglomerate and one of the world's largest container shipping companies. Here's a detailed overview:

## Company Overview

**Founded:** 1904 by Arnold Peter Møller and his father Peter Mærsk Møller
**Headquarters:** Copenhagen, Denmark
**Stock Exchange:** Listed on the Nasdaq Copenhagen

Maersk operates in approximately 130 countries and employs around 100,000 people globally. The company is still partially controlled by the founding family through the A.P. Møller Foundation.

## Core Business Operations

**Maersk Ocean:** The container shipping division operates one of the world's largest container vessel fleets (around 700+ vessels). The company transports approximately 12 million containers annually, representing about 17% of global container shipping capacity.

**Maersk Logistics & Services:** This division provides end-to-end supply chain solutions including warehousing, distribution, customs services, and inland transportation. The company has been strategically expanding this segment to become a true integrator beyond just ocean freight.

**Maersk Terminals:** Operates approximately 75 port and terminal facilities worldwide, handling containers at major global trade hubs.

## Strategic Transformation

In recent years, Maersk has undergone significant transformation from a diversified conglomerate into a focused integrated container logistics company. The company divested non-core businesses including oil and gas operations (Maersk Oil, sold to Total in 2017) and the drilling business (Maersk Drilling, spun off in 2019).

The strategic focus is now on becoming an integrated logistics provider rather than purely an ocean carrier, competing with companies like DHL and Kuehne + Nagel for end-to-end supply chain solutions.

## Market Position & Competition

Maersk consistently ranks among the top three global container shipping lines, competing primarily with Mediterranean Shipping Company (MSC) and CMA CGM Group. The industry has seen significant consolidation through alliances, with Maersk being a founding member of the 2M Alliance with MSC.

## Financial Performance & Challenges

The company experienced extraordinary profitability during the 2021-2022 period due to pandemic-related supply chain disruptions and sky-high freight rates. However, the industry has since normalized with rates returning to more historical levels, creating earnings pressure.

## Sustainability Initiatives

Maersk has set ambitious decarbonization targets, aiming for net-zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2040. The company is investing in green methanol-powered vessels and alternative fuels, positioning itself as a sustainability leader in an industry traditionally heavy on emissions.

## Cultural & Historical Significance

The company holds significant cultural importance in Denmark, representing one of the country's most iconic brands. The seven-pointed white star on a light blue background is one of the most recognized symbols in global shipping.
