**Question:** What does "travel package" mean in the context of this SIM card product description?

**Answer:**

In this context, **"travel package"** refers to the data/service bundle that's loaded onto the SIM card — specifically the **10GB of data usable in Greater China**, tied to the HK$138 price.

The distinction being made is:

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Travel package** | The data allowance bundle (10GB, valid for the China/HK network) |
| **Prepaid SIM card** | The physical card itself |

The validity note is saying two slightly different things:

- The **travel package** (your 10GB data) activates and starts its 365-day clock **the moment you first use it**
- The **prepaid SIM card** itself is also valid for 365 days **after activation**

In practice for this product they're the same — both expire 365 days after you activate. The wording is just SIM industry boilerplate distinguishing the *card* from the *data bundle on the card*, since some SIMs let you reload different packages onto the same card.

**TL;DR:** "Travel package" = the 10GB Greater China data plan included with this SIM.
