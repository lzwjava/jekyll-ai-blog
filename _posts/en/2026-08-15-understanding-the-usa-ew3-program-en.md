## Question: What's USA EW3 migration program?

## Answer

**EW3** is a U.S. employment-based immigration category under **EB-3 (Employment-Based Third Preference)**. It is also called:

> **EB-3 Other Workers (Unskilled Workers)**

It is a **green card pathway**, not a temporary work visa. A U.S. employer sponsors a foreign worker for permanent residence. ([U.S. Department of State - Travel][1])

The EB-3 category has three groups:

| Category             | Meaning           | Requirement                           |
| -------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| EB-3 Skilled Worker  | Skilled jobs      | Usually ≥2 years training/experience  |
| EB-3 Professional    | Professional jobs | Bachelor's degree or equivalent       |
| **EW3 Other Worker** | "Unskilled" jobs  | Less than 2 years training/experience |

([U.S. Department of State - Travel][2])

Examples of EW3 jobs:

* caregivers
* hospitality workers
* food processing workers
* agricultural workers
* cleaners
* some factory workers
* maintenance workers

The job must generally be **permanent, full-time**, and the employer must show there are not enough available U.S. workers for the position. ([Family Immigration Services][3])

---

## Process

Typical flow:

```
US Employer
    |
    | 1. Offer permanent job
    |
    v
DOL PERM Labor Certification
    |
    v
USCIS I-140 immigrant petition
    |
    v
Priority date wait
    |
    v
Consular processing / Adjustment of Status
    |
    v
Green Card
```

The employer normally files the petition; the worker cannot simply apply alone. ([U.S. Department of State - Travel][1])

## Advantages

Compared with many immigration routes:

✅ No bachelor's degree required
✅ No extraordinary talent requirement
✅ Can bring spouse and children
✅ Leads to U.S. permanent residence

---

## Disadvantages

### 1. Long waiting time

EW3 has annual limits. EB-3 overall receives a fixed percentage of employment immigrant visas, and EW3 has an additional cap (up to 10,000 visas annually). ([U.S. Department of State - Travel][4])

Countries with heavy demand (especially India, China, Philippines in many categories) may face longer queues.

### 2. Employer dependency

You need a legitimate employer willing to:

* pay immigration costs
* complete PERM recruitment
* wait years

Many "agents" selling EW3 packages are risky. Fraud is common.

### 3. Job quality

EW3 is designed for labor shortages, not high-skill immigration.

For someone with a software/AI background:

```
EW3  ❌ usually not attractive

EB-2 NIW  ✅
EB-1A     ✅
H-1B -> EB-2/EB-3  ✅
F-1 -> OPT -> employment green card ✅
```

---

## For your background (AI engineer, LLM training, software)

EW3 is probably **not the right path**.

Your profile maps much closer to:

* **EB-2 NIW** (advanced degree / exceptional ability + national interest)
* **EB-1A** (extraordinary ability, if publications/products/impact grow)
* **U.S. Master's → OPT → AI job → EB route**

EW3 is mainly useful for people without degrees or specialized skills who have a willing employer sponsor.
