Getting Hong Kong Permanent Residency (PR) and a Hong Kong Permanent Identity Card offers several significant benefits for mainland Chinese citizens:

**Travel Freedom**
- Hong Kong passport holders enjoy visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to over 170 countries and territories, including the EU, UK, Japan, and many others - far more than mainland Chinese passports
- Easier international travel without lengthy visa application processes

**Tax Advantages**
- Hong Kong has a simple, low tax system with a maximum personal income tax rate of 17% (or standard rate of 15%)
- No capital gains tax, no sales tax/VAT, and no tax on offshore income
- Territorial tax system means you're only taxed on Hong Kong-sourced income

**Business and Employment**
- Freedom to work in Hong Kong without employment visa restrictions
- Easier access to international financial services and banking
- No restrictions on starting businesses in Hong Kong
- Gateway for international business operations

**Education**
- Access to Hong Kong's education system with local tuition rates (much lower than international student fees)
- Easier admission to Hong Kong universities
- International-standard education with English instruction

**Social Benefits**
- Access to Hong Kong's public healthcare system
- Social welfare benefits for permanent residents
- Right of abode - can live, work, and study in Hong Kong indefinitely without restrictions

**Dual Status Flexibility**
- Can maintain mainland Chinese citizenship while holding Hong Kong PR
- Able to apply for Home Return Permit (回乡证) for easier travel between Hong Kong and mainland

Keep in mind that obtaining PR typically requires 7 years of continuous ordinary residence in Hong Kong, and there are specific requirements and conditions to maintain this status.
