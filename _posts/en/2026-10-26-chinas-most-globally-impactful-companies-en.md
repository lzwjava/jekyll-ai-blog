**Answer:**

China has dozens of companies with substantial global footprints. Based on the **2026 China Enterprises Global Impact Report** and the **2026 Fortune Global 500**, here are the most notable ones across key sectors:

### 🏆 Top 10 Most Globally Impactful Chinese Companies (2026)

According to the 2026 China Enterprises Global Impact Report, released at the China Enterprises Global Impact Dialogue, the top 10 Chinese companies with the greatest global impact are:

1. **Lenovo Group**
2. **SHEIN**
3. **ByteDance** (TikTok's parent)
4. **BYD**
5. **PetroChina**
6. **Huawei**
7. **CMOC Group**
8. **Luxshare Precision**
9. **Haier Smart Home**
10. **COSCO Shipping Holdings**

These companies are entering a new phase of globalization — moving beyond exports toward supply-chain expansion, technology deployment, localized operations, and brand building overseas. Their combined overseas operating costs reached 7.93 trillion yuan, up 15%.

### 📊 Scale of Chinese Global Business

In the 2026 Fortune Global 500, **122 Chinese companies** made the list, ranking second only to the United States. Beijing alone is home to 42 Fortune Global 500 headquarters — more than Tokyo (26) and New York (15) combined.

Huawei was the highest-ranked Chinese high-tech company by revenue, reporting over **$122.5 billion**. BYD recorded **$111.85 billion** in revenue, making it the highest-ranked Chinese automaker. Tencent entered the Fortune Global 500 top 100 for the first time.
