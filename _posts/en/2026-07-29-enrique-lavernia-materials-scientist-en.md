Question: Who is Enrique J. Lavernia?

Answer: Cuban-American materials scientist, born 1960. Not AI/CS — his field is nanostructured materials, spray atomization deposition, high-entropy alloys, powder metallurgy.

Career path: Brown University for a BS in solid mechanics (1982), then MIT for MS (1984) and PhD (1986) in materials engineering. Joined UC Irvine in 1987, chaired Chemical Engineering & Materials Science, moved to UC Davis in 2002 where he served as Dean of the College of Engineering (2002–2009, 2011–2015).

In April 2015 he was appointed provost and executive vice chancellor of UC Irvine, effective July 1, reporting to Chancellor Howard Gillman — a role he held until 2021. He's currently a Distinguished Professor of Materials Science and Engineering at UC Irvine's Samueli School of Engineering, though one source (Grokipedia) lists a more recent move to Texas A&M — worth verifying directly if that matters to you, since Grokipedia isn't always reliable.

Credentials: elected to the National Academy of Engineering in 2013, fellow of TMS, Materials Research Society, ASME, AAAS, and ASM International, with over 600 journal publications, 200 conference publications, and 11 patents.
