Question: How to play the hero Kai (also known as 铠/Kai) in King of Glory (王者荣耀)?

Answer:

Kai (铠) is a versatile fighter hero in King of Glory (王者荣耀), excelling as a top laner (confrontation road) or jungler. He features high burst damage, sustain through self-healing, and a powerful ultimate that grants invincibility-like buffs, making him ideal for diving backlines, dueling, and team fights. His playstyle revolves around "one-knife" bursts using stored enhanced basic attacks, precise skill combos, and leveraging his passive for extra damage on single targets.

### Skills Overview

- **Passive: Soul Guard** - Basic attacks and skills deal bonus damage to enemies hit only once. The first target hit is slowed. Hitting enemies grants Kai movement speed, health regen, and cooldown reduction.
- **Skill 1: Vortex Blade (回旋之刃)** - Throws a blade that bounces between enemies, dealing physical damage, slowing the first target, and healing Kai based on damage dealt. Great for poke, sustain, and wave clear.
- **Skill 2: Extreme Tempest Blade (极刃风暴)** - Dashes forward twice, dealing damage and knocking back enemies. Stores an enhanced basic attack after use, which deals massive bonus damage. Core for engages and "stored knife" bursts.
- **Ultimate: Invincible Warrior (灵魂罗刹)** - Summons demon armor, dealing AoE magic damage, boosting attack, max HP, movement speed, and providing damage reduction/block. Lasts ~5s; Kai becomes a "super soldier" for dives.

**Skill Priority**: Max Skill 1 first for sustain/poke, then Skill 2. Unlock Ultimate whenever available.

**Summoner Spells**: Flash (for repositions) or Smite (jungle). Exhaust/Weakening for top lane duels.

### Recommended Arcana (Inscriptions/Mingwen)

Focus on crit, penetration, and attack speed for burst. Common setups:

**Alternatives**: For tankier top lane, swap some Hidden Disaster for Fate (宿命) for HP.

### Item Builds

Kai builds semi-tank for sustain or full burst for snowballs. Core: Shadow War Axe (暗影战斧) for CDR, HP, and pen.

#### Top Lane (Semi-Tank Sustain)

1. Resistance Boots (抵抗之靴) - Tenacity vs CC.
2. Shadow War Axe.
3. Frostbite Storm (极寒风暴) - CDR, armor, slow on hit.
4. Grandmaster's Force (宗师之力) - Crit, enhanced AA damage.
5. Phoenix Eye (不死鸟之眼) - Magic resist, low-HP heal.
6. Endless Blade (无尽战刃) - Crit, burst.

#### Jungle (Burst/One-Shot)

1. Pursuit Blade (追击刀锋) - Jungle clear.
2. Resistance Boots.
3. Shadow War Axe.
4. Ice Grip (冰痕之握) or Violent Armor (暴烈之甲) - Stick/sustain.
5. Grandmaster's Force.
6. Endless Blade or Breaker (破军) for executes.

**Late Game Flex**: Nameless Blade (名刀司命) for survival, Revive Armor if ahead.

### Combo Guide

Kai's damage spikes from **stored enhanced AA** after Skill 2. Always "store knife" in bush/jungle before fights.

- **Basic Poke/Duel**: 1 > AA > 2 > Stored AA > 3 > AA (heal + burst).
- **One-Shot Dive**: Store AA (2) > 3 (Ult) > Stored AA > 2 > 1 > AA. Cancel Ult animation with 2 for speed.
- **Team Fight**: Wait for enemy CC > Flash/2 in > 3 > Stored AA > 2 > 1. Target backline.
- **Escape**: 1 (slow/heal) > 2 (dash) > 3 (buff speed).

**Pro Tip**: 2 stores enhanced AA - use in grass for ambushes. Ult first in combos to avoid overwriting stored AA.

### Playstyle & Tips

- **Early Game (Top/Jungle)**: Farm safely. Use 1 for poke/heal. Gank mid post-4 (Ult unlock). Avoid overextending pre-6.
- **Mid Game**: Rotate for objectives. Dive carries during Ult.
- **Late Game**: Frontline bait, then Ult-flip to ADCs. Tanky build survives dives.
- **Counters**: Kite-heavy mages (e.g., long-range). Play aggressive vs immobile ADCs.
- **Synergies**: Tanks/protectors (e.g., supports with CC).
- **Common Mistakes**: Ult before storing AA; diving first in fights; ignoring sustain with 1.

Master combos and timing for "Kai Daddy" one-shots. Practice in custom modes!
