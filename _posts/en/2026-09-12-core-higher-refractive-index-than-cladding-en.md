Question: In fiber optics, what are the refractive indices of the core and cladding, and which one has a higher refractive index?

Answer:

In fiber optics, the **core** (纤芯) always has a higher refractive index than the **cladding** (包层).

This difference in refractive index is essential for light transmission. Fiber optics rely on **total internal reflection** to guide light signals down the fiber without leaking out through the sides. For total internal reflection to occur, light must travel from a medium with a higher refractive index to a medium with a lower refractive index at an angle greater than the critical angle.

### Typical Refractive Indices

* **Core ($n_1$):** Typically around **1.45 to 1.48** (depending on the doping materials like Germanium).
* **Cladding ($n_2$):** Slightly lower, typically around **1.44 to 1.46** (pure silica glass).

The difference between the two refractive indices is intentionally kept very small—usually only about **0.3% to 1%**.
