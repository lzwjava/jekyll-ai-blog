Dario Amodei was born in **1983 in San Francisco, California**, to a diverse family. ## 💼 From OpenAI to Founding Anthropic

Before founding Anthropic, Amodei's career trajectory placed him at the heart of the AI revolution.

### Early Career Stops
His industry career began with stints at major tech companies, working on foundational AI problems. From late 2014 to 2015, he worked at **Baidu**'s Silicon Valley AI Lab, followed by a role at **Google** working on speech recognition .

### Key Tenure at OpenAI
In **2016**, Amodei joined OpenAI, a move that would define the next phase of his career . He quickly rose to become **Vice President of Research**, where he played a pivotal role in developing some of the most significant large language models (LLMs) .
- He **led the development** of **GPT-2** and **GPT-3**, models that demonstrated the immense power and potential of scaling up LLMs .

Despite these successes, a fundamental disagreement began to form. Amodei grew increasingly concerned that OpenAI was prioritizing **commercialization over a methodical and cautious approach to AI safety** . ### Founding Anthropic
In **2021**, this "values split" came to a head. Dario Amodei left OpenAI, taking a group of like-minded researchers with him . Together with his sister **Daniela Amodei** and other former senior OpenAI employees, he co-founded **Anthropic** . The company's very mission statement reflected their core philosophy: to **"build reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems"** .

## 🏛️ Anthropic: A Company Built on Safety

Under Dario Amodei's leadership as CEO, Anthropic has carved out a distinct identity in the AI landscape, centered on its "safety-first" approach.

### Key Innovations
- **Constitutional AI (CAI):** This is Anthropic's flagship safety technique. Instead of relying solely on human feedback to shape model behavior, CAI uses a set of written principles (a "constitution") to allow the AI to self-critique and correct its own outputs . This aims to make AI models more aligned with human values in a more transparent and scalable way.
- **Claude Model Family:** Anthropic's product line is the **Claude** series of AI assistants. Models like **Claude 3** and **Claude 4** have been lauded for their strong reasoning, coding, and safety performance, often competing directly with offerings from OpenAI and Google .

### Business Success
This principled approach has not hindered commercial success. Anthropic has secured massive investments from tech giants, including **Amazon (reportedly $4 billion)** and **Google (reportedly $2 billion)** . By 2025, the company's valuation was estimated to be around **$60 billion** .
