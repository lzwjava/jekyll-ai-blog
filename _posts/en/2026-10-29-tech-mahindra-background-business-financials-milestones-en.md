**Question:** What is Tech Mahindra — its background, business, financials, and notable milestones?

---

**Answer:**

**Tech Mahindra** is an Indian multinational IT services and consulting company, part of the well-known **Mahindra Group** conglomerate.

---

### 🏢 Origins & History

Tech Mahindra was founded on **October 24, 1986**, by Anand Mahindra, originally as a 60:40 joint venture between Mahindra & Mahindra and British Telecom (BT Group), under the name **Mahindra British Telecom (MBT)**. In its early years, it primarily served the telecommunications industry, with BT as its largest client.

In 2006, MBT was renamed **Tech Mahindra** and went public via an IPO. By December 2012, British Telecom had fully divested its stake, leaving Tech Mahindra as a fully Indian-controlled company.

---

### 📈 Transformative Acquisition: Satyam

Following the Satyam accounting scandal, Tech Mahindra emerged as the winning bidder for a 31% controlling stake in **Satyam Computer Services** in April 2009, beating out Larsen & Toubro. On June 25, 2013, Tech Mahindra completed the full merger with Mahindra Satyam, becoming **India's fifth-largest software services company** with a turnover of $2.7 billion. This merger diversified revenue mix significantly — telecom's share fell from over 90% in 2009 to 47% by 2013.

### 💼 Business Mix

For the year ended March 2025, approximately **85.9%** of revenue came from IT consulting, software application development, and maintenance, while the remaining **14.1%** came from business process services and outsourcing.

---

### 🌍 Global Reach & Notable Facts

- **Headquarters:** Pune, Maharashtra, India, with worldwide operations.
- **Leadership:** Anand Mahindra serves as Chairman, with Mohit Joshi as MD & CEO.
- **Stock listing:** Listed on both BSE (532755) and NSE (TECHM), and a constituent of both the BSE SENSEX and NSE NIFTY 50 indices.
- **Chess:** Since 2023, Tech Mahindra and FIDE have co-organized the **Global Chess League**, a rapid chess franchise competition.
- **AI:** In 2025, Tech Mahindra was selected as one of eight entities by the IndiaAI Mission to develop a **sovereign foundation LLM**.

---

### 🔑 Growth Through Acquisitions

Tech Mahindra has been highly acquisitive, with deals spanning telecom, banking, healthcare, design, cloud, and consulting. Notable buys include Lightbridge Communications ($240M, 2014), Pininfarina — the iconic Italian automotive design house (€168M, 2015), and Com Tec Co (€310M, 2022).
