## Overview

Genshin Impact is a free-to-play open-world action role-playing game (ARPG) developed and published by HoYoverse (formerly miHoYo). Released on September 28, 2020, it features stunning anime-style visuals, a massive fantasy world called Teyvat, and real-time combat centered around seven elements. The game supports cross-platform play and progression across PC, mobile, PlayStation, and Xbox (PS4 support ends April 2026). As of December 2025, it's on **Version 6.2 "Luna III"** (live until January 13, 2026), with ongoing updates every 6 weeks adding new characters, events, and story content.

## Story

You play as the Traveler (male or female), a star-crossed wanderer exploring worlds with your twin sibling. Upon entering Teyvat, you're attacked by an unknown god (Asmoday), separating you from your sibling and sealing your powers. You awaken 500 years later with floating companion Paimon, embarking on a quest to reunite while uncovering Teyvat's secrets. Teyvat has seven nations, each ruled by an Archon (god) tied to an element: Mondstadt (Anemo), Liyue (Geo), Inazuma (Electro), Sumeru (Dendro), Fontaine (Hydro), Natlan (Pyro), and Snezhnaya (Cryo).

## Core Gameplay

- **Exploration**: Traverse a seamless open world—climb mountains, glide from cliffs, swim, solve puzzles, collect resources (chests, oculi, ores), and discover hidden areas. Unlock Teleport Waypoints and Statues of the Seven for fast travel and stamina upgrades.
- **Combat**: Real-time action with a party of 4 characters (switch freely). Each has:
  - Normal/Charged/Plunging Attacks.
  - **Elemental Skill** (cooldown-based ability).
  - **Elemental Burst** (powerful ultimate, needs Energy).
  Build teams around **elemental reactions** (e.g., Pyro + Hydro = Vaporize for big damage; Electro + Dendro = Aggravate).

| Element | Color | Common Reaction Examples |
| --------- | -------- | -------------------------- |
| Anemo (Wind) | Green | Swirl (spreads other elements) |
| Geo (Earth) | Yellow | Crystallize (shields) |
| Electro (Lightning) | Purple | Overload, Electro-Charged |
| Dendro (Nature) | Teal | Bloom, Hyperbloom |
| Hydro (Water) | Blue | Freeze, Vaporize |
| Pyro (Fire) | Red | Melt, Burning |
| Cryo (Ice) | Cyan | Freeze, Superconduct |

- **Gacha System**: Use Primogems (earned via quests/exploration) for "Wishes" on banners to pull 5-star characters/weapons (pity system guarantees after ~90 pulls).
- **Progression**: Raise **Adventure Rank (AR)** through quests and exploration to unlock features (e.g., Dailies at AR12). Level characters/weapons with materials from bosses/domains (uses limited **Resin**). Other systems: Cooking (stat buffs), Crafting, Housing (Serenitea Pot), Fishing.
- **Multiplayer**: Co-op for domains/bosses (AR16+), up to 4 players.

## How to Get Started

1. **Create Account**: Sign up on HoYoverse site/app for cross-save (link platforms).
2. **Download & Launch**: ~25GB install. Choose server (e.g., America, Europe, Asia).
3. **Tutorial**: Pick Traveler gender, follow Prologue Act I (Mondstadt intro).
4. **Prioritize**:
   - **Archon Quests** (main story)—unlocks areas/rewards.
   - Explore Mondstadt fully (chests = Primogems).
   - Unlock Statues of the Seven.
   - Reach AR12 for Daily Commissions (60 Primogems/day).
5. **First Team**: Use starters (Traveler, Amber, Lisa, Kaeya). Pull on beginner banner for free 5-stars.

## Beginner Tips

- **Explore Everything**: 100% maps for Primogems (use Teyvat Interactive Map online).
- **Daily Routine**: Commissions, Resin on talents/bosses, events.
- **Resource Focus**: Build 1-2 teams (don't level everyone). Farm Mora/EXP efficiently.
- **Resin Management**: Don't waste—prioritize character talents > weapons > bosses.
- **Free Rewards**: HoYoLAB check-ins, codes, birthdays (refills Resin).
- **Avoid Pitfalls**: Save Primogems for limited banners; learn reactions via trial domains.
- Play 1-2 hours/day initially—it's grindy but rewarding.

Genshin is F2P-friendly with 50+ hours of story/exploration before needing pulls. It's ongoing, so jump in anytime!
