**Question:** How is this kind of "employment service" content typically offered in Chinese AI/tech training courses, and what should I think about it?

**Answer:**

This is a standard **Chinese vocational training sales pitch** — you'll see this pattern across BootCamp-style courses on platforms like Lagou Education, Geek Time, Mooc.cn, and countless WeChat-based cohort courses. Let me break it down honestly.

---

## What They're Offering (Decoded)

| Claim | Reality |
|---|---|
| "面试指导/模拟" (mock interviews) | Usually generic, not role-specific. Scripted Q&A prep. |
| "简历一对一指导" (1:1 resume help) | Template-filling assistance. Rarely differentiated. |
| "工作内推" (internal referral) | The word "内推" is heavily abused. Usually just submitting your resume via an employee referral link — not a guaranteed fast track. |
| "不包括外包" (no outsourcing roles) | A selling point, but hard to verify. Many "内推" roles are still contractors at big companies. |
| "试用期保驾护航" (probation support) | Means you can ask the teacher questions during probation. Low commitment. |

---

## The Conditions (Red Flags)

The fine print is telling:

- **Must complete all coursework, no absences, no late submissions** — accountability is on you
- **Must score ≥85 on their internal exam** — their exam, their grading
- **"皆为课上所讲内容"** — if you fail, they can say you didn't study hard enough

This is a **conditional guarantee** that's easy for them to void. If you don't get a job, they'll say you missed a class or scored 84.

---

## The "内推免笔试" Claim

> "有一部分岗位内推甚至不用参加笔试"

This is the hook. It's occasionally true at smaller companies with referral programs. At tier-1 companies (ByteDance, Alibaba, Tencent, Huawei), internal referrals skip resume screening but almost never skip technical interviews or written tests. Don't believe otherwise.

## My Honest Take (Given Your Profile)

You have 12 years of real experience, have trained GPT-2 760M on MI300X, built products with 30K users, and consume 1B tokens/month. **You don't need this.** This service is designed for:

- Fresh graduates with no portfolio
- Career switchers with no technical background
- People who need structured accountability to learn basics

For someone at your level, the "employment service" adds zero value. Your GitHub, your blog at lzwjava.github.io, and your actual model training experience are 100x more credible than any referral from a Bootcamp instructor.

---

## When These Services Are Actually Useful

- You're a complete beginner needing structure
- You have zero network in the target industry
- The course teaches something genuinely hard to self-learn (rare)

Otherwise, the **real ROI** from these courses is the curriculum content itself — not the job placement wrapper.
