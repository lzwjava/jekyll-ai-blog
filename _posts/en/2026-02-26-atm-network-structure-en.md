Answer:

The complete and correct statement is:

The ATM network environment consists of two parts: **ATM terminal users** and **ATM network**.

In the context of ATM (Asynchronous Transfer Mode) networking, the system is typically divided into:

- **ATM terminal users** (or ATM end systems / user equipment): devices such as computers, routers with ATM interfaces, LAN switches with ATM uplinks, video codecs, etc. that connect to the ATM network.
- **ATM network**: the core infrastructure consisting of ATM switches, transmission links, and related network management functions that provide the high-speed cell-switching service between endpoints.
