**Quick verdict**

- It's basically an **i7-7700 without the integrated GPU** — same Kaby Lake architecture, 4 cores / 8 threads, 3.5–3.9 GHz. Multi-thread performance beats a 4-core i5-7500 thanks to hyper-threading.
- No iGPU, so you **must pair it with a discrete graphics card**.
- Socket is **LGA 1151**, compatible with Intel 200-series chipsets (B250/H270/Z270) and some C232/C236 boards. Check BIOS support before buying — not all consumer boards accept Xeons.
- At ~$30–50 for a pulled unit, it's a **good value upgrade** if you already have a compatible board and a spare GPU.
- It's from 2017. For a brand-new build today, a modern i3-12100 or Ryzen 5 5600 offers far better performance per dollar and a real upgrade path.

Worth it mainly as a cheap drop-in for an existing 7th-gen platform, not as a basis for a new system.
