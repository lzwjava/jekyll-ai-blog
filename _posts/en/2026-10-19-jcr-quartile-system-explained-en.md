**Question:** What is the international JCR quartile system (国外的 JCR 分区)?

---

**Answer:**

The international JCR quartile system is maintained by **Clarivate Analytics** through the **Journal Citation Reports (JCR)** database. It is the globally recognized standard for ranking academic journals.

### What is JCR?

The Journal Citation Reports (JCR) provides transparent, publisher-neutral data and statistics to help researchers make informed decisions about scholarly publishing. It uses the **Journal Impact Factor (JIF)** to rank journals within subject categories.

### The Q1–Q4 Quartile System

Journals in the JCR are divided into four quartiles within each subject category:

| Quartile | Range | Meaning |
|----------|-------|---------|
| **Q1** | Top 25% | Most prestigious, highest impact |
| **Q2** | 26–50% | Strong, well-regarded journals |
| **Q3** | 51–75% | Moderate impact |
| **Q4** | Bottom 25% | Lowest tier in that category |

### Key Points

1. **Category-specific:** Because assignment happens per category, a journal listed in two Web of Science subject categories receives two separate ranks and can land in two different quartiles — for example, Q1 in a narrow specialty category and Q2 or Q3 in a broader adjacent one.

2. **Annual updates:** Rankings are recalculated every year based on new citation data, meaning a journal can rise to Q1 or drop to Q2.

3. **JCR vs. SCImago (SJR):** SCImago uses the SJR metric and Scopus categories, while Journal Citation Reports uses the Impact Factor and its own categories, so the quartiles from the two systems need not agree.

4. **Academic significance:** Many universities and research committees have specific requirements for publications in Q1 or Q2 journals for tenure and promotions.

### How to Check

- **JCR** (requires institutional access): [jcr.clarivate.com](https://jcr.clarivate.com)
- **SCImago** (free): [scimagojr.com](https://www.scimagojr.com)

> **Note vs. China's system:** China uses its own domestic "中科院分区" (CAS quartile) system which divides journals differently (into 4 zones for each discipline). The international JCR Q1–Q4 system above is the standard used outside China.
