This passage analyzes the argumentative structure and writing technique of Liang Qichao's essay "On Perseverance" (《论毅力》). Let me explain it in detail:

**The three layers of argumentation in the essay:**

1. **Life inevitably encounters both favorable and adverse circumstances** - The essay argues that everyone will necessarily face both good times and hardships in life; this is an objective fact

2. **Favorable and adverse circumstances can transform into each other** - It explains that good times and bad times are not fixed or permanent, but can convert into one another under certain conditions

3. **The value lies in persistence** - The conclusion emphasizes that what matters most is perseverance and persistence, regardless of whether you're facing favorable or adverse situations

**The writing technique:**

Throughout the entire essay, Liang Qichao employs the method of **"正反对举" (contrasting positive and negative examples)** - this means he consistently uses comparisons between opposing situations, contrasting examples, or antithetical arguments to make his points more compelling and clear.

This rhetorical technique helps strengthen the argument by showing both sides of an issue and demonstrating why perseverance is essential in all circumstances.
