**Question: What does “Hong Kong immigration photo meeting requirements” mean?**

Answer:

It means the **passport-style digital photo you upload for your Hong Kong immigration application (for example QMAS)** must follow Hong Kong Immigration Department's photo rules. It is not a normal selfie. ([Immigration Department][1])

Main requirements:

* **Recent color photo**
* **Plain white background**
* **Front-facing face**
* Face must be clear (eyes, nose, mouth visible)
* No hat/head covering
* No strong makeup
* No shadows
* No reflection on glasses
* Face centered
* Normal expression ([Immigration Department][1])

Digital file requirements (for online submission):

* Format: **JPEG**
* File size: **5 MB or below**
* Recommended image size:

  * At least **1200 px (width) × 1600 px (height)** if taken by digital camera ([Immigration Department Web App][2])

Physical photo equivalent:

* Size: **40 mm × 50 mm**
* Chin to crown: **32–36 mm** ([Immigration Department][1])

For your **QMAS online application**, prepare a normal Hong Kong visa/passport photo:

```
photo.jpg
- white background
- 1200x1600 or higher
- JPEG
- <5MB
```

A phone photo can work if:

1. You stand against a white wall,
2. Use good lighting,
3. Camera at eye level,
4. Crop to passport ratio,
5. Export as JPEG.

Many photo shops can also provide “Hong Kong visa photo” directly. ([Photobi][3])
