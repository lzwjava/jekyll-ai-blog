## 4. One major warning: online degree versus Chinese credential recognition

If your goal is to overcome HR filtering in China, this is potentially more important than the tuition.

China's education authorities state that overseas qualifications obtained through cross-border distance learning are generally outside the scope of their overseas degree verification service.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://portal.cscse.edu.cn\&sz=32)

CSCSE+1



That does not mean the degree is invalid. It means you must distinguish three different things:

1. Whether SBS is an accredited institution.
2. Whether SBS can legally award you this MSc.
3. Whether the specific online program and your study arrangement qualify for Chinese overseas credential verification.

The first has supporting evidence. The second and third require confirmation for your exact enrollment route. Don't rely on the brochure's broad statement that the degree is recognized in China.
