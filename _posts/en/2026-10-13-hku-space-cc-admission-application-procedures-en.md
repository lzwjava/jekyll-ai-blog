**Question:** What are the admission application procedures for HKU SPACE Community College?

**Answer:**

This page outlines the admission application procedures for **HKU SPACE Community College (CC)**, which is part of HKU SPACE (The University of Hong Kong School of Professional and Continuing Education).

> ⚠️ **Note:** All applications for 2026/27 admissions have been closed. The information below reflects the most recently published cycle.

---

### Application Channels

Applications could be submitted via the Government's eApp platform or the college's own Online Application portal.

---

### Application Fees

Local applicants pay **HK$200**, while non-local applicants pay **HK$600**.

### Important Notes

- Applicants will be notified of interview details by email, and successful applicants receive a Conditional Offer via email.
- For non-current HKDSE applicants, a Firm Offer (rather than a Conditional Offer) is normally issued if academic documents are in order, and applicants are generally required to settle the 1st instalment of tuition fee within 2–5 working days after the offer letter is issued.
- Students under the age of 18 are required to submit a Parent/Guardian Consent Form via the iEnrol platform before the start of the new academic year.
- Applicants must not offer advantages to College staff regarding their applications, as this may constitute an offence under Hong Kong's Prevention of Bribery Ordinance.
