Question: What does the QMAS application mean by the four categories of work experience, and what are the requirements?

Answer:

These fields are **not four separate requirements**. They are a summary of the qualifying work experience you enter in your application.

| QMAS field                                                              | What it means                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Work experience in multinational companies or reputable enterprises** | Years of **graduate/specialist-level** work at qualifying multinational or reputable companies                                             |
| **Work experience in specific fields/industries**                       | Years of graduate/specialist-level experience in **innovation & technology, finance, or international trade**                              |
| **Work experience with international exposure**                         | Years of work involving substantial **international exposure**, e.g. international business, overseas clients/teams, cross-border projects |
| **Total graduate or specialist level work experience**                  | Your **overall qualifying graduate/specialist-level work experience**, regardless of the above categories                                  |

The important distinction is that **one job can potentially count toward multiple categories**.

For example, if you worked as a software/AI engineer at a reputable multinational company on an international project:

```text
Job
 ├── Graduate/specialist-level experience       → counts toward TOTAL
 ├── Innovation & technology                     → counts toward SPECIFIC FIELD
 ├── International project/clients               → may count toward INTERNATIONAL EXPOSURE
 └── Reputable multinational enterprise          → may count toward REPUTABLE/MNC
```

### For your case

Given your ~11 years of software engineering experience, the key question isn't simply *"How many years have I worked?"*

It is:

> **How many of those years can I substantiate as graduate/specialist-level work, and how many fall into QMAS's specific categories?**

For the **specific-fields criterion**, Hong Kong explicitly says the assessment primarily looks at the **nature of your role, position/designation**, rather than merely the industry classification of your employer.

So a **Backend Engineer / AI Engineer / Software Engineer** working on software, AI, ML, cloud infrastructure, etc. can potentially support the **innovation and technology** category.

Your experience at companies such as **LeanCloud, Beyondsoft, HSBC/PayMe, and Meitai** should therefore be examined **job by job**, rather than simply adding all employment years together.

One particularly important point: **"graduate or specialist level" does not necessarily mean you must have a bachelor's degree for every year of experience.** It refers to the level/nature of the work. But you need credible documentary evidence showing that the role was genuinely professional/specialist-level.

If you're using QMAS's **General Points Test**, these experience categories matter because the scheme awards points for certain types of professional experience. The exact scoring/eligibility criteria should be checked against the current official QMAS rules rather than relying on older summaries.
