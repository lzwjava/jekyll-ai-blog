**Question: What photo format is required for the Online Nonimmigrant Visa Application (DS-160)?**

**Answer:**

For the **DS-160 Online Nonimmigrant Visa Application**, the uploaded visa photo must meet these requirements:

### Digital photo requirements

* **File format:** JPEG (`.jpg`)
* **Maximum file size:** **240 KB**
* **Dimensions:**

  * Square image: **600 × 600 pixels** minimum/standard
  * Must be between **600 × 600 and 1200 × 1200 pixels**
* **Color:** 24-bit color (sRGB)
* **Compression:** JPEG compression is allowed, but the image must remain clear

### Photo appearance requirements

* Taken within the **last 6 months**
* **Plain white or off-white background**
* Full face visible, looking directly at the camera
* Neutral facial expression (or natural expression)
* Both eyes open
* No glasses (since 2016, US visa photos generally do not allow glasses)
* No hats, headphones, or head coverings unless for religious reasons
* Normal everyday clothing (no uniforms except religious clothing worn daily)

### Face size requirements

* Head height should be about **50%–69% of the image height**
* Eyes should be positioned around **56%–69% from the bottom of the photo**

### Practical workflow

1. Take a passport-style photo at a photo shop.
2. Ask for:

   * "US visa digital photo"
   * JPEG format
   * 600×600 pixels
   * Under 240 KB
3. Upload it in DS-160.
4. Use the DS-160 photo validator:
   [https://ceac.state.gov/genniv/](https://ceac.state.gov/genniv/)

If the photo upload fails, you can still continue DS-160 and bring a printed compliant photo to your visa interview (depending on embassy instructions).
