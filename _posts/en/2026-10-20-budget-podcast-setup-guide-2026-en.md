## 🪜 Why Dynamic > Condenser for Cheap Setups

In 2026, every serious podcast mic recommendation starts with a **dynamic** microphone, because dynamics reject room reflections, plosives, and background noise in a way condensers physically cannot. This matters a lot when you're recording in a bedroom or untreated room — which most budget setups are.
