Question: **When did He Qinglian leave China?**

Answer: **June 13, 2001.** He Qinglian (何清涟), author of *The Trap of Modernization* (《现代化的陷阱》) and later *China: Collapsing Without Disintegrating* (《中国：溃而不崩》), left China for the United States in **June 2001**.

The details are quite dramatic:

* She had been under **continuous surveillance for about 1.5 years** because of her writing and criticism of corruption and China's political/economic system. ([Heqing Lian][1])
* She had arranged to go to the **University of Chicago as a visiting scholar**, originally planning to leave on **June 26, 2001**. ([人民报 renminbao.com][2])
* But in June, she became increasingly worried that authorities might fabricate charges against her. She said her home had been secretly searched and materials related to her contacts with the U.S. had been taken. ([人民报 renminbao.com][3])
* She therefore **left suddenly on June 13**, rather than following her original travel plan. She traveled to **Beijing**, bought a ticket at short notice, and flew to the United States with essentially no luggage. ([人民报 renminbao.com][2])
* She subsequently settled in the U.S. and worked as a visiting scholar at institutions including the University of Chicago, CUNY, and Princeton. ([Heqing Lian][1])

The deterioration of her situation caused her to leave much earlier and more abruptly than planned. ([人民报 renminbao.com][2])
