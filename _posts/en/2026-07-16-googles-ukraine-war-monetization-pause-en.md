That's a real, still-active Google Publisher Policy — not a mistake, not spam, not account-specific. Confirmed it's listed in Google's official Regional Monetization Pause Policies, dated March 23, 2022: "Due to the war in Ukraine, we will pause monetization of content that exploits, dismisses, or condones the war."

**Context:**

- It sits alongside related Ukraine/Russia-specific policies: a Feb 26, 2022 pause on monetizing Russian Federation state-funded media, a March 10, 2022 pause on new Russian AdSense/AdMob/Ad Manager accounts, and an August 12, 2024 pause on Russia-based publishers monetizing at all.
- The policy targets specific content, not just mentioning the war. Google's clarifying email specified it covers claims implying victims are responsible for their own tragedy — victim-blaming, e.g. claiming Ukraine is committing genocide or attacking its own citizens.
- It's not new — publishers have reported seeing this exact banner in their AdSense dashboard for years since it first appeared, and it's a recurring point of frustration since it dictates what publishers can say about the war while advertisers who don't want their ads near such content have no real recourse.

**Practical takeaway for you:** if you're running any AdSense-monetized site/blog and touch on Ukraine/Russia geopolitics, war casualties, or territorial disputes in a way that could read as "dismissive" or "condoning," expect demonetization on those specific pages (not necessarily full account suspension) — enforced via automated + manual review under the broader "Dangerous or Derogatory content" policy umbrella.

If you got this as an account-specific warning (not just browsing the policy page), that usually means a specific post/page got flagged — worth checking Policy Center in your AdSense dashboard for the exact URL.
