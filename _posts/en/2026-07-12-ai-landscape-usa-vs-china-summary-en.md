Question: Is the current AI model landscape China = Zhipu + DeepSeek, USA = OpenAI + Anthropic?

Answer:

**Roughly yes, but with some nuance.** If we talk about **frontier LLM companies with global influence**, the ranking looks more like this:

### 🇺🇸 United States

Top tier:

1. OpenAI

   * GPT series
   * Strongest consumer ecosystem (ChatGPT)
   * Strong developer ecosystem (API, agents, tools)
   * Huge compute advantage

2. Anthropic

   * Claude series
   * Very strong in coding, reasoning, enterprise usage
   * Strong alignment/research reputation

Close competitors:

* Google DeepMind (Gemini)

  * Actually a top-2/top-3 frontier lab technically
  * Has enormous TPU infrastructure and research talent

* Meta Platforms (Llama)

  * Dominates open-weight ecosystem
  * Very influential among researchers and developers

So a more complete US ranking might be:

```
OpenAI
Anthropic
Google DeepMind
Meta
xAI
```

---

### 🇨🇳 China

Top tier:

1. DeepSeek

   * Probably the strongest Chinese AI startup internationally right now
   * Strong efficiency/reasoning models
   * R1/V3 changed global perception of Chinese AI

2. Zhipu AI

   * GLM family
   * Strong academic background (Tsinghua)
   * Earlier mover in Chinese LLM ecosystem

But China is more crowded:

* Alibaba Cloud (Qwen)

  * Qwen is arguably one of China's strongest technical models
  * Huge open-source impact

* ByteDance (Doubao)

  * Huge user base
  * Strong engineering capability

* Baidu (ERNIE)

  * One of the earliest Chinese LLM players

A realistic China ranking is closer to:

```
DeepSeek
Alibaba Qwen
Zhipu
ByteDance
Baidu
Tencent
```
