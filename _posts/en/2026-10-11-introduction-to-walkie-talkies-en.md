## Walkie-Talkies (Two-Way Radios) — Introduction

A **walkie-talkie** (无线对讲机) is a handheld, portable radio transceiver that enables half-duplex voice communication over radio frequencies — one person transmits at a time while the other listens, then they switch (hence the "push-to-talk" or PTT button).

---

### How They Work

Walkie-talkies operate by converting your voice into a radio signal, broadcasting it on a specific **channel/frequency**, and receiving signals from others on the same channel. Key concepts:

- **Half-duplex** — only one party talks at a time (unlike a phone call)
- **PTT (Push-To-Talk)** — hold the button to speak, release to listen
- **Channels** — pre-set frequencies; both parties must be on the same channel
- **Squelch** — a noise-suppression feature that mutes static when no signal is received

---

### Common Frequency Bands

| Band | Frequency | Typical Use |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **VHF** | 136–174 MHz | Outdoors, open terrain, marine |
| **UHF** | 400–512 MHz | Urban, indoor, buildings |
| **FRS** (US) | 462–467 MHz | Consumer/personal use, no license |
| **GMRS** (US) | 462–467 MHz | Higher power, requires FCC license |
| **PMR446** (EU) | 446 MHz | European consumer band |

---

### Key Specifications to Know

- **Range** — affected by terrain, buildings, and power (marketed range is often ideal/open-field)
- **Output Power (Watts)** — higher wattage = longer range (FRS capped at 2W; GMRS up to 50W)
- **Battery type** — AA/AAA vs. rechargeable Li-ion
- **IP Rating** — dust/water resistance (e.g., IP67 = waterproof)
- **CTCSS/DCS Tones** — sub-audible tones that filter out unwanted chatter on a shared channel (not true privacy, just convenience)

---

### Types of Walkie-Talkies

1. **Consumer / FRS radios** — cheap, no license, limited range (~1–3 km realistic); Motorola T Series, Baofeng UV-5R
2. **Professional / Commercial radios** — more durable, programmable, licensed frequencies; Motorola DP series, Kenwood TK series
3. **Amateur (Ham) radios** — wide frequency coverage, requires a license; Yaesu FT-60, Baofeng UV-82
4. **Marine VHF radios** — waterproof, dedicated maritime channels (e.g., Channel 16 for distress)
5. **Aviation radios** — specialized for air-to-ground/air-to-air comms

---

### Licensing

- **No license needed**: FRS (US), PMR446 (EU), 免申請頻道 in some Asian regions
- **License required**: GMRS, Ham bands, commercial/professional frequencies
- In **Hong Kong**, the Communications Authority (OFCA) regulates radio device use; most consumer walkie-talkies sold locally operate on licensed-exempt low-power bands

---

### Common Applications

- Hiking, camping, outdoor recreation
- Construction sites and warehouses
- Event coordination and security
- Emergency/disaster preparedness
- Children's toys (low-power consumer models)
- Military and law enforcement
