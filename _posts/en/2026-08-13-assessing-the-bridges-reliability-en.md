Question: Is [https://www.join-thebridge.com](https://www.join-thebridge.com) reliable?

Answer:

Short answer: **it looks like a real startup accelerator/residency program, not an obvious scam, but I would verify details before applying or sharing sensitive information.** ([The Bridge][1])

What I found:

### Positive signals

* The website describes **The Bridge**, a San Francisco residency for early-stage founders, with an $250k upfront investment and connections to investors. ([The Bridge][1])
* It claims to be built by Entrepreneur First (EF), a known global startup builder. ([The Bridge][1])
* There is a LinkedIn company presence showing employees and describing it as an EF-related SF residency program. ([LinkedIn][2])
* Security scanners did not find malware/phishing indicators. One checker rated it around 60/100, noting no major blacklist detections. ([Gridinsoft LLC][3])

### Things to verify

* The domain is relatively new (registered in 2025 according to one analysis), so it does not have a long operating history. ([Scam Detector][4])
* Some automated reputation sites give mixed scores. For example, Scam Detector gave a medium-risk score, mainly because of limited history and automated signals, not because of proven fraud. ([Scam Detector][4])
* The big claims ($250k, investor access, alumni success) should be verified independently. The website itself is not enough proof. ([The Bridge][1])

For someone like you (AI engineer + founder background), the interesting question is **not "is it scam?" but "is it a better path than YC / EF / going directly to SF?"**

I would compare:

* YC → strongest brand, huge investor network, very competitive.
* EF → founder formation model, good for pre-idea founders.
* The Bridge → seems like an EF SF residency variant targeting international founders. ([The Bridge][1])

Before committing, I would ask them:

1. Is the $250k investment guaranteed or conditional?
2. What is the equity taken?
3. Who are the actual investment partners?
4. Can they provide alumni founders you can talk to?
5. Is the visa support real and what visa route?

For your profile (already built products, revenue history, AI infrastructure experience), I would probably also evaluate **YC directly** rather than only residency programs.
