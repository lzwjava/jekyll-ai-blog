Cathay Pacific Airways is Hong Kong's flagship airline and one of Asia's best-known premium airlines. It is headquartered at Hong Kong International Airport and is a major part of Hong Kong's aviation, tourism, and international business ecosystem.

### Company overview

* **Founded:** 1946
* **Headquarters:** Hong Kong
* **Industry:** Aviation / Airlines / Travel / Logistics
* **Employees:** 10,000+
* **Main businesses:**

  * Passenger airline
  * Air cargo
  * Airport services
  * Loyalty programs
  * Travel lifestyle services

Cathay operates under the broader Cathay Group structure, which includes:

* Cathay Pacific Airways — passenger airline
* Cathay Cargo — cargo/logistics
* HK Express — low-cost carrier

### Position in Hong Kong

Cathay is considered one of Hong Kong's "iconic companies" alongside companies such as HSBC, HKT, CK Hutchison, and Swire.

It plays an important role because:

* Hong Kong is a global financial center, and Cathay connects Hong Kong with major cities worldwide.
* Hong Kong International Airport is one of the world's busiest international aviation hubs.
* Many multinational companies rely on Cathay for business travel.

Cathay is also closely associated with Swire Pacific, one of Hong Kong's oldest and most influential business groups.
