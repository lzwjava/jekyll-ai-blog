Question: Do I need to take a short trip, for example to Hunan?

Answer:

Given your current situation, I think a short trip **could be valuable**, but not as a "vacation escape". More like a **reset + exploration + idea generation cycle**.

You have been doing very high-intensity abstract work recently:

* LLM training, GPUs, hardware repair
* immigration research (HK, US, Canada)
* business ideas
* career planning
* house/mortgage decisions
* learning English/Japanese
* building AI projects

This type of work has a hidden cost: your brain stays in "optimization mode" all the time. A few days away can create useful distance.

Hunan is actually a good choice because:

* cheap (low friction)
* close to Guangdong
* food/culture different enough
* mountains + nature (Zhangjiajie, Chenzhou, Hengshan, Dongjiang Lake)
* you can travel without spending a lot of money

I would not do a long tourist-style trip. Something like:

```
Guangzhou
  ↓ train
Changsha (1-2 days)
  ↓
Hengshan / Zhangjiajie / countryside (2-3 days)
  ↓
back home
```

The goal:

1. Walk without headphones for a while.
2. Read papers/books in a different environment.
3. Observe people and local economy.
4. Think about your next 1-3 year direction.

For someone like you, the highest value is probably not seeing attractions. It is getting **new input**.

Your usual loop:

```
computer
 ↓
code
 ↓
AI
 ↓
more computer
```

A short trip adds:

```
people
 ↓
cities
 ↓
business models
 ↓
new observations
 ↓
new ideas
```

Many founders do this. Not because they need rest, but because creativity needs environmental changes.

However, if you currently have a very concrete deadline (for example QMAS documents, a job interview, an important AI release), then don't disappear for a week. Do a **2-4 day trip**.

My recommendation for you:

* Yes, go.
* Keep it cheap.
* Go alone or with family depending on what you need.
* Bring a laptop optionally, but don't spend 12 hours coding.
* Use the trip to ask: "What kind of life/company do I want to build in the next 5 years?"

A 3-day Hunan trip probably gives you more marginal value than another 30 hours of reading AI news.
