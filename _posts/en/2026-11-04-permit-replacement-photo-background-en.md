Yes. For a Mainland China《往来港澳通行证》replacement (补发), both backgrounds are acceptable:

- Pure white (白色) — acceptable.
- Light blue (淡蓝色) — acceptable.

According to the National Immigration Administration's photo requirements, either is valid.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://www.shanwei.gov.cn\&sz=32)

Shanwei Government+2



Other photo requirements:

| Item       | Requirement                        |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Photo size | 33 × 48 mm                         |
| Background | Uniform white or light blue        |
| Photo age  | Within 6 months                    |
| Clothing   | Preferably dark, collared          |
| Expression | Neutral, mouth closed              |
| Face       | Clearly visible, no shadows        |
| Retouching | No modification of facial features |

My recommendation: Choose light blue or white. Both are fine. Ask the photo studio for:

> 往来港澳通行证证件照，33×48mm，白底或淡蓝底，符合出入境证件相片照相指引。
