Question: Is it better to focus only on objective fields like math, nature, coding, and physics — and ignore humans, politics, and society?

Answer:

This is a genuinely interesting philosophical position, and there's real appeal to it — but it has some important flaws worth examining honestly.

**What's attractive about this view**

- Math, physics, and nature operate on stable, consistent principles — they don't lie or manipulate you
- Reducing news/politics consumption genuinely *does* lower anxiety (there's solid research on this)
- Deep focus on objective domains produces real mastery and satisfaction
- It protects your mental energy from things largely outside your control

**Where it breaks down**

- **You are still a social animal** — human connection is one of the strongest predictors of wellbeing and longevity. Dismissing all human relationships doesn't lead to calm; it often leads to isolation and hidden bitterness.
- **Math, physics, and code exist *because* of human society** — they are deeply human endeavors. Separating them from humanity is artificial.
- **"Not caring" is different from "not being controlled by"** — the goal of calm is to engage with the world without being destabilized by it, not to disengage entirely.
- **Selective indifference is fragile** — politics and society will eventually affect you regardless (health policy, economics, technology regulation). Being uninformed doesn't make you immune.
- **It can become avoidance, not peace** — true calm means you *can* face difficult human realities without losing yourself, not that you never encounter them.

**A more balanced reframe**

| Instead of... | Try... |
|---|---|
| "I don't care about humans" | "I choose depth over noise" |
| "Ignore all politics/news" | "Limit news, stay informed minimally" |
| "Only objective things matter" | "I find meaning in ideas *and* people" |

The Stoics, mathematicians, and physicists you might admire — Euler, Newton, Marcus Aurelius — were all deeply embedded in human society. Their calm came from **not being ruled** by it, not from escaping it.
