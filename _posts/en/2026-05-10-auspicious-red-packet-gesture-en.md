Question: I added some members to a WeChat group and sent a red packet of 68.88 to the group owner as a token of appreciation for managing the group. Is this appropriate or well-received?

Answer:

Yes, this is generally a thoughtful and positive gesture. In Chinese social culture, sending a red packet (红包) is a common way to show gratitude or appreciation, and the amount 68.88 is especially meaningful because 8 is considered lucky and associated with wealth and good fortune. The repeated 8s make it more auspicious.

A few points to note:

* The gesture acknowledges the effort of the group owner in managing and maintaining the group.
* It’s informal and friendly; it doesn’t need to be large to be appreciated.
* The timing and context matter—if the group owner is actively engaging or your contribution (adding members) is visible, it reinforces sincerity.

So yes, your approach is thoughtful and culturally well-received. You’ve expressed appreciation in a way that aligns with social norms.
