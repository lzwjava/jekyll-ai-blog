Question: Is the MSI B250M-F + i5-7500 board/CPU combo worth 175 CNY?

Answer:

Short answer: **not bad, but 175 CNY is only OK if it is tested working. For "unknown condition / no return", I would not pay 175.**

Specs:

* CPU: **Intel Core i5-7500**

  * 7th gen Kaby Lake
  * 4 cores / 4 threads
  * 3.4 GHz base, 3.8 GHz turbo
  * DDR4-2400
  * Intel HD 630 iGPU
* Motherboard: **MSI B250M-F**

  * B250 chipset
  * LGA1151 (6th/7th gen only)
  * 2× DDR4 slots
  * M.2 slot
  * PCIe x16

Performance today:

* Office / NAS / router / small server: OK
* Linux machine: OK
* GPU repair test bench: actually useful
* LLM: very limited, but can run small models with GPU
* CPU-only inference: not attractive

Compared with your GPU repair / AI lab direction:

This platform has some advantages:

✅ Cheap DDR4 platform
✅ Native Kaby Lake support (no BIOS headache)
✅ iGPU output, useful for testing discrete GPUs
✅ Low power (~65W CPU)
✅ B250 is more stable than random H110 boards

But:

❌ Only 2 RAM slots
❌ No ECC
❌ No PCIe bifurcation / multi-GPU features
❌ i5-7500 is weak by 2026 standards
❌ Unknown-condition board risk

Price:

Used market examples put i5-7500 + B250M combos around roughly **50,000–70,000 KRW equivalent / ~50–70 CNY in some listings**, while some China listings are higher depending on condition and accessories. ([중고나라][1])

| Condition                                  | Reasonable price |
