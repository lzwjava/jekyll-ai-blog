## 毕业办理步骤 (Graduation Processing Steps)

### **Step 1: 确认信息 (Confirm Information)**

Before applying for graduation, log into the system and verify:

- Your name (姓名)
- Gender (性别)
- ID number (身份证号码)
- Electronic photo (电子相片)
- All course grades (各科成绩)

Only proceed after confirming all information is accurate.

**For undergraduate students**: You must also register your prior academic credentials (前置学历信息) before applying:

- Upper semester: Register by June 1st
- Lower semester: Register by December 1st

---

### **Step 2: 申请毕业并采集毕业证照 (Apply for Graduation & Collect Graduation Photo)**

**Timeline**:

- **Upper semester**: Mid-June (typically June 12-17)
- **Lower semester**: Mid-December (typically December 12-17)

**Process**:

1. **Log into the system**: <https://www.eeagd.edu.cn/selfec/>
   - Click "考生入口" (Student Entrance)
   - Navigate to the graduation management module (毕业管理模块)

2. **Submit graduation application**:
   - The system will automatically verify if you meet graduation requirements
   - Select your graduation processing location (毕业办理地点)
   - Fill in your contact phone number

3. **Collect graduation photo**:
   - The system directly collects the graduation photo and validates it against the student database and cross-checks with authoritative sources like the public security department's population database
   - **Important photo requirements**:
     - Use **rear camera** (后置摄像头), not front camera
     - Keep shoulders level (have someone help you take the photo)
     - Wear dark-colored clothing (avoid light colors as the background is white/light gray)
     - Avoid complex patterns or stripes

4. **Monitor status**: Check the system regularly for photo review results (审核结果)

---

### **Step 3: 打印毕业生登记表 (Print Graduate Registration Form)**

After your graduation photo is approved:

1. **Print the form**: Log into the system and print the《广东省高等教育自学考试毕业生登记表》
   - Print **double-sided** (双面打印)

2. **Fill out the form**:
   - **Front side**: Carefully verify all information
   - **Back side**: Fill in truthfully all required content
   - Get your employer or relevant organization to complete the "思想品德表现" (Ideological and Moral Performance) section and stamp it with their official seal
   - For unemployed persons, refer to the FAQ on the system homepage for guidance

---

### **Step 4: 现场提交材料 (Submit Materials On-site)**

**When**: According to your local examination office schedule (typically a few days after online application)

**Where**: Your selected local examination office (市考办/区招考办)

**Materials to bring**:

- Your ID card (original and photocopy) (身份证原件及复印件)
- Printed《毕业生登记表》(Graduate Registration Form)
- Any special materials required by your program

---

## Important Reminders

- **Browser**: Use Google Chrome or 360 Browser (avoid IE)
- **Deadlines are strict**: Late submissions won't be accepted
- **Monitor your application status** online regularly - no separate notifications will be sent
- All statuses and audit results are displayed in real-time in the system's graduation management module
