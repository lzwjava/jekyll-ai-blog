**Question:** What is Reka AI, is it good, and was it trained using GPT-4 in one year?

---

**Answer:**

**What is Reka AI?**

Reka AI is an enterprise-focused frontier AI lab that designs multimodal foundation models from scratch. It was founded in 2022 by former researchers from Google DeepMind, Google Brain, Meta FAIR, and Baidu.

The company focuses on ultra-efficient training and serving infrastructure to deliver inference at low cost. Its flagship is **Reka Flash**, a multimodal model capable of understanding video, images, text, and audio. It also offers **Reka Vision** (visual search and video understanding) and **Reka Research** (agentic web research platform).

**Is it good?**

Yes, it's well-regarded, especially for multimodal tasks:

- Reka Core ranked **second in multimodal chat** in a blinded third-party evaluation, just behind GPT-4V and ahead of all Claude 3 models. In text-only chat, it placed third behind GPT-4 Turbo and Claude 3 Opus.
- Its model lineup — Spark (1B), Edge (7B), Flash (21B), and Core (67B) — spans from lightweight embedded applications to heavy enterprise workloads. Reka Vision is its most mature product, enabling natural-language semantic search over large video archives.
- As of July 2025, Reka secured $110 million in funding backed by NVIDIA and Snowflake, reaching a $1B unicorn valuation.
- In June 2026, Reka merged with the physical-AI startup Moonvalley to push into world models and robotics.

The main downside: the platform lacks transparent public pricing and is clearly B2B/developer-oriented, making it less accessible to non-technical users or small teams.

**Was it trained using GPT-4 in one year?**

**No — this is a misconception.** Reka Core is a transformer model trained from scratch on a mix of publicly available and proprietary data, including text, images, videos, and audio clips. It was not distilled from or trained using GPT-4. Reka Core was developed from scratch in just a few months, primarily trained on NVIDIA H100 and A100 GPUs. The confusion may come from the fact that its *performance* is **comparable to GPT-4**, not that it was built from it.
