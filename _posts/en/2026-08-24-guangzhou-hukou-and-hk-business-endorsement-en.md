Question: **You are a Guangzhou hukou resident. Does that change how you apply for the Hong Kong business endorsement?**

Answer: **Yes, mainly in where you handle the Mainland-side application.** If your hukou is in Guangzhou, your normal starting point is the **Guangzhou Exit-Entry Administration (公安出入境)** rather than Shenzhen.

For your situation:

```text
Guangzhou hukou
      │
      ▼
Guangzhou Public Security Exit-Entry
      │
      ├── 港澳通行证
      │
      └── 香港商务签注（S）
```

### But there is an important distinction

Your **Guangzhou hukou does not by itself qualify you for an S endorsement**.

The S endorsement is based on the **business qualification /备案 of the enterprise or business unit** and your relationship to that business.

So there are two separate questions:

1. **Can you apply from Guangzhou?** → Yes, Guangzhou is your normal jurisdiction.
2. **Will your HK company alone qualify you for an S endorsement?** → **Don't assume so.** You need to verify your specific situation with Guangzhou Exit-Entry.

If you have a **Guangzhou/Mainland company**, the situation is more straightforward because the Mainland company can potentially be the business entity supporting the business-endorsement application.

If your structure is only:

```text
You
 │
 ▼
Hong Kong Ltd
```

with **no Mainland company**, I would specifically ask Guangzhou Exit-Entry whether your HK-company directorship/shareholding can support an S endorsement.

### What I'd do in your shoes

Before paying an agency, call/visit Guangzhou Exit-Entry and ask this exact question:

> I am a Guangzhou hukou resident. I am the shareholder/director of a Hong Kong limited company. I need to frequently visit Hong Kong for business meetings and client development. Can I apply for a 港澳通行证 with 香港商务签注（S） based on my Hong Kong company? What enterprise备案 or supporting documents are required?

Then you'll know whether you need:

```text
Option A
HK company
   ↓
S endorsement
```

or:

```text
Option B
Mainland company + HK company
        ↓
enterprise备案
        ↓
S endorsement
```

or potentially another route.

**And don't let an agency tell you “we can guarantee the S endorsement because we register HK companies.”** Company incorporation and Mainland exit-entry endorsement eligibility are separate processes.

If your ultimate goal is **“live in Hong Kong and run my AI/software consultancy there,”** I'd treat the S endorsement as the **client-development/travel tool**, not the immigration solution. The eventual work/residence permission is a separate problem.
