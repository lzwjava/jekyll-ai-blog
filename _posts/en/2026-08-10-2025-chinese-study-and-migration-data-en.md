Question: What was the 2025 data for Chinese going to the US for study/migration?

Answer:

The 2025 data shows a **recovery from COVID**, but Chinese students are no longer growing like the 2010–2018 period.

## 1. Chinese students in US universities (2024/25 academic year)

According to **Open Doors 2025**:

* Total international students in US:

  * **1,177,766**
  * +5% year over year

* Chinese students:

  * **265,919**
  * China remained the **#2 source country**
  * Down about **4%** from previous year

* India:

  * **363,019**
  * #1 source country
  * +10% growth

([IIE Open Doors][1])

So the picture:

```
US international students 2024/25

India      363K  ███████████████████
China      266K  ██████████████
South Korea ...
```

China is still huge, but India has overtaken China.

---

## 2. Compared with COVID period

COVID years were abnormal:

```
2019        strong growth
2020-2022   collapse
2023        recovery
2024-2025   mostly normal
2026        new normal
```

The US currently has almost 1.2M international students again, close to pre-COVID scale. ([IIE Open Doors][1])

For Chinese students:

* Visa offices reopened.
* Flights normalized.
* Universities actively recruit international students again.

So **2025/2026 is much easier than 2020-2022**.

---

## 3. But compared with 2015-2018?

It is harder.

Around 2015-2018:

* Chinese middle class was expanding quickly.
* US universities aggressively recruited Chinese students.
* China-US relations were better.
* Visa environment was smoother.

Now:

* More geopolitical screening.
* More competition from Indian applicants.
* Some AI/semiconductor fields receive more scrutiny.

([Reuters][2])

---

## 5. Migration data perspective

Chinese migration is shifting.

Before:

```
China -> US study -> US job -> green card
```

was the dominant path.

Now:

```
China -> US
China -> Canada
China -> Singapore
China -> Hong Kong
China -> Europe
China -> remote/global AI work
```

is more diversified.

The US is still the strongest ecosystem for AI research and startups, but it is no longer the only attractive destination.
