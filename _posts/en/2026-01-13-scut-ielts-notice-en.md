**South China University of Technology IELTS Computer-based Test Center – Candidate Entry Notice**
Published: July 31, 2023
Views: 13,046

To ensure a smooth and orderly examination experience, all candidates choosing the South China University of Technology (SCUT) IELTS test center must read and strictly comply with the following entry regulations.

**Transportation Routes:**

- **Metro**: Take Metro Line 3 (direction: Tianhe Coach Terminal) and get off at **Wushan Station**. Exit from **Exit C** and walk approximately 175 meters to the East Gate of South China University of Technology.
- **Bus**: Take bus routes 197, 20, 755, 53, 23, or 32 to **Huagongda Station** (SCUT Station), then walk about 344 meters to the East Gate of South China University of Technology.
- **Driving**: Navigate to **South China University of Technology Wushan Campus East Gate** or **South Gate**.

**Arriving at the Test Venue:**

The computer-based test and speaking test venue is located on the **5th floor of Building 4**, No. 381 Wushan Road, South China University of Technology. Candidates may enter the campus through the South Gate or East Gate (near Wushan Metro Exit C) on foot. Entry requires presentation of your IELTS Admission Ticket. Follow the campus signs to reach Building 4.

You may also use your phone to search for navigation to **South China University of Technology Wushan Campus Building 4**, follow the route to the entrance of Building 4, then proceed to the IELTS test center on the 5th floor.

Unless absolutely necessary, **private vehicles and accompanying persons are not allowed to enter the campus**.

**Required Documents:**

Candidates must bring the **original valid identification document** used during registration (matching the name on the registration) and the **IELTS Admission Ticket**. At the test center on the 5th floor of Building 4, staff will check documents, verify the admission ticket, register candidates, and store belongings. After all checks are completed, follow staff instructions to enter the exam room.

**Admission Time Reminders:**

Please pay close attention to the following times and allow sufficient time for campus entry checks and walking within the campus to avoid being late.

- **Speaking Test**: Arrive at the test venue at least **30 minutes** in advance. The cut-off time for entering the speaking test is **15 minutes before** your scheduled speaking time.
- **Computer-based Written Test**
  - Morning session: Check-in starts at **08:00 AM**, latest entry at **08:30 AM**.
  - Afternoon session: Check-in starts at **13:00 PM**, latest entry at **13:30 PM**.

**Other Important Reminders:**

During the written test, raise your hand immediately if you have any issues and inform the invigilator.
During the speaking test, if any emergency occurs, press the bell to notify the invigilator. After finishing the speaking test, press the bell and wait for the invigilator to confirm everything is correct before leaving as directed.

After the entire examination ends, please follow the instructions of the test center staff and leave the campus as quickly as possible.

Please closely monitor announcements on this website before the test. Any changes will not be notified separately.
