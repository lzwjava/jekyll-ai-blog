**Question:** When opening an enterprise (corporate) bank account at CMB (招商银行), is an on-site office photo inspection required after getting the business license (营业执照)?

---

**Answer:**

Yes, on-site photo inspection is now a standard step. Here's what to expect:

**On-site Photo is Now Required**

Previously, banks did not require on-site photo verification or only loosely enforced it. However, on-site inspection and photo-taking has now become a **mandatory step** for enterprise bank account opening, primarily to verify the company's actual office location and prevent accounts being opened with fake addresses.

**What the Bank Photos**

The bank's account manager will come to your office to take photos. The address can be your **registered address** or your **actual business address** — even a residential address is acceptable in some cases. Many people also make a simple company signboard (KT board) to put up during the photo visit.

**Typical CMB Opening Flow**


1. After getting your business license and company seals, **contact a nearby CMB branch to schedule an appointment**, then coordinate with the account manager on a time for the on-site photo visit.
2. After the on-site photo is done, bring: business license (original), legal representative's ID (original), online banking administrator's ID, company articles of association, lease contract, and property certificate copy — to the bank branch to complete the process. Usually 1–2 trips.


**Bank's Post-Opening Follow-up**

After account opening, banks may also conduct phone call-backs or additional on-site checks to verify the company name, business address, main business scope, and legal representative information.

**Small/New Companies**

CMB does support a "simplified account opening" service for small micro-enterprises, including situations where the office location hasn't been finalized yet. Over 36 CMB branches support this simplified service — worth asking your local branch if you're a very new company without a fixed office yet.

---

**Practical Tips:**
- Make a simple company name sign/banner before the photo visit
- Keep the legal representative's phone reachable at all times during the process
- Prepare lease contract or property documents for the office space
