In this textbook (specifically **Chapter 6: 推动高质量发展 / Promoting High-Quality Development**), **"High-Quality Economic Development"** is China’s central economic strategy for the New Era.

It marks a strategic pivot away from pursuing pure high-speed GDP growth (高速增长) to prioritizing efficiency, sustainability, technological capability, and public well-being.

---

### 1. Core Definition: What Does It Mean?
* **Transition:** China's economy has transitioned from a stage of *rapid growth* to a stage of *high-quality development*.
* **Goal:** High-quality development means economic growth that directly satisfies people's rising expectations for a better quality of life while maintaining economic competitiveness, security, and resilience.

---

### 2. The 5 Guiding Principles: New Development Philosophy (新发展理念)
High-quality growth is driven by five core principles:
1. **Innovation (创新 - Primary Driver):** Moving from low-cost labor and resource-heavy manufacturing to technology-driven and innovation-led growth.
2. **Coordination (协调 - Internal Balance):** Resolving structural imbalances between regions (coastal vs. interior) and urban vs. rural areas.
3. **Green (绿色 - Sustainability):** Decarbonization, environmental protection, and carbon peaking/neutrality goals.
4. **Openness (开放 - Global Linkage):** High-standard opening up, foreign trade, and participation in global governance (e.g., Belt and Road).
5. **Sharing (共享 - Social Equity):** Ensuring growth benefits everyone, aiming toward *Common Prosperity* (共同富裕).

---

### 3. Institutional Foundation: The Socialist Market Economy
The textbook defines how the economy should operate:
* **"Two Unswervings" (两个毫不动摇):**
  * Unswervingly consolidate and develop the **public/state-owned sector** (state enterprises as strategic anchors).
  * Unswervingly encourage, support, and guide the **non-public/private sector** (private businesses as engines for jobs and innovation).
* **Government vs. Market Relationship:**
  * Let the **market play the decisive role** in resource allocation.
  * Allow the **government to play a better role** in macro regulation, stability, and public services.

---

### 4. Strategic Framework: Dual Circulation (新发展格局)
To ensure economic resilience in an uncertain global environment:
* **Domestic Circulation as Mainstay (以国内大循环为主体):** Unleash domestic consumer demand, build a unified national market, and strengthen internal supply chains.
* **Domestic & International Dual Circulation (国内国际双循环相互促进):** Maintain deep integration with global markets while reducing critical external vulnerabilities.
* **Core Requirement:** High-level self-reliance in science and technology (高水平科技自立自强) and prioritizing the **real economy / manufacturing sector (实体经济)** over financial speculation.

---

### 5. Building a Modern Economic System (建设现代化经济体系)
Concrete steps outlined in Chapter 6 include:
* **Modern Industrial System:** Upgrading traditional manufacturing, expanding strategic emerging industries (AI, clean energy, semiconductors), and expanding the digital economy.
* **Rural Revitalization (乡村振兴):** Modernizing agriculture and narrowing the urban-rural wealth gap.
* **Coordinated Regional Development (区域协调发展):** Major regional strategies (e.g., Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei, Yangtze River Delta, Greater Bay Area).
