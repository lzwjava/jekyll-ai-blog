To get a Hong Kong **temporary visit visa** while you have the ASMTP (Admission Scheme for Mainland Talents and Professionals), here's what you need to know:

## If You're Already in Hong Kong on ASMTP

If you're already in Hong Kong under ASMTP, you generally **don't need a separate temporary visit visa** for short trips in and out of Hong Kong. Your ASMTP allows you to:

- Stay in Hong Kong for work
- Enter and exit freely during your approved stay period

## If You Need to Apply for Someone Else's Visit

If you're asking about bringing family members or visitors to Hong Kong temporarily, they would need to apply for a standard **visitor visa** (not related to your ASMTP status).

## Application Process for HK Visitor Visa

The typical process involves:

1. **Determine if you need a visa** - Check if your nationality requires a visa for visiting Hong Kong
2. **Submit application** through:
   - Chinese diplomatic missions abroad, or
   - Immigration Department of Hong Kong
3. **Required documents** typically include:
   - Completed application form (ID 1003A or ID 1003B)
   - Valid travel document
   - Proof of financial standing
   - Proof of onward/return transportation
