Question: Is a US-version unlocked iPhone 12 Pro with a replaced screen, camera zoom only 1x–5x, no Face ID, 75% battery and a broken compass worth ~500 CNY on Xianyu?

Answer:

**Short version: No, I'd pass.** At 500 CNY it's priced like a parts phone, and it has several problems that suggest more than just a screen swap.

**What the symptoms tell you**
- **No Face ID after a screen replacement.** Face ID failure is common after repairs using non-Apple parts. On the 12 Pro the TrueDepth module sits near the screen and is easy to damage or disturb during a swap. Fixing it properly isn't cheap: one repair shop lists a TrueDepth/dot projector repair at 269 €, and this is a repair many shops can't do at all.
- **Camera zoom only 1x–5x.** That likely means the rear camera or its calibration is off, since the 12 Pro's lenses are tied to the device. Camera modules on the iPhone 12 series can't be swapped freely without Apple's proprietary diagnostic tool, as an iFixit teardown found. A normal 12 Pro should show a 0.5x ultra-wide option too, so something may be missing or faulty.
- **Compass not working.** This usually points to a magnetometer or board-level fault, often from a bad repair, water damage or a mixed-up part. It's hard to fix.
- **75% battery health.** That's low, and replacing the battery would cost roughly another 100–200 CNY.

**The bigger risk is that the phone has had more than a screen swap.** Several faulty features at once suggest water damage, a drop, or a repaired or swapped board. Those problems tend to spread, and Xianyu sellers rarely offer returns.

**If you still want to buy it, check these first:**
1. Ask for photos of Settings > General > About, and confirm the serial number and model match the box and label.
2. Look under Settings > General > About > Parts and Service History for non-genuine part warnings.
3. Confirm it's iCloud-clean and not carrier-locked or MDM-locked.
4. Ask for a video showing every camera, the speaker, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular and charging.
5. Use Xianyu's escrow payment and keep all chat records.

**Better options**
- Used iPhone 12 Pro units with working Face ID usually go for noticeably more, but that gets you a phone that works properly.
- If the budget really is 500 CNY, consider a clean iPhone XR or iPhone 11, which tend to be more reliable than a heavily repaired 12 Pro.

I'm not a repair technician, and I can't verify this specific unit, so treat this as general guidance. Current Xianyu prices may differ from what I've assumed.
