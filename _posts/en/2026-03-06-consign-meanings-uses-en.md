Question: What does "consign" mean?

Answer: 

**Consign** is a verb with several related meanings depending on the context. Here are the most common ones:

1. **To hand over or deliver something to someone's care**  
   (often formally or officially)  
   - Example: The goods were consigned to the shipping company.  
   - Example: She consigned her old furniture to a second-hand store.

2. **To send (goods) to an agent or place for sale**  
   This is the most frequent business/commercial meaning. The owner (consignor) sends items to someone else (consignee) who sells them and usually takes a commission.  
   - Example: The artist consigned several paintings to the gallery.  
   - Related terms: consignment store, consignment agreement

3. **To assign or commit someone/something to an unpleasant or undesirable situation**  
   (figurative, usually negative)  
   - Example: His bad performance consigned him to the bench for the rest of the season.  
   - Example: The old theory was consigned to the dustbin of history.

4. **To banish, put away, or get rid of something** (rather literary)  
   - Example: All memories of the scandal were consigned to oblivion.

**Quick summary of most common modern uses**

| Context              | Meaning                                      | Typical example                              |
|----------------------|----------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Business / commerce  | Send goods for sale by another party         | consigned the dresses to the boutique        |
| General / delivery   | Hand over / entrust to someone               | consigned the package to the courier         |
| Figurative (negative)| Put into an unwanted or forgotten state      | consigned to failure / obscurity / oblivion  |

The word often carries a sense of **formality**, **passing responsibility to someone else**, or **removing something from one's direct control**.
