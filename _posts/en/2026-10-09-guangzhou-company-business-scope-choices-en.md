### 💡 Tips for Choosing

- **Write it broad, not narrow** — add more categories than you immediately need; removing scope later is easy, adding it requires amendment fees (~¥200–500)
- **Avoid licensed items** if you don't need them — anything requiring pre-approval (前置许可) like food production, healthcare, finance slows down registration significantly
- **Guangzhou allows "一般项目"** (general items) + **"许可项目"** (licensed items) — keep licensed items to a minimum for a fast 3-day turnaround
- The scope doesn't need to be exhaustive — you can include a catch-all like **"其他不需要许可的经营活动"** in some districts
