**Answer:**

Here's a practical breakdown of what you're dealing with, based on current 2026 info:

## Your Current Setup

You seem to be on a **legacy/older base plan** (低月租老套餐) with:

- **¥19/month base** — likely a minimal "keep-alive" plan with only ~10GB bundled, not the new promotional cards
- **¥10/add-on packs** valid 3 days — these are standard short-validity top-up data packs (叠加流量包) that Telecom sells via the 电信APP or 10000号

This is a pretty common setup for someone who joined Telecom years ago in Guangdong and never switched to a modern high-traffic plan.

## The Problem With This Setup

You're getting squeezed on both ends:

1. **¥19/month for 10GB** is bad value in 2026. The current market for Guangdong Telecom cards is dramatically better — there are Guangdong-specific packages like the 飞泽卡 at ¥30 for 185GB + 100 minutes of calls, issued to Guangzhou/Shenzhen/Foshan/Dongguan/Huizhou/Zhongshan.

2. **¥10 for 3-day packs** — this is expensive per-GB. You're paying roughly ¥1/GB on the add-on if it's a 10GB pack, but the 3-day expiry means you either use it fast or waste it. Not ideal for a developer whose usage is bursty (big downloads, model pulls, etc.).

## What the Guangdong Telecom Market Actually Looks Like Now

The 电信粤东卡 (Guangdong East card) at ¥39/month offers 205GB national general traffic + 30GB directional traffic + 100 minutes of calls, with a 2-year validity on the large traffic bundle. Notably, the first month is free after depositing ¥100, and months 2–6 cost only ¥29 with a ¥10 monthly credit applied automatically.

There's also: a Guangdong provincial exclusive card at ¥40/month for 210GB + 200 minutes, valid 1 year, available across Guangdong.

## Practical Steps

1. Check your current contract in **天翼生活 APP** → "我的套餐" — see if you're locked in or can switch
