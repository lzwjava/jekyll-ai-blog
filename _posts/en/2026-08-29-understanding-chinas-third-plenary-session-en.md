**Question:** What is the "Third Plenary Session" (三中全会), when is it typically held, and what were the topics of the 18th Central Committee's Third Plenary Session in China?

**Answer:**

**What is the Third Plenary Session (三中全会)?**

The "Third Plenary Session" refers to the third plenary (full) meeting of the CCP Central Committee within each five-year party congress cycle. These sessions are typically economic reform-focused meetings, and on occasion have gone on to spell out important reform-related breakthroughs.

**When is it typically held?**

The CCP typically holds one plenary session per year, usually in the fall. The Third Plenum generally takes place in the first or second year of each five-year cycle.

---

**18th Central Committee — Third Plenary Session**

- **When:** November 9–12, 2013, held at the Banquet Hall, Great Hall of the People, Beijing, with 204 Central Committee members and 169 alternate members attending.

- **Main Topic — Comprehensively Deepening Reform:** The meeting discussed major issues concerning comprehensively deepening reform, adopted the *Decision on Several Major Issues Concerning Comprehensively Deepening Reform*, and decided to establish a Central Leading Group for Comprehensively Deepening Reforms.

- **Background Goals:** The 18th CCP National Congress held in November 2012 had proposed the goals of building a moderately prosperous society in all respects and deepening reform and opening up, including sustained economic development, expansion of people's democracy, enhancement of cultural soft power, improvement of living standards, and progress in building a resource-saving and environmentally friendly society.

- **Historical Significance:** The Third Plenary Session of the 18th CPC Central Committee was considered of epoch-making significance — it marked the start of a new journey of comprehensively deepening reform in the new era with systematic and holistic plans, paving the way for a brand-new stage in China's reform and opening-up endeavors.
