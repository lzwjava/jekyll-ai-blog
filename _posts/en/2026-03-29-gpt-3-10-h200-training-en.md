Question: How long would it take to train **GPT‑3** with 10× H200 GPUs?

Answer:

GPT‑3 is **much larger than Qwen‑3.5 9B**, so the scale difference is huge. Let’s break it down carefully.

---

## 1. GPT‑3 full model specs

| Model        | Params | Dataset tokens   | FLOPs (est.)    |
| ------------ | ------ | ---------------- | --------------- |
| GPT‑3 (175B) | 175B   | 300B–400B tokens | ~3–4×10²³ FLOPs |

Notes:

* 175B parameters → single H200 GPU **cannot fit full model in memory** for training without **model parallelism**.
* You **must use FSDP or tensor model parallelism** across multiple GPUs.

---

## 2. GPU throughput estimate

* Single H200 can handle ~30M tokens/day for 9B model

* GPT‑3 175B is **~20× larger than 9B**

  * Rough scaling: throughput drops by ~20× if you don’t increase batch size
  * So single H200: ~1–2M tokens/day for full GPT‑3 training

* 10× H200 with ideal distributed scaling: ~10–15M tokens/day

> This assumes perfect FSDP, but **network overhead and memory fragmentation** reduce efficiency. Realistic: 70–80% of ideal → ~8–12M tokens/day

---

## 3. Time estimate for full pretraining

* Dataset: 300B tokens
* Throughput: 10M tokens/day (realistic)

[
300,\text{B tokens} ÷ 10,\text{M tokens/day} = 30,000 \text{ days} \approx 82 \text{ years!}
]

✅ Full GPT‑3 training on 10 H200 GPUs is **completely impractical**.

---

## 4. Practical approach

1. **LoRA / instruction-tuning / fine-tuning**

   * Use pretrained GPT‑3 weights
   * Fine-tuning on 1–10B tokens: **1–2 weeks** on 10 GPUs

2. **Partial pretraining**

   * Train small fraction of tokens (~1–10B tokens) for research
   * 10 GPUs → **1–2 weeks**
   * This is useful for experimenting with hyperparameters or testing new training tricks

3. **Model parallelism**

   * Must split 175B across **hundreds of GPUs** in reality (like GPT‑3 in OpenAI’s infrastructure)

---

## 5. Key takeaway

| Scenario                                  | Tokens/day | Time for full dataset |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------- | --------------------- |
| 1× H200                                   | ~1–2M      | ~410 years            |
| 10× H200                                  | ~10M       | ~82 years             |
| LoRA / partial pretraining (1–10B tokens) | 10M        | 1–2 weeks             |

**Conclusion:**

* **Full scratch training** of GPT‑3 on 10 H200 GPUs → impossible.
* **Fine-tuning / partial pretraining** → feasible, practical, and realistic.
* For real GPT‑3 scale, labs use **hundreds of GPUs or TPUs** over 1–2 weeks.
