**Question:** How far is Bangalore to Pune, and what are the ways to travel between them?

**Answer:**

## Distance

| Type | Distance |
|------|----------|
| Road (NH48 + NH48/65) | ~840 km |
| Straight line (air) | ~730 km |
| Train (approx.) | ~1,150 km (longer rail route) |

---

## Travel Options

### 1. Flight ✈️ — Fastest

- **Duration:** ~1.5 hours in the air, but 3–4 hours door-to-door
- **Airlines:** IndiGo, Air India, Vistara, SpiceJet
- **Cost:** ₹2,000–₹8,000 depending on booking time
- **Airports:** Kempegowda Intl (BLR) → Pune Airport (PNQ)
- **Best for:** Time-critical travel

---

### 2. Bus 🚌 — Most Popular Budget Option

- **Duration:** 11–14 hours (overnight buses very common)
- **Cost:** ₹600–₹2,500 depending on bus type
- **Operators:** KSRTC, VRL Travels, SRS Travels, RedBus aggregator
- **Bus types:**
  - Sleeper AC — best comfort for overnight
  - Seater AC
  - Non-AC sleeper (cheapest)
- **Route:** NH48 via Tumkur → Chitradurga → Dharwad → Kolhapur → Pune
- **Best for:** Budget travel, overnight so you save hotel cost

---

### 3. Train 🚂 — Comfortable Mid-Range

- **Duration:** 10–24 hours depending on the train
- **Key trains:**
  - **Udyan Express** (11301/11302) — classic, ~24 hrs, scenic
  - **Siddhaganga Express** — ~22 hrs
  - **Garib Rath** — AC but longer
- **Cost:** ₹300–₹2,000 (Sleeper to 3AC/2AC)
- **Note:** Rail route goes via Solapur which is longer than road
- **Book at:** [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in)
- **Best for:** Comfortable, scenic, no traffic stress

---

### 4. Self-Drive / Car 🚗

- **Duration:** 10–12 hours (traffic and stops factored)
- **Route:** NH48 — well-maintained 4/6 lane highway most of the way
- **Fuel cost:** ~₹4,000–₹5,000 (petrol car)
- **Toll:** ~₹600–₹800
- **Key stops en route:** Tumkur, Davangere, Hubli, Dharwad, Belgaum (Belagavi), Kolhapur
- **Best for:** Road trip, flexibility, group travel

---

## Recommendation by Priority

| Priority | Go With |
|----------|---------|
| Fastest | Flight |
| Cheapest | Overnight bus |
| Most comfortable (no flight) | Train (Udyan Express, 2AC) |
| Group/flexible | Self-drive |

The **overnight sleeper bus** is the most popular choice among locals — you depart ~9–10 PM, arrive ~8–9 AM, and save a night's hotel. Book on [RedBus](https://www.redbus.in) or [AbhiBus](https://www.abhibus.com).
