**Question:** What is 广东流量王黄金尊享版 (Guangdong "Traffic King" Gold Premium Edition)? It's a China Unicom plan.

**Answer:**

It's a regional mobile plan from Guangdong China Unicom (广东联通), part of their 流量王 ("Traffic King") product line, which has several tiers — 纯享版, 白银版, 尊享版, etc. The 黄金尊享版 (Gold Premium) is the high-data tier.

Based on recent (2026) coverage, the mainline pricing is 58 CNY/month, which includes 280GB of nationwide general-purpose data (unused data rolls over to the next month), plus per the 2026 guide, 100 minutes of voice and 1000M broadband in some areas.

Key fine print worth knowing:

- **Throttling:** after exceeding the data quota, speed is throttled to 3Mbps — fine for short video, slow for large downloads.
- **Secondary SIMs:** 58 CNY is the base rate; adding secondary cards (max 2) costs an extra 20 CNY/month, and broadband installation may carry a ~100 CNY setup fee depending on region.
- **"19元" bait pricing:** the "19 CNY Gold edition" floating around online is mostly a short-term promo (first month 19 CNY, then back to 58 CNY) or actually the Silver edition (38 CNY/month, 20GB + 100 minutes). Some channels use low intro prices that jump after the promo period.
- **Contract & region locks:** some versions carry a 12–24 month contract with early-termination fees (e.g., 3 months of rent), and the number is bound to Guangdong.

One caveat: most of the search results on this plan are reseller/agent marketing posts (Sohu/Zhihu affiliate content), which are heavily SEO'd and sometimes inflate the specs. The reliable move is to open the 中国联通 APP, search "广东流量王", and check the official terms there — or call 10010 — before signing up. Confirm specifically: whether the 280GB figure is permanent or promotional, the contract length, and whether rollover applies in your city.
