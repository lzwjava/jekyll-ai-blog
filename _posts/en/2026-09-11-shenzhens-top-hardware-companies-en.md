**Question:** What are the most notable hardware companies based in Shenzhen, like DJI?

**Answer:**

Shenzhen is often called "China's Silicon Valley" and is home to many of the world's most influential hardware companies. Here's a rundown of the hottest ones:

---

### 🚁 Drones & Robotics
**DJI (大疆)** — The most famous. DJI is the world's leading manufacturer of consumer and professional drones, controlling over 70% of the global drone market. Founded in 2006, it has made drone technology accessible for photography, filmmaking, agriculture, surveying, and emergency response. In 2025, DJI posted revenue of roughly CN¥80 billion (~US$11.5 billion).

---

### 📱 Smartphones & Consumer Electronics
- **Huawei (华为)** — The Shenzhen-based firm has made remarkable strides toward semiconductor self-sufficiency, driving the development of 7-nanometer domestic chip manufacturing. Huawei has rolled out its AI-native operating system HarmonyOS across PCs, tablets, and smartphones — once again the top-selling phones in China — while pushing into 6G research. In 2025, Huawei posted revenue of CN¥880.9 billion (~US$126 billion).

- **OnePlus (一加)** — A Shenzhen-based smartphone vendor that ranked 19th in the Kantar BrandZ 2024 Chinese Global Brand Builders report.

- **Honor (荣耀)** — Originally launched by Huawei in 2013, it became an independent entity in 2020. Headquartered in Shenzhen, Honor focuses on producing high-quality smartphones and IoT devices, and has re-established its market presence particularly in Europe and Asia.

- **OPPO** — One of the large technology corporations that grew out of Shenzhen's entrepreneurial, innovative culture.

---

### 🚗 EVs & Batteries
- **BYD (比亚迪)** — BYD started life in 1995 as a rechargeable battery maker. Its founder Wang Chuanfu added a car business in 2003, and now it is best known as China's leading maker of electric vehicles. The company still makes around half the world's mobile phone batteries. BYD produced over 4.6 million vehicles in 2025 and generated revenue of CN¥804 billion (~US$112 billion).

---

### 📡 Telecom & Networking
- **ZTE (中兴)** — One of Shenzhen's prominent tech firms alongside Huawei, Tencent, and DJI. A major global telecom equipment and networking hardware maker.

---

### 🔬 Other Notable Names
- **TCL** — Major consumer electronics and display panel manufacturer
- **Han's Laser (大族激光)** — A laser equipment maker that made Fortune China's Tech 50 list.
- **BGI Group** — A genomics firm also recognized on Fortune China's Tech 50.
