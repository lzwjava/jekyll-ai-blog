Question: What are the disadvantages and shortcomings of the Nezha V?

Answer:

The Nezha V (哪吒V) is an entry-level pure electric small SUV with a very low price, but real owner feedback and reviews highlight several clear shortcomings. These mainly involve weak performance, poor real-world range, quality/reliability issues, terrible after-sales service, and — especially in 2025–2026 — major company-level crises that have made ownership very risky.

### 1. Weak power and slow acceleration
- Acceleration feels sluggish, especially when going uphill or with a full load. Many owners complain that “it starts slowly” or “power is just average.”
- Not suitable for anyone who wants lively or responsive driving.

### 2. Serious range shortfall and fast battery degradation
- Official range is around 400 km (CLTC), but real-world usage — especially in winter or with AC on — is much lower.
- Some owners report that after 1–2 years the usable range drops dramatically (e.g., from ~400 km to only 40–100 km in practice).
- Fast charging is also underwhelming: 30%–80% often takes 55+ minutes, and peak power is lower than advertised.

### 3. Cheap interior materials, cramped space
- Interior is dominated by hard plastics with low-quality feel and dull color schemes.
- Rear seat space is tight — limited legroom and headroom.
- Seats are thin, side support is mediocre, and driver’s seat usually cannot be height-adjusted.

### 4. Frequent quality & safety problems
- **Brake issues** are commonly reported: brake failure, master cylinder problems, even cases leading to accidents.
- Underbody rust appears quickly — some cars show serious rust within 1–2 years.
- Common electronic failures: central screen goes completely black, sudden power loss, charging faults, battery system errors.
- Car app is unstable, navigation and music often lag or drop connection.

### 5. Very poor after-sales service & parts availability
- Service network is sparse; many owners have to travel 100–200+ km to the nearest center.
- Long wait times for repairs (weeks to months for some issues).
- Since mid-2025, Nezha Automobile has faced severe financial and operational crisis: production largely stopped, supply chain collapsed, spare parts are extremely hard to obtain, official warranty is effectively void in many cases, insurance companies often refuse to cover repairs, many dealerships closed or joined owner protests.
- As of early 2026, the official website is frequently inaccessible, and large numbers of owners are stuck with “stranded” or “abandoned” cars.

### 6. Other long-term drawbacks
- Very low resale value — hard to sell second-hand, prices drop sharply.
- Brand awareness and trust remain weak; exterior design is polarizing.
- Early delivery delays were also a frequent complaint when the model first launched.

**Summary**
The Nezha V can be an ultra-cheap option for very short urban commutes on a tight budget, but it is **not recommended** for families, long trips, or anyone who values reliability and future support.
Especially now (2026), buying a new or used Nezha V carries extremely high risk due to the company’s near-collapse and widespread lack of parts & service.

Better alternatives in the same price range usually include the BYD Dolphin, Wuling Bingo / Starlight Mini, or other small EVs from more stable brands. Always test drive thoroughly and check the latest owner groups / complaint platforms before deciding.
