Complacency means a feeling of self-satisfaction or contentment that causes someone to be unaware of potential problems or dangers. It's when you become too comfortable or confident about a situation and stop being vigilant or making efforts to improve or protect yourself.

For example, a company might become complacent after years of success and fail to notice emerging competitors, or someone might become complacent about their health because they've always felt fine, ignoring warning signs.

The word often carries a negative connotation because complacency can lead to carelessness, lack of preparation, or missed opportunities for growth.
