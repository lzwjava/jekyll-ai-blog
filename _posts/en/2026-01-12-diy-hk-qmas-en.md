## How to Apply for QMAS DIY

### **Step 1: Check Your Eligibility**

First, ensure you meet all the **prerequisites**:

- **Age**: 18 years or above
- **Financial requirement**: Demonstrate you can support yourself and dependants for 12 months without public assistance (using bank deposits, real estate, securities, or business interests)
- **Good character**: No criminal or adverse immigration record
- **Basic qualifications**: First degree from a recognized university (or exceptional technical qualifications/professional abilities)
- **Nationality**: Not from Afghanistan, Cuba, or North Korea

### **Step 2: Choose Your Assessment Route**

You can apply under one of two routes:

**A. General Points Test** - Has 12 criteria across 6 aspects:
- Age (50 or below gets points)
- Academic qualifications (master's/doctoral from eligible universities, especially STEM fields)
- Language proficiency (Chinese and/or English, plus foreign languages)
- Work experience (5+ years graduate level; 3+ years in MNCs/specific fields; 2+ years international)
- Annual income (HK$1 million+ in the past year)
- Business ownership (company with HK$5 million+ profit or listed company)

You need to meet the passing threshold to be eligible (check the Immigration Department website for current threshold).

**B. Achievement-based Points Test** - For exceptional talents with:
- Major awards (Olympic medals, Nobel prizes, national/international awards), OR
- Significant peer recognition for contributions to your field

### **Step 3: Prepare Your Documents**

Key documents needed:

**For everyone:**
- Valid travel document/passport
- Recent photograph
- Proof of financial assets (bank statements, property valuations, investment statements)
- Degree certificates and transcripts
- **Verification from third-party credential verification organizations** (check Immigration Department website for approved list)
- Language proficiency proof (test results like IELTS 7.0+, TOEFL 94+, or other evidence)

**For work experience:**
- Reference letters from each employer on company letterhead
- Job descriptions with dates, titles, and detailed duties
- For business owners: company registration, audited financials, tax returns
- For self-employed: client contracts and reference letters

**For income claims:**
- Tax assessment notices
- Salary slips or company profit statements
- Stock option documentation (if applicable)

**For business ownership:**
- Company registration documents
- Audited financial reports
- Shareholding proof

All non-English/Chinese documents must have certified translations.

### **Step 4: Submit Online Application**

Applications must be submitted online through the designated GovHK website:
- Go to: www.gov.hk/en/nonresidents/visarequire/visasentrypermits/applyqmas.htm
- Fill in the complete online form
- Upload all supporting documents (JPEG, PDF, GIF, PNG, or TIF format)
- Pay the **non-refundable application fee** online (by credit card, PPS, or FPS)
- Sign electronically to confirm accuracy
- You'll receive an acknowledgement after submission

**Important**: You can only submit ONE application at a time under ONE assessment route.

### **Step 5: Wait for Selection**

- Selection exercises are conducted regularly
- Processing typically takes 6-12 months
- The Assessment Panel considers manpower needs, your credentials, and Hong Kong's socio-economic needs
- Meeting the threshold doesn't guarantee approval - it's competitive

### **Step 6: If Approved**

- You'll receive a notification with a payment link for the visa issuance fee
- Pay the fee online
- Download/print your e-Visa immediately
- **Mainland Chinese residents** need to obtain an Exit-Entry Permit and exit endorsement from Mainland authorities
- Initial stay: 36 months (General Points Test) or 8 years (Achievement-based)

### **Step 7: Bring Dependants (Optional)**

You can include your spouse and unmarried children under 18 in your initial application, or they can apply to join you later.

### **Key Tips for DIY Success:**

1. **Be thorough with documentation** - incomplete applications cause delays
2. **Use exact job titles** from your employment records
3. **Calculate your points honestly** before applying (General Points Test)
4. **Highlight achievements** that demonstrate value to Hong Kong's economy
5. **Check the Talent List** - if your profession is on it, mention this prominently
6. **Keep copies** of everything you submit
7. **Don't provide false information** - this can lead to prosecution and removal

### **After Arrival:**

For extension of stay, you'll need to prove you've:
- Taken up employment with stable income, OR
- Established/joined a business in Hong Kong

Extensions follow a "3+2" year pattern. After 7 years, you can apply for permanent residency.
