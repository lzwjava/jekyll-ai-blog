## Enterprise WeChat (WeCom) — A Comprehensive Introduction

---

### 1. Background and Origin

For work purposes, companies and business communication, a special version of WeChat called **WeCom** (formally known as Enterprise WeChat, or *Qiye Weixin*, and WeChat Work before November 2020) was launched in **2016**. The app was designed to help employees separate work from private life. In addition to usual chat features, it allowed companies and employees to track annual leave, manage expenses, request time off, and clock in to show attendance.

WeCom is a professional communication and office tool for companies in China and abroad, typically used by brands that already use WeChat for their China business. It interconnects with the WeChat ecosystem, making it the most widely used **SCRM (Social Customer Relationship Management)** tool among Chinese companies. More than **12 million companies** use WeCom, with more than **180 million active users**. Every day, more than 1.2 million corporate employees conduct 140 million service interactions with WeChat users through WeCom.

---

### 2. WeCom vs. Regular WeChat

WeCom is a **separate app** from WeChat, designed exclusively for enterprise use. Key differences include:

WeChat allows an individual account to create a group with up to 500 members, while WeCom allows up to **10,000 group members** for an organizational group. A WeChat account can add up to 5,000 "friends," but an individual WeCom account can add up to **50,000**. WeChat accounts can send up to 200 bulk messages to friends, while a WeCom account has **no limit** for bulk messages.

WeCom notifications are turned off by default after office hours (7pm–7am) to help staff separate work from private life. Users sign up for a WeCom account with their WeChat account — no email or new password required, making onboarding simple for Chinese users.

---

### 3. Core Features

#### 3.1 Internal Communication
The main functionalities include: employees messaging and calling each other, creating task lists, forming communities of up to 100 users, and conducting polls and surveys.

#### 3.2 Video Conferencing
WeCom supports stable HD video conferences accessible from phones, allowing document and screen sharing among up to **300 participants**.

#### 3.3 OA (Office Automation) Tools
Employees collaborate in channels, share files, and automate workflows such as leave requests and expense approvals. WeCom integrates with Mini Programs and third-party APIs, enabling features like **CRM** and real-time analytics.

#### 3.4 Document Collaboration
WeCom includes **Smart Sheets** that help enterprises enhance internal and external office collaboration, making it easy to manage projects, operations, and customers via a single sheet. It also includes **Docs** for collaborative editing and viewing with colleagues, WeChat customers, and partners.

#### 3.5 Email Integration
WeCom offers an integrated email service where users can receive and check emails in real time and respond promptly using an email program geared for corporate customers.

#### 3.6 File Storage
WeCom allows companies to manage files in one place, with **100 GB of free storage** and options for expanded paid storage.

---

### 4. Customer Connection — The WeChat Bridge

One of WeCom's most powerful capabilities is its deep integration with the WeChat consumer ecosystem:

What sets WeChat Work apart is its unique ability to connect businesses directly with **1.3 billion WeChat users** while maintaining enterprise-grade security and data ownership. This approach transforms every employee into a branded service window, fundamentally changing how businesses engage with customers.

WeCom accounts can also share customer information with other WeCom members, enabling seamless work handover and workflow support — a feature not available in standard WeChat.

---

### 5. Security

WeCom is the **first enterprise office solution in China** to achieve **SOC2 Type 2 certification**, with international authority audits and bank-level encryption. End-to-end encryption further protects sensitive corporate information.

WeCom also features messaging archiving and watermarking functions to prevent information leaking via screenshots, addressing modern challenges around data leakage and unauthorized information sharing.

For high-security environments, WeCom has launched a **private deployment version** for state-owned enterprises, financial institutions, and technology-oriented enterprises that require data compliance, allowing data and services to be deployed on self-owned servers with targeted security management strategies based on employee roles, devices, and network environments.

---

### 6. Third-Party Integration

WeCom service providers cover **97 industries**, with 1,232 unique interfaces that seamlessly integrate WeCom with **1,900 third-party applications**, including CRM and project management tools.

---

### 7. AI Capabilities — WeCom 5.0 (2024–2025)

In August 2024, Tencent officially released **WeCom 5.0**, introducing three core AI features: **intelligent search**, **intelligent summary**, and **smart robots**. As of this release, WeCom has connected over **14 million enterprises and organizations**, with over **750 million WeChat users** served through WeCom daily.

In 2024, Tencent also integrated **DeepSeek AI** into WeCom, enhancing customer service automation and predictive analytics.

---

### 8. Pricing

Registering a common WeCom account is **free**. Paid features like WeChat Pay and WeChat Customers currently cost **300 RMB** as a verification cost once every two years. For overseas enterprises, the uniform verification fee is **700 Chinese yuan (approximately USD 99)**, with an audit period of 10–15 working days.

---

### 9. Use Cases by Industry

WeCom is used across retail, education, healthcare, finance, and manufacturing. For example, a retail chain might use WeCom to track inventory across stores or train staff via virtual workshops. WeCom's Group Live Stream function also allows teachers to initiate online classes with students.

---

### 10. Global Reach

In 2023, the WeCom app was downloaded almost **23 million times worldwide**. Although the vast majority of downloads took place in mainland China, WeCom was also downloaded in the US, Hong Kong, and Taiwan. Overseas members can register and log in using their email addresses, and the platform supports multiple languages including Chinese, English, and Cantonese.
