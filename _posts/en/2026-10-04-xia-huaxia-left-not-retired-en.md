Question: **Has Xia Huaxia (夏华夏), Meituan’s former Chief Scientist, retired?**

Answer: **No — the latest public information says he left Meituan, rather than “retired.”**

* Xia Huaxia joined Meituan in **June 2013** after working at Google/Baidu and initially led parts of Meituan’s infrastructure/architecture work. ([Meituan Tech][1])
* By **2023**, CCF listed him as **Meituan VP + Chief Scientist**, as well as head of Meituan's postdoctoral and academician expert workstations. ([CCF][2])
* He subsequently became closely associated with **Meituan autonomous delivery vehicles**, eventually leading the unmanned-vehicle business and reporting directly to Wang Xing. ([NBD][3])
* **April 23, 2026:** Chinese media reported that **夏华夏 had formally left Meituan**. The report describes it as *离职* (left/resigned), **not retirement**. ([NBD][3])
* The unmanned-vehicle business had already been handed to **Zhang Jing (张晶)** in January 2026, shifting the business toward operations/commercialization. ([NBD][3])

So the timeline is roughly:

```text
2013       joined Meituan
2017       focused heavily on unmanned delivery
2020       VP / AI committee leadership
2023       VP + Chief Scientist
Jan 2026   unmanned vehicle leadership → Zhang Jing
Apr 2026   Xia Huaxia officially leaves Meituan
```

**Important distinction:** I don't see reliable public evidence that he has announced **retirement from the industry**. What is documented is **departure from Meituan**.
