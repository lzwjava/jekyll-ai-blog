Question: Are ESTPs known for scattered thinking but strong practicality, especially around money-making?

Answer: Pretty much, yes — with some nuance.

ESTPs are driven by **Se (Extraverted Sensing)**, so their thinking jumps fast between stimuli and opportunities. It can *look* scattered from the outside, but internally it's more like rapid pattern-recognition — they're scanning for what's actionable *right now*.

On the money/practicality side, that's a strong match:

- They're natural **opportunists** — they spot deals, gaps, and leverage points others miss
- They prefer **tangible results** over theory — "does this make money?" is a very ESTP filter
- They're comfortable with **risk and improvisation**, which suits entrepreneurship and trading
- They get bored with long-term planning but excel at **short-term execution**

The "jumping thinking" is real, but it's not scattered in a disorganized way — it's more **stimulus-driven and lateral**. They connect dots quickly, which is actually an asset in fast-moving environments like sales, markets, or deals.

Where they can struggle: sustained focus on slow-burn projects, paperwork, or anything requiring **Ni (long-term vision)** — that's their weak spot.

So: chaotic-looking on the surface, sharply pragmatic underneath. Especially when there's money involved.
