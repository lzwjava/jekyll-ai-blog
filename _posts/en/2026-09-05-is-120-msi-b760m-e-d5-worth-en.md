Question: Is the MSI PRO B760M-E D5 worth **¥120**?

Answer: **Yes — ¥120 is a reasonable price**, especially for your use case as a cheap GPU-testing board.

I'd rank it:

* **¥120 MSI PRO B760M-E D5** → 👍 good buy
* **¥88 Colorful BATTLE-AX B760M-K D5 V20** → 👍 extremely cheap, but the broken RAM-slot clip makes it less attractive
* **MSI MAG B760M Mortar WiFi** → much better board, but overkill and much more expensive

For **GPU repair/testing**, I'd actually prefer the **MSI at ¥120** if it is genuinely fully functional.

Why:

```text
¥120
  ↓
LGA1700
  ↓
B760
  ↓
DDR5
  ↓
PCIe x16
  ↓
GPU test platform
```

You don't need a powerful VRM or Wi-Fi for a GPU diagnostic bench.

### What I'd check before paying

Ask the seller for:

1. **Socket close-up** — LGA1700 pins
2. **PCIe x16 close-up**
3. **BIOS screenshot / boot video**
4. Confirm **both DDR5 slots work**
5. Confirm **M.2 works**
6. Confirm **HDMI output works with iGPU**
7. Confirm it hasn't been repaired

The **HDMI output is particularly useful for GPU repair**: you can remove the GPU under test and still get video from an Intel non-F CPU.

**Verdict: ¥120 → buyable.** If the socket and PCIe slot look clean, I'd take this over the ¥88 board with the damaged RAM-slot clip.
