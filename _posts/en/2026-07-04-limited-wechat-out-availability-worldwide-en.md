Question: Does WeChat Out still work in the international version of WeChat?

Answer:

Yes—but **WeChat Out is now heavily limited and in many regions effectively unavailable or partially supported** in the international version of WeChat by Tencent.

### What WeChat Out is

WeChat Out is a **paid VoIP-to-phone service** that lets you call:

* Mobile numbers
* Landlines
* International numbers

It works differently from normal WeChat voice/video calls because it uses **credit balance** instead of internet-only peer-to-peer calling.

---

### Current reality (important)

As of recent years:

#### ✔ In some regions

* Still visible in the app (as a feature menu)
* Can sometimes buy call credits
* Works for limited destinations

#### ⚠ In many regions (especially US/EU accounts)

* Feature is **hidden or disabled**
* Cannot purchase credits
* Calls may not connect or service is restricted
* Tencent has scaled it down due to:

  * Regulatory restrictions
  * Low usage
  * Carrier VoIP competition (Skype, WhatsApp, etc.)

---

### Why this changed

WeChat Out was never a core feature—it was an **add-on telecom product**, and over time:

* Global messaging apps replaced it
* Telecom licensing became more restrictive
* Tencent shifted focus to core messaging + ecosystem services

---

### Bottom line

* ❌ Not reliably available anymore worldwide
* ⚠ Region-dependent and often disabled for new users
* ✔ If you still see it in your app, it may still work—but not guaranteed long-term
