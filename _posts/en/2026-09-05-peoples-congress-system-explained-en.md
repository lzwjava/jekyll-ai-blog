Question: What is the People's Congress System (人民代表大会制度)?

Answer:

The **People's Congress System** (人民代表大会制度) is China's fundamental political system — the organizational form through which the People's Republic of China exercises state power. It is based on the Marxist-Leninist principle that all power belongs to the people.

**Key features:**

- **Structure:** A multi-level system of "People's Congresses" (人民代表大会) existing at the national, provincial, municipal, county, and township levels.
- **National body:** The highest organ is the **National People's Congress (NPC / 全国人民代表大会)**, which holds supreme state power — legislation, electing top leaders, approving the budget, etc.
- **Election:** Deputies at the grassroots level are directly elected by citizens; higher-level congresses are elected by the congresses below them.
- **Guiding principle:** "Democratic centralism" (民主集中制) — decisions are made collectively, and once made, are binding on all.
- **Party leadership:** In practice, the system operates under the leadership of the **Chinese Communist Party (CCP)**, which guides the policy direction of the congresses.
- **Contrast with Western systems:** Unlike a parliamentary or presidential system with separation of powers, the NPC is theoretically the single unified source of state authority — it does not share power with an independent judiciary or executive in the Western sense.

**In short**, it is China's version of representative democracy, designed to reflect the will of the people through a structured, CCP-led hierarchy of legislative congresses.
