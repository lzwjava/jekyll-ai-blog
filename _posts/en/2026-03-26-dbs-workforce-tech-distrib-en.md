**Question:** How does DBS Bank’s workforce look overall in terms of size and department distribution? Specifically, how large is the bank, what kinds of roles make up most of its employees, and where do IT engineers fit in?

**Answer:**

### 🧑‍💼 **Size of DBS Bank’s Workforce**

* As of recent figures, DBS has around **41 000 employees globally** across its markets in Asia and beyond (including Singapore, India, Indonesia, China, etc.). ([Macrotrends][1])
* This is much smaller than giant global banks like HSBC (which has roughly 200 000+ staff), but DBS is large **relative to the Southeast Asian banking industry**. ([Wikipedia][2])

### 📊 **Overall Workforce Composition**

Like most major banks, the **majority of DBS employees are not exclusively IT engineers**. They span many functions:

1. **Front‑line and Customer‑facing Banking Roles**

   * Branch staff, relationship managers, customer support, personal and business bankers make up a large portion of the workforce.
   * These roles handle deposits, loans, payments, wealth and corporate services directly with customers.

2. **Operations, Risk, Finance & Compliance**

   * Middle‑ and back‑office teams in operations, risk management, compliance, finance & audit are key parts of the workforce, supporting the bank’s daily running.

3. **Technology and Engineering Roles**

   * DBS places **strong emphasis on technology, digital transformation, and data skills**, but tech roles are **one part of the total headcount**, not the majority like in pure software firms. ([dbs.com][3])

   * The bank has dedicated teams in **Group Technology and digital platforms** building and maintaining banking systems, apps, infrastructure, GenAI tools, cloud, cybersecurity, data engineering, etc. ([dbs.com][3])

   > Internal initiatives like DBS Future Tech Academy and structured tech career paths show the bank prioritises tech up‑skilling, but these teams are still a **subset of the whole organisation**. ([dbs.com][4])

### ⚙️ **IT & Technology vs Business Roles**

* **IT engineers and technologists are significant and growing** — embedded across digital banking, analytics, platforms and software development — but they are not the **majority headcount**.
* Most staff are still in **banking, operations, customer service and compliance**, similar to typical large banks.
* DBS has been **reskilling large parts of its workforce for data/AI roles**, indicating technology is becoming more pervasive across functions. About **13 000 staff were targeted for AI/data up‑skilling** as roles evolve. ([ETHRWorld.com][5])

### 📉 **Workforce Changes and Tech Impact**

* DBS has announced plans to phase out around **4 000 temporary/contract roles** over time as AI and automation take over tasks that were previously manual. ([About Singapore][6])
* At the same time, the bank is **creating and expanding tech‑intensive roles** and encouraging staff to move into emerging skill domains like AI, data and automation. ([ETHRWorld.com][5])

### 🧠 **Summary**

* **Total employees:** ~41 000 worldwide. ([Macrotrends][1])
* **Majority roles:** Banking, customer operations, risk/compliance, support functions — similar to other full‑service banks.
* **Tech staff:** A strategic and growing portion, but not the largest segment (unlike a pure tech company).
