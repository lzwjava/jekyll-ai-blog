**Question:** What is the Hong Kong Quality Migrant Admission Scheme (QMAS) Assessment Routes page about?

**Answer:**

This page describes the **Quality Migrant Admission Scheme (QMAS)** run by Hong Kong's Immigration Department — a program designed to attract skilled, talented individuals to settle in Hong Kong. Applicants who have met all the prerequisites may choose to be assessed under either the **General Points Test** or the **Achievement-based Points Test**, and each applicant may only submit one application under one assessment route at a time.

---

**🔵 Route 1 — General Points Test**

This is a scoring system across six categories:

| Category | What's Assessed |
| --- | --- |
| **(A) Age** | Whether the applicant is 50 or under |
| **(B) Academic Qualifications** | Master's or doctoral degree from an eligible university; bonus for STEM fields |
| **(C) Language Proficiency** | Proficiency in two languages (written & spoken); proficiency in English |
| **(D) Work Experience** | 5+ years graduate/specialist experience; 3+ years at MNCs/reputable firms; 3+ years in innovation/tech/finance/trade; 2+ years with international exposure |
| **(E) Annual Income** | HK$1 million or above in the preceding year |
| **(F) Business Ownership** | Owning a business with HK$5M+ annual profit, or a listed company |

The **prevailing passing threshold is 6 out of 12**. Only applicants meeting this threshold may submit applications via the online system, and the threshold is subject to change without prior notice.

---

**🔴 Route 2 — Achievement-based Points Test**

This route caters to individuals with **exceptional talent or outstanding achievements**. Requirements are very high — applicants must meet at least one of the following criteria:

1. Having received an award of exceptional achievement (e.g. Olympic medal, Nobel Prize, national/international award); or
2. Having work acknowledged by peers or contributing significantly to their field (e.g. a lifetime achievement award from their industry).

Failing to meet any criterion results in immediate refusal.

---

**In short**, QMAS is Hong Kong's points-based immigration pathway for high-caliber global talent, offering two routes depending on whether your strength lies in measurable credentials or world-class achievements.
