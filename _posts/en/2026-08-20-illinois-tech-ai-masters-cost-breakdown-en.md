Question: What is the cost of Illinois Tech Master of Artificial Intelligence program?

Answer:

Illinois Institute of Technology Master of Artificial Intelligence (M.A.S. in Artificial Intelligence) is a **30-credit graduate program**. ([Illinois Institute of Technology][1])

Estimated cost:

| Item                                       |                                                                                 Approximate cost |
| ------------------------------------------ | -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------: |
| Tuition                                    |                                                       ~$38,500 total (reported for 2026 intake)  |
| Tuition per credit (2025–26 graduate rate) |                     $1,851/credit × 30 credits ≈ $55,530 ([Illinois Institute of Technology][2]) |
| Fees + insurance                           |               ~$2,000–$4,000/year depending on situation ([Illinois Institute of Technology][2]) |
| Living cost in Chicago                     | ~$15,000–$25,000/year (rent, food, transportation, etc.) ([Illinois Institute of Technology][3]) |

Realistic total budget:

* **Tuition only:** ~$40k–55k USD
* **Including living expenses:** ~$60k–80k USD for the whole degree

In CNY (assuming 1 USD ≈ 7.2 CNY):

* Tuition: about **29–40万人民币**
* Total living + tuition: about **43–58万人民币**

For your situation (11 years engineering experience, LLM training, GPT-2/nanoGPT work), the key question is not only cost. Illinois Tech is a **career-oriented AI master's**, not a top research CS program like CMU/Stanford/MIT. It can help with:

1. US student visa (F-1)
2. OPT/STEM work authorization path
3. US job market access
4. Local networking in Chicago

But spending ~$70k USD is a large investment. Given your existing AI engineering experience, compare it against alternatives:

| Path                           |     Cost | Potential value                                      |
| ------------------------------ | -------: | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Illinois Tech AI Master        | $60k–80k | US entry + credential                                |
| Direct US AI job search        |      Low | Harder because no US work authorization              |
| Tourist visa + client meetings |      Low | Good for business development, but cannot work in US |
| Canadian study/work path       |  Similar | Easier immigration route in some cases               |

For your profile, I would evaluate Illinois Tech mainly as an **immigration + network investment**, not as a way to learn AI fundamentals. You already have practical AI experience beyond many master's students.
