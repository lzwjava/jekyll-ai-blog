**Question: What is the level and technical ranking of Tencent's T4 position?**

**Answer:**

Tencent underwent a major job level restructuring around 2022–2023, shifting from the old system (T1–T6 with sub-levels like T3-1, T3-2, T3-3) to a new simplified numeric system of professional levels from **4 to 17**.

In the **old system** (still frequently referenced in discussions), T4 was the **Expert Engineer** level in the technical (T) track. It was a highly respected and difficult-to-reach rank:

- T1: Assistant Engineer (typically new campus hires)
- T2: Engineer
- T3: Senior Engineer (most common ceiling for many engineers; T3-3 was roughly equivalent to strong senior/staff level)
- **T4: Expert Engineer** — widely regarded as elite technical individual contributor status, often comparable to a technical director level in responsibility and influence.
- T5: Scientist
- T6: Chief Scientist (extremely rare)

Reaching T4 was considered a major milestone. With Tencent having nearly 20,000 R&D staff at the time, only around 500 people (or fewer) achieved T4 — making it roughly top 2–3% of technical staff. The jump from T3-3 to T4-1 was notoriously hard, with many engineers stuck at T3 for 5–7+ years.

In the **current system** (4–17 levels), the old T4 roughly maps to around **level 10–12** (with level 10 often cited as roughly equivalent to old T4 / Ali P7+ level in community comparisons). Higher levels (e.g., 12+) represent very senior technical roles.

**Technical ranking summary**
T4 (old system) or its current equivalent is a **senior expert / principal-level** technical position — strong individual contributor or tech lead / architect role with deep expertise, major project impact, and influence across teams or organizations. It carries prestige in China's tech industry, often seen as comparable to:

- Alibaba P7–P8
- ByteDance 3-1 to 4-1 range
- Huawei 18–19 level (in rough terms)

Salaries at this level (China-based, total package including base + bonus + RSUs) typically fall in the **CN¥600,000–1,200,000+** range annually depending on performance, team, and stock value — though actual numbers vary widely by year, business unit (e.g., game teams often pay higher), and market conditions.

Overall, T4 is viewed as a very solid, high-status technical rank at Tencent — far above average but still below the rare "fellow/chief scientist" tier.
