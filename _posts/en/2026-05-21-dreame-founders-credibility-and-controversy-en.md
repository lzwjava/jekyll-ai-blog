## Answer

### Who is Yu Hao (俞浩)

Yu Hao (俞浩), Dreame's founder, was born in 1987. He's a physics prodigy admitted to Tsinghua University's aerospace program, who abandoned academia for engineering and founded Dreame in 2017.

**The origin story in brief:**

- Born in an ordinary township in Nantong, Jiangsu. At age 12, he completed his first invention — a dual-control switch. In 2005, based on his performance in the Physics Olympiad, he was admitted to Tsinghua University to study Aerospace Engineering.
- In 2007, Yu Hao became one of China's earliest developers of quadcopter drones. In 2009, he invented a tri-rotor drone and founded the geek space "Sky Factory" at Tsinghua University.
- Sky Factory attracted sponsorship from Boeing. Most of its core members later followed Yu into entrepreneurship, forming the founding team of Dreame.
- The decisive pivot came in 2015. Yu dismantled a pricey Dyson vacuum, discovered its core was merely a high-speed motor, and was convinced his aerospace-grade team could build a better one. In 2017, Dreame developed a motor capable of 125,000 RPM — outperforming the international industry leader by 8% — which caught the eye of Xiaomi CEO Lei Jun.
- Dreame secured 14 million yuan in angel funding and became an OEM within Xiaomi's ecological chain. Its first co-branded product, the Mijia Handheld Vacuum Cleaner 1C, raked in 15 million yuan in sales in just six days.

### Is the story credible, or is he bragging?

**The verifiable parts are real and impressive:**

- Physics Olympiad → Tsinghua admission (bypassing gaokao) — independently corroborated
- Boeing-sponsored trirotor drone at Tsinghua — corroborated by multiple sources
- He claims "三保送" — admitted to Tsinghua for undergrad, then master's, both by recommendation without exams. He says both Tsinghua and Peking University offered him spots and he chose Tsinghua. (This specific claim is harder to independently verify but consistent with the pattern of Physics Olympiad winners in China)
- Dreame has had revenue growth exceeding 100% for six consecutive years, with 2025 H1 revenue already surpassing all of 2024.
- As of 2025, Dreame's annual revenue is estimated to exceed 15 billion CNY, with net profit over 1 billion CNY.

So the technical founding story — aerospace engineer builds better motor, disrupts Dyson — is credible and documented. The company's growth metrics back it up.

**Where it gets braggadocious:**

- Yu posted on social media: "I want to push the world's most valuable company up by an order of magnitude — make the Dreame ecosystem reach $100 trillion." For context, that's 23 Nvidias, 25 Apples, or 280 Alibabas.
- At Dreame's "Dreame Night" concert, he publicly said: "A few years from now, when I become the world's richest man, I'll still tell [host] Sa Beining that I have no interest in money."
- An employee publicly called him out in a company-wide group chat, saying "Are you on drugs? Surpassing Nvidia in a year? Surpassing what China's entire auto industry hasn't achieved in 30 years?"

The honest read: the founding story is real and the execution has been solid. The $100 trillion talk is either aggressive vision-casting (Musk-style) or founder delusion — the market debate is literally framed as "Elon Musk or Jia Yueting?" Jia Yueting (贾跃亭) of LeEco famously expanded everywhere, burned cash, and fled to the US. Dreame's current sectoral reach is even broader than LeEco's was at its peak.

### His Unpopular / Contrarian Ideas

1. **"Consensus deserves a question mark"** — His China Newsweek profile is literally titled "Consensus is something you're allowed to put a question mark on." He projects extreme self-confidence and certainty, even on topics where the industry consensus disagrees with him.

2. **Unbounded expansion is the right strategy** — While most hardware companies are told to stay in their lane, Yu is simultaneously going into: EVs (targeting Bugatti Veyron price tier), astronomy (intelligent telescope), smartphones, large home appliances. His contrarian bet: the motor/robotics platform transfers across all of these.

3. **Car manufacturing from a vacuum cleaner company** — Yu frames this as fulfilling a "12-year car manufacturing dream" written in a 2013 Tsinghua business plan, not trend-chasing. Most analysts are skeptical. He doesn't even have manufacturing licenses yet.

4. **"The greatest company in human history"** — He's explicitly stated this as Dreame's mission. His recent 6.5-hour interview with LatePost was full of sharp, provocative statements — dismissing many industry peers and declaring Dreame's goal to become the greatest company in human history with a $100 trillion market cap. The interview went viral, with comment sections calling him arrogant.

5. **Technology from aerospace should lead consumer hardware** — The contrarian insight that actually worked: apply motor tech from aerospace to vacuum cleaners. Most consumer electronics companies source motors off-the-shelf. Yu built core motor IP from scratch with aerospace engineers.

---

### Bottom Line for You

Yu Hao is a legitimate technical founder — Physics Olympiad, Tsinghua aerospace, built real motor IP, created a company with genuine revenue at scale. The founding story is verifiable and impressive. The $100 trillion talk is a mix of vision-casting and founder hubris — in the same family as "we'll make humanity multiplanetary" but with far less product/capital validation behind it. The risk profile is real: LeEco vibes from the unbounded expansion, zero clarity on EV manufacturing licenses, and at least one internal employee revolt over strategy credibility.

Whether he's Musk or Jia Yueting will depend on whether the motor/robotics platform actually transfers across verticals or whether it's just a narrative to keep capital interested.
