Question: **What does “illicit drugs” mean?**

Answer: **Illicit drugs** means drugs whose **possession, production, or use is illegal or not legally authorized** under the relevant law.

Examples in the U.S. include:

* **Cocaine**
* **Heroin**
* **Methamphetamine**
* **MDMA/ecstasy**
* **Illegally obtained fentanyl**
* **Illegally obtained prescription drugs** such as oxycodone

One important nuance: **“illicit” doesn't necessarily mean the drug itself is always illegal.** For example, fentanyl is a legitimate medical drug when prescribed, but illegally manufactured or possessed fentanyl is illicit.

Also, in U.S. statistics, **marijuana is generally included in “illicit drugs,” even though recreational marijuana is legal in some states.** That's because the federal statistical definition is based on federal law.

So when I said **~69 million Americans used an illicit drug**, I meant they reported using at least one drug included in the federal survey's illicit-drug category during the previous year.
