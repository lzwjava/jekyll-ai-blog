**Question:** What is the Hong Kong 商务签注 (Business Visit Endorsement), and how is it used?

---

**Answer:**

## What is 商务签注?

The Business Endorsement (商务签注, code **S**) is issued to Mainland Chinese residents dispatched to Hong Kong (or Macau) for business activities. It comes in **three types**: 3-month single-entry, 3-month multiple-entry, and 1-year multiple-entry.

Each time you enter Hong Kong, the maximum stay per visit is **7 to 14 days** (depending on your endorsement). The endorsement is stamped on your Home Return Permit (往来港澳通行证), not a separate visa document.

---

## Who Can Apply?

Eligible applicants include:

1. Employees **dispatched by a company** to Hong Kong for business — the company must be a legally registered entity with independent legal person status, or a foreign enterprise resident representative office.
2. **Individual business operators** (个体工商户) travelling for business.
3. Drivers of **cross-border transport vehicles** between Mainland and Hong Kong.

> For the multiple-entry types (3-month or 1-year), the company must first complete a **business registration filing (商务备案)** with the local Public Security Bureau (PSB) Exit-Entry Administration.

---

## What Can You Do in HK with It?

Permitted business activities for holders include:

- Signing contracts or participating in **tendering/bidding**
- Inspecting goods or supervising equipment loading
- Attending **exhibitions and trade fairs** (but NOT directly selling goods to the public)
- Resolving claims or participating in civil litigation
- **Business or technology study tours**
- Attending short-term **seminars or conferences**

### ❌ What You CANNOT Do

Under Hong Kong immigration law, visitors admitted as visitors **cannot engage in any paid or unpaid work**. Offenders can face a fine of up to HK$50,000 and up to 2 years' imprisonment.

Using a business endorsement for **gambling, fraud, purchasing goods for resale (带货), or employment** is also strictly prohibited.

---

## How to Apply (Step-by-Step)

**Step 1 — Company Business Registration Filing (for multi-entry)**
The company must have legal person status, a unified social credit code, be operating normally, pay taxes lawfully, and have employees with no violations. This filing can be done online (e.g., via the Guangdong Government Services platform) or in-person at the local PSB Exit-Entry Hall on working days (9:00–12:00 AM, 1:00–5:00 PM).

**Step 2 — Individual Application**
The applicant submits:

- A completed *Application Form for Chinese Citizens' Entry-Exit Documents* (with blue-background photo)
- A company dispatch letter with official seal
- ID and household registration (hukou) documents
- The company's qualification certificate (资格认定书)

**Recent Policy Improvement:**
Mainland Chinese residents can now apply for the business endorsement at **any Public Security Bureau** across Mainland China, not just at the PSB where their sponsoring company is registered — a significant convenience upgrade.

---

## Bonus: 人才签注 vs. 商务签注

A separate **Talent Endorsement (人才签注)** was introduced for 6 categories of talent (outstanding, scientific research, cultural/educational, healthcare, legal, and other talents), allowing stays of **up to 30 days per visit** and validity from **1 to 5 years with unlimited entries** — compared to the 7-day cap on the standard business endorsement. If you qualify, this may be a better option.
