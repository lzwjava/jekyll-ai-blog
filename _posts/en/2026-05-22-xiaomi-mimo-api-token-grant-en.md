**What is MiMo?**

MiMo is Xiaomi's reasoning-focused LLM — they released **MiMo-7B** in early 2025, which got attention for punching above its weight on reasoning benchmarks (math, code) despite being only 7B parameters. The key claim was that their training recipe (heavy RL on reasoning tasks, similar in spirit to DeepSeek-R1) made a 7B model competitive with much larger models on specific tasks.
