3. **Examination Duration**: Usually 150 minutes (2.5 hours) for the written exam
4. **Examination Format**: Closed-book written examination with no reference materials allowed
5. **Passing Score**: 60 out of 100 points is the minimum passing grade
6. **Examination Frequency**: Offered twice per year in Guangdong (typically April and October)

8. **Total Marks**: 100 points total, distributed across different question types
9. **Registration**: Done through Guangdong Provincial Education Examination Authority website

64. **Allegory (寓言)**: Narrative with hidden meaning or moral lesson
65. **Metaphor (比喻)**: Figure of speech making implicit comparisons
66. **Personification (拟人)**: Attribution of human characteristics to non-human things
67. **Hyperbole (夸张)**: Exaggerated statements for effect
68. **Parallelism (对偶)**: Balanced phrases or sentences with similar structure
69. **Allusion (用典)**: Reference to historical or literary figures/events
70. **Imagery (意象)**: Vivid descriptive language appealing to senses
71. **Theme (主题)**: Central idea or underlying meaning
72. **Tone (语气)**: Author's attitude toward subject or audience
73. **Point of view (视角)**: Perspective from which story is told
74. **Characterization (人物刻画)**: Methods of developing character personalities
