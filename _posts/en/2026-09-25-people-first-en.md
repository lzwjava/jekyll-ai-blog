Question: What is the core stance of the Scientific Outlook on Development (科学发展观)?

Answer: The core stance (核心立场) of the Scientific Outlook on Development is **"people first" (以人为本, yǐ rén wéi běn)**.

This means that development must prioritize human beings — placing the interests, needs, and well-being of the people at the center of all governance and economic policy. Specifically, it entails:

- **Serving the people** as the fundamental purpose of the Party's governance
- **Relying on the people** as the driving force of development
- **Respecting people's rights** to economic, political, and cultural benefits
- **Promoting all-around human development** — not just GDP growth, but social harmony, education, health, and equity

The Scientific Outlook on Development itself (科学发展观) was articulated by Hu Jintao's leadership and formally enshrined in the CCP Constitution in 2012. Its full framework can be summarized as:

| Element | Chinese | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First principle | 第一要义 | Development is the top priority |
| **Core stance** | **核心立场** | **People first (以人为本)** |
| Basic requirement | 基本要求 | Comprehensive, balanced, sustainable development |
| Fundamental approach | 根本方法 | Overall coordination (统筹兼顾) |
