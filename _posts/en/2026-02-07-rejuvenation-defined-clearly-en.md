Question: What does rejuvenation mean?

Answer:

**Rejuvenation** means the process of making someone or something look, feel, or function **younger**, **fresher**, **more energetic**, or **more vital** again.

It comes from the Latin word *juvenis* = "young".

### Main meanings and common uses

1. **Personal / biological rejuvenation**
   - Making a person feel or appear younger
   - Examples:
     - "She looks rejuvenated after her vacation."
     - "I feel completely rejuvenated after a good night's sleep."
     - Anti-aging treatments, skincare routines, stem cell therapy, NAD+ boosters, etc. are often marketed as "rejuvenation" products or procedures.

2. **Medical / aesthetic rejuvenation**
   - Facial rejuvenation (Botox, fillers, laser treatments, facelifts)
   - Skin rejuvenation
   - Vaginal rejuvenation
   - Cellular / tissue rejuvenation (research field trying to reverse aspects of aging)

3. **General / figurative use**
   - Giving new life, energy, or strength to something old or tired
   - Examples:
     - "The new manager brought rejuvenation to the failing company."
     - "The city is undergoing urban rejuvenation."
     - "Spring rain brought rejuvenation to the dry garden."

4. **Scientific / longevity context (2024–2026 usage)**
   - "Rejuvenation" is a very common term in modern aging research
   - Refers to partially or fully reversing biological aging markers
   - Examples: cellular reprogramming (Yamanaka factors), epigenetic rejuvenation, plasma dilution, senolytic drugs, partial reprogramming protocols

### Quick summary – most common meanings today

- Feel/look/physically younger or more energetic again
- Restore youthfulness or vitality
- Reverse (some signs of) aging
