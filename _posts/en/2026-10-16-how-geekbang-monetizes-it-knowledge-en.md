**Question:** How does 极客时间 (Geekbang / Geek Time) make money?

---

**Answer:**

极客时间 is operated by 极客邦科技 (Geekbang Technology), a knowledge-as-a-service (KaaS) platform targeting developers and IT professionals. It has been described as an IT knowledge service platform that provides media, meetings, e-commerce, and consulting services. Its revenue comes from several streams:

**1. Individual Course Sales (单课付费)**
Users purchase specific courses à la carte on the app. The App Store listings confirm the app is free to download but offers in-app purchases, meaning individual courses are sold directly to consumers. The platform brings together 1,000+ senior technical experts from well-known IT companies and offers 400+ popular courses across AI, Java, Python, Go, architecture, and more.

**2. Subscription / Learning Pass (学习卡订阅)**
New users can receive a 7-day learning card to freely access 400+ tech courses and 1,400+ industry case studies. This trial funnels users into a recurring subscription model for continued access.

**3. Bootcamps / Training Camps (训练营)**
Bootcamps focus on cutting-edge tech and talent development trends, replicating a deep educational environment with a full "teach → learn → practice → test → evaluate" loop, so learners can hear, understand, and apply knowledge. These intensive programs are priced at a significant premium (often several thousand RMB each).

**4. Enterprise Edition B2B (企业版)**
This is a major growth pillar. 极客时间企业版 is an enterprise-level all-in-one digital technology learning platform that has served over 20,000 companies, covering banking, fintech, industrial manufacturing, internet, telecom, software, and education sectors. It offers an integrated "assess, learn, exam, evaluate" platform service tied to job competency models, helping learners see their progress and helping the learning program deliver results aligned with enterprise digital transformation strategy.

**5. Tech Conferences & Events (会议活动)**
Under its InfoQ brand, the company organizes well-known industry summits. It has held well-known technology summits QCon and ArchSummit for many years, generating revenue from ticket sales and sponsorships.

**6. Senior Executive Community (TGO鲲鹏会)**
Geekbang has initiated and established the TGO Kunpeng Club, a paid membership community for senior tech leaders (CTOs, architects), monetizing through membership dues and networking events.

**Funding backdrop:** Geekbang has raised a total of ~$29.4M, with its most recent round being ~$15.7M about 4 years ago. Funds from the B+ round were mainly earmarked for content development, technology R&D, and brand building.

In short, the business is a hybrid B2C (courses, subscriptions, bootcamps) + B2B (enterprise learning platform) + events model, with the enterprise side increasingly driving higher-value, recurring revenue.
