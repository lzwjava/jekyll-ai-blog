Question: How has Upwork performed recently, especially revenue and growth?

Answer: **Upwork’s business has become much more profitable, but top-line growth is still fairly slow.** The interesting part is that 2025–2026 looks more like a **margin/monetization + AI transition** than a high-growth marketplace story.

### Key numbers

|                    |  FY2024 |        FY2025 | Q2 2025 |      Q2 2026 |
| ------------------ | ------: | ------------: | ------: | -----------: |
| Revenue            |  ~$769M |   **$787.8M** | $194.9M | **~$191.7M** |
| Revenue growth     |       — |       **+2%** |     +1% |         ~-2% |
| Adjusted EBITDA    | $167.6M |   **$225.6M** |  $57.1M |   **$64.1M** |
| Adj. EBITDA margin |    ~22% |       **29%** |     29% |      **33%** |
| Active clients     |   ~832K |      **785K** |    796K |            — |
| GSV/client         |       — | **$5,129 Q4** |  $5,002 |   **$5,230** |

FY2025 revenue was a record **$787.8M**, but only +2% YoY. At the same time, adjusted EBITDA jumped **35% to $225.6M**, and operating cash flow reached **$248.3M**. ([Upwork Inc.][1])

The latest reported quarter, **Q2 2026**, had roughly **$191.7M revenue**: $166.9M marketplace revenue + $24.8M enterprise revenue. Adjusted EBITDA was **$64.1M**, a **33% margin**. GSV per active client reached a record **$5,230**. ([GlobeNewswire][2])

### The more interesting trend

Upwork's revenue growth has slowed dramatically:

```text
2024 revenue     ~$769M
2025 revenue     $788M       +2%
2026 guidance    $760–790M   originally +6–8%
```

The company entered 2026 guiding for **$760–790M revenue** and **$250–260M adjusted EBITDA**. ([Upwork Inc.][3])

But the underlying marketplace isn't simply collapsing. **GSV per client is rising**, while the number of active clients has fallen:

```text
2025 Q2 active clients      796K
2025 Q4 active clients      785K

GSV/client:
2025 Q2                    $5,002
2025 Q4                    $5,129
2026 Q2                    $5,230
```

So the shape is roughly:

**fewer clients × higher spend/client × much better monetization/margins**

rather than:

**lots more users × lots more transactions.**

### AI is becoming a meaningful part of the story

Upwork has been aggressively repositioning around **AI-related work**. Earlier data showed AI-related GSV growing substantially faster than the overall marketplace; in Q2 2024, for example, AI-related GSV grew 67% YoY while clients engaging in AI projects grew 50%. ([Upwork Inc.][4])

The company is now positioning itself as a marketplace for **human + AI work**, rather than merely a traditional freelancer marketplace. Its 2026 materials explicitly emphasize AI, SMB, and Enterprise as growth vectors. ([Upwork Inc.][5])

### My read of the financial structure

The numbers tell a pretty clear story:

```text
                    2024 → 2025 → 2026
Revenue growth       ↓       low/small
GSV/client            ↑       ↑
Active clients       ↓       ↓
EBITDA margin         ↑       ↑↑
Cash generation       ↑       ↑
AI-related work       ↑↑      ↑↑
```

So **Upwork isn't currently a hyper-growth company**. It's becoming a **high-margin cash-generating marketplace with relatively flat revenue**, while trying to use AI and enterprise offerings to restart growth.

For comparison, FY2025's **$225.6M adjusted EBITDA on $787.8M revenue** means nearly **29 cents of adjusted EBITDA per revenue dollar**, which is pretty substantial for a marketplace. ([Upwork Inc.][1])
