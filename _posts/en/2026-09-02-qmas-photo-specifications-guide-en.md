**Question:** For the QMAS application, what are the requirements for the recent photograph — specifically regarding JPEG format, minimum 1,200 × 1,600 px, 1 file only, and 5 MB or below?

---

**Answer:**

Your stated specifications are correct and align with the Hong Kong Immigration Department's official requirements. Here is the full breakdown of photograph requirements for the QMAS online application:

**Technical Specs**

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| File Format | JPEG only |
| File Size | 5 MB or below |
| Min. Digital Resolution | 1,200 px (W) × 1,600 px (H) |
| Scanned Alternative | 600 dpi, physical size 40 mm (W) × 50 mm (H) |
| Number of Files | 1 file only |

The Hong Kong Immigration Department specifies: image type must be JPEG, file size 5 MB or below, and for digital camera images, the minimum size is 1,200 px (W) × 1,600 px (H). If scanned, the scanner must be set to 600 dpi and the physical photograph must be 40 mm (W) × 50 mm (H).

**Content/Composition Requirements**

- The photo must represent your **current appearance** — not older than 6 months.
- The head must be **centered and not tilted** in any direction. Only a straight-on (en-face) view is accepted. A neutral facial expression is recommended, though a natural smile is allowed. Mouth should be closed but not tightened.
- **Selfies are not accepted** by HKSAR immigration services, as they distort facial features. The recommended distance between the camera and the subject is 1.2 to 2 metres.
- Background must be **plain white**.
- Glasses with **non-tinted** lenses are allowed; sunglasses are prohibited.

> ⚠️ **Note:** Even if a photo is successfully uploaded, it has yet to be determined whether it fully meets all requirements until reviewed by the Immigration Department.
