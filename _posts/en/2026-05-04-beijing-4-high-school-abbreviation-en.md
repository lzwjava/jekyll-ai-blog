**Question:** What does “SDSZ” mean in Beijing middle school, and which school does it refer to?

**Answer:**

In the context of Beijing middle schools, **“SDSZ” (四中)** most commonly refers to:

👉 **Beijing No.4 High School** (北京四中)

### Why “SDSZ”?

“SDSZ” is a **pinyin abbreviation**:

* **Si (S)** = 四 = Four
* **Da / Di (D)** = sometimes inserted for pronunciation or shorthand style
* **Shi (S)** = 市 = City (optional in abbreviation)
* **Zhong (Z)** = 中 = Middle school

So “SDSZ” roughly maps to **“Si (4) Zhong Xue” → No.4 Middle School**.

---

### Important nuance

Be careful — “SDSZ” is **not 100% unique**. It *can* theoretically refer to other schools like:

* Shandong Experimental High School (山东省实验中学)
* Other “Si Da Shi Zhong” variants in different cities

But in **Beijing context**, especially in conversations about elite middle/high schools, it **almost always means Beijing No.4 High School**.
