Question: **When did DJI actually begin?**

Answer: **2005–2006**, with **2006 being the actual company founding**.

The clean timeline is:

```text
2005
Frank Wang @ HKUST
    ↓
Final-year project:
helicopter flight-control system
    ↓
prototype works
    ↓
someone offers RMB 50,000 to buy it

2006
    ↓
Wang founds DJI
    ↓
initially operates from HKUST dorm
    ↓
moves to Shenzhen
    ↓
sells flight controllers

2008
    ↓
XP 3.1 flight-control system

2012
    ↓
professional aerial platforms

2013
    ↓
Phantom
    ↓
mass-market explosion
```

Wang himself says **“I completed [the helicopter flight-control project] in 2005. I founded the company in 2006.”** ([Forbes][1])

HKUST's own alumni profile confirms that his **2006 thesis involved a flight-control system for unmanned platforms**, and that he founded DJI based on that system. ([UST Alumni][2])

DJI itself describes the company as founded in **2006 in a college dorm room**, initially trying to make remote-controlled flight much easier. ([ViewPoints][3])

So if you ask **“When did the DJI startup really start?”**, I'd say:

> **2005 = technical origin.**
> **2006 = company origin.**
> **2013 = consumer-business breakout.**

That's an important distinction. **DJI existed for ~7 years before the Phantom made it famous.** The first 7 years were basically Wang turning one hard technical capability—**stable autonomous flight control**—into a product and company. ([scmp.com][4])
