Question: How do I check and enable international online payments for my OCBC Visa debit card?

Answer:

## How to Check & Enable Overseas/International Payments on OCBC Debit Card

> **Note:** For paying Anthropic (a US-based online merchant), the key setting you need is **"Overseas Card Usage"** — this covers international online transactions, not just physical overseas use.

---

### Method 1: OCBC Digital App (Mobile)

Follow these steps in the OCBC app:

1. Tap the **"More"** icon in the bottom navigation bar
2. Tap **"Card Services"**
3. Tap **"Manage overseas usage"**
4. Select your activation duration and tap **"Next"**
5. Confirm to activate

---

### Method 2: OCBC Internet Banking (Desktop/Browser)

Follow these steps on OCBC Internet Banking:

1. Scroll over **"Customer service"**
2. Select **"Card services"**
3. Select **"Manage overseas card usage"**
4. Tick the box to **activate** the feature
5. Indicate your preferred **activation period**
6. Read and accept the Terms & Conditions
7. Click **"Next"** → Review → **"Submit"**

---

### Important Things to Know

**Activation is immediate** — you can activate or deactivate overseas use via OCBC Internet Banking and the OCBC app with immediate effect.

**Deactivate when not needed** — OCBC recommends deactivating overseas usage for your card once it is no longer required, as a security precaution against unauthorized use.

**Online transactions may already work** — you can still use your card to make transactions via chip (EMV), contactless, mobile wallets, and **online transactions** even if the magnetic stripe is not activated. So for online payments like Anthropic, the "overseas usage" toggle mainly covers the payment routing approval.

**Also check your spending limit** — you can check or change your card's daily spending limit via OCBC Digital app under Card Services → "Change card daily limit." Make sure the limit is high enough to cover Anthropic's charges in SGD equivalent.
