**Question:** How do you play Call of Duty for beginners?

**Answer:**

## What is Call of Duty?

Call of Duty has been a premier first-person shooter in the gaming space for over 20 years. It's available on PS5, PS4, Xbox, PC (Battle.net/Steam), and mobile. The latest major entry is **Black Ops 7** (2025).

---

## Step 1: Getting Started

After booting up the game, you are taken to the **COD HQ** hub. From here, navigate to access Campaign, Multiplayer, Zombies, and Private Match modes. If it's your first time playing, select the **Training Course** via the Private Match tile to learn the ropes before jumping into real matches.

---

## Step 2: Understand the Main Game Modes

Call of Duty offers three major game modes:

- **Campaign** — A story-driven solo (or co-op) experience. Black Ops 7's co-op campaign supports up to four players tackling shared objectives and synchronized story moments.
- **Multiplayer** — Fast-paced action across diverse game modes, categorized into Free-for-All (every player for themselves) and Team modes. Popular modes include **Team Deathmatch**, **Domination**, **Search and Destroy**, and **Hardpoint**.
- **Zombies** — A round-based survival mode where you fight off waves of undead. Earn **Essence** (currency) by killing zombies — bonus Essence is earned by targeting critical points like the head — and use it to buy weapons, Perks, and upgrades.

---

## Step 3: Learn Basic Controls & Movement

Familiarize yourself with the core controls: movement, aiming, shooting, and reloading.

The **Omnimovement** system (in newer titles) lets you sprint, slide, and dive in any direction — it's your primary survival tool. Practice sliding sideways into cover when enemies spot you, and execute backward dives when retreating from losing engagements.

---

## Step 4: Build Your Loadout

Weapons are at the heart of Call of Duty gameplay. Experiment with different weapon classes — assault rifles, submachine guns, shotguns, sniper rifles — to find your preferred playstyle. Customize your loadout using perks, attachments, and equipment that suit your style.

Use the **Firing Range** in the Loadout menu to test different weapons and settings before going into a real match.

---

## Step 5: Understand XP & Progression

There are three types of XP to track:
- **Player XP (XP)** — Lets you rank up and unlock equipment and rewards.
- **Weapon XP (WXP)** — Unlocks attachments for weapons you use.
- **Battle Pass XP (BXP)** — Progresses you through seasonal Battle Pass content.

---

## Step 6: Key Beginner Tips

- **Study your death replays** — Most beginner deaths result from poor cover usage rather than inferior aim. Learn common enemy positions on each map and pre-aim these locations during rotations.
- **Explore alternate routes** — If you can't get past a strategically-located enemy or hidden sniper, find a different directional route to flank and attack them.
- **Adjust loadouts mid-game** — If you feel overwhelmed with your current setup, you can swap weapon attachments, perks, and equipment on the fly during a match.
- **Communicate with teammates** — Use in-game voice chat or external platforms like Discord to coordinate strategies and call out enemy positions.
- **Focus on objectives, not just kills** — Objective-focused play always outperforms kill-chasing in modes like Domination or Hardpoint.

---

## Step 7: Warzone (Battle Royale) Basics

If you play **Warzone** (the free-to-play Battle Royale mode):

You start each match with a pistol and must loot Supply Boxes to find better weapons. Collect as much **cash** as possible — it gives you the tools to create your own success during a match.

There are multiple modes: **Bootcamp** (training vs bots), **Battle Royale** (classic last-squad-standing), **Resurgence** (you can redeploy as long as a squadmate is alive), and **Plunder**.

---

**Summary:** Start with the Training Course, practice in Bootcamp or Team Deathmatch, experiment with different weapons, focus on movement and positioning, and communicate with your team. Practice consistently and you'll improve quickly!
