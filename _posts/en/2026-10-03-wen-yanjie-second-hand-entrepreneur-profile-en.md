Question: Who is 温言杰 (Wen Yanjie), the second-hand market entrepreneur?

Answer:

**Overview.** 温言杰 is a Chinese entrepreneur known for building 找靓机 (Zhaoliangji), a second-hand electronics platform. He is the founder and CEO of 找靓机, which was founded in 2016 and became profitable in September of that year.

**Origins.** After a period of searching for a direction, during which the team sold items like balance scooters to cover costs, they found strong demand for second-hand phones sold through WeChat Moments. They launched 找靓机 about half a month later. The headline of one profile says he was defrauded of over 2 million yuan in an earlier venture after graduating.

**Business model.** His core idea was to build trust in a category with non-standard goods. He modeled the company on JD.com's early approach of direct sales with guaranteed authenticity, aiming to be the "JD.com of second-hand 3C products." He argued that the sales end, not recycling, is where a durable competitive barrier can be built, through brand reputation. He also pushed back on the idea that second-hand consumption is limited to lower-tier cities, saying there is substantial untapped demand in first-tier cities.

**Growth.** According to 2019 reports, 找靓机 had over 70 million cumulative downloads, more than 1.5 million daily active users, and annual revenue above 5 billion yuan. Investors included Qingshan Capital, Meihua Venture Capital, Oriza Seedlings, and the actress Christy Chiu (贾静雯).

**Merger with Zhuanzhuan (2020).** In May 2020, the second-hand platform 转转 (Zhuanzhuan) announced a strategic merger with 找靓机, which continued as an independent brand under the group. Wen became president of the merged Zhuanzhuan Group, reporting to CEO 黄炜 (Huang Wei), and the combined company was valued at about US$1.8 billion.
