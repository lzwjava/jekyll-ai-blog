Question: What is LOL (League of Legends)?

Answer:
**League of Legends (LoL)** is a free-to-play multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) video game developed and published by Riot Games. First released on October 27, 2009, for Windows (with macOS support added in 2013), it has grown into one of the world's most popular games, boasting over 150 million monthly players and a massive esports scene.

### Core Gameplay

- **Format**: 5v5 team-based matches where two teams compete on a map called Summoner's Rift (the most popular mode). Each player controls a unique "champion" from a roster of over 160 characters, each with distinct abilities, roles (e.g., tanks, assassins, supports, mages, marksmen), and playstyles.
- **Objective**: Destroy the enemy team's Nexus (their base core) while defending your own. Matches typically last 20-50 minutes.
- **Key Elements**:
  - **Lanes and Jungling**: Players push three lanes (top, mid, bottom) with AI-controlled minions, while jungle monsters provide resources.
  - **Towers and Inhibitors**: Destroy defensive towers to advance and access the enemy base.
  - **Team Fights**: Epic clashes using ultimates, items, and objectives like dragons or Baron Nashor for buffs.
  - **Progression**: Earn gold and experience to buy items that enhance your champion.

The game is strategy-heavy, emphasizing teamwork, map awareness, mechanics, and macro/micro decisions. It's available on PC (Windows/macOS) and has spin-offs like Wild Rift (mobile) and Teamfight Tactics (auto-battler).

### Monetization and Community

- Completely free, with revenue from cosmetic skins, chromas, and battle passes—no pay-to-win elements affect gameplay balance.
- Huge esports presence: Leagues like LCK (Korea), LEC (Europe), LCS (North America), and the annual World Championship draw millions of viewers.
- Expansive universe: Includes lore across regions like Demacia and Noxus, animated series (Arcane), and other games/media.

New players can download it from the official site and try tutorials or bots to learn. It's rated Teen for fantasy violence and mild language.
