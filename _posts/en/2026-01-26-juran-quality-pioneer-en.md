Question: Who is Joseph Juran?

Answer:

**Joseph M. Juran** (December 24, 1904 – February 28, 2008) was one of the most influential figures in the history of **quality management** and is widely regarded as a foundational thinker in **modern quality control** and **Total Quality Management (TQM)**.

### Key Contributions and Legacy

- **Pareto Principle in quality**
  Juran popularized the idea that **80% of problems are caused by 20% of the causes** — commonly called the **Pareto Principle** or **80/20 rule** in quality contexts. He applied it to show that a small number of root causes usually account for the majority of defects, cost of poor quality, or customer complaints.

- **Juran Trilogy** (his most famous framework)
  1. **Quality Planning** — designing products, processes, and services that meet customer needs
  2. **Quality Control** — monitoring and maintaining performance (using statistical methods)
  3. **Quality Improvement** — systematically reducing chronic problems and waste (breakthrough improvement)

- **"Quality is fitness for use"**
  Juran defined quality from the **customer's perspective** rather than just meeting internal specifications. This customer-focused view was revolutionary in the mid-20th century.

- **Major works**
  - *Quality Control Handbook* (first edition 1951) — the "bible" of quality management for decades
  - *Juran on Planning for Quality* (1988)
  - *Juran on Leadership for Quality* (1989)

- **Role in Japan's post-war quality revolution**
  Along with W. Edwards Deming, Juran was invited to Japan in the 1950s.
  While Deming focused more on statistical process control and top-management responsibility, Juran emphasized **practical project-by-project improvement**, **middle-management involvement**, and the **human/managerial side** of quality.
  His teachings strongly influenced companies such as Toyota, Sony, and many others that later became global quality leaders.

- **Juran Institute**
  In 1979 he founded the Juran Institute, which continues to provide training, consulting, and certification in quality management worldwide.

### Quick Comparison: Juran vs. Deming

| Aspect                  | Joseph Juran                          | W. Edwards Deming                     |
|-------------------------|---------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Primary focus           | Managerial + project-based improvement | Statistical methods + system thinking |
| Famous concept          | Pareto Principle, Juran Trilogy       | 14 Points, System of Profound Knowledge |
| Definition of quality   | Fitness for use (customer view)       | Predictable uniformity / loss function |
| Approach to improvement | Breakthrough projects                 | Continuous (kaizen-style) improvement |

Joseph Juran is often summarized in one sentence as:
**"The man who taught the world that quality is a management responsibility and that most quality problems are preventable through proper planning and improvement processes."**
