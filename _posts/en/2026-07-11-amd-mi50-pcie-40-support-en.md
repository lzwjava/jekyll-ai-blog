Question: Is the AMD Instinct MI50 compatible with PCIe 4.0 or PCIe 3.0?

Answer: The AMD Instinct MI50 natively supports **PCIe 4.0 x16**. When it launched, AMD marketed it alongside the MI60 as part of the world's first family of PCIe Gen 4-capable GPUs.

Because PCIe technology is backward compatible, the card will also function perfectly in a **PCIe 3.0** slot, though it will be limited to PCIe 3.0 bandwidth speeds.
