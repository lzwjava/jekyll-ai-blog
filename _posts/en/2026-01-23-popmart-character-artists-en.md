**Question:** Who is the artist behind Pop Mart's cartoon characters?

**Answer:** 

Pop Mart does not have a single artist behind all its cartoon characters. Instead, it is a company that collaborates with multiple independent artists and designers to create its original intellectual properties (IPs) and cartoon-style toy figures, often sold in blind boxes.

Pop Mart works with various artists, each typically creating and owning their own signature character(s). Some of the most prominent examples include:

- **Kenny Wong** — Creator of **Molly**, one of Pop Mart's earliest and most iconic characters (first designed in 2006).
- **Kasing Lung** — Creator of **The Monsters** series, including the hugely popular **Labubu** (inspired by Nordic folklore and developed into a major Pop Mart line).
- **Ayan Deng** — Creator of **DIMOO**.
- **Xiong Miao (XiongMiao)** — Creator of **Skullpanda**.
- **Lang** — Creator of **Hirono**.
- Other notable artists: Pucky (for PUCKY), Libby Frame (for Peach Riot), Yumi (for Pino Jelly), and more.

The company was founded by Wang Ning in 2010, but he is a businessman, not an artist. Pop Mart's model involves partnering with these talented illustrators and toy designers to produce collectible figures based on their cartoon creations.

For a full list of artists and their characters, Pop Mart's official website has an "IP Artist Zone" section highlighting many of them.
