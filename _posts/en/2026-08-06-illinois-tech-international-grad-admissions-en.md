Illinois Institute of Technology (commonly called **Illinois Tech** or **IIT Chicago**) is a private research university located in Chicago, Illinois. It is known for engineering, computer science, architecture, applied sciences, and technology-focused programs. The page you sent explains requirements for **international graduate applicants**. ([Illinois Tech][1])

## 1. Main graduate admission requirements

For most master's programs:

* A **four-year bachelor's degree** (or equivalent) from an accredited institution.
* Usually a minimum **GPA equivalent around 3.0 / 4.0**.
* Department-specific requirements may vary. ([Illinois Tech][2])

Application materials usually include:

* Online application
* Transcripts
* Statement of purpose
* Resume/CV
* Recommendation letters (program dependent)
* English proficiency score (for international students)
* GRE (usually optional for master's programs) ([Illinois Tech][2])

## 2. English requirements

For international students:

Accepted tests:

| Test                  | Minimum |

| TOEFL iBT             | 80      |

| Duolingo English Test | 115     |

Scores are normally valid for two years. ([Illinois Tech][3])

Your IELTS 6.5 (from your background) meets the minimum requirement. ([Illinois Tech][3])

## 3. GRE requirement

For master's programs:

**GRE is not required.**

You can submit GRE if you think it strengthens your application. PhD programs may still require GRE depending on department. ([Illinois Tech][2])

This is good for people with strong engineering experience but weaker academic history.

## 4. Visa / financial requirement

After admission, international students need:

* Passport copy
* Proof of funds
* Financial affidavit

Then Illinois Tech issues the I-20 document for the F-1 student visa process. ([Illinois Tech][3])
