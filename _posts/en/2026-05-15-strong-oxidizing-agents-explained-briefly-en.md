Question: What is 强氧化剂?

Answer:

强氧化剂 (qiáng yǎng huà jì) in English is a **strong oxidizing agent**. It is a substance that **readily gains electrons from other substances**, causing those substances to be oxidized while itself being reduced. Strong oxidizing agents are highly reactive and can trigger chemical reactions, often releasing energy.

**Characteristics of strong oxidizing agents:**

1. **High affinity for electrons** – They can easily accept electrons from other molecules.
2. **Commonly contain elements in high oxidation states** – For example, oxygen, chlorine, fluorine, or compounds like permanganate (MnO₄⁻), dichromate (Cr₂O₇²⁻), and nitric acid (HNO₃).
3. **Can cause combustion or corrosion** – Many are dangerous if mishandled because they react violently with reducing agents, organic matter, or even metals.

**Examples:**

* Potassium permanganate (KMnO₄)
* Potassium dichromate (K₂Cr₂O₇)
* Concentrated nitric acid (HNO₃)
* Fluorine gas (F₂)
* Hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂, especially in high concentrations)

In short, a 强氧化剂 is a chemical that **strongly oxidizes other substances**, making it very useful in chemical reactions but also requiring careful handling.
