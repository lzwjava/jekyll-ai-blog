Question: What's the "Apple Pi" app?

Answer:

If you mean the app called **Pi** on the Apple App Store, it is **not made by Apple**. It's developed by **Inflection AI**, the company founded by Mustafa Suleyman (now at Microsoft AI) and others. The app simply runs on iPhone/iPad. ([App Store][1])

Its focus is quite different from ChatGPT or Claude:

* **Emotionally intelligent conversation** — designed to feel like a thoughtful conversation partner rather than a coding assistant.
* **Excellent voice mode** — one of the first AI apps to make voice conversations feel natural.
* **Memory** — remembers your preferences and previous conversations.
* **Life organization** — now includes reminders, checklists, grocery lists, and simple planning features. ([App Store][1])

Typical use cases:

* Talking through decisions
* Brainstorming ideas
* Language practice
* Journaling
* Casual conversation
* Emotional support (non-clinical)

It is **not** the best choice for:

* software engineering
* writing large codebases
* agent workflows
* deep technical research

For someone building AI agents (like you), Pi is more interesting as a **product design example** than as a coding assistant. It pioneered ideas such as:

* persistent memory
* conversational personality
* natural voice interaction
* long-term user relationship

Many of these ideas have since been adopted by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and others.

If, instead, you meant **"Apple Pi"** (with a capital Apple), there's also a small **unofficial macOS app** called **Apple Pi** from the Pi Coding Agent community. It provides a native sidebar UI for the Pi CLI coding agent by reading local session files and launching your existing `pi` binary. It isn't affiliated with Apple or Inflection AI. ([Reddit][2])
