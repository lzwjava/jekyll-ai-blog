Here's the email structure as a clean template you can reference or share:

---

**Subject:** Request for Endorsement — Right to Apply for [Company] Permanent Role (Job ID: XXXXX)

---

Hi [Name],

Hope you're doing well!

I'm reaching out to formally request [Agency]'s endorsement for me to apply and interview for an internal permanent position at [Company] (Job ID: XXXXX). As part of their process, [Company] requires a written endorsement from [Agency] granting me the right to be considered for this role.

My details for reference:
- Name: [Your Full Name]
- Employee ID: [XXXXXXXX]

Thank you for your support!

Best regards,
[Your Name]

---

**When to use this format:**
- You're a contractor exploring a permanent role at your client company
- Your staffing agency needs to formally consent / endorse your application
- The client's HR process requires written sign-off from the agency before interviewing

**Key elements:**
1. **Endorsement request** — explicitly ask for the right to apply and interview
2. **Job ID** — ties the endorsement to a specific role
3. **Your details** — name + employee ID for the agency's internal records
4. **Keep it short** — one paragraph is enough; the agency knows the process
