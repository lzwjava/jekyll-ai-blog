## Guangzhou Xinshi Hospital (广州新市医院)
### Also Known As: Guangdong Pharmaceutical University Guangzhou Fosun Chancheng Hospital (广东药科大学广州复星禅诚医院)

---

### Overview

Guangzhou Xinshi Hospital is located at No. 79 Xinshi New Street, Baiyun District, Guangzhou, Guangdong Province. It is a **Level-3 (tertiary) comprehensive hospital** integrating medical care, teaching, scientific research, and preventive healthcare, with a full range of clinical disciplines.

---

### History & Development

The hospital was **founded in October 2003**. In May 2004, due to administrative restructuring, it was renamed Guangzhou Xinshi Hospital. In June 2009, it was accepted into Guangzhou's 120 Emergency Medical Network. In August 2009, it entered a co-construction partnership with Guangdong Pharmaceutical University. In May 2013, it was renamed the Third Affiliated Hospital of Guangdong Pharmaceutical University (Guangzhou Xinshi Hospital). A new hospital campus opened in March 2016, and in December 2017, the hospital was officially rated as a **National Grade-3 Hospital**.

In 2021, the hospital joined **Fosun Pharma Group**, becoming one of its member hospitals.

---

### Scale & Staffing

The hospital currently has **48 clinical and medical technology departments**, with over **800 open beds** and more than **882 medical and technical personnel**. Among the staff are 2 individuals receiving special government allowances from the State Council, 2 Central Healthcare Bureau consultation experts, 19 provincial-level specialists, 116 senior-title professionals, and 223 mid-level title professionals.

---

### Accreditations & Designations

The hospital has earned numerous recognitions, including being designated as:
- A Guangzhou 120 Emergency Network Hospital
- A designated hospital for Guangzhou's basic medical insurance, work-related injury insurance, maternity insurance, and public medical care
- A National Health Commission Baby-Friendly Hospital
- A China Ping An Life Insurance designated hospital
- A Guangdong Province designated health examination center for employees and drivers

It has also received honors such as the **Reform & Innovation Brand Hospital**, **Medical Investment Contribution Brand Hospital**, **Anti-COVID Contribution Brand Hospital**, and **Baiyun District Best Service Credit Unit**.

---

### Key Specialties & Strategic Focus

As Fosun Pharma's strategic foothold in the Greater Bay Area, the hospital focuses on building core specialty areas in **cardiovascular disease, oncology, and neurological conditions**. It leverages the establishment of a National Chest Pain Center, Stroke Center, Trauma Center, and MDT (Multidisciplinary Team) model to drive growth across emergency medicine, obstetrics & gynecology, ICU, anesthesiology, gastroenterology, general surgery, urology, and general medicine.

---

### International Medical Services

On **March 26, 2025**, the hospital officially launched its **International Medical Center** and **International Oncology Minimally Invasive Diagnosis and Treatment Center**, aimed at serving residents of the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area, international visitors, and commercial insurance clients. Services include medical treatment, rehabilitation, recuperation, and traditional Chinese medicine health management.

Since its founding in 2003, the hospital has provided quality medical services to tens of thousands of patients from Hong Kong, Macao, Malaysia, Mongolia, South Korea, Uganda, Vietnam, and other countries. The hospital has established medical alliances with major tertiary hospitals in Guangzhou such as Guangzhou First People's Hospital and the First Affiliated Hospital of Guangzhou University of Chinese Medicine, and has signed strategic cooperation agreements with Hong Kong's Gleneagles Hospital and Hong Kong Adventist Hospital.

---

### Technology & Digital Innovation

The hospital has adopted the **Fosun Smart Healthcare Cloud Platform** and cloud-based Hospital Information System (HIS), significantly improving medical management efficiency and care quality. It has also established 15 management committees to build a comprehensive quality and safety governance network.

---

### Mission & Values

The hospital's motto is **"精诚济世，仁德示范"** ("Dedicated Expertise to Serve the World, Benevolent Virtue as a Model"), with a patient-centered service philosophy. The hospital's goals are to achieve **Grade-3A (Triple-A) comprehensive hospital** status, become a high-level medical university affiliated hospital, and be recognized as the best private hospital in the Guangzhou area.
