**Question:** What has been the trend in China's railway freight volume in recent years?

**Answer:**

China's railway freight volume has been on a strong and consistent upward trajectory, hitting record highs year after year. Here's a snapshot of the key milestones:

**Annual Freight Volume (Cargo Tonnes)****Key highlights:**

- **2024:** China's national railway moved 3.99 billion metric tons of cargo, marking the eighth consecutive year of growth.

- **2025 (full year):** Freight volume surpassed 5.27 billion tonnes in 2025, up 2 percent from the prior year. (Note: this figure appears to include broader national transport statistics, as some sources also cite ~4.4B tonnes for the national railway operator specifically.)

- **2025 mid-year momentum:** In the first seven months of 2025, the national railway transported over 2.33 billion tonnes of cargo, a year-on-year increase of 3.3 percent, with daily average loaded freight cars reaching 183,300 — up 4.1 percent.

- **Freight turnover (tonne-km):** China's railway freight total turnover reached 3,686,909 million tonne-km in 2025, an all-time record, up from 3,586,190 million tonne-km in 2024.

**Growth categories leading the way:**

- Coal transport exceeded 1.19 billion tonnes in Jan–Jul 2025, while mineral construction materials and grain freight rose 13.6% and 12.7% year-on-year, respectively.
- In June 2024, container, automobile, and cold-chain cargo shipments rose 18%, 12.1%, and 21.2% year-on-year, all reaching record highs.

**China-Europe rail corridor:**

In 2024, 19,000 China-Europe freight trains operated, transporting 2.07 million containers — increases of 10% and 9%, respectively, compared to the prior year. A historic milestone was also reached when the 100,000th China-Europe freight train journey was completed in December 2024.

**In summary,** China's railway freight has grown steadily and consistently, driven by domestic industrial demand (coal, construction, metals) and expanding international trade links, particularly through the China-Europe rail corridor.
