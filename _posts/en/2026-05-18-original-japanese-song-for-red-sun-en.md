## The Original Japanese Song Behind 红日 (Hung Yat)

### Song Identity

The Japanese original of 李克勤's (Hacken Lee's) *红日* is titled **《それが大事》** (Sore ga Daiji), which means "That Is What Matters Most" in English.

The original singer is **立川俊之 (Tachikawa Toshiyuki)**, the soul figure of the Japanese band group **大事MANブラザーズバンド** (Daiji MAN Brothers Band).

In **1991**, Tachikawa Toshiyuki released *それが大事*. Then in **1992**, Hacken Lee loved it so much that he rewrote the lyrics, purchased the rights, and renamed it *红日*.

*红日* became the theme song for the TVB drama *He Came from Heaven* and has since been regarded as a classic inspirational anthem.
