**Question:** What does “abliterated” mean in English?

**Answer:**
**Abliterated** is a niche/technical internet term derived from **“abliteration.”**

In LLMs, an **“abliterated model”** usually means a model that has had certain **refusal/safety behaviors removed or weakened**, typically by modifying internal model representations.

> **Qwen3-8B-abliterated** ≈ Qwen3-8B with its refusal behavior “abliterated” (removed).
