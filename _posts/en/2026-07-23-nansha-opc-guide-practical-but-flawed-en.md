Question: How accurate and useful is this Guangzhou Nansha OPC (One Person Company) registration guide?

Answer:

Overall: **good practical guide, but it mixes correct rules with some overconfident or potentially inaccurate claims.** For someone like you (AI/software consulting/startup), the direction is reasonable: a Nansha one-person limited company can be a lightweight vehicle. But I would verify several legal details before relying on it.

## ✅ Correct / mostly correct parts

### 1. One natural person can only have one single-person LLC

The statement:

> 1个自然人，全国只能注册1家OPC（一人独资有限公司）

is basically correct under China's Company Law:

* A natural person can establish only **one** one-person limited liability company.
* That company cannot establish another one-person LLC.

This is from the old Company Law and remains in the new 2024 Company Law framework.

---

### 2. No mandatory supervisor for OPC

The statement:

> 南沙特色政策：OPC无需强制设立监事

is plausible, but the wording is misleading.

This is **not a Nansha-only privilege**.

After the new Company Law implementation, small LLC governance became more flexible. A one-person LLC may not need a supervisor if it meets conditions and articles specify the governance structure.

So:

✅ "OPC can simplify governance"

❌ "Nansha invented this policy"

---

### 3. Registered capital advice

This part is good:

> 初创建议：10万～50万，不要盲目填大额。

Very practical.

After the 2024 Company Law:

* subscribed capital must generally be paid within 5 years for new companies.
* putting 10 million RMB registered capital casually creates future pressure.

For an AI consulting / software company:

Typical:

```
registered capital:
100,000 - 500,000 RMB
```

is reasonable.

---

## ⚠️ Needs correction / verification

### 1. "OPC名称规范：广州XX科技（自然人独资）有限公司"

This is likely wrong.

"自然人独资" is usually an ownership type shown in registration information, not necessarily part of the company name.

Normal:

```
广州XX科技有限公司
```

Registration type:

```
有限责任公司（自然人独资）
```

The guide makes it sound like the company name must include it.

---

### 2. "最快半天拿营业执照"

Possible, but don't treat as guaranteed.

Reality:

* simple application
* clean address
* electronic signature
* no review issues

can be same-day.

But:

```
half day ≠ guaranteed SLA
```

---

### 3. Annual audit requirement

This is the biggest questionable point.

The guide says:

> OPC章程每年需要出具财务审计报告（一人独资公司法强制要求）

This is **partially outdated/misleading**.

Historically, one-person LLCs had stronger audit requirements.

But under the new Company Law:

* annual financial reporting obligations exist.
* whether every OPC must hire an external CPA audit every year depends on laws/regulations and specific circumstances.

Do not blindly assume:

```
OPC = mandatory CPA audit every year
```

Need confirmation with a Guangzhou/Nansha accountant.

---

### 4. "OPC创业社区最长36个月免租"

Could be true as a local policy, but it is not universal.

Need check:

* eligibility
* AI company definition
* founder qualification
* application windows

Many government incubation policies are quota-based.

---

### 5. "双15%税收优惠"

Correct direction but easy to misunderstand.

Nansha has preferential corporate income tax policies, but:

```
registered in Nansha
        ≠
automatically 15% corporate tax
```

Usually requires:

* qualifying industry
* encouraged catalogue
* revenue structure
* substantive operation requirements

A pure shell company may not qualify.
