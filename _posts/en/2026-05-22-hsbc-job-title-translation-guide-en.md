**Question:** How to translate "Senior Consultant Specialist" (HSBC job title) to Chinese?

**Answer:**

**高级顾问专家**

Breakdown:

- Senior → 高级
- Consultant → 顾问
- Specialist → 专家

In HSBC's Chinese operations context, you might also see it rendered as **高级专家顾问** (swapping the last two words), but **高级顾问专家** follows the more natural English word order and is the standard HR translation used in Chinese banking/financial institutions.

If this appears on a resume or LinkedIn, **高级顾问专家** is the cleanest choice.
