Question: **When applying to Illinois Tech USA master's program, what visa status should I choose? I only had an expired tourist visa (B1/B2).**

Answer:

For a **master's program at Illinois Institute of Technology (Illinois Tech)**, you normally need an **F-1 student visa**. Do **not** choose your old tourist visa (B-1/B-2) as the desired visa status. Illinois Tech issues an **I-20** for admitted degree-seeking international students, and you use that document to apply for an **F-1 visa**. ([Illinois Institute of Technology][1])

Fill the form like this:

### 1. "Do you currently hold a US Visa?"

Choose:

```
None - I do not currently hold a US visa
```

Because your B1/B2 visa expired in 2025, you currently do not hold a valid US visa.

### 2. "What is your desired visa status?"

Choose:

```
F-1
```

The list has:

```
F-1
F-2
...
```

Select **F-1**.

Your situation:

```
Nationality: China
Applying: MS Computer Science / AI related master's
Current US visa: None
Previous US visa: B1/B2 (expired)
Desired status: F-1
```

The old tourist visa does not help for studying. You cannot enter the US and attend a full master's program using B1/B2 status.

One additional point for your background: since you have significant engineering experience and AI projects, your F-1 interview story should be very clear:

> "I am pursuing a master's degree to deepen my AI/ML research and engineering capability. After graduation I plan to use this expertise in AI engineering/research."

Avoid framing it as "I want to immigrate to the US"; F-1 is a non-immigrant student visa.
