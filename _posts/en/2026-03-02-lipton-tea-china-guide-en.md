**Question: What Lipton tea products are available in China, particularly what tea packets they provide? The user mentions red tea as Lipton black tea packets bought from JD.com.**

**Answer:**

In China, Lipton (known locally as 立顿) offers a range of tea products tailored to local preferences, including various **teabags (tea packets)**. "Red tea" (红茶) in Chinese commonly refers to black tea, so the Lipton black tea packets you bought from JD.com are typical "red tea" products there.

Lipton entered China in 1992 and focuses on convenient teabags, which remain somewhat novel compared to traditional loose-leaf brewing. Their main offerings in China include:

### Teabag (Tea Packet) Varieties
- **Black Tea (Red Tea / 红茶)** — The most classic and popular line.
  - Yellow Label / Classic Black Tea (黄牌精选红茶) — Often the signature strong, brisk black tea blend.
  - Warm & Mellow Black Tea (温润红茶).
  - English Breakfast style or similar invigorating black teas.
  - These are widely sold in packs like 25, 50, 100, or larger office-sized boxes (e.g., 100-200 bags).
- **Green Tea (绿茶)** — Adapted for Chinese consumers, including plain or lightly flavored options.
- **Jasmine Tea (茉莉花茶)** — Scented green tea, a popular local style.
- **Oolong Tea (乌龙茶)** — Including fruit-infused variants like white peach oolong.
- **Flavored / Fruit Blends** — Such as lemon black tea, fruit oolong, or other mixes in small trial packs (e.g., 7-bag boxes).
- **Specialty or Mixed Assortments** — Combinations of black, green, jasmine, oolong, Earl Grey, etc., in variety boxes (e.g., 80-125 bags with multiple flavors).

Lipton also sells **powdered products** like milk tea powder (奶茶) and fruit tea powder, popular for instant Hong Kong-style or flavored drinks, but the core packet products are teabags.

These are commonly available on platforms like JD.com, with many listings for black tea packets (often labeled as red tea), green tea, jasmine, and mixed assortments. Pack sizes vary from small portable packs to large bulk boxes for home or office use.
