**Question:** How have LLMs evolved across recent years?

**Answer:**

The story breaks cleanly into five chapters:

**2022 — The awakening.** The emergence of ChatGPT in late 2022 popularized interactive dialogue and sparked mass adoption. The five-era timeline of LLM evolution spans from foundation (2017–2018), through scaling (2019–2020), scaling breakthroughs (2021–2022), efficiency and democratisation (2022–2024), and finally multimodal and reasoning specialisation (2024–2026).

**2023 — Open source and multimodal.** Open-source projects such as LLaMA and the rise of specialized instruction tuning accelerated progress in 2023 and 2024, paving the way for even larger models with multimodal abilities and longer context windows.

**2024 — The reasoning pivot.** It started when OpenAI released o1-preview in September 2024 — the first public reasoning model — based on an architecture internally called "Strawberry." Meanwhile, small models showed massive performance gains: 4B parameter models would eventually outperform 2024's 70B models.

**2025 — The DeepSeek moment.** In late January 2025, DeepSeek-R1 made mainstream tech and financial news for performance rivaling that of top proprietary models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Google at a significantly lower price point. DeepSeek R1 is approximately 95% less costly to train and deploy compared to OpenAI's o1, making it far more accessible and democratizing high-performance reasoning models.

**2026 — Consolidation.** My reading of the timeline: the models' "core" reasoning had its last big leap in 2024. 2026 is the year of refining the harness — the model+harness combination got much more competent, even with base intelligence nearly stalled.

The arc is clear: from scale → alignment → multimodal → open-source → reasoning → agents. Each year's breakthrough reset what the *next* breakthrough had to be.
