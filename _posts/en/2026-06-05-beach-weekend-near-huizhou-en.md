Question: What about beaches and islands near Huizhou for a weekend trip with two couples and a 5-year-old child?

Answer:

If your family is staying in 惠城区 and only has Saturday + Sunday morning available, I would **skip mountains** and focus on the coast.

### Option 1 (Recommended): 双月湾 (Shuangyue Bay)

This is probably the best choice for your group.

Reasons:

* About 1.5–2 hours drive from 惠城区.
* Beautiful beach scenery.
* Famous viewing platform overlooking the two crescent-shaped bays.
* Plenty of seafood restaurants.
* Kids can play on the beach for hours.
* Less crowded and more natural than some other Guangdong beaches. ([chinatripedia][1])

Suggested itinerary:

#### Saturday

* 08:30 Leave 惠城区
* 10:30 Arrive at 双月湾
* Beach play + sandcastle time
* Seafood lunch
* Hotel check-in (sea-view hotel if budget allows)
* Afternoon beach activities
* Sunset at 双月湾观景台
* Seafood dinner

#### Sunday

* Breakfast by the sea
* Visit 海龟湾 (Sea Turtle Bay)
* Short walk along the coast
* Lunch
* Return to 增城 around 13:00–14:00

Sea Turtle Bay is especially good for a 5-year-old because it has sea turtle conservation exhibits and coastal scenery. ([chinatripedia][1])

---

### Option 2: 巽寮湾 (Xunliao Bay)

Closer to a traditional resort area.

Advantages:

* Long sandy beaches.
* Many hotels and restaurants.
* Easier for families with young children.
* Water sports available for adults. ([Silk Road Travel][2])

Highlights:

* 磨子石公园 (interesting rock formations)
* Beach walks
* Seafood

If your wife and daughter prefer relaxing rather than sightseeing, 巽寮湾 may actually be more comfortable than 双月湾. ([chinatripedia][3])

---

### Option 3: 三角洲岛 (Delta Island)

If you specifically want an island experience.

Advantages:

* Boat ride to the island.
* Clear water and white sand.
* More "vacation island" feeling.
* Fun for photos. ([Silk Road Travel][2])

Disadvantages:

* Need to take a speedboat.
* More expensive.
* Less convenient with luggage and a young child.

For a first family trip, I would treat this as a half-day activity from 巽寮湾 rather than staying on the island. ([Silk Road Travel][2])

---

### My recommendation

For **2 couples + one 5-year-old girl**:

**Friday night:** Stay in 惠城区.

**Saturday:** Drive to **双月湾**, stay one night in a sea-view hotel.

**Sunday morning:** 海龟湾 + beach walk.

**Sunday after lunch:** Drive back to 广州增城.

This minimizes driving, maximizes beach time, and gives the child plenty of opportunities to play in the sand rather than spending the weekend sitting in a car. The sea turtle reserve is also something many children remember more than another park or mountain. ([chinatripedia][1])
