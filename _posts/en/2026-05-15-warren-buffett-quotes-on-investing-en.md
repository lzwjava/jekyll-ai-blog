Question: Can you provide quotes from Warren Buffett?

Answer:

Here are some notable quotes from Warren Buffett:

1. “Price is what you pay. Value is what you get.”
2. “It’s far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price.”
3. “Risk comes from not knowing what you’re doing.”
4. “The most important investment you can make is in yourself.”
5. “Be fearful when others are greedy and greedy when others are fearful.”
6. “Someone’s sitting in the shade today because someone planted a tree a long time ago.”
7. “Our favorite holding period is forever.”
8. “Chains of habit are too light to be felt until they are too heavy to be broken.”
9. “Never depend on a single income. Make investment to create a second source.”
10. “The difference between successful people and really successful people is that really successful people say no to almost everything.”

These reflect his approach to investing, patience, and personal growth.
