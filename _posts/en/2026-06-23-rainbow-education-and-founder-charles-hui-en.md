**Question:** What is 瑞德彩虹教育升学留学中心, and who is its founder 许勤业? What is HUI 香港留学中心?**Question:** What is 瑞德彩虹教育升学留学中心 (Rainbow Education Study Abroad Center), and who is its founder 许勤业 (Charles HUI)?

**Answer:**

**瑞德彩虹教育升学留学中心** (Rainbow Education) is a China-based education and study-abroad consultancy focused on helping mainland Chinese students apply to universities in Hong Kong and Macau. It operates branches in Wuhan, Shanghai, and other cities.

**What they do:**

The Hong Kong Study Center is dedicated to promoting Hong Kong's educational advantages, acting as a bridge for people outside Hong Kong, and providing Hong Kong education consulting services. They offer a large volume of language training and HK/Macau study-abroad services to mainland students.

Services span the full application pipeline: professional positioning consultation, personal statement writing, materials preparation, end-to-end application tracking, plus language and interview preparation. After admission, they assist with student visas and enrollment; after the student arrives in Hong Kong, they provide periodic follow-up on academic performance, extracurricular tutoring, long-term study planning, and life support services.

Coverage includes all eight major Hong Kong universities: HKU, CUHK, HKUST, CityU, PolyU, HKBU, EdUHK, and Lingnan.

**Founder: 许勤业 (Charles HUI)**

Charles HUI is the Principal (总校长) of the Hong Kong Study Center, President of the CUHK Hubei Alumni Association, and former Vice-President of the Wuhan Hong Kong Chamber of Commerce.

His background: He graduated from CUHK's United College in 1985, majoring in Economics. He then worked at the Bank of Tokyo in Hong Kong, followed by a British derivatives securities firm (Cresvale), then Wako Securities Hong Kong. From 1991, he managed Southeast Asian investment funds at a Tokyo financial firm, returned to Hong Kong to join Reuters, and was seconded to Reuters Japan for five years.

He also holds a degree from HKU. After 2001, he relocated to Wuhan and in 2006 founded 瑞德彩虹语言培训中心. Through a referral from CUHK, the school became the base for establishing the CUHK Hubei Alumni Association, with Hui serving as president. He has been called a "Hong Kong university admissions authority" by media.
