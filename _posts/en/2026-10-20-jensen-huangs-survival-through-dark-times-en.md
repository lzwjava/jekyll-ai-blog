**Question:** How did Jensen Huang get through the dark times?

**Answer:**

Jensen Huang's journey with NVIDIA was far from smooth — it was filled with near-bankruptcies, humiliation, and relentless pressure. Here's how he endured:

---

### 🔥 The Dark Times He Faced

**1. Near-bankruptcy in 1995–1996**
NVIDIA almost went bankrupt in 1995 after its first chip, the NV1, failed to attract customers. It had to lay off half its employees before the success of its third chip, the RIVA 128, saved it.

**2. The Sega crisis**
Rapid changes in the industry forced NVIDIA to give up a contract with Sega in a near-death brush with bankruptcy — but Sega's leadership helped avert it. Sega provided NVIDIA with a $5 million investment that kept the company afloat.

**3. The 2008 financial crash**
During the 2008 financial crisis, NVIDIA shares dropped by about 85% from their late-2007 highs. "It was humiliating," Huang said.

**4. The CUDA bet in the mid-2010s**
Huang described periods in the mid-2010s when NVIDIA's stock price collapsed while the company continued pouring money into CUDA, the software platform that later became foundational to modern AI systems.

---

### 💪 How He Got Through It

**1. Training himself to forget yesterday**
Huang said the only way he endured was by training himself not to dwell on setbacks — he called it "forgetting yesterday."

**2. Using worry as fuel**
Huang said living in a constant state of worry has its advantages: "I think during adversity you're more focused."

**3. Humility — asking for help**
Huang said at an NTU commencement: "Confronting our mistake and, with humility, asking for help saved NVIDIA."

**4. Staying committed to dismissed ideas**
NVIDIA's success came from staying committed to ideas others dismissed — especially the belief that graphics chips could someday power a new era of computing beyond video games.

**5. Treating near-death as a permanent mindset**
"I think when you build a company from the ground up, and you've experienced real adversity, and you really experienced nearly going out of business several times, that feeling stays with you," Huang said. Rather than becoming complacent, he keeps that fear alive as a motivator.

---

### 🗣️ His Honest Reflection

Huang admitted he would not have founded the company if he had known in advance the level of pain, pressure, and "humiliation" involved — and said that people overlook the emotional cost and perseverance behind NVIDIA's success.
