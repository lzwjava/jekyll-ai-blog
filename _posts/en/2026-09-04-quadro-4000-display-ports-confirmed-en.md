Question: Does the Quadro 4000 have **2× DisplayPort + 1× DVI**?

Answer: **Yes, if that's what your physical card has, that's likely a different Quadro 4000 variant/OEM design.** My previous answer was too broad.

The original full-size NVIDIA Quadro 4000 (Fermi) reference design is commonly **2× DisplayPort + 1× DVI-I**.

So if yours has:

```text
[ DP ] [ DP ] [ DVI-I ]
```

that's normal.
