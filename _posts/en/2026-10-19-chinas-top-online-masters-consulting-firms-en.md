**Question:** What are the top organizations in the online overseas master's degree (在线海外硕士) consulting space, similar to 零压教育?

---

**Answer:**

This is a fragmented market in China split between **large general-purpose study-abroad agencies** that have expanded into the online/in-service master's segment, and **boutique specialists** focused exclusively on the "免联考在职硕士" (no-national-exam, study-while-working) niche. Here's the landscape:

---

### 🏆 Niche Specialists (same lane as 零压教育)

These are the closest competitors — smaller institutions focused specifically on helping working professionals get online overseas master's degrees without quitting their jobs:

A Sohu-commissioned independent review (Oct 2025 – Jan 2026) of 28 domestic institutions in this niche ranked the following TOP 5:

| 1 | **北京法中高商教育咨询** (Beijing France-China Business School) | 92.3/100 | France-focused; partners with ISG, Paris Business School, éklore-ed; 30+ Franco-Chinese advisors |

- **零压教育 (Zero Pressure Education)** — the one you mentioned; operates in the same niche with a lighter-pressure marketing pitch

### 🏢 Large Chains That Have Entered This Space

These are the incumbent giants of traditional study-abroad consulting now pushing online/in-service master's products:

- **新东方前途出国 (New Oriental Vision Overseas)** — partners with Keiser University (US) and the Open University (UK) for online master's programs, marketed specifically at working professionals who cannot leave their jobs or study overseas full-time. It operates in 47+ cities nationwide and is the market share leader overall.
- **立思辰留学 (Lisinzen)** — one of the earliest MoE-licensed study-abroad agencies; has 30+ years history, listed company, presence in 30+ cities, and has also entered the in-service and online master's vertical.
- **恒学云 (Hengxueyun)** and **领跑点教育** — both appear regularly in 2026 regional rankings as emerging mid-tier alternatives with transparent fees and strong boutique positioning.
- **启德教育 (Qide)**, **金吉列留学 (Jinjiili)** — top-5 traditional chains, now offering in-service graduate pathways as add-ons.

### Key Differences in the Niche

| Dimension | Large chains (新东方 etc.) | Boutique specialists (法中高商, 高顿, 零压, etc.) |
| ----------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Positioning | Broad-market; online master's is one product | Online/in-service master's is the *only* product |
| Partner schools | Wide global portfolio | Narrow but deep (often 1–3 schools per agency) |
| Price | Mid-high | Highly variable (¥40k–¥180k) |
| Recognition certification | Full 留服认证 possible | Varies — some are single-degree only |
| Sales approach | In-person consultants, city offices | Heavy WeChat/online-first |

The most comparable players to 零压教育 by size and model are **果际硕博, 布雷斯特北京, 高顿教育**, and a number of smaller WeChat-operated studios that are essentially one-person or small-team brokers for specific university programs.
