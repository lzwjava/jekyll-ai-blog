**Question:** What happened with the 梦百合 (MLILY) founder — was he cheated by many people and lost hundreds of millions?

**Answer:**

The story is somewhat different from the popular narrative. Based on search results, here's what actually happened with 梦百合 founder **倪张根 (Ni Zhanggen)**:

**The Core Legal Dispute (Not Exactly "Being Cheated by Many")**

梦百合 (SH603313) was involved in a cross-border equity dispute, and was initially ruled by a US court to pay its overseas distributor approximately **$24.71 million** (about **1.59 billion RMB**). After adjustment, this was reduced to **$17.27 million** (~1.23 billion RMB).

The case was initiated by an American, Benjamin Folkins (BJ), who was previously 梦百合's US distributor and an important partner in the US subsidiary. The first-instance verdict ruled that 梦百合 had lost the case and needed to pay about $24.71 million, including $20 million in **punitive damages**. 梦百合 refused to accept this verdict.

In late 2016, BJ wanted to exit and demanded 梦百合 pay over $3 million for his 35% stake in the US subsidiary. 梦百合's wholly-owned subsidiary Henkkang Hong Kong refused. BJ then sued 梦百合, Henkkang Hong Kong, and 倪张根, while 梦百合 counter-sued BJ and his company UM.

**Good News on Appeal**

After 梦百合 appealed to the Tennessee Court of Appeals, the court announced in November 2023 that the jury verdict and the lower court's judgment were **overturned**, and the case was remanded for retrial. 倪张根 publicly declared on social media the day before: "Justice may be late, but it will not be absent." He also said this was a lawsuit he would "fight with his life," as it concerns the dignity of himself and the company.

**Other Business Headwinds**

梦百合 also suffered significant profit declines due to sharply rising raw material prices, labor costs, and overseas shipping costs. The company predicted a net loss of approximately **2.4–3 billion RMB** for 2021, a year-over-year decrease of 163%–179%.

**Summary:** The "hundreds of millions lost" framing comes mainly from **one major US court battle** with a former American business partner, not being cheated by multiple people. The punitive damages were enormous and threatened the company's full-year profits. However, the appeal court sided with 梦百合 and sent the case back for retrial — so the founder did not simply "lose" the money.
