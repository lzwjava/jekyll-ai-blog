Given your current situation, I would not move your wife and two daughters to Beijing yet. I would give yourself 6–8 weeks to validate Beijing as a better base for your AI career, then make the family relocation decision based on evidence.

This is not a recommendation to abandon Beijing. It's a recommendation to make the move when it has a good chance of working.

You have three competing priorities:

1. Rebuild income while managing your mortgage.
2. Compound your AI capabilities and network through model training, inference, post-training, and agent development.
3. Protect your family's stability, particularly with a five-year-old and a one-year-old.

Moving before you have income or a concrete opportunity adds a fourth problem—relocation costs—without yet solving the first one.

There is a real opportunity worth testing. Current listings include foundation-model engineering at Apple, AI research engineering at Intel, and AI infrastructure and research opportunities at other companies in Beijing.&#x20;

## 2. Set explicit conditions for moving

I would use the following thresholds as your decision framework. These are suggested personal guardrails, not market benchmarks.

| Condition        | Threshold before moving everyone                                              |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Income           | A signed offer or reliable, contracted client revenue                         |
| Financial runway | Ideally 6 months of essential household expenses after relocation costs       |
| Housing          | A realistic rental plan that does not depend on selling your home immediately |
| Childcare        | A specific agreement with your wife's mother, not just a possibility          |
| Education        | Verified school eligibility and enrollment options                            |
| Family agreement | Your wife genuinely agrees after discussing the trade-offs                    |

If you get a good offer but cannot yet satisfy all the family conditions, consider relocating alone temporarily or commuting for a defined trial period. Agree on a maximum duration and a reunion date so that a temporary arrangement does not become indefinite.

If your cash runway is too short, prioritize generating income in Guangzhou while interviewing for Beijing positions remotely.

## 3. Be careful about your older daughter's schooling

Your older daughter is approaching primary-school age. Beijing's 2026 enrollment rules require non-local-hukou families to pass the relevant district's eligibility review, involving documents such as employment, residence, household registration, and a Beijing residence permit. The detailed requirements vary by district.&#x20;

## 4. The plan I would execute

Days 1–14: Build your Beijing pipeline

Apply to 20–30 well-matched roles and contact potential consulting clients. Tailor your resume around concrete evidence: your model-training experiments, inference work, production engineering, and AI agents. Aim to get real conversations scheduled.

Days 15–30: Protect the household

Calculate your essential monthly spending, mortgage, current liquid savings, and the cost of two-city living. Keep education spending and relocation spending separate from the money needed to support your family.

Days 31–45: Validate the family arrangement

Have your wife speak directly with her mother about practical childcare. Investigate housing, school eligibility, and whether your wife would have a comfortable daily life in Beijing.

Days 46–60: Decide using evidence

If there is a credible offer, client revenue, and a workable childcare plan, move forward. If not, remain in Guangzhou and continue the search. You can reassess after another 60–90 days rather than making a high-cost move based on hope.

## 5. One important distinction

Don't confuse moving to Beijing with making progress toward your long-term goals.

You can continue training models, improving inference, publishing technical work, and building an AI consultancy from Guangzhou. Beijing becomes valuable when it gives you access to resources, people, or opportunities that you cannot obtain as effectively from your current base.

Likewise, don't let the mortgage force you into a permanent decision before you've compared the alternatives. Selling your home, relocating, and changing your children's environment are separate decisions. You do not need to make all three at once.

My bottom line: stay in Guangzhou for the next 6–8 weeks, pursue Beijing aggressively, and relocate the family only when the opportunity and family logistics are concrete.

One final detail would help me make this recommendation much more precise: how many months can your current liquid savings cover your family's essential expenses and mortgage, with no employment income? That determines whether you have time to run this experiment or need to prioritize immediate cash flow first.
