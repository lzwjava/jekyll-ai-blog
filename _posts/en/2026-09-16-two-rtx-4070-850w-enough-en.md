Question: Is this AK850 850W PSU suitable for a build with two RTX 4070 GPUs?

Answer: **Yes, potentially — but I would not recommend it blindly.**

For two standard **RTX 4070** cards:

* RTX 4070: ~200W each → **~400W total**
* CPU: typically ~65–150W
* Motherboard/RAM/SSD/fans: ~50–100W
* Estimated system load: **~550–650W**
* An 850W PSU has enough wattage **on paper**.

However, the important part is **the PSU's actual quality**, not just the "850W" label. For a dual-GPU workstation, I'd want a reputable PSU with good protections and preferably **native PCIe 5.x / 12V-2x6** support.

Also, if you're using **two RTX 4070s**, check the exact models. Some 4070 variants have different power connectors.

**Bottom line:**
**850W is enough for 2× RTX 4070 from a power-budget perspective, but I wouldn't buy an unknown AK850 specifically for a $1,000+ dual-GPU setup without verifying its manufacturer/model and electrical specs.** A good 1000W PSU would give you substantially more headroom.
