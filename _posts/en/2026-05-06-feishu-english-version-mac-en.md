Question: How is the English version of Feishu on Mac — is it good, and do we need to switch it to Chinese?

Answer:

## Feishu on Mac: English Version Assessment

### What is Feishu (vs. Lark)?

This is a critical distinction first: Feishu exists specifically to service Chinese clients in China, while Lark is its international sister app launched in 2019 for global audiences. As confirmed by ByteDance support, Lark and Feishu are two different apps that do not interact perfectly with each other.

In short: **Feishu (飞书) = China-focused app. Lark = International version.**

---

### English Language Support in Feishu

Feishu **does** officially support English as a display language. You can change the display language of the Feishu app, with supported options including English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong, China), and others.

However, the English quality has notable limitations:

- **Feishu is fundamentally a Chinese-first product.** Its UI, documentation, and support are primarily designed around Chinese users. The English translation covers the core interface, but advanced features, admin panels, or newer features may still appear partially in Chinese.
- According to one review platform, Feishu officially supports only Chinese (Mandarin) as its primary product language — this aligns with user reports that deeper menus and settings can default to Chinese.
- Feishu is described as an AI work platform by ByteDance, offering enterprise collaboration tools deeply integrated into Chinese enterprise scenarios — which means some workflows and features are designed with Chinese business practices in mind.

---

### Do You Need to Set It to Chinese?

**No, you don't have to.** But here's the practical reality:

| Scenario | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Your entire team is Chinese-speaking | Keep Chinese for maximum feature clarity |
| You're a non-Chinese speaker in a Chinese company | Switch to English — it's usable, but expect occasional untranslated UI elements |
| You're outside China using Feishu | Consider whether **Lark** (the international version) would be a better fit for you |

---

### How to Change Language in Feishu (Mac)

1. Open Feishu on Mac
2. Click your **profile avatar** (top left or bottom left)
3. Go to **Settings (设置)**
4. Find **Language / 语言**
5. Select **English**
6. Restart the app

---

### Bottom Line

The English version of Feishu on Mac is **functional but not polished**. For basic messaging, video calls, docs, and calendar — English works fine. But since Feishu is a China-domestic product, you may encounter areas of the app (especially admin/advanced settings) that are still in Chinese or have awkward translations. If your organization is based outside China and you're not required to use Feishu specifically, **Lark** would give you a far better English-language experience as it was built for international users from the ground up.
