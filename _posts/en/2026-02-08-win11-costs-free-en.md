Windows 11 is **not free** for most users. Here's how it typically works:

**Paid options:**
- If you're buying a new PC, Windows 11 usually comes pre-installed, and the cost is included in the computer's price
- If you're upgrading from Windows 10 on an existing PC, you'd need to purchase a Windows 11 license, which costs around $120-140 USD

**Free upgrade:**
- Microsoft offered a free upgrade from Windows 10 to Windows 11 for a limited time after launch (2021-2022), but that period has ended
- If you already have a valid Windows 10 license, you might still be able to upgrade for free, but this isn't guaranteed and depends on various factors

**Other free scenarios:**
- Some organizations or educational institutions get free licenses through volume licensing agreements
- Users with certain accessibility programs might qualify for free copies

So in short, Windows 11 generally requires a paid license unless you already have Windows 10 or fall into one of the special categories above.
