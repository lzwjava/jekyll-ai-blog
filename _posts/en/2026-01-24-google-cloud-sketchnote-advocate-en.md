Question: Who is Priyanka Vergadia, the Google Cloud developer advocate?

Answer:

**Priyanka Vergadia** is a prominent figure in the cloud computing and developer relations (DevRel) space. She is best known for her work as a **Staff Developer Advocate** at **Google Cloud**, where she focused on helping developers and enterprises understand, adopt, and build on Google Cloud Platform (GCP) services.

She is widely recognized by her nickname **"The Cloud Girl"** and for her unique approach of using **hand-drawn sketchnotes**, illustrations, and visual storytelling to explain complex cloud concepts in an accessible and engaging way.

- **Visual Learning Resources** — Created the popular **GCPSketchnote** series on GitHub — quick visual summaries of Google Cloud products, architecture decisions, certifications, and best practices. - **Background** — Originally from India, she moved to the United States and built her career in tech, eventually landing at Google. She holds a **Google Cloud Professional Cloud Architect** certification and has experience in QA engineering, developer advocacy, and technical evangelism.
