Question: When is NeurIPS held and where does it typically take place?

Answer:

**NeurIPS 2026 (next one):**

- **Dates:** Dec 6–12, 2026
- **Main venue:** Sydney, Australia (ICC Sydney)
- **Official satellites:** Atlanta, USA (Dec 8–13) and Paris, France (Dec 9–13) — same accepted papers, live-streamed keynotes, local orals/posters/workshops
- Schedule: Expo Talks Sun Dec 6 → tutorials Mon Dec 7 → main sessions Tue–Thu Dec 8–10 → workshops Fri–Sat Dec 11–12, with a Virtual Pass running the full week

**Typical pattern (last ~5 years):**

- Always **early-to-mid December**, ~1 week long
- Historically North America (New Orleans, Vancouver, San Diego) but has been expanding globally — 2026 is the first time the main event is outside NA/EU (following the success of the NeurIPS and NeurIPS-Mexico City pilots in 2025, they added Sydney + dual satellites this year)
- Submission cycle: abstracts early May, full papers ~1 week later, rebuttals in Aug, rebuttals can be posted until August 3rd AOE, decisions late Sep, workshops in the final two days

If you're targeting a submission for **NeurIPS 2027**, expect the same ~May abstract / early-May paper deadline pattern to repeat — worth checking neurips.cc/Conferences/2027 once it's live (not yet posted as of Aug 2026).
