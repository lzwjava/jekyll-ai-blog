Question: What's [https://www.givingpledge.org](https://www.givingpledge.org)?

Answer:

The Giving Pledge is a **philanthropy movement started by Bill Gates, Melinda French Gates, and Warren Buffett in 2010**. Its goal is to encourage the world's richest people to publicly promise that they will give away **the majority of their wealth** to charitable causes during their lifetime or in their wills. ([The Giving Pledge][1])

The core idea:

```
Founder becomes extremely wealthy
        |
        v
Publicly commits:
"Most of my wealth will go back to society"
        |
        v
Donate through foundations / charities / projects
```

It is **not a legal contract**. It is a public commitment and social movement designed to change billionaire culture around wealth. ([The Giving Pledge][1])

## Famous people who joined

Examples include:

* Bill Gates
* Warren Buffett
* Mark Zuckerberg
* Elon Musk
* Sam Altman ([AP News][2])

The official site says more than 250 wealthy philanthropists from over 30 countries have joined. ([The Giving Pledge][3])

## Why did Gates and Buffett create it?

Their thinking:

1. Extreme wealth creates extreme responsibility.
2. A billionaire does not need billions personally.
3. Large fortunes can solve large-scale problems:

   * climate
   * disease
   * education
   * poverty
   * AI safety/research

Buffett famously believes that his children do not need a huge inheritance; society can benefit more from that capital.

## Startup founder perspective

For entrepreneurs, there is a related idea:

> "Build something valuable first, then use the wealth created to solve problems."

This is different from traditional charity.

Example:

```
Startup success
      |
      v
$10B founder wealth
      |
      +--> $1B invested into AI research
      |
      +--> $1B into healthcare
      |
      +--> $1B into education
```

This creates a second "impact startup" after the first company.

## Criticism

The main criticisms:

* It is voluntary, not legally enforceable.
* Some argue billionaires should pay more taxes instead of deciding privately where money goes.
* Some pledges may happen over decades, so measuring impact is difficult. ([Vox][4])

## Why it matters in Silicon Valley culture

The Giving Pledge represents a particular Silicon Valley belief:

> "Technology creates huge wealth, and founders should eventually use that wealth to improve humanity."

You can see a similar philosophy in:

* Open Philanthropy
* effective altruism
* AI safety funding
* biotech research funding

For someone building AI companies, the interesting question is not only:

> "How do I make a billion-dollar company?"

but also:

> "If I create a $10B company, what should that capital do afterward?"

That is the mindset behind the Giving Pledge. ([The Giving Pledge][5])
