**Question:** For the Aigo (爱国者) Yueguang Baohe Jing Mini PC case (299 CNY), is the tempered glass side panel opened by using a finger inserted into a hole/gap, or is there a different mechanism?

**Answer:**

Based on multiple teardown reviews of the Yueguang Baohe Jing (月光宝盒) series (Mini, T20, 镜, X variants share the same panel design language), the side glass panel is **not** opened by poking a finger through a hole in the glass itself. The actual mechanism is a tool-free clip/latch (卡扣) system:

- The white side panel uses a snap-fit installation — you just pull outward and the glass panel opens. There's no drilled finger-hole in the tempered glass — that would be a structural weak point and defeat the point of showing off hardware.
- Rather than the traditional screw-mount, the side panels use a snap-fit quick-release design, so you don't need to unscrew anything during regular disassembly, which also avoids the risk of the glass panel accidentally falling and cracking.
- The **front panel** (not the side glass) is the one that sometimes has a genuine finger-access gap. In one variant: to remove the front panel, you reach your hand through an opening at the bottom, grip that point, and pull the front panel outward with force — this disassembly method leaves the bottom opening without a dust filter, which risks pulling dust in through the front fan.
- One user account of that same bottom-gap-pull mechanic on a related model wasn't great UX: customer service told them to grip the bottom of the front panel and just yank it hard — doing so snapped one of the internal clips, so it's a bit fragile if you're not careful.

**So, to directly answer your question:**
- **Side glass panels**: pull-out snap latch, no finger-hole needed — grip the edge (usually near a corner or edge seam) and pull outward. The latch fit against the case is precise, so it should release cleanly without excess force.
- **Front plastic panel** (if your Mini variant has a similar layout): yes, there's typically a bottom-edge gap intentionally left for finger access — insert your finger(s), hook the internal lip, and pull outward. Just don't yank aggressively; work it evenly from both sides of the bottom edge to avoid snapping a clip.

Since panel design varies slightly between the "镜" (Mirror), "T20", and "Mini" SKUs, I'd recommend checking your specific box/manual or the JD/Taobao product images for your exact 299 CNY Mini variant to confirm hole placement — but the general Aigo design philosophy across this whole line is snap-fit, tool-free panels rather than screw-mounted glass.
