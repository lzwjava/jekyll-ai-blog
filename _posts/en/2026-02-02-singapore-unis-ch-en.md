## **Important Scholarship Options for Chinese Students:**

### **Government & Institutional Scholarships:**

1. **Singapore International Graduate Award (SINGA)**
   - Open to all nationals except Singapore, for STEM courses
   - Fully funded, covering tuition fees plus monthly stipend
   - Deadline: June 1, 2025
   - Available at NUS, NTU, SMU, SUTD

2. **Tan Kah Kee Postgraduate Scholarship**
   - Open to citizens of Singapore or Chinese nationals
   - One-off sum of SGD 12,000 for studying, no bond required
   - Can reapply annually

3. **NUS Graduate Research Scholarship**
   - Monthly stipend of SGD 3,500 for PhD and SGD 3,000 for master's candidates
   - Full tuition subsidy
