**Question:** What is Avnet, and what is its relationship with Kingston memory chips?

**Answer:**

**Avnet** and **Kingston** are two separate companies with a distributor-manufacturer relationship:

**Avnet** is a global electronic components distributor, supporting more than 1 million customers at every stage of the product lifecycle.

**Kingston Technology** is one of the world's largest independent manufacturers of memory products, serving an international network of distributors, resellers, retailers, and OEM customers on six continents.

**Their relationship:** Avnet Computing Components signed a distribution agreement with Kingston Technology to distribute Kingston's memory products throughout Europe, Asia Pacific, and the Americas. Under the agreement, Avnet markets and distributes Kingston ValueRAM and flash memory products, focusing on customer segments including value-added resellers, system builders, and PC assemblers.

In short, **Avnet is not a memory chip brand itself** — it is an authorized distributor that sells Kingston's memory products (RAM, flash storage, eMMC, industrial SSDs, etc.) to businesses and developers worldwide.
