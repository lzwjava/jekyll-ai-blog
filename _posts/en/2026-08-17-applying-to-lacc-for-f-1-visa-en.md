## Question: How do I apply to Los Angeles City College?

Los Angeles City College (LACC) is a public community college in Los Angeles. If you're applying as an **international student and want an F-1 visa**, there is a separate international-student process.

### If you want an F-1 student visa

The basic flow is:

```text
1. Apply to LACC online
        ↓
2. Submit international-student documents
        ↓
3. LACC reviews your application
        ↓
4. LACC issues I-20
        ↓
5. Pay SEVIS fee
        ↓
6. Apply for F-1 visa
        ↓
7. Enter the U.S.
        ↓
8. Complete LACC orientation + registration
```

LACC's published international-student requirements include:

* Online LACC application through **CCCApply**
* F-1 Student Supplemental Application
* Proof of financial support
* Affidavit of Support, if someone sponsors you
* Proof of English proficiency
* High-school diploma/transcript or equivalent
* Passport
* Other immigration documents depending on whether you're applying from abroad or transferring from another U.S. school ([Los Angeles City College][1])

LACC currently estimates **about $23,000 USD/year** for the financial proof requirement in its international-student materials. ([Los Angeles City College][1])

### Step 1 — Create your application

Start with LACC's online application through CCCApply:

[LACC International Student Application information](https://www.lacitycollege.edu/admissions/international-students?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

You will create an account and select **Los Angeles City College**.

### Step 2 — Prepare your documents

If you're **outside the U.S. and applying for a new F-1**, prepare roughly:

```text
Passport
High-school diploma / equivalent
Academic transcripts
English proficiency evidence
Bank statement / bank letter
Affidavit of Support (if applicable)
F-1 Supplemental Application
```

The bank documentation needs to demonstrate sufficient funds for the program. LACC's materials say financial documents should be current and dated within six months of the term start date. ([Los Angeles City College][1])

### Step 3 — Get the I-20

Once LACC accepts your international application and verifies your financial/document requirements, the school can issue your **Form I-20**. LACC is authorized to issue I-20s to eligible F-1 students. ([Los Angeles City College][1])

Then:

```text
I-20
 ↓
SEVIS I-901 fee
 ↓
DS-160
 ↓
F-1 visa interview
 ↓
F-1 visa
```

After obtaining the F-1 visa, you can travel to the U.S. according to the dates on your I-20. LACC says new students should report to the college/DSO after arrival and bring their passport, F-1 visa, I-94 and I-20. ([Los Angeles City College][2])

### Important distinction for you

If your goal is **not** to get an F-1 visa — for example, you're already in Los Angeles with an immigration status that permits study — the process can be much simpler. You generally apply as a regular LACC student rather than going through the full international F-1 admission process.

Also, LACC's F-1 students are required to maintain **12 units in Fall and Spring** and maintain at least a **2.0 GPA** under the school's F-1 agreement. ([Los Angeles City College][3])
