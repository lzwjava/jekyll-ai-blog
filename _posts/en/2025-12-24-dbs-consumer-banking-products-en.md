Question: What are the major products in the Consumer Banking Group (CBG) department at DBS Bank?

Answer:

The **Consumer Banking Group (CBG)** at DBS Bank, also commonly referred to as the Consumer Banking/Wealth Management segment, focuses on retail and individual customers. It provides a wide range of personal financial products and services designed for everyday banking, wealth accumulation, protection, and borrowing needs.

### Major products include

- **Deposits and accounts**: Current accounts, savings accounts, and fixed deposits.
- **Loans and financing**: Personal loans, home loans/mortgages, consumer finance, and other credit facilities.
- **Cards**: Credit cards, debit cards, and related payment solutions.
- **Payments and remittances**: Digital payments, fund transfers, and international remittances.
- **Investments**: Mutual funds, robo-advisory portfolios (e.g., digiPortfolio), structured products, and other investment options.
- **Insurance (bancassurance)**: Life insurance, general insurance, and protection products distributed through the bank.
- **Wealth management services**: Tailored solutions for affluent clients, including DBS Treasures and DBS Private Bank offerings, such as portfolio management and ESG-focused investments.
- **Digital banking platforms**: Mobile and online banking apps (e.g., DBS digibank, iWealth) for seamless access to all the above.

These products are supported by a strong emphasis on digital innovation, making DBS a leader in consumer digital banking in Asia. The group serves customers across key markets like Singapore, Hong Kong, China, Taiwan, India, and Indonesia.
