Question: How do I get a UK work visa?

Answer: For someone in your position (founder/engineer, not employed by a UK company), the main route is the **Skilled Worker visa**, and it requires a UK employer to sponsor you — you can't self-sponsor easily unless you go the founder/investor route instead.

**Skilled Worker visa (most common path)**

- Need a job offer from a UK employer holding a **Home Office Sponsor Licence**
- Role must be **RQF Level 6+** (graduate-level) — software engineer roles qualify
- Salary: at least **£41,700/yr** or the "going rate" for the role, whichever is higher as of 2026
- English: **B2 level** or exempt if you're from a majority English-speaking country
- Employer issues you a **Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS)**, you apply online within 3 months of receiving it and must submit your application using the CoS within 3 months of it being issued
- Need £1,270 in your bank account to show you can support yourself
- Points system: 70 points needed — 50 mandatory (sponsorship, skill level, English) plus 20 tradeable, usually from salary, a relevant PhD, or being on the Immigration Salary List
- Grants up to 5 years per CoS, with unlimited extensions if you keep meeting requirements, leading to Indefinite Leave to Remain (settlement) eventually

**Given your profile (founder, running your own AI company)**, Skilled Worker visa doesn't really fit unless you take a job at a UK-sponsoring company. More relevant alternatives:

- **Innovator Founder visa** — for starting/running a business in the UK, no salary threshold, but needs an endorsing body to approve your business plan
- **Global Talent visa** — for people recognized as leaders/potential leaders in digital technology; no job offer needed, self-sponsored, endorsed by Tech Nation's successor body (Innovator/Global Talent routes are worth checking given your GPT-2 training / agent dev background — this is the route people with your kind of technical portfolio usually pursue instead of Skilled Worker)

Practical steps if going Skilled Worker:

1. Find UK employer on the [register of licensed sponsors](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/register-of-licensed-sponsors-workers)
2. Get CoS + job offer meeting salary threshold
3. Apply online, pay visa fee + Immigration Health Surcharge
4. Provide biometrics
5. Get eVisa (UK moved fully digital by end of 2026 per full adoption of the eVisa system by 31 December 2026)
