### Very Short History Timeline

Year        | Major Event
------------|---------------------------------------------------------------
**1939**    | Founded in a **garage** in Palo Alto (the official birthplace of Silicon Valley)
**1957**    | HP becomes a **public** company
**1968**    | World’s first **scientific desktop calculator** – HP 9100A
**1984**    | World’s first mass-market **inkjet** & **laser** printer (HP LaserJet – changed office printing forever)
**1999**    | HP splits — **Agilent** (test & measurement) is separated
**2002**    | Very controversial merger with **Compaq** (huge PC business)
**2015**    | **The Big Split** → HP becomes two companies:
• **HP Inc.** → PCs + Printers (what most people think of as "HP")
• **HPE** → Hewlett Packard Enterprise (servers, storage, networking, cloud, supercomputers)
**2019–2025** | Very strong focus on **PC + printing recovery**, hybrid work, subscription printing, gaming (OMEN / HyperX), sustainability

### Interesting Modern Facts / Trends (2024–2025)

- HP is one of the **very few** PC companies that **still designs & manufactures its own silicon** (mostly for printers)
- Very big push on **"AI PCs"** — many new models have powerful NPUs (similar to Intel, AMD & Qualcomm competition)
- Very aggressive **subscription printing** strategy → **HP+** / Instant Ink
- Very strong in **education & government** sectors (lots of Chromebooks + cheap reliable laptops)
- One of world’s **largest** purchasers & recyclers of **recycled plastic** for making new computers
- Very controversial long history of printer **DRM** / ink subscription / chip locking wars
