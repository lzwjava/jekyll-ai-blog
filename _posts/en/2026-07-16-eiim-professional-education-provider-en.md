Question: What's European Institute of Innovation and Management (EIIM)?

Answer:

European Institute of Innovation and Management (EIIM) is a private education/training institution in Spain focused on **business innovation, management, entrepreneurship, and executive education**. It is **not a major public university** like Universidad Politécnica de Madrid or IE University. ([EIIM][1])

Their offerings include:

* **MBA / Executive MBA / business innovation programs**
* **Master programs**
* **DBA (Doctor of Business Administration)**
* Language center
* Custom corporate training programs
* Research centers around innovation and entrepreneurship ([EIIM][1])

The website lists its address as:

> Calle Granvia 40, 9-1, Madrid, Spain 28013 ([EIIM][1])

## Is it a "real university"?

Be careful with terminology.

A real Spanish university normally has:

* recognition by Spanish education authorities
* official degree programs ("títulos oficiales")
* ability to award regulated university degrees

EIIM's website emphasizes programs like MBA, Executive MBA, DBA, and custom programs, but I would verify **whether a specific degree is officially recognized** before using it for immigration, PhD admission, or government employment. ([EIIM][1])
