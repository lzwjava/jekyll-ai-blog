## Robin Ren — A Comprehensive Introduction

### Early Life and Education

Robin Ren was born and raised in Shanghai, China. Before coming to the United States for higher education, he demonstrated exceptional academic talent by winning a **Physics Olympiad** in China — a feat that Elon Musk himself acknowledged, reportedly saying "He was the only person better than me at physics."

He holds a Bachelor of Science and Bachelor of Arts from the **University of Pennsylvania**, where he became a college friend with Elon Musk, and a Master of Science degree in Electrical Engineering from **Stanford University**.

### Friendship with Elon Musk

The two men forged a close bond during their time at Penn. They became partners in the physics lab, where they studied the properties of various materials. In Musk's own words from the Third Row Tesla Podcast: "I graduated from Penn — basically physics and economics. And then we did a road trip to Stanford with Robin Ren, who was in my physics class and now works at Tesla, actually. He grew up in Shanghai. He's a very smart guy."

This friendship — spanning decades from their university days — would later prove enormously consequential for Tesla's global expansion.

### Career Before Tesla

Prior to his Tesla career, Ren managed technical R&D businesses at leading global IT service providers including **EMC**, **VMware**, and **XtremIO**, and also worked at **Yahoo**. His background blended deep technical engineering expertise with business development skills.

### Career at Tesla (2015–2020)

#### Joining and Roles

Ren joined Tesla in June 2015 as **Vice President for the Asia-Pacific region**. In 2018, he became **Vice President of Worldwide Sales**, before assuming the role of **Vice President of Business Development** in May 2019.

#### The China Mission — Tesla's Most Consequential Deal

Robin Ren's most significant achievement at Tesla was architecting the company's entry into China — a move considered pivotal to Tesla's long-term financial success. He was born in Shanghai and was eventually put in charge of Tesla's entire China operation.

His challenge was immense: he could either wear down Musk's resistance and have Tesla form a joint venture (as every other foreign car company had done), or convince China's top leaders to change a law that had defined Chinese manufacturing growth for three decades. He discovered that the latter was easier. Month after month, he lobbied the Chinese government — "We kept explaining why it would help China to have Tesla build an auto factory, even if it wasn't a joint venture," Ren said.

The negotiation culminated in a legendary exchange with Musk. Ren started to go through a slide deck with maps, financing commitments and deal terms, but Musk did not look at them. Instead, he stared out of the window of his plane for almost a full minute. Then he looked Ren directly in the eye. "Do you believe this is the right thing?" Ren was so taken aback that he paused for a few seconds before saying yes.

#### Gigafactory Shanghai

Ren significantly contributed to securing the deal for Tesla to build its **Gigafactory 3 in Shanghai**, which made Tesla the first foreign automaker to wholly own a car manufacturing factory in China.

Along with Musk, he also led Tesla's negotiations for loans from state-owned Chinese banks at better than market rates for the Shanghai Gigafactory. The plant was completed in record time, speeding through approvals and construction, and kicked off production in China in January 2020.

Musk publicly praised him: "Robin is doing a great job running worldwide sales, lots of work done. He was born and raised in Shanghai and has been, along with Tom and Grace and others from the team in China, sort of instrumental in establishing the China factory and making sure that gets done right and having a great relationship with the government."

#### Departure from Tesla

Robin Ren left Tesla in June 2020, having worked there for five years. By helping Tesla secure the critically important deal in China, he left an important legacy for the company.

### Post-Tesla: Founding Carbonology

After leaving Tesla, Ren channeled his entrepreneurial energy into climate technology. He founded **Carbonology**, a green technology startup developing the world's first industrial project to produce **sustainable aviation fuel using direct air carbon capture (DAC) technology**. The firm aims to produce its first barrel of fuel, followed by a pilot plant with annual capacity of around 1,000 tons, and a 100,000-ton commercial-scale project by 2027.

Carbonology has completed an angel financing round, with investments from **HongShan Capital Group** (formerly Sequoia Capital China) and **Yellow River Delta Industry Investment Fund Management**.

Its first R&D center is being established in the **Lingang Special Area** of Shanghai's Free Trade Zone, which focuses on high-end manufacturing and new energy.
