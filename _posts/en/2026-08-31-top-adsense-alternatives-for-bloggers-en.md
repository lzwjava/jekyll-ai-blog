**Question:** Besides Google AdSense, what other products can help monetize a blog by showing ads and earning revenue?

**Answer:**

Here are the top alternatives to Google AdSense for blog monetization in 2026:

---

### 🥇 Top Picks

**1. Ezoic**
For bloggers with 10K–100K pageviews, Ezoic is often the first serious step beyond basic AdSense, with strong potential to increase RPM and overall display ad revenue. It offers AI-powered ad placement optimization and A/B testing.

**2. Media.net**
Media.net is one of the best AdSense alternatives for bloggers with strong English-speaking audiences, especially from the US, UK, and Canada. Powered by the Yahoo–Bing network, it focuses on contextual targeting, showing ads closely related to the content on your page.

**3. Monumetric**
Monumetric's client-side header bidding wrapper and AdOps support help publishers make 25% more ad revenue compared to AdSense. It requires a minimum of 10,000 monthly pageviews to join.

**4. Taboola**
Taboola specializes in native advertising and helps grow ad revenue through programmatic algorithms and AI-driven personalization. It pays better than AdSense, and you can combine these two platforms to broaden your monetization strategy.

**5. Infolinks**
Infolinks focuses on in-text ads that seamlessly integrate with long blog posts, alongside other formats like in-fold, in-tag, in-frame, and traditional graphic banners. It uses a Cost Per Click (CPC) model designed to maximize earnings for publishers.

**6. BuySellAds**
BuySellAds is an advertising marketplace that helps websites, newsletters, and podcasts monetize their audience with contextual ad placements. It offers banner ads, native ads, sponsored content, and email newsletter placements.

**7. Adsterra / PropellerAds**
PropellerAds, Adsterra, and ylliX are known for fast approval, making them suitable for new or small blogs, including Blogspot sites, with no strict traffic thresholds.
