**Question:** As a company founder in Guangzhou, how should I handle social insurance (社保) and housing fund (公积金)?

---

**Answer:**

Here's a practical breakdown covering registration, rates, and your options as a founder.

---

## 1. What's Required — "五险一金"

China mandates **Five Insurances + One Housing Fund** for all employees, including founders who draw a salary:

| Type | Employer Rate | Employee Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Pension (养老) | 15% | 8% |
| Medical (医疗) | 6%* | 2% |
| Unemployment (失业) | 0.5–1% | 0.5% |
| Work Injury (工伤) | 0.2–1.9% | 0% |
| Maternity (生育) | ~0.5% | 0% |
| Housing Fund (公积金) | 5–12% | 5–12% |

As of January 1, 2026, the employer medical insurance rate in Guangzhou was restored from 4.5% back to 6%, following the expiration of the temporary reduction policy.

---

## 2. Contribution Base (Guangzhou 2026)

The 2026 medical insurance contribution base has an upper limit of **¥31,170/month** and a lower limit of **¥6,234/month**.

For pension insurance, the province-wide upper limit is **¥27,501/month**, with Guangzhou's lower limit at **¥5,500/month**.

The Housing Fund contribution base lower limit is **¥2,500/month** (effective from March 1, 2025), with rates between 5%–12% for both employer and employee.

---

## 3. Registration Steps

**Social Insurance Registration:**
To hire staff, your company needs a social insurance/social credit number. You'll need to bring: business license, ID of the legal representative, RMB bank account documents, then enter employee details into the Social Insurance Bureau's system.

**Practical steps:**
1. Register at the **Guangzhou Social Insurance Bureau** (广州市社会保险基金管理中心) — can be done online via 广东政务服务网
2. Register for Housing Fund at the **Guangzhou Housing Provident Fund Management Center** (广州住房公积金管理中心, website: `gzhfgj.gz.gov.cn`)
3. Enroll each employee within **30 days of hiring**

According to the *Housing Provident Fund Management Regulations*, newly established companies must register with the Housing Fund Management Center within **30 days** of establishment, and open accounts for employees within **20 days** of registration. Companies must also contribute on time and in full.

---

## 4. Your Situation as a Founder

| Scenario | What to Do |
|---|---|
| **You take a salary from the company** | Must enroll in 五险一金 just like any employee |
| **You take no salary (zero payroll)** | Technically no obligation as an "employee," but you still need to enroll others you hire |
| **You want personal social insurance coverage** | Can enroll as **灵活就业人员** (self-employed/flexible worker) for pension + medical only |

For flexible/self-employed individuals in Guangzhou, social insurance mainly covers pension and medical insurance. For pension, the minimum monthly contribution is around ¥1,056.8 (base of ¥5,284 × 20%), and you can choose from 9 contribution tiers (60%–300% of the average wage).

---

## 5. Practical Tips

- **Online tools:** Use WeChat mini-programs like **粤省事** or **广州社保** official accounts to manage contributions and check records.
- **Payroll agent:** Many early-stage companies outsource payroll/social insurance handling to a **人力资源代理公司** (HR agency) — costs around ¥50–200/person/month, saves admin headache.
- **Don't skip it:** Failing to enroll employees in social insurance or housing fund is explicitly illegal under Chinese law, and enforcement has tightened significantly in Guangzhou as of 2026.
- **Timing:** Social insurance is collected by the **Tax Bureau (税务局)** — payments are typically due before the 15th of each month.
