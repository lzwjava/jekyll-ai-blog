## Question: How do I apply to De Anza College?

If you're applying as an **international student from outside the U.S.**, the process is different from the normal California community-college application. You should use De Anza's **International Student Application** if you want an F-1 visa. ([De Anza College - Tops in Transfer][1])

### 1. Choose your application route

**If you want to study in the U.S. on F-1:**
Use De Anza's International Student application:

[De Anza International Student — Apply for Admission](https://daweb2.deanza.edu/international/future-students/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**If you're already in the U.S. and don't need an F-1:**
You generally use California's **OpenCCC** application system. De Anza says first-time students apply through OpenCCC. ([De Anza College - Tops in Transfer][2])

[De Anza — Apply for Admission](https://daweb2.deanza.edu/apply-and-register/application-tips.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

### 2. For F-1 international admission, prepare these

De Anza currently requires:

* **Online application**
* **English proficiency**

  * IELTS: **5.5+**
  * TOEFL iBT: **61+** for exams before Jan. 21, 2026; the requirement changed for exams on/after that date
  * Duolingo: **95+**
  * Other accepted tests are listed by De Anza
* **Official school transcript**, translated into English if necessary
* **Financial statement/bank letter:** at least **$26,907 USD** available for the first year
* **$75 non-refundable application fee**
* Passport information / other documents as requested ([De Anza College - Tops in Transfer][1])

Your **IELTS 6.5** should satisfy De Anza's stated IELTS minimum of 5.5.

---

### 3. Pay attention to the deadlines

De Anza admits new international students for **Fall, Winter, Spring and Summer**.

| Intake | F-1 application deadline |
| ------ | -----------------------: |
| Fall   |                  Aug. 27 |
| Winter |                  Nov. 15 |
| Spring |                  Feb. 15 |
| Summer |                  Apr. 15 |

The application opens much earlier, so De Anza recommends applying early because you need time for admission, I-20 issuance and the visa process. ([De Anza College - Tops in Transfer][1])

Since today is **August 17, 2026**, the **Fall 2026 deadline is August 27**, but that is extremely tight if you're starting from outside the U.S. **Winter 2027 or Spring 2027 is much more realistic.**

---

### 4. After you submit

The flow is basically:

```text
International Student Application
          ↓
Upload IELTS + transcript + financial documents
          ↓
Pay $75 application fee
          ↓
De Anza reviews application
          ↓
Admission
          ↓
I-20
          ↓
SEVIS fee + F-1 visa application
          ↓
F-1 visa interview
          ↓
Enter U.S.
          ↓
Orientation
          ↓
Register for classes
```

De Anza uses the quarter system, so once admitted you'll register through **MyPortal**. ([De Anza College - Tops in Transfer][3])

### One important distinction

If your goal is **"go to California community college first, then transfer to a UC/CSU or another university"**, De Anza is actually a very interesting choice. It has strong transfer programs, and you don't necessarily need to pursue an associate degree before transferring.
