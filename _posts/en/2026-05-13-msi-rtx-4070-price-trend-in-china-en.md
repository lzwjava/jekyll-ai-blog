Question: How has the price of the MSI Gaming GeForce RTX 4070 12GB in China changed from 2023 to now? Has it become more expensive or cheaper?

Answer:

The MSI Gaming GeForce RTX 4070 12GB (Gaming X Trio) launched around early 2023 in China with a price roughly between 4,800–5,200 CNY. Since then, the trend has generally been **downward** due to:

1. **New GPU releases** – NVIDIA has released higher-end 40-series cards like the RTX 4080 and 4090, and rumors of next-gen 50-series push demand down for 4070.
2. **Market stabilization** – 2023 saw high post-COVID GPU prices, but by late 2023 and 2024, the supply chain normalized.
3. **Cryptocurrency market decline** – Reduced GPU mining demand has lowered retail prices.
4. **Promotions and retailer competition** – Prices often drop during major sales like Double 11, 618, and New Year promotions.

As of early 2026, typical online prices for the RTX 4070 Gaming X Trio 12GB in China range around **4,000–4,500 CNY** for new cards, meaning it is slightly **cheaper than launch**. Used/refurbished units can go lower, around 3,500–3,800 CNY depending on condition.

So overall, it has **become slightly cheaper** since launch.
