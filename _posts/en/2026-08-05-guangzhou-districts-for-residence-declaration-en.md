Question: Which Guangzhou districts support the “residence declaration system” (住所自主承诺申报制) for company registration?

Answer:

In Guangzhou, this is **not only one district**. The city has gradually expanded the “住所（经营场所）自主承诺申报” system. The exact availability depends on the address type, industry, and whether the address is in the government’s recognized address database. ([Guangzhou Government][1])

Known districts that have implemented or promoted this policy include:

| District                                   | Support situation   | Notes                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Haizhu (海珠区)**                           | ✅ Yes               | One of the earliest districts to implement it. Many internet/software companies used this approach. ([Guangzhou Government][2])   |
| **Tianhe (天河区)**                           | ✅ Yes               | Strongly suitable for software/AI companies; Tianhe Software Park area has many technology companies. ([Guangzhou Government][1]) |
| **Huangpu (黄埔区)**                          | ✅ Yes               | Often used by technology startups and innovation companies. ([Guangzhou Government][1])                                           |
| **Nansha (南沙区)**                           | ✅ Yes               | Has its own market entity registration reform rules and address database system. ([Guangzhou Government][1])                      |
| **Other districts (越秀、荔湾、白云、番禺、增城、花都、从化)** | Generally available | Need to check the specific address and business scope. ([Guangzhou Government][1])                                                |

The key point: don't just ask "does the district support it?" — ask whether **your exact address is in the allowed address database**. The same district may allow one building but reject another. Guangzhou's rules use standard address libraries and negative lists to determine whether a commitment declaration can replace proof documents. ([Guangzhou Government][1])

A practical approach:

1. Find a Tianhe/Huangpu coworking or incubator address.
2. Ask: "这个地址可以做科技有限公司注册吗？支持住所自主申报吗？"
3. Confirm before paying rent.
