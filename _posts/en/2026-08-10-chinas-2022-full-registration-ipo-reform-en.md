Question: Introduction to China's 2022 full registration-based IPO system reform (全面注册制)

Answer:

**全面注册制（Full Registration-Based IPO System）** was a major reform of China's stock market IPO system. It changed the way companies are approved to go public, moving from an **approval-based system (核准制)** toward a more **market-driven registration system (注册制)**.

The reform was officially implemented on:

> **February 17, 2023** — China's securities regulator announced the full implementation of registration-based IPO reform.
> However, the transition started earlier, especially with pilot programs from 2018 onward.

---

## 1. Before: Approval system (核准制)

Historically, Chinese IPOs used an approval model:

```
Company wants IPO
        |
        v
CSRC reviews
        |
        v
Government regulator decides:
"Can this company IPO?"
        |
        v
IPO
```

The regulator focused heavily on:

* profitability requirements
* industry policy
* growth expectations
* financial quality

The IPO supply was controlled.

Effects:

### Advantage:

* Higher quality filtering
* Less speculative companies entering the market

### Problems:

* Slow IPO process
* Limited number of IPOs
* Companies had to wait years
* Market could not efficiently allocate capital

---

## 2. Registration system (注册制)

Under registration:

```
Company wants IPO
        |
        v
Exchange reviews documents
        |
        v
Check disclosure quality
        |
        v
IPO if requirements met
```

The key idea:

> Regulators do not decide whether the company is "valuable"; investors decide.

The focus changes from:

```
Old:
"Is this company good enough to IPO?"

New:
"Did the company fully disclose information so investors can judge?"
```

---

## 3. Timeline

### 2018 — Pilot starts

China launched registration-based IPO experiments:

* Shanghai Stock Exchange STAR Market (科创板)
* Designed to support technology companies

Examples:

* semiconductor companies
* AI companies
* biotech companies

---

### 2020 — ChiNext reform

Shenzhen Stock Exchange ChiNext adopted registration-based IPO rules.

---

### 2022 — Beijing Stock Exchange

Beijing Stock Exchange also used registration-based IPO mechanisms.

---

### 2023 — Full registration system

The reform expanded to:

* Shanghai Main Board
* Shenzhen Main Board

Meaning:

```
STAR Market
      +
ChiNext
      +
Beijing Stock Exchange
      +
Shanghai Main Board
      +
Shenzhen Main Board

= 全面注册制
```

## 4. Why did IPO "破发" increase?

Before registration reform:

IPO stocks were often scarce.

Example:

```
Company value: 10 billion RMB

IPO shares limited

Demand > supply

First day price often ↑
```

People called IPOs:

> "打新稳赚" (IPO subscription almost guaranteed profit)

Because:

* IPO price controlled
* Supply limited
* Investors competed for new shares

---

After registration reform:

More companies could IPO.

Example:

```
Before:

100 companies apply
10 approved


After:

100 companies apply
many can list
```

Supply increased.

At the same time:

* IPO valuations became higher
* investors became more selective
* market liquidity weakened

Result:

```
IPO price: 100 RMB

After listing:

90 RMB

↓

破发
```

## 5. Why your friend mentioned "22年全面注册制那会"

Your friend's point is likely:

> "Don't assume IPOs are guaranteed money. When the registration system came, many new stocks fell below IPO prices."

The old mentality:

```
New IPO
   ↓
Subscribe
   ↓
Sell on listing day
   ↓
Profit
```

became less reliable.

The new environment:

```
New IPO
   ↓
Analyze business
   ↓
Analyze valuation
   ↓
Decide investment
```

is closer to mature markets like the US.

---

## Comparison with US IPO market

US markets (NYSE/Nasdaq) have long used a registration/disclosure model:

```
Company files S-1
        |
SEC reviews disclosure
        |
Investors decide
```

The SEC does not approve whether the company is a good investment.

China's reform moves closer to this philosophy.
