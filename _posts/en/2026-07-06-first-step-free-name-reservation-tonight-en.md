**Three things that changed since the Zhihu article you read — these matter:**

1. **Registered capital is no longer "just a number."** Under the current rules you must actually pay in the subscribed capital within 5 years of incorporation; recommended amounts are 100K–500K CNY for startups, 500K–1M for tech companies. My earlier "100K–500K" advice now means *real money on a 5-year clock*. For a solo AI-tools company with a 900K mortgage, set it at **100K–200K CNY**, not 500K. You can increase capital later trivially; decreasing (减资) is painful.

2. **You may not need a supervisor at all.** A single-natural-person company (自然人独资) no longer requires a 监事; the supervisor/legal-rep separation rule only applies to multi-person companies under the 2024 Company Law. So the wife-as-监事 dependency I flagged earlier is likely moot if you incorporate as 100% sole shareholder. Confirm on the platform when filing — but this removes a blocker.

3. **The process is fully online and faster than the article claimed.** All materials are uploaded online — no paper, no service-hall visits; the 7-step flow takes 1–3 days total, with identity verification done via face recognition on the 登记注册身份验证 app or the 广州工商 WeChat mini-program, valid for 30 days. Seals are free (公章/财务章/发票章/法人章, carved and police-registered automatically via the platform) — so the agency's main remaining value is just the virtual address (government-registered cluster addresses run 1,200–3,000 CNY/year) plus bookkeeping.
