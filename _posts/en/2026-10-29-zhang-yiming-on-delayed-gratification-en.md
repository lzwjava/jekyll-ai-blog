**Question:** How does Zhang Yiming (张一鸣) explain delayed gratification (延迟满足感)?

**Answer:**

Zhang Yiming, founder of ByteDance (TikTok's parent company), considers **延迟满足感** (delayed gratification) one of the most important principles in life and work. Here's how he explains and applies it:

---

### 🔑 His Core Definition

Zhang Yiming views delayed gratification as giving up immediate satisfaction in exchange for a more valuable long-term outcome — and the self-control displayed while waiting.

In a 2016 interview with *Caijing* magazine, the term appeared **10 times** in the resulting article. One of his most-quoted lines from that interview: "Half of many people's life problems are caused by this — lacking delayed gratification."

---

### 🍬 The Stanford Marshmallow Experiment

Zhang has cited the famous Stanford marshmallow experiment as a key influence, along with books like *The Road Less Traveled*. He believes that postponing immediate pleasure for higher-value outcomes leads to greater long-term success.

He saw himself as the child who waited for two marshmallows — at critical moments of temptation, he chose restraint. Delay a little, then delay a little more.

---

### 💼 Applied to Career Choices

He advises not to be swayed by short-term choices like salary differences of a few thousand yuan right after graduation. Around 2006, he recommended that younger students go to Baidu rather than IBM or Microsoft — even though the foreign firms offered more prestige and slightly higher pay at the time. A pay gap of a few thousand yuan when starting out is truly negligible.

He also cautions: if you set your goal right after graduation as buying a small apartment within the 5th ring road of Beijing and spend all your energy on that, your work will suffer greatly.

---

### 🤖 Applied to ByteDance's AI Strategy (2026)

The phrase has been a theme for Zhang for ten years. In August 2026, the rarely-speaking Zhang Yiming made an unusually public internal statement, emphasizing that in building AI models, the team must commit to long-termism and delayed gratification — "rather than using others' output to chase short-term leaderboard rankings."

Zhang also added that ByteDance "should be willing to sacrifice some short-term gains for long-term goals," and drew a red line against using distillation from third-party models to shortcut AI development.

---

### 🚫 His Harshest Warning

Zhang Yiming has gone so far as to say: "People whose level of delayed gratification is not on the same scale simply cannot communicate effectively with each other."
