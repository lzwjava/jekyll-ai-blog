**Question:** How much does Georgia Tech's Online Master of Science in Computer Science (OMSCS) cost?

---

**Answer:**

The OMSCS is famously one of the **most affordable accredited master's degrees in CS** in the US.

### 💰 Current Tuition (Fall 2026 / Spring 2027)

Tuition is **$227 per semester credit hour**, plus a mandatory Online Learning Fee of **$212/semester** (for fewer than 4 credit hours) or **$531/semester** (for 4+ credit hours).

---

### 📊 Total Degree Cost Estimate

The degree requires **30 credit hours** (10 courses × 3 credits each):

| Scenario | Tuition | Fees (est.) | **Total** |
|----------|---------|------------|-----------|
| 1 course/semester (10 semesters) | $6,810 | ~$2,120 | **~$8,930** |
| 2 courses/semester (5 semesters) | $6,810 | ~$2,655 | **~$9,465** |

The total program cost is under **$7,000 in pure tuition** for the complete degree — compared to $45,000+ for the equivalent on-campus program. When fees are factored in, the real all-in cost shifts to closer to **$11,000–$12,000** over the full program duration.

---

### 🔑 Key Points

- The total program cost is approximately **$8,950** (tuition + technology fees), making it the **#1 ranked cheapest accredited online CS master's** in the US.
- No difference in cost for in-state vs. out-of-state or international students — everyone pays the same rate.
- All courses are delivered asynchronously, so you study on your own schedule.
- Most students take 2–3 years to complete the program working part-time.
