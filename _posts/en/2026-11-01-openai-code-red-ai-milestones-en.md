## 🔴 OpenAI Internally

**"Code Red" & Product Pivot**

In early December 2025, CEO Sam Altman declared a "code red" and ordered the company to refocus on immediate improvements to its chatbot, telling employees in a company-wide memo that OpenAI will focus on enhancing the day-to-day experience of ChatGPT.

The memo shelved work on advertising, AI shopping agents, and a personal assistant called Pulse. The Code Red was issued in response to Google's Gemini 3, which outperformed OpenAI on key benchmarks and sent Alphabet's stock soaring.

**Senior Staff Exodus**

Several senior staff departed OpenAI after the company shifted its focus from long-term research to rapidly improving ChatGPT. Projects linked to video and image generation, including Sora and DALL-E, were described as under-resourced, while other non-language model initiatives were reportedly wound down.

One departing economist, in an internal farewell message, cited a "growing tension between conducting rigorous analysis and serving as an informal promotional entity for OpenAI."

OpenAI also plans to nearly double its workforce to 8,000 employees by end of 2026, and one of its internal general-purpose reasoning models autonomously disproved a central geometry conjecture that Paul Erdős posed in 1946 — a result Fields medalist Tim Gowers called "a milestone in AI mathematics."
