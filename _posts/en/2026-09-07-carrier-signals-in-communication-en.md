**Question:** What are the carriers used to convey information in a communication system?

**Answer:**

In a communication system, a **carrier** is the medium or signal that transports information from a source to a destination. Here's a breakdown:

**What is a Carrier?**

A carrier signal is the medium used to transport data, while the data signal contains the actual information being sent. The data signal is modulated onto the carrier, which acts as a vehicle to transmit the information.

**Types of Carriers**

Carrier waves such as acoustic waves and electromagnetic waves — including radio, optical, and infrared waves — travel through space without wires or cables. Signals are man-made variations in amplitude, frequency, phase, polarization, or other physical properties of these carrier waves.

More specifically:

1. **Radio Waves** — Used in AM/FM broadcasting, cellular networks, and Wi-Fi. In radio broadcast, information propagates through the atmosphere as the channel.

2. **Optical Light (Fiber Optic)** — Optical fiber communication systems use carrier frequencies in the near-infrared region of the electromagnetic spectrum, typically around 193 THz at the 1550-nm wavelength range. Compared to radio-frequency carriers, optical carriers offer a massive bandwidth advantage.

3. **Electrical Signals (Copper Wire)** — In telephone networks, information is transmitted through electrical lines.

**How Information is Put onto a Carrier (Modulation)**

There are several types of modulation: Amplitude Modulation (AM), widely used in radio broadcasting where the amplitude of a carrier wave is varied to encode sound; Frequency Modulation (FM), which varies the frequency and provides higher sound quality and noise resistance; and Phase Modulation (PM), where the phase of the carrier signal is modified, integral to digital technologies like Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

**Why Carriers Are Necessary**

By providing a structured platform, carriers ensure that information reaches its destination without distortion or loss, making them integral to modern communication systems. This includes scenarios like internet connectivity, broadcast signals, and cellular communication.
