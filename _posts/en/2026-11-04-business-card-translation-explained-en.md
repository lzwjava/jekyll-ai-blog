**Question:** What is the English translation of "名片"?

**Answer:** 名片 in English is **business card**.

A business card is a small card printed with a person's name, job title, company, and contact information, used when meeting people professionally.

**Related vocabulary:**

- 名片 = business card (general)
- 电子名片 = digital business card / e-card
- 名片夹 = business card holder / cardholder
- 交换名片 = exchange business cards
