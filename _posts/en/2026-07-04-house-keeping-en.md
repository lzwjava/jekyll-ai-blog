## Floor 1

- put the washed dishes into the dishwasher, and run the program
- clean the kitchen
- clean the dining table
- clean the living room, move cans and tissues to the trash bin
- take the garbage bag out of the trash bin in the toilet

## Floor 2

- help wash the clothes: put the clothes into the washer, set the program, and after around 30 minutes, hang up the clothes
- help collect the empty bottles in the study room or bedroom, and put them into garbage bags
- take the garbage bag out of the trash bin in the toilet

## Note

- wash the clothes first — the program takes around 30 minutes
- at the end, throw the garbage bags into the apartment's main trash center
