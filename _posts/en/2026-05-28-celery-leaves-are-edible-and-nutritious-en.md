**Question:** Can celery leaves be eaten, or should they be cut off, leaving only the stalks?

**Answer:**

Celery leaves are **fully edible** — don't throw them away.

**What they're good for:**

- **Flavor** — more intense, slightly bitter celery flavor than the stalks. Think of them like an herb (similar role to parsley).
- **Nutrition** — actually *more* nutrient-dense than stalks: higher in calcium, vitamin C, and antioxidants.
- **Cooking uses:**
  - Toss raw into salads (use sparingly — strong flavor)
  - Add to soups, stews, stock — classic use
  - Stir-fry with garlic (common in Chinese home cooking — 芹菜炒肉 often includes leaves)
  - Blend into green sauces or chimichurri-style condiments
  - Use as garnish like parsley

**When to cut them off:**

Only if the leaves are **yellowed, wilted, or slimy** — that's spoilage, not the leaves being inedible by nature.

**Bottom line:** Keep and eat the leaves. Chinese home cooking uses the whole plant — waste nothing.
