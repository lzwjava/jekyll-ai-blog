In HSBC's global job banding system, **Senior Consultant Specialist** typically maps to **GCB 4** (Global Career Band 4).

HSBC's band structure (high to low):

| Band | Level |
|------|-------|
| GCB 0 | Group CEO |
| GCB 1 | Group Executives |
| GCB 2 | Senior Executives |
| GCB 3 | Senior Leaders / Directors |
| **GCB 4** | **Senior Consultant / Senior Manager / VP equivalent** |
| GCB 5 | Consultant / Manager / AVP equivalent |
| GCB 6 | Analyst / Associate |
| GCB 7 | Junior / Entry level |
| GCB 8 | Support staff |

So **Senior Consultant Specialist = GCB 4**, which is a solid mid-senior level — equivalent to VP in bulge bracket bank terminology, or Senior Manager in consulting firms.

If your title is specifically "Consultant Specialist" (without Senior), that would be **GCB 5**.

> Note: HSBC doesn't always publish internal band mappings publicly, so verify against your offer letter or HR — the band is usually stated explicitly in the contract.
