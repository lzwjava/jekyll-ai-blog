**Question:** How do people typically earn their first million dollars?

---

**Answer:**

Research from Tom Corley's five-year *Rich Habits* study — which involved interviewing 225 millionaires — found that there are generally four paths to wealth, which often overlap: the Saver-Investor, the Big Company Senior Executive, the Virtuoso, and the Dreamer-Entrepreneur.

---

### 🛤️ The 4 Paths to Your First Million

**1. Saver-Investor** *(Safest & Most Accessible)*

The Saver-Investor route requires the least amount of risk. Every Saver-Investor in the study consistently saved 20% or more of their net pay, each paycheck. This path takes an average of 32 years. It demands financial discipline, but automating savings and investing removes willpower from the equation and keeps the plan on track.

**2. Big Company Senior Executive (The "Climber")** *(Moderate Risk)*

The Climber path is best for people who have strong networking skills and are successful at building lasting relationships with powerful people in their industry. This path takes an average of 21 years.

**3. Virtuoso** *(Specialized Skill or Knowledge)*

The Virtuoso path involves devoting an enormous amount of time, and often money, to becoming either a Knowledge-Based Virtuoso or a Skill-Based Virtuoso. Think top surgeons, elite attorneys, or renowned engineers — experts whose rare skills command premium fees. This path averages about 20 years.

**4. Dreamer-Entrepreneur** *(Highest Risk, Highest Reward)*

The dreamer-entrepreneur path involves pursuing a dream that can be monetized. For entrepreneurs, that dream is starting a successful business. This is the fastest path to become a millionaire. Dreamer-Entrepreneurs in one study had an average net worth of $7.4 million — far more than any other group. However, 91% of Dreamer-Entrepreneur millionaires faced setbacks and 34% failed at least once. This path takes an average of 12 years.

---

### 🔑 Common Traits Across All Paths

No matter which path someone takes, a few behaviors consistently appear:

- **High savings rate** — Most people reach their first million in 15 to 25 years by combining a 30% savings rate, steady income growth, and index fund investing.
- **Multiple income streams** — Rich people diversify their sources of income. In addition to wages or salary from their primary jobs, they also earn money from investments and profit-generating side ventures, like rental properties.
- **Frugality** — One of the common denominators for Saver-Investors, Big Company Climbers, and the Virtuoso self-made millionaires was being frugal.
- **Avoiding lifestyle creep** — Lifestyle creep is the single biggest reason high earners fail to build wealth.
- **Patience & persistence** — Plenty earn their fortunes by living frugally, investing wisely, and creating their own income-producing opportunities.

---

### ⏱️ Timeline Summary

| Path | Avg. Time to $1M |
|---|---|
| Dreamer-Entrepreneur | ~12 years |
| Virtuoso | ~20 years |
| Big Company Climber | ~21 years |
| Saver-Investor | ~32 years |

The fastest route carries the most risk; the slowest is the most reliable. Most people blend elements from multiple paths.
