**Question:** What are the key differences between Intel Core i3 and i5 processors?

**Answer:**

Great fundamental question! Here's a clear breakdown:

---

### 🔢 Core & Thread Count

| Feature | i3 (12th Gen example) | i5 (12th Gen example) |
|---|---|---|
| **Cores** | 4 Cores | 6–10 Cores |
| **Threads** | 8 Threads | 12–16 Threads |
| **Hybrid Architecture** | ❌ No E-cores | ✅ Has P+E cores (on some) |

---

### ⚡ Performance

| Feature | i3 | i5 |
|---|---|---|
| **everyday tasks** | ✅ Great | ✅ Great |
| **Multitasking** | ⚠️ OK | ✅ Much better |
| **Gaming** | ⚠️ Decent | ✅ Noticeably better |
| **Video editing / rendering** | ❌ Struggles | ✅ Handles well |
| **AI / ML workloads** | ❌ Slow | ⚠️ Better but limited |

---

### 💾 Cache & Clock Speed

| | i3-12100 | i5-12400 |
|---|---|---|
| **L3 Cache** | 12MB | 18MB |
| **Base Clock** | 3.3 GHz | 2.5 GHz |
| **Boost Clock** | 4.3 GHz | 4.4 GHz |
| **TDP** | 60W | 65W |

---

### 💰 Price Difference (2nd Hand CNY estimate)

| CPU | Approx. Used Price |
|---|---|
| i3-12100 | 300–450 CNY |
| i5-12400F | 400–550 CNY |
| i5-13400F | 500–700 CNY |

---

### 🎯 Who Should Pick What?

| Use Case | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Basic office / web browsing | i3 is enough |
| Light gaming + streaming | i5 is better |
| Heavy multitasking | i5 strongly recommended |
| Content creation / rendering | i5 minimum |
| Running local AI models | i5 or higher |

---

### 💡 Simple Rule of Thumb

> **i3** = fewer cores, budget-friendly, good for light tasks
> **i5** = more cores, better all-rounder, worth the small price gap

For the **MSI B760M-E DDR5** board you have, pairing it with an **i5-12400F or i5-13400F** is a much smarter investment than an i3, especially if the price gap in the second-hand market is only **100–200 CNY**.
