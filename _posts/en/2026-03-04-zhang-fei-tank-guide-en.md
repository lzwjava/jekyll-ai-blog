Question: How to play Zhang Fei in Kong of Honours

Answer: 
Zhang Fei (likely intended as the hero from **Honor of Kings**, a popular MOBA, as "Kong of Honours" appears to be a misspelling or variant with no distinct game found) is a **Support/Tank** (Guardian subclass) played primarily in the **Roaming** lane. He excels at frontline initiation, shielding allies, crowd control (CC), and transforming into a durable damage dealer via his Madness mechanic. His dual-form playstyle—Human (pre-transform) for stacking and support, Ferali (post-transform) for aggression—makes him strong in team fights, protecting carries like marksmen while disrupting enemies.

### Core Mechanics: Madness & Transformation
- **Passive: Dark Potential** – In Human Form, Basic Attacks (AA) and skills grant **Madness** (stack ~3 per hit). Leaping Savior grants extra per shielded ally. Accumulate quickly via AA/minions to enable Ultimate.
- **Ultimate: Unbridled Outburst** – Roar creates wind path: damages/knocks back enemies, stuns at end. Grants **invincibility (1.25s)**, **40% max HP shield (15s)**, +50% MS in wind. **Transforms to Ferali Form**: AA deal bonus damage (+100% Phys ATK), skills slow enemies 50% (2s). Auto-triggers Skill 1 on end.
- **Tip**: Always stack Madness early; don't Ult without full stacks for max impact.

### Skills

| Skill | Description | Tips |
|-------|-------------|------|
| **Skill 1: Forbidden Domain** | Swing spear: 450–950 (+100% Phys ATK) Phys DMG + knockback heroes. +3 Madness on hit. | Poke/farm/repel divers. Post-transform: Core damage tool. CD: 6s. |
| **Skill 2: Leaping Savior** | Leap to area: Shield allies (550–1500 +50% Phys ATK, up to 5). +3 Madness (+1 per ally shielded). | Protect carries/escape. Pre-transform: Pure support; post: Engage + DMG. CD: 10–12s. |
| **Ultimate: Unbridled Outburst** | See above. CD: 40s. | Initiate when enemies cluster/no mobility left. Path for stun. |

**Post-Transform Skills** (Ferali): Enhanced versions—Mad Slaughter (1), Stampede (2)—focus on aggression.

### Skill Upgrade Priority
1. **Ultimate** (max first for transform power).
2. **Skill 2** (shields/engage).
3. **Skill 1** (poke/CC).

### Best Combos
- **Protect Ally**: 2 (shield) > Ult (transform/engage) > 1 > AA.
- **Initiate**: Ult (stun path) > 2 (leap in) > 1 > AA.
- **Ferali Chase**: Post-Ult, AA > 2/1 for slows + bonus DMG.

### Recommended Build (Tank/Support Focus)
Prioritize tankiness (HP, defenses) + shields/CC. Adapt vs. phys/magic heavy teams.

**Battle Spell**: **Heal** (team sustain). Alt: Smite (jungle harass) or Disrupt (tower push).

**Stats**: ~50.6% WR, C-tier (solid pick for beginners/protection).

### Playstyle by Game Phase
- **Early (0-4 min)**: Roam mid/conqueror lanes. Use 1 to clear waves/jungle, stack Madness on minions. Shield ADC with 2, poke with 1. Help ganks but avoid overcommit (weak pre-Ult). Buy sustain items.
- **Mid (4-10 min)**: Rotate for skirmishes/objectives (towers/Overlord). Shield carries, Ult to initiate when enemies group. Farm stacks aggressively.
- **Late**: Frontline beast. Ult into backline for stun/shields, follow with Ferali combo. Absorb focus, peel for team. Synergizes with fragile ADCs (e.g., Hou Yi, Huang Zhong).

### General Tips
- **Strengths**: Team shields, AOE CC, tanky post-Ult, easy for new players.
- **Weaknesses**: Weak pre-stack/transform; vulnerable to burst assassins (e.g., Mulan) or CC cleanses (Zhuangzi).
- **Counters**: Play safe vs. divers; bait mobility first.
- **Team Synergy**: Protect marksmen/fighters needing peel.
- **Pro Play**: Wait for enemy CDs before Ult. Use 2 for vision/escapes. AA between skills for stacks.

Master stacking + timely Ult for game-changing engages!
