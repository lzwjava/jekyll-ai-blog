Question: How is Google Ads revenue now, given that AI agent traffic is already over half of web traffic according to Cloudflare?

Answer:

The interesting part is: **Google Ads revenue has not collapsed yet.** The AI-agent/web traffic transition is real, but Google's business is more resilient because Google owns the **intent layer**, not just the traffic layer. ([Cloudflare][1])

Current picture:

| Area                    | Status         |
| ----------------------- | -------------- |
| Google Search ads       | Still growing  |
| YouTube ads             | Still growing  |
| AdSense/display network | Weakening      |
| Publisher traffic       | Under pressure |
| AI agents crawling web  | Exploding      |

Alphabet Q1 2026:

* Total revenue: **$109.9B**, +22% YoY
* Google Services: **$89.6B**, +16%
* Search & other ads: **+19% YoY**
* YouTube ads: **+11% YoY**

So the short-term answer:

**Google Ads is doing fine.**

But the long-term threat is structural.

---

The old web loop:

```
human
  ↓
Google Search
  ↓
click website
  ↓
publisher earns ads
  ↓
Google earns ads
```

AI agent future:

```
human
  ↓
AI agent
  ↓
answer generated
  ↓
maybe no website visit
```

Cloudflare reported that automated agents/bots now generate more than half of web requests, and AI-related crawling has become a major portion of crawler traffic. ([Cloudflare][2])

This hurts:

* SEO websites
* affiliate sites
* AdSense publishers
* content farms

because fewer humans arrive at pages.

---

But Google has a big advantage:

### 1. Search intent is more valuable than raw traffic

A person searching:

```
best GPU for LLM training
```

has buying intent.

An AI crawler requesting:

```
crawl all GPU articles
```

has almost zero immediate commercial value.

Ads are attached to **commercial intent**, not page views.

---

### 2. Google is moving ads into AI answers

The battle is:

Old:

```
Search page
  |
  +-- organic links
  +-- ads
```

New:

```
AI answer
  |
  +-- sponsored recommendation
  +-- transaction
```

Google wants to own the agent interface.

They already push AI search products while maintaining Search revenue growth. ([The Economic Times][3])

---

### 3. The losers are probably small publishers

Example:

Before:

```
100k Google visitors/month
RPM $20

= $2000/month
```

After AI summaries:

```
10k visitors/month

= $200/month
```

But Google can still show:

```
User:
"buy laptop"

Gemini/Search AI:
"Here are 3 laptops"

Sponsored:
Dell
Lenovo
Amazon
```

Google captures the money.

---

My expectation:

**2026-2028**

* Google Search ads: probably stable/growing
* AdSense ecosystem: decline
* SEO businesses: massive disruption
* AI commerce agents: new ads market

The analogy is:

> Google killed the Yellow Pages.
> AI agents may kill the "10 blue links" web.

But Google is trying very hard not to become the victim; it wants to become the **agent operating system + advertising marketplace**.
