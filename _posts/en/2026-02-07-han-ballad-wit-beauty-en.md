### Detailed Explanation

《陌上桑》 is a famous **Yuefu folk ballad of the Han dynasty**, praised for its vivid narrative and strong artistic techniques. Its main artistic characteristics are as follows:

---

#### **A. 侧面烘托 (Indirect portrayal / side contrast)** ✅

* The heroine **Luo Fu (罗敷)** is not described mainly through direct appearance.
* Instead, her beauty is **highlighted through others’ reactions** (passersby stop working, officials admire her, etc.).
* This is a classic and highly praised technique in the poem.

---

#### **B. 极力铺陈 (Elaborate and extensive description)** ✅

* The poem uses **layered, repetitive, and detailed narration**.
* Clothing, actions, reactions, and dialogue are all carefully expanded.
* This gives the poem a **rich, rhythmic, and dramatic structure**.

---

#### **C. 比兴象征 (Metaphor and symbolic evocation)** ❌

* 比兴 is common in **The Book of Songs (《诗经》)**.
* 《陌上桑》 focuses on **direct narrative and dramatic depiction**, not symbolic evocation.
* Therefore, this is **not a core artistic feature** here.

---

#### **D. 富喜剧性 (Strong sense of comedy)** ✅

* The plot includes humorous tension:

  * Officials trying to flirt
  * Luo Fu’s witty and confident response
* The contrast between **desire and moral restraint** creates comic effect.

---

#### **E. 笔调幽默 (Humorous tone)** ✅

* The language is lively, playful, and ironic.
* The heroine’s clever speech and the exaggerated reactions of others contribute to a **light, humorous tone**.
