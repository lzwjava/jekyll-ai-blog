* **The problem space is still wide open.**

* **Individual researchers can have enormous leverage.**
  In traditional compiler research, a brilliant person might improve a language design or implementation. In AI, a small team can build a model, a tool, or an agent system used by millions.

* **Economic incentives are much stronger.**
  AI has created a direct bridge between research and products. A researcher who discovers a useful technique today can potentially turn it into a company or a widely used tool.

Whether discussing programming languages, academia, physics, health, language learning, music, or daily life, the common thread is **questioning assumptions and trying to rebuild understanding from first principles**.

That mindset is valuable in any era.
