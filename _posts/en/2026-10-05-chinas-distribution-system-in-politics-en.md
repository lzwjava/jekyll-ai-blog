Question: What does 分配制度 (the "distribution system") mean in Chinese politics?

Answer: 分配制度 is the income distribution system, meaning the rules for how China's economic output is shared among workers, capital owners, the state, and society. It is a core part of the Party's ideology and economic policy.

**Official principle.** The formula is "distribution according to work as the mainstay, with multiple forms of distribution coexisting" (按劳分配为主体、多种分配方式并存). Official commentary calls this system an important part of the basic socialist economic system. It is said to show the superiority of socialism and to fit the level of productive forces in the primary stage of socialism.

**Historical evolution.** Before reform and opening-up, distribution was essentially egalitarian. Afterward, egalitarianism was abandoned and "to each according to his work" was gradually improved. With the socialist market economy, it was combined with distribution based on factors of production (such as capital, land, technology, and management), with work-based distribution still leading.

**Three tiers of distribution.** Party documents describe a coordinated framework with three layers:
- **Primary distribution** is market-based. It covers wages, profits, and returns on capital and other production factors.
- **Redistribution** is carried out by the government through taxes, social security, and transfer payments.
- **Tertiary distribution** is voluntary, through charity, donations, and philanthropy.

The 20th Party Congress report (2022) called for these three to be "well coordinated and mutually complementary." It also called for raising personal income as a share of national income, and wages as a share of primary distribution, and for expanding the middle-income group.

**Political significance.**
- It is closely tied to "common prosperity" (共同富裕), which is presented as an essential requirement of socialism with Chinese characteristics and a central theme under Xi Jinping.
- It is the Party's response to the widening income gap that came with market reforms. The tension is between efficiency (growth) and fairness (equality).
- The 15th Five-Year Plan recommendations (2025) repeat the goals of raising the share of household income in national income and the share of labor pay in primary distribution. They also call for labor remuneration to grow in step with labor productivity.
