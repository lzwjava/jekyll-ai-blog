**Question:** What does "BUBALUS" (大水牛) mean, and what is this 2-pin, 12V 0.15A case fan?

**Answer:**

**The Name**

- **大水牛** (Dà Shuǐ Niú) literally means **"Big Water Buffalo"** in Chinese.
- **BUBALUS** is its Latin/English brand name — *Bubalus* is the scientific genus name for the **water buffalo** (*Bubalus bubalis*). So both names mean exactly the same animal.

**The Brand**

BUBALUS (大水牛) is a brand under HEDY Holdings Co., Ltd. (七喜控股股份有限公司), founded in 1997 and based in Guangzhou, China. It is a well-known manufacturer of PC cases, power supplies, monitors, and computer peripherals. The company has over 18 years of professional experience in R&D and manufacturing of computer cases, power supplies, and accessories.

**The Fan Specs (2-pin, 12V 0.15A)**

This is a basic **sleeve-bearing DC case fan**, typical of budget/OEM builds bundled with Chinese PC cases. Here's what those specs mean:

| Spec | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **2-pin** | Simple DC power connector — no PWM speed control, runs at full speed constantly |
| **12V** | Powered directly from a 12V rail (molex or chassis header) |
| **0.15A** | Power draw of 12V × 0.15A = **1.8 watts** — very low, quiet and energy-efficient |

It's a no-frills fan: constant speed, no tachometer signal, no speed control. Perfectly functional for basic airflow, but you can't monitor or adjust its RPM from your motherboard.
