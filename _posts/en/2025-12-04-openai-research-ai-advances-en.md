- Chen highlighted breakthroughs in pre-training efficiency, stating OpenAI can now embed the same level of knowledge into smaller models, reducing the need for massive scaling. He mentioned "strong algorithms" for data efficiency, potentially cutting data needs by up to 40% in some areas.
