**Question:** If a beer has been left in the refrigerator for a day with the can lid open, can we still drink it?

**Answer:**

Yes, **it is usually safe to drink**, provided that:

* The beer was kept **continuously refrigerated**.
* The can was **already opened** but left in the fridge (not left at room temperature).
* Nothing contaminated it (e.g., insects, dust, or someone touching or drinking from it).

However, the **quality will likely be worse**:

* 🍺 It will have lost much of its carbonation and taste flat.
* 🍺 Some aromas may have dissipated.
* 🍺 Oxidation may make the flavor stale or cardboard-like.

If the beer has:

* An unusual smell,
* Visible mold or floating debris,
* Or you suspect it was contaminated,

it's better to discard it.

So, if it was simply an **open can sitting in a clean refrigerator for about 24 hours**, it's generally **safe to drink**, though it probably won't taste as good as when it was freshly opened.
