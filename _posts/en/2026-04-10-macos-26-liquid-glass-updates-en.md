Question: What are the big updates in macOS 26.4.1 and in recent releases over the past half a year?

Answer: 

macOS 26 Tahoe is the current major version of macOS (the successor to macOS 15 Sequoia). It was first released on September 15, 2025, and uses year-based versioning starting from 26. The latest point release as of April 2026 is **macOS 26.4.1** (released April 9, 2026), which primarily includes unspecified bug fixes and security improvements with no major new features highlighted in Apple's release notes.

### Major Features Introduced in macOS 26 Tahoe (Initial 2025 Release)
This was the biggest overhaul in recent years, often described as the most significant design change since around 2013:
- **Liquid Glass design**: A new unified visual language with transparent, customizable interface elements, glossy effects, and more focus on content. It includes options for updated light/dark modes, color-tinted icons, and a clear look for personalization.
- Tighter integration with iPhone and iPad (e.g., better continuity features).
- New **Phone app** on Mac for handling calls.
- New **Games app** for better game discovery and management.
- Expanded **Apple Intelligence** features with improved integration.
- Overhauled **Spotlight** search (one of the most extensive updates to date).
- Other enhancements like updated app icons and personalization options.

### Recent Point Releases in the Past ~6 Months (Roughly October 2025 – April 2026)
Apple has released several incremental updates (26.1 through 26.4.1) focusing on refinements, performance, and smaller features rather than massive overhauls. Key highlights include:

- **26.4 (March 24, 2026)**: Added support for new emoji and AirPods Max 2. It also included various performance tweaks, bug fixes, and security updates. Some reports mention improvements to battery life and overall stability.
- **26.3 / 26.3.1 / 26.3.2 (February–March 2026)**: Focused on stability, with one minor release specific to MacBook Neo hardware. Included refinements to Liquid Glass elements (e.g., Edge Light in an earlier 26.2 update) and tinted interface options.
- **26.2 (December 2025)**: Introduced "Edge Light" effects as part of ongoing Liquid Glass refinements.
- **26.1 (November 2025)**: Added tinted Liquid Glass customization options.

Overall, the past half-year has emphasized polishing the Liquid Glass design system, expanding Apple Intelligence where applicable, improving cross-device integration, and delivering routine security/performance updates. The 26.x series has been relatively iterative compared to the foundational changes in the initial 26.0 release.

macOS 26 remains the actively supported version, with older releases like Sequoia (15.x) still receiving security patches but no major new features.
