Question: Did James Dyson move Dyson’s headquarters from the UK to Singapore?

Answer: **Yes.** More precisely, Dyson moved its **corporate head office and established its global headquarters in Singapore in 2019**.

* In **January 2019**, Dyson announced the relocation of its corporate office from the UK to Singapore. ([Dyson][1])
* Dyson said the rationale was proximity to its **fast-growing Asian markets, suppliers, and manufacturing operations**. ([Dyson][1])
