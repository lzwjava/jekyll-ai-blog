Question: What does the "Circle of Excellence" award mean at HSBC?

Answer:

## HSBC Circle of Excellence Award

The **Circle of Excellence** is one of HSBC's most prestigious **internal employee recognition programs**, specifically designed to celebrate and reward the **top-performing colleagues** within its Wealth and Personal Banking (WPB) / retail banking business division.

---

### What It Is

The Circle of Excellence is HSBC's **flagship global recognition event**, attended by the **top 500 performing colleagues** from across HSBC's retail and personal banking business. The event brings together inspiring figures and business leaders to discuss key topics of importance to HSBC employees, with a strong focus on themes such as diversity, equity, inclusion and accessibility (DEIA), sustainability, supporting local communities, and digital transformation.

---

### Who Receives It

The award is given to employees who demonstrate **exceptional performance** across the retail and personal banking side of HSBC. This is not an award given to all staff — only the very best performers globally are selected. Recipients come from various roles, from front-line bankers and branch staff to relationship managers.

For example, in 2022, Winnie Choi received the **HSBC Global Circle of Excellence Award**, representing the top performers within HSBC's Wealth and Personal Banking division in Canada.

---

### What the Event Involves

The Circle of Excellence event features sessions with business leaders and inspiring external figures. The Q&A discussions with prominent guests are streamed for the top 500 performing colleagues to watch and participate in.

---

### What It Represents

In broader terms, the Circle of Excellence is HSBC's way of:

- **Recognizing outstanding individual achievement** in sales, customer service, and value-aligned behavior
- **Motivating high-performers** by giving them visibility with senior leadership
- **Reinforcing HSBC's culture of recognition** — consistent with HSBC's global "At Our Best" recognition philosophy, which aims to develop a culture of recognition, reward exemplary behavior in line with HSBC values and global standards, and improve overall employee experience.

---

### Summary

In short, receiving the HSBC Circle of Excellence Award means you are recognized as being among the **best of the best** in HSBC's global retail and personal banking workforce — a significant honor that reflects outstanding performance, alignment with HSBC values, and exceptional contribution to the bank's business goals.
