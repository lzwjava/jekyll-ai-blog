Written when he was only 23, the book tells the story of a romantic relationship between a young man and a woman he meets on a flight from Paris to London. The narrative combines a tender, witty love story with deep philosophical reflections on the nature of love, attachment, jealousy, idealism, heartbreak, and emotional growth.

Many readers describe it as both a heartbreakingly accurate portrayal of falling in and out of love and a profound education on how romantic love actually works.
