**Question:** Who are the greatest AI software and hardware full-stack engineers, including figures like George Hotz?

**Answer:**

Here's a breakdown of the most celebrated engineers who span both the software and hardware sides of AI:

---

### 🔧 George Hotz (geohot)

George Hotz (alias "geohot") is an American security hacker, entrepreneur, and software engineer best known for being the first person to unlock the iPhone and for reverse engineering the PlayStation 3. He founded comma.ai in 2015, a company focused on vehicle automation using machine learning. He also founded the tiny corp, which develops tinygrad — a minimalist deep learning framework — and the tinybox AI computer. His stated mission is to "commoditize the petaflop" and break NVIDIA's dominance in AI compute. Hotz is the rare engineer who writes compilers, designs hardware, trains ML models, and ships full products solo.

---

### 🧠 Andrej Karpathy

Andrej Karpathy has worked at OpenAI, Tesla, and as of May 2026, joined Anthropic to lead pretraining research. He was Director of AI at Tesla, leading vision and autonomous driving efforts, applying deep learning to real-world robotics at scale. His "Zero to Hero" deep learning series is legendary in the AI education community. He bridges research, systems engineering, and hardware-aware ML in a way very few can.

---

### ⚙️ Jim Keller

Jim Keller has held key roles at AMD, Intel, Tesla, and Apple, building some of the most successful processor designs in history. He led AMD's Zen architecture, designed Apple's A4 and A5 processors, and created Tesla's custom self-driving silicon. He is now CEO of Tenstorrent, building open RISC-V AI training and inference chips, backed by Jeff Bezos, Samsung, and Hyundai at roughly a $2B valuation. He is arguably the greatest living chip architect.

---

### 🌐 Yann LeCun

Yann LeCun is primarily known for his work in machine learning, computer vision, and computational neuroscience. He is the founding father of convolutional neural networks and received the Turing Award in 2018 alongside Yoshua Bengio and Geoffrey Hinton for contributions to deep learning. He currently serves as Chief AI Scientist at Meta.

---

### 🧬 Demis Hassabis

Demis Hassabis is co-founder and CEO of DeepMind, an AI research lab acquired by Google in 2014. The lab achieved global recognition for developing AlphaGo, which defeated the world champion Go player — a major milestone in AI research. He is also a chess prodigy and neuroscientist, making him one of the most complete intellects in the field.
