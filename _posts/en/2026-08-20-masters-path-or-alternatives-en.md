## 3. Travel visa to find AI clients (not recommended)

A B1/B2 visitor visa is not a work visa.

You cannot simply enter the U.S. as a tourist and work for U.S. clients locally. Employment is not authorized under B visitor status. ([USCIS][2])

You can do things like:

* attend meetings
* negotiate contracts
* visit companies

but not:

* join a U.S. company as an engineer
* provide local labor
* freelance physically inside the U.S.

([U.S. Customs and Border Protection][3])
