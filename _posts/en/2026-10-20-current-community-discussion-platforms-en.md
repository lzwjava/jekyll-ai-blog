**Question:** What community/discussion websites and platforms are currently running — like Discord and others, including open-source and BBS-style systems?

---

**Answer:**

Here's a breakdown by category:

---

### 💬 Chat-Based (Real-Time)

| Platform | Open Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Discord** | ❌ | Dominant chat platform; free, proprietary |
| **Matrix / Element** | ✅ | Decentralized, encrypted; user count in the hundreds of millions, markets well to governments and public sector |
| **Rocket.Chat** | ✅ | Self-hostable Slack alternative; released 2015 |
| **Mattermost** | ✅ | Team-focused, self-hosted |

| **Telegram** | ❌ | Popular for group chats and channels |

### 🌐 Fediverse / Decentralized (ActivityPub)

These platforms federate with each other — users on one can interact with users on another:

| Platform | Type | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mastodon** | Microblogging | ~777K monthly active users as of March 2026 |
| **Lemmy** | Reddit-like link aggregator | Steadily developing in 2026 with regular releases and growing client ecosystem; ~455 instances, ~48,600 MAU |
| **Mbin / Kbin** | Link aggregator | Lemmy-compatible threadiverse alternative |
| **PieFed** | Reddit-like | Growing quickly — from 295 MAU (1 year ago) to ~4,926 MAU now |
| **PeerTube** | Video hosting | YouTube alternative in the fediverse |
| **Bluesky** | Microblogging | Decentralized protocol (AT Protocol), not ActivityPub; reportedly eating Mastodon's lunch right now |

---

### 📊 Where Open-Source Communities Tend to Gather

Among analyzed open-source software communities: Discord hosts 41%, GitHub 32%, Slack 18%, Reddit 14%, and Discourse 5%.

---

**Recommendations by use case:**

- **Want a classic BBS/forum?** → Discourse or Flarum (self-host)
- **Want real-time chat?** → Matrix/Element (open, encrypted) or Rocket.Chat
- **Want a Reddit alternative?** → Lemmy or PieFed (federated, open source)
- **Want Twitter-like microblogging?** → Mastodon or Bluesky
- **Just want plug-and-play?** → Discord (proprietary but free)
