**Question:** What is "六个必须坚持" (the Six Must Upholds)?

---

**Answer:**

**Overview**

First introduced in its current form in Xi Jinping's political report to the 20th National Congress of the CCP in October 2022, "六个必须坚持" (liùgè bìxū jiānchí) — translated as the "Six Must Upholds" or "Six Adheres" — describes the core concepts meant to guide the worldview and methods of the Chinese Communist Party under the overarching governing concept of "Xi Jinping Thought."

These six principles are understood as the **methodological framework** for applying Xi Jinping Thought in practice.

---

**The Six Principles**

| # | Chinese | English |
| --- | --------- | --------- |
| 1 | 人民至上 | Supremacy of the People |
| 2 | 自信自立 | Self-Confidence and Self-Reliance |
| 3 | 守正创新 | Integrity and Innovation |
| 4 | 问题导向 | Problem-Solving Orientation |
| 5 | 系统观念 | Systems Thinking |
| 6 | 胸怀天下 | Global Vision / Cherishing the World |

---

**Brief Explanation of Each**

**1. 人民至上 — Supremacy of the People**
The people are the highest priority. Theories that depart from the people are considered "pale and feeble," and policies must bring prosperity to the people.

**2. 自信自立 — Self-Confidence and Self-Reliance**
China must adhere to a "firm faith" in the tenets of socialism, and a "firm belief" in Xi Jinping Thought. This involves the so-called "Four Confidences" (四个自信).

**3. 守正创新 — Integrity and Innovation**
The leadership emphasizes "science" and having a "scientific attitude" toward problems, as well as emphasizing the pursuit of "truth."

**4. 问题导向 — Problem-Solving Orientation**
The CCP lists out a series of problems or issues to be considered in the process of governance, policy-making, and international relations, asserting the need to "raise new concepts, new thoughts, and new methods that can truly resolve issues."

**5. 系统观念 — Systems Thinking**
This encompasses the notion that the CCP must think and act more holistically, adhering to seven types of "thinking" in its work, including: strategic thinking, historical thinking, dialectical thinking, systematic thinking, innovative thinking, rule of law thinking, and bottom-line thinking.

**6. 胸怀天下 — Global Vision**
China and the CCP must expand their "global view," and must "answer the concerns of the people of various nations." The idea is that China's core interests are interconnected with those of the world and that China can contribute to solving the most pressing issues facing the world.

---

**Important Note**
The same numerical label can refer to different lists — "六个坚持" may mean the six methodological principles of Xi Jinping Thought, the six principles of the 2024 Third Plenum's reform program, or six principles in a particular economic plan. The version above is specifically the one from the **20th Party Congress (2022)**, which is the most widely cited current version.
