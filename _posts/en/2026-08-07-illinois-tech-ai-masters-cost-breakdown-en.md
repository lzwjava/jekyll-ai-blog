For your background (AI engineer, LLM training, 10+ years software experience), the key question is **ROI**:

* $80K–90K total cost is high.
* Illinois Tech is a decent US STEM school, especially because it is in Chicago and has CS/AI programs.
* But it is not in the same tier as CMU, UIUC, Georgia Tech, etc.
* The value is mainly:

  * US degree credential
  * F-1 visa → OPT (3 years for STEM)
  * US job market access
  * networking

You should also check scholarship possibilities. Many Illinois Tech graduate students receive merit scholarships, which can reduce tuition significantly. ([Illinois Institute of Technology][2])

Given your profile (LLM training + production engineering), I would compare it against cheaper options:

* Georgia Tech OMSCS (~$7K–$10K online)
* UIUC MCS (~$40K+)
* UT Austin MSCSO (~$10K)
* Canadian AI master's programs
* US thesis-based MS programs with RA/TA funding

Illinois Tech makes more sense if your priority is **entering the US physically and using OPT**, not purely learning AI.
