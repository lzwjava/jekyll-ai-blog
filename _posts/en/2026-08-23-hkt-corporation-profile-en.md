## Question: What's HKT Corp in Hong Kong?

HKT Limited (often called **HKT**) is one of Hong Kong's largest telecommunications and technology companies. It is the successor to the old Hong Kong Telephone Company and is now the main telecom arm of PCCW Limited.

### Basic profile

* **Founded:** 1881 (as Hong Kong Telephone Company; modern HKT structure formed later)
* **Headquarters:** Hong Kong
* **Industry:** Telecommunications, ICT, cloud, cybersecurity, digital services
* **Parent group:** PCCW
* **Hong Kong Stock Exchange:** listed company (SEHK: 6823)

HKT is basically the "incumbent telecom operator" of Hong Kong, similar to:

* China Telecom in mainland China
* NTT in Japan
* AT&T historically in the US

---

## Main businesses

### 1. Fixed broadband and fiber network

HKT operates one of Hong Kong's largest fiber broadband networks.

Products:

* Home broadband
* Enterprise internet
* Fiber connectivity
* Data centers

Hong Kong has very high fiber penetration, and HKT is one of the major infrastructure providers.

---

### 2. Mobile network

Through the 3 Hong Kong brand, HKT provides:

* 4G / 5G mobile services
* Enterprise mobile solutions
* IoT connectivity

---

### 3. Enterprise IT services

This is probably the most interesting part for software/AI engineers.

HKT provides:

* Cloud computing
* Managed services
* Cybersecurity
* AI solutions
* Data analytics
* Digital transformation consulting
* Enterprise software integration

Customers include:

* Banks
* Government departments
* Large Hong Kong corporations
* Retail chains

---

### 4. PayMe and fintech

HKT operates PayMe, one of Hong Kong's major mobile payment platforms.

It competes with:

* AlipayHK
* WeChat Pay HK
