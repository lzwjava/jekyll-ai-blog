Question: Is 399 CNY a good price for a used MSI B350M MORTAR motherboard plus Ryzen 5 2600 (6-core), sold by an individual with no box, and preferably for local pickup?

Answer:

**Verdict: fair, but not a bargain. I'd try to negotiate down to roughly 320-350 CNY.** I couldn't find current used listings to compare against, so this is based on typical second-hand pricing for these parts. Check Xianyu for recent "B350 + 2600" combo sales before you commit.

**Why it's reasonable**
- The listing says it's a personal-use set that has never been repaired and has all its parts, so the risk of a dead board is lower than average.
- The B350M Mortar is a decent micro-ATX board. It has 4 DDR4 slots, 1 M.2 slot, 4 SATA ports and Click BIOS 5. One review described it as a solid, affordable micro-ATX option when new.
- The Ryzen 5 2600 still handles office work, light gaming and general use fine.

**Why I wouldn't pay full price**
- Both parts are 2017-2018 hardware. Used, the CPU and board are typically each worth well under 250 CNY.
- The original warranty is long expired. MSI backed it with a 3-year warranty.
- It only has one M.2 slot and no PCIe 4.0.
- The listing doesn't mention a CPU cooler or RAM. Ask whether the stock Wraith cooler is included, because buying a cooler adds cost.

**What to check before paying (this is why local pickup is best)**
1. Ask them to boot it with your own RAM, or theirs, and confirm it reaches the BIOS and Windows.
2. Look at the AM4 socket for bent pins, and check the CPU for pin marks or scratches.
3. Check for swollen capacitors, burn marks or sticky residue on the board.
4. Test the rear ports (HDMI, DVI, USB, audio, LAN) and the M.2 and SATA slots.
5. Confirm the CPU model in BIOS or CPU-Z, and run a short stress test to see temperatures.
6. If you ever want a Ryzen 5000 chip, such as a 5600 or 5700X3D, the board needs a BIOS update. Check MSI's CPU support list for this exact board.

If you can't pick it up, pay only through the platform's protected payment and ask for a short boot video.
