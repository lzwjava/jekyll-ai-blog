Question: What are the major tech trends and changes in 2025?

Answer: 

In 2025, technology advanced rapidly, dominated by artificial intelligence while addressing its challenges like energy demands and ethics. Key developments included massive investments in AI infrastructure, progress toward practical quantum computing, and regulatory shifts in social media and data privacy. Below are the major trends and changes, based on reports from leading analysts and real-world events.

### 1. **Agentic and Autonomous AI**
   - AI evolved from generative tools to "agentic" systems capable of independent planning, decision-making, and multi-step task execution.
   - Gartner named agentic AI its top trend, predicting 15% of daily work decisions would be autonomous by 2028.
   - Companies like OpenAI, Google, and Microsoft released advanced models (e.g., Gemini 3) with better reasoning, multimodality, and on-device processing for privacy and speed.
   - AI agents integrated deeply into enterprises, boosting productivity but raising governance needs.

### 2. **AI Infrastructure and Energy Boom**
   - Explosive demand for AI training and inference led to unprecedented data center construction and power consumption.
   - Tech giants announced billions in capital expenditure; nuclear energy revived to meet AI's needs, with investments in small modular reactors.
   - Sustainability focused on energy-efficient computing and green tech, as AI's carbon footprint became a global concern.

### 3. **Quantum Computing Progress**
   - Hardware breakthroughs brought useful quantum systems closer, with higher qubit counts (e.g., 72-qubit prototypes) and improved fidelity.
   - Companies like IBM, Google, IonQ, and others advanced roadmaps, demonstrating error correction and hybrid quantum-classical integration.
   - Post-quantum cryptography emerged as a priority to secure data against future quantum threats.

### 4. **Robotics and Human-Machine Synergy**
   - Polyfunctional robots and advanced robotics gained traction, with applications in manufacturing, healthcare, and logistics.
   - Trends included ambient invisible intelligence (seamless environmental sensing) and neurological enhancements like brain-computer interfaces.

### 5. **AI Governance, Security, and Ethics**
   - Disinformation security and AI watermarking became critical to combat deepfakes and misinformation.
   - Regulations increased, including Australia's social media ban for under-16s and global AI acts.
   - Focus on trustworthy AI, with platforms for governance and ethical standards.

### 6. **Sustainability and Green Technologies**
   - Innovations in structural batteries, cleaner fertilizers, and nuclear advancements addressed climate challenges.
   - AI aided green manufacturing, weather forecasting, and carbon capture.

### 7. **Other Notable Changes**
   - Spatial computing and extended reality (XR) advanced in education and training.
   - Biotech integrations with AI accelerated drug discovery and personalized medicine.
   - Geopolitical shifts influenced tech, with U.S.-China competition in AI chips and fragmented global standards.

Overall, 2025 marked AI's transition from experimentation to core infrastructure, driving economic growth while prompting urgent discussions on energy, jobs, and regulation. Quantum and robotics laid foundations for future disruptions.
