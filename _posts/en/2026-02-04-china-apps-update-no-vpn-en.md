No, you typically **don't need a VPN** to update apps if your App Store is set to the USA region.

Here's what happens:

**App updates work without VPN because:**
- App Store servers for downloads/updates use Apple's CDN (Content Delivery Network)
- These CDN servers are generally accessible in China
- The download process itself doesn't require you to be "in" the USA region

**What you DO need VPN for:**
- **Browsing/searching** the App Store (the storefront interface may load slowly or have issues without VPN)
- **Downloading NEW apps** for the first time (sometimes works without VPN, but often unreliable)
- **Apps that require VPN to function** (like Google apps, Facebook, etc.)

**Practical experience:**
Most people in China with US App Store accounts report that automatic updates work fine without VPN. Manual updates usually work too, though occasionally you might experience slow speeds or need to retry.

**Pro tip:** Enable automatic updates in Settings → App Store → App Updates. This way your apps update in the background without you needing to think about it.

The main limitation is really about accessing the App Store interface itself and downloading apps you don't already have, rather than updating existing ones.
