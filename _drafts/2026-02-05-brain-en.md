Our brain is magic. My IELTS listening score is like 6. After 3 years in a global company in an English environment, my listening score is still 6. That's because I always open Zoom captions in meetings.

I worry I am unable to hear clearly, especially since half the team are Indians.

When I open Zoom captions, after some weeks, though sometimes I still can't hear clearly and have to tell colleagues to repeat, it's just in this case that I improve my listening.

When I open Zoom captions, we think we have captions, and we have AI companion in Zoom, so I just focus on listening when it's highly related to me. Even at that time, I mostly read captions.

I mean our brain is magic—if you give it some time, after some months, its listening will improve a lot.

This is a perfect case study.

Like navigation. 

Like word vocabulary—if we skip new words in daily life, I'll surely keep my vocabulary at around 8,000.

I remember in high school, when my vocabulary was at 3,000, how I used a stupid way to search a printed dictionary to know the meanings of words in Paul Graham essays I'd printed. That's how I gained more vocabulary. And in the latest 10 years, I've searched thousands of times to know meanings.

But still, I skip a lot of new words in my reading.
