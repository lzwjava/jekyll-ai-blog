Question: Why did Tantan (探探) CEO Wang Yu go back to China to start a company?

Answer: Wang Yu didn't return from the US. He came back from Sweden. He was born in Beijing, grew up in Sweden, and earned master's degrees in computer science and industrial economics. After graduating he worked at Sony Ericsson, and in early 2007 he returned to China with Pan Ying to co-found the fashion community P1.

The sources point to a few reasons:

- **Boredom with the work in Sweden.** He said that in Sweden he and Pan Ying (then at PwC) had few projects and spent much of their time chatting over tea, which felt unchallenging. He was working as an R&D engineer at Ericsson at the time.
- **Confidence in the startup recipe.** Watching his boss start a company, he concluded that you find a pain point, solve it with technology, and make money.
- **A bigger market and opportunity in China.** The sources don't state this directly as his motive, but it fits the move and his later comments on China's social landscape.

His first venture, P1, ran for about 7 to 8 years. He described it as neither a big success nor a failure, but said it taught him lessons that made Tantan's early path smoother. In 2014 he and Pan Ying launched Tantan, a Tinder-style app tailored to China. He noted that Chinese young people lack party and bar culture and are shy about approaching strangers, which created demand for it.

Later, Tantan was acquired by Momo. Reports said Wang Yu and Pan Ying stepped back from day-to-day management, and he might start a new venture.
