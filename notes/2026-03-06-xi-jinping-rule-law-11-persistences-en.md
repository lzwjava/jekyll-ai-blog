Question: What are the main contents of Xi Jinping's Rule of Law Thought, and what does the correct answer "Eleven Persistences" (十一个坚持) include?

Answer:

## Xi Jinping's Rule of Law Thought — The "Eleven Persistences" (十一个坚持)

The main contents of Xi Jinping's Rule of Law Thought are embodied in the **"Eleven Persistences" (十一个坚持)**, which is the correct answer **D**.

---

### The Eleven Persistences in Full

| # | Content |
|---|---------|
| 1 | Persist in the **Party's leadership** over comprehensively governing the country according to law |
| 2 | Persist in taking the **people as the center** |
| 3 | Persist in the **socialist rule of law path with Chinese characteristics** |
| 4 | Persist in **governing the country and exercising state power according to the Constitution** (依宪治国、依宪执政) |
| 5 | Persist in advancing the **modernization of the national governance system and governance capacity** on the track of rule of law |
| 6 | Persist in building a **socialist rule of law system with Chinese characteristics** |
| 7 | Persist in jointly advancing **governing the country, governing the Party, and governing administration by law**, and building a law-based country, law-based government, and law-based society as one integrated whole |
| 8 | Persist in comprehensively promoting **scientific legislation, strict law enforcement, just administration of justice, and law observance by all** |
| 9 | Persist in **coordinating domestic rule of law and foreign-related rule of law** |
| 10 | Persist in building a **high-quality rule of law workforce** with both virtue and talent |
| 11 | Persist in **grasping leading cadres as the "key minority"** (关键少数) |

---

### Why the Other Options Are Wrong

- **A — 三个务必 (Three Must-Dos):** This belongs to the Party's self-governance requirements, not Rule of Law Thought.
- **B — 六个必须坚持 (Six Must-Persistences):** This refers to the core principles of **Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era**, not specifically Rule of Law Thought.
- **C — 七个着力 (Seven Key Efforts):** This is associated with economic work priorities, not Rule of Law Thought.

---

### Key Exam Tip
>
> The "Eleven Persistences" is the **core framework** of Xi Jinping's Rule of Law Thought, first systematically proposed at the **Central Conference on Comprehensively Governing the Country According to Law** in November 2020. It serves as the guiding ideology for China's rule of law construction in the new era.
