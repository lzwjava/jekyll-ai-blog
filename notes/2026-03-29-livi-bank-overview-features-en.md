livi bank (stylized as **livi**) is one of the eight virtual-only banks in Hong Kong. Launched in August 2020, it is a joint venture backed by three major corporate heavyweights, aiming to merge traditional banking reliability with modern digital convenience.

### ## Core Background & Ownership

Livi is distinct because of its strong shareholder base, which combines financial, technological, and retail expertise:

* **BOC Hong Kong (Holdings):** Provides banking operations and risk management expertise (44% stake).
* **JD Technology (formerly JD Digits):** Provides digital technology and online operation capabilities (36% stake).
* **Jardine Matheson Group:** Offers a massive retail footprint (including 7-Eleven, Wellcome, and Mannings) for ecosystem integration (20% stake).

---

### ## Key Features & Services

As of 2026, livi offers a range of services for both personal and business users, with a heavy emphasis on "lifestyle" banking.

#### **For Individuals (livi Personal)**

* **liviSave:** A savings account with tiered interest rates. It currently offers competitive rates (around **1.00% p.a.** for the first HKD 50,000) with no minimum balance requirements.
* **livi PayLater:** One of the first "Buy Now, Pay Later" (BNPL) services in Hong Kong, allowing users to split purchases into manageable installments directly via a virtual Mastercard.
* **Insurance:** Offers various plans, including travel and life insurance, often in partnership with BOCG Insurance.
* **Rewards & Gamification:** Uses a "Shake to Earn" feature and various shopping vouchers linked to the Jardine retail network.

#### **For Small Businesses (livi Business)**

* **100% Digital Onboarding:** Accounts can be opened fully via the app, often with approval in as little as 24 hours.
* **SME Financing:** Provides installment loans and is a participant in the government's **SME Financing Guarantee Scheme (SFGS)**.
* **Local Payments:** Supports HKD and CNY transfers via FPS (Faster Payment System).

### ## Who is it for?

Livi is ideal for users who prioritize **mobile-first banking**, frequent shoppers at **7-Eleven or Wellcome** (due to rewards integration), and **SME owners** who need quick, paperless business banking without the hassle of traditional branch visits.

> **Note:** Since livi is a virtual bank, you must be a Hong Kong resident (holding a HKID) or a Mainland resident with a valid permit to open a personal account.
