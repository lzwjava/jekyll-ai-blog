## Operating Principles

The company highlights three firm principles:

1. **No subcontracting** — they only take projects they can handle themselves; no passing work to third parties.
2. **On-site communication available** — they do project outsourcing but not staff augmentation (人力外包).
3. **No unclear projects** — they decline projects without a well-defined product plan, to avoid miscommunication and high education costs.
