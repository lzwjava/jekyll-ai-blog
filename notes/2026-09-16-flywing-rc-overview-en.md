**Question:** What is FLYWING RC, and what do they do?

**Answer:** FLYWING is an RC (radio-controlled) aviation company with a strong focus on making realistic flight accessible to hobbyists worldwide. Here's a concise overview:

**Founded & Background**
FLYWING was founded in 2018, focusing on the development of eVTOL fixed-wing UAVs, and is dedicated to creating a new form of flight entertainment experience. Driven by innovation, the company aims to transform flight from a professional tool into an immersive experience accessible to everyone.

**Products**
The company offers three main product categories:

- **Scale Helicopters** — highly realistic models such as the EC-135, Bell-412, MH-60R Jayhawk, UH-1 Huey, BO-105, Bell-206, and AS350
- **3D Helicopters** — aerobatic models like the FW450L for precision 3D performance
- **Fixed Wings** — including the X Wing Fighter, a high-performance VTOL FPV aircraft
- **Flight Controllers** — including the H1 Pro, ACE Autopilot, H2 Autopilot, and Rotorflight systems

**Mission**
Their mission is to create accessible and ultra-realistic flight experiences for aviation enthusiasts worldwide, and to let every flight awaken a profound sense of freedom deep within the soul.

**Core Values**
Quality First, Customer-Centric, Teamwork, and Continuous Innovation.

**Founder**
Luo Peng, founder of FLYWING, is an engineer driven by a deep passion for flight and control systems. To him, flight is not just about technology — it is about connecting people with space.

**Operations**
The company is located in the core industrial area of Dongguan (China), and operates a full production line covering mold development, composite material forming, precision machining, assembly, and final product debugging. They ship globally and have been featured in *Model Aviation* magazine.
