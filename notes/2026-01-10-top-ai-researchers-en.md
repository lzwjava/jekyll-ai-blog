## The "Godfathers of AI" (Turing Award Winners 2018)

Geoffrey Hinton, Yann LeCun, and Yoshua Bengio received the 2018 Turing Award for their breakthrough contributions to deep learning. Hinton is often called the "godfather of deep learning" and popularized backpropagation for training multi-layer neural networks in 1986.

## Transformer Architecture Pioneers

The "Attention Is All You Need" paper authors were Ashish Vaswani, Noam Shazeer, Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit, Llion Jones, Aidan Gomez, Łukasz Kaiser, and Illia Polosukhin. This foundational 2017 paper introduced the transformer architecture that powers modern large language models.

## GPT Development Leaders

Alec Radford independently built the GPT-1 model and led the development of GPT-2 as the first author, also playing a key role in CLIP and Whisper. Ilya Sutskever and Dario Amodei were instrumental in the creation of GPT-2 and GPT-3.
