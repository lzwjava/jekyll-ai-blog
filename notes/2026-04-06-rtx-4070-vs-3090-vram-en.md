Question: Is it true that the RTX 4070 (12GB) has too little VRAM compared to the RTX 3090 (24GB), and is the price difference on JD.com around 2,000–3,000 RMB?

Answer: **Yes, your assessment is largely accurate**, especially if you are using the card for **AI development, LLMs (Large Language Models), or high-resolution video editing.**

While the RTX 4070 is a much newer and more power-efficient card, the **12GB VRAM** is a significant bottleneck for specific professional workloads compared to the **24GB** found on the RTX 3090.

### 1. The VRAM Factor: Why it matters
* **AI & Deep Learning:** If you are running local LLMs (like Llama 3) or Stable Diffusion with high-resolution "ControlNet" layers, 12GB will often trigger "Out of Memory" (OOM) errors. The 24GB on a 3090 allows you to run much larger models or higher batch sizes.
* **Gaming:** At 1440p, 12GB is plenty. However, at 4K with "Ultra" textures in modern titles, you may start to hit the limit, though the 4070 isn't primarily a 4K powerhouse anyway.

### 2. Market Pricing (JD.com / China Market)
The price gap you mentioned (2,000–3,000 RMB) is the current "standard" for moving between these two tiers in the Chinese market:
* **RTX 4070 (New):** Generally stays in the **¥4,200 – ¥4,800** range depending on the brand.
* **RTX 3090 (Used/Refurbished):** Since the 3090 is no longer in mass production, "New" stock on JD is rare and overpriced. Most users look at "JD Second-hand" (京东二手) or specialized vendors where they go for **¥6,500 – ¥7,500**.
* **The Math:** A price jump of roughly **¥2,000 - ¥2,500** to double your VRAM and gain ~10-15% raw rasterization performance is common.

### 3. Comparison Table

| Feature | RTX 4070 | RTX 3090 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VRAM** | 12GB GDDR6X | **24GB GDDR6X** |
| **Architecture** | Ada Lovelace (Newer) | Ampere (Older) |
| **Power Draw** | **~200W (Very Efficient)** | ~350W+ (Requires beefy PSU) |
| **Special Tech** | DLSS 3 + Frame Gen | DLSS 2 |
| **Best For** | 1440p Gaming / Efficiency | **AI / 4K Gaming / 3D Rendering** |



### Summary Recommendation
* **Keep the 4070 if:** You mostly play games, care about your electricity bill, and want a card with a full warranty.
* **Switch to the 3090 if:** You are serious about **AI/Machine Learning** or heavy 3D production. In those fields, **VRAM is king**, and no amount of "new architecture" can compensate for having half the memory.
