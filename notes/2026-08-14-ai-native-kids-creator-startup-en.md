Question: How about a startup bringing frontier AI to kids/students in China/USA through communities, courses, paid groups, and parent networks?

Answer:

I think this is actually a very strong AI startup direction, but the key insight is:

**Don't sell "AI education". Sell "help my child become an AI-native creator before everyone else."**

Parents are not buying ChatGPT tutorials. They are buying a future advantage.

The market signal is already there: many companies are appearing around AI-native kids education, from AI coding/building programs to AI-first schools. ([Aidya][1])

The opportunity is similar to:

* 2010: parents paid for English classes
* 2015: parents paid for coding/Scratch/robotics
* 2025+: parents will pay for AI-native thinking + building

---

"Learn ChatGPT"

Too weak.

Parents think:

> "My kid can already ask ChatGPT. Why pay?"

## Biggest risk

The risk is becoming another "AI training school".

Low moat.

The stronger vision:

> "The first generation of AI-native builders."

Similar to how early coding schools sold:

"Your child will become a programmer."

Now:

"Your child will become someone who can command AI systems."

---

My view: this is one of the more realistic AI businesses because the buyer is clear (parents), the pain is strong (fear of children falling behind), and distribution through communities is cheap. The hard part is not AI technology; it is building trust, outcomes, and a parent network.
