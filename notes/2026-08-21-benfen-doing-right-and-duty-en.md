**Question:** What is the "Benfen" (本分) philosophy at vivo and OPPO, and how does it relate to the idea of "doing the right thing" or "doing what one should do"?

**Answer:**

Both vivo and OPPO share roots in the former BBK Electronics group (founded by Duan Yongping), and both carry forward a core philosophy called **Benfen (本分)** — a Chinese concept that sits at the heart of their corporate cultures.

**What Benfen means:**

"Benfen" describes the attitude of doing the right things *and* doing things right — which vivo describes as the ideal expression of its mission to create value for society.

It's more nuanced than simply "do the right thing." It carries the sense of staying within one's proper role, fulfilling one's duties honestly, and not overreaching — closer to "do what one ought to do" or "act within one's rightful place."

**For vivo:**

Benfen serves as a moral guide for vivo employees in their day-to-day actions. Every decision is bound by the duty to do the right thing, to be honest with and respectful to customers, business partners, and all third parties. Benfen also ensures that vivo practices meaningful innovation.

Vivo's core values include Benfen, user-orientation, design-driven value, continuous learning, and team spirit — all part of its sustainable development strategy.

**For OPPO:**

OPPO describes Benfen as its core philosophy of always doing the right thing and going beyond what is expected — a spirit expressed through a positive attitude, willingness to share, and commitment to exceptional service.

**The shared origin:**

Both vivo and OPPO trace back to BBK Electronics, the conglomerate founded by Duan Yongping in 1995. The Benfen philosophy is widely attributed to Duan himself, who instilled this value across the companies he built or inspired. It's essentially a Confucian-influenced business ethic: know your role, do your duty, don't cut corners, and don't chase things outside your proper scope.

In short, **Benfen ≈ integrity + duty + staying grounded in what you're supposed to do** — a deliberate contrast to the aggressive, short-term opportunism common in tech competition.
