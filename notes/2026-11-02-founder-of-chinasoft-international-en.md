## Founder of ChinaSoft International

### Dr. Yu Hong Chen (陈宇红) — Sole Founder & Current CEO/Chairman

**Yu Hong Chen** founded Chinasoft International Ltd. Presently, he is Chairman & Chief Executive Officer at the company. In the past, Dr. Chen was Senior Vice President at China National Software & Service Co., Ltd. and Deputy General Manager of China Great Wall Computer Group Co., Ltd. Yu Hong Chen received a **doctorate from Beijing University of Technology**.

He has held the **Founder** title since February 2000, making the company over 26 years old. He also serves as Compliance Officer and was previously a Director/Board Member (2011–2012) before becoming Chairman in May 2012.

His net worth is approximately **$184 million** (as of March 2024), and he holds roughly 10.51% of the company's shares — about 305 million shares.
