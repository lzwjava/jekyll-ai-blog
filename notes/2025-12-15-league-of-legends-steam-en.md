Question: Can you play League of Legends on Steam?

Answer:

No, you cannot play the main **League of Legends** (LoL) MOBA game natively on Steam. It is not available for purchase, download, or launch directly from the Steam store or library. Riot Games distributes LoL exclusively through their own **Riot Client**, which you must download from the official League of Legends website.

### Key Reasons

- **No Official Steam Release**: LoL has never been officially released on Steam as a current, supported title. There was a very brief, limited-time availability years ago (Steam App ID: 20590), but it only downloads an outdated, unplayable version of the game and is effectively obsolete—fewer than 3,000 people reportedly own it.
- **Riot's Policy**: Riot has repeatedly confirmed there are "no current plans" to bring LoL to Steam. They prefer their unified Riot Client for LoL, Valorant, and other titles.
- **Spin-Offs Only**: Steam does host Riot Forge spin-off games like *Ruined King: A League of Legends Story*, *CONVERGENCE: A League of Legends Story*, and *Bandle Tale*, but these are single-player RPGs, not the core multiplayer LoL experience.

### Workaround: Add as a Non-Steam Game

You **can** add LoL to your Steam library for convenience:

1. Install LoL via the Riot Client.
2. In Steam, go to **Games > Add a Non-Steam Game to My Library**.
3. Browse to the LoL executable (usually `C:\Riot Games\League of Legends\LeagueClient.exe`).
4. Add it, then launch from Steam to use features like the overlay, friends list, or controller support.

This doesn't make it a "Steam game"—it still requires the Riot Client and account—but it's a popular way to integrate it.

**Note**: On devices like Steam Deck, LoL faces additional hurdles due to Riot's Vanguard anti-cheat, which doesn't support Linux (SteamOS). Dual-booting Windows or remote play from a PC is required.
