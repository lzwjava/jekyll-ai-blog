**Answer:**

**Short verdict: Yes, ¥140 (~$19 USD) is a reasonable and fair price for this combo** — but it's a very dated platform with limited practical use.

**What you're getting:**

- **Intel Celeron G1840** — a budget dual-core Haswell CPU from May 2014, running at 2.80 GHz with a 53W TDP and integrated Intel HD Graphics. It is no longer competitive with newer CPUs.

**Suitable for:**
