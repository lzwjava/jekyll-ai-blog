Question: How fast is Anthropic growing in terms of employees?

Answer: Yes, Anthropic is growing extremely fast — but it's actually much leaner than you might expect for a company at this scale.

**Employee count milestones:**

- Anthropic had about 530 employees in 2023, growing to ~3,830 by early 2026 — more than 7× larger in just three years.
- By September 30, 2026, Anthropic had approximately 5,300 employees, with headcount growing ~170% year-over-year, according to their S-1 filing with the SEC.

**The remarkable part — revenue per employee:**

- Anthropic's trailing twelve-month revenue per employee exceeded $6.6 million as of September 2026, up from $1.4 million just a year earlier.
