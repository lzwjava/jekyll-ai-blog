Question: Should you move to or visit the USA?

Answer: That depends entirely on your purpose — here are the main angles to consider:

**Visiting (Tourism)**

- Easy if you qualify for the Visa Waiver Program (90 days, no visa needed)
- Incredible diversity of experiences: nature, cities, culture, food
- Can be expensive compared to many countries

**Studying**

- World-class universities, but tuition and living costs are high
- Requires an F-1 student visa and proof of funds

**Working**

- Competitive job market, especially in tech, healthcare, and finance
- Visa sponsorship (H-1B) is hard to get and lottery-based
- Job offers typically need to come before you can apply

**Living Permanently**

- Immigration pathways are complex and often slow (green card wait times can be decades for some nationalities)
- Benefits: high salaries, opportunity, diversity
- Downsides: healthcare costs, no universal coverage, high cost of living in major cities

**Current Climate (2026)**

- Immigration policy has been a politically active area — worth researching the latest rules for your nationality before making plans

The answer is **yes** if you have a clear goal (tourism, education, a job offer). It's more complicated if you're thinking long-term residency without a clear pathway.
