## 2. Your family situation changes the calculation

Your wife's mother being available to help with your daughters could make a big difference. But there is one important distinction: does moving to Beijing bring you closer to reliable family support, or farther away from it?

If your wife's mother lives in Shanxi, moving to Beijing might make visits easier than from Guangzhou, depending on the specific cities and travel arrangements. But that is not the same as having her live with you or provide daily childcare.

With a five-year-old and a one-year-old, you need to consider:

- Your wife's preferences: Does she genuinely want to move, and would she be happy raising the children in Beijing?
- Childcare: Could her mother help regularly, and for how many days each week?
- Education: Your older daughter will soon be entering primary school. Check school admission eligibility, hukou or residence requirements, and the local enrollment rules before committing.
- Your younger daughter: A one-year-old needs substantial daily attention. Your ability to work consistently depends on having a reliable childcare arrangement.
- Your marriage and family stability: A move that creates more opportunities for you but leaves your wife isolated or overwhelmed may not be a good trade.

I would make this a joint family decision, not just a career decision.

## 4. What I would do over the next 60 days

Weeks 1–2: Validate the opportunity

Identify 20–30 Beijing AI companies, research groups, and potential clients. Focus on model training, inference optimization, post-training, and agent infrastructure—not generic Java outsourcing. Apply and arrange technical conversations before moving.

Weeks 2–4: Test the family arrangement

Discuss the move with your wife and her mother. Establish where everyone would live, how childcare would work, and what the children would need for school and daily life.

Weeks 3–6: Calculate the economics

Compare actual after-tax income, rent, mortgage payments, childcare, transportation, and travel to Shanxi. Calculate how many months your current cash can support the family under each scenario.

Weeks 6–8: Make the decision

If Beijing produces real career or business traction, consider a trial period before moving everyone. If it produces little traction, continue building from Guangzhou rather than relocating on speculation.

## 5. My deeper view: choose the environment that compounds

Your long-term goal is not simply to live in Beijing. It is to become a stronger AI engineer and founder, build a sustainable business, and eventually access international opportunities.

Beijing could be a better base for the next stage of your technical development. Guangzhou could be a better base for controlling costs while you build products and consulting revenue. Neither city is inherently the right answer for every stage of your journey.

I would optimize for career opportunity per unit of family and financial risk. You can change cities later; you cannot easily recover money spent on an unsuccessful relocation or time lost to avoidable family stress.
