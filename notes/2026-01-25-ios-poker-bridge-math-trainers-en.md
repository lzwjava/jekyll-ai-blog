Question: What are great iOS app games about poker or bridge poker that involve guessing or calculating?

Answer:

Several excellent iOS apps fit your criteria for poker (or bridge, as "bridge poker" may refer to poker variants or bridge-style calculation games). These emphasize **guessing/estimating odds, equity, or hand strength** and **calculating probabilities**, often in interactive trainer or quiz formats that feel game-like with drills, quizzes, scores, levels, and progress tracking. They train skills like estimating equity vs. ranges, pot odds, outs, or GTO decisions without real-money play. Here's a curated list of the top ones based on user reviews, features, and expert recommendations:

### Top Poker Apps (Focus: Equity/Odds Guessing & Calculation)
- **PokerCruncher - Advanced Odds**
  Professional equity calculator with hand-vs-hand/range simulations (up to 10 players). Game-like analysis of flops/turns/rivers, heat maps, and stats (e.g., flop hit rates, combo draws). Use it to "guess" equities then verify—perfect for training intuition. One-time purchase (~$5-13 depending on version), offline, highly rated by pros.

- **Poker Odds Teacher**
  Explicitly teaches/guesses odds via quizzes on outs, counter-outs, pot/implied equity (Rule of 4&2). Generates random scenarios for practice—estimate mentally, then check. Feels like a game with timed drills. Pay once (~$2), offline, ideal for live poker math training.

- **Hold'em Odds Quizzer**
  Quiz game testing equity knowledge (preflop/postflop, vs. ranges/random hands, multi-player). Random questions, timers, A-F grades, scores saved—pure gamified training. Explains outs/counter-outs. Import to PokerCruncher for deeper calc. Pay once (~$2).

- **SnapShove** (Pro version recommended)
  Trainer for short-stack shoves/calls—guess optimal ranges, calc push/fold equities. Drills with scores; feels like a mobile game. Free basic, Pro (~$15) unlocks full calcs/trainers. iOS/Android.

- **Postflop+ GTO Poker Trainer** / **Preflop+**
  GTO drills: Guess actions/equities in solved spots (preflop/postflop), track ELO/progress. Quiz modes, achievements—addictive game feel. Subscription (~$10/mo), but drills target guessing weaknesses.

### Top Bridge Apps (Calculation/Guessing in Bidding & Play)
Bridge involves heavy bidding math (point counts, distributions) and play guessing (lines, probabilities)—like "poker" in trick-taking.

- **Bridge by NeuralPlay**
  Play vs. AI (6 levels), with **double dummy solver** (calc optimal lines/equity). Hints for bids/plays, review guesses vs. perfect calc. Bidding practice (SAYC/ACOL), stats—game modes like rubber/duplicate. Free with IAP.

- **bid72**
  Bidding puzzle game: Guess optimal bids on rated boards, compete globally (72-hour challenges). Explains calcs, tracks progress—pure calculation trainer. Free trial, sub (~$5/mo).

- **Tricky Bridge**
  Beginner-to-advanced lessons/challenges: Guess bids/plays, quizzes on strategy/math. 57 modules, competitions—fun, gamified learning. Free.
