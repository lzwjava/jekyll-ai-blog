## Question: Introduction to HKCOLO Limited (Hong Kong)

## Answer:

HKCOLO Limited is a Hong Kong-based **data centre and telecommunications infrastructure company** founded in **1999**. It specializes in **carrier-neutral colocation services**, network interconnection, cloud connectivity, and managed infrastructure services. ([HK Colo][1])

Unlike traditional IT outsourcing companies, HKCOLO operates the physical infrastructure layer that supports internet companies, telecom carriers, financial institutions, cloud providers, and enterprises. Its business is similar to companies such as Equinix, Digital Realty, and Telehouse. ([HK Colo][2])

### Core businesses

**1. Data Centre Colocation**

* Provides rack space, power, cooling, security, and operational support for customers' servers.
* Customers can place their own networking and computing equipment inside HKCOLO facilities. ([HK Colo][1])

**2. Carrier-Neutral Network Exchange**

* HKCOLO operates a large **Meet-Me-Room (MMR)** where multiple telecom carriers and network providers interconnect.
* This makes it a connectivity hub between Hong Kong, Mainland China, Asia, Europe, and America. ([HK Colo][1])

**3. Cloud Connectivity**

* Provides connections between enterprises and major cloud platforms.
* Supports hybrid cloud and private connectivity scenarios. ([HK Colo][2])

**4. Managed Infrastructure Services**
Services include:

* Remote hands / onsite engineering support
* Firewall management
* Backup services
* Network operation support ([HK Colo][3])

## Facilities

HKCOLO operates two major Hong Kong facilities:

* **Carrier Colocation Centre (Chai Wan)**
  A major carrier-neutral facility with connections to hundreds of customers and network providers. ([HK Colo][4])

* **Cloud Computing Complex (Tseung Kwan O)**
  A large-scale data centre facility designed for cloud companies, financial institutions, enterprises, telecom operators, and internet service providers. It is described by HKCOLO as a TIA-942 Rated 4/Tier 4 facility. ([HK Colo][4])
