**Question:** I called 10010 from my China Telecom phone, but they wouldn't handle my request — said I need to call from a Unicom number, and I couldn't get through to a human agent. How do I reach China Unicom support?

**Answer:**

This is a known annoyance: 10010 from a cross-carrier phone routes you to a generic IVR with limited menu options, and account-related actions get blocked because they can't verify you. Workarounds, in order of effectiveness:

**1. Dial with the area code prefix**

From your Telecom phone, dial `02010010` (Guangzhou) — i.e., `区号 + 10010`. This explicitly routes you to Guangdong Unicom's hotline instead of letting the network guess. Once connected, don't navigate the menu: repeatedly press `0`, or if it's the voice-assistant IVR, just keep saying **"人工客服"** / **"转人工"** — usually 2–3 repetitions forces a transfer.

**2. Skip the phone entirely — online human chat**

For a pre-sales question like "how do I get 黄金尊享版", these are actually faster and don't require a Unicom number:

- **中国联通 APP** → bottom tab 服务 → 客服 → type `人工` in the chat box. You can register/log in with a non-Unicom number for browsing and new-card orders.
- **WeChat 公众号 "中国联通客服"** → 在线客服 → type `人工`. No Unicom SIM needed.
- **10010.com** web chat, same trick.

**3. Escalation line**

If regular support stonewalls you, `10015` is Unicom's service-quality/complaint hotline — they answer cross-carrier calls and connect to humans much faster, since it's the line regulators watch.

**4. Offline**

For this specific plan, a 营业厅 visit with your 身份证 is often the cleanest path anyway — 流量王 tiers are frequently "online channel only" or "sold out" in the APP, and in-store staff can tell you which versions are actually available in your city right now (and whether the 280GB is permanent or promo).

Practical recommendation: do **#2 first** (APP chat, type 人工) since your question is pre-sales — they can answer eligibility and link you to the order page directly, no Unicom number required.
