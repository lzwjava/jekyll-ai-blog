### 1. GDP per capita comparison

Approximate nominal GDP per capita:

| City      | GDP/person | USD equivalent |
| --------- | ---------: | -------------: |
| Hong Kong |   ~HK$444k |          ~$57k |
| Shenzhen  |   ~RMB220k |          ~$30k |
| Guangzhou |   ~RMB155k |          ~$21k |

Sources: Hong Kong government reports GDP per capita around HK$444,044 in 2025; Shenzhen around RMB213k–220k; Guangzhou around RMB155k. ([Government of Hong Kong][1])

So:

* Hong Kong ≈ **2× Shenzhen**
* Hong Kong ≈ **2.5–3× Guangzhou**

But GDP per capita overstates the difference because:

* Hong Kong is a global financial center.
* A lot of GDP comes from finance, real estate, international services.
* Mainland cities have more manufacturing and lower-cost production.

## 2. Actual economy: what kind of wealth?

### Hong Kong

Economic engine:

* Finance
* Trading
* Logistics
* Professional services
* Real estate
* International headquarters

Very high productivity.

A software engineer, banker, lawyer, consultant can generate much higher economic output.

Example:

A Hong Kong engineer working on enterprise systems may bill:

* HKD 800–2000/hour consulting rates

while Shenzhen engineers are usually lower.

Hong Kong is basically a **global city**, similar to:

* Singapore
* London
* New York (smaller scale)

([Government of Hong Kong][1])

---

### Shenzhen

Shenzhen is different:

It is a **technology manufacturing + startup city**.

Strengths:

* Huawei
* Tencent
* DJI
* BYD
* hardware ecosystem
* electronics supply chain
* AI hardware

GDP is huge because of industrial capability.

Shenzhen is probably the closest mainland Chinese city to Hong Kong in terms of economic power. ([Shenzhen Municipal Government][2])

---

### Guangzhou

Guangzhou:

* traditional commercial center
* automobile
* trade
* manufacturing
* hospitals
* education
* services

Still wealthy, but less aggressive than Shenzhen.

# 3. Average people's life quality

This is where GDP becomes misleading.

## Housing

### Hong Kong

Biggest weakness.

Typical family:

* small apartment
* very expensive housing
* long mortgage/rent burden

Example:

A 60–80 sqm apartment can cost millions HKD.

Many middle-class people live in relatively small spaces.

---

### Shenzhen

Much better space.

A software engineer family can often afford:

* 80–120 sqm apartment
* newer communities
* better facilities

But housing is still expensive compared with salaries.

---

### Guangzhou

Best value among the three.

You get:

* larger apartments
* lower rent
* lower living cost
* good food
* better lifestyle space
