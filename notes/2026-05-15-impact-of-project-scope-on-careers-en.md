Question: In big corporations, is it similar to the outside world or startups, where accumulating experience and participating in many projects—or doing one big impactful project—affects your impact and value? Specifically, does working on multiple projects or one major project influence an AI engineer's promotion or an AI startup's valuation?

Answer:

Yes, the dynamics are conceptually similar but manifest differently depending on context. Let’s break it down:

### 1. **Big Corporations**

* **Multiple Projects vs. Single Big Project**

  * **Multiple Projects:** Demonstrates versatility, reliability, and broad impact. This is valuable if the company rewards cross-functional skills or if your career path benefits from visibility across teams.
  * **Single Big Project:** Can be career-defining if the project is high-profile and strategic. Success here often leads to recognition, promotion, or leadership opportunities because the company sees clear, measurable impact.
* **Impact Measurement:** Corporations usually quantify impact in terms of:

  * Revenue or cost savings
  * Efficiency or performance improvements
  * Strategic importance (e.g., AI model that drives major product innovation)
* **AI Engineers’ Promotion:** Promotions often depend on:

  * Technical depth (building or deploying complex AI systems)
  * Demonstrated impact (results delivered, adoption by business units)
  * Leadership and influence (mentoring, cross-team collaboration)

### 2. **Startups**

* **Multiple Projects vs. Single Big Project**

  * Startups are more sensitive to immediate, visible impact. One highly successful project can significantly raise the startup’s valuation.
  * Versatility helps too, because startup teams are small and everyone wears multiple hats. Being able to pivot across problems adds value.
* **Valuation Drivers:** Investors look for:

  * Product-market impact
  * Technology differentiation
  * Team execution and scalability

### 3. **Comparison**

| Factor                   | Big Corp                          | Startup                              |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Career impact            | Recognition, promotions           | Visibility, equity value             |
| Project scope            | Can be deep or broad              | Often broad due to limited resources |
| Multiple projects value  | Shows versatility, reliability    | Shows adaptability, speed            |
| Single big project value | High visibility, strategic impact | Can drive valuation jump             |
| AI engineer value metric | Promotions, raises, influence     | Equity, reputation, startup growth   |

**Bottom line:**

* In **big corps**, both approaches matter. Doing one big, high-impact AI project can accelerate promotion faster than several smaller contributions if it clearly drives business outcomes.
* In **startups**, flexibility and visible outcomes matter more; even small contributions that materially improve product or market fit can significantly raise your personal and company value.

Think of it like the “leverage effect”: one major lever in a large company can move the machine significantly, while in a startup, multiple small levers can each move the company forward and be equally visible to investors.
