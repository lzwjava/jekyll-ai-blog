**Question:** What are the popular AI generative video tools for WeChat Channel, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, etc. — especially open source?

**Answer:**

---

## 🔓 Open Source Models (Self-hostable)

| Tool | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Wan2.1** | A foundational open-source large-scale video generative model by the Wan team, providing high-quality video generation from text and images. |
| **CogVideoX** | An open source text/image/video-to-video generation project hosting the CogVideoX family of diffusion-transformer models with end-to-end tooling. |
| **Open-Sora** | An open-source initiative aimed at democratizing high-quality video production, offering a user-friendly platform that simplifies the complexities of video generation. |
| **Open Generative AI** | An all-in-one AI creation platform (MIT license, self-hosted) that integrates 200+ AI models — image, video, lip sync, cinema. Described as an open-source Runway + Pika + Midjourney + Kling combined. |
| **ComfyUI** | A free and open source node-based application for generative AI, enabling users to build, create, and share without limits. Great for custom video pipelines. |

---

## ✂️ Open Source Short-Form Automation (Long → Shorts)

| Tool | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **OpenShorts** | An open-source automatic shorts generator on GitHub. It burns in captions automatically and can optionally translate them into other languages for multi-market distribution. |
| **AI-YouTube-Shorts-Generator** | A Python-based tool that automates creation of short-form vertical video clips from longer source videos — ideal for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, or TikTok. |
| **short-video-factory** | An open-source tool that generates product-marketing videos from prompts and assets in a single click. |

---

## 💰 Paid / Commercial (Best Quality)

For TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts in 2026, **Google Veo 3.1** is the strongest all-around choice — native 9:16 vertical, 60-second clips, native audio.

**Kling 2.0** from Kuaishou (Chinese company) pushed real-time generation for social media and short-form content. **Google Veo 2** demonstrated state-of-the-art physical simulation and multi-shot consistency.

Other notable paid tools: **Runway Gen-3**, **Pika 2.5**, **CapCut AI** (good for WeChat/Douyin ecosystem).

---

## ⚠️ Reality Check

The biggest success story of AI video generation in 2026 is short-form content. However, maintaining consistency over longer durations remains problematic — most AI video tools still struggle beyond 30–60 seconds of coherent footage.
