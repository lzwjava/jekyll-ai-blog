Question: Can you completely unscrew the bottom oil valve/plug of a hydraulic jack?

Answer: **Generally, no — don't completely unscrew it while the jack is assembled and pressurized.**

The bottom valve/plug is part of the hydraulic oil circuit. If you remove it completely:

* 🛢️ **Hydraulic oil can leak out**
* Air can enter the hydraulic system
* The jack can lose pressure / stop lifting
* You may need to **refill and bleed** the jack afterward
* If the jack is carrying a load, it can be **dangerous**

Usually, the valve is **opened by turning it a limited amount** (often counter-clockwise) to release hydraulic pressure. You normally don't need to remove the valve completely.
