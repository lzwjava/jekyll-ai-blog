### 🌟 Why It Matters

Those Chinese tech bosses who do speak fluent English often attract more recognition and exposure in the West than some of their peers. Jack Ma is the archetype — he grew up in Hangzhou and at age 12 became interested in learning English, riding his bike 40 minutes each day to a hotel where foreign tourists stayed, giving free tours to practice, and continuing this for eight years.

As Chinese companies increasingly look towards overseas expansion, engagement with global customers has become a must-do thing for many top executives.

The most globally recognized Chinese executive today is arguably **Shou Zi Chew** — born and raised in Singapore, he received his education from a prestigious school and is fluent in both English and Mandarin, and represented ByteDance in high-stakes US Congressional hearings entirely in English.
