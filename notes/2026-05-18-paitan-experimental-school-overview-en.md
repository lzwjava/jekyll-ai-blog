Question: What is an introduction to 广州市增城区派潭镇实验学校 (Zengcheng District Paitan Town Experimental School) in Guangzhou?

Answer:

## 广州市增城区派潭镇实验学校 (Guangzhou Zengcheng Paitan Town Experimental School)

### Overview

The school was officially established on July 30, 2024. Starting from the 2024 academic year, Paitan Town's Second Middle School (派潭镇第二中学), Third Middle School (派潭镇第三中学), and First Primary School (派潭镇第一小学) were merged to form Zengcheng District Paitan Town Experimental School (增城区派潭镇实验学校).

### Location

The school is located in **Paitan Town (派潭镇)**, Zengcheng District, Guangzhou, Guangdong Province. Paitan is known as a provincial-level ecological demonstration town, situated in the northern part of Zengcheng, near the scenic Baishuizhai (白水寨) area.

### Affiliation with Education Group

On July 30, 2024, Paitan Town Experimental School was officially incorporated into the Zengcheng Guangfu Education Group (增城广附教育集团). During the ceremony, the group's leadership presented the school with the official "Zengcheng Guangfu Education Group" plaque.

The joining of the schools into the Zengcheng Guangfu Education Group is part of the district's efforts to promote balanced educational development, with hopes that the schools will benefit from sharing high-quality educational resources, including institutional development, campus culture, curriculum co-building, teaching research collaboration, and teacher mobility and training programs.

### Purpose of Establishment

The establishment of the Experimental School was driven by Paitan Town's efforts to efficiently integrate educational resources from the former Second and Third Middle Schools and First Primary School, helping to relieve enrollment pressure at the junior high level in the 2024 academic year. Infrastructure upgrade and renovation works have already officially commenced.

### Educational Vision

Going forward, the school aims to fully utilize the advantages of its education group membership, introduce advanced educational concepts, continuously strengthen teacher training, and improve teaching quality so that every student can receive a more comprehensive and high-quality education, laying a solid foundation for their future.

### Community & Campus Life

The broader Paitan education community has a vibrant campus sports culture. In collaboration with the Yixin Sports Baseball and Softball Town development project, schools in Paitan have established baseball and softball teams with regular weekly coaching sessions. Paitan Middle School and Paitan Central Primary School were recognized as 2023 National Youth Campus Football Featured Schools. Additionally, intangible cultural heritage activities such as the traditional game "Ji Rang" (击壤) have been introduced into school sports curricula.

In arts education, Paitan's schools have achieved notable results — including a first-prize winning children's dance drama "Lion Awakening" (醒狮), and Paitan Middle School being designated as a Guangzhou-level Intangible Cultural Heritage Inheritance Base (2024–2026).

### Broader Education Context

Paitan Town leaders stated that the incorporation of Paitan Experimental School into the education group is a key measure in Zengcheng District's "Hundred-Thousand-Ten Thousand Project" (百千万工程) to address uneven educational development and to help upgrade relatively weaker rural schools, addressing structural shortages in educational resources and overall improving teaching quality.
