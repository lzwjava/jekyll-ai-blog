Question: What are Jensen Huang’s quotes about GPU training?

Answer:

Here are some well-known quotes from Jensen Huang specifically related to GPUs, AI training, and accelerated computing:

---

### 🧠 On GPU vs CPU (core philosophy)

* “The more you buy, the more you save.”
  *(Referring to GPU scaling—buying more GPUs dramatically reduces training time and total cost in AI workloads.)*

* “GPUs are the instrument of AI.”
  *(Highlighting that GPUs are the fundamental tool enabling modern AI training.)*

* “Accelerated computing is going to replace general-purpose computing.”
  *(Meaning GPU-based architectures will dominate over traditional CPU-only systems for heavy workloads like training.)*

---

### ⚡ On AI training & compute demand

* “AI training is massively parallel, and that’s why GPUs are so effective.”

* “Deep learning training requires enormous computation. The more you buy, the faster you can go.”

* “The cost of computation is going down, but the demand for computation is going up even faster.”

---

### 🚀 On scaling & infrastructure

* “We are building the AI factories of the future.”
  *(Refers to GPU data centers dedicated to training large models.)*

* “A data center is now a unit of compute.”
  *(Not just servers—entire GPU clusters act as a single AI training engine.)*

---

### 🤖 On AI era shift

* “This is the iPhone moment of AI.”
  *(Indicating a breakthrough phase where GPU-powered AI becomes mainstream.)*

* “Generative AI needs enormous amounts of training compute.”

---

### 🏗️ On NVIDIA’s role

* “Our job is to build the computing platform for the age of AI.”

* “CUDA is the most important software we’ve ever created.”
  *(Refers to NVIDIA’s GPU programming ecosystem enabling training at scale.)*

---

### 🔍 Key idea behind all these quotes

Huang’s consistent message is:

* AI training = **massively parallel computation**
* GPUs = **best architecture for parallelism**
* Scaling GPUs = **faster + often cheaper overall training**
* Future = **data centers as giant GPU-powered AI factories**
