Question: Do ESTPs gain energy from interacting with people?

Answer: Yes. The "E" in ESTP stands for Extraversion, which in MBTI means drawing energy from the outside world: people, activity, and new experiences. ESTPs tend to feel recharged by socializing, hands-on action, and spontaneous, fast-moving situations. Long stretches of solitude or quiet routine often leave them restless or understimulated.

A few caveats:
- Even extraverts can get tired after too much social time, so the energy boost isn't unlimited.
- ESTPs often prefer lively, action-oriented interaction (activities, banter, shared experiences) over deep, drawn-out emotional conversations.
- MBTI describes preferences, not fixed rules. Individual ESTPs vary, and the framework has limited scientific support compared with models like the Big Five.
