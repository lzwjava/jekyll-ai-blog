**Question:** What is Monetag, and what should I know about it as a blogger?

**Answer:**

**What is Monetag?**

Monetag is a global ad network that has been operating since 2016, positioning itself as a performance-based advertising platform for publishers. It operates across more than 195 countries and works with around 50,000+ publishers. The network focuses on monetizing desktop, mobile web, in-app, and Telegram Mini App traffic for mainstream publishers such as news, entertainment, blogs, streaming, and social sites.

---

**Key Features**

- **MultiTag (flagship feature):** Monetag's MultiTag technology automatically combines top-performing ad formats like OnClick, Push Notifications, In-Page Push, Vignette, and Interstitials into one seamless tag, using AI-driven optimization to ensure the most relevant and highest-paying ads are displayed.

- **Ad Formats:** Monetag has one of the best ad unit selections for bloggers, offering banners, push notifications, and onclick formats.

- **No Minimum Traffic:** There is no minimum traffic requirement — instant approval for clean domains, with manual review taking under 24 hours. It rejects only incentivized or spam traffic.

- **Global Coverage:** Monetag has strong coverage even in Tier 2 and Tier 3 countries, with high CPM potential on push and pop formats — reports of $2–$10+ eCPM in good geographies.

- **Low Payout Threshold:** The minimum payout starts from $5, with weekly payments. Payment methods include PayPal, Wire, and WebMoney.

---

**Pros & Cons**

| ✅ Pros | ⚠️ Cons |
| --- | --- |
| No traffic minimum | Popunder ads can feel intrusive |
| Fast/instant approval | Earnings vary wildly by niche & geo |
| Works in 195+ countries | Not ideal for brand-safe, premium niches |
| Low $5 payout threshold | Some user complaints about CPM drops |
| AI auto-optimization via MultiTag | Lower RPMs than Mediavine or Raptive |

---

**Best For**

Many publishers run Monetag as a secondary or backup network and still see decent revenue uplift — especially bloggers with global or Tier 2/3 traffic who want quick setup and no approval barriers.
