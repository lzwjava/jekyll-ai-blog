Question: What CPU socket does the JGINYUE B85M-VDH use?

Answer: **LGA 1150**.

It supports **4th-generation Intel Haswell CPUs**, including:

```text
Socket: LGA 1150
Chipset: Intel B85
CPU examples:
  i3-4130
  i5-4590
  i7-4790
  Xeon E3-1231 v3
  Xeon E3-1240 v3
```

For a cheap **P100 test bench**, I'd pick a **Xeon E3-1231 v3 + 16 GB DDR3** if the price is close to the i5.
