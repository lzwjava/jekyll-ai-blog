Question: What is Hong Kong's online second-hand market?

Answer:

Hong Kong's online second-hand market is mainly these platforms:

### 1. [Carousell Hong Kong](https://www.carousell.hk?utm_source=chatgpt.com) (largest general marketplace)

Probably the closest equivalent to:

* China's 闲鱼 (Xianyu)
* US Craigslist / Facebook Marketplace

People sell:

* phones
* laptops
* cameras
* furniture
* watches
* clothes
* collectibles
* cars
* services

Typical usage:

```
Seller posts item
        ↓
Buyer chats
        ↓
Meet in MTR station / bank transfer
        ↓
Trade completed
```

Very common in Hong Kong.

---

### 2. [Hong Kong Golden Forum (HKGolden)](https://forum.hkgolden.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

An older Hong Kong internet community.

Has sections for:

* buying/selling
* electronics
* discussions
* local communities

It has a strong local Hong Kong user base.

---

### 3. Facebook Marketplace

Very popular for:

* furniture
* household items
* moving sales
* cars

Many Hong Kong people use Facebook groups for second-hand trading.

---

### 4. Luxury second-hand platforms

Hong Kong is very strong in:

* watches
* handbags
* jewelry

Examples:

* Rolex
* Patek Philippe
* Chanel
* Hermès

Because Hong Kong has a large luxury market.

---

### 5. Electronics trading

For computer hardware:

* GPUs
* MacBooks
* phones
* cameras

Hong Kong + Shenzhen creates a very active used electronics flow.

Places like Sham Shui Po have many offline shops, while online trading often happens through Carousell.

---

Compared with mainland China's Xianyu:

|                     | Hong Kong                 | Mainland China  |
| ------------------- | ------------------------- | --------------- |
| Main app            | Carousell                 | Xianyu          |
| Language            | Cantonese + English       | Mandarin        |
| Trust style         | Meet-up, reputation, chat | Platform escrow |
| Market size         | Smaller                   | Huge            |
| International users | More                      | Less            |

For a software/AI entrepreneur, Hong Kong's online second-hand market is interesting because it reflects the local culture: **small but efficient, high trust, internationally connected**. It is not as massive as China's market, but it is easier to understand and closer to global business habits.
