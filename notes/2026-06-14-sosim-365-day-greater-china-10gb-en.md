**Question:** What is the SoSIM 365-day Greater China 10GB card?**Answer:**

**SoSIM** is a prepaid eSIM/SIM brand launched by Hutchison Telephone Company Limited (3HK) in October 2020, providing contract-free prepaid SIM card services in Hong Kong.

The **Greater China 10GB / 365-day** plan specifically is part of their "Ai Travel SIM" product line:

- **Price:** HK$138 one-time, no monthly fee
- **Data:** 10GB roaming data usable in **mainland China, Macau, and Taiwan**
- **Validity:** 365 days after activation
- **Bonus:** Comes with FREE 5GB Hong Kong data (30-day)
- **Format:** eSIM — activate by scanning a QR code, set up in as fast as 10 minutes. No physical SIM needed, easy to switch networks, won't be physically lost or damaged.
- **Purchase:** Online via sosimhk.com

**Why it makes sense for your use case (occasional mainland roaming, no monthly commitment):**

- No recurring charges — pay once, use across a full year
- ~HK$13.8/GB effective cost for mainland data

- No VPN headache for basic connectivity in mainland (routed via HK carrier)

**Gotcha to know:** The 10GB is shared across mainland China + Macau + Taiwan for the full year, so it's genuinely only suitable for occasional/light usage — not daily work traffic.
