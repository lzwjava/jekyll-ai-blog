**Question: What is “普通护照签发服务指南” in China?**

**Answer:**

“普通护照签发服务指南” means **the official service guide for applying for a Chinese ordinary passport** (普通护照 = regular passport for Chinese citizens). It is a government document explaining the rules, requirements, documents, and procedures for getting a passport. ([Immigration Service Platform][1])

In China, passports are divided into several types:

* **普通护照** — ordinary passport, used by normal citizens for:

  * tourism
  * studying abroad
  * working abroad
  * visiting relatives
  * business trips
  * immigration/settlement
    (non-official purposes) ([GAT][2])
* **公务护照** — service/official passport
* **外交护照** — diplomatic passport

For most people, if you want to go to the US, Japan, Europe, etc., you need a **普通护照**.

The guide mainly covers:

### 1. Who can apply

Chinese citizens can apply if they go abroad for non-official reasons such as travel, study, employment, business, etc. Applications are handled by public security **exit-entry administration offices** (公安机关出入境管理机构). ([GAT][2])

### 2. Basic documents

Usually:

* Chinese ID card (身份证)
* application form
* passport photo meeting requirements

The official requirements list these basic materials. ([National Immigration Administration][3])

### 3. Passport renewal/replacement

You can apply for a new passport if, for example:

* visa pages are almost full
* passport validity is less than 6 months
* personal information changed
* passport is lost/damaged ([Gat][4])

### 4. Where to apply

You go to:

> 公安局出入境管理大厅
> (Public Security Bureau Exit-Entry Administration)

Many cities allow applications outside your household registration location. The responsible authority is the local exit-entry administration department authorized by the National Immigration Administration. ([Immigration Service Platform][1])

For example, in Guangzhou:

* search **“广州公安 出入境”**
* make an appointment
* bring ID
* take photo/fingerprint
* submit application
* wait for passport issuance

In English, the phrase would be:

> **Service Guide for Issuing Ordinary Passports of the People's Republic of China**

It is basically the Chinese government's "how to get a passport" manual. ([Immigration Service Platform][1])
