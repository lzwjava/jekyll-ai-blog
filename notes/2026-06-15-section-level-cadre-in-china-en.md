**Question:** What does "科级干部" mean?

**Answer:**

"科级干部" refers to a **section-level cadre** (官员/干部) in China's civil service hierarchy. It's the entry-level tier of formal leadership in the Chinese bureaucratic system.

## The Hierarchy (bottom to top)

| Level | Chinese | English Equivalent |
|-------|---------|-------------------|
| 科级 | 科级干部 | Section level |
| 县处级 | 处级干部 | Division/County level |
| 厅局级 | 局级干部 | Bureau/Department level |
| 省部级 | 省级干部 | Provincial/Ministry level |
| 国家级 | 国级干部 | State/National level |

## What it means practically

- Manages a **科 (section/unit)** — smallest formal administrative unit in a government agency or SOE
- Typical roles: section chief (科长), deputy section chief (副科长)
- In a company/bank context, roughly equivalent to team lead or junior manager
- In academia/SOEs, department heads at small units often hold this rank

In your banking context, if you see this in HR or org chart discussions, it's someone at the **lowest rung of named leadership** — above staff, below division director.
