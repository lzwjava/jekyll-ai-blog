**Question:** Which server providers similar to dmit.io support CN2 GIA?

**Answer:**

Several VPS and cloud server providers offer **CN2 GIA** (China Telecom's premium Global Internet Access routing) for optimized, low-latency connectivity to mainland China, similar to **DMIT.io** (which specializes in premium CN2 GIA in locations like Los Angeles, Hong Kong, and Tokyo).

Here are some popular and frequently recommended alternatives (often mentioned alongside DMIT for China-optimized performance):

- **BandwagonHost (搬瓦工)** — One of the most well-known for CN2 GIA lines, especially in Los Angeles (with triple-carrier optimization: CN2 GIA, CMIN2, and Unicom Premium). It offers stable performance but may lack built-in DDoS protection compared to DMIT.
- **GigsGigsCloud** — Provides CN2 GIA in US (Los Angeles), Hong Kong, and Japan locations, with good DDoS protection and premium China routing (often including 9929 and CMI). Popular for balanced speed and security.
- **HostDare** — Focuses on China-optimized CN2 GIA VPS (including NVMe storage and KVM), primarily in Los Angeles and Japan, with strong emphasis on low latency to China Telecom/Unicom/Mobile.
- **Misaka Network** — Frequently cited as a solid CN2 GIA option (sometimes reselling or similar to DMIT infrastructure), known for reliability in premium China routing.
- **xTom / V.PS** — Offers premium lines including CN2 GIA, 9929, and CMIN2 in various locations, often recommended for good connectivity to China.

Other mentions in discussions include providers like AkkoCloud or VMRack for triple-network CN2 GIA setups, but the top consistent alternatives to DMIT are BandwagonHost, GigsGigsCloud, and HostDare.

These providers typically feature in US West Coast (LA/San Jose) or Asian (HK/Japan) data centers for the best China performance. Prices and exact routes vary—CN2 GIA is premium, so expect higher costs than standard lines. Always check current plans, test IPs (looking glasses), and reviews for your specific use case (e.g., latency from your location in China).
