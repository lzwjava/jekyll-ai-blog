Question: Which operating system can be freely modified and redistributed by any person or organization as long as they comply with the GNU General Public License (GPL)?

Answer:
The correct answer is **D: Linux**.

### Explanation

* **Linux** is an open-source operating system licensed under the **GNU General Public License (GPL)**. Under the terms of the GPL, anyone is free to inspect, modify, and redistribute the source code, provided that any modified versions are also made available under the GPL.
* **UNIX** (Option A) is a proprietary operating system originally created by AT&T Bell Labs. While Linux is a Unix-like operating system, original UNIX source code is strictly proprietary and copyrighted rather than distributed under the GPL.
* **NetWare** (Option B) and **Windows** (Option C) are proprietary, closed-source operating systems owned by Novell (Micro Focus/OpenText) and Microsoft, respectively.
