Question: **What’s a global GPU repair brand?**

Answer: If you mean **a globally recognized company specializing in GPU repair**, there isn't really a dominant worldwide brand comparable to NVIDIA/ASUS/MSI. GPU repair is still highly fragmented.

The closest examples I found:

* **GPU Doctor** — probably the most brand-like/global candidate. Claims facilities on **three continents**, component-level repair, and support for NVIDIA/AMD/Intel GPUs. ([GPU Doctor][1])
* **GPU Lab** — focuses on professional GPU repair and unusual upgrades such as **RTX 4090 24GB → 48GB VRAM**, and explicitly says it ships globally. ([GPU Lab][2])
* **Megafix** — component-level repair covering NVIDIA/AMD cards from ASUS, MSI, Gigabyte, Zotac, Palit, Sapphire, etc. ([Megafix][3])
* **Shenzhen repair shops** — actually a significant part of the high-end AI GPU repair market. Reuters reported shops repairing **hundreds of A100/H100-class GPUs per month**, with repairs around $1,400–$2,800. ([Reuters][4])

If you're asking because you're thinking about **building a global GPU repair brand**, I'd say the market is surprisingly open: there is no obvious "Foxconn/AppleCare of GPU repair" yet. **GPU Doctor is closer to that positioning than the others**, but it doesn't appear to have dominant global mindshare. ([GPU Doctor][5])
