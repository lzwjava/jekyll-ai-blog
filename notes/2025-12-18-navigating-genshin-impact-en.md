**Question:** In Genshin Impact, I'm lost and don't know what to do.

**Answer:**

Don't worry—Genshin Impact's massive open world can feel overwhelming, especially with hundreds of hours of content across regions like Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan, and the latest Nod-Krai area in version 6.2 (as of December 2025). Being "lost" usually means you're unsure of your next steps in quests, progression, or exploration. Here's a step-by-step guide to get unstuck and back on track, tailored for beginners to mid-game players (Adventure Rank 1–50+). Prioritize these in order for steady progress.

### 1. **Check Your Quest Log Immediately (Most Common Fix)**
   - Open the **Quest Menu** (press F3 on PC, or the book icon on mobile/console).
   - Look at **Current Quests** tab:
     - **Archon Quests** (gold icons): Main story—do these first! They unlock new regions, characters, and features. Current latest: *Song of the Welkin Moon: Act V - A Nocturne of the Far North* and Act VI (requires AR28+ and prior acts).
     - **World Quests** (blue icons): Side stories with great rewards (Primogems, materials).
     - **Story Quests** (purple icons): Character-specific stories—unlock after meeting them in Archon Quests.
   - Follow quest markers on your minimap (yellow ! or lines). Talk to NPCs with speech bubbles.
   - **Pro Tip**: Talk to **Paimon** (your companion)—she often gives hints like "Let's check the quest log!" or points to objectives.

### 2. **Use the Adventurer's Handbook (Your Daily To-Do List)**
   - Open it via the icon next to the minimap (looks like an open book).
   - Complete **Experience Chapters** for easy AR (Adventure Rank) EXP:
     | Category          | What to Do                          | Rewards                  |
     |-------------------|-------------------------------------|--------------------------|
     | **Daily Commissions** (AR12+) | 4 random tasks/day from Adventurers' Guild (Katheryne in major cities). Claim 60 Primogems daily. | Primogems, Mora, EXP    |
     | **Exploration**   | Unlock waypoints, Statues of The Seven, find Anemoculi/Geoculi/etc. | Stamina, Oculi for levels |
     | **Challenges**    | Domains, Ley Lines (for Mora/HERO EXP), Bosses. | Materials for leveling  |
   - Aim for 100–200 AR EXP daily here—raises World Level for better drops.

### 3. **Explore Actively to Gain Power**
   - Open the **Map** (M key or icon):
     - Teleport to nearest waypoint (blue pillars) if literally lost.
     - Hunt for chests (sparkly spots, follow Seelies—glowing blue orbs), puzzles (pressure plates, elements), and oculi (region-specific orbs).
     - 100% exploration in a region = free wishes (Primogems).
   - **Priority Farms**:
     - **Ley Lines** (blooming flowers on map): Spend Resin for Mora/Character EXP.
     - **World Bosses** (e.g., Stormterror): 40 Resin, drops ascension materials.
     - Save **Fragile/Original Resin** (max 160 + 5/hour) for these—don't waste on low-level stuff.
   - Goal: Level main team to 40–60, weapons to 50–70, unlock talents.

### 4. **Build a Strong Team (Avoid Power Walls)**
   - Use free starters: Traveler (switch elements at Statues), Amber/Barbara for healing, Xiangling/Kaeya from events.
   - **Leveling Priority**: 1 main DPS > Supports > Weapons > Talents (to 6–8 first).
   - Food buffs (cook via menu) for HP/ATK during tough fights.
   - If underpowered: Fail a domain on purpose for feedback, then grind Ley Lines/Bosses.

### 5. **Daily Routine for Long-Term Progress**
   ```
   1. Log in → Claim rewards (events, mail).
   2. Dailies/Commissions (10–15 mins).
   3. Resin dump (Ley Lines/Bosses/Domains).
   4. 1–2 Archon/World Quests.
   5. Explore 30 mins for chests/waypoints.
   6. Events (check Paimon's Adventures tab)—free pulls/characters.
   ```
   - Save Primogems for banners (Standard or limited characters you like).

### 6. **If Still Stuck on a Specific Quest/Boss**
   - Restart game or check Settings > Resources > Verify Integrity.
   - Search "Genshin [Quest Name] guide" (e.g., "Lost in the Sands guide").
   - Communities: r/Genshin_Impact (Reddit), HoyoLab forums.
   - Provide more details (AR, active quest, region) for targeted help!

Follow this, and you'll hit AR45+ quickly with a clear path. The game shines in exploration and story—take it slow, enjoy the views, and glide everywhere. You've got this!
