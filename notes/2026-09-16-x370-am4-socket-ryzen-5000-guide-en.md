Question: What CPU socket does the X370 chipset use?

Answer: **AM4**.

For the **Gigabyte X370 Gaming K3**:

```text
Socket: AM4
Chipset: AMD X370
CPU families:
  Ryzen 1000
  Ryzen 2000
  Ryzen 3000
  Ryzen 4000G*
  Ryzen 5000*
```

The `*` depends on the motherboard BIOS version. Since the seller claims they've updated the BIOS for Ryzen 5xxx, a **Ryzen 5000 CPU such as 5600/5700X/5900X** should be possible, assuming that specific BIOS really is installed.

For a **2× RTX 4070** build, I'd lean toward a **Ryzen 5700X or 5900X** rather than an older Ryzen CPU.
