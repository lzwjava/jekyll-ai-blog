### 1. The "Mental Library" Effect
For someone like Yann LeCun, who has been at the forefront of Deep Learning since the 1980s, discussing advanced topics is like speaking a native language.

### 3. Speech as a "Living Paper"
Your observation about LeCun's speech-to-text quality is spot on. Many top-tier academics develop a **formalized internal monologue**.
* They define their terms before using them.
* They qualify their statements (using "likely," "under these constraints," or "statistically significant").
* They structure arguments with "firstly," "secondly," and "consequently."

### 4. Continuous Exploration
What keeps them sharing is often a genuine **intellectual curiosity**. In the AI industry, things move so fast that even the "godfathers" of the field must remain students.
