### **Overview and History**
- **Founding and Headquarters**: IBM (International Business Machines Corporation) was incorporated in 1911 as the Computing-Tabulating-Recording Company, consolidating three smaller firms specializing in punch-card tabulators and office products. Its headquarters are in Armonk, New York.
- **Market Position**: IBM is a leading American computer manufacturer, holding a major share of the market both in the U.S. and globally. It has been a pioneer in the tech industry for over a century, evolving from electromechanical calculators to modern computing and AI systems.
