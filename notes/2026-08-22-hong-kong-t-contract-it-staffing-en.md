Question: What is the T-Contract in Hong Kong?

Answer: The **T-Contract** is a centrally administered government IT staffing programme in Hong Kong. Here's a breakdown:

**What It Is**

Various policy bureaux and departments of the HKSAR Government may, through a term contract centrally administered by the Office of the Government Chief Information Officer (OGCIO), engage contractors to employ IT contract staff — known as **T-contract staff** — to provide IT services.

**How It Works**

By means of T-Contracts, bureaux and departments can obtain IT staff services from a list of pre-approved contractors within a short period of time. These contractors are selected through an open tendering exercise, and the arrangement allows bureaux/departments to enjoy maximum flexibility as the duration of IT services required can vary to meet operational needs.

**Current Contract (T26)**

The current T26 contract is a 48-month term that started on 1 February 2023, under which the government has awarded standing offer agreements to T-contractors to provide IT contract staff on an as-and-when-required basis.

**Job Categories**

The T-contract framework defines a comprehensive ladder of IT roles — from entry-level IT Assistants managing daily system operations and helpdesk support, to Junior Programmers handling development tasks, all the way up to Senior Project Managers — with clear responsibilities, experience standards, and career pathways.

**Role of CL Technical Services**

CL Technical Services Limited is one of the authorized contractors and has been awarded as a T-contract supplier to the HKSAR Government since 2005.
