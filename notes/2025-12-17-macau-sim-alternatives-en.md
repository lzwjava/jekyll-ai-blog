Question: What other good options are there for mainland China users to access the global unrestricted internet, similar to the China Telecom Macau Easy SIM card?

Answer:

The China Telecom Macau Easy SIM card provides cheap daily unlimited data in mainland China via roaming, with traffic routed through Macau, bypassing the Great Firewall (GFW) for unrestricted access to global sites/apps without needing a VPN.

Similar good alternatives for mainland China users include:

- **Hong Kong roaming SIM cards**: These route data through Hong Kong networks, bypassing the GFW.
  - China Mobile Hong Kong (CMHK) or Three HK (3HK) Greater Bay Area plans: Affordable daily/monthly unlimited or high-data roaming in mainland China + Hong Kong + Macau.
  - CSL or China Unicom Hong Kong prepaid SIMs with China roaming packages: Often available at airports/convenience stores in HK, with options for daily unlimited data.

- **International travel eSIMs**: Easy to buy online before travel, activate instantly, and many route traffic internationally (bypassing GFW automatically, no separate VPN needed).
  - Popular providers: Airalo, Nomad, Holafly (unlimited data), Saily (from NordVPN team, with privacy features), aloSIM, Jetpac, or Trip.com eSIMs.
  - These offer plans from a few GB to unlimited, often cheaper per day than physical SIMs for short trips, and work on China Unicom/China Mobile networks in mainland.

- **Other regional roaming physical SIMs**: Similar to Macau, buy a prepaid SIM in Hong Kong during a visit (easy for mainland users via high-speed rail or ferry) for ongoing use.

These options provide reliable speeds in cities, though rural coverage varies. eSIMs are most convenient if your phone supports them. Always check current plans, as offerings change.
