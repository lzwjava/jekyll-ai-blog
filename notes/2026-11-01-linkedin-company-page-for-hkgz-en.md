**Question:** How to create a LinkedIn company page for a Hong Kong or Guangzhou company?

**Answer:**

## ⚠️ Important Note First

**Guangzhou (Mainland China):** LinkedIn is **blocked in mainland China** since 2021 (professional networking features). You'd need a VPN to access it, or manage the page from outside China. The company can still *have* a LinkedIn page, but accessing it from Guangzhou is restricted.

**Hong Kong:** LinkedIn is **fully accessible** — no restrictions apply.

---

## Prerequisites

You need a **personal LinkedIn account** with a verified email address and a company email address to create a LinkedIn company page.

Your personal profile must be **at least 7 days old** before you can create a company page.

---

## Step-by-Step: Create the Page


**Step 1 — Access Company Page Creation (Desktop)**
1. Log into your personal LinkedIn account
2. Click the **Work icon** (9 dots, top right)
3. Select **"Create a Company Page"** from the dropdown



**Step 2 — Choose a Page Type**
- **Company** — for most businesses (choose this)
- **Showcase Page** — a sub-page connected to an existing company page (not for new setup)



**Step 3 — Fill in Company Details**
- Company name
- LinkedIn URL
- Company website
- Industry, company size, company type
- Logo and description



**Step 4 — Verify & Submit**
Check the verification box, which affirms that you have the authority to create and take ownership of the page, then click **Create Page**.


---

## Tips for HK / China Companies

| Item | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Company name** | Use English + optionally Chinese (e.g., "ABC Tech 科技") |
| **Logo** | 300×300 px |
| **Banner** | 1128×191 px |
| **Language** | Set page language to English for global reach |
| **Email** | Use your company domain email (e.g., you@yourcompany.com) for credibility |

---

## Mobile Option

On iOS or Android, within the LinkedIn app, navigate to any existing company page, tap the three-dot menu (…) and select **"Create a LinkedIn Page+"**, then follow the same data entry steps as the desktop version.
