**Question:** Who is the person who was Zynga's China CEO/GM, later entered the live streaming business, and previously worked at Google?

---

**Answer:**

The person is **Andy Tian (田行智)**.

Here is a summary of his career:

- **Google (2005–2007):** From 2005 to 2007, Andy led Google's mobile business in China.

- **Zynga China:** Andy was GM of Zynga China, operating several top 10 mobile games in 15 languages globally. He later departed when Zynga confirmed the loss of Andy Tian, the head of Zynga China.

- **Live Streaming — Asia Innovations Group (AIG):** Andy is the CEO and co-founder of Asia Innovations Group (AIG), a leading mobile social entertainment group in Asia. AIG's flagship product, UP Live, has become the highest revenue international live video app since launching in June 2016. UP Live reached #1 in Taiwan, HK, and the Middle East.

- **Education:** Andy graduated from MIT with BS and MS in Computer Science, and immigrated to the US at age 10.

In short, Andy Tian's career path was: **MIT → Google China (mobile) → Zynga China GM → Co-founder & CEO of AIG / UP Live (live streaming).**
