Question: How many submarine cables connect mainland China directly to Hong Kong? Is it 7 cables? What are they, and which are the shortest?

Answer:

## Submarine Cables Connecting Mainland China to Hong Kong

Your estimate of 7 is a reasonable approximation, but the actual picture is a bit more nuanced. The answer depends on whether you count cables that **directly and exclusively** link mainland China to Hong Kong, or cables that **touch both** mainland China and Hong Kong as part of a larger multi-country network.

---

### Overall Context: Hong Kong's Cable Connections

As of July 2025, Hong Kong has twelve international submarine cable systems in service: AAE-1, AAG, ADC, APCN-2, APG, ASE, EAC-C2C, FNAL/RNAL, H2HE, SJC, SJC2, and TGN-IA. Of these, several have landing points on both the Hong Kong side and the mainland China side.

---

### The Shortest: H2HE

The Hainan to Hong Kong Express (H2HE) is a 675 km submarine cable system connecting Hong Kong SAR and Hainan Province, Mainland China, with a branch to Guangdong Province. It is by far the **shortest and most direct** cable between the two territories.

H2HE is also notable for being the world's first 16-fibre-pair repeatered submarine cable system, achieving a system capacity of over 300 Tbps, which is sufficient to transmit 57,000 high-definition movies every second.

H2HE was put into service in September 2021 and is operated by China Mobile.

---

### Key Details on Other Notable Cables

- **ADC (Asia Direct Cable):** ADC is a 9,400-kilometre submarine cable connecting Hong Kong with mainland China, Japan, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, and Vietnam. With eight pairs of optical fibres, it carries traffic above 140 Tbps.

- **APCN-2:** The 19,000 km-long APCN-2 cable system is built in a self-healing ring configuration, connecting Hong Kong to Chongming and Shantou in mainland China, along with other Asia-Pacific countries.

- **EAC-C2C:** The EAC-C2C network spans 36,800 km total, with 17 cable landing stations covering Japan, Korea, China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, the Philippines, and Singapore.

- **SEA-H2X (newest):** The SEA-H2X cable connects Hong Kong SAR, Hainan China, the Philippines, Thailand, and Singapore, with a design capacity of 200 Tbps across 8 fiber pairs, and was targeted to be ready for service by end of 2025.

### Summary

- **Your count of ~7 is essentially correct** for cables that have landing stations in both mainland China and Hong Kong.
- **Shortest cable:** H2HE at **675 km**, directly linking Hainan Province and Guangdong to Hong Kong.
- Most of the other cables are multi-country regional or international systems that happen to connect both territories as part of a larger network, with total lengths ranging from ~6,000 km to over 36,000 km.
