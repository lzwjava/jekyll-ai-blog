**Question:** Is Faye Wong's "As You Wish" (如愿) worth listening to?

**Answer:**

Yes. It's the theme song for the 2021 film "My Country, My Parents," with lyrics that honor the revolutionaries who died founding the PRC. The song works on two levels — as patriotic reflection and as pure vocal performance.

The songwriting is strong: poetic imagery ("the moon and cool breeze," "the child walking in your eyes"), layered emotional arc. It has 32.7M streams on Spotify, reasonable for a 2021 single.

More importantly, it's **Faye Wong** — one of the most technically accomplished singers in Mandarin pop. If you care about vocal control, phrasing, and emotional delivery, her version is worth the 4:25. She doesn't oversing it.

**Listen if:** you appreciate skilled singing, poetic lyrics, or context about how contemporary Chinese pop treats historical/patriotic themes.

**Skip if:** you want something sonically experimental or upbeat — it's introspective, chamber-like production.
