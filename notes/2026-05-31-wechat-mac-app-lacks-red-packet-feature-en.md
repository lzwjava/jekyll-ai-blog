**Question:** Can the WeChat Mac desktop app send red packets (红包)?

**Answer:**

Correct — the WeChat Mac desktop client does **not support sending red packets**. This is a long-standing limitation of the desktop version.

What works on Mac desktop:
- Receiving red packets (you'll see them in chat, but must open on mobile)
- Text, files, voice messages, stickers

What doesn't work on Mac desktop:
- Sending red packets
- Sending money transfers (转账 is also limited/absent)
- Some mini-program interactions

**Workaround:** Use your iPhone or Android WeChat to send red packets. There's no official Mac workaround — Tencent has kept financial features locked to mobile, likely for security/auth reasons (mobile has biometric + phone number binding).

If you need to do it from a computer, WeChat Web (`web.wechat.com`) also doesn't support red packets. Mobile only, full stop.
