This is a really interesting and ambitious strategy — here’s my honest take:

**The good:**
- **Dedicated experimentation** — Having a third phone (Android) alongside two iPhones lets you explore app ecosystems freely without cluttering your primary device.
- **AI as a force multiplier** — With AI tools, you can rapidly test, summarize, and compare thousands of apps, learning features that would take years manually.
- **The “enjoy 10,000 things, share 1,000 best” idea** is generous and valuable — many people stick to 10 apps, missing hidden gems you could surface.
- **Your mindset** — seeing the worth despite inconvenience, recognizing others waste time/money while you create a curated path — that’s smart.

**Potential challenges:**
- **Management overhead** — 300–500 apps per phone means updates, notifications, permissions, and clutter. You’ll need a good folder/tag system.
- **Actual usage** — Even with AI help, deeply learning thousands of apps is near impossible. Consider rotating 50–100 active apps every few months.
- **Battery & syncing** — Carrying three phones daily might get heavy, and keeping data/accounts in sync across iOS & Android could be tedious.

**Practical suggestions:**
1. **Assign roles** — one iPhone for communication/productivity, Android for AI/experimental apps, second iPhone for media/creation.
2. **Use AI to filter** — let AI quickly map app categories (note-taking, AI image, automation, privacy tools…) then go deep only on top 5–10 per category.
3. **Share publicly** — your “1000 best” could become a newsletter, database, or YouTube channel. People would follow a human + AI review system.
4. **Lighten the load** — try a crossbody phone pouch/belt instead of pockets, or rotate which third phone you take daily.

**Final thought:**
Welcome, bro. This *is* unusual, but unusual is where innovation lives. If you truly enjoy discovering and sharing, then a little extra weight in your bag is a small price. Just stay disciplined — otherwise 10,000 apps will own you instead of you enjoying them.
