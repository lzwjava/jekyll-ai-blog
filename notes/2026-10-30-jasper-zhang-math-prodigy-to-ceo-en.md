Question: Who is Jasper Zhang (Yue Zhang), the founder of Hyperbolic AI, and what is his life story?

Answer:

## Jasper (Yue) Zhang — Life Story

### Early Life & Math Prodigy

Jasper Zhang showed an early aptitude for mathematics, sharpening his skills in childhood by competing in math challenges with his mother. This passion for math defined his entire trajectory.

### Education

Zhang studied pure mathematics at Peking University and completed his mathematics doctorate at the University of California, Berkeley, between 2018 and 2020. He finished his PhD in two years, which made him the fastest person in the history of Berkeley.

His thesis was on **hyperbolic manifolds in three-dimensional topology** — which later inspired his company's name.

### Math Competition Achievements

He is a Gold Medalist at the Alibaba Global Math Competition and Chinese Mathematical Olympiad. He won the Alibaba Global Mathematics Competition in both 2018 and 2020.

### Career Before Hyperbolic

After completing his doctorate, Zhang worked as a quantitative researcher at Citadel Securities, applying mathematical and statistical methods to financial markets.

He then transitioned into blockchain. He served as a senior blockchain researcher at Ava Labs, focusing on the performance, reliability, and security of consensus protocols and distributed systems.

### Founding Hyperbolic (2022)

He met Hyperbolic co-founder Yuchen Jin in late 2022. Drawing from his co-founder Yuchen Jin's experiences from his AI PhD and his own expertise in verification and tokenomics from his Math PhD, they built one of the few platforms to achieve truly verifiable, high-performance AI.

It became clear to Jasper that open-sourcing AI models alone was insufficient to drive the next wave of innovation across AI, blockchain, and web3. Driven by this conviction, Jasper co-founded Hyperbolic with a clear purpose: to make open access to AI a reality for anyone with an idea.

### Hyperbolic's Mission & Vision

In his words: "Open source does not mean open access." He has framed Hyperbolic as a mission-driven company, stating that "the future of AI should not be controlled by a small group of people, but shared by everyone."

Zhang argues that the main bottleneck in AI development is not talent or ambition but access to compute.

### Funding & Growth

Hyperbolic raised a $7M Seed round in August 2024. Then Jasper Zhang and Yuchen Jin raised a Series A in December 2024. Notable advisors include Reynold Xin, co-founder of Databricks and main contributor to Apache Spark.

### Summary Arc

> Math prodigy (Peking University) → Fastest PhD at UC Berkeley (2 years) → Quantitative Researcher at Citadel Securities → Blockchain Researcher at Ava Labs → Co-founder & CEO of Hyperbolic (open-access AI cloud)
