Question: What are some unpopular opinions about autonomous driving?

Answer:

Here are several genuinely unpopular (or at least contrarian) opinions about self-driving cars that you don’t often see in mainstream tech coverage:

- **Full autonomy (Level 5) will never be solved in our lifetimes.** The “long tail” of rare edge cases is effectively infinite, and the liability + regulatory hurdles make true driverless operation everywhere economically impossible outside of tightly controlled geofenced areas.
- **Tesla’s “Full Self-Driving” approach is closer to a very sophisticated Level 2+ system than a real path to Level 4/5.** Vision-only + end-to-end neural nets may scale impressively in common scenarios, but they still fail catastrophically in novel situations in ways that radar/lidar + explicit mapping (Waymo/Cruise style) handle more robustly.
- **Most people claiming they’ll never trust a robotaxi actually will once it’s 5–10× cheaper and more convenient than owning a car or using Uber.** Human resistance to autonomy melts away remarkably fast when money and time are on the table (see seatbelts, ABS, elevators, airplanes).
- **Autonomous vehicles will increase overall miles driven and urban sprawl, not reduce congestion.** Empty robotaxis cruising around to reposition or pick up the next passenger, plus people sending their cars on errands or to park far away, will add substantial “zero-occupancy” miles.
- **The safety debate is largely settled already: even today’s Level 2 systems (Tesla Autopilot, GM Super Cruise, etc.) have lower crash rates per mile than the average human in many conditions.** The remaining fatalities get disproportionate attention because they’re novel and the companies are forced to report them.
- **Waymo is the only company that has actually solved self-driving in a meaningful commercial way right now (2025), and everyone else—even Tesla—is years behind in real driverless deployment at scale.**
- **The biggest barrier isn’t technical anymore—it’s insurance, litigation, and local politics.** A single high-profile fatality or city council vote can freeze an entire program for years.
- **Self-driving tech will kill more motorcyclists and cyclists than it saves pedestrians.** Motorcycles and bikes frequently get misclassified or predicted poorly because they violate the “normal car” assumptions most systems are trained on.
- **Most promised societal benefits (fewer cars on the road, huge drop in parking lots, drunk driving eliminated) are decades away at best, while the near-term reality is just expensive ride-hail with a safety driver removed.**
- **A lot of the loudest “self-driving will never work” critics secretly hope it fails because they enjoy driving and don’t want their hobby/lifestyle disrupted.**

These views tend to get downvoted or dismissed in enthusiast forums, tech-bro circles, and traditional automotive circles alike—which is exactly why they qualify as unpopular.
