**Question:** What is FreeBuf?**Answer:**

FreeBuf (freebuf.com) is China's leading cybersecurity media and community platform — essentially the Chinese equivalent of Krebs on Security or Dark Reading, but with a strong community/forum angle.

Key facts:

- Founded in 2010, based in Shanghai, it's an online media portal focused on cybersecurity — offering reviews, reports, and user-contributed articles that go through editorial review.
- It aims to be the go-to platform for exchange of internet security technology, targeting hackers and geeks, with daily updates on global infosec news and active forum discussions around security summits.
- Its parent company also runs Vulbox.com (a bug bounty platform) and Tophant, an AI-powered threat detection platform.
- It covers vulnerabilities, web security, system security, network security, wireless security, and more — serving as a tool and resource directory in addition to news.
- It's credible enough to co-publish research — e.g., a 2021 study with 360 and QAX found China had over 170,000 white-hat security researchers.

**TL;DR for you:** If you're doing security research in a Chinese-context bank environment or tracking CVEs/exploits from a Chinese infosec angle, FreeBuf is the primary source. Think of it as the Hacker News + Krebs hybrid for the Chinese security community. Useful for threat intel if your bank work involves China-region attack surface.
