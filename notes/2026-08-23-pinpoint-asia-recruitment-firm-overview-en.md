**Question:** What is an introduction to the company Pinpoint Asia?

**Answer:**

Pinpoint Asia is a specialist IT and investment banking recruitment firm focused exclusively on the Asia-Pacific region. Here's a concise overview:

**Founded & Headquarters**
Pinpoint Asia was founded in 2007 and is headquartered in Hong Kong. They also have a presence in Singapore, having expanded operations there after integrating new automation technology in 2016.

**Core Business**
Pinpoint Asia was born out of a need to match the brightest talent with the best careers, positioning itself as the leading specialist Investment Banking and Technology recruitment firm focused exclusively on the Asia-Pacific markets.

**Geographic Reach**
Their reach extends across the region, covering the financial powerhouses of Hong Kong, Tokyo, and Singapore, as well as up-and-coming centers in Sydney, Shanghai, Beijing, and Seoul.

**Clientele**
Pinpoint Asia has carefully and strategically grown its client base to include a number of the world's top financial institutions, and is now a leading technology search firm for an elite group of Investment Banks, including some of the most renowned names on Wall Street.

**Specialization**
They help businesses of all sizes operating in the finance, insurance, and commerce markets to find the best IT and technology professionals, with a team that prides itself on actively scouting for the best possible talent.

**Reputation**
Their significant market reputation is a direct result of having developed strong working relationships with select financial organizations, based on an intimate understanding of the marketplace and a proven ability to deliver results.
