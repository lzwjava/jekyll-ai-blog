**Question:** What are the good benefits of eSIM?

**Answer:**

## Key Benefits of eSIM

### 1. No Physical SIM Card Needed
- Nothing to lose, break, or swap
- No SIM ejector tool fumbling at airports
- Instant activation via QR code or app

### 2. Multiple Plans on One Device
- Keep your home SIM active while adding a travel SIM (like your SoSIM)
- Switch between plans without swapping hardware
- Pixel and iPhone support multiple eSIM profiles stored simultaneously

### 3. Instant Activation
- Buy and activate a plan in minutes, remotely
- No waiting for mail delivery or finding a local store
- Useful the moment you land in a new country

### 4. Travel-Optimized
- Buy a local data plan before you even board the plane
- Avoid roaming charges without touching your physical SIM
- Your SoSIM Greater China plan is a perfect example — 10GB, 365 days, activated remotely

### 5. Better Device Durability
- No SIM tray = one fewer hole in the device
- More water resistance potential
- Less mechanical wear

### 6. Easier Plan Management
- Switch carriers or plans via software, not hardware
- Compare and switch providers instantly
- No carrier lock-in friction (on unlocked devices)

### 7. Environmentally Friendlier
- No plastic SIM card manufacturing or shipping
- Less packaging waste
