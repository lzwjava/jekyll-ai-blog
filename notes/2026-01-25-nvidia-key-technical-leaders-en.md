### Founders (Core Technical Visionaries)
- **Jensen Huang** — Founder, President, and CEO
  The driving force behind NVIDIA since 1993. He sets the technical direction for GPUs, AI, accelerated computing, and has been the public face of NVIDIA's dominance in AI hardware.
- **Chris A. Malachowsky** — Founder and NVIDIA Fellow
  One of the original architects of NVIDIA's GPU technology. As a Fellow, he continues to influence long-term technical strategy.
