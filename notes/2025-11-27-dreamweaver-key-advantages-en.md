## ✨ Key Advantages of Dreamweaver Templates

* **Consistency and Faster Development:** Templates allow you to define a consistent **structure and design** for all pages in your site. This allows for **reuse of existing elements** (like headers, navigation, footers), which **builds the website faster** and ensures a professional, uniform look across all pages.
* **Centralized Updates (Easy Maintenance):** When you **change the template**, the non-editable regions in **all linked pages** update automatically. This makes site-wide design changes, like changing the navigation or footer information, incredibly **easy to manage and update**, saving a significant amount of time and reducing the risk of inconsistencies.
* **Simplified Site Management:** Templates help **organize the things better** by separating the main design layout from the page content. This makes it **easy to delete, update, and manage** the layout structure independently of the content on individual pages.
