## 2. The bigger issue: EB-3 is not a guaranteed green card

The US Department of State's October 2026 Visa Bulletin lists the EB-3 Other Workers final-action date for mainland China as October 1, 2019. That means visa numbers are currently available only to qualifying applicants with earlier priority dates, subject to the applicable rules. A new applicant should expect a potentially long wait, and the date can move backward as well as forward.&#x20;
