**Question:** Beijing has the hot LLM labs (Moonshot/Kimi, Zhipu/GLM, etc.). What are the hot, prestigious AI startups and companies in Guangzhou and Shenzhen?

**Answer:**

First, the honest framing: GZ/SZ don't have a frontier LLM lab on the tier of DeepSeek (Hangzhou), Moonshot, Zhipu, MiniMax, or StepFun. Hangzhou leads all Chinese cities in the number of large language model companies, while Shenzhen's strengths are distributed across AI marketing, intelligent devices, and embodied intelligence. The Pearl River Delta's edge is different: **hardware + embodied AI + autonomous driving + two big-tech anchors**.

**Shenzhen — big-tech anchors:**

- **Tencent** — Hunyuan LLM family (including open-weight models), Yuanbao app, AI Lab. The most prestigious AI employer in the city.
- **Huawei** — Pangu models, Ascend NPUs, CANN, MindSpore. If you care about the "domestic compute" stack (and DeepSeek-style CUDA-free inference is now a real trend), this is ground zero. Shenzhen is also building an Ascend compute application innovation institute and pushing Ascend operator development and open-source ecosystem.
- **Honor, DJI, Insta360** — on-device AI, vision, drones.

**Shenzhen — startups (mostly embodied intelligence, the hottest local sector):**

- **UBTech (优必选)** — listed humanoid robot maker.
- **LimX Dynamics (逐际动力)** — raised $200M recently amid the embodied-AI funding wave.
- **EngineAI (众擎)**, **Leju (乐聚)**, **Dobot (越疆)**, **Pudu (普渡)** — humanoids/robot arms/service robots.
- **XVERSE (元象)** — local LLM player, open-sourced large MoE models, though not frontier-tier.
- **SmartMore (思谋)** — industrial vision AI.

Shenzhen's city plan targets 10+ new companies valued over ¥10B and 1,200+ embodied-robotics companies by 2027, so capital and policy are concentrated there.

**Guangzhou — the autonomous-driving capital:**

- **Pony.ai (小马智行)** — Nansha HQ, Nasdaq-listed, robotaxi.
- **WeRide (文远知行)** — Huangpu, Nasdaq-listed, robotaxi/robobus.
- **XPeng (小鹏)** — Guangzhou HQ; arguably the most AI-ambitious carmaker in China: in-house Turing chip, VLA driving models, Iron humanoid robot. Their AI infra team works on genuinely large-scale training.
- **Tencent WeChat team (TiT, Guangzhou)** — WeChat's AI integration (ima, search, agents inside WeChat) is built in Guangzhou; underrated as an AI destination.
- **CloudWalk (云从)** — CV "national team," Guangzhou-founded.
- Pazhou is the designated AI district; provincial policy is pushing L3 autonomous driving pilots and intelligent connected vehicle clusters across Guangzhou and Shenzhen.

**Practical read for you (Guangzhou-based, transitioning to AI engineering):** if you want LLM-core work without relocating to Beijing/Hangzhou, the realistic top targets are Tencent Hunyuan (SZ), Huawei (SZ, especially if you build Ascend/CANN skills — scarce and increasingly valuable), XPeng AI infra (GZ — they train foundation-scale VLA models locally), or the robotaxi pair Pony/WeRide (GZ) for ML systems work. Embodied AI startups in Shenzhen are where the 2026 funding heat is concentrated, and your training-from-scratch + agent-tooling background maps well to their "robot brain" (VLA) teams.
