**Question: What does “双非本科” mean?**

**Answer:**

“**双非本科**” (shuāng fēi běn kē) is a Chinese term used in education and recruitment contexts. It literally means:

> **A bachelor's degree that is neither from a “985” university nor a “211” university.**

Breaking it down:

* **双 (two)** = two categories
* **非 (not)** = not belonging to
* **本科 (undergraduate degree)** = bachelor's degree

The two excluded categories are:

1. **985 universities**

   * A group of elite Chinese universities selected under the **Project 985** initiative (started in 1998).
   * Examples:

     * Tsinghua University
     * Peking University
     * Shanghai Jiao Tong University

2. **211 universities**

   * Universities selected under **Project 211** (started in 1995) as nationally important institutions.
   * The 985 universities are also part of 211.

So:

* **985 + 211** → elite universities
* **211 but not 985** → strong national universities
* **双非本科** → all other bachelor's universities

Example:

| University type             | Classification |
| --------------------------- | -------------- |
| Tsinghua University         | 985 + 211      |
| Shenzhen University         | 211? No → 双非   |
| Local provincial university | Usually 双非     |
| Private university          | Usually 双非     |

### Why does it matter?

In China, many companies, government jobs, and graduate programs use university background as a filtering signal. A "双非本科" graduate may face disadvantages in:

* campus recruitment (校招)
* research positions
* some state-owned enterprises (国企)
* master's/PhD admissions

However, it does **not** mean the person is weak. Many strong engineers, entrepreneurs, and researchers come from 双非 universities. In software engineering and AI, practical ability, publications, open-source work, projects, and experience often outweigh undergraduate brand after several years.

A rough analogy:

* **985/211** ≈ top-tier universities (Ivy League / top research universities)
* **双非本科** ≈ regular accredited universities

The term is mainly a **Chinese hiring and social ranking label**, not an academic quality measurement by itself.
