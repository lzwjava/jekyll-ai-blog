**Question:** What is an introduction to Saronic company?

**Answer:**

**Saronic Technologies** (commonly referred to as Saronic) is a U.S.-based defense technology company specializing in the design, development, and manufacturing of **Autonomous Surface Vessels (ASVs)**, also known as unmanned surface vehicles (USVs) or "maritime drones."

Founded in **2022** and headquartered in **Austin, Texas**, Saronic focuses on providing naval and maritime forces with intelligent, scalable autonomous systems to enhance maritime security, domain awareness, operational reach, and survivability. The company integrates advanced hardware, software, and artificial intelligence into fully integrated platforms capable of operating independently or in swarms.

### Key Details
- **Mission**: To redefine maritime superiority for the U.S. and its allies by building cost-effective, rapidly producible autonomous vessels that address modern naval challenges, including contested environments and hybrid fleet operations.
- **Products**: Saronic offers a family of modular ASVs in various sizes, including:
  - Spyglass (6 ft) — compact, modular for custom capabilities.
  - Cutlass (14 ft) — longer-range with roles like sensor deployment and target tracking.
  - Corsair (24 ft) — payload capacity ~1,000 lbs, range ~1,000+ nm.
  - Mirage (40 ft) and Cipher (60 ft) — mid-size platforms with extended range and payload.
  - Marauder (180 ft) — larger medium unmanned surface vessel (MUSV) for more demanding missions.
- **Manufacturing & Expansion**: The company has invested heavily in U.S.-based production, including acquiring and expanding the Franklin shipyard in Louisiana (with a $300M+ investment announced in late 2025 to create thousands of jobs) and planning "Port Alpha," a next-generation shipyard focused on high-volume autonomous vessel production.
- **Funding & Growth**: Saronic has raised significant capital from prominent investors (including Andreessen Horowitz, 8VC, Caffeinated Capital, and others). It reached unicorn status and secured a major **$600M Series C** round in early 2025 (with additional rounds bringing total funding over $800M). It has contracts and agreements with the U.S. Navy, including a large OTA worth over $392M for ASV production.
- **Team**: Founded by veterans from elite U.S. military units (including former Navy SEAL Team 6 and Marine Corps members), with leadership including CEO Dino Mavrookas, COO Doug Lambert, CCO Rob Lehman, and CTO Vibhav Altekar.
- **Presence**: Operations in Austin (HQ), San Diego, Washington D.C., New Orleans, and international outreach (e.g., exhibited at DSEI UK in 2025).

Saronic has rapidly emerged as one of the leading players in the growing autonomous maritime defense sector, competing with companies like Anduril while emphasizing scalable, domestically produced solutions for naval applications.
