Question: How do I replace a lost Mainland China–Hong Kong/Macau Travel Permit (港澳通行证)?

Answer: You can apply for a replacement (补发), rather than applying as a first-time applicant.

If your permit was still valid when you lost it, you are eligible to apply for a replacement.

### 1. Required documents

Generally, you need:

- Mainland Chinese Resident ID (居民身份证).
- A compliant digital ID photo (证件照).
- Chinese Exit-Entry Document Application Form (中国公民出入境证件申请表).
- A declaration that your original permit was lost (遗失声明).

You may also need fingerprint verification.

The replacement application is handled by the local Public Security Bureau's Exit-Entry Administration (公安局出入境管理部门).&#x20;

### 2. You may be able to apply entirely online

Online replacement available in Guangzhou

Since Guangzhou is one of the 20 pilot cities, eligible residents can apply online.

Eligibility:

- Age 16 or above.
- Previously held a Mainland Travel Permit.
- The original permit was still valid when lost.
- Fingerprints were previously collected.
- Not a registered government employee or active-duty military member.

Process:

1. Open the National Immigration Administration (国家移民管理局) government service platform.
2. Select document replacement (换发补发).
3. Select Hong Kong/Macau Travel Permit.
4. Declare the document lost.
5. Submit the application and complete payment.

Official platform: [https://s.nia.gov.cn/](https://s.nia.gov.cn/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

For eligible online applications, the published processing target is 4 working days for review, followed by 3 working days for production and mailing after payment.&#x20;

### 3. If you need to apply in person in Guangzhou

You can:

- Book an appointment through the Guangzhou Public Security Bureau's Exit-Entry service.
- Visit an Exit-Entry Administration service centre.
- Bring your ID and complete the lost-document declaration.

Guangzhou official instructions: [Guangzhou Travel Permit Replacement Guide](https://gaj.gz.gov.cn/jmhd/zsk/crj/content/post_10535516.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

### 4. Three questions to determine the easiest route

1. Are you currently in the US or Guangzhou?
2. Is your household registration (户口) in Guangzhou?
3. Was your lost permit still within its validity period?

With these answers, I can tell you whether you can complete the replacement online without returning to Guangzhou.
