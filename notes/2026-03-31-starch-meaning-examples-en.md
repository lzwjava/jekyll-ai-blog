Question: What does "starch" mean?

Answer:

**Starch** is a type of **carbohydrate** found mainly in plants. It is a major source of energy in many foods.

### Simple meaning

* Starch = complex sugar stored in plants
* Your body breaks starch → glucose → energy

### Common foods with starch

* Rice
* Bread
* Potatoes
* Corn
* Pasta
* Noodles
* Beans

### Example sentences

* Rice contains a lot of **starch**.
* Potatoes are high in **starch**.
* This food is mostly **starch**, not protein.

### Related words

* **Starchy** → containing a lot of starch

  * "starchy vegetables" (potatoes, corn)
* **Starch-based** → made mainly from starch

### Chinese meaning

* starch = **淀粉**

So:

* rice → 高淀粉
* potato → 淀粉很多
* low starch diet → 低淀粉饮食
