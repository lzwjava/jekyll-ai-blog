Question: How does a Canadian study/work path compare with Illinois Tech AI Master's + US path?

Answer:

For your profile (11 years engineering, AI/LLM experience, startup background, Chinese citizen), Canada is actually a very interesting alternative because the **immigration path is usually more structured** than the US.

The typical route:

```
China
 |
 | apply
 v
Canadian university Master (AI / CS / Data Science)
 |
 | 1-2 years study
 v
Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP)
 |
 | Canadian AI/software job
 v
Permanent Residence (PR)
 |
 v
Canadian citizen (optional)
```

Canada's key advantage:

**A Canadian master's can give you a bridge from student → worker.**

For eligible master's graduates, the Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) can be up to **3 years**, even when the master's program is shorter than 2 years (if requirements are met). ([Canada][1])
