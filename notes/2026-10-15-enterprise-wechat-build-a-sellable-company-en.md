Question: How do you build a sellable company using Enterprise WeChat — for sales, knowledge base, customers, and products — so that the entity (主体) can be easily transferred, and why should you avoid using personal WeChat/WhatsApp for business?

---

Answer:

You're thinking about this exactly right. The core principle is: **the company must own its assets, not individuals**. Here's the full framework:

---

## Why This Matters for Sellability

A company is sellable (or investable) when a new owner can step in and keep things running. If your customers, conversations, and knowledge live in *personal* accounts, they leave when the person leaves — making the business worthless to a buyer.

**Enterprise WeChat (企业微信)** solves this structurally.

---

## What Enterprise WeChat Gives You

| Layer | What to Build | Why It's Transferable |
|---|---|---|
| **Sales** | All customer conversations via 企业微信 external contacts | Customer is bound to the company account, not the salesperson's personal ID |
| **Knowledge Base** | 企业微信 + 腾讯文档 / Notion / Feishu wiki | Anyone in the company (or a new owner) can access it |
| **Customers** | CRM tagged to 企业微信 contacts | Reassignable between staff at any time |
| **Products** | Product catalog in shared docs/systems | Not locked in someone's head or phone |

---

## The Entity (主体) Transfer Logic

When everything is on **enterprise accounts**:

- The **WeChat Work enterprise account** is registered to a company (entity)
- Customer contact histories, group chats, and tags belong to the **company**, not the individual
- When ownership changes → update company admins → everything continues
- Staff can be replaced without losing a single customer relationship

When everything is on **personal accounts**:
- Salesperson quits → takes their WeChat contacts with them
- Owner changes → customers don't know who the new contact is
- No audit trail, no handover, **no sale possible**

---

## The Rule: Never Do Business on Personal Channels

| ❌ Don't Use | ✅ Use Instead | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Personal WeChat | 企业微信 (Enterprise WeChat) | Company owns the contact relationship |
| Personal WhatsApp | WhatsApp Business API / enterprise line | Auditable, transferable |
| Personal email | Company domain email (e.g. Google Workspace) | Entity owns the mailbox |
| Personal cloud storage | Company shared drives | Not lost when person leaves |

---

## What Makes the Company Cleanly Sellable

1. **All customer contacts** are in 企业微信 under the company
2. **Knowledge base** is documented and accessible by role, not person
3. **SOPs** exist so any new operator can run it
4. **Revenue is traceable** through company accounts, not cash/personal transfers
5. **Contracts** are signed by the entity, not individuals

The buyer is essentially buying the **system** — and the system only exists if it was never personalized.

---

**Short answer to your question:** Yes — use Enterprise WeChat for everything customer-facing and knowledge-related, never personal accounts. This is the single most important structural decision for making a company transferable or sellable. The 主体 can change hands cleanly because the assets were never attached to a person in the first place.
