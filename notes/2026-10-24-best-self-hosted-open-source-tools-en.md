**Question:** What are the best open source tools that can be self-hosted?

---

**Answer:**

Here's a curated overview of popular self-hosted open source tools by category:

---

### 📁 File Storage & Collaboration

- **Nextcloud** — The closest thing to a self-hosted Google Workspace: file sync and share, plus calendar, contacts, mail, video calls, and a collaborative office suite.

---

### 📝 Knowledge Base & Documentation

- **Docmost** — A collaborative wiki and knowledge base, the self-hosted answer to Confluence or Notion.
- **Outline** — A modern, open-source wiki and documentation tool.
- **AppFlowy** — An open-source AI workspace designed as a strong alternative to Notion, where organizations retain full ownership of their data.
- **AFFiNE** — An open-source, local-first workspace that merges document editing with whiteboard functionality.

---

### 🔐 Security & Password Management

- **Bitwarden** — A secure, open-source password manager.

---

### 📊 Analytics & BI

- **Plausible** — The self-hosted analytics tool that respects your visitors. The Community Edition (CE) costs only VPS hosting: $6–12/month for most sites.
- **Metabase** — An open-source business intelligence tool for creating dashboards and visualizing data.

---

### 🔄 Workflow Automation

- **n8n / Activepieces** — Powerful self-hosted alternatives to Zapier and Make.

---

### 🖥️ Monitoring & Observability

- **Uptime Kuma** — A self-hosted monitoring tool and status page that tracks the availability of your services.
- **Beszel** — Lightweight monitoring for systems and Docker with real-time alerts.

---

### 💻 Developer Tools

- **Gitea** — A highly efficient, open-source Git server that allows teams to host their own code repositories.
- **Woodpecker CI** — A lightweight, open-source continuous integration server that integrates seamlessly with Gitea and other self-hosted Git services.

---

### 🗃️ Database / No-Code

- **NocoDB** — A self-hosted, open-source database interface.

---

### 📰 Blogging & CMS

- **Ghost** — An elegant, open-source alternative to WordPress for content creators and bloggers.

---

### 🎬 Media & Photos

- **Plex** — A personal media streaming server (think "self-hosted Netflix").

---

### 🤖 AI & Machine Learning

- There are now 47+ self-hosted open source AI/ML alternatives, including tools for AI chatbots, photo/video management with local AI (face recognition, semantic search), personal knowledge bases with AI search, and AI camera monitoring systems.

---

> 💡 **Tip:** Most of these tools offer Docker images, so you can run them easily on a $12/month VPS. Self-hosting trades a subscription fee for a bit of your time and attention, but you get full data ownership, privacy, and freedom from SaaS limitations.
