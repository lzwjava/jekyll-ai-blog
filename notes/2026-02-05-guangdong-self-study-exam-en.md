Based on your situation with the Guangdong self-study examination (自考), here's what you need to know:

## Can you use the same exam registration number?

**Yes**, you can typically use the same 准考证号 (exam registration number) for both your associate degree and bachelor degree in the self-study examination system. You don't need a separate registration number.

## Do you need to 预报名 (pre-register) for the bachelor's degree now?

**Yes, you should**. Here's the process:

1. **Timing**: You can start registering for bachelor's degree courses even before completing your associate degree. Many students do this to save time.

2. **Registration steps**:
   - Go to the Guangdong self-study exam system (广东省自学考试管理系统)
   - Use your existing account/exam number
   - When registering for courses, select the bachelor's degree program (本科) courses you want to take
   - Choose your major code (080901 for Computer Science bachelor's level)

3. **Important requirement**: You'll need to present your associate degree certificate when you apply for bachelor's degree graduation (申请本科毕业), but you don't need it to take the bachelor's level courses.

## Recommendation

Since you're close to finishing your associate degree (13 courses passed, 10 remaining), you should:
- Complete your current associate degree courses first
- **Start 预报名 for bachelor courses in the next registration period** (usually before each exam session in January, April, and October)
- You can begin taking bachelor courses while finishing your last associate courses

This way, you won't have a gap between finishing your associate degree and starting your bachelor degree.
