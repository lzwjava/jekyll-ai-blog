**Question:** What is Xi Jinping's Thought on Ecological Civilization (习近平生态文明思想)?

---

**Answer:**

Xi Jinping's Thought on Ecological Civilization (习近平生态文明思想) is China's current official ecological doctrine, rooted in the concept of "ecological civilization." It forms part of the broader Xi Jinping Thought framework and was formally established at the National Ecological and Environmental Protection Conference in May 2018.

**Core Philosophy**

As both a vision and an ideological framework, it paints a picture of China's green future while outlining a path to reach it, and is extensively invoked in Chinese political discourse — used interchangeably as a slogan, policy orientation, historical trajectory, and worldview.

**Key Principles ("Ten Insistences")**

As summarized in 2022, the thought insists on: Party leadership over ecological construction; the idea that civilization flourishes when ecology flourishes; harmonious coexistence between humans and nature; the belief that "clear waters and green mountains are invaluable assets"; that a good ecological environment is the most universal public benefit; that green development represents a profound revolution in development thinking; coordinated management of mountains, rivers, forests, farmlands, lakes, grasslands and deserts; the strictest legal and institutional protections for the environment; making the building of a "beautiful China" a conscious action of all people; and jointly pursuing a path of global ecological civilization.

**Theoretical Roots**

The thought is framed as an inheritance and development of Marxist ecological civilization theory, drawing on the collective wisdom of the Chinese Communist Party and nourished by China's traditional ecological culture.

**Spatial & Governance Dimension**

The thought covers a spatial governance framework — building a "community of life" between humans and nature, converting green landscapes into economic assets, guarding ecological security boundaries, and constructing integrated protection patterns spanning mountaintops to oceans.

In short, it is a comprehensive ideological system that ties environmental protection, economic development, and political governance together under CCP leadership, with ambitions that extend beyond China to shaping global environmental norms.
