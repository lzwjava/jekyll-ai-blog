Question: How do I explore this app?

Answer: The page you uploaded is **Muse from Meta**, a personal AI agent app, not the muse.ai video host from your earlier question. The two are unrelated. Since you're signed in through Facebook, this is probably the one you meant. It's a saved copy of the Library screen, so I'm going by what's visible in it.

**Left sidebar**

- **Chat (top avatar):** Talk to Muse. Click the avatar to see what it's working on, what it finished, and what's next. You can also change its name and look. Shortcut: Cmd+J.
- **Search (magnifier):** Searches across the app. Shortcut: Cmd+K.
- **"…" menu:** Opens the pinned sections: Feed, Ideas, Goals and Library.

**Sections**

- **Feed:** Posts tailored to you. Use the Edit button on "Your feed prompt" to say what topics you want, then click Generate. Right now it only shows intro posts.
- **Ideas:** Suggestions of things Muse can do for you, grouped into Relationships, Shopping, Productivity, Financial Management, and Health & Fitness. Click one to start it.
- **Goals:** Pick a category (Health, Relationships, Finance, Career, Interests, Productivity, or Something else) and describe what you want. Muse tracks it and posts updates. The category buttons look greyed out in your saved copy, so they may only work in the live app.
- **Library:** Everything Muse makes for you.
  - **Artifacts:** All artifacts, Documents, and Web artifacts. It currently says "No artifacts yet."
  - **Media:** Images, Videos, and Podcasts.
  - **System files:** Files the app keeps for itself.

**A good first step:** Go to Chat and tell Muse what you want, for example "build me a tracker for my runs" or "make a packing list for Iceland." The result will appear in Library → Artifacts. Muse is also still waiting for you to tell it your name, since "cook" was unclear.
