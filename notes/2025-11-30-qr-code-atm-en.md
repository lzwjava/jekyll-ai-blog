### QR Code ATM Withdrawals in Chinese Banks

QR code-based cash withdrawals at ATMs (also known as cardless or UnionPay QR Cash withdrawals) allow users to generate a withdrawal request via a bank's mobile app, scan a QR code displayed on the ATM screen, and complete the transaction by entering a PIN—without needing a physical debit or credit card. This service is powered by UnionPay and is widely available across mainland China, Hong Kong, and Macau for participating banks. It's designed for convenience, security, and integration with mobile banking, with typical daily limits around CNY 5,000–20,000 (depending on the bank and account) and a small fee (e.g., 0.3% in some cases).

#### General Notes

- **Availability**: By 2025, over 90% of major banks' ATMs in urban areas (e.g., Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen) support this via the UnionPay network. Rural coverage is growing but may vary. Look for "UnionPay QR Withdrawal" stickers on machines.
- **How It Works (Standard Process)**: 1) Open bank app, select withdrawal amount/account. 2) Generate/confirm request (valid for ~30–60 minutes). 3) At ATM, select QR option to display code. 4) Scan with app, enter PIN, collect cash.
- **Requirements**: Active mobile banking app, linked debit/credit card, and UnionPay-enabled account. Foreign cards may work at select ATMs but with limits (e.g., CNY 10,000 daily overseas cap).
- **When It Began Overall**: The service gained traction in 2018 with UnionPay's QR Cash initiative, driven by China's mobile payment boom (Alipay/WeChat). Major banks like ICBC and CCB piloted in late 2018, with full rollouts by 2019. This was part of broader regulations limiting overseas ATM use but promoting domestic digital alternatives.
- **Limitations**: Fees (0–1%), time-sensitive QR (expires quickly), and app-only access. Not all ATMs support it 24/7 due to location (e.g., indoors).
