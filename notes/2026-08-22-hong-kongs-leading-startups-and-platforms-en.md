**Answer:**

### 🔥 Notable Hong Kong Startups & Tech Companies

**Fintech**
- **Airwallex** — A global payment infrastructure company serving businesses across 95 countries, surpassing $720M in annualized revenue in 2025 with 90% YoY growth and over 100,000 business customers.
- **WeLab** — Builds digital financial products including virtual banking and consumer financing solutions, having raised nearly $900 million.

**Logistics & Delivery**
- **Lalamove** — One of Hong Kong's homegrown unicorn startups, founded in 2013, with over 8 million users and 1.5 million delivery drivers, now operating across 300+ cities in Southeast Asia, China, and Latin America.
- **GoGoX** — The first app-based delivery platform in Asia (est. 2013), connecting businesses to thousands of drivers, now operating across Hong Kong, Singapore, South Korea, mainland China, Taiwan, and India.

**Travel Tech**
- **Klook** — A travel experiences platform connected to 400+ destinations, achieving $3 billion in revenue in 2023, with a planned US IPO targeting a $5 billion valuation.

**AI & Deep Tech**
- **SenseTime** — Specializes in computer vision, facial recognition, and visual data analysis, with applications in finance, retail, and smart city infrastructure, with significant operations and research presence in Hong Kong.
- **Insilico Medicine** — An AI-driven biotech firm established in 2014 with strong and growing momentum in the space.
- **Fano Labs** — A language-focused AI startup with strong enterprise adoption, supported by local innovation programs and expanded across Asia.
