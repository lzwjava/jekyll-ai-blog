## 1. Start with the scientific consensus

In modern ophthalmology:

* Myopia is usually caused by **elongation of the eyeball (axial length increase)**
* Once developed, it is **generally not reversible**
* What *is* possible:

  * Slowing progression
  * Correcting vision (glasses, contacts, surgery)
  * Minor short-term fluctuations

👉 True structural “reversal” is **not an established medical outcome**
