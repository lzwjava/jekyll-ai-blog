**Question:** Why do nightclubs never run out of "pretty girls"? (Sharing a post about the atmosphere-girl economy — thoughts? Maybe a comment worth leaving?)

**Answer:**

The post is describing a textbook two-sided market subsidy, the same mechanics as any marketplace startup. Nightclubs sell to big spenders (卡座大哥), but the product those spenders are buying isn't alcohol — it's atmosphere and the perceived presence of attractive women. So the club subsidizes the supply side: free entry, free drinks, sometimes a base salary of 3,000 CNY/month plus drink commissions for showing up 20 nights. It's structurally identical to Uber subsidizing riders in the early days, or dating apps letting women in free — you pay the side that attracts the side that pays.

What makes the piece sharper than most is the last paragraph: supply isn't scarce because the *asset depreciates on a fixed schedule*. Pretty girls are always being "produced," but the monetization window for each individual is short — a few years. So the pipeline refills itself: high churn, zero recruiting cost, and the incentive structure ("9 out of 10 think they're the exception who marries rich") does the retention work for free. From the club's perspective it's the perfect labor pool — self-replenishing, self-motivated, and paid mostly in optionality rather than cash.

The dark part is the information asymmetry: the girls are betting on a lottery ticket (the rich husband), while the club is running a spreadsheet. One side is playing a game of hope; the other is running unit economics. That asymmetry is exactly why the supply never dries up.
