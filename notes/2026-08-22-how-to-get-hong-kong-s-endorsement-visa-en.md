Question: How can a mainland Chinese citizen get a Hong Kong **商务签注 (S endorsement)** on a 港澳通行证?

Answer:

A **商务签注 (S)** is not something everyone can simply apply for. It is designed for people who have a **real business relationship** with Hong Kong/Macau, such as company employees, business owners, or individual business operators. ([HMO][1])

For you (Guangzhou + software/AI consulting), the usual route is:

## Route 1: Your mainland company sponsors you (most common)

Example:

```
Guangzhou AI Technology Co., Ltd.

Owner:
    You

Business:
    AI consulting
    software development

Need:
    Register company for 港澳商务签注备案

Then:
    You apply S endorsement
```

Requirements usually include:

* company business license
* company registration/filing for Hong Kong/Macau business activities
* proof that you are related to the company
* business purpose explanation
* for multiple-entry applications, often social insurance/employment proof requirements apply depending on situation. ([NIA Government Services Platform][2])

---

## Route 2: Individual business owner (个体工商户)

If you have:

```
个体工商户营业执照
        |
        v
business activities with HK
        |
        v
apply 商务签注
```

you may qualify as a business operator. ([HMO][1])

---

## Route 3: HK company alone is usually not enough

A common misunderstanding:

```
Create Hong Kong company
        |
        X
Automatically get 商务签注
```

No.

A HK company does not automatically give you a mainland 港澳商务签注. The mainland authority mainly looks at your mainland-side qualification and business relationship. ([NIA Government Services Platform][2])

---

## How long can you stay?

Recent policy changes extended the stay period for holders of Hong Kong/Macau business endorsements:

* maximum stay per entry: **up to 14 days** (previously 7 days) for business endorsements. ([NIA][3])

The number of entries depends on your endorsement type:

```
3个月一次
3个月多次
1年多次
```

depending on eligibility and filing status. ([Yantian Government][4])
