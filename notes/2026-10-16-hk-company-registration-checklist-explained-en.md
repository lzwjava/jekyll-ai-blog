**Question:** What does this Hong Kong company registration checklist mean?

**Answer:**

This is a list of documents and information required to register a company in Hong Kong, along with the processing timeline. Here's a breakdown of each item:

| # | Item | Explanation |
|---|------|-------------|
| 1 | **Company Name** | Must include an English name; a Chinese name is optional but can be added |
| 2 | **Registered Capital** | The standard is HK$10,000 — this is a nominal figure only; you do **not** need to actually deposit this money into a bank account |
| 3 | **Shareholder & Director ID** | Photos of both sides of each person's ID card or passport; notably, one person can serve as both the sole director and sole shareholder |
| 4 | **Photo Holding ID** | A selfie-style photo of the person physically holding their ID card or passport (for identity verification) |
| 5 | **Shareholding Ratio** | How ownership is split among shareholders; a single person can hold 100% |
| 6 | **Signature Specimen** | A sample of each director/shareholder's handwritten signature |
| 7 | **Business Scope** | A brief description of what the company does, limited to **30 characters** (including punctuation) |

**Timeline:**
- ~**2 days** → Digital certificate issued
- ~**7 days** → All paperwork fully completed

**Key takeaways:**
- Hong Kong company formation is relatively quick and simple.
- The capital requirement (HK$10,000 ≈ ~US$1,300) is **stated, not paid** — it's just a legal formality.
- A single individual can own and operate the company alone (100% shareholder + sole director).
