**Question:** What is https://grokipedia.com?

**Answer:**

**Grokipedia.com** is an AI-generated online encyclopedia operated by **xAI**, the AI company founded by Elon Musk. It was officially launched on **October 27, 2025**, as a proposed alternative to Wikipedia.

The site is powered by xAI's **Grok** large language model family, which generates articles, performs fact-checking, and handles updates. Its stated goal is to provide "maximum truth-seeking" content, free from what Musk and supporters describe as ideological bias or "propaganda" in Wikipedia. The homepage describes it as "an open source, comprehensive collection of all knowledge."

Key characteristics include:

- Many early articles were initially forked or closely adapted from Wikipedia (some nearly verbatim under Creative Commons licensing), though xAI stated intentions to reduce this over time.
- It has far fewer articles than Wikipedia (around 885,000 at launch vs. Wikipedia's millions in English alone).
- The interface is minimalist, featuring mainly a central search bar, theme options (light/dark/system), and a list of recent edits (many approved by Grok).
- Users can suggest edits, but changes are reviewed/approved via the AI system rather than traditional human community moderation.
- It positions itself within xAI's broader mission "to understand the Universe."

Public reception and analyses have been mixed:

- Supporters view it as a faster-updating, less biased knowledge source.
- Critics argue it introduces its own biases (often described as right-leaning or aligned with Elon Musk's views), sometimes contains hallucinations or disinformation, has thinner sourcing in many articles, and lacks Wikipedia's depth, transparency, internal linking, images, and collaborative editing model.

As of early 2026, it remains an early-stage project (version 0.1 at launch) and is not considered a direct replacement for Wikipedia by most independent observers.
