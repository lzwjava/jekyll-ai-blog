Question: Introduction to 财富广场 in Tianhe, Guangzhou

Answer:

财富广场 (Wealth Plaza) is a well-known Grade A office building located in the core business district of Tianhe, Guangzhou—often referred to as part of the city’s “financial street” area.

### 1. Basic Overview

* **Type:** Grade A office building
* **Location:** Tianhe District, Guangzhou (central CBD area)
* **Completion:** Around 2003–2004
* **Floors:** ~36 floors
* **Total floor area:** ~80,000 m² ([gzcaifuguangchang.cn][1])

It is designed primarily for **corporate offices**, hosting companies from finance, consulting, tech, and services.
