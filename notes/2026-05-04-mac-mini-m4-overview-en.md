Question: Introduction to Mac Mini M4

Answer:

The Mac Mini (M4, 2024) is Apple’s compact desktop computer powered by its latest in-house silicon, the Apple M4 chip. It continues the design philosophy of being small, quiet, and energy-efficient, while delivering strong performance for both everyday and professional workloads.

---

## 1. What is the Mac Mini?

The Mac Mini is a **small desktop computer without a screen, keyboard, or mouse**. You connect your own peripherals, making it flexible and cost-effective compared to laptops or all-in-one desktops.

---

## 2. M4 Chip – Core Upgrade

The biggest change in this generation is the M4 chip.

### Key improvements:

* **Faster CPU & GPU** → smoother multitasking, coding, and creative work
* **Improved Neural Engine** → better AI/ML performance (useful for modern apps, LLM tools, etc.)
* **Higher efficiency** → powerful performance with low power consumption

Compared to earlier chips (like M1/M2), M4 focuses more on **AI acceleration + performance per watt**.
