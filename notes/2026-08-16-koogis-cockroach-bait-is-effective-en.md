Question: Is **KOOGIS 蟑螂药（呋虫胺胶饵剂）** actually useful? Does it work?

Answer:

Yes, **it can be effective**, especially if you have common household cockroaches (德国小蠊 / small kitchen roaches). The active ingredient **呋虫胺 (dinotefuran)** is a real insecticide used in cockroach baits. Research on 0.5% dinotefuran gel bait showed strong killing ability against German cockroaches, including secondary killing through dead roaches being eaten by others. ([Dongguan Health Bureau][1])

The mechanism:

```
roach eats gel bait
        |
        v
dinotefuran damages insect nervous system
        |
        v
roach dies slowly
        |
        v
other roaches eat contaminated body/feces
        |
        v
colony population decreases
```

The "全窝端" marketing is based on this **secondary transfer effect**, but it is not magic. It works best when roaches are already moving around and feeding.

For good results:

1. **Put small dots, not one big pile**

   * behind refrigerator
   * under kitchen cabinet
   * near pipes
   * corners
   * under sink

2. **Do not spray insecticide nearby**

   * Spray smell can repel roaches and make them avoid the bait.

3. **Remove competing food**

   * clean grease
   * seal food
   * dry water sources

4. **Wait**

   * You may see more activity first.
   * Usually noticeable reduction takes several days to 2–3 weeks.

KOOGIS appears to be a popular product listing with very high marketplace sales/reviews, but reviews are not the same as scientific testing; the important part is the active ingredient (呋虫胺) and correct placement. ([JD.com][2])

For a normal apartment kitchen, this type of gel bait is usually one of the better choices compared with sprays because it attacks the nest rather than only killing the visible roaches. ([Dongguan Health Bureau][1])
