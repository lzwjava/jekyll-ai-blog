Question: What are the most popular and active competitive programming platforms right now? Please give the top 10 choices, including Codeforces, LeetCode, AtCoder, etc.

Answer:

Here are the **top 10 most popular and active competitive programming platforms** as of early 2026, based on contest frequency, community size, activity level (participation in rated contests), and overall recognition in the CP and interview-prep communities:

1. **Codeforces**
   The most active pure competitive programming platform with almost daily contests (Div. 1–4, Educational, Global Rounds), huge rating system, and very large global community. It remains the #1 choice for serious CP enthusiasts.

2. **LeetCode**
   Extremely popular for technical interview preparation at big tech companies. Weekly + bi-weekly contests, massive problem library, and high activity from job seekers. Often the first choice for interview-focused coding.

3. **AtCoder**
   Very active Japanese platform with clean problems and regular contests (ABC, ARC, AGC). Highly respected for high-quality tasks and growing international participation.

4. **CodeChef**
   Very active especially in India and among students. Frequent monthly Long Challenges, Starters, Cook-Offs, and LunchTime contests. Strong community and good editorials.

5. **HackerRank**
   Popular for both practice and company hiring contests. Active domain-specific challenges, certifications, and interview kits. Still widely used in recruitment.

6. **TopCoder**
   Classic platform with a long history. Single Round Matches (SRMs) and high-level algorithmic challenges. Remains active among veteran competitive programmers.

7. **Codewars**
   Gamified kata-based training with a large community creating challenges. Very active daily solving and ranking system, great for fun short practices.

8. **SPOJ (Sphere Online Judge)**
   Classic archive with 20,000+ problems. Still active for practice, especially for advanced users and old-school CP training.

9. **GeeksforGeeks (GFG) Practice / Contests**
   Very popular in India for learning + contests. Weekly contests, large problem set, and strong interview preparation material. High student activity.

10. **CodinGame**
    Unique gamified platform with multiplayer clashes, bot programming, and optimization games. Active community and fun competitive elements.

**Notes**:
- For pure **competitive programming** (ratings, fast thinking, hard algorithms): Codeforces > AtCoder > CodeChef > TopCoder.
- For **interview preparation** (FAANG-style problems): LeetCode >> HackerRank > others.
- Activity levels can shift slightly over time, but these platforms consistently show the highest participation and contest frequency in 2025–2026 sources.
