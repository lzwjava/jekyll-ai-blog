**Question:** What happened between NVIDIA and TSMC during the 2009 crisis involving the 40nm node, and how was it resolved — including the $100M+ compensation and the Silicon Valley meeting between their CEOs?

---

**Answer:**

The crisis was actually centered on **TSMC's 40nm process node** (not 14nm — that came years later). Here's what went down:

**The Crisis**

TSMC began 40nm process mass production in 2008, soon securing major clients including NVIDIA, AMD, and Altera. By mid-2009, however, reports of low yield rates surfaced, creating serious market risks for NVIDIA and AMD, whose high-performance computing products depended heavily on the process. NVIDIA's Fermi (GF100) GPU launch was notably delayed as a result.

**Morris Chang Returns**

Morris Chang resolved the 2009 dispute with NVIDIA after returning as CEO of TSMC by taking several key actions. Upon retaking the CEO position, Chang identified the ongoing dispute with NVIDIA as a priority problem. He personally called major customers, including Jensen Huang of NVIDIA, to understand the situation. Chang thoroughly familiarized himself with all aspects of the 40nm problem, including the manufacturing issues, quality concerns, and the financial impact on NVIDIA.

**The $100M+ Compensation**

After weeks of work, Chang decided that the issue was primarily a matter of money. He devised a financial offer, amounting to over $100 million, to compensate NVIDIA for the damages they had suffered.

**The Silicon Valley Dinner Meeting**

Chang emailed Jensen Huang to schedule a meeting at his home in Silicon Valley, starting with a casual family dinner before discussing business. At the meeting, Chang presented the offer and made it clear that the offer was effective for only 48 hours — stating that if NVIDIA didn't accept within that time, they would have to go to an arbitrator, as Jensen had previously suggested to the former CEO.

Chang emphasized that there would be no further negotiations or bargaining, ensuring a quick resolution. Jensen Huang accepted the offer within the 48-hour timeframe.

**The Aftermath**

Despite significant losses during the 40nm incident, NVIDIA recovered swiftly, aided by compensation and TSMC's stable supply. The company realigned its product strategy and leveraged the 28nm process to cement its market leadership. Since then, NVIDIA has prioritized process maturity, emphasizing yield and production stability while steering clear of unproven technologies.

A small correction on your memory: the node in question was **40nm**, not 14nm. The 14nm era came later (around 2014–2016), and TSMC's 14nm was actually called **16nm FinFET**. The dinner was also at **Chang's home** in Silicon Valley — though accounts vary on the exact food served; it's described as a casual family dinner rather than specifically pizza.
