**Question:** What are some famous companies based in Guangzhou, China, similar to XPeng and WeChat?

**Answer:**

Quick note: **WeChat is made by Tencent**, which is headquartered in **Shenzhen**, not Guangzhou. But here are the well-known companies genuinely rooted in Guangzhou:

---

### 🚗 Automotive / EV
- **XPeng Motors (小鹏汽车)** — An electric vehicle manufacturer founded in 2014 and headquartered in Guangzhou. One of China's leading smart EV brands.
- **GAC Group (广汽集团)** — A state-owned automobile company founded in 2000, headquartered in Guangzhou. In 2021, it was ranked 176th on the Fortune Global 500 and is the 6th largest automaker in China.
- **GAC Aion (广汽埃安)** — GAC's EV subsidiary, which has been launching new models and establishing battery swap networks.

---

### ⚡ Energy / Infrastructure
- **China Southern Power Grid (南方电网)** — One of the Fortune Global 500 companies that built its global headquarters in Guangzhou. It manages power distribution across southern China.

---

### 🎮 Internet / Gaming
- **NetEase Games (网易)** — While NetEase's corporate HQ is in Hangzhou, NetEase Games — a leading developer of PC and mobile games worldwide — established its major headquarters in Guangzhou's Tianhe Smart City district. Guangzhou is effectively the home of its game development operations.

---

### 🛍️ E-Commerce / Fashion
- **SHEIN** — The global fast-fashion giant is based in Guangzhou. Three of the four major cross-border e-commerce platforms (including Temu and SHEIN) are headquartered in the Guangzhou area.
- **Vipshop (唯品会)** — A major online flash-sales platform headquartered in Guangzhou, listed on the NYSE.

---

### 💊 Pharma / Consumer
- **Guangzhou Pharmaceutical Holdings (广药集团)** — One of the largest healthcare companies in China, headquartered in Guangzhou, and home to the Wanglaoji (王老吉) herbal tea brand.

---

### 🏦 Finance
- **GF Securities (广发证券)** — One of the first comprehensive financial business companies in China, headquartered in Guangzhou.

---

In short, Guangzhou is especially dominant in **EVs, auto manufacturing, e-commerce, and gaming**. It's a different flavor from Shenzhen (Tencent, Huawei, DJI) but equally impressive in its own right.
