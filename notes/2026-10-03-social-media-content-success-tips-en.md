Creating popular content on social networks involves a combination of understanding your audience, using engaging visuals, and leveraging trending topics. Here are some tips:

1. **Understand Your Audience**: Know who your target audience is, what they like, and how they consume content. This can help you tailor your content to their preferences.

2. **Use Engaging Visuals**: Most people prefer visuals over plain text. Use high-quality images, videos, and infographics to make your content more appealing.

3. **Trending Topics**: Stay updated with trending topics and hashtags relevant to your niche. This can help increase the visibility of your content.

4. **Consistency**: Posting regularly can help build a loyal following and keep your audience engaged.

5. **Interact with Your Audience**: Respond to comments, messages, and feedback. Engagement can boost your content's visibility.

6. **Collaborate with Others**: Partnering with other influencers or brands can help you reach a wider audience.

7. **Use Paid Promotions**: Consider using paid advertising options to boost your content and reach a larger audience.

8. **Analyze Performance**: Use analytics tools provided by social media platforms to track the performance of your posts and adjust your strategy accordingly.
