Question: What does "scrappy" mean?

Answer: "Scrappy" is an informal adjective with a few related senses:

1. **Determined and feisty despite being at a disadvantage** — someone who fights hard, shows grit, and doesn't give up even when outmatched. Example: "The underdog team played a scrappy game and pulled off an upset."
2. **Untidy or made from odds and ends** — something patched together, rough around the edges, or lacking polish. Example: "It was a scrappy little startup running out of a garage."
3. **In business/startup contexts**, "scrappy" often carries a positive connotation — resourceful, lean, and willing to hustle with limited resources rather than sloppy.

The tone (positive "gritty underdog" vs. negative "messy/unpolished") usually depends on context.
