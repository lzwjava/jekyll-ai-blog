## Founders

### 1. He Chaoxi (何朝曦)

**Role:**

* Co-founder
* Chairman
* General Manager (CEO equivalent)

Background:

* Graduated from University of Science and Technology of China in 1998
* Studied automatic control + economic management
* Worked at Huawei Technologies from 1998–2000
* Founded Sangfor in 2000 with Xiong Wu ([Sangfor][1])

He is the main business leader and long-term strategic driver of Sangfor. ([Sangfor][1])

---

### 2. Xiong Wu (熊武)

**Role:**

* Co-founder
* Vice Chairman
* Deputy General Manager

Background:

* Also graduated from USTC in 1998
* Worked at Huawei before founding Sangfor
* Initially focused more on market/business development ([Sangfor][1])

He and He Chaoxi are the two core founders.
