The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) (HKUST(GZ), 香港科技大学（广州）) is a **new research university in Guangzhou**, jointly created by The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) and Guangzhou partners. It was officially established in 2022 and opened in Nansha, Guangzhou. ([hkust-gz.edu.cn][1])

Think of it as:

> HKUST Hong Kong = the mature 1991-founded research university
> HKUST(GZ) = a new "HKUST 2.0" experiment focused on AI, interdisciplinary research, and future technology. ([hkust.edu.hk][2])

---

## 1. Background

HKUST itself is one of China's strongest science/engineering universities.

Founded in 1991, HKUST became famous for:

* computer science
* engineering
* business
* entrepreneurship
* international research

([hkust.edu.hk][3])

HKUST(GZ) was created because the Greater Bay Area wanted another world-class technology university in Guangzhou. The idea:

* combine Hong Kong research culture
* combine mainland China's industrial ecosystem
* focus on future technologies

([hkust.edu.hk][2])

---

## 2. Location

Campus:

* Nansha District, Guangzhou
* about 1.1 km²
* near Qingsheng high-speed railway hub
* around 30 minutes from Hong Kong West Kowloon by high-speed rail

([hkust-gz.edu.cn][4])

The campus is very new.

Pros:

* modern buildings
* good dorms
* advanced facilities

Cons:

* Nansha is far from Guangzhou downtown
* less convenient compared with universities in Tianhe/Panyu

Student discussions often mention the campus is beautiful but somewhat isolated. ([Reddit][5])

## 3. Academic model

The interesting part is the structure.

Traditional university:

```
School
 ├── Department
 │    ├── Major
```

HKUST(GZ):

```
Hub
 ├── Thrust Area
       ├── interdisciplinary research
```

Four major hubs:

```
Function Hub
Information Hub
Systems Hub
Society Hub
```

([ugadmissions.hkust-gz.edu.cn][6])

The goal is to remove traditional department boundaries.

Example:

AI researcher + robotics engineer + manufacturing expert

working together on:

* autonomous factories
* intelligent robots
* AI systems

## 5. Relationship with HKUST Hong Kong

Important:

HKUST(GZ) is **not simply a branch campus**.

It has:

* independent legal entity
* independent operation

but follows the:

> "Unified HKUST, Complementary Campuses"

framework.

They share:

* faculty resources
* courses
* research resources
* student exchange opportunities

([hkust.edu.hk][8])
