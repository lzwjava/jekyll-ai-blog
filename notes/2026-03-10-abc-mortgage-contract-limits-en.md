Question: Can you download a personal house mortgage contract from the Agricultural Bank of China (ABC) mobile app?

Answer:

Based on available information, the Agricultural Bank of China (ABC) mobile banking app **does not directly offer a downloadable copy of your full mortgage/loan contract (借款合同)**. However, the app provides several loan-related features that may help you access key contract information:

## What You CAN Do via the ABC Mobile App

**View loan details and contract number:**
You can open the ABC app, tap "Loan" (贷款) on the homepage, then tap "View Details" (查看详情), and then tap "Tax Information" (报税信息) to see your loan contract number.

**View repayment plan and details:**
You can go to ABC Mobile Banking → Loan → View Details → Personal Housing Loan → Loan Usage Records → Repayment Details → Repayment Plan to view your monthly repayment schedule.

**Manage interest rate adjustments:**
Customers with fixed-rate or benchmark-rate mortgage loans can log in to the ABC Mobile Banking app and navigate to Loan → Existing Mortgage Rate Adjustment (存量房贷利率调整) to handle rate conversion requests.

**View loan clearance certificate:**
You can open the ABC Mobile Banking app, go to "My Account" (我的账户) → "All" → "Loan Clearance Certificate" (贷款结清证明) to view clearance details once the loan is fully repaid.

## What You Likely CANNOT Do via the App

The app does **not appear to offer a direct PDF download** of the complete, signed mortgage loan contract. The original signed paper contract is a legal document typically retained physically. If you need a full copy of your contract, the recommended steps are:

1. **Visit your loan-originating branch (贷款经办行)** in person, bring your ID, and request a printed copy.
2. **Call ABC's customer service hotline at 95599** to inquire about electronic contract retrieval options.
3. **Check if your loan was signed electronically** — if it was an e-contract, there may be options to retrieve it via the app or online banking, but this depends on when and how your loan was signed.
