Question: Intro to “罡”?

Answer:

“罡” (gāng) is a Chinese character with several meanings:

1. **Ancient astronomy / Taoism**

   * Refers to **天罡 (tiāngāng)**, the name of a group of stars in the Big Dipper area in ancient Chinese astronomy.
   * In Taoist culture, “天罡” represents a powerful celestial force.
   * Example: **天罡星**, **天罡步** (a Taoist ritual walking pattern).

2. **Meaning: strong, upright, powerful**

   * The character carries a sense of **正气、刚强、力量**.
   * It is related in meaning to “刚” (strong, firm), though the characters have different origins.

3. **Names**

   * “罡” is often used in Chinese names, company names, or fictional characters because it sounds powerful and has a martial/spiritual feeling.
   * Examples:

     * 天罡
     * 罡风 (strong celestial wind)
     * 九天罡气 (mythological powerful energy)

Character structure:

* **罒** (net/cover radical)
* **正** (upright/correct)
* Traditional association: a force that restrains evil and maintains order.
