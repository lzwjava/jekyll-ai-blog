Question: What is 环氧树脂胶 (epoxy resin adhesive)?

Answer: 环氧树脂胶 is **epoxy adhesive** (also called epoxy glue or epoxy resin glue) — a two-component structural adhesive widely used in industry and everyday life.**What it is:**
Epoxy adhesive consists of two parts — a **resin (A component)** and a **hardener (B component)**. When mixed together, they undergo a chemical reaction (polymerization) and cure into an extremely strong, rigid bond.

**Key properties:**
- Very high bonding strength
- Resistant to heat, chemicals, and moisture
- Can bond metals, ceramics, glass, wood, and some plastics
- Cures at room temperature (some variants need heat)
- Waterproof after curing

**Common uses:**
- Industrial manufacturing and repairs
- Automotive and aerospace parts
- Electronics and circuit board encapsulation
- Construction and structural bonding
- DIY home repairs

**How to use:**
Mix equal parts of resin and hardener, apply to the surface, press together, and allow to cure (anywhere from 5 minutes to 24 hours depending on the formula).
