**Question:** What is an introduction to Arizona State University (ASU)?

---

**Answer:**

**Arizona State University (ASU)** is a large public research university based in **Tempe, Arizona**, and is part of the Arizona public university system. Established in 1885 as a normal school, it has grown into one of the largest universities in the United States, with multiple campuses across the Phoenix metropolitan area and a significant online presence.

**Size & Enrollment**
ASU's fall 2024 full-time equivalent enrollment reached **136,650** — a 5.1% increase over the prior year — driven largely by growth in digital immersion enrollment.

**Academics**
ASU offers hundreds of undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral programs across fields including engineering, business, arts and sciences, health, and sustainability. It has over **400 undergraduate programs** and **450+ graduate** degree and certificate options, with **83 programs ranked in the top 25 nationally** and 38 in the top 10 by *U.S. News & World Report*.

**Innovation & Rankings**
For the **tenth consecutive year**, ASU was named the **nation's most innovative university** by *U.S. News & World Report*. It also ranked **#1 in the U.S. and in the global top 10** for advancing the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals for the fifth straight year, according to the *Times Higher Education Impact Rankings*.

**Research**
With nearly **$904 million in research expenditures** in FY 2023, ASU ranked among the **top five institutions without a medical school** — ahead of Caltech, Princeton, and Carnegie Mellon.

**Online Education**
**ASU Online** surpassed **100,000 graduates** in 2025, offering more than 350 degree programs and certificates, and is ranked **#7** in *U.S. News*'s Online Bachelor's Program Rankings — serving more students than the remaining top-10 programs combined.

**Global Community**
ASU serves a diverse student body from all **50 states and over 155 countries**, fostering a culture of public service and community engagement.

**Athletics**
ASU competes athletically as the **Sun Devils** in **NCAA Division I**.
