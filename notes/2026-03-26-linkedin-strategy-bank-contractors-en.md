Question: On LinkedIn, is it better to list your job title as "Backend Engineer at Chinasoft (or TEKsystems)" while contracting at SCB or DBS Bank, rather than showing the bank directly? And does having a verified LinkedIn email from SCB/DBS help with credibility?

Answer:

## LinkedIn Profile Strategy: Contractor Branding

### Why Listing the Vendor (Chinasoft / TEKsystems) Looks More Stable

When recruiters browse LinkedIn, they assess profile credibility quickly. Here's the comparison:

| Approach | How It Looks |
| --- | --- |
| "Backend Engineer at DBS Bank (Contract)" | Short tenure, raises red flags |
| "Backend Engineer at Chinasoft" | Looks like a perm employee, stable |
| "Backend Engineer at TEKsystems" | Known global staffing brand, credible |

Listing a vendor like **Chinasoft** or **TEKsystems** as your employer hides the "contract" stigma, since viewers see a company name without immediately knowing it's an outsourced role.

---

### The Best Hybrid Strategy

The recommended approach is exactly what you described:

> **Job Title:** Backend Engineer
> **Company:** Chinasoft International (or TEKsystems)
> **Description:** Deployed at Standard Chartered Bank — working on [your actual project/stack]

This gives you:

- ✅ A **permanent-looking employer** (Chinasoft/TEKsystems)
- ✅ **Bank-level project credibility** (SCB/DBS mentioned in description)
- ✅ **Longer tenure appearance** if you stay with the vendor across multiple deployments
- ✅ No short job-hop red flags

---

### LinkedIn Email Verification — Does It Matter?

Yes, this is an **underrated credibility booster**:

- If your **vendor (Chinasoft) doesn't provide a work email**, your LinkedIn verification stays unverified or uses a personal Gmail
- If **SCB or DBS provides you a work email** (e.g. `yourname@sc.com`), you can verify LinkedIn with that
- A verified `@sc.com` or `@dbs.com` email **signals to recruiters you are genuinely embedded in the bank**

> ⚠️ However, note that LinkedIn email verification shows the **domain**, not the full email. So `@sc.com` verified will display visibly.

---

### Summary Recommendation

| Factor | Best Choice |
| --- | --- |
| Company name shown | Chinasoft / TEKsystems (vendor) |
| Project details | Mention SCB/DBS in description |
| Email verification | Use SCB/DBS email if available |
| LinkedIn headline | "Backend Engineer \| Standard Chartered Bank Project" |

This strategy gives you the **stability perception of a perm role** while still showcasing your **top-tier banking client exposure** — the best of both worlds.
