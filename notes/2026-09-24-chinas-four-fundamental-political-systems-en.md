**Question:** What are China's fundamental political systems (基本政治制度)?

**Answer:**

China's political system is built on the foundation that the People's Republic of China is a socialist state under the people's democratic dictatorship, led by the working class and based on the alliance of workers and peasants. The core framework of China's political system consists of four fundamental institutions:

**1. People's Congress System (人民代表大会制度)**
The supreme organ of state power. This is the form of political power organization that corresponds to China's state nature (国体). The National People's Congress (NPC) is the highest legislative body, with local people's congresses at various levels beneath it.

**2. Multi-Party Cooperation and Political Consultation System (多党合作和政治协商制度)**
A basic political system under which the Communist Party of China (CPC), as the party in power, consults all non-Communist parties and representatives of non-party personages to reach a common understanding before taking major decisions. It was formally established in 1949. China's new-type party system includes the CPC, eight democratic parties, and non-party individuals. The eight democratic parties include the Revolutionary Committee of the Chinese Kuomintang, China Democratic League, China National Democratic Construction Association, China Association for Promoting Democracy, Chinese Peasants' and Workers' Democratic Party, China Zhi Gong Dang, Jiusan Society, and Taiwan Democratic Self-Government League.

**3. Regional Ethnic Autonomy System (民族区域自治制度)**
A system that grants ethnic minority regions a degree of self-governance within the framework of the unified Chinese state, allowing them to manage local affairs according to their own customs and needs.

**4. Community-Level Self-Governance System (基层群众自治制度)**
A grassroots democratic system in which rural and urban citizens manage public affairs in their organizations and communities. It is composed of villagers' committees in rural areas, neighborhood committees in urban areas, and conferences of workers and staff in businesses, enabling members to carry out democratic elections, decision-making, and supervision.

Together, these four systems form the core content and basic framework of China's political system and are the concentrated expression of socialist democratic politics.
