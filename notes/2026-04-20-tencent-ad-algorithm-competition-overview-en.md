Question: What is the “2020 Tencent Advertising Algorithm Competition”?

Answer:

The **“2020 Tencent Advertising Algorithm Competition (2020 腾讯广告算法大赛)”** is a **machine learning / AI competition organized by Tencent**, mainly focused on solving real-world problems in **online advertising and recommendation systems**.

### 1. What kind of competition is it?

It is a **data science + algorithm contest**, similar in nature to competitions like Kaggle:

* Participants (students, engineers, researchers) build models
* Use **real (desensitized) advertising data**
* Solve practical problems from Tencent’s ad system
* Compete based on model performance (accuracy, prediction quality, etc.)

Its goal is to:

* Improve **ad targeting accuracy**
* Enhance **user experience**
* Explore new **AI/ML techniques in industry scenarios** ([TrueSight][1])

---

### 2. What was special about the 2020 edition?

The 2020 competition had a specific task:

* **Topic:** *“Ad audience attribute prediction” (广告受众基础属性预估)*
* **Key idea:** “逆算” (reverse inference)

  * Instead of directly knowing user attributes
  * You infer them from **user behavior (clicks, interactions, etc.)**

This makes it challenging because:

* Data is incomplete or indirect
* Requires combining **machine learning + feature engineering + behavioral modeling** ([tc.ccf.org.cn][2])

---

### 3. Scale and influence

* Tens of thousands of participants globally
* Participants from **universities + companies worldwide**
* Considered one of the **top algorithm competitions in China** ([Tencent Cloud][3])

It’s also used by Tencent to:

* Discover talent (many winners get job offers)
* Test cutting-edge AI ideas in real business scenarios

---

### 4. Why people care about it

This competition is valuable because:

* It uses **industrial-scale data (not toy datasets)**
* Problems are **close to real production systems**
* Covers core AI areas:

  * Recommendation systems
  * NLP (e.g., BERT used in 2020 winners)
  * User behavior modeling
  * Ranking algorithms

---

### 5. In simple terms

You can think of it as:

> “A real-world AI competition where you build models to predict what ads users should see — and prove your model is better than others.”

---

### 6. If you see it on a resume

If someone mentions this:

* It usually means they have **strong ML / recommendation system skills**
* Especially relevant for:

  * Ads ranking
  * Recommender systems
  * Large-scale data modeling
