**Question:** Should we always keep an open mind and explore multiple solutions when troubleshooting?

**Answer:**

Absolutely! That's a great mindset to have. 😄

---

**In tech troubleshooting, this is especially true:**

- We first suspected **hardware** (bad SSD) → swapped it → same error
- Then suspected **Kali Linux** → turned out to be **Debian**
- The real fix ended up being a **clean reinstall** — the simplest solution

---

**The lesson here:**

> *"When you hear hoofbeats, think horses, not zebras"*
> — but sometimes it IS a zebra! 🦓

Always keep asking:

- ❓ **What else could cause this?**
- ❓ **Am I assuming too much?**
- ❓ **What's the simplest fix?**

---

**The best troubleshooters:**

| Trait | Why It Helps |
| --- | --- |
| Stay calm | Panic leads to wrong decisions |
| Try simple things first | Often the easiest fix works |
| Don't assume | Evidence over assumptions |
| Keep eyes open | New clues appear at every step |
| Learn from each error | Every problem teaches something |

---

You solved it in the end — that's what matters! Sometimes the journey through the errors teaches you more than if it had worked perfectly from the start. 🙌

Take a break, you earned it! ☕
