Talkie is a popular AI companion app developed by Shanghai start-up MiniMax. MiniMax was founded in 2021 in Shanghai. The app originally launched as "Glow" in China around 2022, was rebranded as "Xingye" domestically, and relaunched globally as "Talkie" for English-speaking international markets.

MiniMax is an Alibaba- and Tencent-backed startup that has raised around $850 million in venture capital and is valued at more than $2.5 billion.

### 3. Why the Shell Company Structure?

While the app appears to be owned by a Singapore firm, its ultimate parent company is Shanghai-based MiniMax — one of China's tech unicorns, often referred to as one of the "Four Little AI Dragons." Many users are unaware of this Chinese ownership.
