**Question:** What were the main topics and outcomes of the 19th National Congress of the Communist Party of China (党的十九大)?

---

**Answer:**

**Overview**

The 19th National Congress of the Chinese Communist Party (CPC) was held at the Great Hall of the People in Beijing from October 18 to 24, 2017, with 2,280 delegates representing the party's estimated 89 million members.

---

**Key Topics**

**1. "New Era" Declaration**
Xi Jinping's report to the Congress declared that socialism with Chinese characteristics had entered a "new era," and that the principal contradiction in Chinese society had evolved into one between unbalanced and inadequate development and the people's ever-growing needs for a better life.

**2. Two Centenary Goals — Long-term Development Plan**
In the first stage (2020–2035), China would build on the foundation of a moderately prosperous society and see socialist modernization basically realized. In the second stage (2035 to mid-21st century), China would be developed into a great modern socialist country that is prosperous, strong, democratic, culturally advanced, harmonious, and beautiful.

**3. Party Building & Anti-Corruption**
The Congress emphasized remarkable outcomes in ensuring full and strict governance over the Party, making sweeping efforts to strengthen party leadership and taking strong action to transform lax and weak governance.

**4. Military & Security**
Much of Xi's three-hour opening speech was devoted to military and security issues, as well as general aspirations about China's growing role in the world economy and global system up to 2049 — the 100th anniversary of the founding of the People's Republic.

---

**Key Results / Outcomes**

**1. Xi Jinping Thought Enshrined in Party Constitution**
One of the most important results was the inclusion of "Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era" as a guiding principle in the Party Constitution — elevating Xi to the level of historic leaders like Mao Zedong and Deng Xiaoping. It marked the first time since Mao Zedong Thought that a living party leader had enshrined an ideology named after himself into the party constitution.

**2. Party Constitution Amended**
The revised constitution framed Xi Jinping Thought as a continuation and development of Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the Theory of Three Represents, and the Scientific Outlook on Development.

**3. Xi Jinping Re-elected as General Secretary**
Xi Jinping was elected General Secretary of the CPC Central Committee for a second term at the First Plenary Session of the 19th CPC Central Committee on October 25.

**4. New Politburo Standing Committee**
Observers eagerly awaited the First Plenum of the 19th Central Committee, when the new members of the CCP's all-powerful Politburo Standing Committee were unveiled.

**5. State Constitution Updated (2018 Follow-up)**
In March 2018, a constitutional amendment was adopted at the National People's Congress, enshrining the statement that "the leadership of the CPC is the defining feature of socialism with Chinese characteristics" into the country's fundamental law.
