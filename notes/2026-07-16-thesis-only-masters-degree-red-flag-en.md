Question: You're describing a thesis-only "research master's" with no coursework — need the actual school name or URL to verify, but the pattern itself is worth flagging immediately.

Answer:

**This pattern is a strong red flag, independent of which school it is.**

Legitimate research master's degrees (MRes/MPhil in EU/UK systems) still require:
- Registered enrollment with ECTS credits tracked
- A supervisor assigned through the university's academic senate
- Formal thesis defense (viva) in front of an examination committee
- The degree appearing in the university's official student registry, checkable via national systems (e.g. Spain's RUCT, Italy's MIUR, UK's HESA)

"Just write a thesis, no classes, no defense mentioned" is the exact selling point used by diploma-mill brokers targeting Chinese professionals — often riding on the real reputation of UK "top-up" degrees (which *do* exist legitimately, e.g. completing a BA(Hons) top-up year after a foreign diploma) but stripping out the actual academic oversight.

Checks that separate real from fake, regardless of name:
1. **CSCSE/留服 recognized list** — search http://yxcx.cscse.edu.cn/rzyxmd for the exact institution name
2. **ENIC-NARIC** — https://www.enic-naric.net — confirms if the institution/degree is recognized in its home country
3. Ask the broker/school for: ECTS credit breakdown, name of assigned thesis supervisor with their faculty profile, and defense date/committee — if any of these are vague or "not required," that's disqualifying
4. Search `[school name] site:.edu` OR `[school name] 学历学位 认证` on Baidu/Zhihu — these mills get called out on Zhihu regularly
