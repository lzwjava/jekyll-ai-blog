**Depletion** means the **gradual reduction or use up of something until there is very little or none left**.

Here are a few examples:

* **Resource depletion:** "The depletion of forests is harming wildlife." (Forests are being used up or destroyed.)
* **Energy depletion:** "After working all day, she felt complete depletion." (Her energy was exhausted.)
* **Ozone depletion:** "Ozone depletion allows more harmful UV rays to reach Earth." (The ozone layer is becoming thinner.)

**Simple meaning:** *using up*, *running low*, or *becoming exhausted*.

For example:

* "The battery is depleted." = The battery has almost no charge left.
* "The depletion of water supplies is a serious problem." = Water supplies are running out.
