## Question: Intro to TVB Limited in Hong Kong?

TVB Limited (formerly **Television Broadcasts Limited**, Chinese: 無綫電視 / 無綫集團) is one of the most iconic media companies in Hong Kong. It is best known as **Hong Kong's first free-to-air commercial television station** and a major producer of Cantonese TV dramas, variety shows, news, and entertainment programs. ([TVB Limited][1])

## What business does TVB do?

TVB is no longer just a TV station. It operates a broader media ecosystem: ([TVB Limited][3])

### 1. Free-to-air television

Its famous channels include:

* **TVB Jade (翡翠台)** — Cantonese flagship channel
* **TVB Pearl** — English-language channel
* News and HD channels

TVB dramas and programs have historically dominated Hong Kong television culture. ([TVB Limited][1])

Examples of famous TVB productions:

* Hong Kong police dramas
* Historical wuxia dramas
* Family dramas
* Variety shows

## Historical importance in Hong Kong

TVB is deeply connected with Hong Kong popular culture.

For decades:

* Many Hong Kong actors became famous through TVB.
* Cantonese dramas spread Hong Kong culture internationally.
* TVB became part of the collective memory of generations of Hong Kong people.

Actors such as Chow Yun-fat, Andy Lau, and many others had connections with the Hong Kong television industry.
