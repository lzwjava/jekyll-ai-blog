Question: For QMAS academic transcripts, should I submit English transcripts or original Chinese transcripts?

Answer:

For Hong Kong QMAS, **Chinese or English documents are acceptable**. If your transcript is in Chinese, you normally **do not need to translate it** because Chinese is one of the accepted languages. Documents that are **not in Chinese or English** require certified translation. ([Immigration Department][1])

For a Mainland Chinese university transcript, the best practice is:

## Preferred submission

### Option 1 (best)

Upload:

```
Transcript_Chinese_English.pdf
```

containing:

1. Original Chinese transcript
2. Official English translation (if your university provides it)
3. University stamp/seal

This is the most internationally friendly format.

---

### Option 2

Chinese-only transcript:

```
成绩单.pdf
```

Usually acceptable because Hong Kong Immigration Department handles Chinese documents. ([Immigration Department][1])

---

## For degree certificate

Prepare:

```
Degree_Certificate.pdf
```

Include:

* 中文毕业证
* 中文学位证
* English versions if available

If your university can issue bilingual certificates, use those.

---

## For QMAS specifically

The important thing is not the language; it is **verification and credibility**:

* University name
* Degree title
* Graduation date
* Transcript
* Official stamp
* Ability for ImmD to verify

QMAS requires supporting documents for academic/professional qualifications, and applicants may need to provide verification proof issued by a third-party credential verification organization or the awarding institution. ([Immigration Department][2])
