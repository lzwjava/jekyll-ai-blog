Yes, you're right to prepare for roadside emergencies. Here's a practical checklist:

## 🚗 What to Keep in the Car

**Spare tire & tire tools**
- Spare tire (full-size or space-saver) — check air pressure periodically
- Jack and lug wrench
- Wheel chocks (to stop the car rolling while jacked up)

**Repair basics**
- Tire repair kit (plugs + sealant) for small punctures
- Portable air compressor (12V)
- Jumper cables / portable jump-starter
- Basic tool kit (screwdrivers, pliers, wrench)
- Duct tape & zip ties

**Emergency & safety**
- Reflective warning triangle or flares
- Flashlight + extra batteries
- First aid kit
- Fire extinguisher
- Blanket, water, non-perishable snacks
- Phone charger / power bank

## 🆘 For a Long Trip

- **Roadside assistance membership** — the single most useful thing. Services like AAA, insurance roadside add-ons, or car manufacturer programs will tow a disabled car, jump-start, bring fuel, or change a tire.
- **Emergency numbers** saved in your phone (local police, your insurer's roadside hotline, towing services).
- **Insurance & registration docs** easily accessible.

## ⚠️ If a Tire Fails on the Road

1. Pull over to a safe, flat spot off the road.
2. Turn on hazard lights and place the warning triangle.
3. If safe and you know how: swap on the spare. Otherwise stay in the car and call roadside assistance.
4. Call emergency services (e.g. 110/122 in China, 911 in the US, 119 in many other countries) if you're in danger or blocking traffic.
