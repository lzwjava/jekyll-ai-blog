**Question:** Besides Monumetric, what other ad networks can I consider to display ads on my blog?

**Answer:**

Here's a breakdown of solid alternatives, grouped by traffic level:

---

**For Low / New Traffic Blogs**

- **Google AdSense** — The most accessible starting point with no strict traffic minimum. Easy to set up, widely supported.
- **Ezoic** — Ezoic has no hard traffic minimum, but at least 10K monthly sessions are recommended. It uses AI to optimize ad placement and boost RPM.
- **Infolinks** — Infolinks specializes in in-text and contextual ad formats that complement traditional display ads. It's not a primary solution but enhances revenue when layered strategically.
- **Media.net** — Powered by the Yahoo/Bing Network, it specializes in contextual advertising and excels for publishers with US and UK traffic. Works well even with modest traffic.

---

**For Mid-Tier Blogs (10K–50K+ pageviews/month)**

- **Journey by Mediavine** — Journey by Mediavine is designed for growing websites to improve ad performance before qualifying for full Mediavine.
- **Monetag (formerly PropellerAds)** — Monetag has one of the best ad unit selections for bloggers, offering banners, push notifications, and onclick formats.
- **Sovrn** — Sovrn helps content creators make more money by providing tools for both advertising and affiliate marketing, helping them be more efficient and profitable.

---

**For Established / High-Traffic Blogs (50K+ sessions/month)**

- **Mediavine** — A premium managed ad network highly regarded for lifestyle, food, and travel blogs with strong RPMs.
- **Raptive (formerly AdThrive)** — Raptive focuses primarily on bloggers in lifestyle niches such as food, home, travel, and parenting, and even guarantees payouts — so even if an advertiser doesn't pay, Raptive still pays you.
- **Amazon Publisher Services (APS)** — A comprehensive suite of cloud-based tools that combines advanced technology with a focus on privacy for monetizing digital content.

---

**Quick Recommendation by Stage:**

| Traffic Level | Best Pick |
|---|---|
| Just starting | Google AdSense or Media.net |
| 10K–50K pageviews | Ezoic or Journey by Mediavine |
| 50K+ sessions | Mediavine or Raptive |
