**Question:** How has the Bigo Live app been performing in recent years, and is it doing well overall?

**Answer:**

Bigo Live's recent story is a mixed bag — strong financial scale, but with serious reputational and regulatory headwinds.

---

**📈 Financial Performance (Mostly Positive)**

BIGO segment revenues hit $1.99 billion in 2024, a 3.3% year-over-year increase, with non-GAAP operating profit at $286.3 million. On the monetization front, in 2025, Bigo Live ranked as the highest-grossing live streaming app worldwide by in-app purchase revenue, beating out Twitch.

However, live streaming revenue has been softening. Live streaming revenues were $439.5 million in Q3 2024, down from $495.8 million in Q3 2023, mainly due to decreased average revenue per paying user (ARPPU). To compensate, other revenues (primarily advertising) grew 67.2% to $119.2 million in Q3 2024.

---

**👥 User Base (Large but Nuanced)**

Bigo Live has over 500 million registered users across more than 150 countries, and as of Q4 2024, reported 38.4 million monthly active users — a 4.5% quarterly increase. July 2025 viewership reached 28.8 million hours watched, surpassing Steam (23.7M) and Facebook Live (22M).

---

**⚠️ Major Controversy & App Store Crisis (Late 2024)**

This is the biggest red flag. A December 2024 New York Times investigation revealed descriptions of child sexual abuse occurring during broadcasts to solicit virtual gifts. This prompted both Apple and Google to remove the app from their stores by mid-December 2024, causing a 3.5% drop in parent company JOYY's share price — significant given Bigo accounted for 89% of its quarterly revenue.

JOYY subsequently announced that Bigo Live was reinstated on the Google Play Store and was in active discussions with Apple for reinstatement on the iOS App Store.

---

**🔮 2025 Strategy**

In 2025, the strategy represents a fundamental shift — from chasing user scale to supporting "mid-tier creators" building loyal niche communities. The platform is also integrating social commerce through "Bigo Marketplace" and in-stream shopping features.

---

**Verdict:** Bigo Live is financially strong and globally dominant in live streaming monetization, but the child abuse scandal of late 2024 was a serious blow to its reputation and app store standing. Its long-term health depends heavily on improving content moderation credibility.
