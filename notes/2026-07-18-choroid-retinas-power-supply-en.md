**脉络膜 = choroid**. It is a layer of tissue in the eye, located **between the retina and the sclera (white outer wall)**.

Eye structure from outside → inside:

```
Sclera (巩膜, white protective layer)
        ↓
Choroid (脉络膜)  ← blood vessel layer
        ↓
Retina (视网膜)   ← photoreceptors capture light
        ↓
Vitreous body (玻璃体)
        ↓
Lens (晶状体)
```

The main functions:

### 1. Supply oxygen and nutrients

The retina consumes a lot of energy, especially the photoreceptor cells (rods and cones).
The choroid contains a dense network of blood vessels that feeds the outer retina.

Think of it like:

```
GPU chip ← cooling + power delivery
retina   ← choroid blood supply
```

The retina is the "sensor", and the choroid is the "power infrastructure".

---

### 2. Remove metabolic waste

Photoreceptors constantly renew their outer segments. The choroid helps remove waste products through circulation.

---

### 3. Control eye temperature and light absorption

The dark pigment in the choroid absorbs stray light, reducing internal reflections and improving image quality.

---

### Relationship with myopia (近视)

This is the interesting part.

In myopia research, scientists study **choroidal thickness (脉络膜厚度)**.

A simplified relationship:

```
Eye grows too long
        ↓
Retina moves backward
        ↓
Image focuses before retina
        ↓
Myopia
```

The choroid can change thickness:

* **Thicker choroid** → often associated with slowing eye elongation
* **Thinner choroid** → often associated with myopia progression

Some myopia-control methods affect choroidal thickness temporarily:

* outdoor light exposure → increases dopamine signaling → often associated with choroidal thickening
* atropine eye drops → can influence choroidal response
* orthokeratology → changes peripheral retinal signals and choroid behavior

But:

**Choroid thickness is more like a biomarker / response mechanism, not a simple "repair switch".**

---

A useful analogy:

```
Camera:

Lens          = 晶状体
Film/sensor   = 视网膜
Power/cooling = 脉络膜
Camera body   = 巩膜
```

The retina takes the picture; the choroid keeps the retina alive and functioning. For myopia, the key issue is usually **eyeball axial length (眼轴长度)**, and the choroid participates in regulating that process.
