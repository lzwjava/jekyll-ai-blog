## Northwestern Polytechnical University (NPU / 西北工业大学)

### Overview

Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU; 西北工业大学) is a public science and engineering university located in Xi'an, Shaanxi, China. It is affiliated with the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, and is part of Project 211, Project 985, and the Double First-Class Construction — China's most prestigious national university programs.

NPU is the only multidisciplinary and research-oriented university in China that simultaneously develops education and research programs in the fields of **aeronautics, astronautics, and marine technology engineering** — a distinction that makes it uniquely positioned among Chinese universities.

---

### History

NPU was founded in **1938** in Hanzhong and moved to Xi'an in October 1957, formed by combining the Northwest Institute of Engineering with the East China Institute of Aeronautics. Later in 1970, the Department of Aeronautics of Harbin Institute of Engineering merged with the university.

NPU was designated as one of the National Key Universities of China in 1960. It was among the first batch of universities selected for Project 211 in 1995 and became part of Project 985 in 2001. It was one of China's first universities to establish a graduate school and a university science park.

---

### Academic Programs & Scale

The university has 16 academic schools, offering 65 undergraduate programs, 120 postgraduate programs, 71 doctoral programs, and 17 postdoctoral programs. NPU currently has 22 disciplines at the doctoral level and 32 at the master's level. NPU has more than 3,500 faculty members, including 15 scholars from the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS) and the Chinese Academy of Engineering (CAE).

For international students, NPU offers 13 English-taught undergraduate programs and 77 English-taught postgraduate programs.

---

### Research & Rankings

NPU is one of the **Seven Sons of National Defence** and a member of SAP University Alliances. In 2024, it was listed among the world's top 300 universities in both the Academic Ranking of World Universities and the U.S. News & World Report Best Global University Ranking. NPU's engineering ranked tenth among China's engineering universities. The university has seven State Key Laboratories and 28 Province/Ministry-level Key Laboratories.

In the QS World University Rankings 2026, NPU is ranked approximately #499 globally.

---

### Campuses

NPU's campuses comprise about 4.58 km², with the **Youyi Campus** (old campus) located in Beilin District, Xi'an (~0.8 km²), the **Chang'an Campus** (new campus) in Chang'an District, Xi'an (~2.6 km²), and the **Taicang Campus** in Suzhou, Jiangsu (~1.18 km²).

---

### Strengths & Reputation

NPU is a top-tier Chinese engineering and research university with exceptional strengths in aeronautics, astronautics, and marine engineering. It offers rigorous technical training, strong industry connections, and good research opportunities — particularly suitable for students focused on aerospace, defense-related engineering, or advanced manufacturing.

NPU hosts a diverse community of students from over 100 countries, positioning itself as a globally engaged institution bridging cultures and fostering excellence in science and technology.

---

### Notable Alumni

Among NPU's notable alumni are **Yang Wei**, president of Zhejiang University and renowned aircraft designer, as well as **Yuan-Cheng Fung**, a pioneering figure in biomedical engineering from one of NPU's predecessor institutions.
