**Question:** What are the top GPU repair companies in China and the USA?

**Answer:**

Here are the most well-regarded GPU repair companies in both countries:

---

**🇺🇸 USA**

**NorthridgeFix** — widely regarded by the hardware community as one of the best GPU repair services in the US, particularly strong on the West Coast.

**GPU Lab** — a highly specialized US firm focused on professional NVIDIA GPU repairs and upgrades, including RTX 4090 48GB VRAM upgrades and Datacenter SXM4/5 repairs. They are IPC7095 BGA certified and manufacture in the USA.

**Ewel Repair Services** — a well-reviewed specialist capable of chip-level work such as GPU core reballing and trace repairs on high-end cards like the RTX 4090 and RTX 3090.

**Prime Tech Support** (Miami) — offers comprehensive GPU repair services for all AMD and Nvidia cards including the 4090 series, with expert microsoldering technicians.

**ZapFixers** (Nationwide/Miami) — provides professional mail-in GPU repair across the US, fixing issues like no display, artifacting, crashes, and power problems.

**Nerds on Call** — a national chain offering graphics card repair, GPU troubleshooting, and replacement services for desktop computers, gaming PCs, and high-performance workstations.

---

**🇨🇳 China**

**Donewin Intelligent Technology (Beijing)** — a professional GPU repair enterprise focused on AI server chip-level repair and system-level diagnosis, with deep expertise in BGA repair and video memory replacement, having serviced hundreds of customers.

> Note: China's GPU repair ecosystem is less consolidated into named brands compared to the US. Many repairs are handled through local electronics markets (like Huaqiangbei in Shenzhen) or individual technicians with strong reputations on platforms like Taobao/1688.
