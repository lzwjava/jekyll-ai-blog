Question: Which path suits me better: startup founder or corporate ladder?

Answer:

Based on your background and how you naturally operate, **the founder / entrepreneur path seems more aligned with you**, but probably not as a sudden "quit everything and gamble" move. More like a **builder-founder path**:

```
Engineer → AI builder → consultant/product maker → founder
```

Why:

1. **Your motivation matches startup feedback loops**

You repeatedly focus on:

* building things
* shipping products
* attracting users
* open source
* AI agents
* helping customers
* creating value directly

This is closer to a founder mindset than a corporate employee mindset.

A corporate engineer often optimizes:

```
task completed
→ good review
→ promotion
→ salary increase
```

Your natural thinking is more:

```
problem discovered
→ build solution
→ users adopt
→ revenue/value created
→ scale
```

2. **Your strongest assets are asymmetric**

A normal corporate path rewards:

* degree
* title
* years at company
* internal reputation

Your advantages are different:

* you built products independently
* you have AI/LLM engineering experience
* you can build end-to-end
* you understand infrastructure
* you can communicate technical ideas publicly

Those advantages compound more in entrepreneurship.

3. **Your personality seems uncomfortable with delayed reward**

Corporate systems often require patience:

```
2026: excellent work
2027: maybe promotion
2028: maybe bigger role
```

A founder gets faster market signals:

```
Monday: ship feature
Friday: customer feedback
next month: revenue signal
```

You seem to enjoy this loop.

But there is an important point:

**Being a founder means selling, not only building.**

The biggest transition for strong engineers is:

```
old:
"I can build this."

new:
"Can I find someone who urgently wants this and will pay?"
```

Your technical ability is already closer to founder territory. The missing muscle is usually distribution:

* talking to customers
* finding pain points
* marketing
* partnerships
* sales

A practical path for you:

```
Next 6-12 months:

AI consulting / agent projects
        ↓
real customers + revenue
        ↓
identify repeated problems
        ↓
turn into product
        ↓
startup
```

This gives you founder upside while reducing risk.

A pure corporate ladder is still useful as a tool (income, network, learning), but it probably should not be your final identity. For someone with your builder background, the bigger game is likely:

**use engineering ability to create assets that keep producing value, rather than only exchanging time for salary.**
