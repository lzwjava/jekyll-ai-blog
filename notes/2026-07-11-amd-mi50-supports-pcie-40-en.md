Question: Does AMD MI 50 use PCIe 4.0?

Answer:
Yes, the AMD Radeon Instinct MI50 natively supports and uses a **PCIe 4.0 x16** interface. When it launched, it was marketed by AMD as one of the world's first PCIe Gen 4-capable server GPUs. It remains fully backward compatible with PCIe 3.0 slots as well.
