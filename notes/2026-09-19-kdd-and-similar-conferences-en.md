**KDD = ACM SIGKDD Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining.**

It is one of the major conferences around **machine learning + data mining + applied AI + large-scale data systems**. The 2026 conference was held **Aug 9–13 in Jeju, Korea**. ([KDD 2026][1])

The important thing is that modern KDD is much broader than the name "data mining" suggests.

### What people actually talk about

KDD 2026 had tracks for:

* **Research** — ML/data mining algorithms and methods
* **Applied Data Science** — real-world ML systems and applications
* **Datasets & Benchmarks**
* **AI for Science** ([KDD 2026][2])

And the 2026 program had a surprisingly large amount of **LLM/agent work**:

* Agentic Software Engineering
* Agentic AI evaluation/trustworthiness
* Enterprise AI Agents
* AI agents for information retrieval
* Scientific/Societal agents
* Secure & trustworthy LLMs
* Graph ML for the large-model era
* Multi-turn RL for LLMs
* RAG
* LLM interpretability
* LLMs writing system kernels for AI accelerators
* AI for reasoning ([KDD 2026][3])

So for someone doing **LLM training, reasoning, agents, RL, evaluation and AI systems**, KDD is definitely relevant now.

## One particularly interesting thing about KDD 2026

The **workshop/tutorial layer** is arguably more interesting for you than just the main papers.

For example, KDD 2026 had a hands-on tutorial specifically on:

> **Multi-Turn Reinforcement Learning for Large Language Models**

and another on **teaching LLMs to write system kernels for AI accelerators**, covering post-training, reasoning and agentic optimization. ([KDD 2026][4])

That is very close to the stuff you've been asking about recently:
