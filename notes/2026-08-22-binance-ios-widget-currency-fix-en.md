**Question:** How do I change the Binance iOS widget display currency from CNY to USD?

**Answer:** The widget currency follows the app's global currency setting. Here's how to change it:The currency setting in the Binance app controls what currency is displayed throughout the app, including the iOS widget. You can find it under **Settings → Currency**.

If the widget still shows CNY after changing, try removing and re-adding the widget from your home screen.
