Question: What are OpenAI and Anthropic currently hiring for?

Answer:

## 🤖 OpenAI

As of late September 2026, OpenAI has around **82 open roles**, representing roughly 0.75% of its ~10,877 employees — a relatively low hiring velocity for a company its size.

**Top hiring departments:**
Engineering (22 roles), Operations (7), and Design (6) lead the open positions.

**Recent notable roles:**
Newly posted openings include Head of Data Science, Applied AI Engineer (Codex), Data Scientist B2B, Model Policy Manager (National Security), Cyber Enablement Lead, Software Engineer (Implicit Signals), and Growth Leads for Southeast Asia and Japan.

**Location focus:**
San Francisco accounts for over 62% of openings, followed by broader U.S. remote roles and New York City.

**Big picture:**
OpenAI has been aiming to nearly double its workforce to 8,000 employees by end of 2026, with most new hires targeted at product development, engineering, research, and sales.

## 🧠 Anthropic

As of late September 2026, Anthropic has around **150 open roles** — about 2.5% of its ~5,997 employees — reflecting steady, moderate growth.

**Top hiring departments:**
Engineering leads with 48 openings, followed by Project Management (11) and Sales (10). Legal and Customer Services also have active listings.

**Recent notable roles:**
Recent postings include AI Deployment (Beneficial Deployments), External Affairs (South Korea), Head of Regulated Industries for Customer Success, Social Escalations Program Manager, and Solutions Marketing Manager roles in Cybersecurity and Healthcare.

Anthropic is also hiring Applied AI Architects, AWS Partnerships Leads, and roles in data center energy and regulatory investigations across global offices.

**Location focus:**
San Francisco (~41%), New York City (~29%), and Seattle (~11%) are the primary hiring hubs, with a growing international presence in London, Tokyo, Seoul, and Munich.

**Big picture:**
Key departments for 2026 include Alignment Science, Frontier Systems, Product Engineering, and Sales, with Anthropic expanding particularly across European and Asian markets.
