The word **"besiege"** is a verb that means to surround a place (such as a city or fortress) with armed forces in order to capture it or force those inside to surrender. It often involves cutting off supplies and communication to weaken the defenders.

**Example:**
*"The army decided to besiege the castle, cutting off all food and water supplies to force the enemy to surrender."*

In a broader sense, it can also mean to overwhelm or flood someone with questions, requests, or problems:
**Example:**
*"After the announcement, the CEO was besieged by reporters asking for details."*
