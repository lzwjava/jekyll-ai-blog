**Question:** What AI-related courses and master's programs does Georgia Tech offer online?

---

**Answer:**

Georgia Tech offers **two main online master's paths** focused on AI/ML:

---

## 1. 🎓 Online Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence (OMS AI) — *New Program*

Georgia Tech's Online Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence (OMS AI) is an interdisciplinary degree program designed for professionals seeking to deepen their expertise in a rapidly evolving, AI-driven world. It provides rigorous instruction in **AI fundamentals, machine learning, deep learning, predictive modeling, decision support, and human-AI interaction**, with applications across business, healthcare, engineering, and other domains.

The curriculum combines technical rigor with practical application, including a **required practicum** for hands-on experience with real organizational challenges. It also covers ethical, privacy, and societal considerations of AI.

The Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia has approved this new OMS AI program; it is specifically designed to support working professionals. Applications are currently being collected for **Fall 2027**.

---

## 2. 🎓 Online Master of Science in Computer Science (OMSCS) — AI & ML Courses

The OMSCS is a well-established program comprising courses taught by world-class faculty at the Georgia Tech College of Computing.

**OMSCS Specializations relevant to AI:**

The **Machine Learning specialization** (15 hours) requires one algorithms course (e.g., CS 6515 Intro to Graduate Algorithms) plus CS 7641 Machine Learning as core, and electives chosen from CS 6476 Computer Vision, CS 6601 AI, CS 6603 AI Ethics, CS 7280 Network Science, CS 7476 Advanced Computer Vision, CS 7641 Machine Learning Theory, and others.

The **Artificial Intelligence specialization** is designed for students with a background in algorithms and computational thinking, with core courses including CS 6601 Artificial Intelligence. The **Computational Perception & Robotics specialization** covers the intersection of perception, robotics, and AI, with core courses including CS 6601 AI, CS 7638 AI Techniques for Robotics, and CS 7649 Robot Intelligence: Planning.
