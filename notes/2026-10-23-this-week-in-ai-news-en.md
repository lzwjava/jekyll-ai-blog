**🔐 US Bans DeepSeek from Federal Systems (Oct 21)**

The Department of Homeland Security ordered all federal civilian agencies to stop purchasing DeepSeek products and remove existing Chinese AI technology from government IT systems, citing national security risks tied to potential data access by Chinese authorities. The ban also extends to third-party services built using DeepSeek technology. China's Foreign Ministry pushed back, calling it an overreach of national security concerns.

ChatGPT now lets users secure sensitive conversations with a four-digit PIN or biometric authentication, keeping private discussions hidden from chat history and search results.

**⚡ Google & Amazon Challenge Nvidia's PyTorch Grip**

Google launched TorchTPU as an open-source project, letting developers run PyTorch apps on Google's TPUs with minimal code changes. Amazon made a parallel move with TorchNeuron for its Trainium chips. Both aim to make it easier to move AI workloads away from Nvidia hardware. Since PyTorch powers roughly 85% of AI apps, this is seen as a serious push to erode Nvidia's advantage.
