Question: How can I assemble an IKEA desk manually without using power tools?

Answer: Constructing an IKEA desk by hand is actually the recommended method, as power drills can easily strip the particle board or over-tighten the cams. Most IKEA desks (like the Malm, Lagkapten, or Alex series) follow a very similar assembly logic.

### 1. Prepare Your Workspace

* **Clear the Floor:** You’ll need a space roughly twice the size of the desk surface.
* **Use the Box:** Lay the cardboard packaging flat on the floor to act as a rug. This prevents the desk finish from scratching and protects your flooring.
* **Inventory Check:** Open all hardware bags and group "like with like" (screws, wooden dowels, cam locks).

### 2. The Manual Toolkit

You don't need a full workshop. Most desks require only:

* **A Phillips-head screwdriver:** Size #2 is standard.
* **A Flat-head screwdriver:** For tightening cam locks.
* **A Small Hammer/Mallet:** For tapping in wooden dowels or plastic feet.
* **The Allen Key:** Usually included in the box for leg attachments.

### 3. Core Assembly Steps

1. **Insert the Dowels:** Gently tap wooden dowels into the side panels. If they are tight, use your palm or a light hammer strike. **Pro tip:** Never use glue unless the manual specifically says so; you might need to take it apart later.
2. **Screw in the Cam Bolts:** Use your screwdriver to turn these into the pre-drilled holes until the "shoulder" of the bolt sits flush against the wood. Do not over-tighten.
3. **Connect Panels:** Align the holes of one panel with the cam bolts/dowels of another. Push them together firmly.
4. **Lock the Cam Locks:** Insert the circular "cams" into the large holes, then turn them clockwise (usually a half-turn) with your flat-head screwdriver until you feel resistance. This pulls the panels together securely.

### 4. Important Tips for Success

* **Orientation Matters:** Look closely at the diagrams for small holes. If a hole is shown on the left in the manual but yours is on the right, you have the piece upside down or backward.
* **Don't Force It:** If a screw isn't going in straight, back it out and try again. Forcing it will crack the laminate.
* **The "Two-Person Rule":** For desks with long tabletops (like the 160cm+ versions), have a friend help you flip the desk upright once assembled to avoid putting too much lateral stress on the legs.
