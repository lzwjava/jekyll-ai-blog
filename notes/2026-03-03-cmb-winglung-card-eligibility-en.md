**Question: How about the CMB Wing Lung Bank case? I have that account already.**

**Answer:**

Yes, as a mainland Chinese citizen with an existing personal account at **CMB Wing Lung Bank** (opened using your Mainland Travel Permit for Hong Kong and Macao, along with other required ID like PRC ID card or passport), you are eligible to apply for certain **credit cards**, particularly as a non-Hong Kong resident.

CMB Wing Lung Bank explicitly allows **non-Hong Kong residents** (including mainland Chinese applicants) to apply for credit cards, with specific requirements tailored for them.

Key points from the bank's policies:

- **Principal Card Applicant (Non-Hong Kong Resident)** must maintain **no less than HK$100,000** (or equivalent in other currencies) of **unpledged assets** in CMB Wing Lung Bank for the latest 3 months (or sometimes stated as maintained in the bank).
- You need to provide identification documents, such as a copy of your valid **passport** / **Exit-Entry Permit for Travelling to and from Hong Kong and Macao** (your entry permit), plus your PRC Resident Identity Card.
- Proof of residential address (within the latest 3 months, e.g., utility bill or bank statement).
- Proof of income or assets: This can include bank statements showing salary deposits (from Hong Kong or mainland China), latest income tax documents (from HK Inland Revenue Department or mainland China), or employer salary certification. For existing customers like you, the asset requirement (HK$100,000+) is a key threshold.
- Special notes for certain applicants (e.g., under Quality Migrant schemes) may apply additional visa/entry proof, but for standard mainland residents, the above covers it.
- One prominent option is the **CMB Wing Lung UnionPay Dual Currency Platinum Card** (HKD + CNY), which has a dedicated application process for non-Hong Kong residents, including a mandatory "Client Declaration – Non-Hong Kong Resident" section. This card supports spending in mainland China (billed in CNY) and elsewhere (billed in HKD), with benefits like annual fee waivers for the first 5 years and overseas transaction fee waivers.
- Other Visa/Mastercard cards (e.g., Platinum, Gold, Classic) may also be available to non-residents if you meet the asset and income criteria, though some general forms mention Hong Kong permanent residents in examples, but the documents required explicitly accommodate non-residents.
- Approval depends on the bank's credit assessment, your income stability, credit history, and meeting the minimum asset threshold in your CMB Wing Lung account. Since you already have an account, this makes the process easier (you can leverage your existing relationship and asset balance).
- It is **not** the case that credit cards require Hong Kong citizenship or HKID only — non-residents are supported, especially for UnionPay dual-currency products and with sufficient assets deposited.

Compared to BOCHK, CMB Wing Lung appears more straightforward for non-residents with the explicit HK$100,000 asset requirement for existing customers, making it a viable option if your account balance qualifies.

For the latest details, confirmation of your specific eligibility (based on your account assets and income proof), or to apply, visit a CMB Wing Lung branch in Hong Kong, use their mobile app/online channels if available for credit card applications, or contact their customer service hotline at (852) 2309 5555. Policies and requirements can be updated, so check directly with the bank.
