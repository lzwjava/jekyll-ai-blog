Question: What does "coalesced" mean?

Answer: **Coalesced** means to come together and merge into a single unified whole. It describes the process of separate things joining, combining, or fusing so they form one cohesive entity.

It can be used in several contexts:

- **Physical:** Droplets of water coalesce into a larger puddle.
- **Abstract/Ideas:** Scattered thoughts coalesce into a clear plan.
- **Social:** Small groups coalesce into a movement.
- **Data/Tech:** Memory blocks are coalesced to reduce fragmentation.

The word comes from the Latin *coalescere* — *co-* (together) + *alescere* (to grow).

> "Over time, the various factions **coalesced** into a single political party."
