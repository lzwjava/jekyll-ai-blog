The question asks: **Which of the following sentences from *The Official Biography of the Five Dynasties' Musicians* (《五代史伶官传序》) is quoted from the pre-Qin classic *The Book of Documents* (《尚书》)?**

---

### **Correct Answer:**
**B. 满招损，谦得益 (Arrogance leads to loss, modesty brings gain)**

---

### **Explanation:**
- The phrase **"满招损，谦得益"** originates from *The Book of Documents* (《尚书》), specifically from the chapter **"大禹谟" (The Counsels of the Great Yu)**. It means that arrogance and complacency lead to harm, while humility and modesty bring benefits.

- The other options are not direct quotations from *The Book of Documents*:
  - **A**: "盛衰之理，虽曰天命，岂非人事哉" (The principles of rise and fall, though called the will of heaven, are they not also human affairs?) is a reflection by the author, Ouyang Xiu, and not a direct quotation from *The Book of Documents*.
  - **C**: "忧劳可以兴国，逸豫可以亡身" (Diligence and worry can strengthen a nation, while indulgence and leisure can destroy oneself) is a famous saying from *The Official Biography of the Five Dynasties' Musicians*, but it is not from *The Book of Documents*.
  - **D**: "夫祸患常积于忽微，而智勇多困于所溺" (Calamities often accumulate from neglect of small things, and wisdom and courage are often hindered by one's own weaknesses) is also Ouyang Xiu's own observation, not a quotation from *The Book of Documents*.

### **Summary:**
The correct answer is **B**, as it is the only option directly quoted from *The Book of Documents* (《尚书》).
