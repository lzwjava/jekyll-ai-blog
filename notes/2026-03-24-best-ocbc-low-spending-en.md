Question: Which OCBC credit card is best for low fees and easy use, given only about S$200 total spend per year?

Answer:

## Best OCBC Credit Card for Low Spending (~S$200/year)

**Important context first:** S$200/year = roughly **S$16–17/month**. This is very low spending, so you want a card with:
- No or very low annual fee
- No minimum monthly spend requirement (or easy to waive)
- Simple, no-hassle rewards

---

## ✅ Top Recommendation: OCBC FRANK Credit Card

This is clearly the best fit for your situation.

The FRANK Credit Card has **no annual fee for the first 2 years**. After that, the annual fee is waived if you spend at least S$10,000 in a year.

However, since you only spend S$200/year, you won't hit that waiver threshold — but OCBC credit card annual fees are usually waived for the first 2 years upon card application, and after that you can request a further fee waiver. Most users successfully get the fee waived by simply calling in or requesting via the OCBC app.

**Why FRANK suits your profile:**

| Feature | FRANK Card |
|---|---|
| Annual fee | Free for 2 years, then ~S$80/year (waivable) |
| Min. income required | S$30,000 (SG/PR aged 21–54) |
| Min. monthly spend for cashback | S$800/month for enhanced rate |
| Flat cashback if below min. spend | **0.3% on all spend** |
| Foreign currency cashback | Yes (online & in-store) |
| Easy to qualify | ✅ Yes — entry-level card for young adults |

The OCBC Frank card is great for young adults and freshly minted working adults, with a low annual fee that remains at S$80 even as you get older.

## 🔁 Alternative: OCBC INFINITY Cashback Card

If you want truly zero hassle and don't want to track minimum spend at all:

The OCBC Infinity Card offers a flat **1.6% unlimited cashback** with no minimum spend required.

However, this card has a higher annual fee (~S$196/year), so it's less ideal if you only spend S$200/year — the fee would cost more than your cashback earned.
