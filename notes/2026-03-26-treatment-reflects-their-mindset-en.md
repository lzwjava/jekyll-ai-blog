Question:
Do people treat us mainly based on their own personality and mindset, and can this change over time (for example, someone kind when young becoming more critical when older)?

Answer:

Yes — how people treat you is influenced **more by their internal traits than by you**. Their personality, beliefs, emotional state, and life experience shape their behavior toward many people, not just you.

### 1. People project their inner world outward

* Kind people usually treat **most people** kindly
* Critical people often judge **many people**, not just one
* Suspicious people tend to **distrust everyone**
* Generous people help **without calculating**

This happens because behavior comes from **mental models**, not from each individual interaction.

For example:

* A pessimistic person sees flaws everywhere
* A confident person assumes good intent
* A fearful person expects danger
* A secure person is more tolerant

So when someone is harsh, it often reflects **their mindset**, not your value. 🧠

---

### 2. People often treat different people similarly

You can observe patterns:

* Someone rude to waiters → usually rude to colleagues too
* Someone patient with children → patient with adults too
* Someone who gossips → gossips about everyone
* Someone respectful → respectful to most people

This means **you are experiencing their personality**, not a unique reaction to you.

---

### 3. Yes — this changes over life

People **do change** based on experience, stress, and maturity.

Common patterns:

#### Young → kinder → older → more critical

Reasons:

* Disappointments accumulate
* Trust broken multiple times
* Life pressure increases
* Cynicism develops
* Ego hardens

They move from:

* "People are good" → "People are selfish"
* "Let's help" → "Protect myself"

This creates more criticism and harshness.

---

#### Or the opposite: young → selfish → older → kinder

Also common:

* Gain empathy from suffering
* Become more patient
* Care less about ego
* Understand others' struggles

They move from:

* "Me first" → "We all struggle"

These people become calmer and kinder. 🌱

---

### 4. Situation also affects behavior

Even kind people become harsh when:

* stressed
* tired
* threatened
* insecure
* competing

Even critical people can be kind when:

* relaxed
* respected
* safe
* emotionally fulfilled

So behavior = personality + current life state.

---

### 5. Important insight

How someone treats you is often:

```
Their personality
+ their past experiences
+ their current emotional state
+ their worldview
```

NOT mainly:

```
Your worth
```

This is why:

* One person praises you
* Another criticizes you
* Another ignores you

But **you didn't change** — they did.

---

### 6. Practical takeaway

When someone is:

* harsh → it's their lens
* kind → it's their nature
* selfish → it's their mindset
* generous → it's their values

Don't over-personalize it.

Understanding this gives emotional stability. 🧘‍♂️

You start thinking:

* "This is how they are"
  instead of
* "Something is wrong with me"

---

### Final summary

Yes:

* People largely **decide how they treat others** based on their mindset
* Their behavior is **patterned across many relationships**
* This **can change over time** due to life experience
* What you receive is often **a reflection of them**, not you

This is why the same person:

* is kind to everyone
* or critical to everyone
* or suspicious of everyone

They carry their **internal world** everywhere.
