Question: What is this arXiv email about?

Answer:

This is an **automatic reply from the arXiv support/moderation team**. It means they received your email, but they have not processed it yet.

The key parts:

* **"If this is a hold query"**

  * A "hold" means your arXiv submission is currently waiting for moderation/review.
  * They will check why it is held and reply when they can.

* **"If this is a request to appeal"**

  * If your paper was rejected, removed, or blocked by moderation and you submitted an appeal, it will take several days.
  * They are telling you not to expect an immediate response.

* **"small, US-based team, working business hours Monday–Friday"**

  * arXiv moderation is handled by humans, not instantly by an automated system.
  * Response time can be several business days.

Likely scenarios:

1. **You submitted a paper to arXiv**

   * Your submission may be under moderation.
   * New authors, unusual topics, AI-generated-looking papers, wrong categories, or papers outside arXiv scope can trigger manual review.

2. **You contacted arXiv about a submission problem**

   * They received your ticket and put it into their queue.

3. **You appealed a moderation decision**

   * They are acknowledging receipt.

The links are official help pages:

* arXiv moderation appeals:
  [https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/appeals.html](https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/appeals.html)
* Submission status:
  [https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit_status.html](https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit_status.html)

Nothing is wrong from this email alone. It is just a **ticket acknowledgement**.

If you recently uploaded a paper, the important question is: **what status does your arXiv submission page show?** For example:

* `Submitted`
* `On hold`
* `Pending approval`
* `Rejected`

That status tells what is actually happening.
