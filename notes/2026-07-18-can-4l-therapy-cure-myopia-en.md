赵阳医生（赵阳）提出的 **“4L 综合疗法”（4L综合疗法）** is a Chinese myopia-control / vision-training concept. The exact content varies depending on the clinic/material, but generally it combines several approaches around **eye habits, visual training, and myopia management**. The "4L" usually refers to four aspects such as:

* **Light（光环境）** — improve reading/work lighting conditions
* **Look（用眼方式）** — manage near work distance, breaks, posture
* **Lens（镜片/光学矫正）** — glasses, special lenses, myopia-control lenses
* **Lifestyle（生活方式）** — outdoor time, eye-use habits, sleep, etc.

(Some versions use slightly different words; the key idea is a multi-factor intervention.)

### Does it "cure" myopia?

For **true axial myopia** (眼轴变长), current mainstream ophthalmology consensus is:

> **No proven therapy can reverse the elongated eyeball and permanently cure established myopia.**

Once the eye grows longer, glasses/contact lenses/surgery can correct the focus, but they usually do not "restore" the original eye shape.

What can work:

### 1. Myopia control (evidence-supported)

These can slow progression:

* **More outdoor time** (especially children)
* **Low-dose atropine** (commonly 0.01%–0.05%, depending on region and patient)
* **Orthokeratology (OK lenses)**
* **Myopia-control soft contact lenses**
* **Defocus spectacle lenses** (e.g., DIMS/HAL designs)

### 2. Vision training

Some exercises can improve:

* accommodation flexibility
* eye strain symptoms
* binocular coordination

But they generally **do not shorten axial length**.

### 3. Adult myopia

For someone like you (your previous prescription history: roughly L -2.5D, R -4.25D in 2024), the question is usually not "cure", but:

* prevent further progression
* monitor retina health
* optimize correction
* manage presbyopia later

At age ~30+, axial myopia progression is usually much slower than in children, unless there are special factors.

### My view

"4L" is probably useful as a **comprehensive eye-care framework**, especially for children who are still growing. But if marketing says:

> "治愈近视 / 摘掉眼镜 / 恢复眼轴"

that claim goes beyond current evidence.

A good analogy:

* Training + lifestyle = like maintaining engine conditions
* Myopia-control lenses/atropine = like limiting engine wear
* Cure = rebuilding the engine block (eye length), which we currently cannot reliably do
