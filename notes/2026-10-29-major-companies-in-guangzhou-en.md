**Question:** What are more famous companies based in Guangzhou, China?

**Answer:**

Here's a much more comprehensive breakdown by sector:

---

### 🚗 Automotive & EVs

- **GAC Group (广汽集团)** — A state-owned automobile manufacturer founded in 1948, headquartered in Guangzhou, and one of the largest automakers in China. It has joint ventures with Toyota, Honda, and Nissan.
- **GAC Aion (广汽埃安)** — Founded in 2017 as GAC New Energy Automobile, it's now one of China's fastest-growing EV brands.
- **XPeng Motors (小鹏汽车)** — An electric vehicle manufacturer headquartered in Guangzhou, founded in 2014. Listed on NYSE.

---

### ✈️ Drones & Air Mobility

- **EHang (亿航)** — A company based in Guangzhou that develops and manufactures autonomous aerial vehicles (AAVs) and passenger AAVs. In March 2026 it became the first eVTOL company in the world to receive a licence to carry passengers commercially, and is planning to start offering flights to the public in Guangzhou and Hefei. Listed on Nasdaq.
- **XAG (极飞科技)** — A startup that provides drone services to the agricultural market, headquartered in Guangzhou and one of the world's leading agri-drone companies.
- **XPeng AeroHT (小鹏汇天)** — A Guangzhou-based company spinning out of XPeng, focused on developing flying cars.

---

### 🤖 AI & Autonomous Vehicles

- **WeRide (文远知行)** — A Guangzhou, China-based smart mobility company developing L4 autonomous driving solutions. Now publicly listed.
- **CloudWalk (云从科技)** — A Guangzhou-based company founded in 2015 specializing in AI-powered facial recognition and computer vision, used widely in banking and smart city infrastructure.

---

### 🛍️ E-Commerce & Retail

- **Vipshop (唯品会 / VIPS)** — Vipshop Holdings Ltd. is headquartered in Guangzhou. The world's largest online discount retailer and the third largest e-commerce platform in China, pioneering the flash sales model. NYSE-listed.
- **SHEIN** — Founded in 2008 in Nanjing, SHEIN grew and established itself in Guangzhou, evolving from a Chinese brand into a global platform. One of the world's most valuable fashion companies.
- **MINISO (名创优品)** — Founded in 2013 by Ye Guofu, who began his entrepreneurial career in Guangzhou where MINISO's headquarters is located. The company began operations in a basement garage in Guangzhou. Now a global lifestyle retail chain in 100+ countries.
- **Perfect Diary (完美日记)** — An e-commerce company offering cosmetics, founded in 2015 and headquartered in Guangzhou. One of China's most successful homegrown beauty brands.

---

### ⚡ Energy & Finance

- **China Southern Power Grid (南方电网)** — One of the Fortune Global 500 companies that built its global headquarters in Guangzhou, managing electricity distribution across five southern Chinese provinces.
- **GF Securities (广发证券)** — One of the first comprehensive securities firms in China, headquartered in Guangzhou.

---

### 🎮 Internet & Gaming

- **NetEase Games (网易)** — NetEase Games, a leading developer of PC and mobile games worldwide, established a major headquarters in Guangzhou's Tianhe Smart City district (its corporate parent is based in Hangzhou).

---

### 💊 Healthcare & Pharma

- **Guangzhou Pharmaceutical Holdings / Wanglaoji (广药/王老吉)** — One of the largest healthcare companies based in Guangzhou, and owner of the iconic Wanglaoji herbal tea brand, one of China's oldest consumer brands.
- **Burning Rock Biotech** — Represents cancer diagnostics in Guangzhou's growing biotech cluster. Listed on Nasdaq.

---

### 🏗️ Real Estate

- **R&F Properties (富力地产)** — One of China's largest property developers, founded and headquartered in Guangzhou.
- **Evergrande (恒大集团)** — Founded in Guangzhou in 1996, once the largest property developer in China (now undergoing restructuring after its 2021 debt crisis).

---

By total tech valuation, Guangzhou's tech ecosystem is ranked #34 globally, #12 in Asia-Pacific, and #5 in China, with a combined tech valuation of $117 billion. The top companies by valuation are NetEase ($81B), XPeng Motors ($12B), Vipshop ($6.2B), Miaoshou Doctor ($4.3B), and MINISO ($3.6B).
