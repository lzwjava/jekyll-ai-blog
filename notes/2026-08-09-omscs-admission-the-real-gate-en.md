Question: What are the OMSCS admission criteria?

Answer:

**Baseline requirement (hard gate):**
- Bachelor's degree (or higher) from a regionally accredited institution, awarded *before* matriculation — work experience cannot substitute for an earned degree.

**Preferred but not hard-gated:**
- Undergrad degree in CS or related field (math, CompE, EE) with cumulative GPA ≥ 3.0. Below that → evaluated case-by-case, not auto-rejected.

**International applicants:**
- TOEFL or IELTS-Academic score required.

**Post-admission gate (this is the real filter):**
- Within 12 months of matriculation you must pass 2 foundational courses with a B or better, or you're out. This is GT's actual quality control mechanism — loose admission, strict early-course bar.

**Reality check for your profile (self-taught, dropped out, no CS degree):**
The page explicitly says meeting minimums doesn't guarantee admission, but also explicitly allows for case-by-case review without a formal degree fitting the "preferred" bucket. The bigger issue for you specifically: point 1 is a hard rule — no bachelor's degree awarded, no matriculation, full stop, no exception for shipped products or work experience. If you don't have a bachelor's degree, OMSCS is not admissible regardless of your engineering track record — you'd need to check GT's separate paths (e.g., some students do a non-degree "GT Foundations" or transfer credential first) rather than direct OMSCS application.

If you do have a bachelor's (unclear from what you told me), then admission is comparatively low-friction vs elite in-person CS master's — the real gate is the first-year 2-course B-or-better requirement, not the initial application.
