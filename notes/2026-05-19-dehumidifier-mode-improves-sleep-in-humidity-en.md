Question: Is using AC dehumidifier (dry) mode instead of cooling mode a valid method to improve sleep quality in a humid city like Guangzhou?

Answer:

Yes, your observation is scientifically well-grounded. Here's a comprehensive breakdown of why it worked:

---

## Why Dehumidifier (Dry) Mode Improves Sleep in Humid Climates

### 1. The Core Problem: Humidity, Not Just Temperature

In a humid city like Guangzhou, the discomfort at night is often caused by **high moisture in the air**, not just heat. High humidity levels make the air feel warmer than it actually is. By reducing humidity, the air feels cooler and more comfortable — even without directly lowering temperature.

Your body also cools itself through sweat evaporation, but in humid air, sweat cannot evaporate efficiently — making you feel hot and restless even at moderate temperatures.

---

### 2. How Dry Mode Works Differently from Cooling Mode

Dry mode slows down the fan and cycles the compressor in short bursts. This allows the AC to pull excess moisture from the air, lowering humidity while keeping the temperature relatively stable — rather than aggressively blasting cold air.

The dry setting is ideal for sleeping or relaxation: it maintains a stable temperature while preventing that sticky, uncomfortable feeling associated with high humidity.

---

### 3. Why ~22°C + Thin Blanket is the Sweet Spot

The ideal indoor humidity level for sleep is typically 30–50%, which keeps the air feeling fresh and comfortable. High humidity above 60% prevents the body from cooling naturally, leading to restlessness and disrupted sleep.

Your setup — **dry mode + ~22°C ambient temperature + a thin blanket** — hits the right balance:

- Dry enough that your skin feels cool and fresh
- Not so cold that your body is fighting to stay warm
- The thin blanket provides just enough warmth for psychological comfort and natural body temperature regulation during deep sleep

---

### 4. Why Cooling Mode Felt Worse

Cooling mode prioritizes rapid temperature reduction by running the compressor at full capacity. It is most effective during extreme heat, but can overcool the room. In Guangzhou's humid weather, cooling mode often overshoots — making you cold AND leaving residual clamminess, which disrupts sleep.

---

### 5. Science-Backed Tip for Your Setup

Night owls can set timers to run dry mode for 6–8 hours with a gentle temperature setting. You'll wake up in comfortable, breathable air without freezing overnight.

Also, overuse of dry mode can lead to very dry air, causing dry eyes or skin. Using a humidity monitor to keep indoor moisture in the 40–60% range is recommended.
