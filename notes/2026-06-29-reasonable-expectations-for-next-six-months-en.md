## 1. AI and Technology (most dynamic area)

* **AI assistants become more “agent-like”**: more tools will autonomously execute workflows (coding, research, scheduling, content creation).

* **AI-related stocks and infrastructure (chips, data centers)** likely remain dominant market narrative

---

## 3. Geopolitics

* Ongoing **US–China tech competition** (chips, AI, trade restrictions)
* Regional conflicts (Ukraine, Middle East tensions) likely remain unresolved but may shift in intensity
* More **export controls on advanced semiconductors and AI hardware**

---

## 4. Climate and Environment

* Increasing frequency of **extreme weather events** (heatwaves, floods, storms), especially in:

  * North America
  * Southeast Asia
  * Southern Europe
* Continued pressure for climate adaptation policies rather than just emissions targets

---

## 5. Work and Society

* **Remote/hybrid work stabilizes** rather than expands further
* More companies adopt **AI-first workflows**, reducing demand for repetitive knowledge work
* Growing tension between:

  * automation efficiency
  * job displacement concerns

---

## 6. Consumer Tech & Internet

* More **AI integrated into search engines, browsers, and operating systems**
* Rise of **personal AI memory systems** (tools that remember user context across apps)
* Social media continues shifting toward **AI-generated content + short-form video dominance**

---

## 7. Big Picture Theme

The next 6 months are unlikely to bring sudden world changes, but rather:

> **“Fast AI adoption + slow economic adjustment + persistent geopolitical tension.”**
