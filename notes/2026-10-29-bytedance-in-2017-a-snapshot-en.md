**Question:** What was ByteDance's status in 2017 — its scale, user base, and revenue?

**Answer:**

In 2017, ByteDance was a rapidly growing Chinese tech company, still largely unknown outside China — but already enormous within it. Here's a breakdown:

---

### 📱 Products & Scale

- **Toutiao (今日头条):** By September 2017, Toutiao had 120 million daily active users, served with news and content across more than 100 content categories.
- **Neihan Duanzi (内涵段子):** This humor/meme app peaked at over 200 million users in 2017, before being shut down by Chinese regulators for "vulgar content."
- **Douyin / TikTok:** ByteDance had launched Douyin domestically in 2016. It rolled out TikTok internationally in May 2017, then aggressively expanded global reach by acquiring Musical.ly for about $800 million in November 2017, which would later be merged with TikTok in 2018.
- **Other acquisitions:** ByteDance acquired Flipagram in February 2017 and launched its UGC short video platform Hypstar (Vigo Video) in Southeast Asia in July 2017, and also acquired global news app News Republic in November 2017.

---

### 💰 Revenue

ByteDance's revenue in 2017 was RMB 16 billion (~$2.3 billion USD), up from RMB 6 billion in 2016 — a near-tripling in one year — driven largely by advertising on Toutiao.

---

### 🏦 Valuation & Funding

In August 2017, ByteDance raised funding from General Atlantic at a valuation of approximately $20 billion. Between 2017 and 2018, the company's valuation then rose dramatically from $20 billion to $75 billion.

---

### 🌍 Global Profile

At the end of 2017, TechCrunch noted that ByteDance was "no longer a secret outside of China," as the company's actions began attracting attention from international media and investors.

---

**In summary:** By the end of 2017, ByteDance was already a $20B company with ~$2.3B in revenue and hundreds of millions of users — mostly in China — and had just made the key moves (TikTok international launch + Musical.ly acquisition) that would transform it into a global powerhouse.
