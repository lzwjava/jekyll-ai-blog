### How Coursera Works

Coursera is a digital learning platform structured as a multi-sided marketplace that connects content creators (universities and companies) with learners worldwide.

* **Content Delivery & Access Models:** Learners can access video lectures, course materials, and discussions through a "freemium" model. Many standalone courses allow users to audit content for free, while access to graded assignments, peer-reviewed projects, and official credentials requires payment.
* **Institutional Partnerships:** Educational content is created by accredited top-tier universities (e.g., Stanford, Yale, University of Illinois) and major industry partners (e.g., Google, IBM, Meta).
* **Program Tiers:** Learning paths range from short guided projects and single courses to multi-course Specializations, Professional Certificates, MasterTrack certificate credits, and full online undergraduate or graduate degree programs.

---

### How Coursera Makes Money

Coursera monetizes its platform through three major revenue streams:

#### 1. Individual / Consumer Segment

* **Subscriptions (Coursera Plus):** Flat-rate access subscriptions (e.g., monthly or annual plans) that unlock unlimited access to thousands of courses, projects, and certificate programs.
* **Single Course & Certificate Purchases:** One-time charges for individuals seeking verified certificates, graded assignments, and credential badges for standalone courses.
* **Specializations & Professional Certificates:** Monthly subscription fees charged for the duration it takes a learner to complete specialized career pathways.

#### 2. Enterprise Segment (B2B)

* **Coursera for Business:** SaaS-style seat licensing for companies to upskill employees using custom learning tracks.
* **Coursera for Campus & Government:** Custom institutional packages provided to universities, government agencies, and non-profit initiatives for large-scale workforce development and retraining.

#### 3. Degrees & Academic Programs

* **Tuition Revenue Share:** Coursera partners with universities to host full online bachelor’s and master’s degrees. Coursera collects platform service fees and revenue shares on tuition from enrolled students.
