**Question:** What types of ads does Monetag show, and are they legal or positive/safe ads?

**Answer:**

---

**Ad Formats Monetag Shows**

Monetag offers several ad formats, automatically selected by its MultiTag AI based on user behavior, device, location, and page context:

| Format | Description |
|---|---|
| **OnClick / Popunder** | Opens a new tab or window behind the current browser window when a user clicks anywhere on the page |
| **Push Notifications** | Clickable browser messages that appear even when the user is not on your site (requires user opt-in) |
| **In-Page Push** | Small native-style notification banners displayed within the page, at corners on desktop or top/bottom on mobile |
| **Vignette / Interstitial** | Full-screen or overlay ads that appear over the page content, usually dimming the background |
| **SmartLink** | A single URL that automatically routes users to the most relevant advertiser based on their profile |

---

**Are the Ads Legal and Safe?**

This is an honest, mixed answer:

✅ **What Monetag prohibits on publisher sites:** Websites that show adult/pornographic content, torrent sites, promotion of illegal substances or activities, software piracy, violence, and bot or incentivized traffic are not accepted. Content must comply with their policy, and all ads are monitored 24/7 by anti-fraud and malware prevention tools.

✅ **What they promise:** Monetag says they work only with trusted, hand-picked advertisers, and that all ads are continuously monitored by anti-ad fraud and malware detection technologies.

⚠️ **The honest caveats:**
- Popunder ads in particular can feel intrusive and may cause user backlash depending on your audience.
- Some publishers have reported seeing low-quality or misleading ad creatives slip through, which is a known risk with most alternative ad networks (not unique to Monetag).
- From a safety perspective, the experience depends on how ad formats behave on your site and how you manage consent and compliance for push-based units.
- Monetag is generally considered **more permissive than Google AdSense** — it accepts niches that AdSense often rejects (entertainment, streaming, file-sharing), which means the ad pool can sometimes include lower-quality advertisers.

---

**Bottom Line**

Monetag is a **legitimate, legal ad network** — but it's not as brand-safe or conservative as Google AdSense. If your blog is family-friendly or professional, the popunder and interstitial formats in particular may feel out of place and could annoy readers. It's better suited for entertainment, news, or global traffic blogs where ad intrusiveness is more tolerated.
