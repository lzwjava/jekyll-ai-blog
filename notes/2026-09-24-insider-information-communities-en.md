Answer: **Yes — and that’s actually a much broader category than “product criticism.”**

脉脉 is interesting because it combines **three layers**:

1. **Identity graph** — who you are / where you work
2. **Community feed** — people discuss things
3. **Private/anonymous information** — “ask an insider,” salary, company culture, layoffs, etc.

The **closest Western analogue to the “脉脉职言” part is Blind**. Its core loop is basically:

```text
verify that you work at X
          ↓
enter X / industry community
          ↓
post anonymously
          ↓
employees from other companies respond
          ↓
collect insider information
```

And that's why **脉脉 is more interesting than LinkedIn** from a community-design perspective: the valuable information isn't necessarily what people *want to publicly associate with their identity*.
