Question: **What are Xi Jinping’s “Four Global Initiatives”?**

Answer:

As of 2026, the **Four Global Initiatives** are a framework of four major foreign-policy initiatives proposed by Xi Jinping between 2021 and 2025:

| Initiative | English                              | Year | Core question                                   |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------ | ---: | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **全球发展倡议** | Global Development Initiative (GDI)  | 2021 | **How should the world develop?**               |
| **全球安全倡议** | Global Security Initiative (GSI)     | 2022 | **How should the world achieve security?**      |
| **全球文明倡议** | Global Civilization Initiative (GCI) | 2023 | **How should different civilizations coexist?** |
| **全球治理倡议** | Global Governance Initiative (GGI)   | 2025 | **How should the world be governed?**           |

China officially presents them as a connected system covering **development → security → civilization → governance**. ([China Diplomacy][1])

### 1. Global Development Initiative — 全球发展倡议

Proposed in **2021**.

Basic idea:

> Development should be more balanced, inclusive and sustainable, particularly for developing countries.

It emphasizes things such as poverty reduction, food security, green development, digital economy, and implementation of the UN 2030 Sustainable Development Goals.

**Think:** economic development.

---

### 2. Global Security Initiative — 全球安全倡议

Proposed in **2022**.

Its central concept is **“common, comprehensive, cooperative and sustainable security.”**

The key argument is that one country's security should not be achieved by making another country's security worse. It emphasizes dialogue, negotiation and political solutions to conflicts rather than bloc confrontation.

**Think:** international security and war/peace.

---

### 3. Global Civilization Initiative — 全球文明倡议

Proposed in **2023**.

The basic idea is:

**Different civilizations should be treated as diverse rather than ranked as “higher” and “lower.”**

It emphasizes:

* respect for civilizational diversity
* common human values
* dialogue between civilizations
* mutual learning
* rejecting civilizational superiority

**Think:** culture, values and civilization.

This is closely related to the concept you asked about earlier, **“common values of humanity”**—peace, development, fairness, justice, democracy and freedom. ([China Diplomacy][2])

---

### 4. Global Governance Initiative — 全球治理倡议

This is the newest one. Xi proposed it in **September 2025** at the SCO Plus meeting in Tianjin. ([State Council of China][3])

Its five stated principles are:

1. **Sovereign equality**
2. **International rule of law**
3. **Multilateralism**
4. **People-centered approach**
5. **Concrete action**

The stated goal is to reform/improve the international governance system toward what China describes as a **more just and equitable global governance system**. ([State Council Information Office][4])

**Think:** who makes the rules of the international system, and how those rules are made.

---

## The interesting part: they're basically a 4-layer model

You can compress the whole thing into:

```text
                    Community with a Shared Future
                              │
        ┌─────────────────────┼─────────────────────┐
        │                     │                     │
   DEVELOPMENT             SECURITY             CIVILIZATION
   全球发展倡议              全球安全倡议             全球文明倡议
        │                     │                     │
   prosperity              peace                 coexistence
        └─────────────────────┼─────────────────────┘
                              │
                       GLOBAL GOVERNANCE
                         全球治理倡议
                              │
                         rules / order
```

Or even more simply:

**GDI = money/development**
**GSI = security/peace**
**GCI = culture/values**
**GGI = institutions/rules**

That's why Chinese official descriptions call them a **systematic framework** rather than four unrelated slogans. ([China Diplomacy][1])

### One important distinction

Don't confuse the **Four Global Initiatives** with China's other major foreign-policy concepts:

* **Belt and Road Initiative (BRI)** → infrastructure/economic connectivity
* **Community with a Shared Future for Humanity** → overarching vision
* **Common Values of Humanity** → values
* **Four Global Initiatives** → development, security, civilization, governance

So conceptually:

```text
                  Vision
     Community with a Shared Future
                       │
          ┌────────────┴────────────┐
          │                         │
      Values                     Initiatives
 Common Values          ┌──────────┼──────────┐
 of Humanity            │          │          │
                        GDI        GSI        GCI
                   development  security  civilization
                                      │
                                     GGI
                                   governance
```

The **GGI is particularly significant** because it was explicitly presented as the fourth piece completing the earlier three. ([State Council of China][3])
