**Question:** How did Patrick Collison, the Stripe founder, meet his wife?

**Answer:**

Patrick Collison, co-founder and CEO of Stripe, met his wife, Silvana Konermann, as teenagers at the **EU Young Scientists and Engineers Contest** (also known as the European Union Contest for Young Scientists).

The competition took place in Moscow in 2005, when both were 17 years old. Silvana, a Swiss scientist, won first place with her project on developing a treatment to prevent urinary tract infections associated with catheter use post-operation. Patrick placed second with his project on creating an online programming language.

They began dating after the event, remained together long-term (described as childhood sweethearts), got engaged around 2019, and married in a private ceremony in Italy in April 2022. Silvana is now a biochemist, Stanford University professor, and co-founder with Patrick of the Arc Institute.
