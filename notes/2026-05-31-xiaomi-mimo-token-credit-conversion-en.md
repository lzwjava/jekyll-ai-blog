**Question:** On the Xiaomi Mimo platform, does 1 Credit = 1 token?**Answer:**

**1 Credit = 1 token** on the Xiaomi MiMo platform — but with model-specific multipliers applied on top.

Here's how it actually works:

**Credit consumption ratio by model:**

- **MiMo-V2.5 / MiMo-V2-Omni** → 1x (1 Credit per token)
- **MiMo-V2.5-Pro / MiMo-V2-Pro** → 2x (2 Credits per token)
- **TTS models** → 0x (free for a limited time, don't consume Credits)

So the base unit is 1 Credit = 1 token, but heavier models charge 2 Credits per token consumed.

**Plan scale for reference:**

Lite = ¥411.84/year → 720M Credits; Standard = ¥1045.44/year → 2400M Credits; Pro = ¥3474.24/year → 8400M Credits; Max = ¥6959.04/year → 19200M Credits.

**Regarding the compensation Credits you received:** Those are just Credits refunded to make up the price difference when upgrading/renewing plans. The platform calculates it by converting your used credit amount into an equivalent value, then having you pay only the price difference to upgrade to the higher package. The Credits themselves follow the same 1 Credit = 1 token (base model) accounting.
