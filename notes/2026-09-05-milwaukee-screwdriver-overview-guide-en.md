**Question:** What is an introduction to Milwaukee (米沃奇) screwdrivers?

**Answer:**

Milwaukee Tool (米沃奇) is a well-known American professional tool brand. Here's an overview of their screwdriver lineup:

---

**🔧 Hand Screwdrivers**

Milwaukee hand screwdrivers are made in the USA, featuring precision-fit, laser-etched tips, and boron-infused US steel for an extended lifespan — built for reliability and durability.

Key features include:
- An all-metal core for up to 4× strength, precision-machined tips for secure fitment and long life, and 360-degree rotating back caps for greater control and driving ability.
- Visible wear indicators, cushion grips, and a limited lifetime warranty. The 1000V Insulated Screwdriver Set meets UL Classified safety standards — ideal for electrical work.

Popular models include multi-bit sets (8-in-1, 11-in-1, 14-in-1 ratcheting), precision sets, and insulated sets.

---

**⚡ Power Screwdrivers**

The M4™ Screwdriver delivers 44 in-lbs of peak torque with a 21-position clutch and auto shut-off for precise torque control. It offers two speeds — 200 and 600 RPM — and at only 244mm long and under one pound, it's easy to use one-handed. It features a ¼" quick-change chuck and can drill more than 200 holes per charge using Milwaukee's RED LITHIUM™ 2.0 battery.

---

**🏭 Applications**

Milwaukee screwdrivers are designed for consistent performance across electrical, mechanical, automotive, and general fastening/maintenance work. The lineup also includes insulated drivers for electrical safety, multi-bit models for tool consolidation, and cushioned grip options that reduce strain during repetitive use.
