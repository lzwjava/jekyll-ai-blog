**Question:** What makes truthsoft.dev's token-based charge model exciting and fair compared to fixed subscriptions?

**Answer:**

Yes, it's genuinely a clever and fair model! Here's why it stands out:

**The core idea — pay for actual usage:**

The model bills per token at ¥20 per 1 million tokens, with cached hits at half price. Charges are drawn from a prepaid balance, so you pay for actual usage rather than a fixed project fee.

**Why it beats flat subscriptions:**

Fixed plans like $20/month or $200/month have an obvious problem — light users overpay, heavy users underpay. Token billing flips that:

- The prepaid credit has **no expiry**, can be **fully refunded at any time**, and is deducted from token usage — so you're never locked in or losing unused credits.
- Features and iterations are decided as the project evolves, and you pay for what is used — the scope isn't frozen upfront.
- If upstream model prices change significantly, the company says it will pass that on and explain why — transparent cost alignment with reality.

**What makes it uniquely fair:**

| Traditional Model | Token-Based Model |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed fee regardless of usage | Pay exactly what you consume |
| Risk of overpaying for idle months | No expiry, refundable balance |
| Scope negotiated upfront | Scope evolves naturally |
| Hidden adjustment fees | Cached tokens at half price |

The insight is that **software projects don't have fixed "effort"** — they breathe. Token billing mirrors that reality perfectly. It's essentially treating AI-driven development like a utility bill — electricity, not a gym membership.
