### Academic Excellence: IPhO Gold Medal

Zhang attended Fuyang High School (富阳中学) in Zhejiang, where he excelled in physics and mathematics competitions.

At the 46th International Physics Olympiad (IPhO) held in India (Mumbai) in 2015, Zhang Chengxing, representing China from Zhejiang's Fuyang High School, won a Gold Medal and finished 6th in the world overall. China's five-member team swept all five gold medals at that competition, making them the only team to achieve a full gold sweep that year.

The IPhO is the world's most prestigious high school physics competition. To represent China at IPhO, a student must first survive a national selection process in which the national training team of 50 students is narrowed down to the top 5, who then represent China internationally. Reaching the IPhO and winning gold — especially ranking 6th in the world — is an extraordinary achievement.
