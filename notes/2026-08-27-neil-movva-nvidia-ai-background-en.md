**Question:** Who is Neil Movva, and what is his background at Nvidia?

**Answer:**

Neil Movva is a systems-oriented deep-learning engineer and AI infrastructure entrepreneur with deep roots at Nvidia. Here's an overview:

**Background & Education**

Movva earned both a B.S. and M.S. in Electrical Engineering from Stanford University in 2019, with a heavy focus on artificial intelligence. He met his co-founder Samir Menon on their very first day at Stanford, where they took exactly the same course load.

**Nvidia Connection**

Neil Movva started his career at Nvidia, working on GPUs and kernels, and developed an unusually deep understanding of inference — spanning software, chips, and power. He watched Nvidia pivot from gaming chips to AI silicon in 2016 and 2017. His work there included optimizing deep learning compute for Nvidia's cuDNN library.

**Career Path**

He describes his journey as: chasing GPU speed-of-light at Nvidia, designing and shipping the world's most efficient computer vision algorithms at Apple, and helping build the fastest LLM inference stack from metal to API at Together.ai. He also founded Blyss (YC W23), a startup focused on end-to-end encrypted AI systems.

**Current Work — Sail Research**

Neil Movva and Samir Menon are described as two of the most brilliant young AI infrastructure architects in Silicon Valley, with big-company experience at Apple, Nvidia, and Intel, high-growth experience at Together AI, and repeat-founder experience at Blyss. His current company, Sail Research, aims for a 1,000x reduction in token cost, targeting background agents that run for hours or days — a workload he argues the industry has largely ignored in favor of low-latency chatbots. Sail recently announced an $80M Seed & Series A round led by Sequoia, with participation from Kleiner Perkins, Redpoint, and others, and is already processing trillions of tokens per week.
