**Question:** What is an introduction to Hong Kong Science & Technology Parks Corporation (HKSTP)?

**Answer:**

Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks Corporation (HKSTP) is a prominent statutory body in the innovation and technology landscape of Hong Kong. Here's a structured overview:

---

### 🏛️ Background & Founding
HKSTP is a statutory body established by the Hong Kong Government in May 2001 to promote innovation and technology development in the region.

---

### 🎯 Mission
HKSTP is dedicated to building a vibrant innovation and technology ecosystem to connect stakeholders, nurture technology talents, facilitate collaboration, and catalyse innovations to deliver social and economic benefits to Hong Kong and the region.

---

### 🏗️ Infrastructure & Scale
HKSTP oversees and develops key infrastructure, including Hong Kong Science Park in Pak Shek Kok (330,000 m² of R&D space), InnoCentre, and three industrial estates in Tai Po, Yuen Long, and Tseung Kwan O — supporting over 1,800 tech companies and 14,000 R&D professionals as of mid-2024.

---

### 🔬 Focus Clusters
HKSTP's work centres around five technology clusters including Biomedical Technology, Electronics, Green Technology, Information & Communications Technology, and Material & Precision Engineering; and the development of three cross-disciplinary platforms: Smart City, Healthy Ageing, and Robotics.

---

### 🚀 Incubation & Startup Support
HKSTP also runs the Technology Incubation Programme (Incu-App, Incu-Tech, Incu-Bio), through which it has assisted more than 1,200 start-ups and incubated over 1,000 enterprises — 80% of which remain active — with six homegrown and six overseas unicorns emerging from the program.

---

### 🌏 Global Outreach
With a mission to turn Hong Kong into a global I&T hub, HKSTP actively partners with regional players and international events to expand cross-border innovation and investment opportunities. Most recently, HKSTP led a delegation of 15 innovative tech companies to GITEX Asia 2025, showcasing solutions in AI, telecommunications, and green and construction tech, highlighting Hong Kong's innovation ecosystem to Southeast Asia.

---

### 💡 Summary
In short, HKSTP serves as Hong Kong's primary engine for transforming the city into a global innovation hub — bridging R&D, talent development, startup incubation, and international market access under one integrated ecosystem.
