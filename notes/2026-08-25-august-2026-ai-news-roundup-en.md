**Question:** What are the major AI news stories from the past few weeks?

**Answer:**

Here's a roundup of the most significant AI developments in August 2026:

---

**🔬 OpenAI Astra: Math Breakthrough**
OpenAI announced on August 1 that an internal version of its next major model, Astra, solved ten previously open problems in mathematics and theoretical computer science — publishing formal Lean proofs on GitHub verifiable by anyone — all for roughly $2,000 in compute. The results include a construction proving the existence of non-sofic groups and new upper bounds on sphere-packing density. However, OpenAI then paused internal development of Astra on August 7, after evaluations found it may be capable of autonomous zero-day exploit development — the first model to trigger the "Critical" cybersecurity threshold under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework.

---

**🛩️ AI-Piloted Military Aircraft**
DARPA successfully completed the first real-world flight of an F-16 fighter jet fully controlled by artificial intelligence, marking a major milestone in autonomous combat aviation.

---

**📋 OpenAI IPO**
OpenAI filed its public IPO S-1 with the SEC in mid-August 2026, managed by Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley, targeting a valuation of $730–852 billion with a September 2026 IPO window. The S-1 includes material disclosures such as a 42-state attorneys general investigation and an Apple trade secrets lawsuit. OpenAI reported $25–33 billion in annualized revenue for 2026.

---

**🤖 New Models & Products**
Meta unveiled Muse Code, an AI-powered coding assistant built on Muse Spark 1.2, capable of writing code, fixing bugs, verifying results, running parallel sub-agents, and resuming interrupted projects automatically. The broader model landscape also saw releases including DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731, GPT-5.6 Luna, Meta Muse Spark 1.1, and Thinking Machines Inkling, with the cadence of major model launches having roughly quadrupled since 2023.

---

**🏢 Google DeepMind Restructuring**
Google consolidated AI leadership at Mountain View — Demis Hassabis stepped back to Chairman, Kavukcuoglu took over operations, and the coding team relocated from London — effectively ending the Brain/DeepMind two-continent split that had hampered Google since 2023. Additionally, after 27 years at Google, Jeff Dean and three of the company's biggest AI pioneers — including Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals — departed to launch their own startup.

---

**🇪🇺 EU AI Act Enforcement Begins**
August 2, 2026 marked the date the European Commission began enforcing obligations covering general-purpose AI models with systemic risk, with powers to request information, evaluate models, order mitigation, restrict availability, and impose fines of up to 3% of worldwide annual turnover. Anthropic responded by introducing machine-readable watermarks for AI-generated content in the EU, with new Claude models from that date required to support the marking.

---

**🔓 Open-Source Agents**
Prime Intellect released an open-source coding agent called Prime Agent that scored 95.5% on ARC-AGI-3. Unlike most AI tools, it runs inside a persistent Python environment where context carries over from one step to the next.
