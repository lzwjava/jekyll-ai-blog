Question: What does the Apple support page [https://support.apple.com/en-us/103258](https://support.apple.com/en-us/103258) say about cleaning a MacBook Air keyboard?

Answer:

The page is Apple’s official guide on **how to clean Mac computers safely**. Regarding the MacBook Air keyboard specifically, it advises:

### Key Points:

1. **Turn off and unplug your Mac** before cleaning.
2. **Use a soft, lint-free cloth** (microfiber recommended).
3. **Dampening:** Lightly dampen the cloth with **water or 70% isopropyl alcohol**.

   * **Do not spray liquids directly** onto the keyboard.
4. **Cleaning method:** Gently wipe the keyboard surface and keys.

   * Avoid excessive force or scrubbing.
5. **Avoid harmful cleaners:** No bleach, window cleaners, or abrasives.
6. **Drying:** Let the keyboard **air-dry completely** before powering on.
7. **Optional tools:** Compressed air can help remove debris **between keys**, but avoid liquid sprays directly.

Apple emphasizes safety and preventing liquid from entering the keyboard mechanism. For a MacBook Air M2 (2022+), this method is fully compatible.
