## 派潭镇中心小学 (Paitán Central Primary School) — Zengcheng, Guangzhou

### Overview

Paitán Central Primary School (广州市增城区派潭镇中心小学), historically known as "派潭兴贤两等小学堂," is a **public (公办), municipality-rated (市一级) primary school** located at **No. 100, Wenzheng Road (文政路100号), Paitán Town, Zengcheng District, Guangzhou**. It was established in **1983** and is recognized for its strengths in science and technology innovation, sports, and affiliation with quality educational institutions.

### Location & Administrative Context

The school is situated in **Paitán Town (派潭镇)**, which is in the northern part of **Zengcheng District (增城区)**, Guangzhou. Paitán is known as an eco-tourism area, surrounded by natural scenery and hot spring resorts. The school serves as the central primary school for the township.

### School Tier & Recognition

The school is listed among Zengcheng District's recognized schools, appearing in the Guangzhou Municipal Education Bureau's list of schools certified as "Higher-Level Safe and Civilized Campuses" (更高水平安全文明校园) in 2023.

### Branch Campus

The school also operates a **branch teaching point at Huangdong (黄洞分教点)**, serving students in more rural or remote communities within the Paitán township area.

### School Type & System

- **Type:** Public (公办)
- **Level:** Primary school (grades 1–6), with a 6-year curriculum
- **Rating:** Guangzhou Municipality Level-1 School (广州市一级学校)
- **Historical name:** 派潭兴贤两等小学堂

### Education Development in the Area

Paitán Town's education authorities have emphasized that improving educational quality is a top priority, particularly through shared resources, teacher exchange programs, curriculum co-development, and joint teaching research activities — all as part of Guangzhou's broader initiative to address uneven educational development in its districts.

### Summary

Paitán Central Primary School is a long-established, publicly funded elementary school that has served as the educational anchor of Paitán Town for decades. As one of the key schools in Zengcheng's northern townships, it plays an important role in providing quality basic education to local children in a relatively rural setting within the Greater Guangzhou area.
