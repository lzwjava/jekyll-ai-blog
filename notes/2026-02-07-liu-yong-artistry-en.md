## 1. Core Artistic Style of *《八声甘州·对潇潇暮雨洒江天》*

This poem is written by **Liu Yong (柳永)**, whose defining literary identity is:

* Leader of **婉约派 (the Graceful and Restrained School)**
* Known for:

  * **Plain, colloquial language (白描)**
  * Strong emotional expression through concrete imagery
  * Appeal to both **refined and popular audiences (雅俗共赏)**

This poem is a **model work** of that style.

---

## 2. Why Option B Is Correct

**Option B:**

> 运用白描语言状物传情，雅俗共赏，堪称婉约风格的当行本色

This matches the poem exactly:

* **白描语言**

  * Simple, direct description: *“潇潇暮雨”“长亭”“骤雨初歇”*
  * No heavy symbolism or grand metaphors

* **状物传情**

  * Emotions (sorrow of separation) are conveyed **through scenery**, not explicit moral or political statements

* **婉约风格的当行本色**

  * “当行本色” means *most authentic, representative form*
  * This poem is a **textbook example** of Liu Yong’s婉约 style

➡️ Therefore, **B accurately summarizes the poem’s main artistic feature**, which is exactly what exam questions care about.

## 3. Why Option D Is Wrong (But Tempting)

**Option D:**

Why this is incorrect:

* **“江山易主” (dynastic change, political loss)**
  ❌ Not a theme of this poem
  ❌ More typical of **Southern Song poets** like Xin Qiji or Jiang Kui
