## Question: Introduction to Automated Systems (H.K.) Limited (ASL)

## Answer

Automated Systems Holdings Limited (ASL) is one of Hong Kong’s oldest and largest IT services companies. Founded in **1973**, the company has more than **50 years of experience** providing enterprise technology solutions. It was listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in **1997** under stock code **771**.

ASL positions itself as a **unified technology services partner**, helping enterprises and government organizations design, implement, and operate large-scale IT systems.

### Main business areas

ASL provides services across:

* **Enterprise IT infrastructure**

  * Servers, storage, networking
  * Data center solutions
  * Cloud infrastructure

* **Cloud computing & digital transformation**

  * Hybrid cloud
  * Cloud migration
  * Managed cloud services

* **Cybersecurity**

  * Security consulting
  * Threat monitoring
  * Enterprise security solutions

* **Application development & integration**

  * Enterprise software systems
  * Digital platforms
  * System integration

* **Artificial intelligence & data solutions**

  * Data analytics
  * AI platforms
  * Automation solutions

* **Managed IT services**

  * Long-term outsourcing
  * Infrastructure operation
  * Technical support

### Regional presence

ASL operates across multiple regions, including:

Its customers include large enterprises, financial institutions, government departments, telecommunications companies, and public organizations.

ASL is not mainly a consumer software company like a startup. It is closer to companies such as:

In Hong Kong, it competes with companies like:
