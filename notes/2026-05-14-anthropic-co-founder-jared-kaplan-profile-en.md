## Jared Kaplan — Co-Founder & Chief Science Officer at Anthropic

### Background & Education

Jared Daniel Kaplan is a theoretical physicist and AI researcher. He holds a bachelor's degree in physics and mathematics from Stanford University and a PhD in physics from Harvard University, where his doctoral thesis focused on aspects of holography, advised by Nima Arkani-Hamed.

He spent the first 15 years of his career as a theoretical physicist in academia. Prior to co-founding Anthropic, he was instrumental in building GPT-3 and Codex at OpenAI.

---

### Role at Anthropic

Jared Kaplan is the **Chief Science Officer and Co-Founder** of Anthropic, an AI lab whose research and products prioritize safety at the frontier. As a public benefit corporation, Anthropic is dedicated to building AI systems that benefit humanity.

In October 2024, Anthropic announced that Kaplan would also serve as the company's **"Responsible Scaling Officer"**, overseeing its Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP). In this role, he determines the safety assessments and precautions to adopt before model release.

---

### Key Contributions to AI

**1. Scaling Laws**
Kaplan's research on **scaling laws** revolutionized the AI industry by providing a framework for understanding and predicting the behavior of advanced AI systems, guiding resource allocation, and shaping the development of transformative technologies. His landmark 2020 paper, *Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models*, established that AI model performance improves predictably with increased size, data, and compute.

**2. Constitutional AI**
Kaplan also helped pioneer **Constitutional AI**, an approach to AI development that aims to create AI systems constrained by and aligned with a set of predetermined principles and values.

**3. GPT-3 & Large Language Models**
While at OpenAI, he co-authored *Language Models are Few-Shot Learners* (2020), one of the foundational papers behind GPT-3.

---

### Academic Work

Kaplan has been an associate professor at Johns Hopkins University's Department of Physics & Astronomy since 2012. His academic research interests span quantum gravity, holography (AdS/CFT), conformal field theory, and related topics in particle physics and cosmology.

As of 2026, Forbes estimated Kaplan's net worth at **$3.7 billion**. He has received notable fellowships and awards including a Hertz Fellowship, a Sloan Research Fellowship, and an NSF CAREER award.
