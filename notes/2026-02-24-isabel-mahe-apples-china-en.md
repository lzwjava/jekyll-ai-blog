Isabel Ge Mahe is a prominent Chinese business executive, currently serving as Apple Inc.’s Vice President and Managing Director of Greater China. Born in Shenyang, Liaoning, China, in 1973, she moved to Canada with her family during her youth. She earned both her Bachelor of Applied Science and Master of Engineering in Electrical Engineering from Simon Fraser University in British Columbia, and later obtained an MBA from the University of California, Berkeley.

Before joining Apple, Isabel held key technical and managerial roles in the wireless technology sector, most notably as Vice President of Wireless Software Engineering at Palm Inc. She joined Apple in 2008 as Vice President of Wireless Technologies, where she oversaw the development of cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, NFC, location, and motion technologies for nearly every Apple product. Her leadership was instrumental in advancing the wireless capabilities of the iPhone and other Apple devices.

In July 2017, Isabel was appointed as Apple’s first-ever Vice President and Managing Director of Greater China, a role in which she reports directly to CEO Tim Cook and COO Jeff Williams. In this capacity, she leads Apple’s operations across mainland China, Hong Kong, and Taiwan, overseeing a team of over 12,000 employees and contributing to nearly 20% of Apple’s global revenue. She has also played a pivotal role in developing China-specific features for the iPhone and iPad, further strengthening Apple’s presence in the region.

Isabel is recognized as one of the most powerful women in international business, having been featured on Fortune’s Most Powerful Women International list for four consecutive years since 2018. She also serves as an independent director on the Starbucks board, contributing to corporate governance and strategy.
