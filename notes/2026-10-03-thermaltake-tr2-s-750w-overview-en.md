**What it is:** The TR2 S is Thermaltake's budget ATX power supply line. The 750W version is a non-modular unit, so its cables are fixed. Its 80 PLUS certification is the entry-level White tier, and it has a 120 mm fan with two 8-pin (4+4) CPU connectors and two 6+2-pin PCIe connectors.

**Key features:**

- **Cooling:** It uses a 120 mm hydraulic-bearing fan, aimed at quiet, cool operation.
- **Cables:** The cables are black and low-profile, which makes routing easier and avoids blocking airflow.
- **+12V design:** It uses a single +12V rail, which delivers power to the GPU and CPU flexibly.
- **Protections:** It includes OCP, OVP, UVP, OPP, SCP and OTP.
- **Size:** About 150 × 86 × 140 mm, a standard ATX size.

**Who it suits:** 750W is enough for a mid-range gaming build. It fits a mainstream PC with a mid-range GPU, such as an office, home or entry-level gaming machine.
