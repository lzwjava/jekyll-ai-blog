**Question:** How do I apply for a US travel (tourist) visa in Guangzhou, given that I currently have a passport renewal in progress?

**Answer:**

Here's a step-by-step breakdown for applying at the US Consulate in Guangzhou:

**⚠️ Passport Issue First**
Your renewed passport needs to be in hand before you can apply. You must bring a passport valid for at least 8 months beyond the visa issuance date. You should also bring any expired or cancelled passports you have. So wait until your new passport arrives, then proceed.

**Step 1 — Complete DS-160 Form**
Fill out the DS-160 nonimmigrant visa application online at ceac.state.gov. This is the standard tourist/business (B1/B2) form.

**Step 2 — Pay the Visa Fee**
The standard B1/B2 fee remains $185 as of April 2026. Pay via CGI Federal using CITIC Bank or Alipay.

**Step 3 — Schedule an Interview**
To schedule an appointment, visit ustraveldocs.com. The US Consulate General Guangzhou is located at 43 Hua Jiu Road, Zhujiang New Town, with entrance on Huaxia Road near Zhujiang New Town Metro Station Exit B1.

**⚠️ Expect Long Wait Times**
Guangzhou consistently has the longest B1/B2 wait times in China, often 4–10 months for an interview appointment. Applicants from Guangdong, Fujian, and surrounding provinces should plan accordingly.

**Step 4 — Prepare Documents for Interview**
Bring two 2×2 inch (50mm × 50mm) full-face color photos with a white background, no eyeglasses, taken within the last 6 months.

**Step 5 — After Approval (EVUS)**
Once you have a B1/B2 visa (valid 10 years, multiple entry), you must enroll in the Electronic Visa Update System (EVUS) every 2 years or before each trip. As of January 2026, this costs $30.75.
