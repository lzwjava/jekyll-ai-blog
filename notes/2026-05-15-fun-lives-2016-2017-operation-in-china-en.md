**Fun Live** is a WeChat-powered technical live streaming platform designed for developers by developers. It focuses primarily on **iOS engineering** but also includes **Android, backend, and AI** content. In China, the platform **operated mainly between 2016 and 2017** and hosted **over 100 online technical sessions**, providing a dedicated space for deep technical sharing and community interaction.
