Question: Why is running a group in the Messages app advantageous compared to posting on WeChat Moments or Facebook, especially when sharing many messages?

Answer:

Running a group in the Messages app has several advantages when you want to share or discuss a lot of information in one batch:

1. **Focused Communication**: Messages groups are private and only include members you invite, which keeps discussions concentrated and relevant. Social media posts, like WeChat Moments or Facebook, are usually visible to a wider audience and can get lost among other content.

2. **Threaded and Continuous Discussion**: In a Messages group, conversations flow naturally with replies, reactions, and quick back-and-forth discussions. On social media, comments are often scattered and harder to follow, especially for long threads.

3. **Reduced Noise**: You can share multiple messages without worrying about overwhelming a public feed or audience. Social media platforms often discourage posting too much at once, as it can annoy followers or get buried in algorithms.

4. **Immediate Feedback**: Messages groups allow members to respond quickly, ask clarifying questions, or react in real time. Social media comments might not get the same immediacy.

5. **Privacy and Control**: You control who is in the group and who sees the content. On public platforms, you have limited control over visibility, sharing, and context.

Overall, the Messages app creates an environment ideal for concentrated, detailed, and interactive discussions without the distractions and visibility constraints of social media feeds.
