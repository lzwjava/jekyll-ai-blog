## 1. Shenzhen–Hong Kong land ports (most used)

| Shenzhen side     | Hong Kong side                                         | Usage                                                        |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Shenzhen Bay Port | Shenzhen Bay Port Hong Kong Boundary Crossing Facility | Very convenient for cars, buses; popular for business travel |
| Futian Port       | Lok Ma Chau Spur Line Control Point                    | Best for metro crossing; connected to Shenzhen Metro Line 4  |
| Huanggang Port    | Lok Ma Chau Control Point                              | 24-hour road crossing; trucks and private cars               |
| Luohu Port        | Lo Wu Control Point                                    | Oldest and busiest; direct MTR connection                    |
| Liantang Port     | Liantang Boundary Control Point                        | Mainly vehicles and cargo                                    |

For a software entrepreneur or consultant going between Shenzhen and Hong Kong:

* **Futian ↔ Lok Ma Chau**: usually the fastest if you live/work near Shenzhen CBD.
* **Shenzhen Bay**: convenient if going to Hong Kong Island/Kowloon by car or bus.
* **Luohu**: easiest for first-time travelers because it is simple and has many transport connections.

## 2. Shenzhen sea ports near Hong Kong

### Shekou Port

* Located in Nanshan District.
* One of the closest Shenzhen ports to Hong Kong.
* Has ferries to:

  * Hong Kong Island
  * Hong Kong Airport
  * Macau
* Popular with business travelers.

### Shenzhen World Exhibition & Convention Center Terminal

* Near Bao'an.
* Passenger/ferry connections in the Greater Bay Area.

### Yantian Port

* One of the world's largest container ports.
* Mainly international shipping, not passenger travel.

### Chiwan Port

* Container and logistics port near Shekou.

For **doing software consulting / AI business**, the practical route is usually:

**Shenzhen Futian ↔ Hong Kong Kowloon/Central**

because:

* Shenzhen tech companies are concentrated in Nanshan/Futian.
* Hong Kong clients and finance companies are concentrated in Central, Wan Chai, Kowloon.
* Same-day meetings are easy.

A lot of Greater Bay Area engineers/business owners live in Shenzhen and visit Hong Kong frequently for clients, banking, networking, and international business.
