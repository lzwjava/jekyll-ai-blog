Question: What's the typical airfare from Bengaluru (BLR) to Hong Kong (HKG)?

Answer:

**One-way economy:** ₹9,000–20,000 (~$110–240 USD), budget carriers (IndiGo, AirAsia) cheapest.

**Round-trip economy:** ₹16,000–30,000 typical range (~$190–360 USD), depending on season/booking window. One-way typically ranges from ₹9,000 to ₹25,000, depending on the airline and how far in advance you book. Roundtrip prices from around $364, with the cheapest month usually being August, and Cathay Pacific flying direct with a ~5h42m flight time.

**Direct vs connecting:**
- Direct: Cathay Pacific (only nonstop carrier, ~5h45m)
- Connecting: Air India, IndiGo, Vietnam Airlines, Thai AirAsia, Singapore Airlines (via SIN/BKK, usually cheaper but 8-10h+)

**Rule of thumb:** budget ₹15-20K round-trip if booking 3-6 weeks out on a connecting flight; ₹35-55K if you want Cathay's direct nonstop.
