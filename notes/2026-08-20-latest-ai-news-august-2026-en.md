**Question:** What are the latest AI news stories recently?

**Answer:** Here's a roundup of the biggest AI stories from August 2026:

**🔬 OpenAI's Astra Solves Unsolved Math Problems**
OpenAI announced that an internal version of Astra, its next major model, solved ten previously open problems in mathematics and theoretical computer science, publishing formal Lean proofs on GitHub — all for roughly $2,000 in compute. The problems span genuine research territory, including establishing the existence of non-sofic groups and new upper bounds on sphere-packing density.

**👥 ChatGPT Hits 1 Billion Users**
ChatGPT crossed 1 billion active users on July 31, 2026 — the fastest consumer software platform in history to hit that mark, doing in under four years what took Facebook over eight.

**✈️ First AI-Piloted F-16 Flight**
DARPA successfully completed the first real-world flight of an F-16 fighter jet fully controlled by artificial intelligence, marking a major milestone in autonomous combat aviation.

**💻 Meta Launches AI Coding Assistant**
Meta unveiled Muse Code, a new AI-powered coding assistant built on its Muse Spark 1.2 model. It can write code, fix bugs, verify results, and manage complex software projects, with support for running multiple sub-agents in parallel.

**🔒 AI Transparency & Regulation**
Anthropic is introducing machine-readable watermarks for AI-generated content to meet the EU's new AI transparency requirements, with new Claude models launched in the EU from August 2 required to support this marking. Meanwhile, US AI regulation began enforcing pre-release government reviews of major models starting August 1.

**⚠️ AI & Cybercrime**
Google DeepMind published what's being called the most disturbing AI paper of the year, evaluating language models for harmful manipulation — raising concerns about AI's ability to systematically alter human beliefs at scale.
