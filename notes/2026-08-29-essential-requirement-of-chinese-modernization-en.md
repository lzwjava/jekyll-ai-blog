**Question:** What is the essential requirement (本质要求) of Chinese-style modernization (中国式现代化)?

---

**Answer:**

The **本质要求** (essential/core requirement) of 中国式现代化 is an **official fixed phrase** from the 20th Party Congress Report (2022). You must know it **word for word** for exams.

---

### The Official Answer (9 Points)

The essential requirement of Chinese-style modernization is:

| # | Chinese | Meaning |
| --- | --------- | --------- |
| 1 | 坚持中国共产党领导 | Uphold CCP leadership |
| 2 | 坚持中国特色社会主义 | Uphold socialism with Chinese characteristics |
| 3 | 实现高质量发展 | Achieve high-quality development |
| 4 | 发展全过程人民民主 | Develop whole-process people's democracy |
| 5 | 丰富人民精神世界 | Enrich the people's spiritual world |
| 6 | 实现全体人民共同富裕 | Achieve common prosperity for all people |
| 7 | 促进人与自然和谐共生 | Promote harmony between humanity and nature |
| 8 | 推动构建人类命运共同体 | Push forward a community with a shared future for mankind |
| 9 | 创造人类文明新形态 | Create a new form of human civilization |

---

### How to Actually Understand It

Think of it in **3 layers**:

---

**🔴 Layer 1 — Political Foundation (Points 1 & 2)**
> Who leads and what system?

- CCP leads everything — non-negotiable
- Must stay socialist — not copying Western liberalism
- These two are the **preconditions** without which nothing else is valid

---

**🟡 Layer 2 — Domestic Goals (Points 3–7)**
> What does modernization look like inside China?

| Point | Core Idea |
| ------- | ----------- |
| 高质量发展 | Not just GDP growth — quality matters |
| 全过程人民民主 | Democracy expressed through the whole governing process, not just elections |
| 精神世界 | Material wealth alone is not enough — culture and values matter |
| 共同富裕 | Everyone benefits — not just the rich getting richer |
| 人与自然 | Green development — ecological civilization |

---

**🔵 Layer 3 — Global Contribution (Points 8 & 9)**
> What does China offer the world?

- China's model is not just for China — it offers an **alternative path** for developing countries
- It creates a **new form of civilization** beyond the Western model

---

本质要求 =
  政治前提 (1+2)
  + 国内目标 (3+4+5+6+7)
  + 世界贡献 (8+9)

### 🔗 How It Connects to Other Concepts

| Concept | Relationship |
| --------- | ------------- |
| 四个自信 | The **confidence** behind pursuing this modernization |
| 四个全面 | The **strategy** to implement this modernization |
| 中国式现代化本质要求 | The **content/destination** of what modernization looks like |

> Think of it as: **四个自信** gives you belief → **四个全面** gives you the plan → **本质要求** defines the actual target.

---

### 📝 Exam Tips

1. **Fill-in-the-blank** — these 9 points appear frequently as blanks. Memorize the exact verbs: 坚持、实现、发展、丰富、促进、推动、创造

2. **Most tested single point** — 全体人民**共同富裕** is heavily emphasized because it distinguishes Chinese modernization from Western capitalism

3. **Essay angle** — if asked *"what makes Chinese modernization different from Western modernization?"* — point to #4 (whole-process democracy vs. electoral democracy) and #6 (common prosperity vs. trickle-down)
