**Question:** Are there many outsourcing and consultancy companies using AI to deliver products, and how do they do it?

**Answer:**

Yes — this is now mainstream, not niche. The AI outsourcing and consulting delivery market is booming in 2026. Here's a breakdown of who's doing it and how:

---

### 🏢 Who's Doing It

**Large Consulting Firms:**
McKinsey, Deloitte, KPMG, EY, BCG, and Accenture are all leading adopters. McKinsey deployed its internal AI tool "Lilli" to 72% of its 45,000 employees. Accenture reported $3.6 billion in AI bookings in FY2025 — a 120% year-on-year increase. KPMG has made AI use mandatory across all staff.

**Outsourcing/Offshore Firms:**
Top-tier AI solution providers in Vietnam, Eastern Europe, and India are now delivering work that competes on quality with Western counterparts, at blended rates 40–70% lower. The decision to outsource is increasingly a quality and speed decision, not just a cost one.

Genpact, Tata Consultancy Services, and Capgemini are among the strongest enterprise AI outsourcing providers, each integrating MLOps, model governance, and lifecycle operations into their delivery.

---

### ⚙️ How They Do It

**1. Hybrid Human-AI Teams**
Successful outsourcing teams in 2026 combine AI-assisted workflows with human expertise — people focus on strategy, governance, quality control, and complex problem-solving, while AI handles the heavy lifting in automation and testing. AI tools automatically generate test cases, identify anomalies, and analyze code quality at a scale not possible with manual testing.

**2. AI-Accelerated Code & Documentation**
Delivery teams use AI to generate and review code, write documentation, extend test coverage, and pull past architectures out of the project graveyard so new teams ramp in hours instead of days.

**3. Research & Proposal Automation**
AI dramatically accelerates the process of moving from raw insights to structured summaries, first drafts of presentations, and clearly articulated recommendations — compressing work that used to take weeks into just hours.

**4. Internal AI Platforms ("Client Zero")**
IBM credits the "Client Zero" approach — testing AI internally before selling it to clients — with $4.5 billion in productivity gains across more than 400 internal workflows.

**5. Client-Facing AI Products**
Consulting firms use AI across nine distinct delivery areas: client research automation, market intelligence, sales and marketing strategy, personalized outreach, proposal generation, client-facing AI portals, knowledge management, content creation, and training and enablement.

**6. Shifting to Outcome-Based Pricing**
McKinsey reported that roughly a quarter of its global client fees in 2025 came from outcome-based contracts — a notable shift from traditional hourly or project-based billing, driven by the fact that AI now does much of the heavy lifting.

---

### 📊 Results

A Harvard Business School study of 758 BCG consultants found that AI-assisted consultants completed 12.2% more tasks, 25.1% faster, and with over 40% higher quality output. Bottom-half performers saw a 43% quality improvement — indicating AI has a significant leveling effect across skill levels.
