**Question:** Can you download audio files from WhatsApp Web?

**Answer:**

Yes, you absolutely can download audio files directly from WhatsApp Web. Here's how it works:

---

## How to Download Audio on WhatsApp Web

**Steps:**

1. Open your browser and go to [web.whatsapp.com](https://web.whatsapp.com)
2. Scan the QR code with your phone to log in (via Settings > Linked Devices)
3. Open the chat that contains the audio message
4. Hover over the voice message — a small downward-arrow download icon will appear in the top-right corner of the message. Click it to save the audio.
5. The file will be saved to your computer's default "Downloads" folder.

---

## File Format

WhatsApp uses the Opus audio format (OGG) to save audio files on the platform, unless the file was originally sent in another format by the user.

So the downloaded file will typically be a `.opus` or `.ogg` file, not an MP3. If you need MP3, you'll have to convert it afterward using a tool like VLC, FFmpeg, or an online converter.

---

## Notes

- This works for both **voice messages** (recorded inside WhatsApp) and **audio files** shared in a chat.
- When you download a WhatsApp voice message to your PC, you'll find it in OGG format.
- The download feature works the same way on both **WhatsApp Web** (browser) and the **WhatsApp Desktop App**.
