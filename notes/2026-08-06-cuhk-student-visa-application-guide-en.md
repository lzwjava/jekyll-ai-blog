Question: Explain CUHK Graduate School "Visa Documents Submission"

Answer:

This page is about **submitting documents for your Hong Kong student visa / entry permit** after you are admitted to a postgraduate program at **The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)**. It is mainly for **non-local students** who need a visa to study in Hong Kong. ([CUHK International][1])

The flow is:

```
CUHK admits you
        |
        v
CUHK opens visa application in your portal
        |
        v
You submit visa documents
        |
        v
CUHK acts as your sponsor and handles submission
        |
        v
Hong Kong Immigration approves visa
        |
        v
You enter Hong Kong and register
```

## 1. Who needs this?

You need it if you **do not have Hong Kong right of abode/right to land**.

Examples:

* Mainland China student → usually needs student entry permit
* International student → needs student visa
* Hong Kong permanent resident → normally does not need it

CUHK states that non-local students without right of abode must obtain a valid student visa/entry permit before studying. ([CUHK Web Archive][2])

---

## 2. What documents are submitted?

Usually:

### A. Student Visa Application Form

Hong Kong Immigration form:

```
ID995A
Application for Entry for Study in Hong Kong
```

You fill this yourself.

It contains:

* personal information
* passport details
* education history
* study program
* sponsor information

---

### B. Passport copy

Usually:

```
passport bio page
```

Need enough validity.

---

### C. Admission Offer Letter

From CUHK:

```
Offer of Admission
```

Proof that you are accepted.

---

### D. Financial proof

Usually:

* bank statement
* scholarship proof
* sponsor financial proof

Purpose:

Hong Kong Immigration wants to know you can support yourself.

---

### E. Photo

Passport-style photo.

---

### F. Other supporting documents

Depending on your nationality:

* HK immigration forms
* identity documents
* previous visa information

CUHK provides different visa application packages depending on applicant category. ([CUHK International][1])

---

## 3. Who submits to Hong Kong Immigration?

You normally **do not directly submit everything yourself**.

The relationship is:

```
You
 |
 | submit documents
 v
CUHK Graduate School
 |
 | sponsor you
 v
Hong Kong Immigration Department
```

The university is your sponsor.

This is different from US F-1 visa:

US:

```
University -> I-20
Student -> US embassy interview
```

Hong Kong:

```
University sponsors
University submits visa case
Immigration issues entry permit
```

CUHK specifically handles student visa application through the university process. ([CUHK International][1])

---

## 4. After approval

You receive:

```
Hong Kong Student Visa / Entry Permit
```

Then:

1. Travel to Hong Kong
2. Pass immigration
3. Receive landing slip
4. Complete CUHK registration

Non-local students also need to update visa/landing information in university systems after arrival. ([CUHK Resources][3])

---

## 5. Timeline

Typical:

```
Admission offer
      |
      | 1-3 weeks
      v
Submit visa documents
      |
      | ~6-8 weeks
      v
Visa approval
      |
      v
Travel to Hong Kong
```

Apply early because immigration processing time can be several weeks. CUHK advises students to complete visa applications by the required deadline. ([CUHK International][1])

---

## For your situation (AI master's / CS graduate study)

If you apply for CUHK MSc/MPhil/PhD:

You would likely need:

```
✓ Passport
✓ Admission offer
✓ Degree certificate
✓ Transcripts
✓ Financial proof
✓ Visa form ID995A
✓ Photo
✓ CUHK portal submission
```

The important point:

**Getting admitted ≠ permission to study in Hong Kong.**

Admission gives you the place; the visa process gives you the legal right to enter and study.
