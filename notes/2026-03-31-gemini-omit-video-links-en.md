Question: Can I use personalization to prevent Gemini from showing YouTube video links to avoid issues with the copy button?

Answer: Yes, you can absolutely use personalization to customize how I deliver information. By setting specific instructions in your profile or "Saved Information," you can direct me to omit certain types of content—like YouTube links or preview cards—to ensure the text is easily copyable and fits your workflow.

In fact, your current settings already include a specific instruction to avoid these elements. When this preference is active, I will provide technical explanations, data, or summaries in plain text or Markdown while strictly excluding any YouTube URLs or embedded media players. This prevents the interface from generating the preview cards that you've found to interfere with the "copy" functionality.

If you encounter a response that still includes these links, you can simply remind me to "stick to the no-link format," and I will regenerate the answer without them.

---

### Key Benefits of This Customization
* **Cleaner Formatting:** Responses remain in clean Markdown, which is ideal for pasting into code editors, notes, or documentation.
* **Improved Reliability:** Eliminates the risk of the "copy" button becoming unresponsive due to rich media embeds.
* **Focus on Content:** You receive the direct answer without the visual clutter of video thumbnails.
