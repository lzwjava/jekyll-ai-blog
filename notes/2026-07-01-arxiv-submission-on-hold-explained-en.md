Question: What does arXiv's "On Hold" submission status mean?

Answer:

It means your arXiv submission has been flagged during moderation and paused before it goes public. arXiv runs every submission through automated checks (text/metadata classifiers, overlap/plagiarism detection, category-fit checks, formatting validation) plus potential human moderator review. When something needs a closer look, the submission enters the "on hold" state instead of proceeding to announcement.

Practically:

- **You usually don't need to do anything.** Editors either resolve it silently or email you with specific instructions. They contact you via the email on your account.
- **It can take several days** due to submission volume — holds are common, not necessarily a sign anything is wrong.
- **Don't resubmit or delete-and-resubmit.** Duplicate submissions trigger more moderation and longer delays. Holds don't expire, so waiting is safe.

Common hold triggers: ambiguous primary category (moderators may reclassify), text overlap with prior work, missing/incomplete metadata, license issues, or being a first-time submitter without endorsement in a given category. None of these necessarily mean rejection — most resolve into a normal announcement, sometimes with a category change.

The one thing worth checking proactively: make sure your chosen primary category genuinely matches the paper, since misclassification is the most frequent cause of a hold that requires your input.
