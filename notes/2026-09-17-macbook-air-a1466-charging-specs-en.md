Question: **What is the charging specification of the MacBook Air A1466?**

Answer: The **MacBook Air A1466 uses Apple's MagSafe 2 charging connector** on most later A1466 generations.

### Charging specs

| Spec           | A1466                                  |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Connector      | **MagSafe 2**                          |
| Charger        | **45 W Apple MagSafe 2 Power Adapter** |
| Output voltage | **14.85 V DC**                         |
| Output current | **3.05 A max**                         |
| Power          | ~**45 W**                              |
| Input          | 100–240 V AC, 50/60 Hz                 |

So electrically:

```text
MagSafe 2
   │
   ├── ~14.85 V DC
   ├── up to ~3.05 A
   └── ≈45 W
```

### Important distinction by year

The **A1466 designation spans several years**, but the later A1466 models use the **45 W MagSafe 2 adapter**.

Older MacBook Air models before the MagSafe 2 transition used the **original MagSafe connector**, so don't buy a random "MacBook Air 45 W charger" without checking the connector.

If you're buying a **used A1466 for hardware/repair experiments**, the useful thing is that MagSafe is **not USB-C PD**. You cannot simply feed it 20 V USB-C power directly into the MagSafe input.
