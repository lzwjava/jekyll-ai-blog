Question: Where is the power connector on the Quadro 4000?

Answer: On this **Quadro 4000**, the external power connector is on the **rear/top edge near the end of the card**, opposite the display bracket. It uses a **6-pin PCIe power connector**.

In the photo, look around the **upper-left area of the card**: the small recessed connector near the PCB edge is the power input, while the other connector(s) are for the SLI interface.

The card therefore gets power from:

1. **PCIe x16 slot** → motherboard power
2. **6-pin PCIe power cable** → PSU auxiliary power
