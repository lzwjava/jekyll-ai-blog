Question: How to block someone on LinkedIn so they are removed as a connection and cannot send you invitations anymore?

Answer:

On LinkedIn, simply **removing a connection is not enough**—they can still view your profile and send you another invitation. To fully prevent contact, you need to **block** them.

### ✅ Best method: Block the person

This both removes the connection and prevents any future interaction.

**Steps:**

1. Go to the person’s profile.
2. Click the **“More”** button (usually next to “Message”).
3. Select **“Report/Block”**.
4. Click **“Block [Name]”**.
5. Enter your LinkedIn password (if prompted) and confirm.

### 🔒 What happens after blocking

* They are automatically **removed from your connections**.
* They **cannot send you connection requests** anymore.
* They **cannot view your profile** (with some rare edge cases like shared groups).
* You also won’t see their profile or activity.

---

### ⚠️ If you only remove connection (not recommended here)

Steps:

1. Go to their profile.
2. Click **“More” → “Remove connection”**.

But:

* They can still **reconnect or message you again**.

---

### 💡 Extra control (optional)

If you don’t want to fully block but still reduce contact:

* Adjust **who can send you invitations** (Settings → Visibility).
* Turn off or limit **message requests**.

---

### Bottom line

* **Remove connection = temporary**
* **Block = permanent control (what you want)**
