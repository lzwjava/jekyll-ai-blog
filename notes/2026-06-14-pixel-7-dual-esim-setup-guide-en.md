Yes. The Pixel 7 supports eSIM. A few key details:

- Pixel 7 and later can run **two eSIMs simultaneously** (Dual eSIM), or one nano SIM + one eSIM.
- The dual eSIM feature requires carrier support — check with your carrier first.

**To verify on-device:** dial `*#06#` — if you see an EID code, eSIM is active and supported.

**To add an eSIM:** Settings → Network & Internet → SIMs → Add SIM → Download a SIM instead.
