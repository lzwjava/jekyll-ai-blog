**Question:** Who is Chris Malachowsky, NVIDIA cofounder? Give a comprehensive introduction.

**Answer:**

**Chris Malachowsky** is an American electrical engineer, inventor, and business executive best known as one of the three co-founders of **NVIDIA Corporation**, alongside Jensen Huang (CEO) and Curtis Priem. He co-founded the company in April 1993 and remains actively involved as a **Founder and NVIDIA Fellow**, serving as a senior technology executive and member of the executive staff.

Born on **May 2, 1959**, Malachowsky initially planned to follow in his father's footsteps as a physician but shifted to engineering during college. He earned his **Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering (BSEE)** from the **University of Florida** in 1980 and his **Master of Science in Computer Science (MSCS)** from **Santa Clara University** in 1986. Both universities have recognized him with Distinguished Alumnus awards, and he has received honorary doctorates from the University of Silicon Valley (2022) and the University of Florida (2023). The University of Florida named **Malachowsky Hall** (a state-of-the-art AI and data science hub) in his honor due to his contributions and NVIDIA's partnership with the institution.

Before NVIDIA, Malachowsky held engineering and technical leadership roles at **Hewlett-Packard (HP)** and **Sun Microsystems**, where he gained expertise in hardware and software engineering, IC (integrated circuit) design, and methodology. At Sun Microsystems, he worked with fellow future co-founder Curtis Priem. The idea for NVIDIA emerged during a casual breakfast meeting at a Denny's in Silicon Valley in 1993, where the trio envisioned specialized chips to accelerate computer graphics—leading to the invention and popularization of the **Graphics Processing Unit (GPU)**.

Malachowsky has been instrumental in shaping NVIDIA from a startup focused on 3D graphics into a global leader in visual computing, parallel processing, accelerated computing, and artificial intelligence. He has managed and driven core technologies, led functions including IT, operations, product engineering, and most recently oversaw the company's world-class research organization developing strategic future technologies. He is a recognized authority on IC design and holds close to **40 patents**, many related to graphics and computing innovations.

As of 2026, with over **40 years** of industry experience, Malachowsky continues contributing to NVIDIA's technical direction amid the company's dominance in AI, gaming, data centers, and accelerated computing. He has emphasized long-term vision, resilience (noting NVIDIA's "overnight success" took 30 years), ethical leadership, and building lasting value for employees, families, and shareholders.

His work has earned accolades, including selection as a 2025 Fellow of the **National Academy of Inventors** and the **2026 IEEE Robert N. Noyce Medal** for pioneering parallel computing architectures and leadership in semiconductor design that transformed AI, scientific research, and accelerated computing.
