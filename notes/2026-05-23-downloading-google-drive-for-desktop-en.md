**Question:** Can we download Google Drive for desktop from this support page?

**Answer:**

Yes. The page is Google's official support doc for **Google Drive for desktop** (the sync client, formerly known as Backup and Sync / Drive File Stream).

For macOS, the direct download link on that page is:

```
https://dl.google.com/drive-file-stream/GoogleDrive.dmg
```

Steps:
1. Download the `.dmg`
2. Open it
3. Follow the installer
4. Sign in with your Google account from the menu bar icon

It mounts your Google Drive as a virtual drive in Finder (streaming, not full local copy by default), with optional selective sync for offline access. Up to 4 Google accounts simultaneously.

Since you're on M2 MacBook Air — it's ARM-native, no Rosetta needed.
