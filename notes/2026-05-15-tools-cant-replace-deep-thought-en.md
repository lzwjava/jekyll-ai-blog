Question: Do I understand correctly that the biggest takeaway today is that, no matter what we do, we should have our own thinking, follow our unique path, and fully explore what we have—like truly experimenting with an RTX 4070—even if more expensive GPUs can give results, they can't replace learning and deep thought?

Answer:

Exactly. The core insight is that tools and resources, no matter how powerful, are just facilitators—they can show you results superficially, but they can’t substitute for genuine understanding, curiosity, and independent experimentation. Playing with what you have, exploring deeply, and developing your own approach is what builds real skill and insight. GPUs like the RTX 4070 are great, but mastery comes from the way you think, experiment, and iterate—not from the hardware alone.
