## Question: Introduction to Guangzhou University

## Answer:

Guangzhou University (GZHU, 广州大学) is a public comprehensive university located in Guangzhou, one of China's major economic centers and the core city of the Guangdong–Hong Kong–Macao Greater Bay Area. ([Guangzhou University][1])

### History

* The university's roots can be traced back to **1927**.
* The modern Guangzhou University was officially established in **2000** through the merger of several higher education institutions, including the former Guangzhou University, Guangzhou Normal College, Guangzhou Institute of Education, and South China Construction Institute West Campus. ([Guangzhou University][1])

### Size

Guangzhou University is a large comprehensive university:

* Around **30,000+ undergraduate students**
* Around **9,000 master's and doctoral students**
* More than **3,000 faculty and staff**
* **26 schools/colleges**
* Offers programs across humanities, sciences, engineering, economics, management, law, education, arts, and interdisciplinary fields. ([Guangzhou University][1])

### Campuses

It has three main campuses:

1. **University Town Campus (main campus)**

   * Located in Guangzhou Higher Education Mega Center (大学城)
   * Modern campus with many research facilities

2. **Guihuagang Campus**

   * Located closer to downtown Guangzhou

3. **Huangpu Campus**

   * Focused on research and innovation activities ([Guangzhou University][1])

### Academic Strengths

Guangzhou University is not usually grouped with China's top "C9" universities, but it is considered one of the stronger **regional comprehensive universities**, especially in Guangdong.

Strong areas include:

* **Engineering**
* **Computer science**
* **Civil engineering**
* **Environmental science**
* **Geography and remote sensing**
* **Materials science**
* **Artificial intelligence-related interdisciplinary research** ([Guangzhou University][2])

The university has several research disciplines ranked highly internationally by ESI, with engineering among its strongest fields. ([Guangzhou University][3])

### Position in China

A simple comparison:

* Higher than many ordinary local universities because Guangzhou is a wealthy city with strong government support.
* Below elite universities such as Sun Yat-sen University and South China University of Technology.
* Comparable to many strong provincial-level universities.

Its advantage is its location: being in Guangzhou gives students access to opportunities in:

* AI companies
* Internet companies
* Manufacturing
* Finance
* Greater Bay Area technology industry

### For Computer Science / AI

For AI and software engineering, Guangzhou University is interesting because:

* Guangzhou has a large technology ecosystem.
* The university is investing in areas such as intelligent applications, big data, and information technology.
* Students can benefit from nearby companies and research collaborations. ([Guangzhou University][2])

It is not a top AI research powerhouse like Tsinghua University or University of Science and Technology of China, but it is a solid choice for regional engineering talent development.
