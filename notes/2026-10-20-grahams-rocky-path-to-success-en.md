**Question:** Was Paul Graham's life smooth? What were his failures and struggles?

**Answer:**

No — far from smooth. Paul Graham's path was defined by drift, detours, near-disasters, and public controversies, even though it ultimately ended in remarkable success.

---

### 🔀 Early Directionlessness

At Harvard, Graham had to confront something about himself — he was not cut out for academia. As the years went by, he would occasionally reflect on the course of his life. There seemed to be no real design or direction to it. It was like the "cheesy hacks" he did in high school — patching things together, figuring out through constant trial and error.

He bounced between disciplines: computer science, philosophy, and then unexpectedly, painting. He returned to the States broke but determined to try his hand at painting. This phase had no clear payoff — just a passion he pursued without certainty it would lead anywhere.

---

### 💸 Viaweb: Close to Failure Multiple Times

At the time Graham co-founded Viaweb, they made several mistakes along the way, and the idea came close to failing several times.

He acknowledged this himself. When they launched Viaweb, it seemed laughable to VCs and e-commerce "experts." They were just a couple of guys in an apartment, which did not seem cool in 1995 the way it does now. And the thing they had built, as far as investors could tell, wasn't even software — to them, software meant big, honking Windows apps.

On his own worst decisions during Viaweb, Graham admitted: the worst mistake he made was probably not being strong enough with investors. Another big mistake was not investigating IP agreements signed by people they hired — that nearly sank them later.

He also noted that starting Viaweb felt like a compromise to him personally — Viaweb felt like more of a compromise than the way he'd been living before, because it was something he was doing mostly for money.

---

### 🎨 The "Failed Painter" Arc

Graham spent years genuinely trying to be a painter — studying at RISD and the Accademia di Belle Arti in Florence. He earned a degree in painting from the Rhode Island School of Design. He never became a professional artist; the painting career effectively ended when he turned toward startups. But he channeled the discipline into his work on Viaweb, his essays, and YC's culture.

---

### 😤 Public Controversies & Criticism

Later in his career, Graham drew significant backlash. He tweeted opinions about founders' backgrounds — specifically about whether the Airbnb founders were poor — that sparked a chorus of people asking him to reconsider. He refused to acknowledge the issue and started blocking critics. People felt that, after becoming a central figure in Silicon Valley, Graham had stopped getting contradictory advice and become complacent.

He was also criticized for antagonizing the entire crypto community, which was seen as entirely unnecessary.

---

### 📌 Summary

| Phase | Struggle |
| --- | --- |
| Harvard years | No direction, not suited for academia |
| Post-PhD | Broke after painting abroad, no clear career |
| Viaweb (1995–98) | Nearly failed multiple times; dismissed by VCs |
| Investor relations | Too passive with investors; IP deal nearly sank company |
| Post-YC fame | Public controversies, seen as out of touch |

Graham's own philosophy — that a haphazard path teaches you more than a planned one — was largely drawn from his own messy life experience.
