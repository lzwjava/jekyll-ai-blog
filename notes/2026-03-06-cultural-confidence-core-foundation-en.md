Question: In the context of adhering to and developing socialism with Chinese characteristics in the new era, which of the "Four Confidences" is considered more fundamental, extensive, and profound?

Answer:

The correct answer is **D. Cultural Confidence (文化自信)**.

### Detailed Explanation:

The "Four Confidences" (四个自信) refer to:
1.  **Confidence in the Path (道路自信)**
2.  **Confidence in the Theory (理论自信)**
3.  **Confidence in the System (制度自信)**
4.  **Confidence in the Culture (文化自信)**

Among these, **Cultural Confidence** is explicitly defined as being **more fundamental, more extensive, and more profound** (更基础、更广泛、更深厚).

*   **Why it is more fundamental:** Culture is the spiritual bloodline of a nation. It underpins the path, theory, and system. The path, theory, and system of socialism with Chinese characteristics are all rooted in China's excellent traditional culture, revolutionary culture, and advanced socialist culture. Without cultural confidence, the other three confidences lack a deep spiritual foundation.
*   **Why it is more extensive:** Culture permeates every aspect of social life and individual behavior, influencing values, ethics, and aesthetics across the entire population more broadly than specific political systems or theoretical frameworks.
*   **Why it is more profound:** It represents the deepest spiritual pursuit of a nation. As stated in official discourse, it is "the most basic, deepest, and most enduring force" (最基本、最深沉、最持久的力量) in the development of a country and a nation.

Your previous selection of **C (System Confidence)** is incorrect in this specific context. While confidence in the socialist system with Chinese characteristics is crucial for ensuring stability and efficiency, it is cultural confidence that provides the underlying value system and spiritual motivation that sustains the system, the path, and the theory over the long term.
