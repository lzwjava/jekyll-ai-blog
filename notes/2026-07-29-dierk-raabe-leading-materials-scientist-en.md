Question: Who is Dierk Raabe?

Answer: German materials scientist, born 18 April 1965 in Hilden. He is a professor at RWTH Aachen University and director of the Max Planck Institute for Sustainable Materials in Düsseldorf.

Not AI/ML — pure metallurgy/materials science. Core work: sustainable metallurgy, hydrogen, steels, aluminium alloys, high entropy alloys, microstructure, alloy design, computational materials science, and atom probe tomography. Recent focus is on AI/ML applied to alloy design though — his lab lists that as an interest area alongside the classic metallurgy topics.

Credentials/impact:
- As of March 2023, he is the most cited computational materials scientist and physical metallurgist, with an H-index of 155
- Recipient of the 2004 Leibniz Prize, 2022 Acta Materialia Gold Medal, honorary doctorate from NTNU (2022)
- Received a 2012 ERC advanced grant, the largest single-researcher grant in Europe
- Mentored more than 40 PhDs (a newer bio puts it at more than 55)
- Adjunct professor at Carnegie Mellon

If you ran into the name via a materials-informatics or AI-for-science paper — that tracks, since his group is now pushing quantum-mechanical simulation + ML into alloy design pipelines.
