Question: Is iOS 26.4 the Apple AI update, and does it have Apple AI in it?

Answer: **iOS 26.4** is a significant update released on **March 24, 2026**, but it is not the "all-in-one" Apple Intelligence (AI) update many were expecting. While it does include several new AI-powered features, the highly anticipated "revamped Siri" with deep system integration is still missing.

Here is how Apple AI is integrated into iOS 26.4:

### **New AI Features in iOS 26.4**
* **Playlist Playground:** A new generative AI feature in Apple Music that allows you to create custom playlists using text prompts (e.g., "90s grunge for a rainy drive"). It even generates a custom AI title and description for the list.
* **Apple Creator Studio (Freeform):** The Freeform app has been merged into a new "Creator Studio" that includes on-device AI tools like **Super Resolution** for upscaling images and **Auto Crop** for better framing.
* **OpenAI Integration:** You can now generate high-quality images in various styles directly within the Creator Studio using generative models from OpenAI.
* **Third-Party AI in CarPlay:** For the first time, CarPlay supports voice-based conversational apps, allowing you to use **ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Claude** while driving (though developers must update their apps to support this).

### **Expanded Apple Intelligence Features**
* **Live Translation:** Now supports more regional variants (like Indian and Singaporean English) and additional languages like Canadian French and Swiss German.
* **Hold Assist:** This AI feature, which waits on hold for you during customer service calls and notifies you when a human picks up, has expanded to more regions, including **Mandarin (Taiwan)** and **Korean**.

### **What is still missing?**
* **The "New" Siri:** The promised version of Siri that can tap into your personal information, perform complex actions across apps, and have "on-screen awareness" is **not included** in this update. Apple has confirmed it is still coming later in 2026.

---

### **Other Key Changes**
* **Keyboard Fix:** Addresses a long-standing bug where the keyboard would miss characters during rapid typing.
* **New Emoji:** Eight new characters, including an orca, trombone, and a "distorted face."
* **Offline Music Recognition:** Shazam/Music Recognition in the Control Center can now identify songs without an internet connection and show results once you're back online.
