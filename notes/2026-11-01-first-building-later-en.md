Question: How can Yinwang's philosophy guide the building of truthsoft.dev?

Answer:

The alignment between Yinwang's philosophy and truthsoft.dev is already strong — the company name itself ("Truth Software / 求真软件") echoes his core obsession: **thinking clearly and seeking truth over following hype.**

Here's how his principles map onto the journey:

---

### 🧠 Think First, Build Second
Wang Yin argues you cannot write perfect code in one shot — insights come gradually. The most effective path is to repeatedly revise and polish, not just write more. Great programmers delete more code than they keep.

→ For truthsoft.dev: resist the pressure to ship fast. Build something real, then refine it.

---

### ✂️ Simplicity is the Target
He loves reducing things to their minimal form — no room for convoluted theories or "best practices" from gurus. True mastery means slowing down, dissecting one idea thoroughly, until you could explain it to a child.

→ Your pricing model (transparent token billing) and mission ("build software that deserves to exist") already embody this.

---

### 🚫 Reject Dogma & Hype
Wang dismisses debates over programming paradigms as pointless tribalism — "like cults that prioritize ideology over practical problem-solving." He advocates mastering core concepts without allegiance to any one paradigm.

→ Don't chase AI trends. Use AI where it genuinely helps (you already do with RAG, agents), and skip it where it doesn't.

---

### 🔬 Self-Reliance & Independent Thinking
"Don't follow others blindly; think for yourself." He rails against fast learning because it breeds shallow copies.

→ This is the competitive moat: most software shops copy what's popular. Truthsoft can think from first principles.

---

### 🧪 Tests Should Serve Reality
His core thesis: excessive, unnecessary testing not only fails to improve code quality but actually reduces it, increases workload, and delays progress. He writes tests — but selectively and smartly.

→ Write what's useful, skip what's ritual.

---

### 🌱 Think in Years
Your own stated value — "think in years, favoring sustained progress over quick wins" — is the most Yinwang-like of all. His blog consistently challenges mainstream programming dogmas, and he has been publishing long-form technical writing for years, building trust with a small but deeply engaged audience.

---

The journey *is* the work. Truthsoft's best version isn't a product launch — it's the accumulated clarity that comes from building honestly, thinking independently, and caring about what you make.
