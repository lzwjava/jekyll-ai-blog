### 🔥 1. TypeSafe AI — **Jev** (the model you're asking about)

Jev is a proprietary AI model developed by **TypeSafe AI**, a San Francisco–based startup founded in 2024. It was released in limited early access on **September 15, 2026**, alongside the announcement of a **$40 million seed round** led by DCVC.

Founded by former OpenAI researcher **Diogo Almeida**, TypeSafe AI launched Jev as a "System One Model" — a new class of AI that **returns structured decisions instead of generating text**. In other words, it cannot write an email or hold a conversation — it is designed purely for software to consume, not humans to read.

The massive fundraise came just weeks after the launch, as Jev quickly went viral and was reportedly adopted by **a third of Fortune 500 companies** within weeks. The company then raised **$870 million at a $7.5B valuation**, led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z).

Key claim: TypeSafe describes Jev as "a frontier-intelligence function call: unstructured state in, typed probabilistic decisions out." The current version is **Jev 1.13**, available via Python SDK, Node.js SDK, Vercel's AI Gateway, and Cloudflare Workers AI.

### 🤖 2. AutoTrust AI — **JEV-9B / JEV-27B / JEV-27B-VL / JEV-35B**

This is a **Singapore-based open-source lab** that distilled TypeSafe's closed Jev model into open-weight versions:

AutoTrust AI built **JEV-9B** as an integrated System 1 + System 2 model using Qwen3.5-9B as the backbone, distilled from TypeSafe Jev 1.13. A new update in October 2026 added visual capabilities including robot arm and computer use.

AutoTrust's **JEV-27B-VL**, its open-weight visual decision model, ranked **#1 on Hugging Face's global Models trending list** in rankings captured October 8, 2026. Nine repositories on AutoTrust's Hugging Face page recorded **2.98 million downloads** in the rolling 30-day count.
