Question: Specifically, for Ping An car insurance, how to use their app to handle a car accident?

Answer:

### Using the Ping An "Good Car Owner" App (平安好车主APP) for Car Accident Handling

Ping An Insurance's primary app for car owners is called **平安好车主** (Ping An Good Car Owner). It provides one-stop services for reporting accidents, claims processing, damage assessment, and more. Download it from official app stores (App Store for iOS, or major Android stores like Huawei, Tencent, etc.).

#### Prerequisites
- Register and log in with your phone number.
- Bind your vehicle (add car plate number) and insurance policy for full access to claims features.
- For best results, ensure you have a Ping An car insurance policy.

#### Step-by-Step Process for Handling an Accident via the App

1. **Immediate Safety and Reporting**:
   - Ensure safety first: Move vehicles if possible, call 120 for injuries, 122 for police if needed.
   - Open the **平安好车主** app.
   - Go to the homepage or bottom navigation: Tap **办理赔** (Handle Claims) or **一键报案** (One-Key Report).

2. **Report the Accident (自助报案 / One-Key Report)**:
   - Select **我要报案** (I Want to Report a Case).
   - The app supports **24/7 online reporting**.
   - Provide details: Accident time, location, description, involved parties.
   - If minor accident: Use **快处易赔** (Quick Processing and Easy Compensation) if available in your region (e.g., Shanghai and others).
     - For single-vehicle: Confirm damage, take guided photos, generate accident info.
     - For two-vehicle (both Ping An insured preferred): Both parties take photos as prompted, agree on fault, generate electronic agreement, then proceed to report.
   - Upload photos of the scene, damage, license plates, etc.

3. **Damage Assessment (车损测算 / Smart Damage Calculation)**:
   - In the claims section: Use **车损测算** feature.
   - Take and upload photos of damaged parts (guided by app).
   - AI quickly assesses damage, provides repair estimate, parts list, and even next-year premium impact.
   - For simple cases: Complete loss assessment online without waiting for an inspector.

4. **Special Features for Faster Claims**:
   - **信任赔** (Trust Claim): If eligible (one person, one car, one quota based on driving history), self-claim within your credit limit – just upload photos and confirm payout (2 steps).
   - **视频理赔** (Video Claims): Contact claims handler via app for live video inspection.
   - **线上极速理赔** (Online Superfast Claims): Upload photos/docs, complete assessment and payout fully online.

5. **Track Progress**:
   - In **办理赔** section: View real-time case status, timeline (时光轴), pending tasks, and required documents.
   - App shows inspector location/ETA if on-site needed.

6. **Submit Documents and Receive Payout**:
   - Upload any required materials (e.g., police report if involved).
   - For eligible cases: "First payout, then repair" – get compensation quickly.
   - Payout via bank transfer (fast for qualifying cases).

#### Additional Tips
- For complex accidents (injuries, disputes): Still call police (122) and hotline (95511), but use app to report simultaneously.
- Both parties Ping An insured: Simpler process, possible "each repairs own car" without police.
- App also offers roads rescue, repair shop booking, etc.
- Procedures may vary slightly by region; app will guide based on location.

This makes minor accidents handleable in minutes online, reducing wait times.
