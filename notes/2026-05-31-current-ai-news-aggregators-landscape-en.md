**Question:** What AI-driven generative news hubs exist now — equivalents of Yahoo News, Toutiao, NewsBreak, InfoQ, Hacker News — that are AI-generated or AI-curated?

**Answer:**

Here's the full landscape, broken down by category:

---

## 1. The Graveyard / Pivots (Important History)

**Artifact** (Instagram co-founders Systrom & Krieger) — the closest thing to an "AI Toutiao for the West." Less than a year after its launch, it shut down in February 2024 due to inability to attract a significant number of users. Its technology was then sold to Yahoo.

**Digg** — tried twice. Relaunched in 2025 as an AI-powered news aggregator with over 9 million graph connections, real-time X ingestion, sentiment analysis, and clustering algorithms — but its open beta shut down in March 2026 due to an overwhelming AI bot invasion. Now it's pivoted again, this time focused specifically on AI news, tracking influential voices and surfacing content worth paying attention to.

## 3. Yahoo News — Did It Get AI?

Yes. Yahoo acquired Artifact's technology when it shut down in April 2024, and has been integrating its AI personalization and summarization stack into Yahoo News.

---

## 4. Toutiao (ByteDance) — The Original AI News

Toutiao is still the most technically sophisticated AI news system ever deployed at scale — recommendation transformer + reinforcement learning on engagement signals. No Western equivalent has matched it. ByteDance's internal moat here is enormous. ByteDance is discussing capex of up to $70B in 2026 for data centers and AI infrastructure, which includes doubling down on recommendation AI.

---

## 5. Fully Autonomous AI Newsroom?

Over 80% of newsrooms in North America now leverage AI, up from 37% in 2019, with LLMs helping systematically with technical tasks like automating "template-ready" articles for sports or weather reports. But a **fully autonomous** generative newsroom — no humans in the loop — doesn't really exist at serious scale yet. The closest attempt: HeyWire AI announced WELLS, a self-prompting fully autonomous AI content generation engine targeting Q3 2024, but it has not had any notable traction.
