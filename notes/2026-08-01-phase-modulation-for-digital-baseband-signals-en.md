Question: What is the modulation method called when the initial phase of a carrier wave varies according to a digital baseband signal?

Answer: The correct answer is **C) Phase Modulation (调相)**.

### Explanation

* **Phase Modulation (PM / Phase-Shift Keying):** In this scheme, the **phase** (or initial phase) of the high-frequency carrier wave is altered in proportion to the changes in the modulating baseband signal.
* **Frequency Modulation (FM):** The **frequency** of the carrier wave changes according to the baseband signal, while its amplitude and phase remain constant.
* **Amplitude Modulation (AM):** The **amplitude** of the carrier wave changes according to the baseband signal, while its frequency and phase remain constant.

Since the question explicitly states that the **initial phase** varies with the baseband signal, it refers to **Phase Modulation (调相)**.
