**Question:** What has TSMC's market valuation trajectory been over the last 10 years?

**Answer:**

As of June 2026, TSMC has a market capitalization of $2.153 trillion USD, making it the world's 6th most valuable company by market cap.

Over the last decade, TSMC's valuation has experienced explosive growth, particularly in the AI boom era:

**Recent Growth (2022–2026):**
TSMC market cap in 2022 was $386.30B. In 2023 it surged 39.6% to $539.33B. In 2024 it surged 89.9% to $1.02T. In 2025 it surged 53.9% to $1.58T. In 2026 it surged 39.8% to $2.20T. Over the 2021–2026 period, TSMC market cap has grown for 4 consecutive years with a +28.7% compound annual growth rate.

**Overall Trajectory:**
Since October 24, 1994, TSMC's market cap has increased from $3.73B to $1.95T, a compound annual growth rate of 21.87%. The acceleration is visible: the company more than quintupled in value over just 4 years (2022–2026), driven primarily by AI chip demand and TSMC's 70% market share in advanced foundry processes.

**What's driving it:**
The 2024–2025 surge (especially the 89.9% and 53.9% jumps) reflects the AI boom. TSMC is the world's largest dedicated chip foundry with about 70% market share in 2025, and cutting-edge process technologies justify premium pricing and gross margins.
