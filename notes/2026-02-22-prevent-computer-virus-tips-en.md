Question: From the perspective of preventing computer viruses, good computer usage habits combined with the use of antivirus software can greatly reduce the risk of what?

Answer:

Good computer usage habits combined with the use of antivirus software can greatly reduce the risk of **computer virus infection** (or simply **infection**).

**Explanation:**
*   **Preventive Measures:** Good habits (such as not opening suspicious email attachments, avoiding untrusted websites, and keeping software updated) act as the first line of defense by minimizing exposure to threats.
*   **Active Defense:** Antivirus software provides real-time scanning and removal capabilities to detect and neutralize malware that might bypass behavioral precautions.
*   **Result:** The combination of these two layers significantly lowers the probability of a system becoming compromised by malicious code, known as a "computer virus infection."
