**Question:** What are the Chinese search keywords for finding a PC case speaker/piezo buzzer?

---

**Answer:**

Here are the most effective search terms:

---

### Most Direct Terms

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **主板蜂鸣器** | zhǔbǎn fēngmíng qì | Motherboard buzzer |
| **PC喇叭** | PC lǎba | PC speaker (buzzer) |
| **机箱喇叭** | jīxiāng lǎba | Case speaker |
| **电脑报警器** | diànnǎo bàojǐng qì | PC alarm beeper |
| **BIOS蜂鸣器** | BIOS fēngmíng qì | BIOS buzzer |

---

### Best to Copy-Paste into Shopee / Lazada / Taobao / 1688

```
主板蜂鸣器
```
```
机箱喇叭 4pin
```
```
PC喇叭 主板
```

---

### What You'll See in Results

The listing will show a small black cylinder with a 4-pin connector wire. Price should be **very cheap** — under RM5 / NT$30 / ¥5 CNY. If it looks like a full audio speaker, that's the wrong item.

---

### Platform Recommendations

| Platform | Best search term |
|---|---|
| Shopee (MY/TW/PH) | `主板蜂鸣器` or `机箱喇叭` |
| Lazada | `PC speaker buzzer motherboard` (English works fine here) |
| Taobao / 1688 | `主板蜂鸣器 4pin` |
| PChome (TW) | `主機板喇叭` |

> Note: `喇叭` (lǎba) is the casual/common word for a small buzzer or speaker in Chinese — most sellers use it for this type of component.
