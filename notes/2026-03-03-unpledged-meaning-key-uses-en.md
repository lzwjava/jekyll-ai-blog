Question: What does "unpledged" mean?

Answer: 

**Unpledged** means **not committed** or **not bound** to any particular side, candidate, party, delegate, or promise.

Here are the most common meanings depending on context:

- **Politics / Presidential primaries (most frequent usage in news)**  
  An **unpledged delegate** (especially in the Democratic Party in the US) is a delegate who is **not required** to vote for any specific presidential candidate at the national convention.  
  Examples:  
  - superdelegates (in past Democratic rules)  
  - some party leaders, elected officials, and DNC members who start as unpledged  
  - certain "unpledged add-on" delegates  

- **General meaning**  
  - Not promised or given as a pledge/guarantee to anyone  
  - Free from any obligation, commitment, or loyalty  

- **Other common contexts**  
  - **Unpledged delegate** → not bound to support a particular candidate  
  - **Unpledged vote** → a voter or elector not committed in advance  
  - **Unpledged assets / funds** → money or property not promised as collateral or donation  
  - **Unpledged support** → support that has not been publicly promised yet  

**Quick examples in sentences:**

- "There are still 100+ unpledged delegates who could decide the nomination."
- "She remained unpledged and kept her options open until the last debate."
- "The super PAC collected millions in unpledged contributions."

In 2024–2026 U.S. political news you will see "unpledged" most often when talking about Democratic Party convention delegates who are not bound by primary/caucus results.
