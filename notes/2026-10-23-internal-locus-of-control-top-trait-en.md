**Question:** What does Alexandr Wang say about internal locus of control?

**Answer:**

**Alexandr Wang** (founder of Scale AI, now Chief AI Officer at Meta) lists **internal locus of control as the #1 hiring trait** he looks for at Scale AI — ahead of problem-solving, being impressive, and collaboration.

Here is how he frames it:

He draws a simple distinction between two kinds of people:

- **Internal locus of control** — you believe the things that happen in your life are a product of what *you* do.
- **External locus of control** — you believe the things that happen to you are mostly the outcome of things *outside* your control.

In his own words (paraphrased from the source): people with internal locus of control will work incredibly hard to make sure the things that matter to them happen the best possible way; people with external locus of control, even if they care, will just throw their hands up and let the world take the wheel.

Scale AI indexes heavily on people who have an internal locus of control. The practical interview question he suggests is simply: **"Do you feel like you have control over your future?"**

**His 4 hiring traits in order:**

| # | Trait |
|---|-------|
| 1 | Internal locus of control |
| 2 | Creative problem-solving |
| 3 | Impressive (raises the bar) |
| 4 | Collaborative |
