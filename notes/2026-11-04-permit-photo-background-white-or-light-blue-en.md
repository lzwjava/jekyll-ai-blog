**Question:** For replacing a 往来港澳通行证 (Mainland Travel Permit to Hong Kong & Macao), should the ID photo background be white or light blue (淡蓝色)?

---

**Answer:** **Both are acceptable** — it's not one or the other. The official requirements allow multiple background colors.

According to the official photo requirements for the 港澳通行证, the background can be **white, light gray (浅灰色), or light blue (淡蓝色)**.

When taking the photo yourself, the guidance is to choose a **white or light blue background**.

So in summary:

| Background Color | Allowed? |
|---|---|
| White (白色) | ✅ Yes |
| Light Gray (浅灰色) | ✅ Yes |
| Light Blue (淡蓝色) | ✅ Yes |

**Other key photo requirements** (from the same official source):

- Size: Paper photo 33mm × 48mm; digital photo width 354–480px, height 472–640px, aspect ratio 3:4
- Sit upright, shoulders level, look directly at the camera
- Natural expression, eyes open naturally, hair must not cover the eyes
- Wear dark-colored clothing with a collar; no uniforms
- No tinted glasses; glasses must not block eyes or cause glare
- No head coverings (except for religious/medical/cultural reasons, as long as the face is not obscured)
- Even lighting, no shadows or bright spots on the face


> **Note:** If you're going to a photo studio, just tell them it's for 出入境证件照 — they'll know the standards. White is the most common and safest choice.
