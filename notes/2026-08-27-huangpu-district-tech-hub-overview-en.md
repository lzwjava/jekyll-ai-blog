**Question:** Besides CETV and 朗国科技 (Languo Technology), what other hardware/tech companies are based in Huangpu District, Guangzhou?

**Answer:**

Huangpu District (黄埔区) is indeed a major hardware and tech hub — it contributes nearly 40% of Guangzhou's total industrial output, and there are more than 2,500 high-tech enterprises in Huangpu District and the main park of Guangzhou High-tech Zone, with 53 listed high-tech companies, accounting for more than half of the city.

Here are notable hardware and tech companies based there:

**Display & Semiconductors**
- **LG Display** — LG Display has an 8.5th-generation OLED project in Huangpu valued at over 10 billion yuan.
- **TCL CSOT (T9 Project)** — TCL CSOT's T9 project is another multi-billion-yuan display investment in the district.
- **CanSemi (灿芯半导体)** — CanSemi has a 12-inch chip manufacturing project in Huangpu.
- **LuxVisions Innovation (灵明光子)** — LuxVisions Innovation has its national headquarters project in Huangpu.
- **Yuexin Semiconductor & Huizhi Microelectronics** — both were selected for the 2022 Hurun Global Unicorn List as Huangpu-based companies.

**Autonomous Vehicles & Smart Mobility**
- **WeRide (文远知行)** — headquartered in Huangpu, WeRide is a leading global L4 autonomous driving tech company, and recently partnered with Farizon Auto to deploy 2,000 Robotaxi units globally in 2026.
- **XPeng Motors (小鹏汽车)** — XPeng has a smart EV manufacturing base project in Huangpu valued at over 10 billion yuan.

**Aerospace / eVTOL**
- **EHang (亿航)** — Guangzhou EHang Intelligent Technology is a Nasdaq-listed aerospace company headquartered in Guangzhou, with operations in Huangpu, recently receiving its Operation Certificate (OC) approved by the Civil Aviation Administration of China.

**Electronics & Other Tech**
- **CVTE (视源股份)** — CVTE has its global headquarters base project in Huangpu, valued at over 10 billion yuan. (CVTE makes display control boards and smart education hardware.)
- **HongKe Electronics (虹科电子)** — headquartered at PCI Technology Park, No. 30 Kaitai Avenue, Huangpu District, established since 1995.
- **Ruqi Mobility / Ontime (如祺出行)** — a smart mobility unicorn also on the Hurun list based in Huangpu.

So the district has a very strong cluster spanning **chips, displays, autonomous driving, eVTOL, and smart hardware** — quite a dense ecosystem beyond just CETV and Languo!
