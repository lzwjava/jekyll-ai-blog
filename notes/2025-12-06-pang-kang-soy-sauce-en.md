Question: Who is Pang Kang, the entrepreneur in China?

Answer:

Pang Kang (Chinese: 庞康; born 1956) is a prominent Chinese billionaire businessman and entrepreneur, best known as the founder and longtime chairman of Foshan Haitian Flavouring & Food Co., Ltd., the world's largest producer of soy sauce and a leading manufacturer of condiments in China. The company, based in Foshan, Guangdong province, generates annual revenue exceeding 26.9 billion yuan (approximately $3.7 billion) and offers over 300 products, including oyster sauce, vinegar, chicken stock, and bean curd, which are exported to more than 100 countries.

### Early Life and Education

Born in 1956 in Shunde, Guangdong (some sources cite Foshan or 1955/1958 as the birth year), Pang earned a bachelor's degree in finance from Guangzhou University in 1980. He maintains a low-profile personal life, is married, has no children, and is known for interests in Chinese calligraphy and painting, as well as philanthropic efforts.

### Career and Rise to Prominence

Pang joined the state-owned Foshan Sauce Shop (originally a collective enterprise dating back to the mid-Qing Dynasty in 1955) immediately after graduation. He quickly advanced, becoming vice chairman by 1982. In 1995, he played a pivotal role in privatizing the entity into Foshan Haitian Flavouring & Food Co., investing $60,000 to become a major shareholder. An employee-led buyout from the Foshan government followed in 2007, and the company went public on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in 2014, marking a significant wealth surge for Pang. He stepped down as chairman in September 2024 but remains a key stakeholder.

Under his leadership, Haitian grew from a regional sauce maker into a global powerhouse, emphasizing innovation, quality, and expansion. The company's roots trace back over 400 years, earning it recognition as a "Chinese Time-Honored Brand" by China's Ministry of Commerce.
