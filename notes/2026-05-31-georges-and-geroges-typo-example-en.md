Question: Are the names "Georges" and "Geroges" really easy for people to mistype?

Answer:

Yes, they are very easy to mistype. "Georges" is a common name, but the letters “r” and “o” are adjacent and can be swapped by accident, resulting in "Geroges." Such errors happen often with names that have repeated letters or uncommon letter sequences. Factors that contribute include:

* **Keyboard layout:** Adjacent letters or common finger movements can lead to transposition errors.
* **Visual similarity:** The sequence of vowels and consonants can confuse the brain when typing quickly.
* **Autocorrect or predictive text:** Sometimes autocorrect may “help” but produce the wrong spelling instead.
* **Frequency of exposure:** Less familiar names are more prone to errors because muscle memory hasn’t formed yet.

So, mistyping “Georges” as “Geroges” is a classic and understandable slip.
