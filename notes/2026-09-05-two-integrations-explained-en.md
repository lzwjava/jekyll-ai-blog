**Question:** What is 两个结合 (The Two Integrations / Two Combines)?

**Answer:**

**两个结合** (Liǎng gè jiéhé), translated as the **"Two Integrations"** or **"Two Combines,"** is a CCP political concept that defines how Marxism should be adapted and applied in China. The CCP's theoretical journal *Qiushi* describes it as "integrating the basic principles of Marxism with China's specific reality and with China's excellent traditional culture."

The two integrations specifically are:

**1st Integration** — Marxism + China's concrete reality
The need to adapt Marxism to China's material conditions was a prerogative established by the 3rd Plenary Session of the 11th Central Committee of the CCP back in 1978. This is the longer-standing principle, rooted in the idea that Marxist theory must be applied to China's specific historical and social conditions rather than followed dogmatically.

**2nd Integration** — Marxism + China's traditional culture
At a symposium on cultural heritage development on June 2, 2023, Xi Jinping stressed the importance of "combining Marxist theory with China's outstanding traditional culture." According to Xi, this combination is necessary to realize his vision of a new "modern Chinese civilization."

**Origins & Political Significance**

The term was first coined in July 2021 at the speech commemorating the 100th Anniversary of the Chinese Communist Party, and was later mentioned in the Resolution on the Major Achievements and Historical Experience of the Party over the Past Century adopted in November 2021.

Xi's addition of the second integration is described as "an important original assertion," and taken together, the two are called "the inherent requirement and inevitable logic of the Sinicization of Marxism."

**Why It Matters**

The concept promotes Marxism and emphasizes its integration with Chinese politics and culture, suggesting that Xi is increasingly embracing ideological legitimacy amid growing economic challenges. In essence, 两个结合 serves as the theoretical backbone justifying Xi Jinping's political vision — that the CCP's ideology is not a foreign import but is deeply rooted in and continuous with Chinese civilization itself.

It is closely related to the broader concept of **中国式现代化** discussed previously, as both serve to frame China's development model as culturally authentic and distinct from the West.
