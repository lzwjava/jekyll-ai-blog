Question: What is Muddy Waters Corp, the entity that pointed out Luckin Coffee's financial cheating?

Answer:

"Mutter Water Corp" appears to be a misspelling or mishearing of **Muddy Waters Research** (also associated with Muddy Waters Capital LLC), a well-known activist short-selling research firm founded by Carson Block.

Muddy Waters Research specializes in investigating and publishing reports on companies suspected of financial fraud, accounting irregularities, or fundamental business problems, often taking short positions in those stocks.

In the case of Luckin Coffee (a Chinese coffee chain that went public in 2019 and was positioned as a rival to Starbucks), Muddy Waters played a key role in highlighting potential fraud:

- On January 31, 2020, Muddy Waters tweeted that it had received an anonymous 89-page report alleging serious fraud at Luckin Coffee.
- The report claimed Luckin inflated sales figures (e.g., items per store per day overstated by 69–88% in Q3–Q4 2019), exaggerated advertising expenses, and had a flawed business model.
- Muddy Waters stated the report was credible and announced it was shorting Luckin stock.
- Although Muddy Waters did not author the report themselves (it was later linked to other sources), they publicly endorsed it and brought widespread attention to the allegations.
- Luckin initially denied the claims, but on April 2, 2020, the company admitted to fabricating approximately RMB 2.2 billion (about $310 million) in sales during 2019, involving its COO and other employees.
- This confession validated the core allegations, leading to Luckin's stock plummeting, delisting from NASDAQ, fines, and bankruptcy restructuring.

Muddy Waters' actions effectively "pointed out" and amplified the cheating, contributing to the exposure that forced Luckin's admission. The firm has a history of similar reports on other companies, particularly Chinese firms listed in the US.
