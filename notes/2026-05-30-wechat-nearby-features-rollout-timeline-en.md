Question: When was the WeChat feature for red packets nearby or nearby sharing of files/photos released?

Answer:

The **location-based red packet feature** (“红包附近”) and nearby sharing enhancements have been rolled out gradually over the years, primarily in China:

1. **Red Packets Nearby**

   * Tencent first introduced the **“Red Packets Nearby”** feature around **2017–2018**.
   * It was initially tied to special events (like Chinese New Year) and then became a standard feature in WeChat Wallet under certain regions.
   * Availability depends on app version and geographic restrictions; not all countries have it enabled.

2. **Nearby Sharing of Files/Photos**

   * “People Nearby” and **Nearby Sharing** for files, photos, and mini-programs came around **2018–2019**.
   * WeChat later integrated it more tightly with the **“Me → Files/Photos → Share with Nearby”** workflow in recent updates (2022–2023), improving accessibility.

3. **Ongoing updates**

   * Tencent continuously experiments with these features, sometimes merging location-based promotions (like nearby red packets) with nearby content sharing.
   * The exact UI elements on the **“Me” page** might appear in **2022–2024 updates**, but the underlying features have existed for several years.

So, the **core functionality has been around since 2017–2018**, with refinements and integration with nearby sharing continuing into 2022–2023.
