Question: Can you give me several short, easy-to-remember prompts for automatically extracting key information from webpages?

Answer:

Here are **10 short, high-utility prompts** you can reuse. Each one is designed to be easy to remember and still powerful:

---

### 🧠 1. Core Filter

“Extract only what matters for my goal. Ignore everything else.”

---

### 🎯 2. Action Focus

“Turn this into actions I need to take.”

---

### ⏰ 3. Deadline Focus

“Extract all deadlines, dates, and requirements only.”

---

### 📌 4. Exam Mode (your use case)

“Show only exam-relevant info and registration steps.”

---

### 🧹 5. Noise Removal

“Remove noise, keep only important points.”

---

### 📊 6. Priority Sort

“Rank info: must know / useful / ignore.”

---

### 🪄 7. Summary Mode

“Summarize into key points in bullets only.”

---

### 🔍 8. Title Scanner

“List only important headings and notices.”

---

### ⚡ 9. Decision Mode

“What decisions do I need to make from this?”

---

### 🧭 10. Next Step Mode

“What should I do next based on this?”

---

## 💡 How to use them

You don’t need to think much:

* For exams → use **#4**
* For admin pages → use **#1 + #3**
* For general reading → use **#5 or #7**
* For productivity → use **#9 + #10**
