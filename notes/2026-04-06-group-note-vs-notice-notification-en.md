Question: Will WeChat “group note” (the one at the bottom near group tools) send notifications to all group members, or is it just like a poll?

Answer:
**Short answer:**

* **Group Notice (群公告)** → sends a **strong notification to all members**.
* **Group Note (群笔记 / bottom group tools)** → **does NOT notify everyone**; it behaves more like a **shared document/poll-like collaborative note**.

### Difference

**1. Group Notice (群公告)**

* Sent by admin/owner
* Automatically **@all members**
* Everyone receives a notification
* Usually pinned at top
* Used for announcements

This feature explicitly “notifies all group members at one time” and functions as a forced reminder. ([China Clife][1])

**2. Group Note (群笔记 / 接龙 / 群工具里的 note)**

* Shared editable note / checklist / poll-like entry
* People **open it manually**
* Members can fill in items
* Usually **no push notification to everyone**
* Appears in “Group Tools” area

So your understanding is correct:

* It is **not like group notice**
* It is **closer to a poll / shared list**
* It **does not alert everyone automatically**

### Behavior comparison

| Feature      | Notify everyone | Push notification | Purpose                  |
| ------------ | --------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------ |
| Group Notice | Yes             | Strong            | announcement             |
| @all message | Yes             | Strong            | urgent ping              |
| Group Note   | No              | Usually none      | collect info / checklist |

So if you want everyone to **see immediately**, use:

* group notice
* @all

If you use **group note**, people will only see it when:

* they open group tools
* they scroll chat
* they manually check
