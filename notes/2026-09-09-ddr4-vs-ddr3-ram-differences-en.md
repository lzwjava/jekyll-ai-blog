Question: What are the key differences between DDR4 and DDR3 RAM?

Answer:**Key Takeaways:**

- **Speed & Bandwidth:** DDR4 offers roughly 2× the bandwidth of DDR3, making it significantly better for memory-intensive workloads like video editing, gaming, and virtualization.
- **Power Efficiency:** DDR4 runs at 1.2V vs DDR3's 1.5V — about 20% lower power draw, which matters for laptops and servers.
- **Latency nuance:** DDR3 has lower *absolute* CAS latency numbers (e.g., CL9), but DDR4's *relative* latency (in nanoseconds) is comparable or better at higher speeds.
- **Capacity:** DDR4 supports much larger per-DIMM capacities, which is critical for servers and workstations.
- **Not interchangeable:** DDR3 and DDR4 use different slot notches and voltages — they are physically and electrically incompatible.
- **Current relevance:** DDR4 is now the mainstream standard. DDR5 has begun replacing it on the latest platforms (Intel 12th gen+, AMD Ryzen 7000), so DDR3 is effectively legacy.

**Bottom line:** Unless you're upgrading an older system that only supports DDR3, DDR4 is the clear choice for any modern build.
