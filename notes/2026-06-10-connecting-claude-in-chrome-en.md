To connect Claude in Chrome:

1. **Install the extension** — go to the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com) and search for "Claude" by Anthropic, or find it at [claude.ai/chrome](https://claude.ai/chrome).

2. **Sign in** — click the extension icon in Chrome's toolbar and sign in with your Anthropic account (same as claude.ai).

3. **Keep Chrome open** — the extension needs Chrome running in the foreground. If Chrome is minimized or the extension tab is closed, the connection drops.

4. **Check the extension is active** — the extension icon should be colored/lit, not grayed out. If grayed, click it and re-authenticate.

Once connected, I can take screenshots, click, navigate, and read page content directly in your browser instead of fetching raw HTML.
