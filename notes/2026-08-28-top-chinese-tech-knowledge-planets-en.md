**Question:** What are some well-known 知识星球 communities among Chinese engineers and programmers?

**Answer:**

Here are the most prominent and well-regarded tech/engineering Planets in the Chinese developer community, organized by category:

---

**🔴 Coding Interview & Job Hunting**

**代码随想录 (Carl's LeetCode Planet)**
One of the most established coding interview planets, with over 20,000 members and running for 885+ days as of mid-2023. Priced around 196 RMB/year, it offers two-plus years of accumulated content including interview prep materials, resume templates, and a famous "八股文" (standard tech interview Q&A) collection. Very popular among students preparing for big-tech offers.

**二哥的编程星球 (ErGe's Java Planet)**
Run by the blogger "沉默王二," whose WeChat Official Account achieved over 30 million reads in 2024 and whose open-source Java knowledge repo 《二哥的Java进阶之路》 has 10k+ GitHub stars. Heavy focus on Java advancement and career growth.

**程序员马丁 (Martin the Programmer)**
Run by an Alibaba P7 business architect who started blogging in 2019, winning recognition on CSDN, Juejin, and Alibaba Cloud. He open-sourced the thread-pool framework Hippo4j which gained 6.5k stars and has been adopted by nearly 50 companies in production. The Planet covers system design, source code analysis, and career mentorship.

---

**🔴 Java & Backend Engineering**

**芋道源码 / 鱼皮 (YuDao / YuPi)**
Described as the largest Java tech community on 知识星球, officially recommended by the platform, with 3,000+ days of accumulated technical content. Run by a P8+ tech leader from a top-3 Chinese internet company with 10+ years of experience. Covers full-stack learning paths, source code analysis (Spring, Dubbo, Netty, JDK), interview banks across 20+ tech stacks, and real-world project walk-throughs including SaaS, RBAC, payment systems, ERP, IoT, and AI integration.

---

**🔴 AI / Machine Learning & Algorithms**

**AI算法求职星球 (Amusi's AI Job Planet)**
Focused on algorithm engineers and researchers job-hunting at top companies. The community includes members and mentors from Apple, Google, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Tencent, Alibaba, Baidu, ByteDance, Meituan, and more. Shares a deep learning interview question bank with 900+ Q&As, internal referrals, and a curated LeetCode + AI course guide for students from sophomore to grad school level.

**Raina的AI&测试实战圈**
An AI-for-QA engineering planet covering how to apply the latest AI tools (MCP, Dify, AI IDEs, Openclaw) into software testing workflows end-to-end. Includes 130+ practical notes, an 18-topic interview question bank, a self-built test platform, and direct 1-on-1 Q&A with the host.

---

**🔴 Side Income / Creator Economy for Developers**

**程序员小灰的副业星球 (Xiaohui's Side Income Planet)**
Run by a well-known programmer blogger, this planet focuses on helping engineers build side income streams. It features quarterly member spotlights — active members get promoted to the host's large public account — and is targeting 10,000+ members.

---

**A note of caution**

The community on 牛客网 (China's equivalent of LeetCode forums) has mixed opinions — some experienced engineers argue that 99% of CS knowledge is freely available, and that paid planets mainly serve beginners who haven't yet learned how to find resources themselves. So the value depends heavily on your level and what you're looking for (structured mentorship vs. raw knowledge).
