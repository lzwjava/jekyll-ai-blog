**Question:** Did Zhang Yiming, ByteDance's founder/CEO, demonstrate strong 心态 (mindset) and emotional control throughout his ByteDance journey?

---

**Answer:**

Yes — Zhang Yiming is widely regarded as having an exceptionally grounded mindset and emotional composure. His philosophy is most clearly expressed through the concept of **"普通心" (ordinary mind / 平常心)**, which he repeatedly advocated for himself and his employees.

### 1. The "Ordinary Mind" (平常心) Philosophy

Zhang placed his core belief in the Buddhist-rooted concept of "ordinary mind" — defined as remaining unbiased and not paranoid under all circumstances. In modern psychological terms, it essentially means doing one's best, going with the flow, and staying calm. In his own words: "When hungry, eat; when tired, sleep" — a simple yet profound way of focusing on the present without overthinking or being distracted by anxieties about past or future.

### 2. Staying Calm Under External Pressure

As ByteDance's valuation surpassed that of Coca-Cola and IPO rumors swirled, Zhang told employees in an internal speech to keep an "ordinary mind," avoid thinking of the company as a "large company," and make decisions without being distracted by specific growth goals. He said: "This year, I hope that the company's mindset will slow down to a certain extent."

During crises, Zhang's consistent advice was to remain calm, make rational decisions, and not panic, as this often leads to the best possible outcomes.

### 3. Self-Awareness Over Ego

One of the most striking demonstrations of his emotional maturity was his voluntary resignation. In an unusually candid open memo, Zhang admitted: "The truth is, I lack some of the skills that make an ideal manager" — preferring reading and thinking deeply over managing people day-to-day. Rather than clinging to power, he handed the CEO role to his college roommate Liang Rubo.

### 4. Healthy Attitude Toward Competition

Zhang urged his team to treat competitors with an ordinary mind — seeing competition as the norm rather than a threat. He cautioned against competing just for competition's sake, and emphasized keeping an open mind when drawing conclusions by always considering other possibilities.

### 5. Treating Success & Failure Equally

Zhang emphasized not misattributing success or failure — specifically warning against treating luck as ability or external causes as internal ones. He hoped his team could maintain an ordinary mind even when ahead of rivals.

### 6. Long-Term Perspective Over Short-Term Emotion

Even in the high-pressure AI competition of recent years, when ByteDance faced significant pressure in large language models, Zhang made the deliberate call: "To achieve long-term goals, we should be willing to sacrifice some short-term interests" — choosing patience over reactive decision-making.

---

**In summary:** Zhang Yiming's 心态 is genuinely remarkable. He built one of the world's largest tech companies while consistently preaching groundedness, self-acceptance, and calm. His willingness to publicly admit personal limitations and step down gracefully — rather than holding on to status — is perhaps the strongest proof of his emotional control.
