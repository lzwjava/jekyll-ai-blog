This paper addresses how to improve LLM inference quality on resource-constrained mobile devices by leveraging historical interactions from the cloud.

**KDD 2025**

This paper proposes a framework where each mobile device maintains a user-specific distribution to dynamically generate user embeddings, allowing cloud-based language models to deliver personalized services without ever receiving explicit user identifiers — preserving privacy while improving inference accuracy.
