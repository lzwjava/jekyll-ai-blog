**Question:** How do people do marketing nowadays — TikTok, WeChat Video Channel, etc.?

---

**Answer:**

Modern marketing in 2026 has fundamentally shifted from broadcasting to **community, video, and commerce all fused together**. Here's a breakdown by key area:

---

## 🎬 1. Short-Form Video is the Core

Short, vertical video on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts now drives the majority of engagement across social platforms. It's no longer optional — it's the primary channel.

TikTok leads the way in reach with 28,482.90 interactions per post on average, making it a priority for social media marketers.

On social search, brands are now optimizing captions with searchable keywords in plain language — social media SEO is its own discipline. People search TikTok and Instagram before they Google things.

---

## 🛒 2. Social Commerce — Buy Without Leaving the App

U.S. social commerce sales surpassed $100 billion for the first time in 2026, and U.S. livestream e-commerce sales grew nearly 50% to $14.64 billion — with livestream conversion rates reaching up to 30%.

Livestream commerce (live shopping) is the love child of traditional infomercials and modern influencer marketing. Viewers tune in and interact in real time with a trusted influencer (KOL) who showcases products.

---

## 📱 3. WeChat Video Channel (视频号) — China's Biggest Story

WeChat Channels (视频号), which launched in 2020, is now the platform's biggest growth story. Channels e-commerce GMV hit RMB 433 billion in 2025 (up 74.6% year-on-year), and Tencent's own 2026 guidance points toward it crossing the trillion-yuan mark this year.

Channels has effectively become the content hub of the whole WeChat app — Moments, search, and official accounts all point traffic into it. It plays a different game than Douyin: it leans on who your audience already knows, not just what an algorithm predicts they will like.

**The full WeChat loop:** A mini program, an official account, and WeChat Pay checkout now function as one closed loop — a user never leaves the app.

**Private domain (私域) is king:** The cost of buying attention on public platforms keeps climbing, which is why WeChat's owned-audience model ("private domain" / 私域) has become the center of gravity. WeChat's own figures put enterprise-grade WeCom business accounts past 18 million in 2026, with a combined private domain reach north of 1.5 billion people.

---

## 🤝 4. Influencer / KOL Marketing — Micro > Mega

Influencer marketing continues to surge into 2026, with the industry now valued at over $24 billion globally.

Traditional influencer strategies have shifted to focus on deeper audience trust, niche relevance, and measurable business outcomes. Startups are no longer limiting KOL campaigns to single platforms.

With social networks saturated by ads and brand posts, consumers turn to trusted micro-creators to cut through the noise — a well-executed influencer marketing campaign is often make-or-break for social commerce success.

---

## 🤖 5. AI is Now the Foundation

AI is no longer just a competitive edge — it's the foundation of modern digital marketing in 2026. AI is used for content creation, influencer selection, ad targeting, personalization, and social listening at scale.

---
