**Question: Why does Genshin Impact have such a large app size, even for basic resources like 22 GB?**

**Answer:**

Genshin Impact's large file size stems from its ambitious scope as a free-to-play open-world action RPG with high-fidelity graphics, extensive content, and full localization across platforms. As of late 2025 (Version 5.x+), the game requires significant storage due to ongoing updates adding new regions, characters, events, and assets. Here's a breakdown:

The "basic resources" option on Android (~21.8-22 GB) downloads core gameplay files (maps, models, UI) with minimal voice packs, while "full resources" adds extras like high-quality audio. Even basic is large because the game's world of Teyvat spans multiple massive regions with detailed environments.

### Key Reasons for the Size
1. **Audio Files (Largest Contributor: 40-50% of Total)**
   - Full voice acting for **every line of dialogue** in 10+ languages (English, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, etc.). Each pack is 5-10 GB.
   - Thousands of hours of voiced story quests, events, NPC chatter, and combat lines.
   - High-bitrate music tracks and sound effects for immersive audio.
   - **Tip**: Delete unused languages in settings to save 20-40 GB on PC/mobile.

2. **Visual Assets and Pre-Rendered Videos (~25-35%)**
   - High-resolution textures, 3D models, and animations for characters, weapons, enemies, and environments (optimized lower on mobile).
   - **Pre-rendered cutscenes**: ~25 GB of videos, as mobile devices can't render cinematic-quality scenes in real-time without performance issues.

3. **Open-World Content (~20-30%)**
   - Massive map with 7+ regions (Mondstadt to Natlan+), each adding GBs of unique assets (buildings, foliage, puzzles).
   - 100+ playable characters, artifacts, bosses, and items with custom models/animations.

4. **Platform Differences and Updates**
   - PC/PS5 use uncompressed 4K textures; mobile compresses aggressively.
   - Live-service model: Patches (5-15 GB each) accumulate without full reinstalls, bloating size over 5 years.
   - Temporary files during updates can double space needs (e.g., 150 GB temp on PC).
