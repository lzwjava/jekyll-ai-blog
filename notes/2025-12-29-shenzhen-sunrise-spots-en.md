Dapeng New District (Dapeng Peninsula) in Shenzhen is an excellent location for watching sunrise over the sea. It has a long coastline with clear waters, and many spots face east, offering spectacular views of the sun rising slowly from the sea horizon. Here are some of the most popular recommended spots (based on tourist reviews and travel guides):

1. **Yangmeikeng Lüzui Villa / Lüzui Sunrise Trail**
   Located at the easternmost tip of the Dapeng Peninsula, it's known as the place that "welcomes the first ray of sunlight in Shenzhen." This is the classic spot for sunrise viewing in Shenzhen, with an open, unobstructed view. It's often praised as the top choice for sea sunrise. You can walk or cycle along the Lüzui Sunrise Trail (about 10 km long, from Xindong Road to Lüzui Villa) while enjoying the views of Daya Bay. It's recommended to head to Lüzui Beach or the cliff viewing platform at the villa early in the morning.

2. **Xichong Beach**
   One of the most beautiful beaches in Shenzhen, stretching 4.5 km with fine sand and clear water. Many guides call it "the best sunrise viewing spot in all of Shenzhen," especially at beaches 1-4, the hilltop viewing platform, or toward Lover's Island. Walking eastward along the coast provides an even better view. It's great for camping or getting up early to wait on the beach.

3. **Qiniang Mountain (Dayanding Peak or Viewing Platform)**
   The highest point on the Dapeng Peninsula, surrounded by sea on three sides. Watching the sunrise over the sea from the summit is breathtaking, with panoramic views of the entire Dapeng Bay. Ideal for hiking enthusiasts—climb early or camp overnight for a chance to see sunrise with sea of clouds, which is even more dreamy.

4. **Jiaochangwei Beach**
   A trendy area with many guesthouses and B&Bs. The beach is just a few minutes' walk away. It's suitable for sunrise viewing in autumn and winter (in other seasons, it might be blocked by mountains). If staying in a guesthouse, you can enjoy sea views from your window and head straight to the beach the next morning.

**Tips**:
- Sunrise time: Around 6:30-7:00 in winter, 5:30-6:00 in summer (check a weather app for exact times).
- Transportation: If driving, book a Dapeng entry permit in advance via the "Shenzhen Traffic Police" WeChat public account. By bus, take E11 or similar to Dapeng and transfer locally.
- Best season: Autumn and winter for clearer skies and better visibility. Get up early, use mosquito repellent, and bring sunscreen and water.

These spots all offer stunning sunrises. Choose based on your accommodation and fitness level—Yangmeikeng Lüzui or Xichong are the most popular!
