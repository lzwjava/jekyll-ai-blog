Question: How much water and cooking time do I need to cook pork with Chinese yam on a traditional Chinese stove?

Answer:

To cook a traditional Chinese Yam and Pork Soup (山药排骨汤) using a typical traditional Chinese stove (灶头), you will generally need **8 cups of water** and you will cook it **2 times** (once for blanching the meat, and once for the actual stewing).

Here is the exact breakdown for your preparation:

### Water Amount: Choose 8 Cups

* **Why 8 cups?** A traditional Chinese brick or wood-fired stove (灶头) produces a wide, intense flame that causes water to evaporate much faster than modern electric stoves. **4 cups of water is not enough**; it will boil down too quickly, leaving you with very little broth and potentially burning the ingredients.
* **Rule of thumb:** 8 cups of water (about 2 liters) is ideal for roughly 1 pound (500g) of pork and 1 stick of Chinese yam. The liquid should fully submerge all your ingredients by at least 1 to 2 inches.

---

### Cooking Times: 2 Separate Sessions

You need to heat or boil the pot **2 separate times** to get the cleanest, most flavorful soup.

* **Time 1: The Blanching (飞水 / 焯水) — 5 Minutes**
Place your raw pork in a pot with cold water (just enough to cover the meat). Bring it to a boil on the stove for about 3 to 5 minutes. This releases the bloody impurities and foam. Dump out this water entirely and rinse the pork clean.
* **Time 2: The Main Stewing — 1 to 1.5 Hours total**
Clean the pot, add your 8 cups of fresh water, the blanched pork, ginger, and any other dry ingredients (like red dates or goji berries).
* Bring it to a rolling boil over high heat.
* Once boiling, reduce the heat to a steady simmer (manage your firewood/gas on the 灶头) for **1 hour**.
* Add the fresh sliced Chinese yam (山药) during the last **20 to 30 minutes** of cooking so it becomes tender but does not completely dissolve into mush. Season with salt right at the end.
