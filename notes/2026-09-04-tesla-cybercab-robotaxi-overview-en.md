**Question:** What is the Tesla Cybercab?

**Answer:**

The Tesla Cybercab is Tesla's purpose-built autonomous robotaxi. Here's a breakdown of what it is:

**What it is:**
A small, two-seater vehicle with no steering wheel or pedals that uses cameras and AI to navigate the world. It relies entirely on Tesla's Full Self-Driving software — there is no way for a human inside to take over if the system behaves unexpectedly.

**Key specs:**
It's a 2-door liftback coupe with scissor doors, a 47.6 kWh battery, roughly 293 miles of range, and 219 hp. It's assembled at Gigafactory Texas in Austin.

**Price & production:**
Elon Musk said the Cybercab would be priced below $30,000. Production began in February 2026, with a target of 2 million units per year at full factory capacity.

**Charging:**
Unlike other EVs, it doesn't have a plug-in charger — it uses inductive (wireless) charging instead.

**Current rollout (as of Sept 2026):**
Tesla launched the Cybercab commercially in Austin, Texas on September 3, 2026. The company had already been running a driverless robotaxi pilot using modified Model Y SUVs in Austin before folding the Cybercab into that service. Tesla's unsupervised fleet grew 200% since the end of Q2 2026, from 57 vehicles on June 30 to 156 on August 31.
