Question: How can I understand the relationship between Xiaomi models and Hermes for a two-week free trial period?

Answer:

Based on the information available, here's how the Xiaomi MiMo models and Hermes Agent integration works:

## Partnership Overview

Nous Research partnered with Xiaomi to integrate the MiMo V2 Pro inference model into its Hermes Agent framework, allowing users to access AI capabilities through the Nous Portal for a limited two-week free trial.

## How It Works

**Hermes Agent Integration:**

- Users can connect their Hermes agent to Xiaomi's MiMo V2 Pro model through the Nous Research partnership with free access for two weeks
- Xiaomi's MiMo V2 Pro model is available to Hermes Agent via the Nous Portal, completely free to use for the next 2 weeks

**Setup Process:**

1. Create a free billing account on the Nous Research portal
2. Connect Hermes Agent to MiMo V2 Pro on your VPS
3. The Nous Portal now supports the free-tier Xiaomi MiMo v2 Pro model for auxiliary tasks such as compression, vision, and summarization

## OpenRouter Alternative

In addition to the Hermes integration:

- MiMo-V2-Flash is available for free on OpenRouter with 309B total parameters and 15B active parameters
- MiMo-V2-Pro costs $1 per million input tokens and $3 per million output tokens on OpenRouter

## Additional Free Trial Options

Xiaomi offered one-week free trials through multiple partners:

- MiMo-V2-Pro partnered with five major agent development frameworks including OpenClaw, OpenCode, KiloCode, Blackbox, and Cline to offer one week of free API access for developers worldwide
- MiMo-V2-Pro and MiMo-V2-Omni were completely free to use in Kilo Code for a full week

The Hermes integration provides a two-week trial period, which is longer than the one-week trials offered through other agent frameworks. After the trial period ends, users can switch to other free models on the Nous Portal or configure alternative paid providers.
