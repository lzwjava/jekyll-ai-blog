**Question:**
How can you apply for a temporary ID card using the “Yue Sheng Shi (粤省事)” digital platform?

---

**Answer:**

You can apply for a **temporary Chinese ID card (临时居民身份证)** directly through the **WeChat mini-program “粤省事”**. The process is fully digital for the application, and the card can either be picked up or mailed to you.

### 1. Open the Yue Sheng Shi mini-program

1. Open **WeChat**.
2. Search for **“粤省事”** and enter the mini-program.
3. Log in with your real-name account.

### 2. Navigate to the ID service

Follow this path inside the mini-program:

```
首页 / 办事
→ 户政（治安）
→ 居民身份证
→ 临时身份证申领
```

Then tap **“开始办理” (Start Application)**. ([shouyou.3dmgame.com][1])

### 3. Fill in the application

You will need to:

* Confirm your **personal information**
* Fill in **contact details**
* Choose how to receive the card

After verifying the information, submit the application.

### 4. Choose how to receive the temporary ID

Two options are available:

* **现场领取 (Pick up in person)** at the police service center
* **邮政速递 (Postal delivery)** to your address ([bwc.gzist.edu.cn][2])

### 5. Fees and processing time

* **Fee:** about **10 RMB**
* **Processing time:** usually **within 3 working days** after acceptance ([shouyou.3dmgame.com][1])

### Important requirement

You **must already have an ID card application in progress** (for example, you are replacing or renewing your ID).
If there is **no existing ID application record**, the system will not allow a temporary ID request. ([bwc.gzist.edu.cn][2])

---

### Why it worked in your exam scenario

Because Guangdong integrated many **public security services into “粤省事”**, you can:

* apply for temporary ID
* show digital identity info
* track application progress

This allows situations like yours:
**exam → lost ID → open phone → apply temporary ID / print admission ticket → continue exam.**

It’s a typical example of **China’s “政务数字化” (digital government services)**.
