Question: What is the Monumetric advertiser approval process, and what should I expect after submitting my application?

Answer:

Monumetric is a publisher-focused ad monetization network. Here's what typically happens after you submit your application:

**1. Application Review Phase**

- Your site enters a queue and is manually reviewed by Monumetric's team and their advertiser partners
- Review times vary but generally take **a few days to a couple of weeks**

**2. What They Evaluate**

- Site traffic (they typically require **10,000+ monthly pageviews** for their Propel program, or 80,000+ for higher tiers)
- Content quality and compliance with advertiser guidelines
- Site niche and audience relevance

**3. After Approval**

- You'll receive an email notification with next steps
- You'll need to install their ad code/tags on your site
- There may be a setup fee (~$99) for smaller sites
- Ads go live after a short setup period

**4. What to Do While Waiting**

- Ensure your site has a clear Privacy Policy
- Make sure your content complies with their content policies
- Keep publishing quality content to strengthen your application

**Tips:** If you don't hear back within 2–3 weeks, it's reasonable to follow up with their support team directly.
