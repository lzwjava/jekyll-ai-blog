## Question: Intro to Knight Frank Hong Kong

## Answer

Knight Frank is a **global real estate consultancy firm**, not a property developer. It provides professional property services to investors, companies, landlords, tenants, developers, and governments. The company was founded in London in **1896** and has grown into one of the world's major independent property advisory firms. ([Global][1])

Its main business areas include:

* **Commercial real estate**

  * Office leasing
  * Industrial/logistics property
  * Capital markets (property investment transactions)
  * Tenant representation
  * Asset management

* **Residential property**

  * Luxury / prime residential sales
  * International property
  * Private client advisory

* **Professional services**

  * Property valuation
  * Market research
  * Development consultancy
  * ESG advisory
  * Property management ([Knight Frank][2])

### Hong Kong business

Knight Frank Hong Kong was established in **1972** and is one of its oldest Asian operations. It expanded across Greater China with offices in:

* Hong Kong
* Beijing
* Shanghai
* Guangzhou
* Shenzhen
* Taipei
* Macau

It has more than 50 years of local market experience in Hong Kong and Greater China. ([Knight Frank][3])

### Company scale

Globally, Knight Frank operates:

* 740+ offices
* 50+ territories
* 27,000+ employees/professionals

according to its global profile. ([Knight Frank OSS][4])

### Company reputation

In Hong Kong, Knight Frank is considered a **top-tier international property consultancy**, competing with firms such as:

* CBRE Group
* JLL
* Savills
* Cushman & Wakefield

It has a strong reputation among:

* multinational companies
* institutional investors
* family offices
* property developers
* high-net-worth clients

because Hong Kong is one of the world's biggest real estate markets. ([Knight Frank][5])
