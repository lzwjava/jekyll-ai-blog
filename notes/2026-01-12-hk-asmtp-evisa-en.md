I'll search for the most current information about obtaining an electronic visa under the ASMTP scheme in Hong Kong.Based on the information from Hong Kong's Immigration Department, here's how to get your electronic visa (e-Visa) after ASMTP approval:

## Steps to Get Your e-Visa:

**1. Application Approval**
After your ASMTP application is approved (which typically takes 4 weeks), you'll receive a notification of the application result via email, fax, or post.

**2. Pay the Visa Issuance Fee**
The notification will include an online payment link where you need to pay the visa issuance fee. The fee varies based on your length of stay (either HK$600 or HK$1,300).

**3. Download Your e-Visa**
After payment, you can instantly download or print the e-Visa yourself through the GovHK website or the Immigration Department Mobile Application.

**4. Apply for Exit-Entry Permit (Important!)**
As a Chinese citizen from the Mainland, you must also apply for an Exit-entry Permit for Travelling to and from Hong Kong and Macao (EEP) and a relevant exit endorsement from the relevant Mainland authorities.

**5. When Entering Hong Kong**
Upon arrival at immigration clearance, present your EEP with the exit endorsement along with the e-Visa saved on your mobile device or printed on A4 white paper, and scan the encrypted QR code on the e-Visa at the immigration counter.

**Note:** Since December 2021, Hong Kong no longer issues physical sticker-type visas - everything is done electronically through the e-Visa system.

If you need technical assistance with downloading your e-Visa, you can call the support hotline at +852 3128 8668 (available 7am-11pm Hong Kong time daily).
