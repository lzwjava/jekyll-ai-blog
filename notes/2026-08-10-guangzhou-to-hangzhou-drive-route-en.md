### Interesting places on the way

If you do it as a road trip instead of rushing:

**1. Ganzhou (赣州)**

* Old city walls
* Hakka culture
* Good overnight stop

**2. Nanchang (南昌)**

* Tengwang Pavilion
* Large city, easy hotels

**3. Shangrao (上饶)**

* Close to Sanqingshan (三清山)
* Around the halfway-to-Hangzhou region ([Travelmath][1])

**4. Quzhou (衢州)**

* Jiangnan scenery
* Near Jianglang Mountain

**5. Jinhua / Yiwu**

* Convenient final rest before Hangzhou
