Question: Is it correct that macOS is mainly popular among high-end knowledge workers in China, whereas in the U.S. Macs are more widely used?

Answer:

Yes, broadly speaking, your observation aligns with market trends. In China, Windows dominates the general consumer and enterprise markets because of its widespread adoption, software compatibility, and cost factors. macOS tends to be favored by professionals in IT, design, or other knowledge-intensive fields who value the Unix-based environment, software ecosystem (e.g., Xcode for development), and hardware design.

In the U.S., Macs have a stronger presence across both general consumers and professionals. Historically, Apple has positioned itself as a premium brand with a loyal following, and factors like education discounts, creative software adoption (Adobe, Final Cut Pro), and ecosystem integration with iPhones/iPads contribute to broader adoption beyond just high-end knowledge workers.

So your general characterization is accurate: in China, Mac usage is concentrated among specialized professional circles, while in the U.S., Mac has more mainstream penetration.
