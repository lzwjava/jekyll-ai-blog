**Question:** What does "冯谖市义" (Féng Xuān shì yì) mean in the classical text *冯谖客孟尝君* (Féng Xuān kè Mèng Cháng Jūn)?

---

**Answer:**

"冯谖市义" literally means **"Feng Xuan buys righteousness/benevolence"** (市 = to buy/purchase; 义 = righteousness, moral goodness, loyalty of the people).

Here is the story behind the phrase:

Lord Menchangjun had retainers in Qi, and one of them, **Feng Xuan** (冯谖/冯驩), was particularly unusual. One day, Mengchangjun posted a notice seeking someone to go to his fief of **Xue** to collect debts. Before departing, Feng Xuan asked his lord, "What shall I buy upon return?" Mengchangjun replied, "Buy whatever our household lacks." So Feng Xuan went to Xue, verified all the loan contracts, and then — **forging Mengchangjun's order** — burned all the debt contracts. The people rejoiced and cheered "Long live!" Feng Xuan hurried back and reported: "I saw that your household has plenty of food, clothing, horses, dogs, and beautiful maids — so I bought **'righteousness'** (义) for you."

He explained that Mengchangjun had been charging high interest on the people, causing them suffering, and so by forging the command and burning the debt contracts, he had **won the hearts of the people** (民心) for his lord — this is what it means to "buy righteousness." He said what Mengchangjun's household truly lacked was **ren yi** (仁义) — benevolence and moral righteousness toward the people.

So **"市义"** is an act of political wisdom: instead of collecting money, Feng Xuan "purchased" **goodwill and moral legitimacy** for Mengchangjun by canceling the people's debts. This laid the popular foundation that later helped Mengchangjun regain his position as Prime Minister.

The text *冯谖客孟尝君* is selected from *Zhanguo Ce · Qi Ce* (战国策·齐策), and it records the various political and diplomatic activities Feng Xuan undertook to consolidate Mengchangjun's power, including **burning the debt contracts to buy righteousness** (焚券市义), planning to restore his prime ministership, and building an ancestral temple at Xue — all demonstrating Feng Xuan's political insight and extraordinary talent for using contradictions to resolve contradictions.
