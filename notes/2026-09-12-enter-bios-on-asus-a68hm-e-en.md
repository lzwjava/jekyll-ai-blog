**Question:** How do I enter the BIOS on the ASUS A68HM-E motherboard?

**Answer:**

There are two main ways to enter the BIOS on the ASUS A68HM-E:

**Method 1 — During Boot (Recommended)**

1. Power on or restart your PC.
2. As soon as the ASUS logo appears on screen, press the **Del** key to enter the BIOS setup.
3. Keep tapping it repeatedly until the BIOS screen appears. If you miss the window, just restart and try again.

> Also useful: press **F8** to open the **Boot Menu** directly (e.g., to select a USB drive for OS installation).

**Method 2 — From Windows (via Advanced Startup)**

1. In Windows, hold **Shift** and click **Restart**.
2. Go to **Troubleshoot → Advanced Options → UEFI Firmware Settings**.
3. Click **Restart** — your device will enter the BIOS configuration after the restart.

**Inside the BIOS:**

By default, the EZ Mode screen appears when you enter the BIOS. It provides an overview of basic system information and lets you select display language, system performance mode, and boot device priority. Press **F7** to switch to **Advanced Mode** for more detailed settings.
