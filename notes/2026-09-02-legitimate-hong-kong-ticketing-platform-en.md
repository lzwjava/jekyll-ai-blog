Question: **What is `hkt.hkticketing.com`?**

Answer: **HK Ticketing is a legitimate Hong Kong ticketing platform**, used for concerts, sports, theatre, family entertainment, and other events. The domain `hkt.hkticketing.com` is its current online ticket-booking system.

### What company is behind it?

HK Ticketing is associated with **HKT**, the large Hong Kong telecommunications/technology group. HKT describes itself as a technology, media and telecommunications company and is part of the Pacific Century Group ecosystem. ([HKT][1])

So the relationship is roughly:

```text
HKT / PCCW
    │
    └── HK Ticketing
          │
          ├── Concert tickets
          ├── K-pop / Western pop
          ├── Theatre
          ├── Sports
          └── Other entertainment
```

### Is it a real ticket seller?

**Yes.** It is not merely a ticket-information website.

For example, official event organizers explicitly direct customers to `hkt.hkticketing.com` for ticket sales. Kai Tak Sports Park lists HK Ticketing as the online booking channel for events, including public sales. ([Kai Tak Sports Park][2])

The Hong Kong Tourism Board also lists **Hong Kong Ticketing Limited** as an e-ticket agent and links directly to `hkt.hkticketing.com` for events. ([PartnerNet][3])

### What can you do there?

The site currently lists things such as:

* 🎤 K-pop concerts
* 🎵 Hong Kong / Chinese pop concerts
* 🎸 Western artists
* 🎭 Theatre and musicals
* 👨‍👩‍👧 Family events
* 🏟️ Sports/venue events
* 🎫 Credit-card priority sales and presales

For example, the current site lists **MAMAMOO**, **Post Malone**, **NCT WISH**, **EUNHYUK**, etc. ([HK Ticketing][4])

### One important thing for your case

Since you were looking at **concert presales**, HK Ticketing is particularly relevant because it supports **credit-card priority booking**.

For example, Hang Seng's official 2026 promotion explicitly says eligible cardholders should use the **HK Ticketing website** for priority booking. ([Hang Seng Bank][5])

So if you see something like:

```text
HSBC Mastercard Presale
        ↓
HK Ticketing
        ↓
Enter first 6 digits of card BIN
        ↓
Priority booking
```

that is a normal model for Hong Kong concert presales.

**But:** the fact that HK Ticketing itself is legitimate does **not automatically mean every event URL, presale, or third-party link you receive is legitimate**. For a particular concert, I would verify the event organizer + HK Ticketing event page + bank/card promotion together.
