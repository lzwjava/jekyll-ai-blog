Question: What does this declaration section of the Hong Kong QMAS application mean?

Answer: This is the **declaration/consent section** of your Hong Kong Quality Migrant Admission Scheme (QMAS) application. By submitting it, you are legally confirming that the information you provided is truthful and giving the Hong Kong Immigration Department permission to verify it.

The important parts for you are:

1. **Permission to investigate**

   * You agree that Immigration Department can make whatever enquiries are necessary to process your QMAS application.

2. **Permission to share your information**

   * You allow Immigration Department to disclose your application information to relevant organisations/authorities, including organisations **outside Hong Kong**, when necessary for processing.

3. **Permission for organisations to provide records**

   * You authorise government and private organisations to give Immigration Department records/information they need.
   * This could include information relevant to employment, education, finances, immigration status, etc.

4. **Verification with other government departments**

   * Immigration can use/share your information with departments such as the **Inland Revenue Department**, Labour and Welfare Bureau, MPF authorities, etc., to verify your information.

5. **Immigration Director has final discretion**

   * Even if you appear to satisfy the published QMAS criteria, the Director of Immigration has discretion to decide whether you meet the assessment criteria and whether to approve or reject the application.
   * They don't necessarily have to give you a reason for refusal.

6. **Don't provide false information**

   * If you knowingly give false information or make a statement you know isn't true:

     * you can be prosecuted;
     * your application can be refused;
     * an already-issued visa/entry permit can become invalid;
     * your dependants' permits can also potentially become invalid;
     * you can ultimately be removed from Hong Kong.

7. **Net worth declaration**

   * You are formally declaring that your **personal net worth is at least the amount you stated in Part 2** of the application.
   * This is an important declaration, so your assets/liabilities figures should be defensible if Immigration asks for evidence.

8. **Mainland Chinese resident requirement — particularly important**

   * This paragraph applies to **Chinese residents of Mainland China**, including someone temporarily staying in Hong Kong/Macao.
   * If your QMAS application is approved, you agree to obtain the **Exit-Entry Permit for Travelling to and from Hong Kong and Macao (EEP / 港澳通行证)** and the appropriate exit endorsement from Mainland authorities for travelling to Hong Kong to take up residence.
   * You must present:

     * your EEP with the appropriate endorsement, **and**
     * the Hong Kong entry permit
       when entering Hong Kong under QMAS.
   * The declaration explicitly says that if you try to enter Hong Kong under QMAS using another travel document, you can be **refused entry**.

9. **Final declaration**

   * You are declaring:

   > Everything in my application is correct, complete, and true to the best of my knowledge and belief.

### What the blank numbers mean

The numbering looks strange:

> 1. ...
> 2.
> 3. ...
> 4.
> 5. ...

Those empty numbers are almost certainly **checkbox/form-layout artifacts** from the electronic application form. They aren't additional declarations that you're missing.

### For your application

The two things I'd pay particular attention to before clicking **Submit** are:

**① Net worth**

Make sure the number you entered in Part 2 is consistent with the supporting evidence you could provide if requested.

**② Mainland Chinese resident clause**

If you're applying as a Mainland Chinese resident, don't interpret QMAS approval as meaning you can simply fly to Hong Kong on a passport and use the QMAS approval. The declaration specifically commits you to using the required **EEP + relevant endorsement + Hong Kong entry permit** for taking up residence.

And your **Date: 02-09-2026** simply means you are making this declaration on **2 September 2026**.

In short: **this is a serious legal declaration, but nothing in it looks unusual for a QMAS application. If all the information you've submitted is accurate and you meet the relevant document/entry requirements, it's essentially the consent + truthfulness confirmation you need to submit the application.**
