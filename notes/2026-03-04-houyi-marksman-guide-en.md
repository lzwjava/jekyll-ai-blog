Question: How to play Hou Yi in King of Glory (Honor of Kings)

Answer:

Hou Yi (also known as Hoi or Hui in some guides) is a beginner-friendly marksman (ADC) in *Honor of Kings* (international version of *King of Glory* / *王者荣耀*), excelling in the Farm Lane (gold lane). He deals high sustained physical damage through multi-arrow basic attacks, provides team-wide crowd control with his long-range ultimate, and thrives in team fights with proper positioning. His kit is simple but requires kiting, stack management, and ult prediction to master.

### Abilities

- **Passive: Chastising Shot**
  Basic attacks grant attack speed and 1 stack (up to 3). At 3 stacks, enhanced attacks fire **3 arrows** (main target full damage + 2 splash at 50-60% damage each). Prioritize stacking via AA (auto-attacks) for massive poke and wave clear.

- **Skill 1: Arrow Volley** (Main DPS skill; max first)
  Enhances basic attacks for 5s: increased damage, hits 2 additional nearby enemies (50% damage). Combos perfectly with passive for multi-target shredding.

- **Skill 2: Afterglow** (Max second)
  Drops an arrow in an area: damages, slows enemies, reveals vision. Use for poke, slows, bush checks, or zoning.

- **Ultimate: Burning Sun Arrow** (Global range)
  Fires a flaming arrow across the map. Stuns the first hit (0.75-3.5s based on travel distance), explodes for AoE damage + 90% slow (1s). Predict paths for engages, picks, or escapes.

**Skill Priority**: Skill 1 > Skill 2 > Ult.

### Core Combos

Hou Yi's combos revolve around Ult setup + enhanced AA spam. Always AA between skills to stack passive.

1. **Standard Engage/Chase**: Ult (stun) > Skill 2 (slow) > Skill 1 (enhance) > AA spam.
2. **Ambush/Bush**: Skill 1 > Ult > Skill 2 > AA.
3. **Poke/Wave Clear**: Skill 2 > Skill 1 > AA (stack passive for 3-arrow clears).
4. **Anti-Dive**: Ult yourself (self-stun cancels assassin jumps) > Skill 1 > kite with AA.

### Recommended Build (Attack Speed / Crit / Sustain Focus)

Focus on attack speed (to stack passive fast), crit (for burst), and lifesteal (survivability). Adapt vs. tanks (add pen) or burst (add defense).

**Arcana (Talents)**: 10x Hunt (AS/Move Speed) + 10x Eagle Eye (AD/pen) + 10x Red Moon / Calamity (crit/blast).

**Battle Spell**: Flash (escape/engage) or Frenzy (more AA speed).

### Gameplay Tips by Phase

- **Early Game (Laning)**: Play safe, farm minions to stack passive. Use Skill 2 for poke/slows. Ult for ganks or turret dives. Push waves but freeze if ganked. Rotate for buffs post-level 4.
- **Mid Game**: Join objectives (towers, Tyrant). Ult to stun grouped enemies or save allies. Group with tank/support for protection.
- **Late Game**: Position at max range, AA from backline. Ult predicts dives/escapes. You're a hyper-carry—farm > fights early, carry late.
- **General Tips**:
  - **Kite Constantly**: Strafe while AA to avoid skillshots/assassins.
  - **Ult Mastery**: Use map camera for long-distance snipes. Longer flight = longer stun.
  - **Counters**: Mobile assassins (e.g., Li Bai). Build anti-burst (Succubus Cloak). Avoid overextending.
  - **Synergies**: Tanks/roamers like Zhang Fei, Cai Yan (heals + stuns amp your DPS).
  - Win Rate ~51-52% in high ranks; easy to pick up, hard to master positioning.

Practice in normals: Focus on passive stacks (aim for 100% uptime) and ult accuracy for quick rank-ups.
