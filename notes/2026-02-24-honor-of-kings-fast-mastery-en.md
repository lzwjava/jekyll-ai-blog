*Honor of Kings* is a fast-paced 5v5 multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game where two teams compete to destroy the enemy's base crystal . Matches typically last around 15 minutes, making it a perfect game for on-the-go play . This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to get started on your path to victory.

## 🏛️ Understanding the Basics

Before diving into the heat of battle, it's essential to understand the core elements of the game.

### 🎯 Victory Condition

The goal is simple: work with your team to push through the enemy's defenses, destroy their towers, and ultimately destroy the **Crystal** located in the heart of their base .

### 🗺️ The Map: Hero's Gorge

The primary battlefield, Hero's Gorge, is divided into three main paths, or "lanes," with a jungle in between .

- **Clash Lane (Top/Blue Side):** Grants more experience points (EXP) from minions. It's typically home to durable **Fighters** or **Tanks** .
- **Mid Lane (Center):** The shortest and most direct route. It's the natural home of **Mages**, who can quickly clear waves and rotate to help other lanes .
- **Farm Lane (Bottom/Blue Side):** Grants more gold from minions. It's where the gold-dependent **Marksman** and their **Support** partner go to farm up for the late game .
- **The Jungle:** The area between the lanes filled with neutral monsters. Defeating them grants buffs and is the job of the **Jungler**, typically an **Assassin** or **Fighter** .
- **The River:** The path that separates the two sides of the map, running through the jungle. Key powerful monsters, like the Tyrant and the Overlord, spawn here .

### 🛡️ Enemy Towers

Each lane is protected by three towers. These structures deal massive damage, so be careful !

- **Targeting:** Towers target the first enemy unit that enters their range. You'll know you're safe when the circle around them is **green**, and you're targeted when it turns **red** .
- **Strategy:** Always let your **minions** (the small AI-controlled soldiers) enter the tower range first to absorb the damage while you attack the structure .

## 🎮 Core Gameplay Mechanics

Mastering these fundamental mechanics will give you a solid foundation.

- **Attacks and Skills:** Your hero's abilities are mapped to buttons on the bottom right of your screen . The **sword icon** is your basic attack. The three buttons around it are your skills: two normal skills and one ultimate. These are more powerful and have cooldowns . You can learn each hero's skill combos by visiting the **Heroes** menu from the main screen .

- **Farming and Gold:** You earn gold by defeating minions, monsters, and enemy heroes. Use this gold to purchase items from the shop, making your hero stronger during the match . A key advanced technique is **last-hitting**—landing the final blow on a minion. This grants about **50% more gold** than if a minion dies near you .

- **Leveling Up:** Defeating enemies also grants experience points (EXP), which cause your hero to level up. Each time you level up, you can upgrade one of your skills, making them more potent .

- **Healing and Recovery:** Staying alive is crucial . You have a few options to recover health :
  - **Recall:** Use the `Recall` button to teleport back to your base, where you'll quickly heal to full.
  - **Healing Dew:** Small health packs located behind the outer towers in each lane.
  - **Recovery Skill:** A personal regeneration skill with a long cooldown.

## 💡 Tips for Your First Battles

Here are some actionable tips to improve your gameplay from your very first match.

- **🔄 Focus on two roles.** In ranked mode, you select primary and secondary roles. Mastering at least three heroes across these two roles is more efficient than trying to learn everything at once .
- **🗺️ Check the minimap constantly.** The minimap in the top-right corner is your best friend. Glance at it every few seconds to track enemy positions and see if your teammates need help . If enemies disappear from the map, they might be coming for you!
- **🏃‍♂️ Don't overextend alone.** Avoid pushing too far into an enemy lane without backup, especially if you don't know where the enemy team is. If you can't see your allies nearby, it's often safer not to fight .
- **💬 Communicate with your team.** Use the quick-chat icons in the top-right corner to signal your intentions, ask for help, or warn teammates . A coordinated team is much harder to beat.
- **⏱️ Learn item timings and "power spikes."** Certain items are crucial against specific enemy heroes (e.g., anti-heal items against healers) . A "power spike" is when your hero becomes significantly stronger, often after buying a key item or reaching a certain level .
- **🧠 Stay calm and learn from mistakes.** Everyone has bad games. If you die, take a moment to think about what happened. Did you push too far? Did you not see the enemy jungler on the map? Learning from errors is the fastest way to improve .
- **⚙️ Customize your settings.** Dive into the settings menu. You can adjust the UI, change targeting priorities (e.g., "lowest HP" for damage dealers), and enable **advanced controls** to get buttons for attacking only minions or towers, which gives you much more precision .
