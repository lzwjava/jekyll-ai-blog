**Question:** What are the 10 advantages of Hong Kong identity/residency status as described in the promotional material?

**Answer:**

This is a promotional/marketing piece targeting mainland Chinese residents, outlining why obtaining Hong Kong permanent residency or identity is beneficial. Here are the 10 advantages listed:

---

### 1. 🪪 Dual Identity — Keep Both Statuses
You can hold Hong Kong identity **and** maintain your mainland household registration (户口) simultaneously, with no impact on your mainland life, work, or asset arrangements.

---

### 2. 🎓 Overseas Chinese Unified Exam (华侨生联考) Advantage
Children can enter top-tier mainland **985/211 universities** with a score of just ~400 points, bypassing competition with the ~10 million annual gaokao (高考) candidates.

---

### 3. 📚 DSE Exam Advantage
Children can sit the Hong Kong **DSE exam** and gain easier entry to Hong Kong or overseas universities. Roughly **80% of admission spots** at Hong Kong's 5 world top-100 universities are reserved for Hong Kong residents.

---

### 4. ✈️ Hong Kong Passport — Visa-Free to 175 Countries
The Hong Kong SAR passport grants **visa-free or visa-on-arrival access** to 175 countries and regions, making international travel and business significantly more convenient than a mainland PRC passport.

---

### 5. 💰 Greater Bay Area Personal Income Tax Subsidy
In the **Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area (GBA)**, if your effective tax rate exceeds 15%, the government subsidizes the difference. Example given: on a ¥1M annual salary, you could receive ~¥95,000/year in tax rebates.

---

### 6. 🏢 Lower Corporate Tax via Hong Kong Company
Hong Kong's **territorial tax system** means profits **not generated in Hong Kong** are not subject to HK profits tax. Investments back into the mainland may also qualify for additional subsidies.

---

### 7. 🏠 GBA Property Purchase Advantages
Hong Kong residents can directly purchase property in the **Greater Bay Area** using Hong Kong bank financing and may be eligible for property purchase subsidies.

---

### 8. 🚪 No "Immigration Jail" (没有移民监)
Unlike some other residency/immigration schemes, Hong Kong has **no mandatory residency requirement** — you can freely choose to live and work in either Hong Kong or the mainland.

---

### 9. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 One Application, Whole Family Benefits
When one person qualifies, their **spouse and minor children** can be included as dependents and all receive Hong Kong identity together.

---

### 10. 🌏 Springboard to Western Immigration
Holding Hong Kong identity makes it **easier to subsequently immigrate** to the UK, US, Australia, or Canada, opening broader pathways for education, visas, and overseas development.

---
