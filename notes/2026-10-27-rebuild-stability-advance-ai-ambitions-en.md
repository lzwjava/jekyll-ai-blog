This week

## 4. What I would deliberately avoid

- Don't start too many projects. Fun Live, your AI consulting business, an AI community, your blog, and LLM research may all be worthwhile. For now, choose one research project and one primary route to income.
- Don't make a master's degree your immediate escape route. First verify eligibility, total cost, financing, and whether the credential materially improves your target job prospects.
- Don't sell your house solely because you're scared. Equally, don't keep it solely because selling feels like admitting defeat. Compare the financial consequences of each choice, including transaction costs and alternative housing.
- Don't confuse studying AI with progressing in AI. Read papers, but also run experiments, write code, measure performance, and publish results.
- Don't wait for permission to become a researcher. You can already conduct experiments and build a public record of technical work, even without a degree.
