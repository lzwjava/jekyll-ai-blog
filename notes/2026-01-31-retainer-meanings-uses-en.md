A **retainer** has several meanings depending on the context:

**Most common uses:**

1. **Legal/professional services** - A fee paid upfront to secure someone's services, especially for lawyers, consultants, or freelancers. For example, you might pay a lawyer a monthly retainer to have them available for legal advice as needed.

2. **Dental device** - A removable or fixed appliance worn in your mouth (usually after braces) to keep your teeth in their new position.

3. **A person who serves** - Historically, a servant or follower in the household of a noble or important person. Less common today except in historical contexts.

4. **Something that retains** - Any device or mechanism that holds something in place.
