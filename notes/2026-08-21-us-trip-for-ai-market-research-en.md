Question: Should I travel to the USA?

Answer:

Given your situation (AI engineer, building AI consulting/agent business, considering US/Canada study paths, already thinking about US clients), **yes, I think you should travel to the USA — but not as a vacation. Treat it as market research + opportunity discovery.**

A good strategy:

## 1. First trip: 2–4 weeks, not relocation

Goal:

* Understand the AI market
* Meet potential clients
* Attend AI events / meetups
* Visit universities
* Test whether you actually like living there

Possible route:

```
San Francisco
    ↓
Seattle
    ↓
New York / Boston
    ↓
Los Angeles
```

Why:

* SF: AI startups, VC, OpenAI ecosystem
* Seattle: Microsoft/Amazon ecosystem
* Boston: universities, research
* LA: easier lifestyle, Chinese community, startup opportunities

## 2. Your strongest reason to go: AI business

Your profile is unusual:

* trained GPT-2 from scratch
* ran GPU experiments
* built agents
* backend + AI engineering
* Chinese market experience

The US has many companies that need:

* internal AI agents
* LLM fine-tuning
* RAG systems
* AI automation

Your advantage is not competing for a junior SWE job.

Your better angle:

```
China AI engineering cost
        +
US AI demand
        =
AI consulting bridge
```

A B1/B2 visitor visa can allow activities like business meetings, consultations, conferences, and contract negotiations, but it does not allow employment or working in the US. ([Travel.state.gov][1])

So:

Allowed:

```
meet founders
talk to customers
attend conferences
negotiate projects
```

Not allowed:

```
go there and work as an employee
```
