In Guangzhou winter at 10-15°C, drinking room temperature water is generally fine and quite common. Here's what you should know:

**Room temperature water is okay** — At those temperatures, it's not dangerously cold, and many people drink room temperature water year-round without issues. Your body will warm it up as you drink it anyway.

**However, warm water has some benefits** — In traditional Chinese medicine and common practice in China, warm water is preferred because it's considered easier on digestion, especially for people with sensitive stomachs. Some people find it more soothing and easier to drink larger quantities of.

**Practical considerations for your situation:**

- **A simple electric kettle** — These are inexpensive, quick to heat water, and take up minimal space. You could heat water in batches and let it cool slightly before drinking.
- **A small desk water warmer** — Some people use compact, affordable electric warming dispensers that keep water at a warm temperature.
- **Thermos bottle** — Heat water once and keep it warm for hours.
