Question: **What are the top IT / AI companies in Guangzhou?**

Answer:

Guangzhou is not as AI-model-centric as Beijing (Zhipu, Baidu) or Hangzhou (Alibaba, DeepSeek ecosystem), but it has strong **AI application, cloud, enterprise software, robotics, intelligent hardware, and industry AI** companies.

For an AI engineer / LLM infra background, I would rank them roughly like this:

| Company                        | Main AI / Tech Direction                                | Why interesting                                                                               |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tencent (Guangzhou R&D)        | LLM, Tencent Cloud, Hunyuan, recommendation, NLP, infra | The strongest AI engineering brand in Guangzhou. Tencent has Guangzhou R&D offices in Tianhe. |
| CVTE                           | AI education, smart conference, robotics, edge AI       | Very strong engineering culture; combines hardware + AI software. ([CVTE][1])                 |
| Jiadu Technology               | Computer vision, smart city, intelligent transportation | One of Guangzhou's representative AI companies. ([NF News][2])                                |
| Huawei Guangzhou               | Cloud, AI infrastructure, distributed systems           | Good fit for systems engineers. Guangzhou has Huawei software R&D presence.                   |
| iFlytek Guangzhou              | Speech AI, NLP applications                             | Strong in voice, education, enterprise AI. ([iFlytek][3])                                     |
| Jinghua Information Technology | Enterprise software, AI knowledge systems               | More enterprise AI / digital transformation.                                                  |
| Guangzhou Ruifan Technology    | Data platform, AI analytics                             | Enterprise data + AI applications.                                                            |
