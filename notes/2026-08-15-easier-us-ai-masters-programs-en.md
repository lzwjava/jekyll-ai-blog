## Question:

What US master's programs in AI / Machine Learning / Deep Learning / NLP / LLM are relatively easier to apply to?

## Answer:

Given your profile:

* 11+ years software engineering
* backend + full stack
* Python/PyTorch
* trained GPT-2 models
* LLM fine-tuning/inference experience
* IELTS 6.5
* no traditional bachelor's degree completed (important constraint)

Your biggest issue is **not technical ability**. It is **credential filtering**. Many US MS programs require a bachelor's degree first. For example, USC AI MS explicitly requires a bachelor's degree or equivalent, although it evaluates applicants holistically. ([USC Viterbi | Prospective Students][1])

You should target:

1. **Professional master's programs**
2. **Online/hybrid programs**
3. **Less competitive state universities**
4. **AI/Data Science programs accepting industry experience**

Avoid MIT/Stanford/CMU-type programs unless you first solve the degree issue.
