**Question:** What does "SXM" stand for in NVIDIA's GPU context?

**Answer:**

SXM stands for **Server PCI Express Module**.

Despite containing "PCI Express" in its full name, SXM is actually NVIDIA's *proprietary high-bandwidth socket solution* that goes beyond standard PCIe — it was designed to overcome the bandwidth limitations of the PCIe bus by integrating NVLink directly into the socket interface.
