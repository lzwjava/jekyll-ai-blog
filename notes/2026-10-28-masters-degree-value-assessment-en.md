## 1. What changes if you earn a CSTU master's?

More HR filters may open

Some job postings require a master's degree or a bachelor's degree plus experience. A master's may satisfy the education requirement, depending on the employer's rules and whether it accepts CSTU's credential.

Your technical level does not automatically change

A degree alone will not make you a stronger LLM researcher, inference engineer, or staff engineer. Your training experiments, code, benchmarks, and engineering results remain the evidence of your technical ability.

Some employers may still prefer better-known universities

A hiring manager choosing between candidates with similar experience may favor a graduate from a well-known research university. CSTU's smaller institutional profile means you should not assume its degree has the same signaling value.

International mobility is not guaranteed

A recognized U.S. degree can help with some educational and employment requirements, but it does not automatically grant a visa, work authorization, immigration eligibility, or admission to another university.
