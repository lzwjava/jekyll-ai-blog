**Question:** What are the dimensions of the iPhone 14 Pro Max?

**Answer:**

**iPhone 14 Pro Max (2022):**

- **Dimensions:** 160.7 × 77.6 × 7.85 mm
- **Weight:** 240 g — notably heavy due to the stainless steel frame (later Pro Max models switched to titanium and dropped weight)
- **Display:** 6.7" Super Retina XDR OLED, 2796×1290 (460 ppi), ProMotion 120 Hz, always-on display, Dynamic Island
- **Storage:** 128 GB / 256 GB / 512 GB / 1 TB

Quick comparison within the 14 lineup:

| Model | Dimensions (mm) | Weight | Display |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| iPhone 14 | 146.7 × 71.5 × 7.8 | 172 g | 6.1", 60 Hz |
| iPhone 14 Plus | 160.8 × 78.1 × 7.8 | 203 g | 6.7", 60 Hz |
| iPhone 14 Pro | 147.5 × 71.5 × 7.85 | 206 g | 6.1", 120 Hz |
| iPhone 14 Pro Max | 160.7 × 77.6 × 7.85 | 240 g | 6.7", 120 Hz |

Interesting detail: the 14 Plus is physically slightly *larger* than the Pro Max in footprint but 37 g lighter — aluminum frame vs stainless steel. The 240 g Pro Max was the heaviest iPhone at the time; the 15 Pro Max cut that to 221 g with titanium.

Chip-wise the Pro Max also differs from the base 14: it runs the A16 Bionic (4nm) instead of the A15, plus a 48MP main sensor. Battery is ~4,323 mAh — best endurance of the generation alongside the 14 Plus.
