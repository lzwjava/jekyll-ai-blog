Question: **What does QMAS Criterion (8) mean, and would my work experience qualify?**

Answer:

It means Hong Kong QMAS is asking:

> **Do you have at least 3 years of graduate/specialist-level work experience in one of these specific fields?**

The listed fields are:

* **Innovation and technology**
* **Finance**
* **International trade**, including:

  * cross-border marine transportation
  * aviation transportation
  * supply-chain management

### The important part

QMAS says they look primarily at **what you actually did in your job**, not simply what industry your employer belongs to.

For example:

| Your job                   | Company industry   | Likely interpretation                                                |
| -------------------------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AI/ML Engineer             | Banking            | **Innovation & technology**                                          |
| Backend Engineer           | Fintech            | **Innovation & technology**                                          |
| Software Engineer          | Logistics          | **Innovation & technology**, potentially supply-chain related        |
| Financial Analyst          | Technology company | **Finance**                                                          |
| Supply Chain Manager       | Manufacturing      | **International trade / supply chain**                               |
| CEO of a logistics company | Logistics          | They may consider the company's business because CEO is a broad role |

So **being employed by a finance company does not automatically make your experience "finance."** If you were a software engineer there, your role itself may be considered technology.

### For your situation

Based on the work history you've described, your strongest argument would likely be **"innovation and technology."**

You have substantial experience as a:

* Backend Engineer
* Full-stack Engineer
* AI/ML-oriented engineer
* Software developer

So the relevant question isn't:

> "Did I work for a technology company?"

It's:

> **"Can I demonstrate ≥3 years of professional, graduate/specialist-level work whose actual nature was software engineering / AI / IT / technology?"**

Given your ~11 years of software engineering experience, **you appear very likely to satisfy the 3-year duration requirement**, assuming the relevant employment can be documented and is accepted as graduate/specialist-level experience.

The wording **"graduate or specialist level"** is important: they're not simply asking for *any* three years of employment. They want professional-level specialized work.

### What I would put in the application

If the form simply asks:

> **Please confirm if you fulfill the above requirement(s).**

and your documented experience supports it, the answer would be:

**Yes.**

Then your supporting employment documents should make the technology nature obvious—for example, job title, employment dates, and descriptions showing software engineering / AI / backend / full-stack responsibilities.

I would **not** try to classify your experience as finance merely because one employer was a financial-services company. Your strongest and cleanest route is **Innovation and Technology → Software Engineering / AI / IT**.
