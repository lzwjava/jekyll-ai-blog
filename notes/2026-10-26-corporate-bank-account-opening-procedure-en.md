**Question:** What is the procedure for opening a corporate bank account (对公账户)?

**Answer:**

Here's the full step-by-step process for opening a 基本存款账户 (the primary corporate account) at 招商银行增城支行 for 广州智维深算信息技术有限公司.

---

**Step 1 — Decide the Account Type**

| Type | Purpose | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| 基本存款账户 | Main daily operating account (payroll, payments, taxes) | **1 per company** across all banks |
| 一般存款账户 | Secondary accounts (for loans, etc.) | Multiple allowed |

As a new company you almost certainly want the **基本户** first.

---

**Step 2 — Prepare Documents**

Core documents required:

| Document | Notes |
|---|---|
| 营业执照正本 + 副本 | Original + photocopies |
| 法定代表人身份证 | Original; legal rep should attend in person |
| 经办人身份证 | If someone other than the legal rep handles it |
| 授权委托书 | Required if legal rep doesn't attend in person, with company seal |
| 公章 (company seal) | Must bring the physical stamp |
| 财务专用章 | Financial seal |
| 法人章 | Legal representative personal seal |
| 经营场所证明 | Lease agreement or property deed for 中新镇福中路 address |

Banks also typically require a filled application form and may request photos of the business premises to verify your operating address.

---

**Step 3 — Make an Appointment (预约)**

Call 招商银行增城支行 at **(020) 82747632** or visit in person first to:
- Confirm the exact document checklist for your company type
- Book a specific time slot (reduces waiting)

---

**Step 4 — Visit the Branch**

The legal representative (法人) should ideally attend in person — some banks require on-site identity verification and photos. Bring all original documents plus copies. The bank will have you fill in an account opening application form (开户申请书) on-site. The whole in-branch session typically takes 1–2 hours.

---

**Step 5 — Bank Review & Due Diligence (尽调)**

Under current anti-fraud regulations, banks treat a stable operating background and a fixed business address as necessary conditions for corporate account opening. The bank may:

- Call the legal representative to verify identity
- Conduct an on-site visit to your business address (中新镇福中路) — be prepared for this
- Request additional proof of actual business operations if the company is newly registered

---

**Step 6 — Account Activation**

From appointment to account activation, the average processing time is about 1–2 business days. Once approved:

- You receive your account number
- Set up 网上银行 (corporate online banking / U-shield)
- Set up 企业手机银行 if needed

---

**Step 7 — Post-Opening (Important)**

After the account is open, you'll need to:

- **Tax bureau binding**: Link the account to your tax registration at the local 税务局 (for invoicing / VAT purposes)
- **Social insurance & provident fund**: Register the account with 社保局 and 公积金中心
- **Payroll setup**: If using the account for salary payments, configure payroll services
