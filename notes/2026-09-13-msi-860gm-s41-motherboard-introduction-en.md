**Question:** What is an introduction to the MSI 860GM-S41 motherboard?

**Answer:**

The MSI 860GM-S41 is a budget-friendly Micro ATX motherboard from MSI designed for AMD AM3 platform desktop builds. Here's a quick overview:

---

**Form Factor & Chipset**

It uses the AMD 780L chipset, specifically the AMD RS780L (RS760) North Bridge paired with an SB710 South Bridge. The board follows a Micro ATX form factor.

---

**CPU Support**

The socket is AM3, supporting AMD Phenom II, Athlon II, and Sempron processors.

---

**Memory**

It has 2× DDR3 DIMM slots with a maximum capacity of 8 GB, supporting dual-channel DDR3 at speeds of 1333/1066/800 MHz.

---

**Expansion Slots**

The board offers 1× PCIe x16 graphics slot, 1× PCIe x1 slot, and 1× PCI slot, all running on PCIe 2.0.

---

**Storage & I/O**

Storage is handled by 4× SATA II interfaces. For connectivity, there are 8× USB 2.0 ports (4 internal + 4 rear), 1× DVI, 1× VGA, PS/2 mouse and keyboard ports, and 1× RJ45 network port.

---

**Onboard Audio & Network**

Audio is provided by an integrated Realtek ALC662 6-channel chip, and networking uses an onboard Realtek RTL8111E Gigabit network card.

---

**Power**

The board uses one 8-pin and one 24-pin power connector.

---

**Summary:** The 860GM-S41 is a straightforward, no-frills budget board aimed at entry-level AMD builds. It supports older AM3 CPUs with modest expansion options — suitable for office use, light media, or a basic desktop system.
