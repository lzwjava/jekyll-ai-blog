Question: Who is Shreyas Doshi?

Answer:

Shreyas Doshi is a well-known **product management leader, advisor, and educator** in the tech industry.

### 1. Career background

He built his reputation by working at several major tech companies:

* Stripe → one of the earliest product managers and later the **first PM manager**, helping build the product management function from scratch
* Google → worked on key areas like AdWords
* Twitter and Yahoo → led and scaled multiple products and teams

At Stripe specifically, he led important products such as:

* Stripe Connect
* Stripe Terminal ([PR Newswire][1])

### 2. What he does now

After leaving full-time roles, he shifted into:

* **Startup advisor** (helping founders with product, strategy, hiring)
* **Executive coach**
* **Instructor** (teaching product management courses)
* **Content creator** (sharing frameworks on product, leadership, and thinking)

He has advised **100+ startups and executives** and teaches thousands of product professionals. ([Stackforce][2])

### 3. Why he’s popular

He’s widely followed because he:

* Breaks down product management into **clear frameworks and mental models**
* Focuses on **product sense, decision-making, and leadership**
* Shares practical insights from real experience at top tech companies

He has built a large audience (hundreds of thousands of followers) through platforms like Twitter/LinkedIn. ([Stackforce][2])

### 4. Reputation (important nuance)
