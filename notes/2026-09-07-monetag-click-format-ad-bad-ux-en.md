Question: Is Monetag's **click-format ad** a bad user experience because it suddenly redirects users to sites like Trip.com?

Answer: **Yes — generally, it's a bad UX for a technical/content site.**

If a user clicks something and gets unexpectedly redirected to **Trip.com, an app-install page, a new tab, or another advertiser**, that's essentially an aggressive performance-ad format. Even if it generates higher RPM/CTR, it can hurt:

* **User trust** — users think your site itself is redirecting them.
* **Content experience** — especially bad for a technical blog where users are trying to read/code.
* **SEO signals indirectly** — frustrated users may bounce immediately.
* **AdSense/quality positioning** — it makes the site look more like an ad-driven site.
* **Repeat visitors** — your existing technical audience is much more valuable than a few extra clicks.

The important distinction is:

```text
Normal display ad
    user sees ad
    ↓
    consciously clicks
    ↓
    advertiser

Aggressive click/redirect format
    user clicks page/UI
    ↓
    unexpected advertiser redirect
    ↓
    "Why the hell did this open Trip.com?"
```

For **lzwjava.com**, I'd strongly prefer **standard display/native ads** over forced redirects, popups, popunders, or misleading click areas. Your audience is technical; preserving trust is probably worth more than squeezing maximum short-term RPM.
