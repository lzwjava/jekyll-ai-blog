Question: How do I play a muse.ai video from Facebook?

Answer: Your question could mean a few things, so here are the most likely cases.

**1. You see a muse.ai link in a Facebook post and want to watch it**

- Tap or click the link. It opens in Facebook's in-app browser, or in your default browser if you choose "Open in browser."
- If the video doesn't play in the in-app browser, open the link in Chrome or Safari instead (tap the ⋯ menu, then "Open in browser").
- Some muse.ai videos are private or password-protected, so you'll need the password from whoever shared it.

**2. You want to share your own muse.ai video on Facebook**

- Copy the video's share link from your muse.ai dashboard and paste it into a Facebook post. Viewers click it to watch on muse.ai's player.
- muse.ai is a hosting platform, not a native Facebook video source, so the video may not play inline inside the post the way a Facebook-uploaded video does. Facebook shows a link preview, and the video plays once people click through.
- Make sure the video is set to public or unlisted. Private videos won't play for viewers.

**3. You want to play it on your own Facebook Page or website**

- muse.ai supports embedding videos and collections. Embed the player on a web page you control, then share that page's link on Facebook.

**4. The video won't play**

- Try a different browser or disable ad or content blockers.
- Check that you have a stable connection, then reload.
- Check that the video hasn't been removed, made private, or restricted from embedding. muse.ai has embed restrictions and access controls.
