My overall assessment: The article is compelling as a personal reflection, but I would not publish it in its current form.

There are three different kinds of claims mixed together:
1. Verifiable facts: historical events, company rankings, university accreditation, immigration policies, technical claims.
2. Personal claims: your salary, employment history, business revenue, family circumstances, exam results and achievements. These need documentary evidence rather than general web research.
3. Opinions and interpretations: office politics, your father's influence, the future of AI, entrepreneurship and the meaning of life. These don't need fact-checking, but some should be presented as opinions rather than established facts.

1. Claims that are factually wrong or misleading

Must correct

Original:

The scientific evidence does not support this recommendation. The European Society of Ophthalmology and International Myopia Institute guidance explicitly says that undercorrecting myopia is not recommended.
