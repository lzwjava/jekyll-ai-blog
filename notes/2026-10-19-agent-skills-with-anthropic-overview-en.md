## Course Overview

**Agent Skills with Anthropic** is a short course produced by DeepLearning.AI in partnership with Anthropic, taught by **Elie Schoppik** (Head of Technical Education at Anthropic). Note: while the series lives on Andrew Ng's DeepLearning.AI platform, the instructor for this specific course is Elie Schoppik, not Andrew Ng himself.

The course was announced on **January 28, 2026**.

---

## What Are "Skills"?

Skills are **folders of instructions** that extend agent capabilities with specialized knowledge. Because they follow an open standard format, you can build them once and deploy across any skills-compatible agent.

---

## What You'll Learn

The course covers:
- **Why use Skills** (Part I & II)
- **Skills vs. Tools, MCP, and Subagents** — understanding the conceptual differences
- **Exploring Pre-Built Skills** — Anthropic's built-in skills for Excel, PowerPoint, and Skill Creation
- **Creating Custom Skills** — for code generation, data analysis, and research workflows
- **Skills with the Claude API, Claude Code, and the Claude Agent SDK**

---

## Target Audience & Duration

The course is aimed at **any AI builder who wants to extend agent capabilities with reusable workflows and specialized knowledge**. Total duration is approximately **2 hours 19 minutes**, at a beginner level.
