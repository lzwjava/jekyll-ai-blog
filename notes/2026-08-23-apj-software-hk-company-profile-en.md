## Question: Intro to APJ Software (HK) Company Limited (APJHK), a Hong Kong software company

## Answer

APJ Software (Hong Kong) Co., Ltd. (APJHK, 愛浦京軟件（香港）有限公司) is a Hong Kong-based **enterprise software development and IT consulting company** founded in 2002. It focuses on helping Hong Kong enterprises build, maintain, and modernize business systems through software outsourcing, consulting, project delivery, and offshore development services. ([APJ Software (HK) Co., Ltd.][1])

The company is mainly an **IT service provider / system integrator**, rather than a pure product startup. Its business model is similar to many Hong Kong enterprise IT vendors: understand customer requirements, provide developers and consultants, deliver software projects, and maintain enterprise systems. ([APJ Software (HK) Co., Ltd.][2])

### Core business areas

**1. Enterprise software development**

* Custom business applications
* Web platforms
* Mobile apps
* Digital transformation projects
* Integration with existing enterprise systems

APJ mentions serving industries including transportation, utilities, financial services, manufacturing, education, retail, and other enterprise sectors. ([LinkedIn][3])

**2. Offshore Development Centre (ODC) model**

This is their major strength.

They help Hong Kong companies create an offshore engineering team in Mainland China. APJ provides:

* Developers
* Project managers
* QA engineers
* Development processes
* Delivery management

Their offshore teams are based mainly in Guangdong and other Mainland cities. The company says it has supported Hong Kong enterprises through ODC services for more than 20 years and has development resources in Zhuhai, Guangzhou, Changsha, and Wuhan. ([APJ Software (HK) Co., Ltd.][1])

**3. Software testing and maintenance**

* QA process design
* Automated testing
* Application maintenance
* Production support

([APJ Software (HK) Co., Ltd.][2])

## Relationship with Mainland China

APJHK has a strong **Hong Kong–Mainland software outsourcing model**.

This is common in Hong Kong because:

* Hong Kong enterprises need lower-cost engineering resources.
* Mainland China has a much larger software engineering talent pool.
* Companies like APJ act as the bridge.

Their positioning is basically:

```
Hong Kong enterprise customer
          |
          |
    APJ Hong Kong
   (sales + consulting +
    project management)
          |
          |
 Mainland engineering team
 (development + testing)
```
