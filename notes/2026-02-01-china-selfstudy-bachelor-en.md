Great question! Yes, this is a real and well-known pathway in China. Here's how it works:

**What you already have** is an Associate Degree (专科学历) from Guangdong University of Foreign Studies via the National Higher Education Self-Study Examination (全国高等教育自学考试, or 自考).

---

**To get a Bachelor's Degree (本科学历) through Self-Study Exam, the general process is:**

Since you already have an Associate Degree, you would be doing what's called **专本科衔接 (Associate-to-Bachelor bridging)** through the self-exam system. This is actually a common and straightforward path.

**Step 1 — Choose a Major and an Enrolling Institution (主考学校)**
Each province has designated universities that act as the "examining institution." In Guangdong, this is typically **Guangdong University of Foreign Studies** or another approved school. You pick a bachelor's major offered by that institution.

**Step 2 — Register (报名)**
You register through the local examination bureau (考试局), usually under the Provincial Education Examination Authority (省教育考试院). In Guangdong, this is the **广东省教育考试院**.

**Step 3 — Pass the Required Exams**
As a 专科 holder, you don't need to retake all courses — only the **remaining bachelor's-level courses** that weren't covered at the associate level. This typically means around **6–10 exams**, depending on the major. Each exam is taken individually, usually offered **twice a year** (spring and fall sessions).

**Step 4 — Complete Any Practical/Thesis Requirements**
Some majors require a **capstone project or thesis (毕业论文)**, supervised by the enrolling university.

**Step 5 — Apply for Graduation**
Once all required exams and coursework are passed, you apply to the enrolling institution for your **Bachelor's Degree certificate (本科毕业证书)**.

---

**A few practical tips:**

The self-exam bachelor's degree is **nationally recognized** and legally equivalent to a regular bachelor's degree in China — it's not a lesser qualification. The main trade-off is that it takes **self-discipline and time**, since there's no regular class schedule. Many people complete it in **1.5 to 3 years** while working. Some universities also offer **online tutoring or prep courses** to help you study for each exam, which can make it much easier.
