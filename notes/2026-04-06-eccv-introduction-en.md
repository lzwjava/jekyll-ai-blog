## ECCV — European Conference on Computer Vision

### Overview

The **European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV)** is a biennial premier research conference in Computer Vision and Machine Learning, managed by the **European Computer Vision Association (ECVA)**. It is held on even-numbered years and gathers both the scientific and industrial communities in these areas.

The first ECCV was held in **1990 in Antibes, France**, and has subsequently been organized all over Europe. Paper proceedings are published by **Springer Science+Business Media**.

---

### Prestige and Ranking

ECCV is considered to be one of the **top conferences in computer vision**, alongside **CVPR** (Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition) and **ICCV** (International Conference on Computer Vision). It holds an **'A' rating** from the Australian Ranking of ICT Conferences and an **'A1' rating** from the Brazilian Ministry of Education.

It is essentially the European counterpart to ICCV — similar to ICCV in scope and quality, ECCV is held in the years when ICCV is not. The conference is usually held in **autumn in Europe**, and it also presents the **Koenderink Prize** annually to recognize fundamental contributions in computer vision.

---

### Research Topics Covered

Topics of interest at ECCV span a wide range of computer vision and pattern recognition subjects, including:

- 3D from a single image and shape-from-x
- 3D shape modeling and processing
- Action and behavior recognition
- Adversarial learning
- Biometrics
- Computational photography
- Datasets and evaluation
- Detection and localization in 2D and/or 3D
- Efficient training and inference methods
- Explainable AI for Computer Vision
- Face analysis
- Fairness, accountability, transparency, and ethics in vision
- First-person (egocentric) vision
- Image and video synthesis
- Low-level and physics-based vision
- Low-shot learning
- Machine learning architectures
- Medical, biological, and cell microscopy
- Motion and tracking
- Neural generative models
- Optimization and learning methods
- Recognition and classification
- Representation learning
- Scene analysis and understanding
- Segmentation, grouping, and shape

---

### Awards

ECVA recognizes outstanding work through several awards given at each ECCV:

- **Koenderink Prize** — for fundamental contributions to computer vision
- **Young Researcher Award** — for outstanding young researchers (≤35 years old) at European institutions, with a **€5,000** prize
- **PhD Award** — for outstanding dissertations in computer vision defended at European institutions, with a **€2,500** prize

---

### Summary

In short, ECCV is one of the **"Big Three" computer vision conferences** (along with CVPR and ICCV), held every two years in Europe, and is a premier venue where academics and industry professionals present and discuss cutting-edge research in AI-powered image and video understanding.
