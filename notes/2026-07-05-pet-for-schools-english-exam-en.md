Question: What is the PET exam for kids in English?

Answer:

**PET** usually means the **Cambridge English: Preliminary (B1)** exam.

It is an **English test made by Cambridge** for students (often teenagers, but adults can take it too). There is also a version called **“PET for Schools”**, which uses topics that are more suitable for children and school life.

### What level is it?

* PET = **B1 level (intermediate English)**
* It means you can use everyday English in real situations like school, travel, shopping, and simple conversations ([Cambridge Basel][1])

### What does the exam test?

It checks **4 English skills**:

* Reading
* Writing
* Listening
* Speaking ([Cambridge Basel][1])

### What can a student do after passing PET?

A student at PET level can:

* Understand simple texts and articles
* Write short emails or letters
* Talk about daily life and opinions
* Understand basic spoken English ([Cambridge Basel][1])

### PET vs PET for Schools

* Same difficulty level (both B1)
* Same exam format
* Only difference:

  * **PET for Schools** = topics about school, teenagers, daily student life
  * **PET** = more general / adult topics (work, jobs, etc.)

### Simple summary

👉 PET is an **intermediate English certificate exam** for learners who already know basic English and want to prove they can use English in real-life situations.
