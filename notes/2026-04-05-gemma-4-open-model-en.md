**Question:** How good is Gemma 4 from Google?

**Answer:**

Google released Gemma 4 on April 3, 2026 — making it a brand-new release. Here's a comprehensive overview of how it performs and what makes it notable:

---

## Overview

Gemma 4 is Google's most intelligent open model family to date, purpose-built for advanced reasoning and agentic workflows. Google claims it delivers an unprecedented level of intelligence-per-parameter, built from the same world-class research and technology as Gemini 3.

---

## Model Sizes

Gemma 4 comes in four sizes: 2B and 4B models optimized for phones and edge devices, and 26B and 31B models designed to fit on a single 80GB GPU. The smaller models enable offline AI experiences, while the larger versions deliver reasoning that rivals much bigger proprietary systems.

---

## Performance & Rankings

The 31B model currently ranks as the **#3 open model in the world** on the industry-standard Arena AI text leaderboard, while the 26B model secures the **#6 spot**. Remarkably, Gemma 4 outcompetes models **20× its size** in these rankings.

---

## Key Strengths

**1. Advanced Reasoning**
Gemma 4 is capable of multi-step planning and deep logic, demonstrating significant improvements in math and instruction-following benchmarks.

**2. Agentic Workflows**
Native support for function-calling, structured JSON output, and native system instructions enables developers to build autonomous agents that can interact with different tools and APIs and execute workflows reliably.

**3. Runs Locally on Your Own Hardware**
Unlike many AI models that rely on cloud infrastructure, Gemma 4 is built to run locally across a range of devices — from smartphones and laptops to GPUs — depending on model size. This reduces latency, improves speed for real-time tasks, and gives teams handling sensitive data more control and privacy.

**4. Multimodal Capabilities**
The edge models (E2B and E4B) prioritize multimodal capabilities and low-latency processing, enabling them to handle audio and visual inputs without relying on external servers.

**5. Open License**
Gemma 4 is distributed under an **Apache 2.0 license**, addressing enterprise concerns over model access and control.

**6. Fine-tuning & Ecosystem**
By using these highly optimized models, developers can fine-tune Gemma 4 to achieve state-of-the-art performance on specific tasks. Earlier Gemma versions have been downloaded over 400 million times, with more than 100,000 community variants created.

---

## Hardware Compatibility

Gemma 4 is optimized for industry-leading hardware out of the box — from NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano to Blackwell GPUs, AMD GPUs via the ROCm stack, and Google's own Trillium and Ironwood TPUs for massive scale.

---

## Verdict

Gemma 4 is **exceptionally strong** for an open-source model family. Its standout feature is the remarkable intelligence-per-parameter ratio — the 31B variant competes at a frontier level while being deployable on a single GPU. For developers, researchers, and enterprises that want powerful AI without full cloud dependency, Gemma 4 is one of the best open model choices available in 2026.
