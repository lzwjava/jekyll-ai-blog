In Apple's history, **a few figures besides Steve Wozniak** had strong expertise in electronics and digital systems — people who understood the kinds of topics covered in **"Fundamentals of Electronic Technology (III)"** at a **deep engineering level**. These individuals were crucial in building Apple’s hardware foundation, especially during the **Apple I/II, Macintosh, iPod, and early iPhone** eras.

---

## 🔧 Engineers at Apple with Deep Hardware/Electronics Knowledge

### 🥇 **Steve Wozniak**

* **Era**: 1976–1985 (formally), lifetime influence
* **Strengths**: Analog/digital design, microprocessors, low-level hardware, TTL logic
* **Legacy**: Designed almost the entire Apple I and II hardware himself

---

### 🧠 **Burrell Smith**

* **Era**: Joined 1979 (Macintosh Team)
* **Role**: Hardware Designer for the original **Macintosh 128K**
* **Expertise**: Digital logic, analog video circuits, board-level integration
* **Contributions**:

  * Created a cost-effective, high-performance digital board for the Mac using clever TTL logic tricks (e.g., using fewer chips than standard).
  * Was a **genius with logic gates and board optimization**, similar to Woz.

---

### 🔬 **Tony Fadell**

* **Era**: 2001–2008 (iPod, iPhone development)
* **Role**: Led the iPod team, involved in iPhone hardware
* **Expertise**: Systems design, embedded electronics, power management
* **Contributions**:

  * Deeply involved in the **SoC (System-on-Chip)** integration for iPod/iPhone
  * Oversaw hardware-software integration, including low-level logic and power design
  * Later founded **Nest**, showing strong grounding in sensor/mixed-signal systems

---

### 🔧 **Bob Mansfield**

* **Era**: 1999–2013 (then returned for Apple Watch and AR work)
* **Role**: SVP of Hardware Engineering
* **Expertise**: System-level architecture, power electronics, integration
* **Contributions**:

  * Led hardware design across MacBook, iMac, iPad, and iPhone
  * Oversaw **PCB layout**, signal integrity, power management, and **thermal design**
  * Less hands-on than Woz, but deeply knowledgeable about electronics design at system scale

---

### ⚡ **Johny Srouji**

* **Era**: 2008–present
* **Role**: SVP of Hardware Technologies
* **Expertise**: **Semiconductor design**, **SoC architecture**, **mixed-signal and digital logic**
* **Contributions**:

  * Led creation of Apple’s in-house **A-series chips** (used in iPhones, iPads)
  * Expert in **digital circuits**, **custom silicon**, **FPGA/ASIC**, and system performance
  * Overlaps deeply with **Part II (Digital Electronics)** and **PLD/HDL** topics from the textbook

---

### 🖥️ **Mike Culbert**

* **Role**: Veteran Apple hardware engineer (often behind the scenes)
* **Expertise**: Power systems, system architecture, low-level hardware
* **Contributions**:

  * Credited on multiple Apple patents related to power management and efficient circuit integration
  * Likely had deep knowledge of **DC power**, **switching regulators**, **signal processing**
