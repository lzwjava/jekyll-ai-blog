Question: What maps does PUBG Mobile provide?

Answer:

PUBG Mobile offers a variety of battle royale maps, primarily in Classic mode, with sizes ranging from 1x1 km to 8x8 km. These maps feature unique terrains, player counts, and gameplay paces. The core maps available as of 2026 are:

| Map Name   | Size     | Player Count | Theme/Terrain                  | Key Notes |
|------------|----------|--------------|--------------------------------|-----------|
| Erangel   | 8x8 km  | 100         | Temperate island with urban/rural areas | Original map, most popular for balanced gameplay. |
| Miramar   | 8x8 km  | 100         | Desert with cities and open fields | Sniper-friendly, features vending machines. |
| Sanhok    | 4x4 km  | 100         | Jungle with hills and rivers   | Fast-paced, close-quarters combat. |
| Vikendi   | 6x6 km  | 100         | Snowy mountains and towns      | Slippery terrain, train system. |
| Karakin   | 2x2 km  | 64          | Desert with destructible areas | Quick matches, unique oil rigs. |
| Livik     | 2x2 km  | 52          | Nordic forests and cities      | Fast action, 15-minute matches. |
| Nusa      | 1x1 km  | 32          | High-tech urban island         | Ultra-fast, respawn mechanics. |
| Rondo     | 8x8 km  | 100         | Chinese-inspired urban/rural   | Newest addition, recent loot rebalance. |

**Additional Details:**
- **Availability**: Maps rotate in queues (e.g., Ranked, Classic, Payload). Players can download individual maps to save space. Erangel, Miramar, and Rondo are staples in competitive play.
- **Other Modes**: Arena modes use smaller, custom maps (e.g., for Team Deathmatch). Payload and Infection use select BR maps.
- **Recent Updates**: Loot Trucks added to Erangel, Livik, and Miramar in v4.1 (Nov 2025). Paramo is in beta/testing but not fully released globally yet.
- Maps may vary slightly by region/server, but these are standard in Global.
