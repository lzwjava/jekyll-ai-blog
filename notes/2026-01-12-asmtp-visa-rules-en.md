Yes, that's correct! Under the Admission Scheme for Mainland Talents and Professionals (ASMTP), if one spouse successfully obtains approval to work in Hong Kong, the other spouse can apply as a **dependent**.

Here's how it works:

**Primary applicant** (husband or wife):

- Applies under ASMTP with a confirmed job offer from a Hong Kong employer
- Must meet the eligibility criteria (relevant qualifications and skills)

**Dependent spouse**:

- Can apply for a **dependant visa** once the primary applicant's ASMTP is approved
- The dependent spouse will be allowed to enter/remain in Hong Kong
- **Important**: Dependants are generally allowed to work in Hong Kong without needing a separate work visa

**Children** can also be included as dependants in the application.

**Key points to remember**:

- Only ONE spouse needs to qualify under ASMTP (the one with the job offer)
- The dependent application is separate but linked to the primary ASMTP approval
- Both spouses don't need to have job offers
- Processing times may differ between the primary and dependent applications

This makes it easier for families to relocate together to Hong Kong, as long as one spouse can secure qualified employment.
