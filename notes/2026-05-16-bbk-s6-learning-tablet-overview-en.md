### 🧠 Core AI Features

The S6 is built around a three-step AI learning system:

1. **AI Diagnosis (一诊 — Diagnose)**
   AI can quickly diagnose the child's homework and test papers, identifying the underlying weak knowledge points.

2. **AI Precision Learning (二学 — Learn)**
   Based on the diagnosed weak points, AI recommends personalized learning content tailored to the child, with clear and focused learning goals.

3. **AI Targeted Practice (三测 — Test)**
   The system pushes targeted practice questions based on weak areas, efficiently checking learning outcomes and eliminating the need for mindless bulk drilling.

---

### 📚 Key Learning Functions

- **AI Exam Paper Diagnosis:** One-tap photo scanning of math homework or tests using AI big data to detect weak knowledge points — currently supports elementary school math, with ongoing updates.

- **AI Essay Correction:** Chinese composition is corrected with one photo tap, featuring smart scoring, detailed commentary, writing tips, and excellent essay examples for reference.

- **AI Personalized Knowledge Map:** AI diagnosis covers different learning stages — sections, units, and pre-exam — generating a personalized knowledge map so the system can recommend content based on weak areas.

- **AI Error Notebook:** Wrong answers are added to the error notebook with one tap, eliminating manual copying. AI recommends similar questions for further practice, and the notes can also be printed as a quiz sheet.

- **AI English Speaking Assessment:** Built-in support for mainstream national English textbooks, with one-tap pronunciation evaluation and correction for words, phrases, and sentences, plus phonics support.

- **Comprehensive Subject Coverage:** Nine subjects synchronized, covering mainstream national textbook editions; enrichment courses in music, art, programming, and sports; and short knowledge videos spanning science, geography, and daily life to broaden children's knowledge base.
