The M60 situation is even worse than the P100 and P40. It’s basically excluded from mainstream rental platforms, and even if you could list it, recouping your cost would be extremely difficult.

### 🚫 Biggest hard blocker: the platform doesn’t support it

Vast.ai’s official CLI GPU list does **not** include the Tesla M60 at all. It only supports cards like the P100, P40, V100, etc. With no platform entry point, “renting it out” is out of the question.

### 💰 Even if it could be rented, income is extremely low

The M60 is 2015 Maxwell architecture with **8GB VRAM** (usually recognized as a single GM204). That amount of VRAM struggles badly with modern models, and the rental price ceiling is very low. Based on similar low-end cards on Vast.ai, the hourly rate might be only **$0.05 or less**, and optimistic monthly income would be just **200–300 RMB**.

### ⚡ Electricity could directly eat your income

The M60’s TDP is as high as **300W**, and full-load power draw can even reach **250–270W**.
At a Chinese residential electricity rate of **0.5 RMB/kWh**, the monthly electricity cost would be **90–97 RMB**. That means electricity could take **one-third or even half** of the rental income.

### 📌 Conclusion

Don’t bother with the M60. It’s different from the P100—the P100 at least has 16GB VRAM and can run some models. The M60 can’t even get past the platform threshold. If you already have this card, keep it for light local tasks or as a display card; that’s far more worthwhile than listing it for rent. If you really want to recoup your cost through renting, you still need to go back to the **P40 (24GB VRAM)** route.
