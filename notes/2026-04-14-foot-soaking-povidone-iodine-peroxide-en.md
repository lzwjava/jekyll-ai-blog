**Question:**
What is “碘伏” and how does it compare to hydrogen peroxide for foot soaking (e.g., odor, sweating, mild infection)? How many times is it typically used?

---

**Answer:**

### 1. What is “碘伏”?

“碘伏” refers to **Povidone-iodine** (often abbreviated as PVP-I).

* It’s a **broad-spectrum antiseptic** widely used for:

  * disinfecting skin
  * treating minor wounds
  * fungal/bacterial infections (mild cases)
* It releases iodine slowly → **effective but less irritating** than pure iodine tincture.

---

### 2. Comparison with Hydrogen Peroxide

| Aspect                                      | Povidone-iodine (碘伏)                             | Hydrogen Peroxide                                 |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Entity                                      | Povidone-iodine                                  | Hydrogen peroxide                                 |
| Mechanism                                   | Releases iodine → kills bacteria, fungi, viruses | Releases oxygen → bubbles and mechanically cleans |
| Effect on fungi (foot odor, athlete’s foot) | ✅ **Strong**                                     | ⚠️ **Weak**                                       |
| Skin irritation                             | Mild (generally safe diluted)                    | Can irritate / damage tissue with repeated use    |
| Use for soaking feet                        | ✅ Common (diluted)                               | ❌ Not recommended for soaking                     |
| Healing effect                              | Supports infection control                       | Can slow healing if overused                      |

👉 **Key insight:**

* For **smelly feet, sweating, or mild fungal issues**, 碘伏 is **more appropriate**.
* Hydrogen peroxide is mainly for **initial wound cleaning**, not routine soaking.

---

### 3. How to Use 碘伏 for Feet

**Typical method:**

* Add a small amount (capful-level) into warm water (diluted, light tea color)
* Soak feet for **10–15 minutes**
* Dry thoroughly (very important)

**Frequency:**

* Usually **1 time per day**
* Improvement often seen in **3–5 days**
* Mild cases: **3–7 sessions total**
* More persistent odor/fungal issues: up to **1–2 weeks**

---

### 4. Important Notes (Most People Miss These)

* **Moisture is the real enemy** → soaking alone is not enough
  → Always dry between toes completely
* Rotate shoes, avoid sealed footwear in summer
* Consider antifungal powder if recurring

---

### 5. When NOT to Use

Avoid frequent soaking if:

* skin is cracked/bleeding heavily
* iodine allergy
* thyroid issues (long-term excessive iodine exposure—not typical but worth noting)

---

### Bottom Line

* **碘伏 = povidone-iodine → better for odor + mild fungal issues**
* **Hydrogen peroxide → not suitable for repeated foot soaking**
* Usually **a few days (3–5 times)** already helps if the cause is mild
