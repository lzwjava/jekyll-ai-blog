Here are some genuinely unpopular (often controversial) opinions about modern dating that frequently trigger strong pushback when expressed openly:

- **Love is not enough to sustain a long-term relationship.** Shared values, timing, financial compatibility, and mutual life goals matter far more than intense feelings, which fade.

- **Monogamy is not the natural human default** — it’s a cultural and religious construct that works great for raising children but requires constant effort and compromise most people aren’t willing to give.
- **Ghosting is often the kindest option.** A polite rejection still leaves the other person hoping for closure or arguing why you’re wrong; disappearing spares everyone an awkward, painful conversation.
