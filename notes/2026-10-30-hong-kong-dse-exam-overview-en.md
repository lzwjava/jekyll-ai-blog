**Question:** What is the DSE exam in Hong Kong?

**Answer:**

The **DSE (Hong Kong Diploma of Secondary Education Examination, HKDSE)** is Hong Kong's main public exam for senior secondary students. Here's an overview:

**What it is**
The HKDSE is a public examination in Hong Kong, held since 2012, used to assess the academic level of Form 6 (Grade 12) students. It is internationally recognized, and candidates can use DSE results to apply for universities and post-secondary institutions both locally and overseas.

**Subjects**
DSE subjects are divided into 3 categories: Category A (senior secondary subjects), Category B (applied learning subjects), and Category C (other language subjects). Candidates can choose up to 8 subjects from these 3 categories.

The 4 **core subjects** are:

- Chinese Language
- English Language
- Mathematics
- Citizenship and Social Development (formerly Liberal Studies)

Plus elective subjects of the student's choice.

**University Admission**
Day school students can use their DSE results to apply through JUPAS for bachelor's degree programs at HKU, CUHK, HKUST, PolyU, CityU, HKBU, Lingnan, EdUHK, HKMU, and SFX.

**Scale**
In 2026, over 58,500 candidates registered for the exam, an increase of 5% year-on-year. Around 80% of exam-takers are day school students.

**Grading**
Results are graded on a scale from **Level 1 to Level 5**, with **5* and 5\*\*** being the top distinction levels. The minimum university admission requirement is generally "332A33" (Level 3 in Chinese, English, and Math, plus Attained in Citizenship and Social Development, and Level 3 in two elective subjects).

**2026 Highlights**
The 2026 HKDSE saw a record **24 top scorers**, with over one-third of secondary student candidates — around 16,000 — meeting the minimum university admission requirement.

**International Expansion**
Hong Kong's education authorities are also planning to establish an internationally benchmarked version of the DSE on a pilot basis, to allow foreign students to apply for admission to higher education institutions in Hong Kong and mainland China.

In short, the DSE is Hong Kong's equivalent of the A-Levels (UK) or Gaokao (mainland China) — the key gateway to university education.
