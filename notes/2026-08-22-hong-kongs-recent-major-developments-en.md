**Question:** What are the major news stories from Hong Kong in recent years?

**Answer:**

Here's a summary of the biggest developments in Hong Kong over the past few years:

---

**🏛️ Politics & Civil Liberties**

- Beijing and Hong Kong authorities have systematically degraded Hong Kong's political autonomy and civil rights through the 2020 National Security Law (NSL) and the 2024 Safeguarding National Security Ordinance (SNSO).
- In May 2025, the Hong Kong government enacted new subsidiary legislation under the SNSO to empower Beijing's Office of Safeguarding National Security (OSNS), requiring city employees to assist in investigations and establishing criminal penalties for disclosing information about OSNS activities.
- Hong Kong authorities have surveilled and prosecuted people who criticized the government, and have reportedly applied the NSL and SNSO extraterritorially in acts of transnational repression.

---

**🔥 Major Disasters**

- A devastating residential fire on November 26, 2024 killed at least 168 people. Authorities arrested five individuals who called for an independent investigation into the cause of the fire.

---

**📈 Economy & Finance**

- In 2025, Hong Kong reclaimed the top spot in global IPO market rankings for the first time since 2019, raising over HK$280 billion (~US$36 billion) — a surge of 224% year on year.
- Deloitte predicted that in 2026, approximately 160 new listings would raise over HK$300 billion in Hong Kong's stock market.

---

**🧳 Investment & Talent**

- A New Capital Investment Entrant Scheme launched in March 2024, requiring a minimum investment of HK$30 million (US$3.83 million) to qualify for residency.
- Hong Kong's fintech sector saw sustained expansion between 2022 and 2024, with over 1,200 fintech companies operating in the city by late 2025.
