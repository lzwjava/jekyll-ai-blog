**Question:** Should I disable auto-renewal on my Namecheap lzwjava.shop domain to avoid the $48/year renewal charge after the initial ~$10 intro price?

**Answer:**

Yes, turn it off immediately. You're correct — this is standard domain registrar practice: introductory rates (~$10) vs. full renewal rates ($48+). Auto-renewal will charge you the full price silently.

**How to disable on Namecheap:**

1. Log in to Namecheap dashboard
2. Go **Domain List** → find lzwjava.shop
3. Click **Manage**
4. Scroll to **Auto Renewal** section
5. Toggle **OFF**
6. Save

After that, the domain will expire on its renewal date. You'll get email reminders ~60 days before expiration, then you can decide:
- Renew elsewhere (cheaper registrars often have competitive renewal rates)
- Renew at Namecheap if prices change
- Let it expire and re-register later if needed

**Cheaper alternatives for renewal:**
- **Porkbun**: Often ~$9-12/year for .shop renewals
- **Cloudflare Registrar**: Cost-basis pricing (no markup)
- **Google Domains** / **Squarespace**: Competitive renewal pricing

Check renewal prices *before* your domain expires — some registrars discount renewals if you switch early.

Auto-renewal is a dark pattern across all registrars. Disabling it is the right move.
