Question: When did DeepSeek become globally famous?

Answer: Mid-to-late January 2025, triggered by R1.

Timeline:

- **Dec 2024** — V3 released quietly (671B MoE, ~37B active). Nerds noticed the $5.6M training cost claim, but no mainstream buzz yet.
- **Jan 10, 2025** — DeepSeek released the chatbot app, based on the R1 model, for iOS and Android.
- **Jan 20, 2025** — DeepSeek-R1 itself dropped — 671B open-weight reasoning model with a technical paper, matching OpenAI o1 on several benchmarks at a fraction of the API price.
- **Jan 27, 2025** — the actual "Sputnik moment": R1 surpassed ChatGPT as the most-downloaded free app on the US iOS App Store, causing an 18% drop in Nvidia's share price. Nvidia's share price plummeted 17% and lost nearly $600 billion in market cap — the largest one-day loss for a single company in US stock market history.
- Same day, a large-scale cyberattack hit DeepSeek's servers, forcing them to restrict new registrations to mainland China phone numbers, email, or Google login.
- **Late Jan–Feb 2025** — bans cascaded: Taiwan banned it from government agencies, Texas restricted it on state devices, and NASA/US Navy issued internal bans over data security concerns.

The narrative that actually drove the panic wasn't the model quality — it was the cost claim. Worth noting the figures got conflated in most reporting: the $5.6M number belongs to V3's Dec 2024 training run; R1's actual training cost was later disclosed as $294,000 in a September 2025 Reuters report. Since then: V3.1 (Aug 2025) merged thinking/non-thinking modes, and V4-Pro/V4-Flash preview shipped April 2026.
