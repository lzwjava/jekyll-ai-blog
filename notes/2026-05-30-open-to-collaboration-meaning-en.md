Question: What does “Collab” mean in the Hugging Face status options? Is it a website or something else?

Answer:

In the Hugging Face status options, “Collab” stands for **collaboration**. Selecting **“Open to Collab”** indicates to the community that you are open to collaborating on projects, research, or model development. It is **not a website or tool**—it’s simply a way to signal your willingness to work with others in the Hugging Face ecosystem.

This status badge will appear on your profile, making it easier for other users to know you are available for joint work, contributions, or partnerships on AI/ML projects.

So:

* **Open to Work** → looking for job opportunities
* **Open to Collab** → willing to collaborate on projects
* **Building on HF** → actively developing models or apps on Hugging Face
* **Hiring** → looking to recruit
* **In a Training Loop** → currently learning or training models
* **On Vacation** → temporarily unavailable
