**Question:** What are the Chinese alternatives to Google AdSense for website/publisher monetization?

**Answer:**

Since Google AdSense is blocked in China, the domestic ad ecosystem is dominated by the "BAT" giants — Baidu, Alibaba, and Tencent — each operating their own closed ad networks. Here are the main alternatives:

---

### 🔵 Baidu — Baidu Union (百度联盟)

Baidu's network reaches over 600,000 third-party websites and software providers. Its **Baiyi** platform lets publishers target desktop users based on interests, keywords, and device type. This is the most direct AdSense equivalent — contextual ads based on search intent, placed across Baidu's partner publisher network.

---

### 🟠 Alibaba — Alimama / TANX

Alibaba's advertising arm, **Alimama**, places a heavy focus on e-commerce. It also launched **TANX** (Taobao Ad Network and Exchange), which provides both SSP and DSP services for publishers and advertisers — most comparable to Google Display Network and DoubleClick respectively. Best suited for publishers with audiences that shop online.

---

### 🟢 Tencent Ads (腾讯广告)

**Tencent Ads** spans many platforms and is among China's most popular ad networks. It can place ads and gather data across WeChat, QQ, Tencent News, and Tencent Video, offering deep insights into customers' personalized demands. Great for publishers targeting social and mobile audiences.

---

### 🎵 ByteDance / Pangle (穿山甲)

**ByteDance** is among the newer entrants that have more recently entered the programmatic market in China. Their **Pangle** ad network (the publisher-facing arm of ByteDance) monetizes traffic via Douyin/TikTok's advertiser pool — strong for mobile and video-adjacent content.

---

### ⚠️ Key Considerations

Large publishers in China prefer building their own SSPs to sell traffic at the highest price, while **small publishers typically sell their ad inventory to the big ad networks like the BAT**.

Unlike North America, publishers in China build and rely on their own programmatic ad technology. The dominant players (BAT) operate as **closed silos independent of one another**, so ad campaigns cannot be centrally optimized across platforms.

Getting started with these platforms can be difficult for non-Chinese businesses — you'll want to speak with the platform directly or use a marketing consultant.
