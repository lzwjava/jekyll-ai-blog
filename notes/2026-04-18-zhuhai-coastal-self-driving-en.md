Question: Plan a 3-day self-driving trip in Zhuhai, Guangdong.

Answer:

Here’s a well-structured **3-day self-driving itinerary in Zhuhai (Guangdong, China)** that balances coastal scenery, islands, city highlights, and relaxed pacing.

---

# 🚗 Overview

* **Best for:** Relaxed coastal driving + light exploration
* **Driving style:** Short distances, scenic stops
* **Base:** Stay near Xiangzhou / Lovers’ Road for convenience

---

# 🗓️ Day 1 — City Highlights + Coastal Drive

### 🌅 Morning: Arrival & Check-in

* Stay near **Xiangzhou District**
* Easy access to main attractions + food

---

### 🌊 Midday: Lovers’ Road Scenic Drive

* Drive along **Lovers' Road**
* One of China’s most beautiful coastal roads
* Stop for photos, cafés, and walking

**Key stop:**

* **Zhuhai Fisher Girl Statue**

  * Iconic symbol of Zhuhai
  * Good photo spot

---

### 🏝️ Afternoon: Wild Beaver Island (Ye Li Island)

* Visit **Yeli Island**
* Features:

  * Opera house exterior (nice architecture)
  * Quiet seaside paths
  * Great sunset views

---

### 🌆 Evening: Huafa Mall Area

* Go to **Huafa Mall**
* Dinner + casual shopping
* Good introduction to Zhuhai lifestyle

---

# 🗓️ Day 2 — Islands & Nature (Best Day)

### 🌄 Morning: Drive to Xiangzhou Port

* Park your car and take ferry

---

### 🚤 Destination: Dong’ao Island (Recommended)

* **Dong'ao Island**

**Why:**

* Clear water (rare in Guangdong)
* Less crowded than popular islands
* Hiking + beaches

---

### 🏖️ Activities:

* Swimming / beach relax
* Light hiking (viewpoints)
* Seafood lunch

---

### 🌇 Evening:

* Stay overnight on island **OR**
* Return to city and relax

---

# 🗓️ Day 3 — Hengqin + Macau View

### 🌉 Morning: Hengqin New Area Drive

* Drive to **Hengqin New Area**
* Modern district next to Macau

---

### 🎢 Option A: Theme Park

* Visit **Chimelong Ocean Kingdom**

  * One of the largest ocean parks in the world
  * Good if traveling with family

---

### 🌿 Option B: Relaxed Exploration

* Coastal parks + cafés
* Scenic Macau skyline views

---

### 🌆 Afternoon: Macau Border View

* Drive near **Gongbei Port**
* See Macau skyline without crossing border

---

### 🛍️ Optional:

* Underground shopping mall near Gongbei

---

# 🚗 Driving Tips (Important)

### ✔️ Road Conditions

* Zhuhai roads are:

  * Clean
  * Low congestion (compared to Guangzhou/Shenzhen)
* Perfect for relaxed driving

---

### ✔️ Parking

* Easy near malls and attractions
* Islands → must park at ferry terminal

---

### ✔️ Navigation

* Use:

  * Amap (高德地图)
  * Baidu Maps

---

# 🍜 Food Recommendations

Try local Cantonese seafood:

* Steamed fish
* Garlic scallops
* Shrimp dishes

Areas:

* Xiangzhou seafood streets
* Hengqin modern dining

# ⚖️ Strategy Insight (Important)

Zhuhai is NOT about:

* Intense sightseeing
* Packed schedules

It’s about:

* Slow coastal lifestyle
* Clean environment
* Relaxation

👉 So don’t overpack your itinerary.
Driving slowly along the coast is actually the highlight.
