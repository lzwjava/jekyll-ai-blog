## Question: Intro to 九安医疗 (Andon Health) and how it invested in DeepSeek and Kimi

## Answer

九安医疗 is a Chinese medical device company famous for its **iHealth brand**. It originally focused on consumer healthcare hardware:

* Electronic blood pressure monitors
* Blood glucose meters
* Home diagnostic devices
* Digital health products

The company became extremely profitable during COVID because its iHealth COVID-19 antigen self-test kits sold massively overseas. In 2022, revenue reached about RMB 26.3 billion and net profit about RMB 16 billion, creating a large cash reserve. ([Jiemian News][1])

After accumulating cash, the company changed from a pure medical-device company into a **technology investment holding company**, looking for "next decade" opportunities in:

* Artificial intelligence
* Large language models
* Robotics
* Semiconductors
* Biotech

Its own description says it has invested in areas including AI, biotechnology, advanced manufacturing and other frontier technologies. ([iHealth Labs][2])

## 1. Investment in Kimi (Moonshot AI)

Kimi is developed by 月之暗面.

九安 invested through offshore and fund structures:

### First investment

August 2023:

* 九安香港 (Andon Hong Kong) invested about **US$10 million** into Moonshot-related entities. ([CNInfo][3])

### Second investment

March 2024:

* Through Tianjin Jiushang No.1 investment vehicle
* Invested about **US$20 million** more. ([CNInfo][3])

Total:

```
九安 → Moonshot AI

≈ $30 million investment
```

The company later clarified that its ownership percentage was small and it did not control Moonshot. ([Sina Finance][4])

The interesting part is timing:

```
Aug 2023
    |
    | 九安 invests
    ↓
Mar 2024
    |
    | Kimi long-context AI becomes famous
    ↓
Market discovers 九安 = Kimi investor
```

This was a classic venture-style asymmetric bet:

* Downside: $30M
* Upside: exposure to a future AI giant

## 2. Investment in DeepSeek

DeepSeek is developed by 杭州深度求索人工智能基础技术研究有限公司.

According to disclosed information:

* 九安香港 invested through 天津拾象投资合伙企业
* Investment amount: about **RMB 750 million**
* The investment was completed.
* After looking through the structure, 九安 owns about **0.21%** of DeepSeek. ([Sina Finance][5])

The calculation:

```
DeepSeek valuation ≈ hundreds of billions RMB

九安 stake:
0.21%

750M RMB investment
        ↓
paper value potentially much higher
```

But 九安 itself emphasized:

* It does not participate in DeepSeek management.
* It cannot control operations.
* Investment risk remains. ([Sina Finance][5])

## 3. Why did a medical company invest in AI?

This is actually the interesting strategic question.

The logic is:

### Medical data + AI = huge future market

A company like 九安 already has:

```
Medical devices
      +
Consumer health users
      +
Health data
      +
AI models
```

Potential future products:

```
Smart blood pressure monitor
          |
          v
AI health assistant
          |
          v
Personal medical agent
```

Similar to Apple's health ecosystem:

```
Device → Data → Intelligence → Service
```

Large language models make this much more valuable.

## My view

九安医疗 is an interesting example of a Chinese public company transforming itself:

```
Medical device company
        |
        v
COVID cash machine
        |
        v
Technology investment platform
        |
        v
AI ecosystem investor
```

The DeepSeek + Kimi combination is especially notable because they picked two of China's strongest reasoning-model companies.

However, the financial impact should not be exaggerated:

* Equity stakes are small.
* Valuation gains are paper gains until exits happen.
* AI startups have enormous execution risk.

The strategic lesson is more interesting:

**A company with a strong cash-generating business can buy "future technology options" before the market realizes their value.**
