Mental fatigue after 8 hours of intensive learning is completely normal - your brain has been working hard! Here's what I'd suggest:

**For the immediate fatigue:**
A short nap (20-30 minutes) can be really helpful if you're struggling. This won't disrupt your nighttime sleep routine and can give you a cognitive refresh. Just keep it brief - longer naps might make you groggy or interfere with your regular sleep.

**For those remaining 3-4 hours:**
Instead of pushing through more intense study, switch to lighter activities that still feel productive:
- Review material passively (watch related videos, listen to podcasts on the topic)
- Organize your notes from the day
- Do something physical - exercise actually helps clear mental fog
- Engage in a hobby or social activity to give your brain a real break
- Light reading on different topics (not the same material)

**The bigger picture:**
If this is happening regularly, consider restructuring your study schedule. Your brain absorbs information better with breaks built in - maybe 90-minute study blocks with 15-minute breaks, or using techniques like the Pomodoro method. Also, varying the type of learning throughout the day (reading, practice problems, discussion, etc.) helps prevent this kind of burnout.
