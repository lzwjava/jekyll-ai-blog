**Question:** What are the hot topics right now in the AI / LLM field?

**Answer:**

Here's a breakdown of the most actively discussed topics in the AI/LLM space as of September 2026:

---

**1. Inference-Time / Test-Time Compute Scaling**

One of the biggest shifts is allocating more compute *during inference* — letting the AI "think harder" on tough problems. Instead of one quick pass, models take multiple reasoning steps before delivering an answer. Rather than just making models bigger, the focus is now on making them *think more* when needed. This reframes the core scientific question of what "reasoning" even means in neural systems.

---

**2. Post-Training & RLVR (Reinforcement Learning from Verifiable Rewards)**

Most LLM R&D focus has shifted from pre-training to post-training optimization, particularly reasoning capabilities, since pre-training is already sophisticated but post-training still has low-hanging fruit. RLVR emerged as a defining breakthrough, and reasoning models have become the new standard.

---

**3. Agentic AI & Multi-Agent Systems**

Researchers are exploring agentic workflows, including where multi-agent systems add real value and where reliability constraints still dominate system design. Topics like agentic AI architectures, context engineering, and real-world deployment challenges are now core conference themes.

---

**4. Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) Architectures**

Mixture-of-experts architectures are a key innovation, balancing cost and performance — allowing massive total parameter counts while only activating a fraction per token, keeping inference efficient.

---

**5. Open Source vs. Closed Models**

The democratization of LLM technologies is a major trend — what was once the exclusive domain of tech giants is now becoming more accessible to smaller organizations and individual developers through open-source models and affordable cloud computing.

---

**6. Long Context & Memory**

Models now routinely support context windows of 200K–1M tokens, enabling complex multi-step reasoning, agent workflows, and long-document processing.

---

**7. Physical AI & Robotics**

A second major frontier is Physical AI — world models, robotics foundation models, simulation, humanoids, and autonomous labs — representing the push to move AI from digital reasoning into the physical world.

---

**8. China vs. US AI Competition**

The geopolitics of the AI race, particularly the US vs. China dynamic, is a major ongoing discussion — covering model competitiveness, compute access, and long-term implications for the industry.
