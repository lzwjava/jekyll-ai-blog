**Question:** What is the bar at the bottom (or left) of the macOS screen called?

**Answer:**

It's called the **Dock**.

That's it — officially "the Dock" in Apple's terminology. It sits at the bottom by default, but you can move it to the left or right side via **System Settings → Desktop & Dock → Position on screen**.
