Question: Does DMIT.io allow changing the IP address for a server in the Los Angeles region?

Answer:

Yes, **DMIT.io does allow IP address changes** for servers, including the Los Angeles (LAX) region, but with specific rules and conditions depending on your plan type.

---

## ✅ Free IP Replacement

You can request a **free** IP replacement if all of the following conditions are met:

- The instance must have been purchased for **at least 7 days** (eligible from the 8th day onward).
- At least **15 days** must have passed since the last IP change request (including paid requests).
- **ICMP and all TCP ports are blocked** on the current IP.
- The service must have a **remaining validity of at least 7 days**.

---

## 💰 Paid IP Replacement ($5 USD)

You can request a **paid** IP replacement ($5 per change) if:

- The instance has been purchased for **at least 24 hours** (eligible from the next day).
- At least **24 hours** have passed since the last IP change request.
- The service must have a **remaining validity of at least 3 days**.

---

## ⚠️ Important Restrictions & Notes

- IP addresses are **randomly assigned** by the system — you **cannot** specify a particular IP range or a specific IP address.
- The assigned IP address **does not guarantee** any geographic information for a specific region or the availability of particular websites/services.
- **IP address changes cannot be reversed or refunded** once the invoice has been paid and the IP change has been completed.
- Abuse of this feature may result in being **banned** from using the IP change function.

---

## 🚫 LAX.sPro Series Exception

The IP address of **LAX.sPro series** products is **not allowed** to be replaced under normal circumstances. However, you can ask for a replacement within **24 hours** if the IP allocated to you is unreachable in any country or region.

Also, the **Lite profile** is not included in the free IP replacement policy, and there is no guarantee that the IP has connectivity with all countries or regions, even at first allocation.
