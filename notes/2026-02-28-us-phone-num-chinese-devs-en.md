Question: Why does a Chinese citizen who is a software engineer need a USA phone number?

  Some AWS global features, GitHub advanced security tools, certain Vercel/Cloudflare enterprise plans, or third-party SaaS products tied to US identity occasionally require or strongly prefer US phone verification.

- **Two-factor authentication (2FA) & account recovery**

- **Freelance & remote job platforms**  
  Upwork, Toptal, and some US-based remote opportunities ask for phone verification and may limit visibility or payment options for Chinese-registered accounts.

- **Crypto & Web3 services**
