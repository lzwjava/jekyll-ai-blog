Question: What is the connotation of the "Great Founding Spirit of the Party" as elaborated by Xi Jinping in his speech at the Celebration of the 100th Anniversary of the Founding of the Communist Party of China?

Answer: 

The **Great Founding Spirit** (伟大建党精神) was explicitly defined by Xi Jinping in his important speech at the ceremony celebrating the centenary of the Communist Party of China on July 1, 2021.

Its core内涵 (connotation) consists of **four closely linked aspects**:

- **Uphold truth and adhere to ideals**  
  (坚持真理、坚守理想)  
  This reflects the Party’s commitment to Marxist scientific truth and its unwavering pursuit of communist ideals and socialist beliefs.

- **Practice the original aspiration and undertake the mission**  
  (践行初心、担当使命)  
  This emphasizes staying true to the Party’s founding purpose of seeking happiness for the Chinese people and rejuvenation for the Chinese nation, and courageously shouldering historic responsibilities.

- **Fear no sacrifice and bravely struggle**  
  (不怕牺牲、英勇斗争)  
  This highlights the revolutionary spirit of heroism, readiness to make sacrifices, and tenacious fighting capacity that runs through the Party’s hundred-year history.

- **Remain loyal to the Party and never betray the people**  
  (对党忠诚、不负人民)  
  This underscores absolute loyalty to the Party organization and deep-rooted commitment to serving the people wholeheartedly without fail.

These 32 Chinese characters constitute the most authoritative and concise expression of the **Great Founding Spirit** as put forward by Xi Jinping. It is regarded as the spiritual source of the Communist Party of China and the spiritual hallmark that distinguishes it from other political parties.
