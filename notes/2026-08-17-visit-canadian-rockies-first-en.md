If you've never been to Canada, **go to Banff**.

The key places:

* **Lake Louise**
* **Moraine Lake**
* Lake Minnewanka
* Banff town
* Sulphur Mountain
* Johnston Canyon
* Icefields Parkway

**Moraine Lake** is probably the single place I'd put at the top of your list.

The turquoise water + Ten Peaks looks almost unreal. Parks Canada currently says Moraine Lake is accessible only through shuttles, licensed commercial operators, or certain accommodations; personal vehicles aren't permitted. ([Parks Canada][1])

If you're going **right now in August 2026**, this is actually a very good season: Lake Louise and Moraine Lake are open, although they're extremely busy. ([Parks Canada][2])
