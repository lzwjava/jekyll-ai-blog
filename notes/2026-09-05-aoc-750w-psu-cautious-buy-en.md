### What I would check before buying

Ask the seller for a **photo of the PSU nameplate**. Specifically check:

```text
Model:
+12V:       ___ A
+12V power: ___ W
80 PLUS:    ?
PCIe:       6+2 pin × ?
CPU:        4+4 / 8 pin?
```

The **+12V rating is the most important number** for your application.

For example, if it says:

```text
+12V  62.5A
+12V  750W
```

that's substantially more convincing than:

```text
+12V  45A
+12V  540W
MAX 750W
```
