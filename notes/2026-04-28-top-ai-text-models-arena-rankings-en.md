### ⚠️ Important insight (many people miss this)

* Arena rankings are **dynamic and noisy**
* Small Elo gaps (5–20 points) are **not meaningful differences**
* Models are often **A/B tested anonymously**, so:

  * Prompt style can change results
  * Updates can reshuffle rankings quickly
