**Question:** What were the major events that happened in 2025?

**Answer:**

2025 was a highly turbulent year marked by geopolitical shifts, ongoing wars, major U.S. policy changes under a second Trump administration, natural disasters, religious milestones, and cultural moments. Here's a summary of some of the most significant events:

### Politics and U.S. Developments
- **Donald Trump's second inauguration** (January 20): Trump returned to the presidency for a nonconsecutive second term, implementing aggressive policies including large-scale tariffs on imports (starting with "Liberation Day" in April), withdrawal from the Paris Agreement and WHO, attempts to downsize federal agencies (via DOGE initiatives), mass immigration crackdowns, and a new National Security Strategy. These moves disrupted global trade, reinvigorated the U.S.-China trade war, and were described by some analysts as ending the era of Pax Americana.
- Widespread government shutdown (one of the longest in U.S. history) and domestic tensions, including National Guard deployments in cities and incidents like lawmakers being shot.

### Armed Conflicts and International Crises
- **Gaza/Israel-Hamas War**: Escalated with famine and humanitarian crisis; saw a temporary Biden-era ceasefire early in the year, followed by a fragile, U.S.-brokered ceasefire in October under Trump (involving hostage/prisoner exchanges and plans for demilitarization/international stabilization force). Fighting resumed sporadically, with no full lasting peace.
- **Russia-Ukraine War**: Entered its fourth year with continued fighting; Trump engaged in peace negotiations with Putin, though no full resolution was achieved.
- **Sudanese Civil War** and other conflicts persisted or escalated (e.g., U.S. strikes on Iranian and Venezuelan targets).
- Gen Z-led protests in multiple countries (Nepal, Madagascar, Philippines, Peru, Morocco) resulted in some government overthrows or major changes.

### Disasters and Attacks
- Major wildfires in Southern California (Los Angeles area, including Palisades and Eaton fires) caused massive destruction ($76–131 billion in damages) and dozens of deaths.
- New Orleans truck attack on New Year's Day: A vehicle-ramming incident killed 14 people and injured dozens.
- Other incidents included mid-air collisions, boat strikes, and a Louvre crown jewels heist.

### Religion and Historic Firsts
- **Death of Pope Francis** (April) and election of **Pope Leo XIV** (Cardinal Robert Prevost from Chicago), the first American-born pope.
- First woman appointed Archbishop of Canterbury (Sarah Mullally).
- First women heads of government in Japan (Sanae Takaichi) and Namibia (Netumbo Nandi-Ndaitwah).

### Other Notable Events
- Commercial space milestone: First successful soft landing of a private lunar lander (Firefly Aerospace’s Blue Ghost).
- Cultural/pop moments: Taylor Swift engaged to Travis Kelce; Beyoncé won Grammy for Album of the Year (*Cowboy Carter*); first videos of colossal squid in natural habitat.
- Technology: Release of Chinese AI chatbot DeepSeek, impacting markets (e.g., massive Nvidia value drop); continued AI arms race.
- Assassination of conservative activist Charlie Kirk.

Overall, 2025 was characterized by instability, with heightened conflicts, major leadership transitions, economic disruptions from tariffs, and few unifying positive global moments.
