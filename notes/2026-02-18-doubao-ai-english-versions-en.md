Question: Does Doubao AI by ByteDance have an English version?

Answer:

Yes, Doubao AI by ByteDance does have English support, but with important distinctions between its domestic and international versions:

## Domestic Version (China) - "Doubao" (豆包)

- The main Doubao app available in China defaults to Chinese as its primary interface language [[1]]
- It may support limited English prompts for input, but the user interface and most features are optimized for Chinese users [[1]]
- Available via doubao.com and Chinese app stores [[9]]

## International Version - "Cici" (formerly) / "Dola"

- ByteDance launched an international version of Doubao called **Cici** in August 2023, which was later rebranded as **Dola** [[3]]
- This international version supports **18 languages**, including English, Russian, Arabic, French, German, Japanese, and Korean [[5]]
- It is accessible globally through international app stores, separate from the China-only Doubao app [[5]]

## Key Takeaways

| Feature | Doubao (China) | Cici/Dola (International) |
| --------- | --------------- | --------------------------- |
| Primary Language | Chinese | Multiple (English supported) |
| Interface Language | Chinese-dominant | English and 17 other languages |
| Availability | Mainland China | Global markets |
| Account Login | Phone/ByteDance account | International login options |

If you are looking to use Doubao AI in English, your best option is to download the international version (**Cici/Dola**) from your device's app store outside of China. The domestic Doubao app can understand some English input but is primarily designed for Chinese-language interaction.
