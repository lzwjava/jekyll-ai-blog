**Question:** What are the "Eleven Persists" (十一个坚持) in Xi Jinping Thought?

**Answer:**

The **十一个坚持** (Eleven Persists / Eleven Adherences) specifically refers to the eleven core principles of **Xi Jinping Thought on the Rule of Law** (习近平法治思想), not the broader Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism. They were first formally articulated at the Central Work Conference on Comprehensive Rule of Law on November 16–17, 2020, and systematically presented as the "Eleven Persists" (十一个坚持) to expound Xi Jinping's legal/rule-of-law thought.

The eleven principles are:

1. **坚持党对全面依法治国的领导** — Uphold the Party's leadership over comprehensively governing the country according to law.
2. **坚持以人民为中心** — Put the people at the center.
3. **坚持中国特色社会主义法治道路** — Adhere to the socialist rule-of-law path with Chinese characteristics.
4. **坚持依宪治国、依宪执政** — Govern the country and exercise power in accordance with the Constitution.
5. **坚持在法治轨道上推进国家治理体系和治理能力现代化** — Advance the modernization of the national governance system and capacity on the track of rule of law.
6. **坚持建设中国特色社会主义法治体系** — Build the socialist rule-of-law system with Chinese characteristics.
7. **坚持依法治国、依法执政、依法行政共同推进，法治国家、法治政府、法治社会一体建设** — Jointly advance rule of law in governance, administration, and Party leadership; build a law-based country, government, and society as an integrated whole.
8. **坚持全面推进科学立法、严格执法、公正司法、全民守法** — Comprehensively promote scientific legislation, strict law enforcement, fair justice, and law-abiding by all citizens.
9. **坚持统筹推进国内法治和涉外法治** — Coordinate domestic rule of law with foreign-related rule of law.
10. **坚持建设德才兼备的高素质法治工作队伍** — Build a high-quality rule-of-law workforce with both virtue and competence.
11. **坚持抓住领导干部这个"关键少数"** — Focus on leading cadres as the "key minority."

> **Note:** The "十一个坚持" is sometimes confused with other numbered frameworks in Xi Jinping Thought, such as the **14 Principles** (十四条基本方略) of Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics, or the **Six Musts** (六个必须坚持) from the 20th Party Congress in 2022. The eleven-point list is specific to the **rule-of-law domain**.
