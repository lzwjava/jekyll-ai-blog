**Question:** What is 构建新发展格局 (Building a New Development Pattern) as it appears in the course?

---

**Answer:**

> **Note:** The correct term is **新发展格局** (not 结局 — 结局 means "ending/outcome", 格局 means "pattern/structure"). Easy to mix up in pinyin!

---

### What Is 新发展格局?

The official definition from the textbook is:

> 以**国内大循环**为主体，**国内国际双循环**相互促进的新发展格局

In English:
> A new development pattern where **domestic circulation** is the mainstay, and **domestic and international dual circulation** mutually reinforce each other.

---

### The Core Concept — 双循环 (Dual Circulation)

Think of it as **two loops** working together:

```
        国内大循环 (Main Loop)
    ┌─────────────────────────┐
    │                         │
 生产 →→ 分配 →→ 流通 →→ 消费  │
 (Production→Distribution     │
  →Circulation→Consumption)   │
    └─────────────────────────┘
              ↕ 相互促进
        国际循环 (Secondary Loop)
    ┌─────────────────────────┐
    │   进口 ←→ 出口           │
    │  (Imports ↔ Exports)    │
    └─────────────────────────┘
```

### Breaking It Down — 3 Key Points

---

**① 为什么提出? — Why was it proposed?**

The textbook gives these reasons:

| Reason | Explanation |
| -------- | ------------- |
| 外部环境变化 | Global environment became hostile — US-China trade war, deglobalization |
| 国内市场潜力 | China's huge domestic market is underutilized |
| 发展阶段变化 | China moved past the "cheap export" growth model |
| 安全需要 | Reduce dependence on foreign technology and supply chains |

> In short: **the old model of "export everything" no longer works** — China needs to rely more on its own internal economy.

---

**② 核心内涵 — What does it actually mean?**

**国内大循环 = Main Engine**

- Stimulate **domestic consumption** (内需)
- Strengthen domestic **supply chains**
- Develop **domestic technology** (科技自立自强)
- Key idea: China's 1.4 billion people are themselves a massive market

**国际循环 = Supporting Role**

- Still engage with the world — not isolation
- Use international resources and markets to **supplement** domestic circulation
- Continue opening up — but on China's own terms

---

**③ 关键支撑 — What supports the new pattern?**

The textbook identifies these pillars:

| Pillar | Chinese | Role |
| -------- | --------- | ------ |
| Technology self-reliance | 科技自立自强 | Remove foreign tech dependency |
| Supply chain security | 产业链安全 | Don't let others cut off critical supplies |
| Domestic consumption | 扩大内需 | Drive growth from within |
| High-level opening up | 高水平对外开放 | Still connect with the world smartly |

---

### 🏠 Simple Analogy

> Think of a **household economy**:
>
> **Old model** = family earns money entirely by doing work for neighbors (exports), spends little at home
>
> **新发展格局** = family builds a strong internal economy first — grows own food, makes own goods, trades with neighbors only when mutually beneficial
>
> The family doesn't isolate itself — it just stops being **dependent** on neighbors

### ⚠️ Most Common Misunderstanding

| Wrong idea | Correct understanding |
| ----------- | ---------------------- |
| 新发展格局 = closing China off from the world | ❌ Wrong — international circulation still exists |
| 国内循环 = ignoring exports | ❌ Wrong — exports still matter, just not the **primary** driver |
| It's a response to one specific problem | ❌ Wrong — it's a **long-term structural shift**, not a short-term fix |

> The textbook is very clear: 新发展格局 ≠ 闭关锁国 (isolation/closed-door policy)

### 📝 Exam Tips

1. **Must memorize** the official definition word for word:
   > 以**国内大循环**为主体，**国内国际双循环**相互促进

2. **Common fill-in-the-blank** — examiners blank out 主体 or 双循环 or 相互促进

3. **Common essay question**: *"Why does China need to build 新发展格局? Does it mean China is closing itself off?"*
   - Always answer NO to the second part — use the phrase **高水平对外开放** to show China remains open

4. **Connect to current events** — mention 科技自立自强 and supply chain security as real-world motivations (chip shortage, trade war context)
