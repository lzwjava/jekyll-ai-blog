**Question:** Is LinkedIn Premium Careers (linkedin.com/premium/careers) no longer available to Chinese citizens?

**Answer:**

Yes, that's essentially correct — and the situation has been building for years. Here's the full picture:

**LinkedIn's withdrawal from China (timeline):**

- In 2021, LinkedIn shut down its flagship networking service in China, citing compliance issues and a "significantly more challenging operating environment."
- Instead of fully leaving, LinkedIn replaced its Chinese site with a stripped-back job-board app called "InCareer," on which Chinese users could not share posts or news articles.
- LinkedIn then discontinued InCareer by August 9, 2023, and as of that date, no longer supported local LinkedIn Career Pages for Chinese Mainland-based customers.

**Current situation (2026):**

- As of 2026, LinkedIn has no dedicated Chinese product. The international LinkedIn website is reachable but degraded, and the domestic Chinese professional networking landscape is dominated by local players.
- LinkedIn is effectively blocked in China. For job searching, BOSS直聘 is recommended; for networking with Chinese professionals, WeChat is the primary tool everyone uses.

So **LinkedIn Premium Careers** — which is a global product — is technically not "banned for Chinese citizens" worldwide, but it is inaccessible from mainland China without a VPN, and LinkedIn no longer offers any localized career or premium product for the Chinese market.
