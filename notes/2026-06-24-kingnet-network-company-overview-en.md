**Question:** What is 恺英网络 (Kingnet Network) — company introduction?

**Answer:**

**恺英网络 (Kingnet Network Co., Ltd.)** — stock ticker **SZ:002517** — is a publicly listed Chinese internet gaming company headquartered in Shanghai.

**Basics:**

- Founded in 2008, headquartered in Shanghai's Minhang District. Team of ~1,000+ employees.
- Listed on the Shenzhen A-share market (SZ.002517), it's one of China's well-known publicly traded internet gaming companies.

**Core Business:**

Primary business covers R&D, operation, and distribution of mobile games and browser/web games (手游 + 页游); operation of web game platforms and mobile app distribution platforms; and active expansion into overseas markets.

**Strategy:**

The company maintains "focusing on the gaming core business" as its central strategy, supported by three pillars: R&D, publishing/distribution, and investment+IP. They've also made long-term bets on VR and metaverse, plus global publishing expansion.

**Key Products/IPs:**

Their game portfolio spans *That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime: New World* (IP-licensed strategy), *Mecha Party* (VR multiplayer on PlayStation and Steam), *Chinese Paladin: A New Beginning*, *Mobile Suit Gundam* series, *Legend of Sword and Fairy*, and many more.

Other notable titles include *全民奇迹* (MU Origin), *蓝月传奇* (Blue Moon Legend), *王者传奇*, and *新倚天屠龙记*. Their XY.COM platform aggregates mobile, H5, and web games with over 100 titles launched cumulatively.

**Platform play:**

Beyond games, they've built a closed-loop internet ecosystem — **XY游戏** (web game platform), **XY苹果助手** (iOS app distribution), and mobile gaming platform **啪啪游戏厅** — forming a "traffic acquisition → traffic operation → traffic monetization" flywheel.

**TL;DR for you:** Mid-tier Chinese gaming company (~5,000–10,000 employees), publicly listed since 2016, known for MMORPG-style mobile games with strong IP licensing, some VR/metaverse experimentation, and a platform+content strategy. Not a tech frontier company — squarely in the gaming/entertainment sector. If you're evaluating them as a potential employer or partner, their AI investment is not a public focus area.
