## Question: What did Duan Yongping say about finding what you love and not complaining about your environment?

The quote you're remembering is very close to his actual words. In a 2025 interview with Tian Pujun, Duan Yongping said:

> “努力去找自己喜欢的事情，实在不行就努力喜欢自己正在做的事儿，你也能找到乐趣。”

English translation:

> “Make an effort to find the things you love doing. If that doesn't work, make an effort to love what you're already doing. You can find joy in it, too.”

He connected this to another point about complaining:

> “我觉得好多人喜欢抱怨，环境不好，错都是别人的，赚到都是自己的，这个至少对大家没有什么帮助。”

In English:

> “I think many people like to complain that their environment is bad, blame others when things go wrong, and take credit for their own successes. At the very least, that doesn't help anyone.”

## Three related ideas worth remembering

1. Find what you love. Don't blindly follow conventional definitions of success. Explore different kinds of work until you find something you genuinely enjoy.
2. Learn to love what you do. You don't necessarily discover your passion before starting. You may discover it through deep involvement, practice, and the satisfaction of getting better.
3. Stop blaming; take responsibility. You cannot control every external condition, but you can choose what to learn, what to build, whom to work with, and when to change direction.

Duan also expressed a related idea in his 2016 speech at Zhejiang University: devote substantial effort to finding work you love, while recognizing that you may develop a love for your work only after investing yourself in it. [Source: Duan Yongping's 2016 Zhejiang University interview](https://www.ayaseeri.com/duanyongping-2016nianzheda60zhounianzhuanfang?utm_source=chatgpt.com) 【turn400310search3】

My interpretation: This isn't a command to tolerate every bad situation or pretend you enjoy something you hate. It's a call to actively search for the right work, engage deeply with what you're doing, and avoid wasting your energy on unproductive complaints. If you discover that something is fundamentally wrong for you, change direction rather than complain indefinitely.
