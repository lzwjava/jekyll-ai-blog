**Question:** Who is Huang Hongsheng (黄宏生), the founder of Skyworth Group (创维集团)?

---

**Answer:**

**Huang Hongsheng (黄宏生)** is a Chinese entrepreneur best known as the founder of Skyworth Group (创维集团), one of China's largest consumer electronics conglomerates.

**Background & Early Life**

Huang Hongsheng was born in 1956 in Lingao, Hainan Province, and attended South China University. His family was poor, and Hainan at the time lacked the tourism development it has today — meaning limited money, work, education, and sometimes even food.

**Career Before Skyworth**

His 1980 graduation thesis was titled "The Design of Black & White TVs," foreshadowing his future career. He then worked as an engineer at the Electronics Import & Export Company of China South Group in Guangzhou for eight years, saving up 30,000 yuan.

**Founding of Skyworth**

In 1987, at age 28, Huang resigned from his comfortable position as executive vice general manager of South China Electronics Import & Export Company to start his own business. He moved to Hong Kong and set up Skyworth Industrial, trading in electronic parts — but the company failed within a year. Undaunted, he moved to the booming city of Shenzhen, re-established Skyworth, took out a loan, gathered college classmates and his brother Huang Peisheng, and the team began developing remote controls for TVs — their first major success, as many Chinese TV manufacturers adopted it.

**Growth of Skyworth**

By 1994, Skyworth was producing 400,000 color TVs annually with sales of 550 million yuan. By 2000, Skyworth reached 7 billion yuan in sales, ranking among China's top three TV makers, and was listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange.

**Legal Troubles & Imprisonment**

Over the years, the outspoken founder was imprisoned for three years for misappropriating funds, and upon his release in 2009, he founded an electric vehicle (EV) company.

**New Ventures in EVs**

At age 65, Huang became the oldest entrepreneur to enter China's electric car business. In 2021, he launched the 创维汽车 (Skyworth Automobile) brand under his 开沃新能源汽车集团 (Kawo New Energy Vehicle Group), describing his decade-long journey in new energy commercial vehicles as "forging a sword for ten years" — building the ecosystem from commercial vehicles to passenger cars to components and charging infrastructure.

**Succession**

Huang stepped away from Skyworth long ago, handing the reins to his son Lin Jin (林劲), who became chairman in 2022.
