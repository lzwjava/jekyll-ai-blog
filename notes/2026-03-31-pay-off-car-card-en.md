**Question:** Can I pay off my 广发车主卡 (GF Bank Car Owner Credit Card) debt in full at once, instead of monthly? I currently have about 27,000 CNY remaining.

**Answer:**

## Yes — You Can Pay Off Your 广发车主卡 Installment Debt Early in Full

**Early full repayment (提前全额还清) is supported** by 广发银行 (Guangfa Bank) credit cards, including the 车主卡. However, there are important rules and fees to be aware of before you do it.

---

### ✅ How to Apply for Early Full Repayment

You can apply through the following channels:
1. **Call customer service** at **95508** or **400-830-8003**
2. **Online banking or Mobile App** — Log in, go to the installment payment section, select your installment order, and tap "Early Repayment" (提前还款申请). The system will automatically calculate the total amount due including remaining principal and any applicable fees.

After submitting the application in the app, confirm it is successful — then log in again to verify the installment has been fully cleared. The credit limit will be restored after settlement.

---

### ⚠️ Key Fee Rules You Must Know

This is the most critical part — **early repayment does NOT mean you skip the installment fees**:

When you apply for early full repayment of an installment plan, you must pay all remaining unpaid amounts in a single payment — this includes the remaining principal **and** any unpaid installment fees and penalties. Fees already paid are non-refundable.

Even if you repay early, you are still required to pay the service fees for all the periods you originally signed up for. For example, if you split 1,000 CNY into 12 installments and repay in month 2, you still owe the handling fees for all 12 months.

However, 广发银行 has historically been slightly more favorable compared to other banks — some older policies indicate that upon early repayment, 广发 only charges one additional period's handling fee instead of requiring all remaining periods' fees. That said, this depends on your specific agreement.

---

### 💰 Estimated Fee Calculation for Your 27,000 CNY Debt

广发银行's standard installment fee rates are:
- **6-month or 12-month plans**: 0.65% per month
- **18-month or 24-month plans**: 0.70% per month

If you have been paying monthly fees (分摊方式), when you repay all remaining principal early, you will also need to pay **3% of the remaining principal** as the residual handling fee in one shot.

For 27,000 CNY at 3% = approximately **810 CNY** in additional fees on top of the principal, but confirm the exact figure via the app or customer service before proceeding.

---

### 📋 Recommended Steps

1. **Check your installment details** in the 发现精彩 App → 分期付款 → view remaining principal, fee rate, and remaining periods.
2. **Call 95508** to confirm the exact total payoff amount for your specific installment contract.
3. **Submit the early repayment application** via app or phone.
4. Transfer the full required amount to your credit card account.
5. **Confirm settlement** by logging in again to verify the balance is zero.

---

### ✅ Benefits of Paying Off Early

- Credit limit is restored immediately after payoff
- Eliminates ongoing monthly payment obligations
- Can have a positive effect on your credit record

> **Note:** Always confirm the exact total with 广发 customer service before transferring money, as fee structures may vary based on your specific installment agreement date and product type.
