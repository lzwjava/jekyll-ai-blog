**Question:** Could a salary payment (代发工资) record help with Jin Kui Hua (金葵花) at China Merchants Bank?

**Answer:**

Yes, a **代发工资** (payroll/salary payment) record can help significantly with **金葵花** (Jin Kui Hua/Golden Sunflower) services at China Merchants Bank (招商银行), primarily in two main ways based on common practices and user reports as of recent years (including into 2025-2026):

1. **Exemption from account management fees**:
   The Jin Kui Hua card (a premium debit card for high-net-worth clients) normally charges a monthly management fee of 150 RMB if the monthly average assets fall below 500,000 RMB for a continuous period (typically after an initial 6-month grace period).
   However, if the card is set up as a **代发工资** account (i.e., your salary is deposited via official payroll from your employer's corporate account through CMB's system), the management fee is often fully exempted—even if your assets do not reach the 500,000 RMB threshold.
   - In many cases, one payroll deposit can exempt fees for 6 months; doing it twice a year keeps it exempt ongoing.
   - Users report this requires active application/request to a client manager (客户经理), as the system may not apply it automatically.
   - This is a popular "zero-asset" or low-asset workaround to hold and maintain a Jin Kui Hua card without paying the fee.

2. **Qualifying for Jin Kui Hua status or related privileges**:
   In some scenarios (especially older or specific branch rules), having payroll deposited at CMB (often with a minimum like 15,000 RMB/month average over recent months) can help qualify for opening or upgrading to Jin Kui Hua status, as an alternative to the 500,000 RMB asset requirement.
   However, the primary/official qualification remains monthly average assets of 500,000 RMB (including deposits, funds, insurance, etc.). Payroll helps mainly as a fee waiver rather than a direct replacement for asset-based qualification in most current cases.

**Notes/Cautions**:

- Policies can vary by branch, card type (e.g., standard vs. Mastercard variant), or over time—some 2025-2026 reports mention potential changes or stricter rules for certain cards.
- For payroll to count officially as "代发工资", it typically needs to come through CMB's corporate payroll system (from an employer's CMB corporate account), not just manual transfers.
- If your company doesn't use CMB for payroll, some users arrange alternative methods (e.g., via friends' companies or services), but this carries risks and may not always be recognized.
- Always confirm directly with your local CMB branch or client manager, as rules are not always uniformly applied nationwide and can depend on your specific situation.

In summary, yes—**代发工资** is one of the most reliable and commonly used methods to avoid the Jin Kui Hua management fee and maintain the account benefits without high assets.
