Question: Is WeChat group voice/video calling good, and can it be used for presentations like sharing a phone screen?

Answer:

Yes, group voice and video calls in WeChat are quite strong for casual collaboration and small team communication, and they *can* be used for simple presentations.

### 1. Group calls (voice/video)

* WeChat supports multi-person voice and video calls in groups.
* It’s widely used for meetings, family chats, and informal discussions.
* Stability is generally good, especially on mobile networks in China.

### 2. Screen sharing (for presentations)

Yes — WeChat does support screen sharing, but with some conditions:

* **Mobile (iOS/Android):**

  * In a group video call, a participant can start screen sharing.
  * This allows you to show slides, apps, or anything on your phone.
  * It works well for light presentations, demos, or explaining something visually.

* **Desktop (Windows/Mac WeChat client):**

  * Screen sharing is more stable and commonly used for presentations.
  * You can share full screen or specific windows (like PowerPoint, browser, etc.).

### 3. Practical limitations

* Not as powerful as Zoom or Teams for professional meetings.
* Fewer controls (no advanced webinar tools, breakout rooms, etc.).
* Screen sharing on mobile is more basic and can be less smooth depending on network.

### 4. Best use cases

* Quick presentations or demos
* Small team discussions
* Teaching or explaining something visually
* Informal remote collaboration

### Summary

Yes, WeChat group calls are good for lightweight meetings, and screen sharing is supported, making it usable for simple presentations—especially when convenience matters more than advanced meeting features.
