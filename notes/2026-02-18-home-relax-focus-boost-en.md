Here are some effective and accessible activities to help you relax and regain focus while at home:

### **Physical Relaxation**

- **Warm bath or shower**: Add Epsom salts or essential oils (like lavender) for extra relaxation.
- **Stretching or yoga**: Gentle movements to release tension and improve circulation.
- **Deep breathing**: Try the 4-7-8 technique (inhale for 4, hold for 7, exhale for 8).

### **Mindful Breaks**

- **Hydrate**: Drink water or herbal tea to refresh your mind.
- **Snack mindfully**: Choose something light and healthy, like fruit or nuts.
- **Listen to calming music**: Instrumental or nature sounds can reset your mood.

### **Mental Reset**

- **Short walk**: Even indoors, moving around can boost creativity and focus.
- **Journaling**: Jot down thoughts or to-do lists to clear mental clutter.
- **Power nap**: 10–20 minutes to recharge without grogginess.

### **Sensory Comfort**

- **Aromatherapy**: Use a diffuser with scents like eucalyptus or chamomile.
- **Soft lighting**: Dim harsh lights or use warm-toned lamps.
- **Tactile comfort**: Wrap yourself in a cozy blanket or use a stress ball.

### **Digital Detox**

- **Step away from screens**: Close your eyes or look out a window for 5 minutes.
- **Read a book**: A few pages of fiction or poetry can shift your mindset.
