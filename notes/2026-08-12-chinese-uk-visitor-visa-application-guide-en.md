Question: How does a Chinese citizen apply for a UK Standard Visitor visa?

Answer:

For a Chinese passport holder, the process is mainly:

```
Prepare documents
      |
      v
Online application (GOV.UK)
      |
      v
Book Visa Application Centre appointment
      |
      v
Biometrics (photo + fingerprints)
      |
      v
Wait for decision
      |
      v
Receive passport + visa
```

The official process requires applying online before travel and attending a visa application centre for identity checks and biometrics. ([GOV.UK][1])

## 1. Check eligibility

Typical purposes:

* tourism
* visiting friends/family
* attending meetings/conferences
* some business activities

You need to show:

* you are a genuine visitor
* you will leave the UK after the visit
* you have enough money for the trip
* your purpose matches visitor rules

Usually maximum stay:

```
up to 6 months
```

([GOV.UK][2])

---

## 2. Prepare documents

Common documents:

### Identity

```
Passport
Old passports (if useful)
Photo information
```

### Financial proof

Example:

```
Bank statements
Salary records
Tax records
Employment certificate
```

The key question for UKVI:

> "Can this person afford the trip and do they have reasons to return?"

---

### Employment / business proof

For an engineer:

Possible:

```
Employment contract
Company letter
Payslips
Business registration
Client contracts
```

If self-employed:

```
Company registration
Business income records
Invoices
Tax documents
```

---

### Travel plan

Example:

```
London:
Day 1-3 sightseeing

Oxford:
Day 4

Cambridge:
Day 5

Return to China:
Day 10
```

You do not need a perfect itinerary, but your story should be consistent.

---

## 3. Apply online

Go to the UK government Standard Visitor application page.

You fill:

* personal information
* passport information
* travel purpose
* employment
* finances
* travel history

The application can be started online and saved. ([GOV.UK][1])

---

## 4. Pay visa fee

Current official fee:

```
Standard Visitor visa:
£135

Stay:
up to 6 months
```

([GOV.UK][1])

---

## 5. Book biometrics appointment

After submitting:

You choose a Visa Application Centre (VAC).

Usually handled by:

* VFS Global locations in China

At appointment:

```
Passport check
Fingerprint
Photo
Document submission
```

([GOV.UK][1])

---

## 6. Wait for decision

UKVI reviews:

* immigration history
* financial situation
* purpose
* ties to home country

Possible outcomes:

```
Approved
    |
    v
Visa sticker / electronic status

or

Refused
```

---

## For your situation (AI engineer / entrepreneur)

A stronger visitor application would usually look like:

```
Purpose:
AI conference + tourism + meeting companies

Evidence:
- software engineer background
- company ownership
- income
- family ties
- property/financial ties
- return plan
```

Avoid writing:

> "I want to go UK and find AI jobs"

because visitor visa is not a job-search visa.

Better:

> "I am visiting UK for technology events, meetings, and tourism, then returning to continue my work."

```
Visitor visa
    |

    |
    +--> conferences
    +--> universities
    +--> startup meetings
    |
    v
Later apply:
Student / Skilled Worker / Global Talent
```
