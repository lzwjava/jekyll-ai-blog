Question: Do I need to get a Hong Kong job offer first before applying for QMAS, or can I apply for QMAS first and find work later?

Answer:

Yes, **you can apply for QMAS first without a Hong Kong job offer**. A job offer is **not required** at the application stage. QMAS is designed exactly for attracting skilled people who want to move to Hong Kong and then find opportunities there. ([Immigration Department][1])

The normal path is:

```
Apply QMAS
      |
      v
Pass points assessment + selection
      |
      v
Get Hong Kong entry permission
      |
      v
Move to HK
      |
      +--> Find a job
      |
      +--> Start a business / consultancy
```

Under QMAS General Points Test, successful applicants normally receive an initial **36-month stay** without being tied to a specific employer. During this period, they can take employment or establish/join a business. ([Immigration Department][1])

For your situation:

* 11+ years software engineering experience
* AI/LLM experience
* English IELTS 6.5
* Guangzhou background (easy HK access)
* Potential AI consultancy/business plan

QMAS is actually more suitable than the normal employment visa route.

Comparison:

| Route                           | Need HK job offer?                   | Advantage                                     |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| QMAS                            | ❌ No                                 | Freedom to find job, freelance, start company |
| General Employment Policy (GEP) | ✅ Yes                                | Easier if you already have HK employer        |
| ASMTP (Mainland professionals)  | ✅ Yes                                | Employer-sponsored route                      |
| Entrepreneur route              | Usually need business plan/resources | For starting HK business                      |

([Immigration Department][2])

For your case I would think:

**Phase 1 (now):**

* Calculate QMAS points
* Prepare evidence:

  * employment history
  * AI projects
  * publications/blog/GitHub
  * income proof
  * education documents
  * English ability

**Phase 2:**

* Apply QMAS
* At the same time:

  * interview HK AI companies
  * contact HK enterprises for AI consulting
  * explore HK company setup

You do not need to wait until you find a job first.

One important point: QMAS gives you the **right to live/work**, but it does not guarantee income. When extending after the initial period, Hong Kong expects evidence that you have settled and contributed, such as employment or a reasonable business. ([Immigration Department][1])

Given your profile, I would compare:

```
QMAS + AI consultancy/business
        vs
HK company + entrepreneur route
        vs
Find HK AI job + work visa
```

QMAS is probably the most flexible starting point.
