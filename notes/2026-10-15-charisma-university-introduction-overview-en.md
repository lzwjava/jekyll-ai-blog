**Question:** What is an introduction to Charisma University (魅力大学)?

**Answer:**

Charisma University was **founded in 2011** by **Dr. Peterchris Okpala**. It is a higher institution approved by the Turks and Caicos Islands Ministry of Education, situated in the **Grace Bay area** on the northeast coast of the island of Providenciales, Turks and Caicos Islands (TCI).

> **Note:** The institution's primary campus is in the **Turks and Caicos Islands** (a British Overseas Territory), not the continental United States — though in 2023, it was approved to award post-secondary degrees in the **State of Montana**, and established a branch there to offer US degrees.

**Key Facts:**

- **Type:** Non-profit private university
- **Delivery:** Primarily online, enabling students to earn degrees regardless of location or employment status
- **Programs:** Undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctorate degrees across faculties including business, education, law, philosophy and religion, psychology and behavioural science, and health science — with over 40 degree programmes available
- **Accreditation:** Accredited by the Accreditation, Certification, and Quality Assurance Institute (ACQUIN)
- **Rankings:** Ranked 1001+ on the Times Higher Education Impact Rankings
- **Focus on Flexibility:** The university is committed to adult learners, with degree structures that allow students to schedule coursework around other responsibilities like jobs or childcare
- **Global Partnerships:** The university has partnerships with other universities worldwide, offering students the chance to study abroad for a year
