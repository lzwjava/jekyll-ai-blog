## 1. Think of a degree as an access-control bypass

Credential: bachelor's degree

Potentially unlocks master's programs, degree-required job listings, and standardized HR filters. This is the most important missing credential if you lack a recognized bachelor's degree.

Signal: master's degree in AI or CS

Adds a recognized graduate credential, potentially improves access to AI engineering and research-oriented positions, and can help with international mobility. It does not guarantee that recruiters will stop filtering you out.

Compounding asset: shipped products and AI work

Keeps generating customer revenue, technical credibility, open-source distribution, and relationships regardless of whether you finish a degree.

The strategic goal is to obtain the minimum credible academic qualification that removes the most barriers, not to maximize years spent studying.
