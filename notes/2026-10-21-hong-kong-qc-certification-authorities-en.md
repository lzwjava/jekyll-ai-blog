**Question:** Where to find Hong Kong QC (Quality Certification) certification?

**Answer:**

"香港QC认证" most commonly refers to the **Hong Kong Q-Mark Certification Scheme** (香港Q嘜认证). Here are the key sources and bodies:

---

### 1. 🏅 Hong Kong Q-Mark Council — Primary Body

The Hong Kong Q-Mark Schemes, established in July 1978, are administered by the Hong Kong Q-Mark Council under the auspices of the **Federation of Hong Kong Industries (FHKI)**.

- **Official Website:** [qmark.org.hk](https://www.qmark.org.hk)
- **Address:** 31/F, Billion Plaza, 8 Cheung Yue Street, Cheung Sha Wan, Kowloon, Hong Kong
- **Tel:** 2732 3188 / 2732 3195
- **Email:** <qmd@fhki.org.hk>

Enterprises from Hong Kong, Macau, and the Mainland can apply for "Q-Mark" certification and choose from different schemes according to their business nature. These include:

- **Hong Kong Q-Mark Product Scheme**, **Q-Mark Service Scheme**, **Q-Mark Food Certification Scheme**, **Green Mark**, **Q-Carbon**, and **Q-ESG Certification Scheme**.

---

### 2. 🏛️ Hong Kong Accreditation Service (HKAS) — Accreditation Authority

All endorsed test reports and certificates issued by HKAS-accredited organisations bear the HKAS accreditation symbol. HKAS' accreditation is recognized in over 100 economies, including all major trading partners such as the US, EU, Mainland China, and Japan.

- **Website:** [www.hkas.gov.hk](https://www.hkas.gov.hk)

---

### 3. 📋 Hong Kong Council for Testing and Certification (HKCTC) — Industry Overview

For general information on Hong Kong's testing and certification sector:

- **Website:** [www.hkctc.gov.hk](https://www.hkctc.gov.hk)

---

### 4. 🔍 FHKI Member Services Page

FHKI also offers a dedicated Q-Mark services portal for enterprises seeking certification.

- **FHKI Q-Mark page:** [industryhk.org/en/services/qmark](https://industryhk.org/en/services/qmark)
