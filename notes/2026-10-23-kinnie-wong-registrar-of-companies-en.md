**Question:** Who is Ms. Kinnie Wong, Registrar of Companies, Hong Kong SAR?

**Answer:**

Ms. **Kinnie Wong** (full name: **Wong Kit Yee, Kinnie, JP**) is the current Registrar of Companies of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

Key facts about her:

- **Position:** Registrar of Companies, Companies Registry, Government of the HKSAR
- **Assumed office:** She has held the post since 3 July 2025, serving as an ex-officio member and Representative of the Financial Secretary, Government of the HKSAR.
- **Honorary title:** She is a Justice of the Peace (JP).
- **Prior government career:** Before assuming the post of Registrar of Companies in July 2025, Ms. Wong served in various bureaux and departments of the Government of the HKSAR, including the Chief Secretary for Administration's Private Office, the former Home Affairs Bureau, the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau, the Chief Executive's Office, the Constitutional and Mainland Affairs Bureau, the Tourism Commission, the former Financial Services Bureau, the former Health and Welfare Bureau, the Home Affairs Department, and the former Industry Department.
- **International role:** She serves as **Treasurer** on the Corporate Registers Forum (CRF) Executive, representing Hong Kong, China / Companies Registry.

She succeeded **Miss Helen Tang**, who had served as Registrar of Companies from July 2022.
