### 2. Core Structure & Themes of the Book
The book systematically introduces the theoretical framework of Xi Jinping Thought, structured across an introduction, 16 core chapters, and a conclusion:

1. **Introduction & Theoretical Foundation (导论 & 第1–4章):**
   * New Era context and upholding Socialism with Chinese Characteristics.
   * Chinese Modernization (中国式现代化) to achieve national rejuvenation.
   * The Party's overall leadership (坚持党的全面领导) and people-centered development (坚持以人民为中心).

2. **Five-Sphere Integrated Plan (五位一体 / Chapters 5–10):**
   * **Economy:** High-quality economic development (高质量发展).
   * **Politics:** Whole-process people’s democracy & Socialist rule of law (全过程人民民主、全面依法治国).
   * **Culture:** Building a socialist cultural powerhouse (社会主义文化强国).
   * **Society:** Enhancing people's wellbeing and livelihood (增进民生福祉).
   * **Ecology:** Ecological civilization & "Beautiful China" (美丽中国建设).

3. **Security, Sovereignty, Diplomacy & Governance (Chapters 11–16 & Conclusion):**
   * Holistic National Security Concept (总体国家安全观).
   * Defense and military modernization (国防和军队建设).
   * "One Country, Two Systems" and national reunification (祖国完全统一).
   * Major-country diplomacy & Building a Community with a Shared Future for Mankind (人类命运共同体).
   * Comprehensive and strict Party self-governance (全面从严治党).
   * Methodology & philosophy: *"Ten Clarifications"* (十个明确), *"Fourteen Commitments"* (十四个坚持), *"Thirteen Historic Achievements"* (十三个方面成就), and *"Six Musts"* (六个必须坚持).
