**Question:** What does "tagline" mean?

**Answer:**

A **tagline** is a short, memorable phrase or slogan associated with a brand, product, movie, or campaign. Its purpose is to quickly convey the essence, value, or identity of something in a catchy, concise way.

There are two main contexts:

**1. Marketing / Branding**
A brief slogan tied to a company or product.
- Nike: *"Just Do It"*
- Apple: *"Think Different"*
- McDonald's: *"I'm Lovin' It"*

**2. Movies / Entertainment**
A short phrase on a poster or ad that captures the film's mood or premise.
- *Alien*: *"In space, no one can hear you scream."*
- *Jurassic Park*: *"An adventure 65 million years in the making."*

In short, a tagline = **a punchy phrase that sticks in your memory and represents something bigger**.
