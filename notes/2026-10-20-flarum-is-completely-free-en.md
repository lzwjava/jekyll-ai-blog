**Question:** Is Flarum (https://github.com/flarum/flarum) free?

**Answer:** Yes, Flarum is completely free. Flarum is open-source software licensed under the **MIT License**, which is one of the most permissive open-source licenses available. This means you can:

- Use it for free (personal or commercial projects)
- Modify the source code
- Distribute it freely
- Use it in proprietary software

Flarum is a fast, simple, and extensible discussion platform built with PHP, powered by the Mithril JavaScript framework. You self-host it on your own server, so there are no subscription fees — though you'll need to pay for your own hosting infrastructure.
