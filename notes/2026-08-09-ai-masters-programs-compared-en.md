Question: What programs are similar to Illinois Tech's Master of Artificial Intelligence (M.A.S.)?

Answer: IIT's program is a professional (non-thesis) MS — ML, NLP, CV, bioinformatics electives, on-campus or online, STEM-OPT eligible. Closest comparables:

**Similar tier (professional MS in AI specifically, not general CS):**

- **USC Viterbi — MS in Artificial Intelligence** — dedicated AI degree, on-campus/online, strong NLP/robotics faculty
- **Johns Hopkins — MS in Artificial Intelligence** — online-heavy, part-time friendly, similar core (ML/NLP/CV/reasoning)
- **Northwestern — MS in AI (MSAI)** — newer program, project-based, industry-adjacent like IIT's
- **Colorado State — MS in AI and Machine Learning** — online, async, 8-week terms, cheaper than IIT
- **Penn State World Campus — MPS in Artificial Intelligence** — applied/professional not thesis, working-professional target

**Cheaper/higher-prestige alternative if cost matters more than brand:**

- **Georgia Tech OMSCS — Machine Learning specialization** — ~$7-10K total, same rigor as on-campus GT MSCS, no dedicated "AI" degree title but ML/AI track covers it. This is the standard recommendation over IIT for anyone comparing cost-per-value.

**If you want research/thesis-track instead of professional MS:**

- **CMU — MSAII (Master of AI and Innovation)** — heavier industry-project focus, Pittsburgh
- **Stanford MSCS — AI track (part-time online option)** — most selective, best brand, most expensive

Given your background (self-taught, already training models on MI300X, running an AI company), none of these add technical uplift over what you already do — the value in any of them would be purely the credential/visa/network angle, not the curriculum. If it's not for a visa or immigration purpose, OMSCS ML track is the only one worth the time-cost since it's near-free and self-paced.
