Question: What are the top digital banks in the UK?

Answer:

Here are the **top digital (neobank / challenger) banks in the UK** — the most widely used and considered leaders.

---

# 🇬🇧 Top UK Digital Banks

## 1. Monzo

* One of the **most popular UK digital banks**
* Strong budgeting tools, “pots”, instant notifications
* Early salary access, virtual cards
* FSCS deposit protection (UK bank license)
* Best for: **everyday spending + budgeting**
  👉 Often ranked **#1 overall digital bank in UK** ([thetop10.uk][1])

---

## 2. Starling Bank

* Full UK banking licence
* Personal + business accounts
* No monthly fees
* Strong savings “spaces” features
* Best for: **main bank replacement**
  👉 Frequently listed as **top challenger bank overall** ([RemitFinder][2])

---

## 3. Revolut

* Very strong **multi-currency + global features**
* Crypto, stocks, FX exchange
* Multiple tiers (Standard → Ultra)
* Recently obtained **full UK banking licence (2026)**
  👉 Becoming full bank competitor to Monzo/Starling ([MoneyWeek][3])

Best for:

* international users
* travel
* multi-currency
