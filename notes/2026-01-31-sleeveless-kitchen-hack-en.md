Yes, that's a practical approach! Wearing sleeveless or tank top ("bei xin" - vest/tank style) clothing for kitchen cleanup makes a lot of sense, especially in winter when you're working with warm water. Here's why this works well:

**Benefits of this approach:**

- Your arms stay completely free and won't get wet sleeves when reaching into the sink or loading the dishwasher
- You avoid the annoying problem of sleeves getting soaked or needing to constantly roll them up
- The warm water and activity of cleaning keeps you warm despite the sleeveless outfit
- Much easier movement and flexibility when scrubbing, rinsing, and organizing dishes

**A few tips to make it even better:**

- Keep the kitchen reasonably warm so you're comfortable
- You might want to keep a cardigan or hoodie nearby to throw on when you're done, since you'll cool down quickly once you stop moving
- An apron can protect your torso from splashes while keeping your arms free

This is actually a very practical solution that many people discover works better than wearing long sleeves and dealing with wet, uncomfortable cuffs!
