**Question: What's Oyunfor, the Turkish product/platform?**

**Answer:**

Oyunfor is a Turkish **digital gaming commerce platform**, mainly focused on selling **game e-pins, digital codes, and in-game currencies**. Think of it as a Turkey-focused version of platforms like **G2A, Eneba, or Razer Gold marketplace**, but with stronger local payment/distribution relationships. ([Oyunfor.com][1])

Main products:

* 🎮 **Game e-pins**

  * MMORPG currencies
  * Online game credits
  * Game activation codes

* 💳 **Digital gift cards**

  * Google Play
  * Apple
  * Microsoft/Xbox
  * Steam-related products, etc. ([Oyunfor.com][1])

* 🕹️ **Mobile game top-ups**

  * Mobile games
  * In-game purchases
  * Regional payment solutions ([Google Play][2])

Business model:

```
Game publishers
       |
       v
  Oyunfor (distribution + payment + localization)
       |
       v
 Turkish gamers
```

They act as a **local distributor/reseller** for many game companies. Oyunfor says it works with 85+ gaming brands, including companies such as Google Play, Microsoft, Apple, Tencent, Riot Games, Rockstar, Sega, etc. ([Oyunfor.com][1])

Company background:

* Founded around **2006–2010** (different company profiles cite establishment dates differently). ([Oyunfor.com][1])
* Operated by **MGM Bilgi Teknolojileri A.Ş.** ([Google Play][2])
* Headquarters: Aydın, Turkey region. ([LinkedIn][3])

Why it is interesting:

From a startup/platform perspective, Oyunfor is basically a **vertical fintech + gaming commerce company**:

```
Gaming demand
      +
Local payment rails
      +
Publisher relationships
      +
Digital goods inventory
      =
Gaming marketplace
```

Turkey is an interesting market because:

* young gaming population
* high mobile gaming adoption
* local currency/payment friction
* international publishers need regional distribution

A company like this is not building games; it is building the **commerce infrastructure around games**. Similar examples:

* China: 5173, 淘宝游戏充值 ecosystem
* Global: G2A, Eneba, Razer Gold
* Southeast Asia: Codashop

Oyunfor has also explored Web3/digital identity integrations with Moca Network, showing attempts to expand beyond simple top-up sales into gamer identity and loyalty systems. ([Oyunfor.com][4])
