Question: How can I sell a low-priced AI course (e.g., 25 CNY) through WeChat, similar to models used on Pinduoduo, given that I already have a WeChat Subscription Account and Video Channel?

Answer:

You’re already in a strong position (Subscription Account + Video Channel). What you’re missing is a **transaction layer + funnel design**. Let’s break this into **practical steps + strategy**.

---

# 1. Choose Your Selling Infrastructure (3 realistic options)

## Option A — WeChat Mini Program Store (Most scalable)

This is the “Pinduoduo-style” approach inside WeChat.

### How to do it:

* Register a **WeChat Mini Program**
* Enable **WeChat Pay merchant account**
* Use a SaaS tool (no coding needed), such as:

  * Youzan (有赞)
  * Weimob (微盟)
  * Youdian (有店)

### Pros:

* Full store (product page, payment, delivery)
* Supports **low-price impulse buying (25 CNY fits perfectly)**
* Can bundle, upsell, memberships

### Cons:

* Setup takes time (business license required)
* Small fees + transaction cut

👉 This is closest to your “Pinduoduo cheap course” model.

---

## Option B — Knowledge Payment Platforms (Fastest start)

Platforms specialized for selling courses:

* Xiaoe Tech (小鹅通)
* Knowledge Planet (知识星球)
* Dedao-style tools

### How it works:

* Upload course
* Generate **purchase link or QR code**
* Share via:

  * Subscription account articles
  * Video channel comments / bio
  * Private chat

### Pros:

* Very fast setup (1–2 days)
* Built-in course hosting + DRM
* No need to build system

### Cons:

* Platform takes commission
* Less control over branding

👉 Best for testing your idea quickly.

---

## Option C — Private Domain + Manual Delivery (Lean MVP)

This is how many creators start.

### Flow:

1. Content → attract users
2. Add WeChat (or group)
3. Send payment QR (WeChat Pay)
4. Deliver course via:

   * Notion / Google Drive
   * Private group
   * Recorded videos

### Pros:

* Zero cost
* Maximum flexibility

### Cons:

* Not scalable
* Manual work

👉 Good for first 50–100 users.

# 2. Connect Your Existing Channels (This is critical)

You already have:

* **Subscription Account**
* **Video Channel**

Here’s how to turn them into a sales funnel:

---

## Funnel Design (simple but powerful)

### Step 1 — Content (Video Channel)

Post short videos like:

* “1 AI trick saves 2 hours/day”
* “Prompt that replaces junior dev”
* “Build X in 5 minutes”

👉 Goal: attract curiosity, not sell directly

---

### Step 2 — Lead Capture

In video description or comments:

* “Comment ‘AI’ to get full course”
* “Click link in bio”
* “Add WeChat for full tutorial”

---

### Step 3 — Conversion (Subscription Account or Mini Program)

Inside article:

* Pain → demo → result → **25 CNY offer**
* Add:

  * Purchase button (Mini Program)
  * QR code (course platform)

---

### Step 4 — Delivery

* Course platform / Mini Program
* Or private group

# 3. Pricing Strategy (why 25 CNY works)

You’re basically using **Pinduoduo psychology**:

* Low price → low decision friction
* High volume → scale
* Impulse buy

But here’s the key:

👉 Don’t rely on one course.

Use a ladder:

* 25 CNY → entry
* 99 CNY → deeper course
* 299+ → premium / coaching

# 4. What Most People Get Wrong

Let’s correct a few assumptions:

### ❌ “I just open a store and people will buy”

No. Traffic > store.

### ❌ “Course quality is the main factor”

At 25 CNY:

* Packaging > content depth
* Speed to value matters more

### ❌ “One product is enough”

You need:

* Continuous content
* Iteration based on feedback
