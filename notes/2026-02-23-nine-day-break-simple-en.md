Here is the breakdown of how the 9-day break is composed based on your understanding:

- **The 9-Day Break:** February 15 (Sun) to February 23 (Mon)
- **The 2 Weekend Days:** February 15 (Sun) and February 21 (Sat) are naturally occurring weekends within that period. (Note: February 22 is a Sunday that is absorbed into the holiday, but February 21 is the main Saturday).
- **The 2 Adjusted Days:** To make the break continuous, **February 14 (Sat)** and **February 28 (Sat)** are swapped. You work on those Saturdays so you can have **February 16 (Mon)** and **February 23 (Mon)** off.

So, the formula is:
**2 (Original Weekend) + 2 (Adjusted/Shifted Days) + 5 (Actual Statutory Leave) = 9 Days off.**
