**Question:** Where to go in Xunliao Bay on Sunday afternoon with a 5-year-old daughter during light rain, with return to Zengcheng before evening?

---

## Answer

You're right to think about timing—Xunliao Bay to Zengcheng is roughly **1.5–2 hours** drive, depending on traffic. For a 5-year-old and light rain, here's the practical play:

### Best Activities Right Now (Light Rain)

**1. Rose Chapel** — Go now. Open all day with a triangular roof design backed by blue sea and sky, perfect for family photos. Light rain actually helps visibility and keeps crowds away. Take one good family photo with your daughter, stay 20–30 minutes. Free.

**2. Beachcombing at Chisha** — Fine soft sand for building sandcastles, and at low tide you can dig for little crabs and collect shells—bring small buckets and shovels. A 5-year-old will stay entertained for 1–1.5 hours. The light rain won't ruin this; it actually makes shells easier to spot. Park is free.

**3. Mozi Stone Park** — Free to explore unique rock formations for family photos. 20–30 minutes of climbing on rocks, minimal crowds during rain.

### Bad Idea in Light Rain

**Skip Delta Island** for now—speedboat rides and water activities aren't practical in rain with a small child.

### Logistics

**Timeline for safe return:**

- Now → 2:00 PM: Rose Chapel + Beachcombing (~90 min)
- 2:00–3:00 PM: Grab lunch at any local seafood spot near the beach
- 3:15 PM: Leave for Zengcheng
- **Arrive Zengcheng: ~5:00 PM** (comfortable buffer before dark)

The drive back hits late afternoon traffic around Dongguan/Guangzhou border, but Sunday evenings are lighter than Friday nights.

**Pro tip:** Download offline maps now—Xunliao Bay cell signal is spotty in some areas. Weather is your friend here—light rain keeps the beach peaceful and you won't overheat.
