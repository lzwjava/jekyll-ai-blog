**"Feng Xuan as a Retainer of Lord Mengchang" (冯谖客孟尝君)** is a classical Chinese text from the *Strategies of the Warring States* (*Zhanguo Ce*, 战国策), which chronicles the political intrigues and diplomatic maneuvers during China's Warring States period (475-221 BCE).

This narrative tells the story of Feng Xuan (also written as Feng Yuan), an initially unassuming retainer who enters the service of Lord Mengchang, one of the Four Lords of the Warring States known for his hospitality to scholars and guests. Through his clever strategies, Feng Xuan secures his patron's long-term security and prosperity, demonstrating themes of wisdom, foresight, and the complex relationship between advisors and rulers in ancient China.

The text is a staple in Chinese literature courses for its rich language, historical significance, and the strategic wisdom it illustrates.
