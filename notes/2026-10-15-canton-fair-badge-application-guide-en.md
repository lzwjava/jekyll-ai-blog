Question: What does this document about the 140th Canton Fair badge application guide cover?

Answer: This is a comprehensive guide for obtaining credentials to the **140th Canton Fair (广交会)**, scheduled for **October 15 – November 4, 2026** in Guangzhou Pazhou (three phases). Note: **Phase 1 begins today.**

---

### Four Types of Eligible Attendees

| Type | Who Qualifies | Cost |
| ------ | -------------- | ------ |
| **Overseas Buyer** | Foreign companies/buyers with a genuine purchasing need | Free |
| **Domestic Buyer** | Qualifying domestic enterprises (by invitation only) | ¥300/day + ¥50 processing fee |
| **Exhibitor** | Companies with an assigned booth | Allocated per booth |
| **Accompanying Staff** | Translators or assistants brought by overseas buyers | ¥300/day + ¥50 processing fee |

---

### Key Details Per Type

**① Overseas Buyers (Free — most common route)**

- Requires passport / HK-Macao permit / Taiwan permit + genuine purchase intent
- Register via the overseas buyer pre-registration portal; e-badge issued upon approval
- Walk-in registration also available at airports, stations, ports, and designated hotels

**② Domestic Buyers (Invitation-based)**

- Must be a registered Chinese company with annual revenue ≥ ¥50 million, business scope matching the fair's exhibition categories, and not currently exhibiting
- Application deadlines: **Phase 1 — Oct 10 (already passed)** | Phase 2 — Oct 19 | Phase 3 — Oct 27
- VIP tiers (¥200M+ / ¥1B+) receive more free passes and larger quotas

**③ Exhibitors**

- Enterprise-only channel; must already have a secured booth
- Applied through provincial commerce bureaus or trade delegations

**④ Accompanying Staff (Translator/Assistant) — Most relevant for ordinary individuals**

- This is the most accessible route for regular people to enter the fair
- **Translators** must hold one of: CATTI certificate, NAETI certificate, or a Chinese university foreign-language degree
- **Critical rule:** The translator/assistant **cannot apply themselves** — the overseas buyer must submit the application on their behalf
- Quota: max 2 per buyer per phase (1 translator + 1 assistant)
- Must be submitted **at least 2 calendar days** before entry (e.g., for Oct 15 entry → submit by Oct 13)
- HK/Macao/Taiwan buyers and Chinese passport holders can only apply for "assistant," **not** "translator"

---

### Top 5 Pitfalls to Avoid

1. Translators cannot self-apply — confirm the buyer has a valid credential first
2. Don't wait until the last 2-day minimum — leave buffer time for review delays
3. The accompanying relationship must be genuine — fake relationships can result in blacklisting with no refund
4. Avoid third-party credential brokers — all applications must go through the official system
5. Previous credential violations may trigger additional review this time — check your account status early
