**Question: What is the UK "top-up" degree?**

**Answer:**

In the UK education system, a **top-up degree** usually means a **one-year final-year undergraduate program** that converts an existing qualification (often a Higher National Diploma, Foundation Degree, or associate degree) into a full **Bachelor's degree (BA/BSc/BEng)**.

Typical path:

```
College / Polytechnic
        |
        v
HND (2 years)  OR  Foundation Degree (2 years)
        |
        v
Top-up year (1 year)
        |
        v
Bachelor's Degree (3-year equivalent)
```

Example:

* You study **Computer Science HND for 2 years**
* Then enter a university's **BSc Computer Science Top-up**
* After 1 year:

  * receive **BSc Computer Science**
  * same level as students who completed the normal 3-year bachelor

---

### Why does the UK have this?

The UK has historically had multiple higher education routes:

```
University route:
A-Level
  ↓
Bachelor (3 years)

Vocational route:
College
  ↓
HND / Foundation Degree
  ↓
Top-up
  ↓
Bachelor
```

Top-up allows students from vocational/higher education routes to "upgrade" their qualification.

But not exactly the same.

A UK top-up is usually **not a new bachelor's from year 1**. It is a "completion year".

### For international students

Many UK universities offer:

```
3-year Bachelor:
Year 1
Year 2
Year 3

vs

Top-up:
Existing diploma
+
1 year university
=
Bachelor degree
```

Advantages:

* cheaper
* faster
* accepts previous study credits

Disadvantages:

* university choices are fewer
* some employers/academics may view it differently from a full 3-year degree
* depends heavily on the awarding university

### Example for a software engineer

Someone with:

```
Computer Science diploma
+ 5 years programming experience
```

could do:

```
BSc Computing Top-up (1 year)
        |
        v
MSc Artificial Intelligence (1 year)
```

Total:

```
2 years -> Bachelor's + Master's
```

This route is quite common for mature students.

---

For your background (self-study degree + 10+ years engineering + AI work), a UK top-up is interesting because it can be a **credential bridge**: diploma/associate-level qualification → recognized UK bachelor's → MSc AI/Data Science. It is mainly useful if you need the formal bachelor's requirement for overseas MSc/immigration/job filters.
