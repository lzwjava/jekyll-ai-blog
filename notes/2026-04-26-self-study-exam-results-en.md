Answer:

## Notice on the Release of November 2025 Self-Study Examination Practical Assessment Results

**Issued by:** Self-Study Examination Office, Guangdong University of Foreign Studies (GDUFS)
**Date:** November 21, 2025

---

### 📋 Key Information

**Results Release**
- The November 2025 Self-Study Examination practical assessment results were published on **November 21, 2025 at 17:00** via the **Qida Management System**.
- ⚠️ The score data will be **cleared before the next practical assessment registration**, so students are urged to check their results promptly.

**Score Reporting Rules** (per Guangdong Provincial Examination Authority regulations)
- **Failing scores** will **not** be reported to the provincial system.
- **Passing scores and above** will be published on the **Guangdong Province Self-Study Examination Management System** in **mid-December** (before graduation application processing).

---

### 1️⃣ How to Check Your Results

- **URL:** [http://service.qidaedu.com/practiceLogin.jsp](http://service.qidaedu.com/practiceLogin.jsp)
- Log in using your **examination admission number** and **password**.

---

### 2️⃣ Score Review (Re-check) Application

If you disagree with your score, you may apply for a **score review**:

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **Application Period** | November 22–25, 2025 (based on postmark/send date) |
| **How to Apply** | Submit a written application to the GDUFS Self-Study Exam Office; download and fill in the attached form, then mail it to the office |
| **Scope of Review** | Only checks for arithmetic errors (wrong addition, missed addition, or wrong score entry) |
| **Not Included** | Re-grading or reassessment of the examiner's judgment |
| **Deadline** | Late submissions will be treated as having no objection to the score |

---

### 3️⃣ Review Process & Result Feedback

- On **November 26**, the office will organize staff to conduct the review.
- If an error is found → score will be **corrected** and the student notified **by email**.
- If the reviewed score matches the original → **no reply** will be sent.
