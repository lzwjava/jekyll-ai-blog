**Question:** How has Singapore's economy performed in recent years?

**Answer:**

Singapore's economy has shown impressive resilience and strong growth over the past few years, driven largely by manufacturing, trade, and the global AI boom.

**2024 — Solid recovery**
Singapore's economy expanded by 4.4% in 2024, with the global AI-tech boom giving it a notable boost, especially in the latter half of the year. In particular, the electronics cluster in manufacturing and the machinery and equipment segment of wholesale trade grew robustly due to the upturn in the global electronics cycle.

**2025 — Strongest growth in years**
Singapore's economy expanded 5% in 2025, easing slightly from 5.3% growth in 2024 but exceeding official advance estimates. Full-year growth was driven primarily by manufacturing, wholesale trade, and finance and insurance, with robust AI-related electronics demand providing a key lift. This marked the fastest growth rate since the post-pandemic rebound in 2021.

**2026 — Upgraded forecasts**
In the first half of 2026, Singapore's economy grew 6.1%, with Q1 expanding 6.3% and Q2 at 5.9% year-on-year. Growth was driven by manufacturing (up 12.5%), wholesale trade, and finance and insurance, with robust global AI-related demand boosting electronics and precision engineering. Reflecting stronger-than-expected first-half performance and an improved outlook for AI-related capital spending, the Ministry of Trade and Industry upgraded its 2026 GDP growth forecast to 4.5%–5.5%.

**Key growth drivers:**
- **AI & electronics:** Surging global demand for AI-related hardware has been the single biggest catalyst since 2024.
- **Finance & insurance:** Singapore continues to strengthen its role as a regional wealth management and green finance hub.
- **Tourism:** Over 13 million tourists visited Singapore in 2023, with tourism receipts reaching US$24.5–26 billion.
- **Workforce:** Foreign talent makes up about 36% of Singapore's workforce, supporting its skilled labor base across industries.

**Risks ahead:**
Singapore kept its 2026 GDP growth forecast range cautious, citing downside risks including the impact of the Middle East conflict on global trade conditions.
