## 2. History

### Pre-colonial era

Senegal had several powerful kingdoms, including:

* Jolof Empire
* Waalo Kingdom
* Cayor Kingdom

The region was an important trade area connecting inland West Africa with the Atlantic coast.

### Colonial period

From the 1600s onward, European powers competed for influence. France eventually controlled Senegal, making it one of France's most important African colonies.

A famous historical place is Île de Gorée, which became associated with the Atlantic slave trade. ([The World Factbook][1])

### Modern era

Senegal gained independence in 1960. Compared with many African countries, it avoided military coups for decades and developed a reputation for democratic stability. ([worldbank.org][2])

# 4. Economy

Senegal is a **lower-middle-income developing economy**. Main industries:

### Traditional sectors

* Agriculture:

  * peanuts (groundnuts)
  * millet
  * rice
  * livestock

* Fishing:

  * important export industry

* Mining:

  * phosphates
  * gold
  * minerals

### New growth areas

Senegal has discovered offshore oil and gas resources, creating expectations for faster economic growth. ([The World Factbook][1])

GDP growth has been relatively strong compared with many African countries, but challenges remain:

* youth unemployment
* poverty
* unequal development between Dakar and rural areas
* dependence on imports ([reuters.com][3])

# 5. Society and culture

## Language

French is the official language because of colonial history.

However, **Wolof** is the everyday language for many Senegalese people and is increasingly influential in media and culture. ([AP News][4])

## Music

Senegal has one of Africa's strongest music cultures.

Famous style:

* Mbalax — a fusion of traditional rhythms, drums, and modern pop

Internationally known Senegalese musicians include:

* Youssou N'Dour
