**Question:** What are some cheap CPUs compatible with the MSI PRO B760M-E DDR5 motherboard?

**Answer:**

The MSI PRO B760M-E DDR5 uses an **LGA 1700 socket** with the Intel B760 chipset. It supports Intel Core 14th, 13th, and 12th Gen processors, as well as Intel Pentium Gold and Celeron processors. Here are the best budget-friendly options to consider:

---

### 🥇 Best Budget Pick — Intel Core i3-14100F (~$80–$95)
The Core i3-14100F is below $100, available on both DDR4 and DDR5 platforms, and even comes with a decent bundled cooler considering the TDP. It's the top recommendation for tight budgets right now.

> ⚠️ Note: The "F" suffix means **no integrated graphics** — you'll need a discrete GPU.

---

### 🥈 Great Value — Intel Core i5-12400F (~$100–$120)
The Intel Core i5-12400F delivers top-tier performance for its price, offering great value for gamers and general users who want a modern, well-supported CPU for a balanced build. It has low power consumption and runs cool — frequently mentioned positives among users.

---

### 🥉 Entry Level — Intel Core i3-12100F (~$80–$100)
The i3-12100F maintained 144 FPS in CS2 at 1080p high settings and handled most modern games at 60+ FPS when paired with a decent graphics card. Solid for light gaming and everyday use.

---

### 💡 Need Integrated Graphics? — Intel Core i3-12100 / i5-12400
Historically priced around the $120 mark, the i3-12100 offers an excellent performance-to-cost ratio, proving itself to be a fantastic option for budget-conscious builders. The non-"F" versions include Intel UHD 730/770 iGPU, so no discrete GPU is required.
