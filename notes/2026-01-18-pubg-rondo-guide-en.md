Question: Which map in PUBG Mobile has the Yu Lin location, and what places does this map contain?

Answer: 
The **Rondo** map in PUBG Mobile features the Yu Lin location. Rondo is an 8x8 km battle royale map introduced in the 3.7 update (March 2025), supporting up to 100 players. It blends traditional Eastern architecture with modern urban elements, diverse terrain (forests, deserts, lakes, bamboo groves, ridges), and unique features like escalators, EMP zones, destructible terrain, two flight routes, in-map stores, and exclusive items such as the JS9 SMG.

Yu Lin is a serene, traditional area surrounded by lush forests, lakes, ponds, and greenery, offering a mix of architecture and nature—ideal for stealthy playstyles but prone to close-quarters fights in tight streets.

Rondo contains around 20 named locations, categorized below for clarity:

### Major Hot Drops (High Loot, High Action)
- **Jadena City**: Bustling metropolis with skyscrapers, neon lights, bridges, and escalators.
- **Stadium**: Multi-purpose sports arena in a central spot.
- **NEOX Factory** (or Neox Factory): Industrial hub with vehicles like the Pico Bus; central location.
- **Rin Jiang**: Riverside area with a floating restaurant, docks, and waterfront homes.
- **Test Track**: Vehicle testing area with charging stations.

### Natural/Traditional Areas (Stealth & Mid-Tier Loot)
- **Yu Lin**: Dense forest/garden with traditional buildings and ponds.
- **Tin Long Garden** (or The Long Garden): Towering stone mountains, waterfalls, Japanese-inspired architecture.
- **Bamboo**: Lush bamboo groves for vehicle break-throughs.
- **Mey Ran**: Lotus-filled serene ponds.
- **Lo Hua Xing**: Cliffside fortress with premium loot.

### Other Notable Locations
- **Jao Tin**: Cultural/theater area for balanced risk-reward.
- **Hemay Town**, **Rai An**, **Mu Ho Pan**: Desert-like zones.
- **Hung Shan**, **Fong Tun**, **Bei Li**: Forest/urban mixes.
- Smaller spots: Min Hu, Nan Chuan, Kun Xia, Yun Gu, Lan Po, Dan Ching, Tu Ling, Long Ho.

Rondo's design promotes varied playstyles: urban combat in cities, stealth in forests, and vehicle-heavy rotations via unique rides like Coupe SUV.
