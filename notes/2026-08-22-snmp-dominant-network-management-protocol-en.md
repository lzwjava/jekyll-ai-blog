Question: What is the most widely used network management protocol in TCP/IP networks?

Answer: **SNMP** (Simple Network Management Protocol)

SNMP is the dominant network management protocol in TCP/IP networks. It operates at the application layer and enables administrators to monitor and manage network devices (routers, switches, servers, printers, etc.) from a central system. Its key components include:

- **Manager** – the central system that queries devices
- **Agent** – software running on each managed device
- **MIB** (Management Information Base) – a structured database of device parameters

SNMP is widely supported across virtually all network equipment vendors, making it the de facto standard for network management.
