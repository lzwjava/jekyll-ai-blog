### The Honest Challenges

| Challenge | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| **Privacy of others** | Tracking people's trajectories requires using only *public* data ethically |
| **Signal vs. noise** | Without good personal profiling, you get flooded with irrelevant alerts |
| **Trust & verification** | AI-curated opportunities can be low-quality or manipulated |
| **Personalization cold start** | The system needs time and feedback to learn what you truly want |
| **Filter bubble risk** | Over-personalization can narrow your worldview |

---

### The Right Next Step for You

Given your interest, the most practical path right now is:

1. **Build a personal profile** — define your skills, goals, values, risk tolerance
2. **Set up a feed aggregator** — use tools like Feedly, Inoreader, or a custom RSS pipeline
3. **Layer an AI summarizer** — Claude, Perplexity, or a local Ollama model to synthesize daily
4. **Add a personal CRM** — Clay.com is the best current tool for tracking people in your network
5. **Connect to World Monitor** — for macro-level signals that affect your micro-decisions

The future you're describing — **a personalized intelligence layer that makes you a better-informed, better-connected, higher-quality-life individual** — is absolutely the right direction. The tools are 70% there. The remaining 30% is integration and personalization depth.
