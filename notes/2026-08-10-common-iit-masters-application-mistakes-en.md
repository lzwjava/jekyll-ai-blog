Question: What mistakes do applicants commonly make applying to master's programs at Illinois Institute of Technology (IIT Chicago)?

Answer:

**Portal/paperwork mistakes**

- Applying via Common App — IIT grad programs use their own **online Application Portal**, not Common App (that's UG only). Wrong portal = wasted week.
- Missing that the **application fee ($100) is credit-card only**. PayPal/wire won't work.
- Not whitelisting `grad.admission@illinoistech.edu` — status update emails land in spam and people miss "missing document" notices, blowing the deadline silently.
- Submitting GPA without conversion. Requirement is **3.0/4.0 minimum** — Chinese 100-point or 5.0-point scale GPAs need explicit conversion (WES or similar), not just pasted raw.

**LOR mistakes**

- Only **1 LOR is required for most master's programs** (2 for PhD) — people either submit too few or waste time hunting for 3 when 1 solid one suffices. Check the specific department page though, it varies.
- Recommenders who don't know your actual work — generic "she attended my class" letters read as weak signal vs. a manager/professor who can cite specific project contributions.

**SOP mistakes (this is where most rejections/delays actually happen)**

- Generic, copy-pasted SOP not customized to IIT/Chicago specifically — admissions + later visa officers both flag boilerplate language instantly.
- No logical narrative link between your bachelor's field, your work experience, and the specific MS program (e.g., unrelated undergrad major → CS master's with zero explanation of the pivot).
- For F-1 visa stage specifically: SOP that doesn't demonstrate clear **intent to return home** ("non-immigrant intent") — this is the #1 cited reason for US F-1 refusals, not academic weakness.

**GRE confusion**

- Most IIT master's programs are **GRE-optional/not required** now — people waste months prepping GRE for a program that doesn't need it, or the inverse: skip it for a program (usually more research-heavy MS/PhD) that does still want it. Confirm per-department, not university-wide.

**Post-admission / visa stage**

- I-20 errors (misspelled name, wrong DOB, wrong program dates) not caught before the visa interview — any mismatch with your passport triggers delays/refusal at the border. Check the I-20 the moment you get it.
- Underestimating visa processing time — apply for the interview **the day you get your I-20**, not weeks later. Processing time is routinely double what's officially quoted.
- Financial proof mismatch — bank statement amount not matching the I-20's declared cost of attendance.

**Timing**

- Missing that IIT runs **rolling admissions across Fall/Spring/Summer** with priority vs. final deadlines per program — people assume one fixed deadline and apply too late for scholarship/funding consideration, which closes earlier than the hard deadline.
