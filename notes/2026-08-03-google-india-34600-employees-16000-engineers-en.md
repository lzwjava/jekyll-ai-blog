Question: Give an intro to Google India — how many engineers/employees are there?

Answer:

**Overall Alphabet headcount:** Alphabet employed 194,668 people at the close of Q1 2026, with Engineering as the largest functional group at 46.5% of total headcount.

**India's share:** India accounts for 17.8% of Google's global workforce, making it the second-largest location after the United States (46.8%). That puts India headcount at roughly **34,600 people** (17.8% × 194,668), and if you apply the ~46% engineering ratio, roughly **16,000 engineers** — though Google doesn't publish an exact India-only engineering breakdown, so treat this as an estimate from the aggregate ratios, not an official figure.

**Why India is scaling up right now:**

- India headcount for major US tech giants (Facebook, Amazon, Apple, Microsoft, Netflix, Google) collectively grew 16% over the last 12 months — the biggest jump in a three-year period, per Xpheno.
- Google opened its largest campus in Bangalore, leasing 650,000 sq ft of office space, and has advertised hundreds of roles ranging from AI practice directors in cloud to chip designers and ML specialists, many requiring PhDs.
- US H-1B visa restrictions are pushing companies to hire more engineering talent directly in India rather than relocating them to the US.
- YouTube is hiring engineers in India specifically to build generative AI tools, and India's appeal isn't just talent — tens of millions of new internet users come online yearly as potential AI/chatbot customers.

**Key India engineering centers:** Bangalore (largest, AI/cloud/chip design), Hyderabad, Gurugram, Pune, Mumbai. Bangalore is the flagship for infra/cloud/ML roles.

**Caveat:** No official Alphabet filing breaks out India-specific engineering headcount — the 10-K reports total global headcount, and location/function splits above come from third-party labor-data firms (Revelio Labs, Xpheno), not Alphabet directly. Treat as directionally accurate, not exact.
