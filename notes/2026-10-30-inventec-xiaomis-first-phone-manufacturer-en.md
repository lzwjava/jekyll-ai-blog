**Question:** Introduction to 英华达 (Inventec Appliances Corporation) — the company that helped Xiaomi build its first phone.

**Answer:**

**英华达 (Inventec Appliances Corporation, IAC)** is a Taiwanese ODM (Original Design Manufacturer) specializing in smart handheld and wireless communication products. Here's a concise overview:

---

### Company Basics

**英華達股份有限公司** (Inventec Appliances Corp, IAC) is a publicly listed company that specializes in smart terminal products, smart networking, new energy products, and ODM manufacturing services, headquartered in New Taipei City (Wugu District), Taiwan.

IAC manufactures and sells wired and wireless communication products, including Wi-Fi phones, wireless smart clients, smart handhelds, network applications, and other products.

---

### Founding & History

IAC was established on **May 12, 2000**, with an initial capitalization of NT$1.1 billion. In 2001, it marketed the **OKWAP i108**, Taiwan's first color-screen cellular phone. By 2002, capitalization had grown to NT$2.35 billion.

IAC has four domestic operational sites and two overseas sites, providing global OEM/ODM services for many world-famous companies.

---

### Relationship to Inventec Group

英华达 (IAC) is the **smart device subsidiary** of **Inventec Corp** (英業達, TWSE: 2356) — a larger Taiwanese tech conglomerate. Inventec Corp has been expanding production capacity in AI servers, notebooks, and automotive electronics while simultaneously supporting IAC's capacity expansion.

---

### Role in Xiaomi's First Phone

The **Xiaomi Mi 1**, released on August 16, 2011, had its hardware design completed entirely by Xiaomi's in-house team (originally from Motorola), and the phone was **manufactured through contract manufacturing by Foxconn (Hon Hai Precision Industry) and Inventec**.

This makes IAC/Inventec one of the two key manufacturing partners behind Xiaomi's debut in the smartphone market — a pivotal moment that launched what would become one of the world's top smartphone manufacturers, achieving the largest market share of smartphones sold in China by 2014.

---

### What IAC Does Today

IAC now focuses on **3D printing services, smart wearable devices, unmanned aerial vehicles (drones), and Industry 4.0 technologies**, having accumulated extensive patents, product design, and manufacturing expertise.

In short, 英华达 (IAC) is the Taiwan-based ODM powerhouse that, alongside Foxconn, gave Xiaomi the manufacturing muscle to bring its very first smartphone to market.
