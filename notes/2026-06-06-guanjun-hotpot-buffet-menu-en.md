Question: What food does 冠菌自助火锅 (Guanjun Self-Service Hotpot) in Huizhou have?

冠菌自助火锅 is a hotpot chain with a location in Huicheng (惠城), Huizhou. It's a self-service buffet format where you get a 120-minute meal window. Here's what they typically offer:
