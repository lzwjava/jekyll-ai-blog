### 🧡 If It’s for Parents (Easy, Comfortable, Low Maintenance)

**Top pick: Linux Mint**

* Very gentle learning curve
* Traditional desktop (like Windows)
* Excellent out-of-the-box support for hardware
* Large community + tons of tutorials

**Runner-ups:**

* **Ubuntu** — great support & very popular; lots of tutorials
* **Zorin OS** — beautiful, simple, familiar UI for ex-Windows users
* **Pop!_OS** — especially good on laptops and for general use

### 🧠 Why Not Fedora / Arch for Parents?

* **Fedora** is solid but updates faster, which may require occasional learning.
* **Arch Linux** is powerful and customizable, but it’s meant for advanced users (installation, system updates, and troubleshooting are hands-on).

### 🛠 Installer & Setup Notes (for your parents)

* Most good distros come with **graphical installers** — easy next/next/finish style.
* Linux Mint / Ubuntu both boot a live USB so they can **try before installing** without touching the hard drive.
* All have **software centers** for one-click app installs.

### 📌 My Simple Recommendation

If you’re installing for **non-technical parents who want comfort and minimal fuss**:

➡️ **Linux Mint (Cinnamon edition)**
or
➡️ **Zorin OS (Core or Lite)**

Both feel polished, stable, and *friendly*.

If you want **Ubuntu compatibility + long-term support**, go with **Ubuntu LTS** (e.g., 24.04).
