Question: What does “dedicated person” mean?

Answer:
A **dedicated person** is someone who is **strongly committed to a goal, task, or responsibility**, and consistently puts in effort, time, and focus to achieve it. This person stays persistent even when things are difficult.

### Key Characteristics

* 🎯 Strong commitment to a goal
* ⏳ Willing to spend time and effort
* 💪 Persistent despite challenges
* 📈 Focused on improvement and results
* 🤝 Reliable and responsible

### Simple Examples

* A **dedicated student** studies every day and keeps improving even after failing exams.
* A **dedicated engineer** spends extra time debugging and refining code until it’s correct.
* A **dedicated parent** consistently supports and cares for their child.

### Similar Words

* Committed
* Devoted
* Hardworking
* Persistent
* Focused

### Example Sentences

* She is a **dedicated** teacher who helps students after class.
* He is **dedicated to** learning machine learning.
* A **dedicated person** doesn’t quit easily.
