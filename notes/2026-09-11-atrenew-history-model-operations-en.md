## 🏢 Company Overview

ATRenew (formerly AiHuiShou, 爱回收) is a Chinese company established in **2011** and headquartered in Shanghai, founded by Kerry Xuefeng Chen. It operates two main business segments: a second-hand product trade and service system, and an urban green industry chain business. It is listed on the NYSE under the ticker symbol **RERE**, with an IPO date of June 18, 2021.

In June 2019, AiHuiShou integrated JD.com's Paipai Marketplace, enabling it to manage JD.com's second-hand, idle, inventory, and resale businesses within the 3C (computer, communication, and consumer electronics) categories. On September 22, 2020, AiHuiShou rebranded to ATRenew and launched AHS Recycle, offering recycling services through chain stores, self-service kiosks, and online platforms.

## ⚙️ Business Model & Operations

ATRenew operates four main business lines: **爱回收** (AiHuiShou — consumer recycling), **拍机堂** (PJT Marketplace — B2B platform), **拍拍** (Paipai — second-hand e-commerce), and **AHS Device** (overseas B2B operations).

It primarily sells mobile phones, laptops, tablets, drones, digital cameras, and also vintage bags, watches, liquor, gold, and various household goods through its online platforms and offline stores, and provides services to third-party merchants.

The company has introduced AI-powered tools to improve the accuracy of device grading and pricing, enhancing customer trust and satisfaction.

For offline presence, as of December 31, 2024, ATRenew's offline stores covered 283 cities nationwide with a total of **1,861 stores**, of which 62% were the new generation stores with larger size and stronger multi-category recycling capabilities. The company plans to add 800 more stores and 1,000 additional home-visit staff in 2025.

## 📊 Financial Performance

**Full Year 2025:**
Total net revenues grew 28.9% to RMB 21.05 billion (~US$3.0 billion) for the full year 2025, versus RMB 16.33 billion in 2024. Income from operations reached RMB 171.6 million for Q4 2025 alone, compared to RMB 53.1 million in the same period of 2024.

**Q2 2026 (Most Recent):**
ATRenew reported Q2 2026 total net revenues up **32.4% year-on-year** to RMB 6.609 billion. Income from operations rose **95.7%** to RMB 178.3 million, and net income increased **78.6%** to RMB 129.1 million.

**Outlook:**
The company expects Q3 2026 revenue of CNY 6.34–6.44 billion, representing 23.1% to 25.1% year-over-year growth.

## 🌍 Overseas Expansion

ATRenew's overseas strategy focuses on the B2B platform **FoneSquare**, which accounts for roughly 90% of overseas revenue. The goal is to make FoneSquare a global version of its PJT Marketplace within 3 years, expanding into Dubai in H2 2026 and Southeast Asia thereafter. Its consumer-facing overseas brand **ReRe** ("Revalue what you own, Renew the way you choose") is in early exploration, with kiosks in Europe and a store in Hong Kong.

---

## 🌱 ESG & Sustainability

In November 2024, ATRenew's circular economy practices were included in the United Nations Global Compact (UNGC) publication of "20 Years, 20 Best Practices," making it the only company in China's second-hand consumer electronics industry to be selected.
