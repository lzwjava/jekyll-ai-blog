### Background

Born in 1991, he's the founder and CEO of **星辰教育** (Stellar Education), and the self-titled 厂长 of 恒星私域工厂. He made Forbes China's **30 Under 30** in 2017, and was listed in **Hurun 30×30** startup leaders in both 2018 and 2019.

He grew up in a teacher's family in Ji'an, Jiangxi, and started experimenting with entrepreneurship during college. After graduation he quit a state-owned enterprise job in 2015 to found an online education brand called **轻课** (Light Course).

---

### Career Arc

During university (at 对外经济贸易大学 — UIBE Beijing), he ran offline tutoring, freelanced on graphic design and digital marketing for companies like COFCO and Hanwei Investment, and served as brand director for the GSVC China competition.

After graduating in 2012, he joined a financial institution and, on the side, built multiple online communities in China, gaining strong internet operations and growth experience.

The pivot that made him famous:

In 2019, 轻课 was rebranded to **星辰教育**. In 2020, the company made over **600 million RMB** in revenue almost entirely through WeChat private domain (微信私域) channels.

That 2020 number — ¥600M+ from WeChat private traffic — is what put him on the map as a practitioner of private domain monetization at scale.
