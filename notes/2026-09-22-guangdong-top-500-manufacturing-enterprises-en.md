Question: **What is “广东省制造业500强” (Guangdong Top 500 Manufacturing Enterprises)?**

Answer:

**广东省制造业500强** is an annual ranking of the largest/leading manufacturing companies in Guangdong Province. It has been published since **2013**, organized by the **Guangdong Manufacturing Association**, **Jinan University Institute of Industrial Economics**, and **Guangdong Development and Reform Research Institute**, with guidance from provincial government departments including the Department of Industry and Information Technology. ([Jiangmen Government][1])

The key point: **it is primarily a scale ranking, not a pure technology or profitability ranking.**

### How companies get ranked

The ranking uses **annual operating revenue as the main sorting criterion**, supplemented by other indicators/data. Companies voluntarily apply, and an expert committee reviews and determines the final ranking. ([Jiangmen Government][1])

For the **2025 edition**, companies registered in Guangdong with independent legal-person status could apply; the threshold for the main Top 500 category was **RMB 4 billion+ revenue in 2024**. ([Unknown][2])

So roughly:

```text
Guangdong manufacturing companies
            │
            ▼
      Revenue / other data
            │
            ▼
    Expert review & ranking
            │
            ▼
   ┌─────────────────────┐
   │ Guangdong Top 500   │
   │ Manufacturing Cos.  │
   └─────────────────────┘
```

### What does being Top 500 actually tell you?

It is useful as a **proxy for manufacturing scale and industrial importance**.

The 2025 list had:

* **500 companies**
* **RMB 6.52 trillion** combined revenue
* **RMB 130.44 billion** average revenue/company
* **435 profitable companies**
* Strategic emerging industries accounted for **81.6%** of the companies. ([GD Government][3])

Geographically, it is heavily concentrated in the Pearl River Delta. In 2025, **Shenzhen had 86**, **Jiangmen 69**, **Foshan 66**, **Guangzhou 64**, and **Dongguan 50** companies on the list. ([Nfgb Epaper][4])

### One important distinction

Don't confuse **“广东省制造业500强”** with **“广东企业500强.”**

They measure different universes:

```text
广东企业500强
└── all major enterprises
    ├── manufacturing
    ├── finance
    ├── internet
    ├── energy
    ├── services
    └── ...

广东省制造业500强
└── manufacturing enterprises only
```

For example, Tencent can be extremely large economically but isn't what this particular manufacturing ranking is designed to measure.

Also, **“Top 500” doesn't mean an official government administrative designation like a license or certification**. It is an annual enterprise ranking/research publication conducted by the above organizations, with government departments providing guidance. ([CNGMA][5])

If you're looking at this from an **AI/enterprise-sales angle**, the list is actually quite useful: it's essentially a pre-filtered database of **~500 large Guangdong manufacturing companies with significant revenue and potentially substantial AI/automation budgets**.
