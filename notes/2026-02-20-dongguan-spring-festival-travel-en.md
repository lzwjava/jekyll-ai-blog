Question: Where to visit in Dongguan during this Spring Festival?

Answer:

During the 2026 Spring Festival (Chinese New Year, around early to mid-February), Dongguan offers a vibrant mix of traditional festive events, lantern displays, flower markets, parks for praying and viewing, and cultural activities. Many attractions feature special New Year decorations, markets, performances like lion dances, and family-friendly experiences. Here are some top recommendations based on current events and popular spots:

### Festive Events and Lantern Shows
- **Dongguan Longwan Lantern Festival (龙湾花灯会)** — A major highlight running from late January to early March 2026. It features large-scale themed lantern installations along Wandao Road, connecting Longwan Outlets and South China MALL. Enjoy dragon and lion dances, traditional performances, calligraphy activities, Yingge dance, face-changing shows, and a Spring Festival market with many stalls. Perfect for evening visits with immersive light displays and festive atmosphere.
- **2026 Spring Festival Thousand Lanterns Gala (新春千灯会·梦幻龙凤夜)** — A spectacular lantern event focused on enchanted dragon and phoenix themes, ideal for night views and photos.

### Flower Markets and Spring Fairs
- **Dongguan Spring Festival Flower Market (迎春花市)** — Held around February 8–16, 2026, near Guanming Road (southwest of Dongguan Central Park). It includes seven zones for flowers, food, pets, a Spring Festival fair, and lantern exhibitions. Easy subway access and full of festive vibes for buying flowers and enjoying the atmosphere.

### Parks and Nature Spots (Great for Family Outings and Prayers)
- **Qifeng Park (旗峰公园)** and **Liuyan Park (榴花公园)** — Popular free parks reachable by metro. Great for climbing hills to pray for good luck (登高祈福), enjoying city views, and experiencing holiday crowds with New Year cheer.
- **Dongguan Botanical Garden (东莞植物园)** — Hosting a refreshed 2026 Spring Garden Fair from February 12 to March 17, with flower viewing, garden walks, markets, and even small train rides inside. A relaxing spot for flower appreciation and festive activities.
- **Songshan Lake Park** — A scenic lake area for biking, walking, and enjoying nature—often peaceful even during the holiday.

### Other Highlights
- Many town-specific events (e.g., in Wanjiang, Houjie, or Qingxi) include flower fields, folk performances, and local markets.
- Temples or parks like Huang Daxian Park for traditional praying and blessings.
- Shopping areas and malls often have extended hours, fireworks (in some spots), and New Year sales.

Tips: Spring Festival is peak travel time—expect crowds, book tickets early for events like lantern shows if needed, use metro for easy access to many spots, and check local apps or WeChat for real-time updates on free activities. Focus on evening lantern views for the most magical experience.
