Question: What are some popular and notable apps that have been built using Flutter?

Answer:

## Hot & Notable Apps Built with Flutter

More than 1 million apps on the Play Store have been built with Flutter, spanning industries from fintech to gaming to automotive. Here are the most notable ones:

---

### 🔵 Google's Own Apps

- **Google Ads** — Google's own mobile app for managing ad campaigns, viewing stats, and receiving real-time alerts is built with Flutter, showcasing Google's trust in its own framework.
- **Google Pay** — Google Pay uses Flutter to deliver a seamless user experience across Android and iOS, ensuring stability and fast performance for millions of users worldwide.

---

### 🛒 E-Commerce & Marketplace

- **Alibaba (Xianyu)** — Alibaba improved the functionality and user experience of its Xianyu app, which has millions of users, by utilizing Flutter's rich UI capabilities and single codebase to ensure a seamless, native-like experience on both iOS and Android.
- **eBay Motors** — eBay chose Flutter to build their Motors app, which allows users to buy and sell cars, trucks, and motorcycles. Notably, there is also a macOS version of the app, highlighting Flutter's multi-platform capability.
- **Groupon** — Groupon is a popular Flutter app with 50 million downloads, available in two forms — a consumer version and a merchant version — because Flutter enables both from a single codebase.

---

### 🏦 Finance & Fintech

- **Nubank** — Nubank adopted Flutter and grew their app user base to 70 million while maintaining a lean development team. This is one of the most impressive Flutter fintech success stories.

---

### 🚗 Automotive

- **BMW (MyBMW App)** — MyBMW lets BMW owners interact with their car — lock, unlock, locate, monitor, and navigate using their mobile device. It serves as a single-point solution for managing car ownership. BMW's engineering team reported that Flutter reduced their mobile app delivery time by nearly 50%.

---

### 🎭 Entertainment & Media

- **Hamilton Musical** — The Hamilton team built the official Broadway musical app in just three months with Flutter, featuring user engagement tools such as a lottery, trivia, and stickers. They considered React Native and Xamarin but found Flutter's tools superior.
- **New York Times (KenKen Game)** — The New York Times used Flutter to create their KenKen game, once the framework allowed coding for both web and desktop platforms.

---

### 🏠 Smart Home & IoT

- **Philips Hue** — Philips Hue uses Flutter to deliver a consistent smart home lighting control experience across devices, with real-time controls and an elegant design that highlights Flutter's capability to manage interactive, challenging interfaces.

---

### 💬 Tech Giants

- **Tencent** — Tencent, one of the biggest tech firms in the world, uses Flutter for many of its apps, including those in social networking, gaming, and financial services, ensuring homogeneous user experience and quick development cycles.
- **Xiaomi** — Xiaomi uses Flutter to fuel innovation across its product ecosystem.

---

### 🎮 Gaming

- **Supercell (Supercell ID)** — Flutter helped Supercell reduce their Supercell ID core code size by 45%. Supercell is the maker of global hits like Clash of Clans and Brawl Stars.
