Question: How do you make wontons (云吞)?

Answer:

Wontons (云吞) are a staple of Cantonese cuisine, known for their thin, delicate wrappers and flavorful meat filling. Here is a classic step-by-step guide to making traditional Cantonese pork and shrimp wontons at home.

**Ingredients**

* **For the Filling:**
* 200g (7 oz) ground pork (preferably with a 20% fat ratio for juiciness)
* 150g (5 oz) fresh shrimp, peeled, deveined, and coarsely chopped
* 1 tbsp soy sauce
* 1 tsp sesame oil
* 1 tsp Shaoxing rice wine
* 1/2 tsp white pepper
* 1/2 tsp sugar
* 1/2 tsp grated ginger
* 2 green onions, finely chopped
* 1 egg white


* **For Assembling:**
* 1 pack of yellow Cantonese wonton wrappers
* Small bowl of water (for sealing)


* **For Cooking:**
* Chicken stock or seasoned bone broth
* Leafy greens (such as choy sum or bok choy)
* Chopped green onions and sesame oil for garnish



---

**Step 1: Prepare the Filling**

1. Place the chopped shrimp on a cutting board and lightly crush them with the flat side of a knife before coarsely chopping them. This creates a bouncy texture.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine the ground pork, chopped shrimp, soy sauce, sesame oil, Shaoxing wine, white pepper, sugar, grated ginger, green onions, and egg white.
3. Stir the mixture vigorously in **one direction** for 2–3 minutes until it becomes sticky and paste-like.

---

**Step 2: Assemble the Wontons**

1. Place a wonton wrapper flat on your hand.
2. Place a small tablespoon of filling (about 1 tsp to 1/2 tbsp) in the center of the wrapper.
3. Lightly wet two adjacent edges of the wrapper with water using your fingertip.
4. Fold the wrapper in half to form a triangle or rectangle, pressing the edges together to seal out any air bubbles.
5. Bring the two bottom corners together, overlap them slightly, and dab a drop of water to pinch them firmly closed. (This forms the classic "gold ingot" shape).

---

**Step 3: Cook and Serve**

1. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil.
2. Gently drop the wontons into the boiling water. Stir lightly once to prevent them from sticking to the bottom.
3. Cook for **3 to 5 minutes**. The wontons are done when they float to the top and the wrappers look translucent.
4. Remove the wontons with a slotted spoon and transfer them to bowls.
5. Ladle hot chicken stock over the wontons, add cooked greens, and garnish with chopped green onions and a drizzle of sesame oil.
