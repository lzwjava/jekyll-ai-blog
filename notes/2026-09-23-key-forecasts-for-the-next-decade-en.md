**Question:** What will the next 10 years look like — what major changes can we expect by ~2036?

**Answer:**

Here's a broad overview of what experts and futurists predict across the key areas of life:

---

**🤖 Artificial Intelligence**

AI will be among the most significant drivers of change, with artificial superintelligence, quantum computing, polyfunctional robots, and brain-computer interfaces set to reshape industries and everyday life by 2035. By 2036, AI will be so embedded in daily professional life that describing it will feel like describing oxygen — every knowledge worker will have AI systems that know their work style, priorities, and professional history. The central question won't be whether to use AI, but how to maintain distinctly human judgment and creativity that AI cannot replicate.

---

**🏥 Healthcare**

Personal health monitoring will cross a threshold where most chronic disease is managed in real time rather than treated after the fact. Continuous, non-invasive wearables will monitor blood glucose, cardiac rhythms, inflammation markers, and hormonal levels, giving individuals and physicians a real-time biological picture that makes today's annual physical look like guesswork.

---

**🌍 Climate & Energy**

Energy prices are expected to fall, led by the development of renewables becoming cheaper than fossil fuels. Battery technology will be significantly improved, though fusion power will likely still be "10 years away." Global temperatures will likely have risen by another 0.2–0.5°C, possibly surpassing the 1.5°C threshold.

---

**💼 Work & Society**

Experts from the Pew Research Center have deep concerns about people's overall well-being alongside great expectations for advances in health care, scientific progress, and education. Humanoid robots will become much more common and increasingly commoditized. Lifestyle expectations will have risen, making raising children more expensive, and by the mid-2030s, those aged 80 and over will globally outnumber infants.

---

**📱 Technology & Daily Life**

Just as transformative products like Uber, Instagram, and smartphone GPS didn't exist 10 years before they became essential, the next decade will likely bring technologies equally hard to imagine today — including displays that could project images directly to the retina, eliminating the need for screens or headsets entirely.

---

The overarching theme: the pace of change will accelerate, the benefits will be unevenly distributed, and the biggest decisions will be social and political — not just technological.
