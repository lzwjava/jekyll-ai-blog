**Question:** For ASU's Psychology program — can someone with only an associate degree (专科/Zhuanke, from China) apply? And is there a 1+1 program?

---

**Answer:**

This is an important and nuanced question, especially for Chinese students. Here's the full picture:

---

### 🔴 Direct Graduate Admission with 专科 (Zhuanke)? — NO

Zhuanke (专科) programs in China, typically three years in length, are offered by vocational or specialized colleges and emphasize applied skills and professional training. Graduates receive only a Zhuanke Graduation Certificate, not a bachelor's degree. As a result, Zhuanke graduates — despite completing years of postsecondary study — are **not currently admissible** to graduate programs at Arizona State University (ASU) or most U.S. institutions.

So a 专科 holder **cannot** directly apply to ASU's MS or PhD in Psychology.

---

### 🟢 BUT — ASU Has a Special Pathway Just for 专科 Graduates

ASU recognized this gap and created a dedicated program:

**Graduate Certificate in Psychological Science (Mandarin Pathway)**
- This program is offered **for Mandarin-speaking students in China**, with instruction **entirely in Mandarin**. Eligibility is strictly limited to Mandarin-speaking applicants from China. Applicants who hold a **Benke Graduation Certificate or Zhuanke without a bachelor's degree equivalent are eligible to apply.**
- By completing this **15-credit certificate**, students develop deeper knowledge of psychology across scientific and cultural domains, strengthening their preparation for advanced graduate study while also gaining a stand-alone academic credential.
- This certificate builds **a pipeline to the general MS in Psychology** program.

There's also a lighter-weight option:

**Psychological Fundamentals of Well-being (MicroCertificate, Mandarin Pathway)**
- The **9-credit microcertificate** is designed for **Benke-without-degree** students — those who completed four years of undergraduate study but did not receive a formal degree.

---

### ❓ Is There a "1+1" Program?

There is **no formally labeled "1+1" program** at ASU for Psychology. However, the intended pathway is:

> **Graduate Certificate in Psychological Science** (Mandarin, ~15 credits) → **MS in Psychology (Mandarin Pathway)**

ASU offers an online **Master of Science in Psychology (Mandarin Pathway)** — taught in Mandarin — as one of four degrees offered through the ASU ZaiXian (在线) platform for learners in China.

This **Certificate → MS** route is what Chinese education agencies often describe as a "1+1" structure, but ASU itself does not use that label. Completing the Graduate Certificate first demonstrates graduate readiness, which then qualifies you to apply to the full MS program.
