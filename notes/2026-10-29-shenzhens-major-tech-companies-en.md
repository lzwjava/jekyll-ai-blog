### 🌐 The Bigger Picture

Shenzhen now has more than 2,600 major AI companies, with an industry spanning AI chips, algorithm frameworks and foundation models, as well as software and hardware applications.

At CES 2026, Chinese firms claimed more than 100 out of 247 total innovation awards, with several Shenzhen-based companies winning Best of Innovation in major categories — a sign that Shenzhen has moved well beyond "factory city" into deep R&D territory.
