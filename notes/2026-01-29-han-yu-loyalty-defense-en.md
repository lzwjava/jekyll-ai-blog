**Question:** Introduction to 《张中丞传后叙》

**Answer:**

《张中丞传后叙》 is one of the most famous **"后叙" (postscript / afterword / supplementary biography)** pieces in the history of Chinese literature.  
It was written by **Han Yu (韩愈)**, the great leader of the Tang Dynasty Ancient-Style Prose Movement (古文运动).

### Quick Facts

- **Author**: Han Yu (韩愈, 768–824)  
- **Year**: around 815–819 (most scholars believe 815 or 817)  
- **Type**: argumentative biographical essay + eulogy + polemic essay  
- **Length**: about 900 characters — short but extremely powerful  
- **Status in literary history**: regarded as one of the **"Three Great Postscripts" (三大书后/三大后叙)** in Tang prose, together with  
  - 《圬者王承福传后叙》 (also by Han Yu)  
  - 《种树郭橐驼传后叙》 (also by Han Yu)

### Core Background Story

**Main figure**: **Zhang Xun (张巡)** — Tang general  
During the **An-Shi Rebellion (安史之乱)**, in 757 AD, the city of **Suiyang (睢阳)** was besieged by rebel forces led by Yin Ziqi (尹子奇) for a very long time.

- The Tang army and civilians inside the city ran out of food completely.  
- They ate tree bark → paper → horses → women & children → old people → eventually large-scale cannibalism occurred.  
- Zhang Xun killed his own maid and concubine to feed the soldiers, then killed and ate the citizens’ wives and children.  
- Despite this extreme sacrifice, the city still fell.  
- Zhang Xun, Xu Yuan (许远), Nan Jiyun (南霁云) and 36 other loyal officers were captured and executed by the rebels.

After the rebellion was suppressed, some officials and historians began to **criticize Zhang Xun**, saying:

- “He should have abandoned the city earlier and saved the people.”  
- “Killing and eating people is inhumane — even if for loyalty, it is still wrong.”

### What Han Yu Did in This Piece

Han Yu wrote **《张中丞传后叙》** as a fierce **defense** of Zhang Xun and his loyal followers.

He used three main layers of argument:

1. **Attacked the critics directly**  
   → pointed out that those who blamed Zhang Xun mostly **did not experience the siege themselves** and spoke from a safe distance after the war → very unfair.

2. **Used concrete, vivid testimonies**  
   Han Yu collected and quoted many eyewitness accounts (especially from Nan Jiyun’s follower), describing:
   - how Zhang Xun remained extremely calm and dignified even when eating human flesh  
   - how he encouraged soldiers with powerful and righteous words  
   - how he refused to surrender until the very last moment

3. **Raised the moral height dramatically**  
   Han Yu argued:

   > “When a city is besieged and there is no help coming, is it better to die for righteousness and make the enemy pay a huge price, or to abandon the city and survive?”

   He strongly supported the first choice and portrayed Zhang Xun as a **tragic hero** who chose **“to die so the meaning of loyalty survives”**.

### Most Famous Sentences (still quoted today)

- 疾呼于众曰：「吾为节度使，义不与贼俱生！」  
- 「南霁云之未绝也，抽矢射佛寺浮图，矢着其上砖半箭，曰：『吾破贼后，则必为此矢所中！』」  
- 「一世之杰也，而后世或不与，斯可谓知言也已！」

### Literary Value & Why It Is So Important

- Extremely strong **emotion + logic combination**  
- Uses **dialogue + vivid detail + sharp refutation** → very close to modern reportage literature  
- Created a model for later **“后叙” / “书后”** writing  
- Demonstrates the highest achievement of **Tang guwen (ancient-style prose)**: **clear reasoning + strong emotion + vivid narration**

In short:  
《张中丞传后叙》 is **not just a defense of Zhang Xun** — it is **Han Yu using one historical event to express his lifelong ideal of “文以载道” (literature should carry moral principles)** and his uncompromising attitude toward loyalty, righteousness, and historical justice.
