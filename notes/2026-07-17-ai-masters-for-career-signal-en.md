## But check one important thing: "Título Propio"

The biggest risk is not the major.

It is the **type of degree**.

Spain has two categories:

### Official Master's Degree

Spanish:

```
Máster Universitario Oficial
```

Usually:

* recognized under European Higher Education Area
* ECTS
* can lead to PhD
* listed in official registries

### Own Degree

Spanish:

```
Título Propio
```

Usually:

* issued by university
* professional education
* not equivalent to official master's
* recognition depends on employer

The conversation mentions:

> "很多西班牙人都是拿这个 Title Propio"

This is the part I would verify carefully.

For employment:

```
Foreign company:
maybe OK

Immigration:
depends

PhD:
usually no

Government jobs:
may not count
```

So before paying 20k RMB, ask:

1. Is it **Máster Universitario Oficial** or **Título Propio**?
2. Is it registered in Spain's RUCT?
3. How many ECTS?
4. Who issues the diploma?
5. Does the diploma say "Master's Degree" or "Certificate"?
