**Answer:**

**About Dataland (迪兰 / 迪兰恒进)**

Dataland (迪兰) is a brand under Beijing Dataland Technology Co., Ltd. (北京市迪兰恒进科技有限公司), founded in 1999. It was one of the most prominent GPU brands in mainland China, focusing exclusively on graphics cards.

Dataland was the first brand to officially sell ATI graphics cards in mainland China, and also the first to be authorized by ATI as an "ATI Best Partner" in the region. For years it was AMD's primary strategic partner in China.

Behind the scenes, Dataland was actually the mainland China sales agent for PowerColor (撼讯), a GPU brand under the TUL Group. When PowerColor eventually entered the Chinese market directly under its own name, Dataland lost its footing.

Dataland's last product generation was the RX 6000 series — they produced cards as high-end as the RX 6900 XT — but they never released any RX 7000-series products. As of May 2024, their official website has expired and closed, their flagship stores on JD.com and Taobao have shut down, marking the brand's definitive end.

**About the Dataland R9 270 GPU**

The R9 270 was an AMD Radeon mid-range card from around 2013–2014, and Dataland released several variants under their "酷能" (Cool Power) product line:

| Spec | Value |
| --- | --- |
| GPU Chip | AMD Radeon R9 270 |
| Stream Processors | 1,280 |
| Core Clock | 930 MHz (955 MHz Boost) |
| Memory | 2 GB GDDR5 |
| Memory Bus | 256-bit |
| Memory Clock | 5,600 MHz |
| Power Connector | 6-pin × 1 |
| Recommended PSU | 500W+ |
| Outputs | HDMI, DVI, DisplayPort |
| Cooling | Dual-fan |

The card measured 212 × 111 × 36 mm and came with a DVI-to-VGA adapter, driver disc, and user manual. It was positioned as a good-value gaming card at the time, competing favorably with NVIDIA's GTX 760 in the same price range.
