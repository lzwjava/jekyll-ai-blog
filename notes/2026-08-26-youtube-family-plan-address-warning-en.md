Question: How should I handle a YouTube Premium Family Plan warning that says I don't live at the same address as the family group manager, and my membership may be suspended in 14 days?

Answer:

This is a policy enforcement notice from YouTube. Here's what you can do:

**Understand the Situation**

YouTube Premium Family Plans require all members to reside at the same household address as the plan manager. YouTube detects address mismatches using signals like IP address, location data, and device info.

**Your Options**

1. **If you genuinely live at the same address:**
   - Click the "Contact us" link in the email to dispute the decision.
   - Explain that you do share the same household and ask them to review your case.
   - You may need to provide verification (e.g., confirm your home address in your Google account settings).

2. **If you do NOT live at the same address:**
   - YouTube's policy does not allow sharing a Family Plan across different households — this is a terms of service violation.
   - You should get your own individual YouTube Premium subscription, or ask the family group manager to remove you and subscribe separately.

3. **If you do nothing:**
   - After 14 days, your Premium benefits will be suspended.
   - You will still remain in the family group and can watch YouTube with ads, but Premium features (ad-free, background play, downloads) will be unavailable.

**Steps to Take Right Now**

- Check that your **home address in your Google Account** matches the family manager's address (if applicable).
- Go to [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com) → Personal info → Address, and update it if needed.
- Then contact YouTube support to request a review before the 14-day window closes.
