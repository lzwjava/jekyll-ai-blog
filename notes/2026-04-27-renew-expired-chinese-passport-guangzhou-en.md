**Question:** How do I renew (replace) my expired Chinese passport in Guangzhou, Huangpu District, as a Guangzhou citizen? My passport was valid from 2015 to 2025 (expired about one year ago), and it contains an expired US visa.

---

**Answer:**

## Overview of Your Situation

Your passport has **expired** (not just near expiry), which qualifies you for a **passport replacement (换发/换领)**. In China, there is no "renewal" — you apply for a brand-new passport while surrendering your old one. The expired US visa in the old passport is **not an obstacle** to replacing your Chinese passport, but note that the old passport (with the visa) will be retained by the authorities or returned to you cancelled. If you ever need to use the unexpired portion of that visa for travel purposes, check with the destination country or US consulate first.

---

## ✅ Step 1 — Check If You Qualify for Online ("全程网办") Processing

Guangzhou is one of the 20 pilot cities where residents aged 16 and above (excluding government workers and active military) can submit passport replacement applications entirely online through the National Immigration Administration's service platform — via webpage, APP, WeChat, or Alipay mini-programs, without needing to visit in person at all.

**Online platforms to use:**
- **移民局12367** WeChat/Alipay mini-program
- **国家移民管理局政务服务平台** (National Immigration Administration website)

If you qualify (Guangzhou hukou, 16+, not a registered government employee or active military), you can complete the entire process without stepping outside.

---

## 🏢 Step 2 — If You Prefer or Must Go In Person (Huangpu Location)

The Huangpu District exit-entry service hall is located at: **黄埔区香雪三路3号政务服务中心2楼B区** (Huangpu District Xiangxue Administrative Service Center, Building 2, Floor 2, Zone B). Consultation phone: **12367**. Reception hours: Monday–Friday 9:00–12:00 / 13:00–17:00; Saturday 9:00–12:00 / 13:00–17:00. (Holiday arrangements vary, and advance appointment is required.)

---

## 📋 Step 3 — Make an Appointment (Required)

You **must** book an appointment before visiting. Options:

- Open the **广州微警务** (Guangzhou Police) WeChat mini-program → tap "出入境预约" (Exit-Entry Appointment) → fill in your ID number, name, hukou location, phone number → select the Huangpu service center, preferred date and time → choose service type: **普通护照 → 换发 (Ordinary Passport → Replacement)** → enter your old passport number, expiry date, and destination/reason for travel.

- Alternatively, use the **广东政务服务网** (Guangdong Government Services website).

---

## 📄 Step 4 — Required Documents

The documents you need to bring are:
1. **Application form** (can be pre-printed or filled out at the hall)
2. **One recent front-facing, hatless, blue-background color photo**
3. **Digital photo verification receipt** (valid within Guangdong Province — obtained through the "照片回执号" WeChat mini-program)
4. **Valid National ID card (居民身份证)**
5. **Your current (expired) passport** — original required, since you are replacing an expired passport

> Since your passport expired over a year ago (not just under 6 months), you still qualify — a passport that is expired or has less than 6 months' validity remaining is a valid reason to apply for replacement.

**Note on the US visa:** The expired US visa in your old passport will not affect your Chinese passport replacement. However, bring both documents in case officers ask to note prior visa history.

---

## 💰 Step 5 — Fee

The cost to replace a passport in Guangzhou is **¥120 per passport**.

---

## ⏱️ Step 6 — Processing Time

The standard processing time for a passport issued at your place of household registration (户籍地) is **7 working days**.

**Regarding your expired US visa:** The visa is in your old passport which will be surrendered. If you plan to travel to the US in the future, you will need to apply for a new US visa with your new Chinese passport. The expired visa itself provides no benefit after expiry.
