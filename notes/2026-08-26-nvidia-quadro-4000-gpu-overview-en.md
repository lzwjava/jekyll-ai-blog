**Question:** What is the NVIDIA Quadro 4000 chip, and what is it used for?

**Answer:**

The **NVIDIA Quadro 4000** is a professional-grade workstation GPU (Graphics Processing Unit) made by NVIDIA, designed for professionals rather than everyday gaming. Here's a breakdown:

---

### 🔹 What it is

It was launched in July 2011 as a desktop workstation GPU built on the **Fermi architecture**, produced on a 40nm manufacturing process.

---

### 🔹 Key Specifications

It features **256 CUDA cores**, a 475 MHz core clock, 32 ROPs, and 32 texture units. It comes with **2 GB of GDDR5 memory** on a 256-bit interface, delivering 89.6 GB/s of memory bandwidth, and has a maximum power draw of **142W**.

---

### 🔹 What it's used for

The Quadro 4000 is not just a graphics processor — it drives an entire visual supercomputing platform, supporting advanced capabilities like **stereoscopic 3D**, scalable visualization, and 3D high-definition broadcasting.

It delivers ultra-fast performance across a broad range of **design, animation, and video applications**, and supports Microsoft DirectX 11.0 and Shader Model 5.0 hardware acceleration.

---

### 🔹 Who it's for

It's targeted at **professional users** — engineers, designers, 3D artists, and animators — not gamers. The "Quadro" brand from NVIDIA has always meant workstation-class reliability and optimized drivers for professional software (like AutoCAD, Maya, etc.).

> **Note:** There is also a newer **Quadro RTX 4000** (2019), which is a completely different, much more powerful card based on the Turing architecture with ray tracing support. Don't confuse the two.
