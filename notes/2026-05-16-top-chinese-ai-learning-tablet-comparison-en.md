## Chinese AI Learning Tablets Comparison (2025): Which One Is Best for Your Child?

These five brands dominate China's K12 smart learning device market. Each has a distinct identity, strength, and target audience. Here is a comprehensive breakdown:

---

## 🏆 Market Overview

According to iimedia Research, the top three brands in China's intelligent learning tablet market in 2025 are **作业帮 (Zuoyebang)**, **学而思 (Xueersi)**, and **科大讯飞 (iFlytek)**.

In terms of market share on JD.com (京东), iFlytek and Xiaodu (百度) together account for over 40% of the market. Back in 2021, 步步高 (BBK) led with nearly 30% market share, but by 2023 AI-focused brands like iFlytek overtook it.

---

## 1. 🟠 作业帮学习机 (Zuoyebang)

**Best for: Exam prep, heavy practice, middle & high school students**

The 作业帮 T30 features a "real exam + sync practice" dual mode, integrating the DeepSeek large model for intelligent test paper generation, and draws from a question bank of China's top 100 schools. Its 12.7-inch paper-like screen uses AG anti-glare technology with a writing latency as low as 35ms. The AI supports voice-based Q&A and step-by-step math problem solving. Its question bank is updated monthly with over 50,000 new questions, making it especially suited for exam sprint preparation.

Both 作业帮 and 学而思 are well-known K12 education companies with rich self-developed curriculum resources. 作业帮's synchronized courses cover 9 subjects from elementary through high school, giving it a longer useful lifespan.

**✅ Strengths:** Deep question bank, fast updates, strong AI exam coaching, good math/science step-by-step breakdowns
**❌ Weaknesses:** Handwriting stylus is not included in standard package; less comprehensive for young children

---

## 2. 🔵 科大讯飞 (iFlytek)

**Best for: English learning, AI precision, tech-savvy families**

iFlytek is a leading AI speech and language company in Asia-Pacific. Its AI learning tablet uses an AI knowledge graph and learning sequence technology — diagnosing weaknesses, recommending knowledge points, and reinforcing them in a three-step method. It pioneered AI "1-on-1" precision learning and AI tutoring, making study more efficient.

iFlytek has a clear advantage in English translation and spoken English due to its expertise in language AI. However, it lacks self-developed curriculum resources across all subjects and must source content from third parties, which can make learning less systematic in non-English subjects.

In a 2025 multi-brand comparison test, iFlytek's X3 5G version stood out most clearly in AI precision learning, thanks to its powerful Spark (星火) large model.

The budget-friendly iFlytek P30 retains core AI features with an 11-inch screen, 6GB+256GB specs, split-screen learning support, and a special 3D animation module for breaking down difficult science concepts. Its system is frequently updated and curriculum resources align well with China's new national curriculum standards.

**✅ Strengths:** Best AI/speech technology, excellent English speaking & pronunciation coaching, strong AI engine
**❌ Weaknesses:** No proprietary curriculum team, weaker subject depth outside English

---

## 3. 🟢 学而思 (Xueersi / Good Future)

**Best for: Math excellence, structured learning, all ages**

学而思, founded in 2003 under Good Future (好未来), uses strong tech and content capabilities for lifelong learning. Its learning tablet covers all grades from early childhood through high school and all subjects, keeping content updated to the new curriculum standards. It includes 60+ AI features such as essay correction, translation, dictation, and mental math practice, aiming for a personalized experience.

学而思 is particularly strong in mathematics, though other subjects are comparatively average. It has the advantage of being a fully structured educational company with self-developed courses.

**✅ Strengths:** Outstanding math curriculum, complete content ecosystem, 60+ AI tools, good for families who want structured courses
**❌ Weaknesses:** Non-math subjects are less impressive; premium models can be expensive

---

## 4. 🟡 步步高 (BBK / eebbk)

**Best for: Young children (preschool–elementary), English, comprehensive family use**

步步高 has been in the education electronics space for 30 years. Its flagship S9 excels at English learning and homework coaching with AI-guided tutoring that uses Tsinghua/Peking University students' reasoning style rather than just giving direct answers. The AI personalization planner acts like an expert to customize a child's learning path.

The S9 and A8 models cover over 95% of national mainstream textbooks from elementary to high school. The X6 model is designed for preschool to Grade 3, featuring Hongen (洪恩) international early education courses permanently free, Oxford reader resources, and a "5P Learning Through Play" system for young children.

The A8 model is a cost-effective option for elementary and middle schoolers focused on practice — it supports one-touch switching to a "study-only mode" that disables all entertainment apps, and features AI-generated error-based practice sets.

**✅ Strengths:** Trusted brand, strong English focus, best for young children (X6), New Oriental (新东方) exclusive course content, excellent offline/after-sales service nationwide
**❌ Weaknesses:** Flagship S9 is expensive; heavier device; entry models lack advanced AI features

---

## 5. 🟣 希沃 (Seewo)

**Best for: Interactive classroom-style learning, parental monitoring**

希沃's learning tablet features a high-resolution screen with efficient processing for lag-free interactive learning. Its anti-blue-light screen design reduces eye fatigue during long sessions. The parent management system lets parents check learning progress and identify strengths and weaknesses remotely.

For English learning, 希沃 uses voice recognition to accurately assess children's pronunciation and provide immediate feedback, functioning like a personal tutor. It also covers math, Chinese, and other subjects through interactive Q&A and a diverse question bank.

**✅ Strengths:** Strong hardware, good eye-protection screen, interactive features, solid parental controls
**❌ Weaknesses:** Less brand recognition in the dedicated learning tablet market compared to iFlytek or 作业帮; fewer proprietary educational resources
