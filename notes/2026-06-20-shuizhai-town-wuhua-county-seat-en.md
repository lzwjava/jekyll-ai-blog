**水寨镇 (Shuizhai Town / Shuǐzhài Zhèn)** is a town in **Wuhua County (五华县), Meizhou City, Guangdong Province**, southern China.

Key facts:

- It serves as the **county seat** of Wuhua County — the seat of government for the county's 16 towns is located in Shuizhai.
- Wuhua County itself covers **3,237.8 km²** in eastern Guangdong, situated in the upper reaches of the Han River (韩江上游).
- The name 水寨 literally means **"water village" or "water stronghold"** — 水 (shuǐ) = water, 寨 (zhài) = fortified village or stockade, historically built near water sources or for riverside defense.
- The area is predominantly **Hakka (客家)** — Wuhua/Wuhua County is a core Hakka region in Guangdong, and Shuizhai as the county seat is culturally Hakka.
- Wuhua County borders Fengshun to the east, Jiexi and Lufeng to the south, Zijin/Dongyuan/Longchuan to the west, and Xingning to the north.
