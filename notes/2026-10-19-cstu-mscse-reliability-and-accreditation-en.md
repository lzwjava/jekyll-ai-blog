### About CSTU (美国加州科技大学)

Here is a fact-based breakdown:

**What it is:**
CSTU was founded in September 2011 in Silicon Valley and is located at 1601 McCarthy Boulevard in Milpitas, California. It offers Bachelor's and Master's degree programs in Computer Systems and Engineering (BSCSE/MSCSE), Business Administration (BSBA/MBA), and an Emerging Technology Training Program (ETTP).

**Accreditation:**
CSTU is accredited by the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges (ACCSC), recognized by the U.S. Department of Education, and is also a member of the Council for Higher Education Accreditation (CHEA).

However, **ACCSC is a vocational/career-school accreditor**, not a regional accreditor (like WASC, which covers California's major universities). Regional accreditation is the U.S. gold standard and is what most employers and graduate schools expect. An earlier school catalog explicitly disclosed that "CSTU and its degree programs are not accredited by an accrediting agency recognized by the United States Department of Education" — this changed only when CSTU received ACCSC accreditation in 2022, but the vocational-track nature of that accreditation remains an important caveat.

**CPT (Curricular Practical Training) — Key Red Flag:**
CSTU heavily markets its MSCSE and MBA programs as "CPT Friendly" — meaning F-1 visa students can receive CPT authorization from their very first day of class, with no one-year waiting period requirement. Schools that market day-one CPT as a primary selling point are commonly known as "CPT mills," and they draw significant scrutiny from U.S. immigration authorities. Following directives from the Student and Exchange Visitor Programme (SEVP) tightening oversight of CPT authorization, several major U.S. universities including UC Berkeley, NYU, USC, and UPenn paused or restricted CPT applications. Using CSTU primarily as a CPT vehicle carries immigration risk.

**Scale and focus:**
According to CSTU's 2023 annual report filed with California's BPPE, only 32 students were enrolled in diploma or certificate programs. It is an extremely small, for-profit institution catering mainly to Chinese international students.

**Chinese recognition:**
CSTU is recognized as a higher education institution by China's Ministry of Education (教育部), and degrees may be certified through China's study-abroad credential services. If your goal is credential recognition back in China, this is a positive point.

**Cost:**
For Chinese students studying in Beijing classrooms, total tuition is approximately 79,000 RMB; students who travel to the U.S. campus pay around $21,000 USD (as of 2024).
