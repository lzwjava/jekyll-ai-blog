Question: Can I eat 天海藏安格斯烤牛肉 if baked at 160°C for 15–20 minutes but it is still not fully cooked?

Answer:

It depends on whether the product is **raw beef** or **pre-cooked beef**.

* If it is **raw Angus beef (生牛肉 / needs cooking)**:

  * 160°C for 15–20 minutes may **not be enough**, especially if the piece is thick or started cold from the refrigerator.
  * Beef can look brown outside while the center is still undercooked.
  * The safest way is to check the internal temperature:

    * **Medium rare:** ~54–57°C (higher risk if not from a trusted source)
    * **Medium:** ~60–63°C
    * **Well done / safer:** ≥70°C
  * If you do not have a thermometer, continue baking until the center is no longer cold/red and juices are clear.

* If it is a **ready-to-eat roast beef product (熟制品 / 即食)**:

  * Heating is mainly for warming and texture; it is usually already cooked.
  * A slightly pink color can be normal for roast beef because of curing/cooking methods.

For packaged products like 天海藏安格斯烤牛肉, check the label:

* "即食" / "熟制" → usually already cooked.
* "生制品" / "需加热熟制" → cook thoroughly.

If you already ate some:

* A slightly pink center does not automatically mean danger.
* Monitor for symptoms such as stomach pain, vomiting, diarrhea, or fever over the next few days.
* Higher caution is needed for children, pregnant people, elderly people, or people with weakened immunity.

For future batches, a simple method:

1. Preheat oven to 180–200°C.
2. Put beef in.
3. Use a meat thermometer in the thickest part.
4. Remove only when the target internal temperature is reached.
