**Question:** What is Upwork's mission and philosophy?

**Answer:**

---

## 🎯 Mission

**"Our mission is to create economic opportunities so people have better lives."**

---

## 👁️ Vision

**"Our vision is independent talent at the heart of every business."**

And more broadly: to connect businesses with great talent to work **without limits**.

---

## 💡 Core Philosophy

**1. Work Without Borders**
The founding philosophy from day one was that geography should never limit who you can work with. Upwork exists to create opportunity in every era of work.

**2. Economic Empowerment**
The mission centers on connecting businesses with talent at scale, with core values focused on trust, transparency, and outcomes.

**3. Skills Over Location**
The vision emphasizes a future where work is skills-driven and flexible, shaping AI matching, client growth, and platform policies.

**4. AI-Human Collaboration (2025 direction)**
In 2025, Upwork embedded Uma™, their "Mindful AI," into every step of how work gets discovered, scoped, and delivered — with the belief that the more work that happens on Upwork, the more opportunity they create.

---

## 📊 Scale (2025)

Upwork completed a three-year transformation journey, achieving over $4 billion in GSV, $788 million in revenue, and $226 million in adjusted EBITDA in full year 2025.

---

In short, Upwork's philosophy is simple: **talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not** — and their job is to fix that gap.
