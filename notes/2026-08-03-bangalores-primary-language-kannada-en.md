Question: What language do people in Bangalore speak — is it Kannada, not Tamil?

Answer: Correct, not Tamil. Bangalore is the capital of Karnataka state, so Kannada is the official and primary language. That said, Bangalore is a major tech/immigrant hub, so you'll hear a lot of English (common in professional/IT settings) and Hindi too, plus other regional languages from migrant workers (Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam included) since people move there from across India for work. But the native/local language is Kannada.

Tamil is spoken in Tamil Nadu (Chennai), which is a different state south-east of Karnataka.
