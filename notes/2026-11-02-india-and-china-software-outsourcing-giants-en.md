**Question:** What are the major companies in China and India doing software outsourcing for the USA and globally?

**Answer:**

## 🇮🇳 India — The Global Leader

India has been the world's go-to destination for software outsourcing for over two decades — and the case has gotten stronger. Today's top companies are shipping complex SaaS platforms, building AI-integrated products, and running full product cycles for clients across North America, Europe, and APAC.

India's IT sector surpassed $300 billion in revenue for the first time in fiscal 2026, driven by rapid AI-driven changes. North American firms remain the main engine of India's expansion, accounting for two-thirds of new setups.

## 🇨🇳 China — Rising High-Tech Player

China is a global leader in emerging technologies such as AI, big data, and IoT, making it a strong choice for companies seeking innovative and large-scale software development solutions. Backed by massive government investment in tech and a growing base of experienced engineers, China continues to rise as a serious player in the software outsourcing space. Rates: $30–$60/hour. Best for AI/ML, IoT, R&D-intensive projects and enterprises seeking large-scale, high-performance software systems.

## 📊 Market Size

The global IT outsourcing market hit **$638 billion** in 2026. 92% of Global 2000 companies outsource at least one IT function, and 44% of outsourcing contracts now include AI and automation components.

India's offshore technology centers alone generated **$98.4 billion** in revenue for fiscal 2026, nearing levels earlier projected for 2030.
