Question: What is a comprehensive introduction to the Zhuhai Museum and Planning Exhibition Hall on Qinglv Road?

Answer: The **Zhuhai Museum** (珠海博物馆) and the adjacent **Zhuhai Planning Exhibition Hall** (珠海规划展览馆) are iconic cultural landmarks located at the intersection of **Qinglv Road** (Lovers' Road) and Haihong Road. Opened in their new location in late 2020, this dual-venue complex offers a deep dive into the city’s ancient history and its ambitious future.

### **Architectural Design**

Designed by the renowned German firm **gmp Architekten**, the complex is composed of two distinct volumes that symbolize the city's "past" and "future":

* **The Horizontal Tract:** Facing the mountains, this section houses the Zhuhai Museum, focusing on history and local culture.
* **The Vertical Tract:** Facing the sea, this section houses the Planning Exhibition Hall, focusing on urban development and the city's future vision.
* **The Shared Foyer:** A central glass atrium connects the two, offering panoramic views of the coast and the nearby **Zhuhai Opera House** (the "Sun and Moon Shell").

---

### **Zhuhai Museum (The Past)**

The museum spans three main floors and preserves over 15,000 artifacts.

* **History & Folklore:** Exhibits cover everything from the Neolithic era to the Tanka people's "water weddings" and traditional maritime culture.
* **Maritime History:** Significant focus on the Xiangshan salt industry and the naval wars of the Song and Yuan dynasties.
* **Modern Reform:** Displays documenting Zhuhai's transition into one of China's first Special Economic Zones.

### **Planning Exhibition Hall (The Future)**

This section is highly interactive and visually stunning:

* **Giant City Model:** The highlight is a massive **30 x 40 meter scale model** of Zhuhai, showing current infrastructure alongside planned future developments.
* **The Spiral Walkway:** Visitors ascend a spiral gangway that winds through eight floors of cascading exhibition rooms, providing a literal "ascent" through the city's planning history.
* **Interactive Tech:** Includes 4D theaters and flight simulators that allow visitors to "fly" over the future cityscape.

### **Getting There**

The museum is easily accessible via the scenic **Qinglv Road**. You can take public buses (Lines 2, 3, 3A, or 10) to the **Huazishi East** (华子石东) station or use the Zhuhai Sightseeing Bus which stops directly nearby.
