**Question:** What's the latest on OpenAI's new model GPT-6 Astra?

**Answer:**

GPT-6 Astra is very fresh — it just dropped a few days ago. Here's the rundown:

**Release**
GPT-6 Astra is OpenAI's frontier AI model released on September 3, 2026. OpenAI describes it as its most capable and aligned model so far, with major gains in computer use, coding, scientific reasoning, cybersecurity, and professional workflows.

**What makes it different**
Astra is built more like a computer operator than a chatbot — it can use software, inspect screens, build websites, generate documents, run QA checks, work in coding environments, analyze scientific data, and even model environments in Blender.

**Benchmarks**
OpenAI claims GPT-6 Astra earned perfect or near-perfect scores in key reasoning benchmarks, beating both its prior model GPT-5.6 Sol and rival Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.

**AGI claims**
OpenAI president Greg Brockman called it a "generational leap" and said it could eventually be seen as the arrival of artificial general intelligence (AGI). It also marks the first time OpenAI has used other AI models in a significant role to supervise a model's training.

**Cybersecurity**
Astra is the first model OpenAI has designated as reaching its "critical" cybersecurity threshold under its Preparedness Framework — meaning it can potentially find and exploit previously unknown vulnerabilities across well-protected systems without step-by-step human guidance. OpenAI slowed the release to add safety testing after discovering this.

**Rollout & Availability**
GPT-6 Astra is rolling out first to a limited set of organizations, and over the coming days will become available to all ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users, as well as through the OpenAI API and AWS.

**Pricing (API)**
The API is priced at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens, with a 1,050,000-token context window, 128K max output, and a knowledge cutoff of April 30, 2026.
