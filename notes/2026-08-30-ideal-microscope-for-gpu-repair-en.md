**Question:** Is the 博珂视觉 (BK7045) 7-50X continuous zoom binocular stereo microscope with 0.5X auxiliary objective suitable for GPU card / electronics repair?

---

**Answer:**

**Yes — this is a genuinely good choice for electronics repair.** This is a proper professional-class tool, not a toy.

Here's a full breakdown:

**✅ What makes it excellent for repair work:**

| Feature | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **7–50X continuous zoom** | A range of 7–50X is ideal because it balances high magnification with enough physical space to use tools under the lens — you can zoom out to navigate the board and zoom in to inspect solder joints |
| **Stereo / 体视 type** | Gives true 3D depth perception — essential for soldering and component placement |
| **连续变倍 (continuous zoom)** | No fixed steps — smooth zoom exactly like professional repair shops use |
| **Marketed for 手机维修/电路板** | Designed for phone repair and PCB work from the ground up |
| **0.5X auxiliary objective** | This is a key accessory — the 0.5X auxiliary lens gives about 16 cm of working distance, while reducing magnification to 3.5X–22.5X, which gives you much more room to fit a soldering iron or tweezers underneath |

**Understanding the magnification numbers:**

The "90倍" claim in the listing uses the 0.5X objective *inverted* logic — ignore that marketing number. What matters practically:

- **Base setup (no aux lens):** 7X–45X, working distance ~100mm
- **With 0.5X aux lens:** ~3.5X–22.5X, working distance ~160mm+ (more tool room)
- The image quality with no extra lens and the 10X eyepieces is absolutely stunning, and the same can be said when using the 2X auxiliary lens

**Things to check before buying:**

- **Does it include a stand?** — Make sure the listing includes a boom arm (摆臂支架) or post stand (立柱支架), not just the microscope head
- **Light source** — Check if it includes a ring light (环形灯); if not, budget for one separately
- **双目 vs 三目** — This is binocular (双目); if you later want to attach a camera, you'd need the trinocular (三目) version
