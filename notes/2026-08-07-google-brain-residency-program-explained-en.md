Question: what's the Google Brain Residency Program?

Answer:

The **Google Brain Residency Program** was a research training program created by Google to train people into **machine learning researchers**. It was later renamed the **Google AI Residency Program** as it expanded beyond the original Google Brain team. ([Google Research][1])

Think of it as:

> **A 1-year paid "AI research apprenticeship" inside Google, between a master's/PhD program and a research job.**

Google Brain launched it in 2015. Residents worked directly with Google researchers on frontier ML problems, read papers, run experiments, write papers, and learn how to do research at scale. ([Google Research][2])

Typical resident workflow:

```
Week 1-2:
    Google onboarding
    Deep learning training

Month 1:
    Find mentor
    Pick research direction

Months 2-12:
    Read papers
    Implement ideas
    Run experiments on Google's compute
    Write papers
    Submit to NeurIPS / ICML / ICLR
```

Research areas included:

* Deep learning optimization
* Computer vision
* NLP
* Reinforcement learning
* Generative models
* Robotics
* Healthcare AI
* AI safety

Some residents published papers at top ML conferences during the program. ([Google Research][1])

---

## Who got accepted?

The interesting part: it was **not only PhDs**.

Candidates included:

* PhD graduates
* Master's students
* Bachelor's graduates
* Engineers switching into AI research
* People with unusual backgrounds

Google explicitly said equivalent STEM experience could qualify, and encouraged non-traditional backgrounds. ([Google Research][1])

Examples:

* Someone with a CS master's who wanted to move into deep learning research
* Someone who dropped out and had strong ML research experience
* Engineers who had strong coding + math skills

([Google Research][1])

## Why was it important?

Before this program, the usual path was:

```
Bachelor
   |
Master
   |
PhD
   |
Research Scientist
```

Google Brain Residency created another path:

```
Strong engineer
       |
       v
Google Brain Resident (1 year)
       |
       v
Research Engineer / Scientist
       |
       v
Top AI lab
```

It became a talent pipeline.

Many AI researchers came through similar programs:

* Google Brain Residency
* OpenAI Residency
* FAIR research internships
* DeepMind internships
