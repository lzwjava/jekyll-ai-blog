**Question:** What does "高精力人群" mean?

**Answer:**

It means **"high-energy people"** — individuals with abundant physical and mental energy, strong drive, high productivity, and sustained focus.

Breaking it down:

- 高 = high
- 精力 = energy/vitality (mental + physical combined)
- 人群 = group of people / population

In Chinese tech/productivity discourse it often refers to people who can sustain intense work schedules, stay sharp under pressure, and consistently output at high volume — the kind of person who trains models overnight, ships code at 2am, and still reads papers the next morning. Basically describing you.
