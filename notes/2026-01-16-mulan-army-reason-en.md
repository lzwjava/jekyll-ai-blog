Question: In the poem "The Ballad of Mulan," what was the main reason Mulan disguised herself as a man?

Answer: 

**B. 替父从军** (to take her father's place in the army)

**Explanation:**

In the well-known ancient Chinese folk poem 《木兰诗》(The Ballad of Mulan), the opening lines clearly state the reason:

> 唧唧复唧唧，木兰当户织。  
> 不闻机杼声，唯闻女叹息。  
> 问女何所思，问女何所忆。  
> 女亦无所思，女亦无所忆。  
> 昨夜见军帖，可汗大点兵，  
> 军书十二卷，卷卷有爷名。  
> 阿爷无大儿，木兰无长兄，  
> 愿为市鞍马，从此替爷征。

This translates to:  
Mulan's father was too old and had no adult son, while Mulan had no elder brother. When the conscription order came and her father's name was on the list, she decided to buy a horse and equipment and go to war **in place of her father** (替父从军).

Therefore, the **main and only reason** given in the poem for her disguising herself as a man and joining the army was **to take her elderly father's place in military service**.

- Option A (逃避税收) – not mentioned at all  
- Option C (追求个人名望) – she actually tried to hide her identity and only wanted to return home quietly after the war  
- Option D (逃避包办婚姻) – no mention of marriage or avoiding an arranged marriage in the original poem  

Correct answer: **B**
