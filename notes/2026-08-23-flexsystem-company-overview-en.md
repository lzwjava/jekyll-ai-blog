**Question:** What is FlexSystem, and what does the company do?

**Answer:**

FlexSystem is a well-established Hong Kong-based enterprise software company with a strong regional and global presence. Here is an overview:

**Company Background**

FlexSystem has been in business since 1987 and is a leading international provider of enterprise management solutions, delivering high-quality business software and services to companies of all sizes and complexity. The company currently has between 201–500 employees and is privately held.

**Market Reach**

Over 3,000 companies use FlexSystem's solutions, including 1 in 10 Forbes Global 2000 companies. Headquartered in Hong Kong, FlexSystem has multiple offices and operations in Macau, PRC, Taiwan, and Malaysia, supporting users in over 38 regions. As you noted, it serves more than 20% of listed companies in Hong Kong.

**Core Products & Solutions**

FlexSystem is the official provider of several key enterprise systems, including FlexAccount (accounting/financial management), FlexHRIS (human resources management), and FlexWorkflow (business process management). Their broader portfolio includes:

- **Financial Management** – FlexAccount covers finance, fixed assets, consolidation, budgeting, and intercompany management.
- **HR Management** – FlexHRIS handles personnel, leave, attendance, payroll, and employee self-service (ESS).
- **Trading System** – FESA Trading automates sales and purchase order processes and improves inventory management.
- **Workflow & Document Management** – FlexWorkflow supports business process automation.
- **BI Tools & Property Management** – Additional modules for analytics and real estate portfolio management.

**Technology & Innovation**

FlexSystem's ERP system runs on its own Enterprise Software Architecture (FESA), a flexible, scalable, and robust platform with a built-in computing engine to fulfill dynamic business changes. The company is also focused on digital transformation, incorporating Generative AI (GenAI) into key areas like Finance, Human Resources, and Procurement.
