**Question:** Who is Zachary Lipton and what is his work?

**Answer:**

**Zachary Lipton** is a prominent ML researcher and entrepreneur. Here's the breakdown:

**Academic Role**

He's the Raj Reddy Associate Professor of Machine Learning at Carnegie Mellon University (CMU), with appointments in the ML Department (School of Computer Science), Heinz School of Public Policy, and Societal Computing.

**Research Focus (ACMI Lab)**

He directs the Approximately Correct Machine Intelligence (ACMI) Lab at CMU. The lab's focus areas are directly relevant to your AI engineering trajectory:

1. **Distribution shift & robustness** — building systems that handle natural distribution shifts and adversarial/strategic manipulation
2. **Causality + representation learning** — intersection of the two
3. **Social impact of ML** — philosophically coherent analysis
4. **Clinical ML** — applying ML to medicine, especially with NLP

**Industry: Abridge (CTO & Cofounder)**

He cofounded Abridge, a healthcare AI company in the ambient listening space. Their product listens to doctor-patient conversations, ingests EHR content, and uses a reasoning engine to generate after-visit notes — freeing doctors from documentation burden. This is a serious LLM-in-production system.

**Notable Contributions**

He co-authored *Dive into Deep Learning* (d2l.ai) — an interactive textbook teaching deep learning through exposition, math, and code in Jupyter, compiled to HTML and PDF, forthcoming from Cambridge University Press. He also runs the *Approximately Correct* blog, launched in 2016 to bridge technical and social ML perspectives.
