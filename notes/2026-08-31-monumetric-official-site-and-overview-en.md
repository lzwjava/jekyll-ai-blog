Question: **Is `monumetric.com` the official site, and what is Monumetric?**

Answer: **Yes. `https://www.monumetric.com` is Monumetric's official website.** The site itself identifies Monumetric as a full-service **website advertising / ad-revenue management company**. ([Monumetric][1])

[Monumetric official website](https://www.monumetric.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### What Monumetric does

Think of it as an **alternative to Google AdSense**, but more like an **ad-management partner** than a simple self-service ad network.

The basic flow is:

```text
Your website
     │
     ▼
Monumetric
     │
     ├── Header bidding
     ├── Ad exchanges / advertisers
     ├── Direct/private demand
     ├── Video ads
     └── Ad optimization
             │
             ▼
        Ads displayed
        on your website
             │
             ▼
       Ad revenue
```

Monumetric handles things like:

* ad placement
* header bidding
* advertiser demand
* optimization of CPM/RPM
* testing different ad configurations
* reporting
* payment aggregation

They describe themselves as a **"full-service ad revenue partner"** rather than simply an ad network. ([Monumetric][2])

### Interesting part: they take a percentage

According to their current website, Monumetric says it generally keeps **15–30%** depending on the advertising technology used, with the publisher receiving the remainder. ([Monumetric][3])

They also pay publishers on **Net 60** terms. ([Monumetric][3])

### Their traffic tiers

Their current programs are roughly:

| Program     | Monthly pageviews |
| ----------- | ----------------: |
| **Propel**  |           10k–80k |
| **Ascend**  |          80k–500k |
| **Stratos** |          500k–10M |
| **Apollo**  |              10M+ |

([Monumetric][2])

So if you're thinking about using it for **lzwjava.github.io / your blog**, the interesting question isn't really *"is Monumetric legitimate?"* — it is.

The more useful question is:

> **Monumetric vs Google AdSense vs Raptive vs Mediavine — which gives the best RPM for a technical/AI blog with your traffic profile?**

Monumetric explicitly accepts **technology/content websites**, so an AI/programming site is within the type of publisher they target. ([Monumetric][4])

They've also been around since **2012**, originally under the name **The Blogger Network**, and rebranded to Monumetric in 2016. ([Monumetric][5])

### One thing I'd check before switching

Don't compare just the headline RPM. Measure:

```text
net revenue / 1,000 pageviews
```

after:

```text
ad revenue
- Monumetric's cut
- any lost traffic / UX impact
- page-speed impact
```

For a technical blog, **page speed and reader experience can matter more than squeezing another 10% out of ad RPM**.
