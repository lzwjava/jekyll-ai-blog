Question: What notable Chinese companies have collapsed/gone bankrupt in recent years (2023-2026)?

Answer: Three sectors account for most of the carnage — property, shadow banking, and EV startups.

**Property & shadow banking**
- **China Evergrande Group** — A Hong Kong court ordered liquidation on Jan 29, 2024, marking the largest bankruptcy in China's history, with debt of 2.39 trillion yuan (~$330B, ~2% of GDP). It was delisted from HKEX on Aug 25, 2025. Its auditor PwC Zhong Tian also got hit — a six-month mainland suspension plus a 441M yuan fine in Sept 2024 for audit failures.
- **Zhongzhi Enterprise Group** — one of China's largest shadow-banking conglomerates. A Beijing court ordered liquidation of Zhongzhi and 300+ affiliates in April 2026, after it first filed for bankruptcy in Jan 2024.

**EV startups (the sector has been a bloodbath)**
- **WM Motor** — the biggest Chinese EV insolvency to date, ~100K vehicles sold 2019-2022, filed for bankruptcy in a Shanghai court in late 2023/2024, backed by Baidu.
- **Human Horizons / HiPhi** — filed for pre-bankruptcy restructuring in Aug 2024 after suspending production in Feb, unable to pay suppliers despite EU expansion attempts.
- **Jiyue** (Geely x Baidu JV) — collapsed in late 2024, part of a wave alongside Enovate's closure and Evergrande Auto's demise.
- **Neta (Hozon)** — the top-selling startup to go bankrupt, ~500K units sold 2018-2024 before insolvency.
- Also gone: **Byton**, **Aiways**, **Singulato**, **Qiantu Motor** (parent CH-Auto filed for bankruptcy liquidation Jan 2025, failed US IPO), **Levdeo**, **Bordrin**, **LeEco EV**.
- Looking forward: ~50 more loss-making Chinese EV makers are at risk of collapse heading into 2026 as subsidies dry up and 2026 could see China's first car-sales contraction since 2020.
- Scale: roughly 400 Chinese EV companies ceased operations between 2018-2025.

Bigger picture: China's bankruptcy system is brutal on the way in — ~83% of companies that reach bankruptcy court end up liquidated vs. only ~5% in the US, so "bankruptcy" in China usually means real death, not Chapter 11-style reorg. The regulatory response is also shifting — Xiamen rolled out personal bankruptcy protection rules in Nov 2025 and there's a draft Enterprise Bankruptcy Law amendment aimed at rescuing small businesses/entrepreneurs.
