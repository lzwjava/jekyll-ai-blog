## Who is Ming-Chi Kuo (郭明錤)?

Ming-Chi Kuo is one of the most well-known and respected technology analysts in the world, specifically focused on Apple Inc. Here is a comprehensive overview:

---

### Background & Career

Ming-Chi Kuo has been a well-known Apple analyst for over a decade. His career first gained prominence at DigiTimes, where he reported on unreleased iPhone models. He then moved to Concord Securities in 2011, followed by KGI Securities in 2013, where he spent the better part of a decade building his reputation in the Apple rumor and analysis space.

He is currently an analyst at TF International Securities, a financial services group in the Asia-Pacific region. He gathers intelligence from his contacts in Apple's Asian supply chain and translates that information into research notes for clients.

---

### Why Is He So Good?

His track record has elevated his Apple predictions to near-gospel status. He has been called "the most accurate Apple analyst in the world" by Business Insider, and the "most reliable industry watcher on the planet when it comes to Apple" by BGR.

His key strengths lie in device-specific details and specs — including finer points like color, size, chipsets, and ports. He not only uses obtained supply chain data to predict future Apple products, but also provides guidance on how well a product might sell, arriving at shipment numbers based on component order volumes.

As of February 2021, his predictions have been measured at approximately **76.6% accuracy**, and he has been referred to as the "best Apple analyst on the planet."

---

### Notable Accurate Predictions

He has had a long string of accurate forecasts: the delayed white iPhone 4 release in 2011, the 2012 MacBook Pro with Retina display, Touch ID details in 2013, the iPhone SE, Apple Watch Series 2, and many more. More recently, he was among the first to correctly predict that the iPhone 15 would switch to USB-C and that the iPhone 14 Pro would feature a 48MP camera sensor.

---

There is an element of mystery to Ming-Chi Kuo — he shuns the spotlight and does not do interviews. Very few photos of him exist online, even when searching in Chinese. Despite this low profile, he is undoubtedly KGI's and TF International's most famous analyst.
