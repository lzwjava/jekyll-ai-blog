**Question:** What is the reunification strategy (统一战略) regarding the Taiwan issue (台湾问题) as covered in the course textbook?

---**Question:** What is 新时代党解决台湾问题的总体方略 (the overall strategy for resolving the Taiwan issue in the New Era) as covered in the course textbook?

---

**Answer:**

This is covered in **Chapter 15** of the textbook: *"坚持'一国两制'和推进祖国完全统一"*. It is a major exam topic.

---

### Where It Comes From

The term "新时代党解决台湾问题的总体方略" was first officially proposed in the Resolution passed at the Sixth Plenary Session of the 19th Central Committee. The 20th Party Congress Report then explicitly required "坚持贯彻新时代党解决台湾问题的总体方略."

---

### The Core Goal (核心)

The core of the overall strategy is to **promote the complete reunification of the country and realize the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation**. It is a complete ideological system: with the people at the center, peaceful negotiation (but not renouncing the use of force) as the means, ending political confrontation and achieving reunification as the purpose, rule of law as the guarantee, "One Country, Two Systems" as the reunification framework, and national rejuvenation as the ultimate goal.

---

### The 5 Key Pillars of the Strategy

The overall strategy consists of the following core components:

---

**① 坚持党中央集中统一领导 — Party Leadership is the Fundamental Guarantee**

CCP Central Committee's centralized and unified leadership over Taiwan-related work is the root guarantee for achieving reunification — it is where the advantage, the key, and the foundation all lie.

---

**② 坚持一个中国原则和"九二共识" — One China Principle**

The One China Principle is the political foundation of cross-strait relations. The "1992 Consensus" that embodies the One China Principle clearly defines the fundamental nature of cross-strait relations — its core is that both sides of the strait belong to one China and jointly strive for national reunification.

---

**③ 推动两岸关系和平发展、融合发展 — Peaceful & Integrated Development**

This means upholding the path of peaceful development of cross-strait relations, deepening integration across all fields, sharing development opportunities with Taiwan compatriots first, improving policies that benefit Taiwan residents' wellbeing, promoting joint promotion of Chinese culture, and fostering spiritual unity between compatriots on both sides.

---

**④ 坚持"一国两制"，推进和平统一 — One Country, Two Systems**

In 2019, Xi Jinping put forward five major policy proposals for advancing cross-strait relations: jointly promoting national rejuvenation to achieve peaceful reunification; exploring a "Two Systems" Taiwan solution; upholding the One China Principle; deepening cross-strait integrated development; and promoting spiritual unity among compatriots.

---

**⑤ 坚决反对"台独"，不承诺放弃使用武力 — Oppose Taiwan Independence, Reserve Use of Force**

The struggle for reunification is a process of continuously fighting against "Taiwan independence" separatist forces and external interference until final victory. The use of force is not renounced — this is directed not at Taiwan compatriots but at separatist forces and foreign interference.

### 🗺️ Simple Framework to Remember

```
总体方略 (Overall Strategy)

```

### 📝 Exam Tips

1. **Most tested phrase** — memorize this from the 20th Party Congress Report:
   > 坚持贯彻新时代党解决台湾问题的**总体方略**，牢牢把握两岸关系**主导权和主动权**

2. **Common essay question**: *"Why is reunification an inevitable requirement of national rejuvenation?"*
   — Answer: because without complete territorial reunification, the rejuvenation of the Chinese nation is incomplete

3. **Key distinction** — "不承诺放弃使用武力" is directed at **foreign interference and separatist forces**, NOT at ordinary Taiwan people — exams often test whether you understand this distinction

4. **Chapter connection** — Chapter 15 covers both Hong Kong/Macao (一国两制 success) AND Taiwan (一国两制 future application) — know both sides
