Question: Is the US Consulate General in Guangzhou currently backed up on visa processing (B1/B2 or other visa types)?

Answer: No — it's actually the fastest post in China right now. Per the official State Department Global Visa Wait Times page, last updated 21-JUL-2026, Guangzhou shows:

- **B1/B2 (tourist/business)**: 1 month average wait, 1 month for next available appointment
- **F/M/J (student/exchange)**: 1.5 months
- **H/L/O/P/Q (petition-based work visas)**: 2 months
- **Crew/Transit (C/D)**: 1.5 months

Compare that to the other China posts on the same table:

- Beijing: 2.5mo (B1/B2), 4.5mo (F/M/J)
- Shanghai: 3.5mo (B1/B2), 4mo (F/M/J)
- Wuhan: 3.5mo (B1/B2), 4.5mo (F/M/J)
- Shenyang: <0.5mo (B1/B2) — actually faster than Guangzhou

So Guangzhou is running noticeably lighter than Beijing/Shanghai/Wuhan, roughly on par with Shenyang. If you're picking a consulate for a B1/B2 or work visa, Guangzhou or Shenyang beat Beijing/Shanghai right now by 2-3 months.

One caveat: this table doesn't include 221(g) administrative processing delays, and slots open up daily from cancellations — the state.gov appointment system itself is the real-time source of truth over any monthly-updated table.
