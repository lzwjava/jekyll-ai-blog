**Question:** What is Riyue Bei (Day Moon Bay) in Zhuhai, including when it was built, its popularity, visitor feedback, and best times to visit?

**Answer:**

Riyue Bei, officially known as the **Zhuhai Grand Theatre** or **Zhuhai Opera House**, is a stunning architectural landmark located in Zhuhai, Guangdong Province, China. The name "Riyue Bei" (日月贝) translates to "Sun and Moon Shells," referring to its distinctive twin shell-shaped design.

## Architecture and Construction

The theatre was designed by Chen Keshi, a renowned Chinese architect, and features two shell-like structures that represent the sun and moon. The larger shell houses a 1,550-seat grand theatre, while the smaller shell contains a 550-seat multifunctional hall. The design draws inspiration from Zhuhai's coastal location and incorporates elements of seashells and pearls, creating a harmonious blend with the surrounding seascape.

Construction of the Zhuhai Grand Theatre was completed and it officially opened in **2016**. The building has become one of Zhuhai's most iconic landmarks and a symbol of the city's cultural development.

## Popularity and Visitor Experience

The Zhuhai Grand Theatre is extremely popular among both tourists and locals. Its striking architecture makes it a favorite spot for photography, especially during sunset when the white shells are illuminated by golden light. The theatre sits on the shores of the South China Sea at Yeli Island in the Xiangzhou District, offering spectacular ocean views.

The venue hosts various performances including operas, ballets, concerts, and theatrical productions, attracting culture enthusiasts from across China and internationally. Beyond performances, many visitors come simply to admire the architecture and enjoy the surrounding coastal scenery.

## Visitor Feedback

Feedback from visitors is overwhelmingly positive, with particular praise for:

- **Architectural Beauty**: The unique shell design is frequently described as breathtaking and photogenic
- **Location**: The seaside setting provides a picturesque backdrop, especially during golden hour
- **Cultural Value**: The theatre serves as an important cultural hub for the Pearl River Delta region
- **Facilities**: Modern, well-maintained facilities with excellent acoustics

Some visitors note that experiencing a performance inside enhances the visit, as the interior design is equally impressive as the exterior.

**Seasonal Considerations:**
