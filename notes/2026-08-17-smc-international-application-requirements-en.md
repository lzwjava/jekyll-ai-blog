Question: **How do I apply to Santa Monica College (SMC), and what are the requirements?**

Answer: If you're applying **from outside the U.S. as an international student**, SMC's process is fairly straightforward. You apply specifically as an **F-1 international student**.

### 1. Basic requirements

For the regular academic program, SMC currently requires:

* **Age:** generally 18+ at initial attendance. SMC also accepts some 16–17-year-olds under special guardian requirements. ([Santa Monica College][1])
* **Previous education:** transcripts from your previous school(s).
* **English proficiency:** you need to provide acceptable proof of English proficiency. ([Santa Monica College][1])
* **Financial proof:** an official bank statement showing you can afford tuition/living expenses. ([Santa Monica College][1])
* **Passport copy.**
* **500+ word personal essay** explaining why you want to attend SMC, your intended major, whether you want an Associate degree/transfer, and intended transfer universities. ([Santa Monica College][1])
* **$75 non-refundable application fee.** ([Santa Monica College][2])

There isn't the kind of highly selective admissions process you'd see at UCLA/Stanford. The important issue is completing the international-student requirements correctly.

### 2. Application process

The flow is basically:

```text
Create SMC account
       ↓
Submit international application
       ↓
Pay $75
       ↓
Upload documents
       ├── Passport
       ├── Transcripts
       ├── Financial statement
       ├── English proficiency
       └── 500-word essay
       ↓
SMC reviews application
       ↓
Admission
       ↓
SMC issues I-20
       ↓
Apply for F-1 visa
       ↓
Enter U.S.
       ↓
Orientation + placement
       ↓
Register for classes
```

SMC says international applications take approximately **4–6 weeks to process**, excluding the F-1 visa process. ([Santa Monica College][2])

[SMC International Student Application](https://www.smc.edu/admission-aid/apply/international-students/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 3. The important part: English requirement

SMC accepts several forms of English proficiency. You don't necessarily need to attend their Intensive English Program.

They have an official list of accepted tests/pathways, so I would check the current list rather than assume that a particular IELTS/TOEFL score is sufficient. ([Santa Monica College][3])

Given that **you already have IELTS 6.5**, this is worth checking specifically against SMC's current accepted-score table before paying the application fee.

### 4. Financial requirement

This is separate from the $75 application fee.

For **Fall 2026–Summer 2027**, SMC lists international tuition at approximately:

**$486/unit**

and estimates **$11,664 for 24 units / two semesters**, before other costs such as health insurance and living expenses. ([Santa Monica College][4])

So the financial statement isn't just proving that you can pay the $75 application fee. You're demonstrating sufficient funds for the period required by SMC.

### 5. F-1 requirement after admission

Once accepted, SMC sends you an **I-20**, which you use for the F-1 visa process. ([Santa Monica College][5])

For F-1 status, SMC requires:

```text
Fall/Spring
12+ units
    ↓
At least 9 units on-campus/hybrid
    ↓
Maximum 1 online class
```

SMC explicitly states that only one online class of ≤3 units can count toward the 12-unit requirement. ([Santa Monica College][6])
