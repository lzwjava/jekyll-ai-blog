**Question:** What startup hubs are there similar to Li Zexiang's XbotPark, but focused more on AI/LLM?

**Answer:**

XbotPark is primarily a **hardware + robotics** deep-tech hub. Here are the most comparable hubs and ecosystems that share its "campus + community + capital" philosophy, but tilted toward **AI/LLM**:

---

### 🇺🇸 AI House (formerly AI2 Incubator) — Seattle, USA
AI2 Incubator, founded in 2014 inside the Allen Institute for AI (created by Microsoft co-founder Paul Allen), has rebranded as **AI House**. It spun off from Ai2 in 2022 and opened a physical hub in March 2025 with 108,000 sq ft of space — a gathering place for founders, engineers, researchers, and investors. It operates across three pillars: Community, Incubator, and Capital, partnering with applied AI founders from day one as a hands-on co-founding partner, not a passive investor. Portfolio includes companies acquired by Apple, DocuSign, and Baidu.

---

### 🇫🇷 Station F / F/ai — Paris, France
Station F, a Paris-based startup hub founded by French billionaire Xavier Niel, is running its **F/ai accelerator** specifically to help AI-focused startups move from early product to real revenue. F/ai is backed by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Microsoft, and Mistral, offering $1M+ in compute credits with **0% equity taken** — making it one of the most founder-friendly AI programs globally.

---

### 🇺🇸 Y Combinator — San Francisco, USA
Not a physical campus in XbotPark's sense, but YC now has ~60% AI companies in its 2026 batches, up from 40% in 2024, investing $500K per company ($125K for 7% + $375K uncapped SAFE). It's the closest Western equivalent in terms of brand pull and alumni density.

---

### 🇺🇸 SOSV / HAX — Hardware + AI, China & USA
HAX provides up to $550K at ~8% equity and is specifically designed for hardware and AI founders who need **physical infrastructure** alongside capital — the most XbotPark-like model in the West. SOSV portfolio companies raised $7.5 billion in follow-on funding between 2021 and 2025.

---

### 🇦🇪 Hub71+ AI — Abu Dhabi, UAE
Hub71+ AI is a specialist ecosystem designed to support startups at the forefront of AI innovation, offering advanced technical resources and market access. It is government-backed by Mubadala and positions Abu Dhabi as a global AI hub, particularly relevant for founders targeting the Middle East or seeking sovereign-backed support.

---

### 🇺🇸 NVIDIA Inception — Global (Virtual + Physical Events)
NVIDIA Inception has 19,000+ member companies and offers $100K in DGX Cloud credits, making it the most GPU-compute-centric program — especially relevant for LLM training and inference startups.

---

### Key Difference vs. XbotPark
XbotPark's unique edge is its tight integration with **Shenzhen's manufacturing supply chain**. Most AI/LLM hubs above are software-first. If you're building **AI + hardware** (robots, edge devices, etc.), HAX and XbotPark itself remain the strongest options. For pure LLM/software AI plays, **AI House** and **Station F/F/ai** are the closest philosophical equivalents.
