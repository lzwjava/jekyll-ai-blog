Question: Are there many businesses in China generating around 10 million to 30 million CNY in revenue that sell globally through exporting?

Answer:

Yes, there are hundreds of thousands of Chinese small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) operating in that revenue bracket while selling goods worldwide.

In China, a company generating 10M to 30M RMB (roughly $1.4M to $4.2M USD) falls squarely into the micro-to-small enterprise category. Private enterprises account for over 56% of China’s total foreign trade, and these small-scale businesses form the backbone of global cross-border trade.

### Primary Operating Models

* **Cross-Border E-Commerce (B2C):** Selling directly to overseas consumers via platforms like Amazon, AliExpress, Temu, and SHEIN, or through independent Shopify storefronts.
* **B2B Export Manufacturing:** Operating factory or workshop operations in manufacturing hubs (such as Shenzhen, Yiwu, Ningbo, or Foshan) and exporting via Alibaba, Global Sources, or trade agencies.
* **Domestic Trading Companies:** Buying from local manufacturers and marking up products for sale to foreign buyers.

### Why the 10M–30M CNY Range Is Common

* **Low Capital Entry:** Reaching 10M RMB in export revenue requires relatively small teams (often 5 to 20 employees) leveraging direct-to-consumer e-commerce or niche wholesale orders.
* **Specialized Clusters:** Industrial clusters across provinces like Guangdong and Zhejiang allow small firms to source components, package goods, and ship globally with minimal overhead.
* **Favorable Policy Support:** Government tax rebates, streamlined customs procedures, and subsidized logistics hubs make exporting accessible even to smaller companies.
