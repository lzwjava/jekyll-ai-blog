**Question:** What is an introduction to the ASUS B85 motherboard — its key features and specifications?

**Answer:**

The ASUS B85 is a lineup of motherboards built around Intel's B85 Express chipset, targeting mainstream desktop builders who want a reliable, feature-rich platform without the premium cost of higher-end chipsets.

**Platform & CPU Support**

The Intel B85 Express chipset is a single-chipset design that supports the LGA1150 socket, compatible with Intel's 4th-generation (Haswell) Core i7, i5, i3, Pentium, and Celeron processors. It provides improved performance by utilizing serial point-to-point links for increased bandwidth and stability.

**Memory**

The chipset allows up to four DIMM slots accepting up to 32 GB of DDR3 1600 MHz memory modules, with dual-channel support.

**Storage & Expansion**

For storage, four SATA III and two SATA II connectors are available for optical drives, hard drives, and SSDs. Expansion slots include one PCIe 3.0 x16 and two PCIe 2.0 x1 slots. Note that the B85 chipset does **not** support SLI or CrossFire multi-GPU configurations.

**USB & Connectivity**

The B85 natively supports up to four USB 3.0 ports, delivering up to ten times faster transfer rates than USB 2.0. ASUS also adds **USB 3.0 Boost** with UASP support for 170% faster transfer speeds than traditional USB 3.0.

**Audio & Networking**

Audio is handled by a Realtek ALC887-VD2 chip providing 7.1-channel HD audio, and networking is provided by a Realtek 8111G Gigabit Ethernet controller.

**Video Output**

Rear panel video outputs include D-Sub (VGA), DVI, and HDMI, allowing use of Intel's integrated graphics without a discrete GPU.

**ASUS-Specific Features**

- **5X Protection** — includes DIGI+ VRM digital power design, ESD-tested components, resettable fuses around DRAM slots, and corrosion-resistant back I/O shields for reliability and durability.
- **UEFI BIOS** — features a smooth mouse-controlled graphical interface, custom shortcuts, an activity log of setting changes, and a user-friendly EZ mode with detailed fan controls and XMP profile settings.
- **Fan Xpert** — dedicated CPU and case fan controls for optimized cooling.
- **Network iControl** — real-time network bandwidth management.

Overall, the ASUS B85 series is a solid mid-range platform suited for everyday builds, office PCs, and light gaming rigs using 4th-gen Intel processors.
