# 🧠 What you actually get (core benefits)

### 1) Huge cloud storage (main upgrade)

* **5 TB storage** for:

  * Google Drive
  * Gmail
  * Google Photos
* Recently upgraded from **2 TB → 5 TB with no price increase** ([Yahoo Tech][1])

👉 This is the **biggest practical value** of the plan.

---

### 2) Full access to Google AI (Gemini ecosystem)

You’re not just buying storage — you’re buying AI:

* Gemini (Google’s ChatGPT competitor)
* AI inside:

  * Gmail
  * Docs
  * Sheets
  * Slides
* Advanced features like:

  * **Deep Research**
  * **Image generation/editing**
  * **Video generation (Veo, Flow, etc.)** ([Google One][2])

👉 This is similar to paying for ChatGPT Plus — but integrated into Google tools.

---

### 3) Monthly AI usage credits

* You get **AI credits every month** (for heavy tasks, long sessions, etc.) ([Google Help][3])

---

### 4) Family sharing (important)

* You can **share with up to 5 people**
* They can use:

  * storage
  * AI features (depending on setup) ([Google One][2])

👉 This makes the $19.99/month much cheaper per person.

---

### 5) Extra bundled perks

* **Google Home Premium (Standard)** included (~$10 value) ([Google One][4])
* Better Google Meet / Calendar features
* Google Photos AI tools (like video generation)

# 🧩 Should you get it?

### Worth it if you:

* Use Google Drive heavily
* Store lots of photos/videos
* Want **AI inside Gmail/Docs**
* Replace ChatGPT + cloud storage with one subscription
