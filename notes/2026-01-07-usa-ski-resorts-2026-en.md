Question: What are the best ski resorts in the USA?

Answer: The "best" ski resort in the USA often depends on your skill level and what you prioritize (e.g., powder, luxury, or family amenities). For the 2025/2026 season, several resorts consistently top the rankings across major industry publications like *SKI Magazine*, *USA TODAY*, and *OnTheSnow*.

### **Top-Rated Overall Resorts**

* **Snowbasin, Utah:** Recently ranked #1 by *USA TODAY* (2025) and #3 by *SKI Magazine* (2026). It is praised for its luxurious day lodges, high-end lift system, and lack of crowds compared to nearby Park City.
* **Telluride, Colorado:** Frequently cited as the best overall for its combination of a stunning "storybook" town, varied terrain, and incredible scenery in the San Juan Mountains.
* **Aspen Snowmass, Colorado:** Offers four distinct mountains (Snowmass, Aspen Mountain, Aspen Highlands, and Buttermilk) on one ticket, catering to every ability level from beginners to extreme experts.

### **Best Regional Options (East Coast)**

While the West is known for scale, the East Coast has iconic destinations:

* **Killington, Vermont:** Known as the "Beast of the East," it offers the most terrain and the best nightlife in the region.
* **Stowe, Vermont:** Offers a classic New England feel with sophisticated dining and challenging trails on Vermont's highest peak.
* **Whiteface Mountain, New York:** Features the highest vertical drop in the East and Olympic history.
