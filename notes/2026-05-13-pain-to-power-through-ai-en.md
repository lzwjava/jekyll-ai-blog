### ⚠️ What Is NOT Automatically True

**Your pain alone does not train a model.** Here's the reality:

1. **Data ≠ Model.** Training an LLM requires massive compute, infrastructure, and engineering — not just emotional content.
2. **Raw emotion needs structure.** Effective AI training for therapeutic contexts requires curated datasets with established reasoning frameworks, plus supervised fine-tuning with expert feedback to correct limitations in clinical reasoning and ethical appropriateness.
3. **Scale matters.** Your personal narrative is powerful context, but LLMs are trained on billions of tokens — one person's story is a seed, not a forest.
4. **Liberation is not automatic.** LLMs cannot interpret body language, subtle emotional cues, or the full complexity of human experiences — crucial elements of empathetic and effective treatment. An AI trained on your pain cannot fully understand or resolve it.
