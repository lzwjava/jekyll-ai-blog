**Question:** How should I describe my blog's page view achievement — honestly showing the past (17K/month), the present (75K/month), and the future potential — while keeping a humble, low-profile tone that doesn't attract competitor attention?

**Answer:**

The goal here is quiet confidence. Let the numbers speak plainly, without framing or spin. Here are ready-to-use phrasings, from most minimal to slightly more detailed:

---

## Recommended Phrasings

**Shortest / Most Humble (one line):**
> Personal blog at lzwjava.github.io — grown from ~17K to ~75K page views/month as of April 2026.

---

**Resume / Portfolio Line:**
> Maintained a personal technical blog since [year]; organic readership grew from ~17K to ~75K monthly page views in early 2026.

---

**About / Bio Section (2–3 sentences):**
> I write about software, learning, and ideas on lzwjava.github.io. Readership has grown steadily — from around 17K page views a month to 75K as of April 2026. I write primarily to think clearly, and share in case it helps others.

---

**With a subtle nod to potential (still low-key):**
> Personal blog tracking roughly 75K page views/month (up from 17K two months prior). Still early days, but the trajectory feels encouraging.

---

## Why This Works

- **No annualized projection** — skipping the "900K/year" framing keeps it grounded and avoids sounding like marketing copy
- **The delta (17K → 75K) does the heavy lifting** — a 4× growth in two months is self-evidently impressive; you don't need to editorialize it
- **"As of April 2026"** pins it to a real moment, not a claim about the future
- **Phrases like "still early days" or "I write to think clearly"** signal humility and genuine intent — the opposite of bragging
- **No mention of competitors, rankings, or comparisons** — nothing for anyone to push back on or feel threatened by

---

## What to Avoid

| Too loud | Humble alternative |
| --- | --- |
| "~900K annualized page views" | "75K/month as of April 2026" |
| "Rapidly growing blog" | "Readership has grown steadily" |
| "Top personal tech blog" | *(say nothing — omit entirely)* |
| "4.4× growth in 2 months" | "from ~17K to ~75K monthly" |

---

The quietest version of truth is often the most credible. Just state what happened, when, and let anyone reading do their own math.
