Question: Should I ask old friends for around 20,000 CNY (or 10,000 CNY) because I am unemployed, to cover mortgage or use it as startup capital for an AI consultancy/software outsourcing business?

Answer:

Given your background, I would **not frame this as "borrowing money because I am jobless."** I would frame it as a **short-term bridge loan to buy execution time**.

Your situation is unusual: you are not a beginner with no income capability. Your resume shows 12 years of engineering experience, HSBC/enterprise system experience, full-stack + AI work, open-source projects, LLM training experiments, and previous entrepreneurship (Fun Live). ([Zhiwei Li][1]) The question is not "can you ever make money?" The question is "how do you avoid making bad decisions under cash pressure?"

I would separate the money into two categories:

## 1. Mortgage survival money: acceptable

Asking a few close friends for **10k–20k CNY as a temporary bridge** is reasonable if:

* you clearly state the repayment plan;
* you set a deadline;
* you do not hide your situation;
* you ask people who can comfortably afford it.

Example:

> "I am between jobs and building my next AI consulting business. I need a 3-month buffer for mortgage and living expenses. Could you lend me 10,000 CNY? I plan to repay after I get consulting income or employment. No pressure if inconvenient."

This is very different from:

> "Invest in my startup."

The first is a personal bridge. The second creates investor expectations.

## 2. Startup fund: be careful

I would **not raise friends' money yet for a startup fund**.

Why?

Because 20k CNY is not really startup capital. It is survival capital.

For an AI consultancy, your biggest bottleneck is not:

* GPU cost
* office
* company registration
* servers

Your bottleneck is:

* finding customers
* converting trust into contracts
* getting paid

Your own consultancy page already points toward a high-value service model: LLM/Agent projects, GPU infrastructure, enterprise AI systems, with rates above 500 CNY/hour. ([Zhiwei Li][2])

The fastest validation path is probably:

```

    v

    |
    v

```

Not:

```

Business experiments:
```

Not:

* buying GPUs
* building a large SaaS before customers
* hiring people

## How I would approach old friends

Do not ask 20 people for 1k each. That damages relationships.

Better:

* choose 3-5 genuinely close friends;
* ask individually;
* give them an easy "no";
* update them periodically.

Example:

```
Hey, long time no talk.

I recently left my job and am exploring AI consulting/startup again.
I have enough skills and experience to generate income, but I need a short runway because of mortgage pressure.

Would you be comfortable lending me 10k CNY for 3-6 months?
I will repay after income stabilizes.

Totally okay if not. I value our friendship more than the money.
```
