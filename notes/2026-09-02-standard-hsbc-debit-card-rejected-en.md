The problem is that a **generic HSBC Mastercard debit card is NOT on the eligible list** for this presale. Read the eligible products carefully — only these seven qualify:

1. HSBC Privé
2. HSBC Premier Mastercard *(credit)*
3. HSBC Red Mastercard *(credit)*
4. HSBC Gold Mastercard *(credit)*
5. HSBC Mastercard *(credit)*
6. **HSBC Global Private Banking Mastercard Debit Card**
7. **HSBC Premier Elite Mastercard Debit Card**

Notice that the only **debit** cards in the list are the two premium tiers:

- **HSBC Global Private Banking Mastercard Debit Card**, and
- **HSBC Premier Elite Mastercard Debit Card**.

A regular HSBC Mastercard debit card (e.g., the standard Everyday/PowerVantage/One Mastercard debit, or a standard HKD savings debit card) is **not** one of these, so the BIN whitelist rejects it with *"The current promo code is invalid."* The system only checks the first 6 digits (BIN) against the list of eligible BIN ranges HSBC registered for this event — and your card's BIN isn't in one of those two premium debit BIN ranges.

## What you can do

- **If you actually hold HSBC Premier Elite or HSBC Global Private Banking status**, check which physical card that corresponds to and use that card's first 6 digits — those are the only debit BINs accepted.
- **If you only have a standard HSBC Mastercard debit card**, you cannot use the HSBC Mastercard Presale. Your options are:
  - **Public Sale — 4 Sep 2026 (Fri) 12nn** (up to 4 tickets, no card required).
  - **Live Nation Presale — 3 Sep 2026 (Thu) 12nn–11:59pm** (no HSBC card needed, just a registered HK Ticketing account).
  - **STAY 6TH GENERATION Presale — 1 Sep 2026** (requires STAY 6th Gen membership code, not a card BIN).
- **If you also have an HSBC credit Mastercard** (Red/Gold/Premier/standard), use that card's first 6 digits instead — credit cards have far more eligible BIN ranges on this list than debit.
- To confirm your specific card's eligibility, call the hotline: **+852 31 288 288** (daily 10am–8pm).

In short: "HSBC Mastercard debit" alone is too broad — only the two top-tier debit products (Global Private Banking / Premier Elite) are whitelisted. That's why your BIN is being rejected.
