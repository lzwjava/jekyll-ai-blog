**Question:** What are the rules for a company's registered address (企业住所) in Guangzhou, and what are the practical ways to get one?

Let me check the current Guangzhou rules since address policy has changed a few times.**Question:** What are the rules for the registered address (企业住所) when registering a company in Guangzhou via 一网通, and what are the practical ways to get one?

**Answer:**

## The rule: self-declaration + standard address library + negative list

Guangzhou uses a **住所自主承诺申报制** (address self-declaration commitment system). If the address you declare is in the city's standard address library (标准地址库) and not on the negative list, the 一网通 platform auto-matches it and offers a standard-address selection; you just commit that the address is legal, real, and valid — no property certificate or lease contract upload required. This is why the 一网通 notice you read mentioned "非自主申报地址" needing extra documents: only addresses *outside* the standard library need proof.

So the model is:

```
address in standard library + business not on negative list
  → tick a commitment checkbox, done (zero documents)

address NOT in library, or negative-list business
  → upload 场地使用证明 (proof of right to use: property cert + lease, or park committee certificate)
```

**"一址多照" is explicitly allowed**: with written consent from the property owner or its authorized manager, multiple business entities can register the same address as their 住所. This is the legal basis for address-hosting services.

## What's on the negative list (can't self-declare)

Two dimensions — the *business* and the *property*:

Businesses: entertainment (KTV, arcades, internet cafés, beauty/hair salons, spas), hotels, all restaurant/catering, heavy-pollution industries (printing, dyeing, paper), hazardous chemicals, car wash / vehicle repair, medical services and 月子中心. The 2022 list also added local financial organizations — micro-lending, pawnshops, financing guarantee, leasing, commercial factoring, etc.

Properties: property whose legal use is residential (住宅), government subsidized housing, military property, foreign-entity property.

Note: **住宅 is on the negative list for *self-declaration***, not an absolute ban. A pure residential-use property is effectively unusable for a software company unless you go through the 住改商 conversion process (neighbors' committee + housing bureau consent — painful, not worth it). Commercial-residential dual-use (商住两用) apartments generally work.

## Your practical options (a software/AI company, cheapest → most formal)

1. **地址挂靠 / cluster registration (集群注册)** — an address-hosting company (托管机构) lets many companies register at its address. Common in Nansha, Huangpu, and incubators. ~1,000–3,000 CNY/year, some park-affiliated ones are free for the first 1–3 years. In Nansha this is formalized: multiple entities register at one 托管机构's address, with a written hosting service contract; you provide your Guangzhou property/lease proof and ID to the host. Caveat: some banks are pickier about opening accounts for hosted-address companies, and tax bureaus occasionally do site checks — pick a reputable host that handles mail/inspection forwarding.

2. **Incubator / park / co-working space** — if the address is inside a government-approved industrial park, science park, incubator, or strategic emerging industry base, a certificate from the park management committee serves as the site-use proof. Many Tianhe/Pazhou co-working spaces (and AI-focused incubators) include a registration address with a hot desk. Best signal-to-cost ratio if you want an occasional workspace plus a clean address.

3. **Rent a real office / commercial unit** — if it's in the standard address library, self-declare with zero paperwork. Overkill for a one-person AI company pre-revenue.

4. **Your own commercial-use property** — if you or family own a 商住 apartment or shop unit, use it directly (self-declare if in the library).

## Risk model to keep in mind

The commitment system trades upfront paperwork for backend enforcement: market supervision does random spot checks, and if the company can't be reached at the registered address or declared fake info, it gets listed in the 经营异常名录 (abnormal operations directory) and publicly shamed; submitting fake materials is prosecuted. 经营异常 status breaks bank operations, bidding, and visa/invoice matters — so whatever address you use, make sure someone actually receives mail there (hosting companies handle this; a dead address doesn't).

For your situation — pre-startup, testing side projects with minimal burn — a **reputable cluster/hosting address or incubator address (~1–3K CNY/year)** is the standard move. It keeps registration cost near zero and is trivially changeable later (address change is a simple 变更登记) when you get a real office.
