**Question:** What are the top conferences for AI, ML, Deep Learning, and NLP?

**Answer:**

Here's a breakdown by category:

---

### 🏆 The "Big Three" (ML / Deep Learning — Research Tier 1)

These are the most prestigious venues where cutting-edge research is first revealed:

1. **NeurIPS** (Neural Information Processing Systems)
   The flagship conference for machine learning, covering everything from deep learning theory to applications in neuroscience. Known for its massive scale and high impact.

2. **ICML** (International Conference on Machine Learning)
   Known for its rigorous peer-review process and highly selective acceptance rates, ICML showcases state-of-the-art work across core ML theory, deep learning, optimization, probabilistic modeling, and emerging areas such as foundation models and alignment.

3. **ICLR** (International Conference on Learning Representations)
   The newest of the "big three," ICLR has rapidly become a premier venue, particularly for deep learning research, and is famous for its open and transparent peer-review process.

---

### 💬 Top NLP Conferences

4. **ACL** (Association for Computational Linguistics) — the premier NLP venue
5. **EMNLP** (Empirical Methods in NLP)
   A top-tier NLP conference with a strong emphasis on data-driven and empirical methods, with prestige on par with ACL.
6. **NAACL** (North American Chapter of ACL)

---

### 🤖 Broad AI Conferences

7. **AAAI** — A highly respected and comprehensive AI conference covering a wide range of topics including search, planning, knowledge representation, robotics, and ML.
8. **IJCAI** — One of the oldest and most prestigious AI conferences, covering all areas of AI.

---

### 👁️ Computer Vision / Industry

9. **CVPR / ICCV / ECCV** — Top venues for computer vision and deep learning applied to vision
10. **NVIDIA GTC** — One of the most influential enterprise-focused AI conferences globally, centered on the infrastructure and systems that power modern ML, including GPUs, accelerated computing, and AI platforms.
