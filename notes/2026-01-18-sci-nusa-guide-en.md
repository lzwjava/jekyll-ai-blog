**Question: Which map in PUBG Mobile has the Science Center, and what places does this map contain?**

**Answer:**

The Science Center is a key point of interest (POI) on the **Nusa** map in PUBG Mobile.

Nusa is PUBG Mobile's smallest battle royale map at **1x1 km**, introduced in the 2.2 update. It supports up to 32 players and focuses on fast-paced matches (around 8-10 minutes) with quick blue zone shrinks (70% faster than larger maps), abundant loot for immediate fights, unique features like Mecha soldiers (AI enemies in certain buildings), elevators (e.g., in Telepak Town), boost pools in water bodies, and token stores. It's a tropical island with urban, beach, and resort areas, ideal for aggressive playstyles.

**Key places (POIs) on Nusa include:**

- **Science Center**: Centrally located hot drop with high loot (weapons, armor). Multi-building complex similar to a school; popular for early fights.
- **Flame Village**: Central field area with large buildings and a token store; high loot density.
- **Shipyard**: Northern port (mini-Georgopol) with containers, warehouses, and ships; good loot and chaos.
- **Telepak Town (Telepathic Town)**: Largest city with a 7-story apartment (elevator + Mecha soldiers on top); urban hotspot.
- **Regal Resort**: Compound with 2-story buildings for monitoring fights; strategic loot.
- **Bulan Bay**: Coastal area.
- **Pink Beach**: Beach zone.
- **Sunset Street**: Urban street area.
- **Crystal Bay**: Bay location.
- **Love Island**: Island spot.

These ~11 POIs emphasize close-quarters combat and rapid rotations. Science Center and Flame Village are top hot drops for loot and action.
