**Question:** What is an introduction to the NVIDIA Quadro 4000 graphics card?

**Answer:**

There are actually two distinct cards that carry the "Quadro 4000" name. Here's an overview of both:

---

## 1. NVIDIA Quadro 4000 (Original — 2011)

Launched in July 2011, the NVIDIA Quadro 4000 is a desktop workstation GPU based on the **Fermi** architecture, produced on a 40nm manufacturing process. It features 256 CUDA cores, a 475 MHz core clock, and comes with 2 GB of GDDR5 memory over a 256-bit interface, delivering up to 89.6 GB/s of memory bandwidth. Its maximum TDP is 142W and it connects via PCIe 2.0 x16.

It was the first professional graphics solution in its class to integrate high-performance computing with advanced visualization, and with its Scalable Geometry Engine technology, it could process up to 890 million triangles per second — enabling professionals in design, animation, and video workflows to iterate and deliver higher quality results faster.

---

## 2. NVIDIA Quadro RTX 4000 (Modern — 2018/2019)

The Quadro RTX 4000 is based on NVIDIA's **Turing TU106** GPU (similar to the GeForce RTX 2060/2070). Its GPU boosts up to 1,545 MHz and is paired with 8 GB of GDDR6 memory over a 256-bit interface at 13 Gbps, offering up to 415 GB/s of peak memory bandwidth.

Built on a 12nm process, it packs approximately 13.6 billion transistors, 2,304 CUDA cores, 288 Tensor cores, and 36 RT cores — all within a slim, single-slot form factor.

Key highlights of the RTX 4000:

- **Ray Tracing:** New dedicated hardware-based ray tracing technology allows real-time rendering of film-quality, photorealistic objects with physically accurate shadows, reflections, and refractions — working with NVIDIA OptiX, Microsoft DXR, and Vulkan APIs.
- **AI / Deep Learning:** Its 288 Tensor cores are purpose-built for deep learning matrix arithmetic, delivering 8x the TFLOPS for training compared to the previous generation.
- **Display Support:** It supports up to four 5K monitors at 60Hz, or dual 8K displays per card, with HDR color support for 4K at 120Hz.

---

In short, the "Quadro 4000" line represents NVIDIA's **professional/workstation GPU tier**, designed for CAD, 3D animation, scientific visualization, and other demanding professional applications — distinct from consumer GeForce cards in their certified drivers, stability, and ECC memory support.
