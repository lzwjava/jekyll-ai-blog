**Question:** What is ByteDance's current valuation?

**Answer:**

ByteDance's valuation has surged dramatically in 2026, driven by regulatory clarity around TikTok and strong revenue growth. Here's the timeline:

- **August 2025:** ByteDance announced a new employee share buyback valuing the company at more than $330 billion.

- **November 2025:** Capital Today purchased a block of ByteDance shares at a $480 billion valuation in a secondary auction, with Fidelity and T. Rowe Price marking the company above $410–$450 billion respectively.

- **February 2026:** General Atlantic was reportedly selling an equity stake valuing ByteDance at $550 billion — a 66% jump from the $330 billion employee share buyback the prior year.

- **April 2026 (record high):** ByteDance's valuation surged to a record high of more than **$600 billion** after a proposed equity sale from one of the founding shareholders, after multiple parties expressed interest and the seller raised the asking price from the original $550 billion figure.

- **As of late August 2026:** Nasdaq Private Market estimates ByteDance's price per share at $351.83 as of August 28, 2026. (Note: ByteDance remains a **private company** with no public stock listing.)

The key catalyst behind this valuation surge was regulatory: the January 2026 U.S. government approval of TikTok's restructuring into a majority U.S.-owned joint venture removed the single largest regulatory overhang on ByteDance's price.
