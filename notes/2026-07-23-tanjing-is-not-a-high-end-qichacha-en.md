**Question: Is 探迹 (Tanjing) a high-end 企查查 (Qichacha)?**

**Answer:**

Short answer: **not exactly**. They overlap, but they are solving different problems.

企查查 is mainly a **business intelligence / company database** product:

* Search any company
* Ownership structure
* Legal representative
* Investment relationships
* Risk information
* Patents, trademarks
* Litigation
* Business registration data

Think:

> "Who is this company? Is it trustworthy? What is its background?"

---

探迹 is closer to **AI-powered B2B sales intelligence / lead generation**, not simply a premium company lookup database.

Think:

> "Which companies should I sell to, who should I contact, and how do I close them?"

Typical capabilities:

```
Enterprise data
      |
      v
AI profiling
      |
      v
Lead discovery
      |
      v
Sales automation
      |
      v
CRM workflow
```

Example:

A machinery manufacturer wants to find new customers.

企查查:

```
Search:
"Guangdong manufacturing companies"
↓
Get company list
```

探迹:

```
Input:
"We sell industrial robots"

AI finds:
- factories expanding production
- companies hiring automation engineers
- companies importing equipment
- decision makers
- contact channels

Then:
- generate sales leads
- recommend outreach
- manage pipeline
```

A better analogy:

| Product   | Category                            | Main user                                          |
| --------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| 企查查       | Enterprise information database     | Investors, lawyers, procurement, sales researchers |
| 探迹        | AI sales intelligence platform      | B2B sales teams                                    |
| 天眼查       | Similar to 企查查                      | General enterprise search                          |
| Apollo.io | Global equivalent of 探迹             | SDR/sales teams                                    |
| ZoomInfo  | Enterprise-grade sales intelligence | Large sales organizations                          |

So a more accurate description:

> 探迹 = 中国版 ZoomInfo + AI sales agent
> 企查查 = 中国版 company registry + risk database

They may use similar underlying enterprise data, but the **product layer is different**.

The interesting AI angle is:

Old workflow:

```
Sales person
 ↓
Search companies manually
 ↓
Export Excel
 ↓
Find contacts
 ↓
Call/email
```

AI-native workflow:

```
Agent:
"I sell GPU servers. Find me 500 Chinese companies likely to buy."

LLM + enterprise graph + web crawler:
 ↓
ICP matching
 ↓
lead ranking
 ↓
personalized outreach
 ↓
CRM update
```

This is the direction companies like 探迹 are moving toward. It is closer to **AI agent for sales** than "high-end 企查查."
