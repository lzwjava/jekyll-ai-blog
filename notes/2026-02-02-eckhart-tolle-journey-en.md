Eckhart Tolle is a German-born spiritual teacher and bestselling author whose book "The Power of Now" has become one of the most influential spiritual guides of the modern era. Here's a comprehensive look at his life:

### Early Life and Background

Born Ulrich Leonard Tölle on February 16, 1948, in Lünen, a small town north of Dortmund in the Ruhr region of Germany, Tolle experienced a difficult and turbulent childhood in post-war Germany. He spent the first thirteen years of his life in Germany before moving to Spain at age 13 to live with his father.

### Education

Tolle attended the Universities of London and Cambridge, eventually working toward his doctorate at Cambridge. However, his academic path took an unexpected turn in 1977.

### The Transformative Awakening

The pivotal moment in Tolle's life occurred when he was 29 years old. After experiencing continuous anxiety with periods of suicidal depression, he had a profound spiritual awakening. He describes hearing the words "resist nothing" as if spoken inside his chest, followed by a sensation of falling into a void. The next morning, everything had changed. He describes experiencing the world as fresh and pristine, picking up ordinary objects like a pencil or empty bottle and marveling at their beauty and aliveness.

Following this transformation, Tolle abandoned his doctoral studies at Cambridge. The experience essentially dissolved his old identity and set him on an entirely different life path.

### The Wandering Years

After his awakening, Tolle entered what some might call his "wilderness" period. He stayed with friends in a Buddhist monastery or otherwise slept rough on Hampstead Heath, leading his family to think him "irresponsible, even insane".

He changed his first name from Ulrich to Eckhart. According to various accounts, this was either in homage to the medieval German mystic Meister Eckhart, or based on a dream where he saw the name on a book cover he had written, later confirmed when a psychic friend spontaneously called him Eckhart.

During this period, Tolle spent several years understanding and integrating his transformative experience. Former Cambridge students and acquaintances began asking him about his beliefs, and he started working as a counselor and spiritual teacher.

### Emergence as a Teacher

Tolle moved to Glastonbury, a center of alternative living, where students continued to come to him. In 1995, he moved to Vancouver, British Columbia, where he would eventually settle permanently and meet his partner, Kim Eng, who is also a spiritual teacher.

### Literary Success

"The Power of Now" was published in 1997 by Namaste Publishing. The book experienced a gradual rise to fame, becoming a word-of-mouth phenomenon. In 2000, Oprah Winfrey recommended the book in her magazine O, and by August 2000 it reached The New York Times Best Seller list, reaching number one two years later. Oprah later credited the book with helping her through September 11, 2001.

By 2008, the book had been translated into 33 languages, and by 2009, total sales of "The Power of Now" and his second major book "A New Earth" in North America were estimated at 3 million and 5 million copies respectively. The Power of Now has now sold over 16 million copies worldwide.

In 2008, The New York Times called Tolle "the most popular spiritual author" in the United States.

### Other Works and Recognition

His other major publications include:

- "Stillness Speaks" (2003)
- "A New Earth: Awakening to Your Life's Purpose" (2005)
- "Guardians of Being" (2009)

In 2011, the Watkins Review ranked him first in their inaugural list of "The 100 Most Spiritually Influential Living People," and he has been included in the top five every year since, an achievement only matched by the Dalai Lama.

In 2008, he partnered with Oprah Winfrey to produce a groundbreaking webinar series based on "A New Earth," which attracted millions of viewers.

### Teaching Philosophy

Tolle's teachings draw from traditions such as Zen Buddhism, Christian mysticism, Sufism, and Hinduism, although he remains unaffiliated with any particular religion. His central message emphasizes living in the present moment and transcending the egoic mind. He teaches that psychological suffering stems from identification with one's thoughts rather than recognizing oneself as the awareness observing those thoughts.

Despite his commercial success, in 2003 he stated he had no intention of creating "a heavy commercial structure" or setting up an ashram or center, though his website does sell books and spiritual guidance materials, and streams monthly group meditations.

### Current Life

Tolle continues to live in Vancouver with his partner Kim Eng, teaching through books, talks, retreats, webinars, and online content. He remains one of the most influential spiritual voices of the 21st century, with his core message of presence and awakening continuing to resonate globally.
