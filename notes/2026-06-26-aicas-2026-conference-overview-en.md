**Answer:**

AICAS 2026 is the 8th IEEE International Conference on Artificial Intelligence Circuits and Systems — the premier conference of the IEEE Circuits and Systems Society (CASS) focused on the intersection of AI algorithms, circuits, architectures, and systems.

Key facts:

- **Dates / location:** 16–18 September 2026 · Ha Long Bay, Vietnam, jointly hosted by IEEE CASS Vietnam and IEEE CASS China (General Chairs: Yongfu Li and Xuan-Tu Tran).
- **Scope:** New computing paradigms for AI workloads spanning the full stack — from edge devices to cloud servers and from circuit to system levels. This is a hardware/systems venue, not a pure ML-algorithms one: think accelerators, in-memory compute, neuromorphic, efficient inference silicon, edge AI.
- **Publication:** Proceedings go to IEEE Xplore, with selected papers recommended to the IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems for AI (TCAS AI) or IEEE Transactions on VLSI Systems (TVLSI) for a Special Section.
- **Format:** Full-length papers of a maximum of 5 pages, in-person presentation required.

Deadlines:

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| Full-paper submission | April 07, 2026 |
| Notification of acceptance | June 18, 2026 |
| Camera-ready / advance registration | July 10, 2026 |

Note: don't confuse it with **ISCAS 2026** (Shanghai, the broader CASS flagship symposium) or **ISICAS 2026** (Torino, integrated circuits) — different conferences in the same society family.

Relevance to your trajectory: AICAS sits where your GPU/inference-optimization and model-training interests meet silicon. If you ever wanted to publish from your MI300X/inference work — e.g., kernel-level optimization or MoE serving efficiency — this is a venue that takes systems-level AI compute seriously, though it skews toward circuit/VLSI authors rather than software-only LLM work.
