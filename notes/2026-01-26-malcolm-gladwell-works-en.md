**Question:** What books has Malcolm Gladwell written?

**Answer:**

Malcolm Gladwell is a bestselling author known for his works of nonfiction that explore psychology, sociology, and human behavior through engaging storytelling. Here is a complete list of his published books in chronological order of publication:

- **The Tipping Point: How Little Things Can Make a Big Difference** (2000)  
  Examines how ideas, products, and behaviors spread like epidemics.

- **Blink: The Power of Thinking Without Thinking** (2005)  
  Explores rapid, intuitive decision-making and "thin-slicing."

- **Outliers: The Story of Success** (2008)  
  Analyzes the factors behind extraordinary success, including the famous 10,000-hour rule.

- **What the Dog Saw: And Other Adventures** (2009)  
  A collection of his best articles and essays from The New Yorker.

- **David and Goliath: Underdogs, Misfits, and the Art of Battling Giants** (2013)  
  Looks at how disadvantages can become advantages in certain contexts.

- **Talking to Strangers: What We Should Know About the People We Don't Know** (2019)  
  Investigates misunderstandings and miscommunications with strangers.

- **The Bomber Mafia: A Dream, a Temptation, and the Longest Night of the Second World War** (2021)  
  A historical narrative about World War II bombing strategies and technology.

- **Revenge of the Tipping Point: Overstories, Superspreaders, and the Rise of Social Engineering** (2024)  
  A follow-up to his debut book, revisiting social epidemics with new stories and a focus on modern manipulation of tipping points.

These are his eight major books (as of 2026). Gladwell has not published additional full-length books beyond these based on current records. He also hosts the podcast *Revisionist History* and contributes essays, but the above list covers his primary authored books.
