## Important Distinction

The GEP is designed for foreign professionals and excludes Mainland Chinese nationals. However, Chinese citizens have different pathways depending on their residency status:

## For Mainland Chinese Residents

Mainland China nationals residing in Mainland China cannot apply under GEP; instead, they must apply through the Admission Scheme for Mainland Talents and Professionals (ASMTP). This scheme has similar requirements to GEP but is specifically for Mainland residents.

## For Overseas Chinese Citizens

If you are a Chinese national living overseas, you may be eligible to come to Hong Kong under the GEP provided that you have permanent residence overseas, and have been residing overseas for at least one year before your application.

Specifically, you must meet both conditions:
1. Hold permanent residence outside Mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan
2. Have been residing overseas for at least one year immediately before submitting the application, which must be submitted from overseas

## Key Requirements (ASMTP or GEP)

- Possess special skills, knowledge, or experience not readily available in Hong Kong
- Have a confirmed job offer
- Meet salary standards that match market rates
- Have no serious criminal record
- The employing company must be legitimate with local presence

## Application Process

Applications are submitted online to the Hong Kong Immigration Department with all supporting documents. Processing typically takes 4 weeks. Successful applicants will also need to obtain an Exit-entry Permit (EEP) and exit endorsement from Mainland authorities if coming from Mainland China.
