## Apple Mac mini M4 (2024) — 24GB Model Overview

The 2024 Mac mini is Apple's most significant redesign of the product line in years, available in two chip variants — **M4** and **M4 Pro** — both of which offer a 24GB unified memory configuration.

---

### Two Paths to 24GB

There are actually **two different 24GB Mac mini models** to be aware of:

**1. Mac mini with M4 chip (24GB) — ~$999**

This model features a 4.4 GHz Apple M4 processor with 10 cores (4 performance cores and 6 efficiency cores), a 10-core GPU, a 16-core Neural Engine, and 24GB of RAM paired with 512GB of storage.

**2. Mac mini with M4 Pro chip (24GB) — ~$1,399**

This model features a 4.5 GHz Apple M4 Pro processor with 12 cores (8 performance cores and 4 efficiency cores), a 16-core GPU, a 16-core Neural Engine, and 24GB of RAM paired with 512GB of storage.

---

### What is "Unified Memory"?

Unlike traditional PCs where CPU and GPU have separate RAM pools, Apple's unified memory architecture means the CPU, GPU, and Neural Engine all share the same high-bandwidth memory pool. The M4 chip uses LPDDR5X-7500 SDRAM at 3750 MHz with 120 GB/s memory bandwidth. This makes the 24GB feel much more capable than 24GB of conventional RAM in a Windows PC.

---

### Design & Form Factor

At just five by five inches, Mac mini is designed to fit perfectly next to a monitor and is easy to place just about anywhere. The 2024 redesign is notably **smaller** than previous generations while being more powerful.

---

### Connectivity

Connectivity on the M4 model includes Wi-Fi 6E (802.11ax), Bluetooth 5.3, two USB-C ports (up to 10Gb/s) and a 3.5mm headphone jack on the front, and three Thunderbolt 4 (USB-C connector; up to 40Gb/s), HDMI 2.1, and Gigabit Ethernet on the back.

The M4 Pro model steps things up further: the M4 Pro Mac mini is the first Mac to carry Thunderbolt 5, with rear ports capable of up to 80 Gb/s, and up to 120 Gb/s in "Bandwidth Boost" mode oriented toward display experiences.

---

### Display Support

The M4 model simultaneously supports up to 3 displays at the same time — two 6K displays at 60Hz via Thunderbolt 4 plus one 5K display at 60Hz via Thunderbolt 4, or a 4K display at 60Hz via HDMI.

---

### Performance Highlights

The M4 chip delivers significant performance improvements including up to 6x faster speed than comparable PCs and up to 2.2x faster gaming performance than prior Mac mini models.

User reviews are overwhelmingly positive, highlighting the significantly smaller design, powerful M4/M4 Pro chips, and enhanced performance. The device is frequently noted for its quiet operation, even when performing intensive tasks.

---

### Apple Intelligence

macOS Sequoia and later supports Apple Intelligence on any Mac with Apple Silicon. The 24GB configuration is particularly well-suited for running local AI features and on-device language models that benefit from more unified memory headroom.

---

### Sustainability

Mac mini is designed as Apple's first carbon-neutral Mac, made with over 50% recycled materials, manufactured using 100% clean electricity, and prioritizes sustainable shipping methods to reduce carbon emissions.

---

### Which 24GB Model Should You Choose?

|---|---|---|

The **M4 24GB** at $999 represents exceptional value for most users — developers, content creators, and power users who want ample memory without jumping to the Pro tier.
