**Question: Estimate how many people born around 1994–1995 were in Guangdong when you took the 2013 high school entrance/college exam, including those who did not participate.**

I assume you mean **2013 Gaokao (高考)** in Guangdong, not the junior high exam (中考). The age matches:

* Born in **1994** → mostly took Gaokao in **2013** at age 18–19.
* Born in **1995** → some took it in 2013 if they skipped a grade or were born early, but most took it in 2014.

In 2013 Guangdong had about **727,000–730,000 Gaokao registrants**. ([高考在线][1])

To estimate the **whole birth cohort**, we need to add:

1. People who did not enter high school.
2. People who entered high school but did not take Gaokao.
3. Migrated out / changed education paths / vocational routes.

A rough demographic calculation:

### Step 1: Estimate Guangdong births around 1994–1995

China had about **21–22 million births/year** in the mid-1990s.

Guangdong population share was roughly 5–6%, so:

```
21.5M × 5.5% ≈ 1.18M births/year
```

So Guangdong likely had:

```
1994 cohort: ~1.1–1.2 million
1995 cohort: ~1.1–1.2 million
```

### Step 2: Compare with Gaokao participation

2013 Gaokao:

```
~730k participants
```

Participation rate:

```
730k / 1.15M ≈ 63%
```

That is plausible because not everyone continues through academic high school.

### My estimate

| Group                     |        Approx people |
| ------------------------- | -------------------: |
| Born 1994 in Guangdong    |      1.1–1.2 million |
| Born 1995 in Guangdong    |      1.1–1.2 million |
| Combined 1994+1995 cohort |  **2.2–2.4 million** |
| 2013 Gaokao participants  |                ~730k |
| Not taking Gaokao         | **~1.5–1.7 million** |

> "How many people around my age (1994/1995 birth) were there in Guangdong, including those who didn't take the exam?"
