Question: Why do people use the Vivaldi browser?

Answer:

## Why People Use Vivaldi Browser

**Vivaldi** is a feature-rich, privacy-focused web browser developed by Vivaldi Technologies, founded in 2016 by a former co-founder of Opera. Here are the main reasons people choose it:

---

### 1. 🔒 Strong Privacy & No Tracking
Vivaldi does not track or profile its users, and does not collect usage data. The company is based in Norway and Iceland, where privacy laws are strong, keeping user data away from Big Tech's reach. They have no external investors, meaning there's no pressure to monetize user data.

---

### 2. 🛡️ Built-in Ad & Tracker Blocker
Vivaldi comes with a built-in tracker and ad blocker. The tracker protection guards against malicious websites that collect user data, while the ad blocker eliminates annoying pop-ups and ads that might otherwise disrupt the browsing experience.

---

### 3. 🎨 Extreme Customization
Unlike other browsers that offer limited customization, Vivaldi allows users to tweak almost every aspect of the browser — from color schemes and layout to custom shortcuts and gestures — giving users complete control over their browsing experience.

---

### 4. 📑 Advanced Tab Management
Vivaldi makes browsing multiple tabs more efficient through features like Tab Stacking, which lets users group two or more tabs of the same subject together. It also offers task management tools to help keep the workspace clutter-free.

---

### 5. 🧰 All-in-One Productivity Tools
Vivaldi includes a built-in email client and a feed reader, making it designed for people who want to use their browser for more than just browsing the web. Other highlights include saving notes on the fly and viewing email and calendar directly from the browser — features most other browsers lack.

---

### 6. 🌐 Free Uncapped VPN (via ProtonVPN)
Vivaldi not only includes a free VPN like Opera and Edge, but its implementation — courtesy of ProtonVPN — is uncapped, giving users unlimited encrypted browsing.

---

### 7. ⚡ Chromium-Based Compatibility
Vivaldi is built on the open-source Chromium engine, like Chrome, Brave, and Edge, so it supports extensions from the Chrome Web Store, allowing users to bring their favorite tools over easily.

---

### 8. 💻 Low System Resource Usage
Vivaldi is light and easy on system resources and won't stress your CPU, RAM, and GPU while running.

---

### 9. 🤖 No Forced AI Integration
Unlike many modern browsers, Vivaldi has committed to keeping generative AI out of the browser, which appeals to users who want to remain in control of their own browsing rather than having an AI filter what they see.

---

### Who Is It For?
Vivaldi is best suited for **power users**, privacy-conscious individuals, developers, and anyone who feels mainstream browsers like Chrome or Firefox are too limited or too invasive. It offers versatility and flexibility in customization that most other browsers simply don't match, with a combination of security, privacy, and productivity all in one place.
