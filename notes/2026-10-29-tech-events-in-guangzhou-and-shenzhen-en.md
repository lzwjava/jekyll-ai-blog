### 🔵 Shenzhen

**1. Shenzhen Big Catch Up 2026**
A networking/social event on **Friday, November 13, 2026**, from 5:00–9:00 PM, organized by W.Media — good for tech and startup professionals.

Takes place **November 26–28, 2026**, at the Shenzhen World Exhibition and Convention Center in Bao'an. One of China's largest annual high-tech showcases.
