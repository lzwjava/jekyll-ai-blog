Here’s a practical, step-by-step plan to shift your sleep from erratic late nights (like 5am–1pm) to a consistent **2am–10am** routine. The key is gradual adjustment, strong cues, and removing blockers—your body can adapt in 1–2 weeks if you’re consistent.

### 1. **Pick a 7-day “reset” plan (gradual shift)**

- **Don’t jump straight to 2am.** Your circadian rhythm hates big leaps.
- **Shift bedtime 15–30 min earlier every 1–2 days.**
  - Day 1–2: Aim for 4:30am → 12:30pm
  - Day 3–4: 4am → 12pm
  - Day 5–6: 3:30am → 11:30am
  - Day 7: 3am → 11am
  - Then fine-tune to **2am–10am**

   *Track it in a simple note: “Bed at __, woke at __”*

### 2. **Anchor your wake-up with LIGHT + MOVEMENT**

- **Wake at 10am NO MATTER WHAT** (even if you only slept 3 hours).
- **Within 5 min of waking:**
     1. Open curtains → **30 min bright light** (or 10k lux lamp if dark).
     2. Drink water + **move** (10 push-ups, walk, stretch).
- This tells your brain: “Morning = NOW.” It pulls bedtime earlier automatically.

### 3. **Wind-down ritual starting 2 hours before target bedtime**

   For 2am sleep → start at **midnight**:

- **12:00am**: Dim all lights (<50 lux), blue-light blocker glasses or phone night mode.
- **12:15am**: No screens. Read paper book, journal, or listen to boring podcast.
- **12:45am**: 5-min stretch or shower (warm → cool).
- **1:00am**: Bed. Same order every night.

### 4. **Kill the “awake in bed” cycle**

- **If not asleep in 20 min → get up.** Sit in dim light, do nothing stimulating, return when sleepy.
- **No phone in bed.** Charge it in another room.

### 5. **Daytime rules (non-negotiable)**

   | Time | Rule |
   |------|------|
   | **After 3pm** | No caffeine (half-life = 6h) |
   | **After 8pm** | No heavy meals, alcohol, or naps >20 min |
   | **2pm–4pm** | Optional 20-min nap ONLY if crashing (set alarm) |

### 6. **Fix your “5am crash” triggers**

   Common culprits:

- **Doomscrolling** → Use app blockers (Freedom, Cold Turkey) at 1am.
- **Overthinking** → 5-min “worry dump” journal at 11pm.
- **Room too warm** → Keep bedroom 16–19°C (60–67°F).

### 7. **Week 2: Lock it in**

- Once you hit 2am–10am 4/7 nights, **protect the wake-up time rigidly**.
- If you slip to 4am one night, **still wake at 10am**—the sleep debt forces earlier bedtime next day.

### Quick checklist (print or screenshot)

```
[ ] Wake 10am → light + move
[ ] No caffeine after 3pm
[ ] Wind-down at 12am (dim + no screens)
[ ] Bed only for sleep
[ ] Shift bedtime 15–30 min earlier every 2 days
```

**Pro tip**: Melatonin (0.5mg) 3 hours before target bedtime can speed the shift, but use <1 week.

You’ll hit 2am–10am consistently within 10–14 days if you treat wake-up time as sacred.
