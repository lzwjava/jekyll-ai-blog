I compared both resumes. Here's my recommendation.

## Use `simple-resume-en/` — it's the better submission for the Illinois Tech Master of AI application

### Key difference (the deciding factor)

| | `resume-en/` | `simple-resume-en/` |
|---|---|---|
| Length | **15 pages** (a CV) | **3 pages** (a resume) |
| Sections | 13 sections | 6 focused sections |
| Summary | 11 bullets with personal info | 4 tight, career-focused bullets |
| Extras | blog posts, papers, tools, knowledge, spirit/mindset, links | none |

### Why the simple one wins

1. **Admissions committees expect a 1–2 page resume**, not a 15-page CV. 15 pages will get skimmed (at best) — and the key AI work gets buried. 3 pages is already slightly long; the simple one is in the right ballpark.

2. **The AI content you *need* to show is front-and-center** in the simple version: SEC-EDGAR-GPT paper, nanoGPT multi-GPU training (H200/H100/MI300X), the AI blog platform, DeepLearning.AI certificates, IELTS 6.5. This is exactly what an AI Master's wants to see.

3. **The 15-page version is padded with material that hurts, not helps, an academic application**:
   - Personal details (birth year, dropped out of university, daughter, travel history, "air fryer lover", life hacks like drying hair with a car vent) — irrelevant and unprofessional in an academic context.
   - 11-bullet summary reads like a self-narrative rather than a credentials summary.
   - Blog/papers/tools/knowledge/spirit-mindset sections overwhelm the reader and dilute the core message.

4. **Tone**: the simple version reads like a professional engineer targeting an AI program; the long one reads like a personal life story.

### One suggestion

Even at 3 pages, consider trimming the simple resume to **2 pages** for the application:
- Merge/drop the "AI-Powered Story Bot" project (weakest, most generic of the five).
- Condense the older non-AI roles (2014–2019) to 1–2 bullets each.
- Keep everything AI/ML-related at full detail — that's what gets you admitted.

If the program's portal allows only one PDF, submit `latex/en/simple-resume-en/simple-resume-en.pdf`.
