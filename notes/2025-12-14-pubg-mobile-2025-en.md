## 4. Movement & Positioning

- **High Ground Always:** Control sightlines; enemies uphill struggle.
- **Edge/Diamond Strategy:** Play zone edge—safe back, third-party fights.
- **Jiggle Peek:** Quick peek corners while crouched/jumping to bait shots.
- **Vehicles:** Use for rotations, but ditch early (loud). Prone in grass for stealth.
- **Avoid:** Running in open; hug cover/trees.

## 5. Combat Mastery
