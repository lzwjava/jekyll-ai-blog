Question: You removed the hydraulic jack’s bottom valve, oil spilled everywhere — what now?

Answer: **Don’t panic. The jack is probably recoverable.** You essentially opened the hydraulic reservoir/circuit, so you likely lost some hydraulic oil and introduced air.

Do this:

1. **Do not put a load/car on the jack yet.**
2. Put the jack **upright on a level surface**.
3. Clean the spilled oil and the valve/plug area.
4. **Reinstall the valve/plug** and tighten it normally. Don't overtighten it.
5. Check the jack's oil-fill port. You will probably need to **top up with the correct hydraulic jack oil**.
6. Then **bleed the air**:

   * Open the release valve.
   * Pump the handle several times.
   * Close the release valve.
   * Pump the jack up and down several times with **no load**.
   * Repeat until the lifting action becomes smooth and consistent.
7. Check carefully for leaks around the valve you removed.

⚠️ **Important:** Don't substitute brake fluid, engine oil, ATF, or random lubricating oil unless the jack manufacturer explicitly specifies it. Use hydraulic jack oil of the specified grade.

I can also walk you through a **no-load test to determine whether the jack still holds pressure** before you ever put the car on it.
