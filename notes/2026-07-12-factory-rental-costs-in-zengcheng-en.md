Question: How much does it cost to rent a 200 m² factory in Guangzhou suburbs like Zengcheng?

Answer:

For a **small 200 m² factory/workshop in Zengcheng (增城)**, a realistic range is:

| Type                                      |               Price | 200 m² monthly rent |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------: | ------------------: |
| Old industrial building / simple workshop |  ¥8–12 RMB/m²/month |  ¥1,600–2,400/month |
| Normal factory space                      | ¥12–20 RMB/m²/month |  ¥2,400–4,000/month |

Current listings in Zengcheng show many industrial spaces around **¥9–15 RMB/m²/month** for ordinary factory/warehouse areas, while newer parks can be around **¥10+ RMB/m²/month** or higher depending on facilities. ([99厂房网][1])

So for a small AI hardware / electronics / robot workshop:

* Rent: **~¥2,000–5,000/month**
* Deposit: usually **2–3 months**
* Utilities: electricity is important:

  * normal electricity: maybe ¥0.8–1.2/kWh
  * industrial electricity varies
* Property management: maybe ¥0.5–2 RMB/m²/month
