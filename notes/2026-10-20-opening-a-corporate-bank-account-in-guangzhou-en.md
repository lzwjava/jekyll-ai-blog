**Question:** In Guangzhou, with a 营业执照 (Business License) already obtained, how do you open a 企业银行账户 (Corporate Bank Account)?

---

**Answer:**

Opening a corporate bank account (企业银行开户) in Guangzhou after receiving your 营业执照 follows a structured process. Here's a step-by-step guide:

---

### Step 1 — Understand the Account Types

You must open a **基本存款账户 (Basic Deposit Account / 基本户)** first — this is the primary settlement account every registered company must have. You can later add:
- **一般存款账户** — for borrowing/loan proceeds
- **专用存款账户** — for specific purposes (payroll, project funds, etc.)

---

### Step 2 — Prepare Required Documents

Basic required documents include:

| Document | Notes |
|---|---|
| 营业执照 (Business License) | Original + copies |
| 法定代表人身份证 | Legal Representative's ID, original + copies |
| 经办人身份证 | Handler's ID (if not the legal rep), original + copies |
| 公司章程 (Articles of Association) | As filed with SAMR |
| 授权委托书 | If someone other than the legal rep is handling it |
| 公司印章 | 公章, 财务章, 法人章 |

Your company seals (公章, 财务章, 法人章) must have been registered at the Public Security Bureau (PSB) beforehand — they are legally binding and required for banking documentation.

> Some banks may also request additional information about company ownership structure and beneficial owners.

---

### Step 3 — Choose Your Bank

Major banks available across Guangzhou include:

- **State-owned Big Four:** ICBC (工行), CCB (建行), BOC (中行), ABC (农行)
- **Joint-stock banks:** 招商银行, 广发银行, 中信银行, 浦发银行
- **Local banks:** 广州银行, 广州农商银行

Since August 2020, 1,256 bank branches across Guangzhou (including ICBC, ABC, BOC, CCB, 交通银行, 广州银行, 广州农商银行, 招商银行, 中信银行, 广发银行, 浦发银行, and 华夏银行) are authorized to handle enterprise formation services, including bank account pre-registration.

**Tip:** Choose a bank near your registered business address — some banks prefer the company's registered address to be within their service area.

---

### Step 4 — Use the Guangzhou "一网通" Platform (Optional but Recommended)

Applicants starting a business through the Guangzhou "Business One-Link Platform" (广州市开办企业一网通平台) can fill in corporate account information at one time. After being authorized by the enterprise, the platform delivers the information to the selected bank, and enterprises can obtain their reserved account numbers through the platform's e-Cert service, making it more convenient for withholding taxes, paying social insurance, and other matters.

Platform access:
- PC: `https://air.scjgj.gz.gov.cn/`
- WeChat Mini Program: search "广州市开办企业一网通平台"

---

### Step 5 — Visit the Bank Branch

Bring all the documents to your chosen bank, fill in the account application form and affix the company seal (公章), submit to the bank staff for review, and after passing, the bank will open the basic account.

**Important:** Since Guangdong province eliminated the enterprise bank account permit (开户许可证) requirement from April 28, 2019, the system changed from approval-based (核准制) to filing-based (备案制). The People's Bank of China no longer issues an 开户许可证. However, to prevent fraudulent account opening, the bank will still verify the 法定代表人's intent to open the account. The legal representative typically needs to be present or verified remotely.

---

### Step 6 — Timeline & Fees

CCB (建行), for example, will complete the account opening procedure within **3 business days** from the scheduled appointment date for enterprises that meet the conditions. The account can be used for fund receipt and payment from the day it is formally opened. The account opening fee is **100 RMB/account**.

For small micro-enterprises (小微企业), simplified account opening (简易开户) can complete a basic RMB account within **1 business day** — though initially with limited functionality (only tax/social security deductions), with full features unlocked after a full KYC review within 3–10 business days.
