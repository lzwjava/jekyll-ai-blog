**Question:** What are the main goals (主要目标) that China needs to achieve by 2035 according to the course textbook?

---**Answer:**

The 2035 goals come from the **20th Party Congress Report (2022)** and are covered in the textbook as **"两步走"战略** — Step 1 (2020–2035) and Step 2 (2035–2049). The 2035 goals represent the **completion of Step 1**.

---

### Big Picture First — 两步走 (Two-Step Strategy)

The overall strategic arrangement for comprehensively building a modern socialist country is divided into two steps: from 2020 to 2035, basically realizing socialist modernization; from 2035 to the middle of this century, building China into a strong, democratic, culturally advanced, harmonious, and beautiful modern socialist country.

---

### The 8 Main Goals by 2035 (八大目标)

The 20th Party Congress Report from 8 aspects made clear the overall goals for China's development by 2035 in the first step. Here they are in full:

---

**① 经济目标 — Economy**

Economic strength, scientific and technological strength, and overall national strength will increase significantly, GDP per capita will reach a new high level, and China will move toward becoming a manufacturing power, quality power, aerospace power, transportation power, cyber power, and digital China.

---

**② 创新目标 — Innovation & Science**

Achieving high-level self-reliance and self-improvement in science and technology — basically realizing scientific and technological modernization, building more world-leading science centers and innovation hubs, with a large number of national research institutions, research universities, and leading technology enterprises entering the world's top ranks, forming a high-level open innovation ecosystem.

---

**③ 法治目标 — Rule of Law**

Basically realizing the modernization of the national governance system and governance capacity, with the whole-process people's democracy system becoming more sound, and basically building a law-governed country, law-governed government, and law-governed society.

> ⚠️ Key exam trap: it says **基本建成** (basically built) — NOT **完全建成** (completely built).

---

**④ 文化目标 — Culture & Education**

Building an education power, science and technology power, talent power, cultural power, sports power, and Healthy China, with the country's cultural soft power significantly enhanced. The average years of education for the working-age population will be further increased, a modern education system serving lifelong learning for all will be built, and overall educational modernization will be achieved. China will become an important global talent center. The influence of Chinese culture and the cohesion of the Chinese nation will be significantly enhanced.

---

**⑤ 民生目标 — People's Wellbeing**

People's all-round development and common prosperity for all people will achieve more obvious and substantive progress.

---

**⑥ 社会目标 — Social Governance**

The social governance system will be more complete, society will be full of vitality while remaining orderly, and people's sense of gain, happiness, and security will be significantly enhanced.

---

**⑦ 生态目标 — Ecology**

Green production and lifestyle will be widely formed, carbon emissions will stabilize and gradually decrease after reaching a peak, the ecological environment will fundamentally improve, and the goal of Beautiful China will be basically realized.

---

**⑧ 安全目标 — National Security**

National security system and capacity will be significantly strengthened, and the ability to safeguard national security will be significantly improved.

### 📝 Exam Tips

1. **Number to remember** — there are exactly **8 goals** (八个方面) — exams often ask "how many"

2. **Most tested trap** — the word **基本** (basically/fundamentally):
   - Goal #3: **基本**建成法治国家 (NOT 完全建成)
   - Goal #7: 美丽中国**基本**实现 (NOT 完全实现)
   - Exams love to substitute 完全 for 基本 as a wrong answer

3. **Most tested single goal** — Goal #5 (共同富裕取得更明显的**实质性**进展) — the word **实质性** is always tested

4. **Essay connection** — when asked about 2035 goals, always link back to the **两步走** strategy and explain that 2035 is Step 1, while the final goal (富强民主文明和谐美丽) is achieved by **2049**
