Question: What is the new State Council exit-entry regulation published on July 31, 2026, and what does it mean?

Answer:

You are right — I missed this new regulation. The link you provided is the official Xinhua release:

**《国务院关于出境入境管理的规定》**
Published: **July 31, 2026**
Effective: **September 15, 2026** (according to the regulation text)
([news.cn][1])

This is a new State Council regulation under the framework of the **Exit and Entry Administration Law**. It is not the same as the earlier “外国人入境出境管理条例”. ([Baiyin District Government][2])

Main points:

## 1. China establishes an "exit security risk prevention system"

Article 2:

* China will establish and improve a system to prevent exit risks for Chinese citizens.
* Government departments will publish overseas security warnings.
* For people traveling to high-risk countries/regions, immigration authorities may:

  * remind them,
  * advise caution,
  * and in extreme cases **persuade them not to go**.

The regulation says this applies especially to:

* war/conflict areas,
* severe security risks,
* major disasters,
* epidemics, etc.

([news.cn][1])

This does **not mean ordinary overseas travel is banned**. The focus is risk areas.

---

## 2. New grounds for preventing Chinese citizens from leaving

Article 4 is the more significant part.

A person may be restricted from leaving China if:

### A. Fraudulent exit documents / illegal border crossing

Example:

* fake passport documents,
* fake visa materials,
* illegal exit.

Possible restriction:

* after administrative punishment:
* **6 months to 3 years no exit**.

([news.cn][1])

---

### B. Overseas criminal activity harming national security/interests

If a Chinese citizen:

* commits crimes overseas,
* harms national security or national interests,

authorities may decide:

* after returning to China,
* **6 months to 3 years no exit**.

([news.cn][1])

---

### C. Technology/export-control related restriction

This is the part attracting attention among tech workers.

Article 4:

> Violating export control, technology import/export management rules, and potentially harming national industrial security or technology security

may allow relevant authorities to decide:

* **not to permit exit**.

([news.cn][1])

This is likely related to:

* sensitive technology transfer,
* controlled technology,
* export-control compliance.

For normal software engineers traveling abroad, this does **not automatically apply**. It is aimed at people involved in regulated technology transfer.

---

## 3. Some restrictions may not be fully disclosed immediately

Article 6:

Normally, a person restricted from leaving should receive:

* reason,
* legal basis,
* appeal/review channel.

However:

> if notification may affect national security or criminal investigation, notification may be withheld.

([news.cn][1])

This is one of the more controversial parts because it expands administrative discretion.

---

## 4. Immigration / overseas-study / visa agencies get stricter regulation

Articles 7-10:

Exit-entry service agencies must:

* register with immigration authorities,
* keep compliance systems,
* protect personal data.

They cannot:

* create false documents,
* assist illegal immigration,
* leak personal information,
* mislead customers.

Existing agencies have a transition period to register.

([news.cn][1])

---

## Practical impact for an ordinary Chinese citizen

For most people:

✅ Tourism
✅ Studying abroad
✅ Working overseas
✅ Visiting family

should continue normally.

The affected groups are mainly:

1. People using fake immigration materials.
2. Illegal immigration organizers.
3. People involved in sensitive technology/export-control violations.
4. People traveling to extremely dangerous regions.
5. Certain security/criminal cases.

([news.cn][1])

---

For someone like you (AI engineer, LLM training, GPUs, model training):

The relevant part is **Article 4(3)**.

Your risk is not "knowing AI" or "working in AI". The issue would be if someone:

* works with controlled technologies,
* transfers restricted technical data/models/hardware,
* violates export-control rules.

Given your interests (LLM, vLLM, Qwen, GPUs), the practical lesson is:

* keep employment/IP ownership clear,
* avoid exporting restricted datasets/models/hardware without compliance,
* understand US/EU/China export-control rules if working internationally.

This regulation is more about **state security + technology control + illegal migration**, not a general travel restriction.
