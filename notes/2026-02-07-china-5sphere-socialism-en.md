Question: What is the overall layout of the cause of socialism with Chinese characteristics explicitly stated in the report to the 19th National Congress of the CPC?

Answer:

The correct answer is **C. “五位一体”** (Five-sphere Integrated Plan / "Five in One" overall layout).

### Detailed Explanation

The **19th National Congress of the Communist Party of China** (held in October 2017) formally established **Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era** as the guiding ideology of the Party.

In this report, the **overall layout** (总体布局) of the socialist cause with Chinese characteristics is clearly defined as the **“五位一体”** (Five-sphere Integrated Plan), which consists of:

1. **Economic construction** (经济建设)
2. **Political construction** (政治建设)
3. **Cultural construction** (文化建设)
4. **Social construction** (社会建设)
5. **Ecological civilization construction** (生态文明建设)

This "Five-sphere Integrated Plan" is the **strategic overall layout** for building socialism with Chinese characteristics in the new era.

At the same time, the **strategic layout** (战略布局) is defined as the **“四个全面”** (Four Comprehensives):

- Comprehensively building a moderately prosperous society (全面建成小康社会)
- Comprehensively deepening reform (全面深化改革)
- Comprehensively advancing law-based governance (全面依法治国)
- Comprehensively governing the Party with strict discipline (全面从严治党)

Additionally, the report emphasized strengthening the **“四个自信”** (Four Confidences):

- Confidence in the path (道路自信)
- Confidence in theory (理论自信)
- Confidence in the system (制度自信)
- Confidence in culture (文化自信)

### Why the other options are incorrect

- **A. “三个代表”** (Three Represents)
  This was the important thought put forward by Jiang Zemin at the 16th National Congress (2002). It is not the overall layout.

- **B. “四个全面”** (Four Comprehensives)
  This is the **strategic layout** (战略布局), not the **overall layout** (总体布局). The 19th Congress report distinguishes these two concepts clearly.

- **C. “五位一体”**
  This is the precise term used in the 19th Congress report for the **overall layout** of the socialist cause.

- **D. “四个自信”** (Four Confidences)
  This refers to the ideological and spiritual foundations, not the overall structural layout of the socialist cause.
