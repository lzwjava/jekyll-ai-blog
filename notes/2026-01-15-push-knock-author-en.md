Question: Who is the author of the line “鸟宿池边树，僧推（或敲）月下门”?

Answer:

The correct answer is **B. 贾岛** (Jia Dao).

This famous couplet comes from the Tang Dynasty poem **《题李凝幽居》** (Inscription on Li Ning's Secluded Residence) by the poet **贾岛** (Jia Dao).

The well-known story behind “推” (push) vs “敲” (knock) is that Jia Dao was originally undecided between the two words and, while riding on a donkey and repeatedly gesturing “push” and “knock,” accidentally bumped into the carriage of **韩愈** (Han Yu). Han Yu suggested that “敲” (knock) sounded better, which is why the phrase “推敲” (tūi qiāo) later became a Chinese idiom meaning “to weigh one's words carefully” or “to polish/refine wording.”

- **韩愈** (Han Yu) was a great prose writer and poet, but not the author of this line.
- **柳宗元** (Liu Zongyuan) and **白居易** (Bai Juyi) were also major Tang poets, but this couplet belongs to Jia Dao.

So the correct choice is **B**.
