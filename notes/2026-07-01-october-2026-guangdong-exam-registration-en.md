Question: When can participants register for the October 2026 Guangdong self-study exam?

Answer:

For the **October 2026** session, the registration window (per secondary sources, since the official 须知 for October hasn't posted yet):

- **New-student pre-registration (新生预报名):** around **Aug 25, 10:00 – Aug 28, 12:00**
- **Formal registration (正式报名):** Aug 25, 10:00 – Aug 28, 17:00
- **Course registration / 报考 (selecting subjects):** Sep 1, 14:00 – Sep 4, 17:00

So practically: **late August (Aug 25–28) for registration, early September (Sep 1–4) to pick your exam subjects.**

The official registration 须知 for October typically posts in **August**. The pattern holds reliably though: registration is consistently **~8 weeks before the exam** (April exam → Feb registration; October exam → Aug registration).
