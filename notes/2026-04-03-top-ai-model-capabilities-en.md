## 🧠 **Frontier Proprietary Models**

These are closed‑source, high‑performance models from major AI labs — usually impressive in benchmarks, reasoning, or multimodal capabilities.

### **GPT‑5.x Series (OpenAI)**

* **Strengths:** Excellent general reasoning, balanced performance, multimodal input support (text, images, etc.), and ecosystem compatibility (plugins, tools). ([Saeree ERP | Saeree ERP][1])
* **Deep Thinking:** Very capable in multi‑step reasoning and abstract problems; scores highly on benchmarks like ARC‑AGI‑2 and advanced math tests. ([Reddit][2])
* **Notes:** The “Thinking” or “Pro” modes trade latency for deeper reasoning and more context. Good at chaining detailed logical steps.

**Best for:** Broad reasoning + multimodal tasks where you want one model that does *everything well*.

---

### **Gemini 3 Pro / Deep Think (Google)**

* **Strengths:** Massive context windows (up to millions of tokens) — useful for long documents, books, or research summaries. ([Saeree ERP | Saeree ERP][1])
* **Deep Thinking:** The *Deep Think* variant is explicitly engineered for advanced math, logic, and hypothesis exploration using parallel reasoning techniques. ([Android Central][3])
* **Multimodal Focus:** Excellent with images, video, and document understanding, integrated deeply with Google tools. ([Saeree ERP | Saeree ERP][1])

**Best for:** Long‑context reasoning, multi‑modal “screen” reasoning, and tasks relying on large document analysis.

---

### **Claude Opus / Sonnet (Anthropic)**

* **Strengths:** Among the leading models for reasoning, coding, and sustained cognitive workflows. ([TECHi®][4])
* **Deep Thinking:** Excellent at multi‑step logical tasks, structured breakdowns, and deep analysis over long conversations. Claude Opus especially excels at complex real‑world workflows like large coding projects. ([TECHi®][4])
* **Consistency:** Often produces clearer, more consistent outputs even on dense prompts compared with some competitors. ([Tom's Guide][5])

**Best for:** Deep, persistent analysis and structured long‑form reasoning (e.g., technical writing, code generation, multi‑stage plans).

## 🧠 **Other Notable Models & Families**

These aren’t in every user’s mind but are becoming significant in comparison conversations.

### **Grok (xAI)**

* **Positioning:** Trained at large scale with a focus on rapid inference and real‑time data integration (e.g., web and social media streams). ([IBM][6])
* **Deep Thinking:** Not typically leading on deep multi‑step logic by benchmark scores — focused more on *speed, real‑time context*, and accessibility.

**Best for:** Fast, real‑time tasks, open ended exploration, or where quick judgment is more important than deep reasoning.

### **Llama 4 (Meta)**

* **Open‑Source Leader:** MoE (Mixture of Experts) variants like *Scout* and *Maverick* offer extremely long contexts and good reasoning performance — now competitive with proprietary models. ([Saeree ERP | Saeree ERP][1])
* **Deep Thinking:** Very strong in logic and comprehensive reasoning across long texts; excellent as a self‑hosted option. ([Saeree ERP | Saeree ERP][1])

**Best for:** Users who want deep reasoning with local control (self‑hosting), especially for large documents and multimodal inputs.

---

### **Mistral Series**

* **High Efficiency:** Models like **Mistral Large 2** deliver strong reasoning and coding support with excellent cost‑efficiency. ([IBM][6])
* **Deep Thinking:** Solid performance on math, reasoning, and code benchmarks, though generally a tier below the absolute top proprietary reasoning leaders.

**Best for:** Cost‑effective yet capable reasoning workflows, especially where budget matters.

---

### **OpenAI o‑Series (o1/o3)**

* **Reinforcement‑Learning‑Enhanced:** Trained to “think before answering,” driving strong STEM reasoning and coding. ([IBM][6])
* **Deep Thinking:** Very capable for complex quantitative tasks; especially strong in chain‑of‑thought support.

**Best for:** STEM‑oriented reasoning and structured math problems.
