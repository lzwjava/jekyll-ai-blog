**Question:** Is trust a critical factor in business, and does the selection of partners and collaborators matter greatly?

**Answer:**

Absolutely — trust is arguably the *foundation* of all sustainable business relationships. Here's why it matters so deeply:

**Trust reduces friction.** When you trust a partner, you spend less time verifying, auditing, and second-guessing. That saved energy goes into actual work.

**Partner selection is a force multiplier.** The right collaborator can accelerate your progress dramatically. The wrong one can drain resources, create legal exposure, or damage your reputation — sometimes irreparably.

**A few key dimensions to evaluate:**

- **Competence trust** — can they actually deliver what they promise?
- **Integrity trust** — do they keep their word even when it's costly?
- **Alignment trust** — do they share your values and long-term vision?
- **Communication trust** — will they tell you bad news honestly, or hide it?

**The asymmetry is brutal.** Trust takes years to build and seconds to destroy. One dishonest act, one broken commitment, one hidden conflict of interest — and the relationship is effectively over.

**Warren Buffett's famous filter** is worth remembering: he looks for three things in people — *intelligence, energy, and integrity* — and adds that without integrity, the first two will kill you.

In practice, **due diligence before committing** (checking track records, talking to past partners, starting with small low-stakes collaboration) is the rational way to build evidence of trustworthiness before the stakes are high.

The Chinese saying captures it well: **路遥知马力，日久见人心** — *a long road reveals a horse's strength; time reveals a person's heart.*
