Question: What does “buzzwords” mean?

Answer:

**“Buzzwords”** are trendy or fashionable words and phrases that become popular for a period of time, especially in business, technology, or media.

They are often:

* Used to sound modern, smart, or impressive
* Repeated frequently in conversations or marketing
* Sometimes vague or overused, without clear meaning

### Simple explanation

A buzzword is a **“hot word”** that people use because it’s popular—not always because it adds real value.

### Examples

* In tech: *AI*, *blockchain*, *cloud-native*, *Web3*
* In business: *synergy*, *disruption*, *scalable*, *innovation*

### Key idea

Buzzwords can be useful shorthand when everyone understands them—but they can also become **empty jargon** if people use them without substance.

### Example sentence

> “The presentation sounded impressive, but it was full of buzzwords and lacked real details.”
