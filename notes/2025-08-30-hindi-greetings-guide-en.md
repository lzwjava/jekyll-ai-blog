In Hindi, **"Namaste" (नमस्ते)** is the most common greeting. It literally means *"I bow to you"* and is used both for hello and goodbye.

You can also use other greetings depending on context:

* **Namaskar (नमस्कार)** – a slightly more formal version of Namaste.
* **Pranam (प्रणाम)** – very respectful, often used when greeting elders.
* **Suprabhat (सुप्रभात)** – good morning.
* **Shubh Sandhya (शुभ संध्या)** – good evening.
* **Shubh Ratri (शुभ रात्रि)** – good night.
* **Namaste ji / Namaskar ji** – adding *"ji"* makes it more polite.

👉 If you want a friendly, everyday greeting, **“Namaste”** is the safest and most widely understood.
