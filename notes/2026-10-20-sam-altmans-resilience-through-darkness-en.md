**Question:** How did Sam Altman go through dark times?

**Answer:**

Sam Altman has faced several significant challenges throughout his career, and his responses to them reveal a lot about his character and resilience.

---

### 1. 🔥 The OpenAI Firing (November 2023) — The Biggest "Dark Time"

This is the most dramatic episode. While Altman was at the peak of his success and touring the world to promote AI, OpenAI's board members were secretly turning on him — they said they had lost trust in him, claiming he wasn't candid in ways that made it hard to ensure OpenAI's work was benefiting humanity. In November 2023, the board fired him.

**His immediate emotional reaction:**
Altman revealed the experience felt like a dream. He didn't understand what was happening and felt the termination was handed down in an "unprecedentedly, in my opinion, crazy way."

**How he snapped out of it:**
In an interview with The Verge, Altman said his immediate reaction "was sort of one of defiance," and that "it took me a few minutes to snap out of it and get over the ego and emotions."

**What kept him going:**
He collected himself, told himself he could move on and still work on AGI somehow. He then started receiving messages from people saying they wanted to come work with him wherever he went. He flew back to California to meet with people and stayed very focused on moving forward — remarkably, he said nothing disparaging against OpenAI during the whole ordeal.

**The outcome:**
In just five days, OpenAI cycled through two interim CEOs, nearly had its entire team poached by its largest investor (Microsoft), and ultimately brought Altman back. Nearly all OpenAI employees threatened to quit and follow him out of the company. This loyalty was a powerful testament to the culture he'd built.

---

### 2. 📉 Early Startup Failure — Loopt

Before the AI era, Altman's first startup, Loopt (a location-sharing app), was founded in 2005 and was one of the first companies to receive funding from Y Combinator — but it never achieved the scale he hoped for. It was eventually acquired by Green Dot in 2012. Rather than giving up, he pivoted into investing and mentorship through Y Combinator.

---

### 3. 🧠 His Core Mindset — How He Deals With Darkness

Altman's resilience philosophy is captured in his own words:

> *"A big secret is that you can bend the world to your will a surprising percentage of the time — most people don't even try, and just accept that things are the way that they are."* — Sam Altman

He sees risk-taking as essential: it's often easier to take risks early in your career because you don't have much to lose and potentially have a lot to gain.

He also identified not wanting to be turned down as one of the hardest life skills to learn — and one every founder must overcome. Either you decide to live in fear of rejection, or you move past it.

| Challenge | How He Handled It |
