**Question:** What are the two ports on the NVIDIA Quadro 410, and what are they?

**Answer:**

The NVIDIA Quadro 410 has two display connectors: **1× DVI-I (single link)** and **1× DisplayPort**. Here's what each one is:

**1. DVI-I (Digital Visual Interface – Integrated)**
A classic video output port that carries both **analog and digital** signals. It can connect to older monitors using a VGA adapter or digital monitors directly. The "single link" variant supports resolutions up to **1920×1200**.

**2. DisplayPort**
A modern digital display interface that supports higher resolutions and refresh rates. The Quadro 410 supports **DisplayPort 1.2**, which also includes features like HDMI 1.4 output (via adapter) and HDCP content protection.

In short, the card can drive **up to 2 monitors simultaneously** — one via DVI, one via DisplayPort — making it suitable for basic dual-display workstation setups.
