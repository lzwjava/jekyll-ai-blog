Question: Give quotes from Jensen Huang (NVIDIA CEO)

Answer:

Here are some notable quotes from Jensen Huang, CEO of NVIDIA, covering leadership, AI, mindset, and engineering philosophy:

---

### 💡 On mindset & resilience

* “People with very high expectations have very low resilience.” ([The Times of India][1])

* “You benefit from the kindness of all the people that support you.” ([Wikiquote][2])

---

### 🚀 On innovation & engineering

* “We need things to be as complex as necessary, but as simple as possible.” ([Global Advisors][3])

* “You should engineer something from first principles… limited only by physics.” ([Global Advisors][4])

* “Your organization should be the architecture of the machinery of building the product.” ([Wikiquote][2])

---

### 🧠 On learning & leadership

* “I’ll take every possible opportunity… and use it to shape everybody else’s belief system.” ([Global Advisors][5])

* “There’s a language to proteins… imagine the scale we can understand and generate.” ([Wikiquote][2])

---

### ⚡ On execution & anxiety

* “There are some things you can do something about… let’s go do it.” ([Global Advisors][6])

---

### 🤖 On AI & the future

* “AI is going to impact every industry.” ([Supply Chain Today - Home Page][7])

* “20 years ago… AI was science fiction. Today, we are living it.” ([Supply Chain Today - Home Page][7])

---

### 🎯 On work, struggle & success

* “I wish upon you plenty of pain and suffering.” (on building something great) ([YouTube][8])

---

These quotes reflect a consistent philosophy:

* Think from **first principles**
* Embrace **difficulty and pressure**
* Focus on **execution over complexity**
* View AI as a **transformational force across all industries**
