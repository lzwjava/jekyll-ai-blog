**Answer:**

The article, published by **Xinhua News Agency on May 18, 2018**, is titled **"Huang Hongsheng: Building Skyworth into a Globally Competitive 'China Chip' Smart Home Appliance Enterprise."** It covers an exclusive interview with Huang Hongsheng (黄宏生), the founder of **Skyworth Group (创维集团)**.

---

**Key Points:**

**1. Who is Huang Hongsheng?**
He is the founder of the Skyworth (创维) brand. In 1987, at age 28, he left a secure government-linked job to start his own business. By 2018, he had built Skyworth into a major multinational consumer electronics conglomerate.

**2. Skyworth's Growth**
By 2018, Skyworth Group had grown into a comprehensive group covering black appliances, white appliances, digital set-top boxes, and other full home appliance categories, with its business and products covering markets in Asia, the Americas, Europe, and other parts of the world. It owns multiple global brands including Skyworth, Coocaa, and Metz, as well as several overseas manufacturing bases.

**3. Strategic Vision**
Skyworth's overall transformation and upgrading strategy for the next 5 years revolves tightly around achieving a revenue target of 100 billion yuan, aiming to go from China's "TV King" to the world's "TV King," and to transform from home appliance manufacturing into a globally leading smart home solution provider.

**4. Talent Strategy — Three "Forces"**
Skyworth's talent strategy consists of three groups: the first is top STEM graduates fresh from university, recruiting around 600–700 annually; the second is overseas returnees who studied abroad and then joined Skyworth; the third is elites who left state-owned enterprises during restructuring to join Skyworth's entrepreneurial wave.

**5. AI & Self-Developed Chips**
Skyworth has developed TV chips with independent Chinese intellectual property rights. Over ten years ago, Huang Hongsheng established a research team starting with analog circuits, later forming a new team for CPU and analog-digital circuit design, pursuing a chip design path. He also noted that achieving strong chip competitiveness still requires a long road ahead.

**6. R&D Investment**
Over 30 years, Skyworth has established 10 global R&D centers in China, the UK, Austria, and other locations. By March 2018, Skyworth had cumulatively applied for 5,710 patents and been granted 3,794 patents. In 2017, Skyworth won China's National Science and Technology Progress First Prize.
