## Answer:

### What problem does it solve?

Traditional international shipping is fragmented:

```
Manufacturer
    |
Freight forwarder
    |
Shipping line / airline
    |
Insurance
    |
Bank financing
    |
Customs
```

FreightAmigo tries to make this a single digital workflow:

```
Customer
   |
FreightAmigo Platform
   |
AI + Big Data
   |
Shipping + Insurance + Financing + Tracking
```

The platform provides:

* Freight quotation comparison
* Air / sea / rail / trucking booking
* Shipment tracking
* Customs-related services
* Cargo insurance
* Trade finance solutions

It describes itself as combining **FreightTech + FinTech + InsurTech + AI + Big Data**. ([Freight Amigo Help][2])

### 3. Trade finance

They combine logistics data with financing.

Example:

A Shenzhen exporter ships goods to Europe.

Bank asks:

> "Should we give this SME a $500k credit line?"

FreightAmigo can provide:

* shipment history
* customer behavior
* transaction data
* logistics risk signals

This is similar to alternative credit scoring.
