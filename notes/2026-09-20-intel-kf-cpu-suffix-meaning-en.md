**Question:** What does "KF" mean in Intel CPU naming?

**Answer:**

Great question! Intel uses **suffix letters** after the model number to indicate specific features.

### 🔤 Intel CPU Suffix Breakdown

| Suffix | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **K** | Unlocked multiplier = **overclockable** |
| **F** | **No integrated graphics** (needs dedicated GPU) |
| **KF** | Both — **overclockable AND no integrated graphics** |

### 📊 Quick Comparison Example (i5-13600 family)

| Model | Overclockable | Integrated Graphics |
|---|---|---|
| i5-13600 | ❌ | ✅ |
| i5-13600K | ✅ | ✅ |
| i5-13600F | ❌ | ❌ |
| i5-13600KF | ✅ | ❌ |

### 💡 Why buy KF over K?

- **KF is usually slightly cheaper** than the K version
- Because removing the iGPU cuts manufacturing cost
- Makes sense if you **already have a dedicated GPU** (which most gamers do)
- You lose nothing performance-wise if you're using a discrete GPU anyway

### ⚠️ Remember

If you buy any CPU with **"F"** in the name, you **must** have a dedicated GPU — otherwise you'll get no display output at all.
