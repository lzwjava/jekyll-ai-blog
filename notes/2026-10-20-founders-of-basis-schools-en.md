**Question:** Who is the founder / founding team of BASIS (贝赛思)?

**Answer:**

BASIS Schools was founded by **Dr. Michael Block** and **Olga Block** — a married couple who are both economists by training.

---

### 👨‍🎓 Dr. Michael Block

Michael Block is the executive chairman of BASIS Educational Ventures and Chairman of BASIS.ed. He earned a BS, MS, and PhD in economics from **Stanford University**, served as a senior research fellow at Stanford's Hoover Institution for six years, and then enjoyed a 25-year career as a professor of economics at the **University of Arizona**.

---

### 👩‍🎓 Olga Block

Olga Block grew up in Prague when her country was still called Czechoslovakia, and worked as a university professor and dean. As an immigrant from the Czech Republic, she wanted a more rigorous education for her daughter and decided to start her own school with the help of her American husband Michael.

---

### 🤝 How They Met

Olga and Michael met in 1992, when Michael was teaching a seminar in Austria that Olga attended. They got married, and Olga moved to the States in 1996.

---

### 🏫 The Founding Idea

Co-founder Dr. Michael Block discovered that students in his university classroom who were educated abroad were more prepared to excel in their studies. Meanwhile, Olga Block couldn't find a high school program in the U.S. for her daughter that matched the content depth of her own education from Eastern Europe. This dual frustration led them to build BASIS from scratch.

The first BASIS Curriculum School, **BASIS Tucson**, was founded in Tucson, Arizona in **1998**, with the intent to educate students at an internationally competitive level.

---

### 🌏 Expansion to China

The network then expanded globally. The first BASIS International School opened in **Shenzhen in 2015**, recognized as the largest opening of an international school in the history of southern China. The Huizhou (Park Lane Harbour) campus followed in 2018.
