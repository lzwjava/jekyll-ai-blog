**Question:** Has the demand for software engineering increased or decreased in recent years as AI/ChatGPT becomes popular?

**Answer:**

The real picture is nuanced — **overall demand is recovering, but the profession is being restructured**, not eliminated.

---

### 📉 What's Declining

- US software development job postings remain **27.5% below their February 2020 level**, though a recovery is underway.
- Entry-level coding jobs are declining — tasks like CRUD operations and basic testing are now automated, leading to fewer opportunities for junior developers.
- Stanford University's 2026 AI Index found that employment in AI-exposed fields for those aged 22 to 25 has fallen **16% since the rise of generative AI**, with early-career software developers seeing a **20% decline**.
- The BLS expects **computer programmer jobs to shrink 7%**, while data scientist jobs grow 35%.

---

### 📈 What's Growing

- Software development postings have grown **almost 15% since early 2025**, with **71% of that increase coming from senior roles** and 37% from AI-titled jobs.
- Tech hiring overall was **18% higher** than a year earlier in August 2026, but AI and machine-learning job postings **increased 101%** — more than five times as fast.
- AI/ML job postings grew **163% from 2024 to 2025** and are up a further **74% year-over-year** in 2026.
- AI engineers can earn up to **41% more** than traditional developers, and AI engineer job openings remain active for a median of **74 days**, compared with 44 days for general software engineer roles.

---

### 💡 The Big Picture: Restructuring, Not Elimination

| Role Type | Trend |
|---|---|
| Junior / entry-level | ⬇️ Shrinking fast |
| Senior / architect | ⬆️ Strong growth |
| AI/ML Engineer | 🚀 Explosive growth |
| QA / Manual Tester | ⬇️ Being absorbed by AI |
| Backend / Data Engineer | ⬆️ Rising demand |

As Eric Kutcher of McKinsey noted: "It wasn't that long ago that we were reading the headline that there will be no more need for software developers." Instead, as companies are producing more code than ever before, software developers who work with AI remain in high demand.

A wage premium for AI skills now stands at **62%**, and jobs requiring AI skills grew **69%**, against just 9% for the job market as a whole.

---

**Bottom line:** Demand is not simply "up" or "down" — it has **shifted**. The floor has dropped out for junior generalist roles, while senior engineers and AI specialists are more valuable than ever.
