Question: When employees of an international company travel to a different office, do they typically stay at a hotel near that office?

Answer: Usually yes, with a few concrete patterns depending on trip length and company size:

- **Short trips (1-5 days):** Corporate hotel near the office, usually pre-negotiated corporate rate. Company uses a travel platform (Concur, Navan/TripActions, Egencia) with pre-approved hotel lists — employee doesn't pick freely, picks from options within policy price cap (e.g. $200-400/night depending on city).
- **Medium trips (1-4 weeks):** Extended-stay hotels or serviced apartments (Marriott Residence Inn, Oakwood, Airbnb corporate housing) — cheaper per night than a regular hotel for multi-week stays, has kitchen.
- **Long relocations (>1 month):** Company either pays a relocation/housing allowance (flat monthly stipend, employee finds own place) or uses a relo vendor (Cartus, SIRVA) that arranges temp housing then permanent housing.
- **Distance from office:** Policy usually caps it at "within X km / Y min commute" — not literally next door, but reasonable walking/transit distance, since companies don't want expense reports with taxi receipts eating the travel budget.
- **Client-site vs HQ-office visits:** If visiting a client office rather than a company office, hotel is picked near the client, not near your home office.

Small companies/startups: often looser — just "book something reasonable, expense it," no formal platform.
