Question: How can I get European software projects while living in China?

Answer:

Yes, this is possible. The model is usually **China-based software/AI outsourcing + global sales**. You do not need to move to Europe first. The hard part is not engineering; it is **trust, positioning, and customer acquisition**.

Europe is actually a reasonable target because many companies need software, cloud, and AI skills but do not want to build everything internally. Outsourcing demand remains strong, especially for SMEs. [CBI](https://www.cbi.eu/market-information/outsourcing-itobpo/software-development-services/market-potential?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

For someone with your background (LLM, agents, full-stack, banking systems), I would not sell "cheap programmers". I would sell **AI engineering outcomes**.

## 1. Pick a narrow offer

Bad:

> "We provide software development services from China."

Too generic. You compete with thousands of agencies.

Better:

### AI automation agency

Example:

> "We help European SMEs automate internal workflows with AI agents in 4-8 weeks."

Projects:

- customer support AI agent
- document processing
- RAG knowledge systems
- internal Copilot
- ERP/CRM automation
- AI coding agents for legacy systems

Europe has strong AI adoption pressure and a shortage of AI developers, which creates opportunities for external providers. [CBI](https://www.cbi.eu/market-information/outsourcing-itobpo/artificial-intelligence-ai-and-machine-learning-ml/market-potential?utm_source=chatgpt.com)


### Legacy modernization

Your HSBC experience is valuable.

Offer:

> "We modernize old enterprise systems using AI-assisted development."

Many European companies have:

- old Java systems
- SAP integrations
- internal tools
- manual processes

This is less crowded than building consumer apps.

### Method A: LinkedIn outbound

You become their backend team.
