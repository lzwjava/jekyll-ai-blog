Question: What are the current usage rules and limits for OpenAI's ChatGPT free tier?

### 1. Free Tier Limits
*   **Model Access:** Free users typically have access to the standard model (often GPT-4o mini or a similar optimized variant) and limited access to more advanced models (like GPT-4o) during non-peak times.

### 3. Dynamic Adjustments
*   **Peak Times:** During periods of high traffic, OpenAI may temporarily tighten limits for free users to ensure service stability for all users.

Since limits can be personalized and dynamic:
1.  Look for notifications within the ChatGPT interface when you approach your limit.
2.  Check the **Settings > Data Controls** or **Subscription** page for any specific usage metrics provided by OpenAI.
