**Question:** Who is 郭跃华 (Guo Yuehua) in table tennis?

**Answer:**

郭跃华 (Guo Yuehua) is one of China's most celebrated male table tennis players of the 1970s–80s era.

He was born on February 3, 1956, in Xiamen, China, and played right-handed with a penhold grip style. He is a 6-time World Champion — three times in the team event, twice in singles, and once in mixed doubles — and also won two World Cup titles in singles. He reached World Ranking No. 1 in 1983.

His early development began at age 8, when a coach spotted him playing table tennis at school and noticed he could already execute a loop drive, which led to him entering the Xiamen Amateur Sports School. By age 15, he had joined the Fujian Provincial Table Tennis Team and began his professional career.

From the mid-1970s onwards, his rapid rise filled the void left by Zhuang Zedong's departure, and he won eight world championships in total between 1975 and 1983, becoming an idol for a generation. He was regarded by Swedish legend Jan-Ove Waldner as the most revered table tennis player of that era.

In 1983, at the 37th World Table Tennis Championships, he won the men's singles championship, mixed doubles championship, and men's team championship — after which he officially retired from the Chinese National Team.

He also won the inaugural Table Tennis World Cup, claiming the $12,500 first prize.

In short, Guo Yuehua was the dominant men's table tennis figure of his era and a giant in the history of the sport.
