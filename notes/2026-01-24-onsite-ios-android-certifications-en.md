Question: Are there any iOS or Android developer certifications similar to the Google Professional Cloud Developer that offer onsite exams?

Answer: Yes, there are professional-level certifications for both iOS and Android that offer onsite, proctored exam options at physical testing centers. While many vendors have shifted toward online proctoring, major providers like **Pearson VUE** and **Android ATC** continue to maintain physical locations for these exams.

---

## 1. Android Certifications

For Android, the primary "Professional" level credential that mirrors Google's Cloud Developer path is provided by the **Android ATC (Authorized Training Center)**.

* **Android Certified Application Developer:** This is the industry standard for Android specialization.
* **Exam Delivery:** You can take this exam onsite at over 130 authorized training centers worldwide. These centers provide a controlled environment with a physical proctor.
* **Google Associate Android Developer:** While Google previously offered this directly, it is currently undergoing transitions. For 2026, Google has shifted focus toward **Play Academy Certificates** and developer verification, but onsite options for original Android exams are largely handled by partners like Firebrand Training in specific regions.

## 2. iOS (Apple) Certifications

Apple's certification ecosystem is primarily managed through **Apple Training** and delivered via **Pearson VUE**.

* **App Development with Swift (Associate & Certified User):** These are the official Apple-backed certifications for mobile development.
* **Exam Delivery:** These exams are hosted on the **Pearson OnVUE** platform. While "OnVUE" is known for online proctoring, Pearson VUE’s partnership with Apple allows candidates to book exams at **physical Pearson Professional Centers** in many countries.
* **IT-Focused Certifications:** If you are interested in the deployment and management side (similar to Cloud operations), the **Apple Certified IT Professional** (covering iOS and macOS deployment) also offers onsite testing at select Pearson locations.

## 3. Comparison of Key Onsite Options

| Certification | Platform | Exam Type | Primary Focus |

### Summary of Differences

Unlike the **Google Professional Cloud Developer** exam (which focuses heavily on infrastructure and GCP services), these mobile certifications focus on **language proficiency** (Swift/Kotlin) and **OS-specific frameworks**. If you prefer the physical "testing center" experience to avoid technical issues with home proctoring, the **Android ATC** and **Apple/Pearson VUE** paths are your best choices.
