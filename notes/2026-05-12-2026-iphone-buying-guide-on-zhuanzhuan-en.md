## Is Zhuanzhuan (转转) Still Good in 2026?

**Yes, Zhuanzhuan is still active and reputable in 2026**, though it has both strengths and weaknesses.

### What Zhuanzhuan Does Well

Zhuanzhuan is a Tencent-invested second-hand trading platform and the pioneer of official quality inspection services in China. It operates three major quality inspection centers nationwide and employs over 2,000 real quality inspectors who perform comprehensive checks on second-hand items.

For officially inspected items, Zhuanzhuan offers a 7-day no-questions-asked return policy, professional warranty, and after-sales support, along with an "item-by-item inspection" model that generates detailed inspection reports for transparency.

Zhuanzhuan operates as a C2B2C model — a combination of C2C and B2C. For high-priced products like smartphones and laptops, it set a quality standard with Foxconn, providing quality inspections and transaction guarantee services. Second-hand products collected go through standardized management including recycling, cleaning, disinfection, and renewal.

As of 2024–2025, Zhuanzhuan has also been expanding into physical "Super ZhuanZhuan" offline stores, adding services like inspections, trade-ins, and refurbishing — allowing buyers to test products and verify quality in person before purchasing.

### Weaknesses / Things to Watch Out For

Zhuanzhuan features a large proportion of individual sellers, leading to inconsistent product quality — buyers must exercise their own judgment for non-officially-inspected listings. Its user interface is also considered less polished compared to competitor Xianyu, with occasionally less fluid operations.

Transaction security on Zhuanzhuan is backed by WeChat Pay escrow, which, while effective, is generally seen as slightly less robust than Xianyu's Alipay escrow system.
