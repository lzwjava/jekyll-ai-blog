**Question:** How can I improve my IELTS score from 6.5 to 7.5, based on my current results?

**Answer:**

Your current scores are:
- **Listening:** 6.0
- **Reading:** 8.5 ✅ (already excellent — no major effort needed here)
- **Writing:** 6.0
- **Speaking:** 5.0

Your overall is approximately **6.5**. To reach **7.5**, you need to push Listening to ~7.5, Writing to ~7.0, and Speaking to ~7.0. Here's a focused plan per skill:

---

### 🎧 Listening (6.0 → 7.5)
Your biggest gap after Speaking. The official feedback says:

You should listen to longer recordings such as interviews and films — with and without English subtitles — pausing and repeating as necessary, and noting words you didn't understand so you can review them.

**Additional tips:**
- Do timed IELTS Listening practice tests (Sections 3 & 4 are hardest — focus there).
- Train yourself to understand **implied meaning**, not just stated facts. The test loves inferencing questions.
- Use BBC Radio 4, TED Talks, and academic lectures daily.

---

### ✍️ Writing (6.0 → 7.0)
Practice writing to give information and make arguments, covering all the points needed, with supporting ideas and details. Reread your work and see if you can make your points clearer — sometimes rearranging ideas makes things clearer. Try joining or separating sentences using the right connecting devices.

**Additional tips:**
- **Task 2 is worth more** — master the essay structure (intro → body × 2 → conclusion).
- Learn **cohesive devices** properly (however, furthermore, in contrast) — don't overuse them.
- Build a vocabulary of **formal, precise words** — words can be similar in meaning but differ in formality, tone, and implications; focus on learning the best word for the task, situation, and what you want to say.
- Aim to write complex sentences intentionally: relative clauses, conditionals, passive voice.

---

### 🗣️ Speaking (5.0 → 7.0)
This is your **biggest bottleneck** and the hardest to improve quickly. The feedback identifies limited vocabulary range, frequent errors in complex grammar, and pronunciation issues.

Think of unfamiliar and abstract topics, learn as many English words related to them as you can, then speak about those topics using those words in meaningful "chunks." Notice words you find hard to pronounce and repeat them until you get them right.

**Additional tips:**
- Practice **Part 2 monologues** (1–2 min talks) daily on abstract topics: technology, environment, globalization.
- Record yourself — identify fillers ("um", "uh"), self-corrections, and unnatural pauses.
- Find other English speakers to have discussions with. If you don't know words for something, try to say it differently using other words — then note what you didn't know so you know what to learn next.
- Work with a tutor for mock speaking tests with real feedback.
