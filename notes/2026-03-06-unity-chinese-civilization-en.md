Question: What outstanding characteristic of Chinese civilization is reflected in the phrases "六合同风" (unified customs across all directions) and "四海一家" (all people under heaven as one family)? The correct answer is D (统一性/Unity), not B (和平性/Peacefulness). Please explain why.

Answer:

## Explanation

### The Two Phrases

| Phrase | Literal Meaning | Core Idea |
|---|---|---|
| 六合同风 | The six directions share the same customs | Cultural and social unification across all regions |
| 四海一家 | The four seas are one family | All people under heaven are unified as one |

Both phrases originate from classical Chinese thought and emphasize **uniform culture, customs, and identity** across a vast, diverse territory.

---

### Why the Answer is D — 统一性 (Unity), NOT B — 和平性 (Peacefulness)

The **key analytical focus** is on what these phrases are actually describing:

- **"六合同风"** — "六合" refers to the six directions (east, west, south, north, up, down), meaning *everywhere*. "同风" means *shared customs/norms*. → This emphasizes **cultural uniformity and cohesion**.
- **"四海一家"** — "四海" metaphorically means *all lands/peoples*. "一家" means *one family*. → This emphasizes **a unified identity and belonging**.

The **core message** of both phrases is: despite geographic and ethnic differences, China is **one unified civilization** with shared customs and a shared identity. This directly reflects **统一性 (Unity)**.

---

### Why NOT the Other Options?

| Option | Characteristic | Why It Doesn't Fit |
|---|---|---|
| A | 创新性 (Innovation) | Neither phrase discusses new ideas or creativity |
| **B** | **和平性 (Peacefulness)** | **These phrases do NOT discuss peace, non-aggression, or harmony with other nations** |
| C | 连续性 (Continuity) | Neither phrase discusses historical continuity over time |
| **D** | **统一性 (Unity) ✅** | **Both phrases directly express unified customs and identity** |

---

### Common Mistake: Confusing Unity with Peacefulness

Many students choose **B (和平性)** because "四海一家" sounds like it promotes harmony and goodwill — which *does* have a peaceful connotation. However:

- **和平性** refers to China's tradition of **peaceful coexistence with other civilizations**, not conquering or dominating others.
- **统一性** refers to the **internal cohesion** of Chinese civilization — one culture, one identity, one nation across diverse regions.

The phrases "六合同风" and "四海一家" are about **internal unification** of customs and people, **not** about foreign policy or inter-civilizational peace. Therefore, **D is correct**.

---

### Summary

> "六合同风" and "四海一家" reflect the **统一性 (Unity)** of Chinese civilization, because they emphasize that all regions share the same customs and all people belong to one unified family — highlighting cultural and national cohesion rather than peacefulness toward other nations.
