**Question:** What is The Hong Kong Jockey Club, and what does it do?

**Answer:**

**Overview**

Founded in 1884 to promote racing and riding, The Hong Kong Jockey Club (HKJC) is today one of the world's foremost racing and sports wagering organisations, as well as one of its largest charity donors, contributing very significantly to the economic and social development of Hong Kong.

**Integrated Business Model**

The Club has a unique integrated business model comprising four pillars: racing and racecourse entertainment, a membership club, responsible sports wagering and lottery, and charities and community contribution. Through this model, the Club generates economic and social value for the community and supports the government in combatting illegal gambling.

**Community Contribution**

In 2024/25, the Club returned a total of HK$39.1 billion to the community. This included HK$30.1 billion to the HKSAR Government in duty, profits tax and Lotteries Fund contributions, and HK$9.0 billion in approved charity donations — making it the largest single taxpayer in Hong Kong.

**Recent Milestones**

This year marks The Hong Kong Jockey Club's 140th anniversary — fourteen decades of standing alongside the people of Hong Kong. Throughout its history, the Club has not only brought the thrill of world-class horse racing to fans across the city and beyond, but more importantly, made lasting contributions to social progress.

**Strategic Direction**

In May 2025, the Club announced a strategic initiative to reposition itself as a global sports entertainment brand, integrating horse racing with equestrian events, music, and other leisure activities to promote Hong Kong as a hub for international exchange, connectivity, and tourism. This transformation includes a partnership with XIX Entertainment to enhance global outreach and diversify revenue beyond traditional betting.
