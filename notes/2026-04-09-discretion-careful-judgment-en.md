Question: What does “discretion” mean?

Answer:

**Discretion** means **the ability to make careful decisions** or **to behave in a way that avoids causing problems or revealing sensitive information**.

It has two common meanings:

### 1. Careful judgment (deciding wisely)

You choose what to do based on the situation, not rigid rules.

* The manager used **discretion** when approving the request.
* Teachers have **discretion** in grading participation.

👉 meaning: freedom + responsibility to decide wisely

---

### 2. Being careful with sensitive information (keeping things private)

You avoid saying things that could cause trouble.

* He handled the issue with **discretion**.
* Please use **discretion** when discussing internal problems.

👉 meaning: tact, confidentiality, not speaking carelessly

---

### Related expressions

* **use discretion** → be careful in decisions
* **at your discretion** → you decide
* **with discretion** → carefully, privately
* **lack of discretion** → said something you shouldn't

---

### Examples in workplace context

* Commenting about team conflict publicly shows **lack of discretion**
* Good managers value engineers who show **discretion**
* Sensitive info should be handled **with discretion**

So in short:

**discretion = careful judgment + knowing what not to say**
