Question: Which Etcher version should I choose for a MacBook Air M2?

Answer:

For a **MacBook Air with M2 chip** (Apple Silicon / ARM64 architecture), choose:

**ETCHER FOR MACOS (ARM64)**

This is the native version built specifically for Apple Silicon Macs (M1, M2, M3, etc.). It will give you the best performance and full compatibility without relying on Rosetta 2 emulation.

- The regular **Etcher for macOS** (listed as x64) is for Intel-based Macs. It can still run on your M2 Mac via Rosetta 2 translation, but it's not as efficient as the ARM64 version.
- Always download from the official site: https://etcher.balena.io/ (or https://balena.io/etcher) to get the latest safe version.

The MacBook Air M2 uses Apple Silicon (ARM64), so the ARM64 build matches your hardware perfectly.
