**Question:** How did China establish (确立) its socialist system in 1956?

---

**Answer:**

China's establishment of socialism by 1956 was the result of a multi-pronged transformation process launched after the founding of the People's Republic in 1949. It unfolded through three major pillars:

**1. The General Line for the Transitional Period (过渡时期总路线)**

After completing post-war economic recovery, the Communist Party of China put forth a general line for the transitional period, calling for the long-term realization of socialist industrialization alongside the socialist transformation of agriculture, handicraft industry, and capitalist industry and commerce.

**2. The Three Major Socialist Transformations (三大改造)**

- **Agriculture:** Peasants were organized into agricultural cooperatives, collectivizing land ownership away from individual farmers.
- **Handicrafts:** Small workshops and artisans were merged into cooperative organizations under collective or state oversight.
- **Capitalist Industry & Commerce:** Businessmen in major Chinese cities underwent "socialist transformation," being required to accept "joint state-private" ownership of their firms. By the end of January 1956, private industry accounted for only about 2% of China's industrial output, down sharply from 16% at the start of the prior winter.

**3. The First Five-Year Plan (第一个五年计划, 1953–1957)**

The period of officially designated "transition to socialism" corresponded to China's First Five-Year Plan (1953–57), which was characterized by efforts to achieve industrialization, collectivization of agriculture, and political centralization, with the development of heavy industry stressed on the Soviet model.

**The Culmination: The 8th National Congress of the CPC (September 1956)**

By 1956, the three major socialist transformations had been basically completed. In the same year, the convening of the Eighth National Congress of the Communist Party of China marked the formal establishment of the socialist system, signifying that New China had begun to enter a period of all-round socialist construction.

In summary, 1956 was the landmark year when China declared its socialist system fully established — economically through the elimination of private ownership, and politically through the CPC's consolidation of state power and ideological direction.
