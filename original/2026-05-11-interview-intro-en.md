## Intro

I was born in 1995. I ranked in the top 300 in the Guangdong Province Informatics Competition, failed the college entrance exam, dropped out of university, lost 500,000 RMB in a startup, led a team for two years to complete 50 small projects and earned enough to repay investors.

I was fired three times, though later reconciled and maintained contact with my bosses. I carefully worked for three years in foreign-bank outsourcing, suffered a 1 million RMB loss from buying a house, and corrected all my mistakes to pass fourteen subjects in self-study for an associate degree.
