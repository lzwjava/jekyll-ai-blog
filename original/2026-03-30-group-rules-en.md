Group Rules (Updated: 2026-03-30):

1. This is a community focused on AI, coding, and lifelong learning.

2. Members treat each other with equality and respect; the group owner may share more to keep discussions active.

3. We don’t pursue large numbers; the wisdom of a small group is enough to stay ahead of the times.

4. Topics related to circumvention tools or sensitive politics should be discussed in private chats to remain compliant.

5. The group is open 24/7; avoid @-mentioning others late at night to reduce notifications.

6. qclaw will be added later to enable more automation.

7. No ego — truth-seeking debate is normal; focus on ideas, not people.

8. Different viewpoints are welcome; conclusions should be based on experiments, data, and code.

9. Sharing is open by default; please remove sensitive or company information when necessary.

10. Members are free to leave at any time. Before recommending a new member, please communicate with the group owner in advance; there should be mutual understanding and a basic entry bar.

11. Content shared in the group is, by default, treated as MIT-licensed; members may reuse it for prompts, notes, or derivative work (state exceptions explicitly).

12. When a mistake is made, one needs to compensate by sharing a 10 CNY red packet in the group.
